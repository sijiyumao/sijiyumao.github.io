About Yumao Novels

Yumao Novels is a personal translation space created by sijiyumao (思及羽猫), dedicated primarily to sharing English fan translations of Chinese webnovels.

I hope to make stories I enjoy more accessible to English-speaking readers while keeping readers connected to the original works and their authors. Original publication sources are linked on each novel page whenever available.

Translation notice
Translations on Yumao Novels are unofficial fan translations. All rights to the original works belong to their respective authors and publishers. Please support the original works through the linked official sources.
