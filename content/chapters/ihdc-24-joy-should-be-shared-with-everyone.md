As the New Years break came to an end, the members of Fight gradually returned.

Before they came back, Ling Suyu had been visibly nervous. He’d hit the door when walking out, spill water over himself while drinking, and even trip while walking normally.

The only thing that didn’t change was his 0-deaths record in the game.

Seeing his anxiety, Tang Yu’an held back his laughter and introduced him to Fight’s main members in advance. Ling Suyu listened very seriously, even taking notes.

However, as the people within his notes appeared before him one-by-one, why did he suddenly feel like something wasn’t right?

In regards to Fight’s mid-laner Dou Jiayan, Tang Yu’an had introduced him this way: “A naughty kid with lots of nonsense to say. Though he’s a bit shy towards strangers, so you might have trouble getting along in the beginning. Once you become familiar he’ll stick to you nonstop.”

Then the “shy-towards-strangers” Dou Jiayan’s first words as he came through the door were “Where’s Feather?”, before his eyes locked onto Ling Suyu and he made a 100-meter-dash towards him.

Ling Suyu: ?

As for Fight’s manager Ye Xiran, Tang Yu’an’s introduction was: “Looks like a decent person, but he’s pretty mean-spirited, you should stay away from him.”

Then Ye Xiran, upon arriving, shook his hand and showered him with compliments, saying that captain Tang’s vision wasn’t half bad, and that after his arrival the club felt even more filled with passion.

Not only that, he’d also gotten a practice match with YJ so that Ling Suyu could take his revenge for the White Fox Cup.

These people are so nice!

—Ling Suyu commented in his heart.

That night, Fight’s jungler Yu He returned.

About him, Tang Yu’an had said: “One word: pretender. A few more, needs a beating. When you meet him, you have to remember to hold back your hands. It wouldn’t be good if you were banned from competing.”

Hearing this description, Ling Suyu couldn’t help but imagine some big boss with a cigar and sunglasses who looked at people with his nostrils.

Yet the Yu He in reality had clean and delicate facial features and a head of short, choppy hair. Upon seeing Ling Suyu, he raised an eyebrow and casually said: “Oh, Feather? I’m Yu He,” as he reached out a hand.

Ling Suyu nodded in a daze, shook his hand in a daze, and watched as he brushed past him, walking up the stairs to sort out his luggage, still in a daze.

In what way did this person need a beating?

He threw Tang Yu’an a gaze full of protest.

Tang Yu’an’s expression was innocent: “What?”

“They’re different from what you said.” Ling Suyu felt wronged. All the notes he’d painstakingly taken were in vain.

Tang Yu’an couldn’t resist chuckling, before putting on an exaggerated look of hurt: “I didn’t trick you. Once you get to know them you’ll understand, first impressions are always deceptive!”

“What about me then?” Ling Suyu suddenly felt curious, “What was your first impression of me like?”

“My first impression of you…let me think.” Tang Yu’an thought back to the scene of when he’d first met Ling Suyu. That little penguin in a white puffer jacket, though expressionless, was incredibly cute…

Nevertheless, in terms of actual first impressions, it still had to be: “You were super handsome when trash-talking Ma Mingxi during the White Fox Cup.”

Ling Suyu was stunned.

“At the time, I felt that you were cold, very cool, and very eye-catching,” Tang Yu’an said, his tone unconsciously softening, “After we truly interacted, I realized you were neither that cold nor that cool.”

Ling Suyu blinked. Was that good or bad?

Actually, what Tang Yu’an wanted to say was that before interacting, he didn’t think that he would be this cute.

But he was afraid that Ling Suyu wouldn’t like others calling him cute, so ultimately he said instead: “How should I put it? It made me want to protect you.”

“Huh?” Ling Suyu’s brain crashed, not knowing how to react to those words.

The rational part of him told him that Tang Yu’an could be referring to protecting him in-game.

Emotionally, however, he couldn’t help but overthink.

At that moment, the club’s doors were once again pushed open. Ling Suyu instinctively looked over only to see a slightly muscular man around Tang Yu’an’s height walk in with a backpack slung over one shoulder.

His hair was cut rather short, and his face looked like he wasn’t to be messed with.

Despite being similar to Tang Yu’an in height, he carried a sense of pressure that Tang Yu’an lacked.

Yet it was also different from the pressure he felt from Jiang Hao.

Jiang Hao gave off a sense of psychological pressure—it felt as if something bad would happen to those who crossed him, like being beat up.

On the other hamd, the person who just walked in was visually oppressive—so tall and well-built, similar to the pressure from a mountain.

After he walked in, he first nodded to Tang Yu’an: “Captain Tang.”

Then he looked towards Ling Suyu: “Feather?” As he spoke, he reached out a hand.

Ling Suyu nodded and gave a sound of affirmation, grasping his hand, before hearing his self-introduction: “My name is Mo Xiaoyun, and I play top lane. I look forward to working with you.”

Mo Xiaoyun.

Ling Suyu recalled how Tang Yu’an had introduced him: “Doesn’t speak much, has a good temper, and is a pretty reliable person.”

Was his temper…good?

Ling Suyu calmed down slightly: “En, I look forward to working with you.”

After the two shook hands, Mo Xiaoyun didn’t immediately leave, instead following up with:

“I’ll be blunt, Fight isn’t some shelter, and not just anyone can join. Your strength better measure-up to our club’s name.”

After saying that, he didn’t even give Ling Suyu a chance to respond. He once again nodded to Tang Yu’an before walking past the two of them, heading upstairs.

Ling Suyu: QAQ; 

Tang Yu’an coughed lightly: “Let me translate for him: Fight didn’t take you in because we pitied you, but rather because of your skills. You got into Fight with your strength, so play well in the upcoming competitions. Let’s work hard together, and get Fight the championship!”

Ling Suyu’s gaze towards him was full of distrust.

Tang Yu’an, seeing his expression, couldn’t stop himself from reaching out and patting him on the head: “Don’t overthink it and prove yourself using your strength. What else would we do as professional players?”

“En.” Feeling his comfort, Ling Suyu nodded.

Indeed, he’d always thought this way—in regards to those that didn’t acknowledge him, he didn’t need to explain anything. Directly using his skills to prove himself would suffice.

He’d also thought that during the White Fox Cup.

But…

As expected, he still needed cooperation from his teammates.

Knowing what he was worried about, Tang Yu’an said relaxedly: “What are you panicking for? You’ve got me.”

Those words instantly dispelled Ling Suyu’s anxiety.

Yeah, the situation was different compared to the White Fox Cup. This time, there was at least a support standing steadfastly by his side.

Ling Suyu let out a breath he’d been holding, before suddenly thinking of something else:

“Will Chen Jie come?”

“Oh, Ah-Chen. That’ll be up to him,” Tang Yu’an replied, “His hands should be better now, but he definitely won’t be able to play during the Spring Seasonals. Even if he did come back, it would be as a coach. I told him he could return whenever he felt like it, so it’s his decision.”

“Coach?” Ling Suyu only just realized this important issue, “Who’s Fight’s coach?”

He didn’t recall hearing Tang Yu’an bring it up.

Although how far a team could go also depended a lot on their coach, because UH’s coach had always forced Ling Suyu to play marksmen that he didn’t like, he didn’t have a good impression of coaches. When asking this question, his tone was clearly uncertain, as if he didn’t really want to know the answer.

Hearing the uncertainty in his voice, Tang Yu’an could generally guess what it was about— Ling Suyu’s Guardian Spirit, while not bad, could in no way compare to his godlike mastery of Cat Spirit. However, he continued to use the former during the White Fox Cup, which was likely the arrangement of UH’s coach.

He was scared, scared that Fight’s coach would be similar to UH’s, forcing him to use heroes without movement abilities that relied less on actual skill and more on version advantages.

Tang Yu’an suddenly felt like teasing him, and said: “Oh, our coach. Fight’s coach is pretty strict, and all the starters are completely obedient to him.”

Ling Suyu gulped, his expression already fading.

Tang Yu’an held back a smile, saying: “If he thinks that starters aren’t performing well, he’ll even go up and compete himself.”

Ling Suyu furrowed his brows, before…tilting his head, and showing a face full of confusion.

“He’s 1.86 meters tall, super handsome, to the extent of being able to enter entertainment circles.”

Ling Suyu: ?

Doesn’t something seem wrong here?

“But don’t you worry, he can play any position except for marksman, so even if he snatches others’ positions, he won’t take yours.”

Ling Suyu: !

Wait! This description!

“Are you talking about yourself?” Ling Suyu finally realized.

Tang Yu’an let out a “pfft” and burst out laughing, one hand clutching the back of the sofa beside him: “Hahaha you only just realized! How are you this cute…”

Uh oh, he accidentally let slip and called him cute.

It was a good thing Tang Yu’an had a thick face, and he pretended that nothing happened, continuing to smile as he said: “Yeah, Fight doesn’t have a coach. If you needed to pinpoint one, that would be me, are you surprised?”

“Huh?” Ling Suyu didn’t focus at all on the fact that Tang Yu’an called him cute, still shocked from the reality that Fight had no coach.

Fight didn’t have a coach?

They got into the top 4 without a coach?!

Fight was this strong?

Tang Yu’an was this strong?

Just how good were the prospects for this team that he’d joined?

Was he really not dreaming?

Tang Yu’an raised a hand and waved it in front of Ling Suyu’s eyes: “What’s up?”

Ling Suyu shook himself out of his thoughts, his eyes suddenly lighting up.

Then, in front of Tang Yu’an’s astonished gaze, he clearly and determinedly opened his mouth, saying: “I think we can win this Spring Seasonal.”

Tang Yu’an froze.

He’d also said these exact same words before.

Ling Suyu’s reaction at the time was—Don’t say that, you’re scaring me.

Yet now, these words came from his own mouth.

Although he was pleased at this show of confidence, Tang Yu’an was still curious as to why he said it: “Why all of the sudden?”

Was he that scared of the coach?

Ling Suyu raised a hand and rubbed his nose, feeling his heart and palms itch with a sort of eagerness: “I’m also not sure…but, I believe in you. If you’re the coach, then Fight can definitely win. I don’t know why I feel this way, it’s just…I just do.”

“En, we’ll win,” Tang Yu’an replied gently, understanding.

Humans are social animals.

When a bunch of them are together in a group, there will always be either those who lead or those who are led.

If a leader wasn’t strong enough, wasn’t confident enough, and couldn’t give the people they led a sufficient feeling of safety, then those being led would be uneasy, resist, and try to overturn the current leadership in search of a new one, or become leaders themselves.

On the other hand, if a leader was strong and confident, and could provide enough reassurance, then those under them would feel reassured receiving their guidance, believing that all things could be successfully accomplished under their lead.

Although Ling Suyu’s words sounded a bit suggestive, for a self-proclaimed leader like Tang Yu’an, this was an acknowledgement of his leadership.

As such, Tang Yu’an was very happy.

Originally, he’d only felt that Ling Suyu was quite cute, liking both his appearance and personality. Now, however, he had another thing to comment about him: he was good at recognizing and finding excellent leaders to lead him, a cream-of-the-crop follower.

People like him never thought of taking the lead themselves, but were especially sensitive towards those they followed. And once they decided their leader, they’d become unstoppable.

“Come one, let’s go to the training room,” Tang Yu’an said in a good mood, one hand on Ling Suyu’s back, “Once they’re done unpacking, we’ll do a five-stack. You can play any hero you’d like, like I said before, I won’t give my marksman any restriction on the heroes they want to use—”

“Yeah!”

When Mo Xiaoyun returned to the club, it was around 6 in the evening.

Originally, they weren’t going to train on the first day back from the break, and everyone would just shower and go to sleep after unpacking.

But because Fight had gained a new member, among Fight’s starters, be it those who acknowledged Ling Suyu or those who doubted him, they all wanted to gauge his strength.

And so, when Tang Yu’an proposed that they “five-stack before going to bed”, nobody protested.

Rather, there were those who agreed.

“Sure sure!” Dou Jiayan immediately logged onto his account, looking like he was itching to get started, “Let’s get used to playing together early on and catch YJ off guard!”

Yu He looked at him subtly from his left: “You forgot about Jie-ge this quickly?”

“No I didn’t!” Dou Jiayan refuted, “Jie-ge will always be my big bro! But since little Feather’s joined Fight, then he’s one of us! Little Feather’s also going to be playing as a starter in the Spring Seasonals, so isn’t it better if we get used to playing together sooner?”

Because he kept calling him little Feather, Ling Suyu felt a bit complicated—if he remembered correctly, wasn’t this mid-laner even younger than him?

He may have guessed what he was thinking, as Tang Yu’an retorted from the side: “You’re calling him little Feather? Who’s small and who’s big doesn’t matter, I’ve you’ve logged in then get the room open!”

“Oh——” Dou Jiayan obediently started a team room, dragging his teammates who were online into the room one by one.

Fight’s training room desks were placed in a row, with Tang Yu’an in the leftmost seat by the door and Ling Suyu beside him. To Ling Suyu’s right was Yu He, then Dou Jiayan, and even further was the top-laner Mo Xiaoyun.

After Dou Jiayan added Ling Suyu as a friend, he invited him into the room.

Right after he loaded in, he saw a row of “Fight”s.

Fight-Cloud; Fight-Swallow; Fight-Crane; Fight-Yuan; Everyone was on their main account, including Tang Yu’an.

In the midst of a bunch of domineering main accounts, his own ID appeared out of place, breaking the pattern, and unconventional, like a sudden firework.

His ID read…soft and chewy osmanthus cake orz; “Wait, let me change my ID!” Ling Suyu cancelled his preparation, using a name-change card.

While he was still a part of UH, his ID had been UH-feather, and everyone called him Feather. Probably because he was already used to it, he didn’t plan to change it, but Fight’s members had the first letter of their names capitalized, so, going along with the flow— Soon, Ling Suyu’s ID changed.

His teammates looked over: Fight-Feather.

Although they had long known since before the New Years that their captain had brought the marksman from UH into Fight, it’d never felt real.

Until they saw this ID.

It really was Feather.

Feather really became their teammate.

Would they really be able to work together well?

—Aside from Tang Yu’an, the others couldn’t help but think about this problem, including

Dou Jiayan who had long acknowledged Ling Suyu.

But one round would give them the answer to this question.

Once Ling Suyu hit [Ready], Dou Jiayan clicked on [Matchmake]. Soon, they got a match, and entered the game!

In the Glorious ranked matches, the first phase would of course be the ban phase, with both sides getting three ban positions. The banned heroes would be disabled for both sides.

During this ban phase, they could see the other side’s IDs.

Ling Suyu took a glance and was shocked.

On their side was a column of Fights, and on the other side was a column of S.MGs.

—The other side was also a professional team!

Although pro-players were often five-stacked, not all five-stacked matches were filled with professional players.

Their luck really sucked to get a professional team on their first try.

Oh well, the other side’s luck was the same.

“What the hell!” Dou Jiayan yelled in shock.

“S.MG? I remember they have a lot of tricks?” the jungler Yu He asked.

“Yeah, that’s why they’re [What the hell!][^1]” Dou Jiayan replied, “Hell knows what kind of lineup they’ll come out with!”

Ling Suyu only realized after a bit that [What the hell] wasn’t an insult towards the professional team they’d matched with, but rather how Dou Jiayan referred to SMG.

This…

He had to admit, at least it made them easier to remember.

“What the hell is in the top 8?” Mo Xiaoyun asked.

“Fourth place in the White Fox Cup,” Yu He responded.

“What the hell placed fifth in the last Summer Seasonals, right below us, don’t tell me you all forgot,” Tang Yu’an said.

“How could I! We fought against them in the group stages, and also in the first stage of the quarterfinals, and every single round they’d come out with some strange lineup that we’d have no experience against. I was pretty much about to throw up, what the hell!” After Dou Jiayan finished ranting, he stuck his tongue out and pretended to puke.

Ling Suyu pretty much didn’t recognize the three words [what the hell] anymore…

“But didn’t we win?” Tang Yu’an said with a smile, “This is pretty good. Since the enemy’s strong, we can prove our strength by winning, so play good this round!”

“Yeah yeah yeah,” Dou Jiayan replied reluctantly. Yu He and Mo Xiaoyun both let out an [en].

Ling Suyu blinked, feeling his teammates’ trust in Tang Yu’an.

Hearing their replies, he suddenly felt rather confident.

Although it was his first time five-stacking with them, and the other side was also full of professional players, he wasn’t worried at all.

The system had given the first ban choice to the other side, a.k.a. S.MG.

S.MG hesitated slightly before banning the version’s meta midlaner Phoenix Soul.

When it came Fight’s turn to ban, Tang Yu’an asked Dou Jiayan: “Yanzi, who do you want to play?”

Dou Jiayan responded with two words: “Anything’s fine.”

“Then go with Piano Demon,” Tang Yu’an quickly made the decision for him, before adding, “Ban Beast Master, snatch Piano Demon.”

“Ok.” Mo Xiaoyun banned Beast Master.

Then it was the other side’s turn to ban, and this time they didn’t even hesitate before banning Piano Demon.

“Within my expectations,” Tang Yu’an wasn’t surprised at all, “Then let’s not go for midlaners and go for junglers. Ban Realm God, Yu He uses Twin Demonblades.”

“Alright.” Yu He banned Realm God.

Dou Jiayan sprawled in front of his monitor, shouting: “Damn! It’s so hard to want to play the version meta!”

Just as he said that, the other banned another midlane hero, Array Specter.

Dou Jiayan: “...”

“Pfft,” Ling Suyu couldn’t hold back a laugh.

This season, the meta midlaners were much harder to deal with compared to the meta marksmen, so the heroes being banned were pretty much always midlaners with barely any marksmen.

Originally, Ling Suyu was the type to mind his own business, and UH’s midlaner didn’t have much of a reaction to their heroes being banned, so Ling Suyu always felt it wasn’t that big of a deal.

That was until he met Fight’s midlaner Dou Jiayan whose emotions were practically written across his face and was outspoken, pretty much saying whatever was on his mind.

From his reactions, Ling Suyu clearly felt the other’s pain of having his mid-lane heroes banned. And while he felt bad, he couldn't help but feel a fit of schadenfreude from watching the drama.

He couldn't stop himself from laughing, and immediately covered his mouth afterwards. It’s over, he thought, he laughed at his teammate during their first five-stack, there was no way he could get along with the others in Fight now.

Yet Dou Jiayan, hearing him laugh, immediately jumped up from his computer: “Who laughed? Feather, was it you? Which heroes are you good at? I’ll ban them right now!

Happiness should be shared!”

Ling Suyu: 0.0; “Stop messing around.” Before Ling Suyu even got the chance to respond, Yu He replied first, “Since we want to snatch junglers, we naturally have to ban junglers, ban Tempest Blade.”

Tang Yu’an also followed up with, “Feather, don’t get tricked by Yanzi, he rarely trains with the version meta. If he gets his hands on them, he might not even know how to use them.”

“Hey! Just because I don’t practice them doesn’t mean I don’t know how to use them!” Dou Jiayan exposed himself, “The reason why the meta’s the meta is because the heroes are clearly strong on their own. Even if I don’t practice them I can still sweep!”

“Oh please, we all know who took Phoenix Soul during the Summer Seasonal and got killed 11 times,” Yu He retorted.

Right after that, the taciturn Mo Xiaoyun also opened his mouth: “Yanzi, don’t mess around, the other side’s all professional players so treat this like a training match and let Feather take what he’s most comfortable with.”

Hearing those words, Ling Suyu froze, widening his eyes in disbelief.

The first thing Mo Xiaoyun had done was warn him that Fight wasn’t a shelter, and that he’d better have actual strength.

Ling Suyu had thought that the other didn’t like him, but…

—Let Feather take what he’s most comfortable with.

Was it possible that he just didn’t trust how strong he was and was waiting for him to prove himself?

Clearing up the misunderstanding, Ling Suyu couldn’t stop the corners of his lips from raising, and he joined his teammates’ discussion: “You all can pick first, I’ll pick last. I want to play Cat Spirit.”
