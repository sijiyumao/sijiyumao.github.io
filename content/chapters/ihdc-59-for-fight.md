The quarterfinals officially began on January 27. By January 26, all participating teams had arrived at the venue and checked into the hotels arranged by the organizers.

Then, on January 26, the drawing ceremony took place—the eight quarterfinalist teams had to draw lots to determine the order of play.

Currently, the schedule displayed on the big screen showed only numbers, with no team names.

For example, the first match on the first day was No. 1 vs. No. 2, the second match was No. 3 vs. No. 4, and so on.

“Yumao, what’s your favourite number?” Tang Yu’an asked while waiting in the wings for their turn to take the stage.

Ling Suyu thought for a moment: “7, I guess.”

Tang Yu’an: “Why?”

Ling Suyu: “No particular reason, 7 just feels special?”

Tang Yu’an: “Then I like 7 too~”

Ling Suyu: ?

Since the teams advancing to the quarterfinals this time were exactly the same as last year’s Summer Season quarterfinalists, the order of the draw was based on their rankings from last year’s Summer Season.

BigC, ranked eighth, would go first, while Winne, ranked first, would go last. Anticipation was at an all-time high.

“Giving it our all, who will be victorious! Welcome to the draw ceremony for the 13th BPL Spring Quarterfinals! The week-long quarterfinal matches are about to begin! This week’s match schedule will be determined by the results of today’s draw!” The host’s enthusiastic voice filled the venue. “First, let’s give a warm round of applause and welcome the first team to the stage to draw their number! Welcome, Big.C!”

Amid thunderous applause, Big.C’s players took the stage wearing their white uniforms accented with red and black patterns.

Known as the “Tank Squad,” this team not only favoured ultra-tanky heroes but also gave off a rugged, burly vibe—their mere presence exuded a commanding aura.

After their five starting members briefly introduced themselves, their captain walked to the draw table and pulled a small ball from a bucket.

The host took the ball from his hand, glanced at it, and loudly announced the number he’d drawn: “Number 8!”

Upon hearing this number, Big.C’s captain furrowed his brow ever so slightly.

Almost immediately, on the massive screen behind him, the 8s that had been displayed was completely replaced with “Big.C.”

“Sheesh, they’ve always been the ‘perennial eighth-place team,’ and now they’ve drawn number 8 again. Their feelings must be pretty mixed right now,” Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but remark.

【Next up is XY! I wonder what number they’ll draw this time? Let’s give a warm round of applause to welcome XY’s players!】

After Big.C’s players stepped off the stage with complicated emotions, they were immediately followed by last season’s seventh-place team, XY.

XY’s five starters took the stage wearing their red-and-blue uniforms.

This was a team that relied on finding their rhythm. While other teams tended to grow more fatigued and make more mistakes as the game went on, they were the opposite—they got better and better as the game progressed, becoming increasingly difficult to handle.

That was why their BO3 matches often saw them come back from a 1-0 to win 2-1.

Like igame’s captain, XY’s captain was a jungler with an unremarkable appearance.

As if in a hurry to get the formalities over with, he rushed over to the draw bucket in a few strides, quickly pulled out a ball, and handed it to the host without even looking at it himself.

After the host made out the number on the ball, he held it up for the cameras and shouted the number aloud: “Number 6!”

Almost immediately, every instance of “6” on the screen was replaced with “XY.”

It then became clear that the two teams that had just been drawn would face off in the fourth match the following day.

“Woah! That’s exciting!” Dou Jiayan couldn’t help but exclaim. “Wait until Winne goes up to draw. If it’s number 7, Da Diao’s reaction is sure to be even more priceless!”

“Or maybe we’ll go up and draw ‘1,’ and then Winne will go up and draw ‘2,’” Yu He said, mercilessly pouring cold water on his enthusiasm.

Dou Jiayan snorted, “We aren’t afraid of facing Winne—we’re here to win the championship!”

As the two discussed, the host’s voice rang out again: “Now, let’s give a warm round of applause to welcome our third team to draw their match—S.MG!”

Dou Jiayan: “Oh, what the hell!”

Mo Xiaoyun: “It’s already [what the hell]’s turn? That was quick.”

Yu He: “I wonder what the hell ‘what the hell’ will draw.”

Ling Suyu: “……”

Honestly, he was starting to lose track of what “what the hell” even meant anymore!

The SMG players came across as very lively as they practically bounced onto the stage, but their uniforms were black, accented only by a row of fluorescent green along the edges, making them quite eye-catching.

Unlike Big.C, who followed the script to the tee, and unlike XY, who rushed through the proceedings, S.MG gave a very detailed, and some might even say verbose, self-introduction, even telling the audience a joke.

Then, urged on by the host, their captain, mid-laner Linghu Gaomin, finally made his way to the draw table. He pulled a ball out of the bucket and visibly froze for a moment upon seeing the number on it.

“What happened?” Dou Jiayan craned his neck, curious to know why he’d reacted that way.

But immediately afterward, the host called out the number on the ball: “Number 4!”

Well, as Chinese people, didn’t the number 4 inevitably feel unlucky?

“What’s the big deal? We finished fourth overall last season, and it’s not like we ever complained?” Dou Jiayan spoke up in defense of the innocent number four.

Tang Yu’an, however, pointed out: “Three teams have finished drawing, and we still don’t know who’s playing whom on the first day.”

The first day’s schedule currently displayed on the screen was:

No. 1 vs. No. 2

No. 3 vs. S.MG

No. 5 vs. XY

No. 7 vs. Big.C

【Next up to take the stage for the draw is igame! Let’s continue to welcome them with a warm round of applause!】

Accompanied by the host’s unwavering enthusiasm, igame’s starting lineup took the stage.

igame’s uniforms were purple, truly dreamlike, outlining the shape of their dreams.

Not to mention their uniforms—even their logo was shaped like a heart. After all, the “i” in their team name was a homophone for “love[^1].”

After the entire team introduced themselves, igame’s captain, Ye Xunjie, swiftly ran forward to the draw bucket. He carefully rummaged through it for a while before pulling out a ball, his face lighting up with delight upon seeing it.

“What’s up? Are they facing Da Diao first?” Dou Jiayan speculated.

“Hey, don’t look down on Da Diao!” Tang Yu’an said, then quickly added, “Get ready, we’re up next.”

No sooner had he finished speaking than the host announced the number igame had drawn: “Number 1!”

“Okay, now I get why he’s so happy.” Tang Yu paused, then couldn’t help but complain, “Seriously, this is just the order of the matches, not the rankings! Is everyone really this superstitious?”

After Ye Xunjie led his team off the stage with a satisfied smile, the host called out to Fight: “Next up is Fight!”

Before he could even finish speaking, thunderous applause erupted throughout the arena!

Ling Suyu was taken aback, and so were the rest of Fight’s members.

Although the tournament was being held in City B and Fight’s club was based there, giving them home-field advantage, surely it couldn’t be this extreme, right?

There were plenty of clubs in City B—Winne, UH, and YJ were all there.

Amidst the thunderous applause and screams, the host finished his sentence: “Please welcome Fight’s players as they take the stage for the draw!”

Just as he said this, Tang Yu’an led his team onto the stage.

Their red uniforms were already eye-catching, but under the lights, they shone even more brilliantly, like blazing flames.

“Hello, everyone. I’m Tang Yu’an, Fight’s support, as well as the team’s captain.”

“Hello, everyone. I’m Cloud, Fight’s top laner.”

“Hey everyone! I’m Fight’s mid laner Yanzi! Wait no, Swallow!”

“Jungler, Yu He.”

“Marksman, Feather.”

Ling Suyu was at the very back of the line, right behind Yu He. While Tang Yu’an was introducing himself, Ling Suyu had mentally rehearsed “Hello everyone, I’m Feather, Fight’s marksman” several times. But when the microphone was passed to Yu He, Yu He simply said, “Jungler, Yu He,” which completely threw Ling Suyu off.

Before his brain could process it, the words “Marksman, Feather” just slipped out.

Ling Suyu regretted it so much. He wanted to add “from Fight”—otherwise, it just felt like something was missing.

Fortunately, the microphone was still in his hand, so he still had a chance.

With that in mind, he quickly added, “For Fight!”

“Pfft—” Tang Yu’an burst out laughing on the spot, raising his hand to cover his face.

I, marksman Feather, fight for Fight!

—With that sudden burst of chuunibyou energy, what kind of super-cute diet were you raised on?

Ling Suyu felt a bit embarrassed as soon as the words left his mouth, but the applause and screams from the audience, growing louder and louder, gave him a huge boost of confidence.

He nodded in satisfaction and handed the microphone back to Yu He.

Yu He was still tense as he reached out to take the microphone, but the moment he caught it: “Haha—”

Ling Suyu: QAQ;

Fortunately, the little mishap passed quickly, and Tang Yu’an headed to the draw station on behalf of Fight.

Before reaching into the draw bucket, he took a deep breath and frantically chanted to himself: “Pick number 7, pick number 7, because Yumao likes 7.”

After finishing his chant, he pulled out the ball and looked at it. Hmm…

Tang Yu’an handed the ball to the host with a pensive look on his face. His expression terrified his teammates—surely they weren’t going up against igame?!

It also terrified igame—please don’t come over here!

But in the end, the host announced: “Number 5!”

Number 5.

Not the number 7 that Ling Suyu liked, which was a bit of a shame.

But it wasn’t bad either.

Ling Suyu had been in the esports scene for five years, and had been silenced for five years.

Let this BPL tournament be the stage where you let all your brilliance from those five years shine!

Soon, the 5s originally displayed on the screen were replaced with “Fight.”

Fight’s opponent for the first day of competition was confirmed. It was XY, who had drawn number 6.

The match against igame would be the first game on the final day.

Seeing this result, Fight’s entire roster breathed a silent sigh of relief, and so did igame.

XY’s members, however, couldn’t help but frown.

Did they have to face Fight on the very first day?

Fight had been nothing short of terrifying this season. They had no idea how well they’d be able to hold their own against them.

SMG also breathed a sigh of relief upon seeing Fight’s draw. Their match against Fight was on the fourth day. Thank goodness.

However, they’d have to face igame, who’s been just as fierce this season, on the second day. Hopefully, they could stand their ground.

After Fight’s draw had concluded, only three teams remained to be drawn: the champion, runner-up, and third-place finisher from the last Summer Season.

First up was the third-place team, UH. The entire squad was dressed in blue uniforms, which looked great, but as they took the stage, the audience’s applause was clearly mixed with boos. Hearing such obvious booing, UH’s starters all looked a bit awkward.

They rushed through their self-introductions—so quickly, in fact, that it seemed almost hurried—and then captain Jiang Hao headed to the draw table to select their number.

【Number 2!】

“Number 2!” Dou Jiayan exclaimed in unison with the host, turning his head toward igame’s side. “So the first match tomorrow is UH versus igame? I’m kind of looking forward to that!”

As he said this, excited cheers erupted from the crowd; clearly, the audience was also eager for tomorrow’s opening match.

“UH vs. igame?” Tang Yu’an chuckled. “Well then, before we take on UH, let’s have igame give them a beating first!”

Ling Suyu blinked and took a closer look at the match schedule on the screen.

The first match of Day 1 was igame vs. UH.

The third match was Fight vs. XY.

Then, the first match of Day 2 was UH vs. Fight.

Ling Suyu couldn’t help but clench his fists.

Although he’d long been prepared to face UH, he hadn’t expected them to meet so soon.

But it didn’t matter. No matter who the opponent was, he would help Fight win!
