As he expected, the discussion centered on how Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu had formed a “couple bond” on their alt accounts to hide by pretending to be in a relationship.

Many posts in this thread not only included the hashtag #FightBotLaneDuoSharesSweetMoments# but also #FightsCaptainHasNoShame#.

No wonder the second trending topic was #FightsCaptainHasNoShame#.

However, the discussion in this thread wasn’t limited to just the alternate accounts and the “couple bond.”

Chen Jie:

Chen Jie felt he couldn’t keep scrolling through Weibo anymore. As an “insider” who knew Tang Yu’an was gay and that he liked Ling Suyu, but also that the two weren’t together, or rather, hadn’t gotten together yet, scrolling through Weibo was truly agonizing for him. He wanted to explain things for them, but was afraid his explanations would just make things worse.

He could already imagine what would happen if he were to reveal Tang Yu’an’s sexual orientation.

The esports community would likely be in an uproar, and the AnYu fans would probably celebrate all night long.

Chen Jie logged out of Weibo, then instinctively smacked Dou Jiayan on the head!

“Whoa!” Dou Jiayan was caught off guard by the slap and let go of his phone, letting it fall between his legs.

He clutched his head with both hands and turned to look at Chen Jie, his eyes full of accusation. He voiced out loud: “Why’d you hit me?!”

“You’re an esports player, not a celebrity. What do you need trending topics for? Is that going to make you play better or get you closer to the trophy?” Chen Jie said as he stood up, then gave Dou Jiayan a tug. “Stop looking at your phone. Go to sleep! We’ve got a match against UH tomorrow!”

Upon hearing the words “match against UH,” the accusatory look on Dou Jiayan’s face vanished instantly, replaced by intense fighting spirit: “Yeah! I’ll get a good night’s sleep—we’re going to crush UH!”

With that, he actually grabbed his phone and headed to his bedroom.

Chen Jie didn’t follow him back to his room right away. Instead, he sat back down and glanced up at Tang Yu’an, who was sitting across from him at the coffee table.

Now that Mo Xiaoyun and Dou Jiayan had both gone to their rooms to rest, and Ling Suyu and Yu He were playing on the PCs in the hotel’s computer room, only he and Tang Yu’an were left in the lounge.

The moment Chen Jie looked up, Tang Yu’an noticed his gaze. Guessing what he wanted to say, he spoke first: “Oh my, I’m trending.”

Chen Jie: “……”

Tang Yu’an: “I didn’t even realize I was being so gentle with Yumao. They’re quite good at digging~”

Chen Jie: “……”

Tang Yu’an: “Too bad I can’t use that alt anymore. I’ll ask Ye Xiran to help me buy two new ones!”

Chen Jie sighed, then spoke calmly: “How do you plan to handle this?”

“What?” Tang Yu’an blinked, looking completely bewildered. “Is there something that needs handling?”

Chen Jie raised a hand to rub the corner of his forehead, suppressing his irritation as he explained more clearly, “Those people calling you shameless…”

“So they actually found out,” Tang Yu’an sighed, then curved the corners of his lips in a teasing smile. “I thought I could keep it hidden a little longer~”

Chen Jie: “……”

Okay, he really shouldn’t have gotten involved in Tang Yu’an’s mess. The guy didn’t care at all what others thought of him.

Seeing Chen Jie looking as if he’d been cornered and was about to explode, Tang Yu’an finally stopped testing his limits and adopted a slightly more serious tone: “Don’t worry. It’s not like I committed murder or arson, or got caught on hidden camera in a hotel room with someone. It was just a harmless joke. Let them complain all they want, as long as it doesn't involve Yumao.”

“You checked into a hotel room with someone?” Chen Jie took a cue from Dou Jiayan’s knack for getting straight to the point.

Tang Yu’an was indeed caught off guard for a moment, then replied helplessly with two words: “I didn’t.” After a pause, he added, “Do I seem like that kind of person to you?”

Chen Jie: “Yes.”

Tang Yu’an: “……”



Seeing Tang Yu’an’s taken-aback expression, Chen Jie felt a little better and got back to the point: “I don’t know who bought that trending topic. If neither you nor Yumao care, I’ll just pretend I didn’t see it. But I wonder—does Yumao really not care?”

Fight had always respected its members’ personal space and free time, never confiscating members’ phones or stopping them from scrolling Weibo.

Although he knew Ling Suyu wasn’t in the habit of checking Weibo, he’d find out about this sooner or later—and who knew how he’d react.

They could have taken their time to process it; even the biggest news would eventually be forgotten.

But tomorrow was the match against UH. It wouldn’t be good if it affected Ling Suyu’s mindset.

As Chen Jie spoke, he looked at Tang Yu’an, who, upon hearing his words, instinctively glanced toward the computer room.

From where he stood, all he could see was the door to the computer room and the light spilling out from under it; he couldn’t see Ling Suyu inside.

So he only stared in that direction for a moment before quickly looking away and replying, “I know what you’re worried about, but Yumao isn’t as fragile as you think. He might care, or he might not, but whichever’s the case, he’ll give it his all during the game. Believe me, even if I went to confess my feelings to him right now, whether he accepts or rejects me, he’d still go all out to crush UH tomorrow.”



“Yumao may be sensitive, but he’s not fragile. He’ll sense others’ ill will toward him and take it to heart, but that will never affect his performance in a match.”

“If he truly has a style, then this is it.” At this point, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but laugh. “Don’t let his usually gentle demeanor fool you; in-game, he’s super bossy. It’s not him that’s obeying my lead, but rather me that’s cooperating with him.”

Chen Jie raised an eyebrow: “So when we play in a duo, am I the one obeying your lead?”

Tang Yu’an: “If you don’t like the word ‘obey,’ you can replace it with ‘follow.’”

“Why not ‘support’?” Chen Jie protested. “I was supporting you.”

“When has a marksman ever supported a support? It’s always the support who supports the marksman, while the marksman follows the support,” Tang Yu’an insisted.

“Fine, fine.” Chen Jie didn’t really care about their “dynamic” in the game.

And, in a sense, he had indeed been following Tang Yu’an all along.

When he played in a duo with Tang Yu’an, he felt more like a blade held in his hand. Wherever he wanted him to strike, he had to strike there.

But watching the replays of Fight’s matches these past few days, he noticed that even though they were both marksmen, Ling Suyu was so quite the maverick. Although Tang Yu’an always stood in front of him during team fights, during regular gameplay, Ling Suyu didn’t just follow wherever Tang Yu’an went. On the contrary, it was Tang Yu’an who was following him.

In particular, when Ling Suyu played marksmen with mobility, Tang Yu’an, who lacked it, sometimes struggled to keep up, which gave Chen Jie a strange sense of… satisfaction?

He can almost picture Tang Yu’an’s expression—a mix of exasperation and admiration.

If Tang Yu’an were to circle around Ling Suyu like that in real life, it would definitely be even more satisfying.

The other members of Fight may or may not know, but Chen Jie was well aware: Tang Yu’an might seem gentle on the surface, but deep down, he was arrogant. This guy had always thought himself a big shot—that everyone should look up to him, revolve around him, and let him guide their lives.

Although Chen Jie admitted that Tang Yu’an really was impressive, with both high IQ and high EQ, excelling not only at gaming but in other areas as well, and there was almost nothing to fault him for, aside from his sexual orientation.

But sometimes, he really wanted to see what he’d look like when he got his comeuppance.

With that in mind, Chen Jie said pointedly, “You’re going to confess to Yumao right now? Fine. I’d love to see if your judgment is really that spot-on. If you scare Yumao and it affects tomorrow’s match…”

“Yumao!” Tang Yu’an called out, turning his head.

Chen Jie was terrified: “Don’t! Forget I said anything! Competition comes first! If you dare to say anything reckless that bothers Yumao, I’ll tear you apart!”

“Hm?” Hearing the call, Ling Suyu tentatively stood up, pushed open the computer room door, and poked his head in.

Chen Jie instantly fell silent, his gaze toward Tang Yu’an filled with warning.

Tang Yu’an, however, seemed oblivious to his look. Meeting Ling Suyu’s somewhat bewildered gaze, he smiled gently: “I forgot to tell you—it looks like your alt account isn’t going to work anymore. I’ll have Ye Xiran buy you a new one. Do you want Jungle Hunter’s beta skin? I see you don’t have it on either account.”

“Yes!” Ling Suyu’s eyes lit up, and the words almost slipped out before he could stop them. Only after speaking did he realize something, and he hesitated, asking, “It’ll be really expensive, right?”

“It’s just a game account, how expensive could it possibly be?” Tang Yuan’an said, immediately sending a message to Ye Xiran on her phone.

Chen Jie watched him, knowing he’d been played again. He raised a hand to his temple and sighed silently.

It made sense. Tang Yu’an wasn’t the type to act recklessly. Even if he trusted Ling Suyu, he wouldn’t take that risk.

After leaving the message, Tang Yu’an said, seemingly offhandedly, “Our opponent tomorrow is UH. Go to sleep early tonight. Stop gaming, and don’t waste your time on boring stuff like scrolling Weibo or browsing forums.”

“Okay~” Ling Suyu obediently agreed.

Thinking about how he’d soon have Jungle Hunter’s closed-beta skin, the corners of his mouth curled upward, and he said cheerfully, “Don’t browse too much either and get some more sleep too, okay?”

Don’t browse too much either.

Either.

Meaning he’d already done so.

Tang Yu’an lifted his eyelids and glanced at Chen Jie—See? He doesn’t care.

Chen Jie’s expression was a bit complicated—I just don’t get you guys.

Ling Suyu truly did plan to go to sleep.

His alt account had been exposed. Playing with it was just like playing with his main account—it drew way too much attention.

He didn’t like drawing attention to himself.

As for Weibo, he’d seen those trending topics and clicked through to check them out. Seeing everyone bashing Tang Yu’an put his mind at ease… not.

The main thing was, although there were plenty of comments criticizing Tang Yu’an, none of them were written in a harsh, offensive tone. Most were just good-natured teasing. He figured that, given how thick-skinned Tang Yu’an was, he wouldn’t take any of it to heart.

So, after hearing what Tang Yu’an had to say, he cheerfully shut down his computer and went to bed.

In the living room, Tang Yu’an met Chen Jie’s gaze with a hint of smugness: “Doesn’t it feel like Yumao’s not only unaffected, but even a bit happy?”

Chen Jie rolled his eyes and said irritably, “Well, since you’re so fearless, why don’t you just confess to him right now!”

Tang Yu’an chuckled. “Who’s the one who’s actually afraid…” But he only made one sarcastic remark before immediately softening his voice. “If I confess, Yumao certainly won’t be affected, but I will.”

Chen Jie was taken aback for a moment.

“If I get rejected, I’ll be heartbroken,” Tang Yu’an said earnestly. “At that point, forget about leading the whole team—I won’t even be able to lead myself.”

“Where’s your usual confidence?” Chen Jie asked, surprised.

He was genuinely surprised.

In all the time he’d known Tang Yu’an, he’d never seen him truly worry about anything.

Even when he’d injured his hand and was gritting his teeth through the pain to help Fight in the semifinals, Tang Yu’an had still carried that same air of confidence—the kind that could be a bit annoying: “Just a few more matches. Hang in there. It’s okay if you slack off a little during the game. I’m here. I’ll do my best to take the pressure off you. Let’s win the championship together, and then we’ll go to the hospital.”

In the end, he couldn’t hold on. As a support, Tang Yu’an, even with a damage-dealing support, couldn’t simply carry the team without him.

But even after the defeat, Tang Yu’an still smiled confidently: “Looks like we’ll have to wait until next year to win the championship. Let’s head to the hospital first, immediately.”

Then, after hearing the doctor describe the severity of his hand injury, Tang Yu’an showed a look of frustration for the first time, but only for a moment. The next instant, he said decisively, “Just focus on your treatment, I’ll cover all the medical expenses. Don’t worry about the games—it’s fine if you skip lousy tournaments like All-Stars and the White Fox Cup. As for the championship trophy, it’s right there; it’s not like anyone’ll steal it. When your hand heals, that’s when we’ll go claim it.”

Tang Yu’an had always been like this. No matter what happened, even if the sky itself were to fall, he would never panic or lose his composure. Instead, he’d figure out how to prop the sky back up and then carry on with his plans.

He had clear goals and moved steadfastly toward them, calmly overcoming every obstacle that stood between him and his objectives.

It was hard to imagine someone like that getting so discouraged by a rejected confession that it would affect his performance in a match…

Wait a minute, wasn’t that how people normally reacted?

He’d never expected Tang Yu’an to be surprisingly normal when it came to romance.

At that thought, Chen Jie couldn’t help but laugh out loud: “I’m suddenly kind of looking forward to seeing your reaction after Yumao rejects you.”

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow: “But I think he’ll say yes. The reason I’m not confessing right now is simply that I don’t think the time is right yet. A confession should be a victory ceremony, not a battle cry, especially between two people of the same gender. I hope that after I confess, the next line isn’t ‘Let’s date,’ but ‘Let’s do it.’”

Chen Jie: “……”

Okay, he’d misunderstood. This jerk was still as confident, narcissistic, and shameless as ever!

Just listen to what he’s saying! Even though he knew he was serious about this, it really sounded no different from what a total scumbag would say!

For someone as outstanding as Ling Suyu to be with this jerk would really be a step down!

Chen Jie grabbed a pillow from the couch and smacked it right across Tang Yu’an’s face with a “poof”!

Tang Yu’an: ?

Chen Jie: “Sorry, my hand slipped.”

“I see. No worries.” Tang Yu’an smiled, then flung the pillow back at Chen Jie. “Oops—my hand slipped too.”

Then Tang Yu’an was beaten all the way to his bedroom by a furious Chen Jie, crying out for mercy.

“I’m sorry, I was wrong! I’ll never do it again! You cheated by attacking too quickly, at least let me shield-up first!”

The match between Fight and UH was the first game of the next day, scheduled to start at 1:00 p.m.

So, for the first time ever, Fight’s entire roster got up before noon. They finished breakfast by 9 a.m., quickly reviewed yesterday’s match, then began analyzing UH.

UH, the team Ling Suyu had been with for a full five years, was as familiar to him as could be.

So, that day, Ling Suyu stepped in as a guest coach: “Yao Teng, top laner, loves playing lane dominators like Black Angel. He plays aggressively, the kind who’ll push into the tower to secure a kill even on low health. For him, a one-for-one trade is a win, so you absolutely can’t back down when facing him. If you do, he’ll dominate you. On the other hand, if you can put pressure on him, he’ll make more mistakes, making it easier to find openings and further widen the gold gap.”

“Wang Jingxuan, mid laner, likes to hit and run just like Yanzi. He often makes risky moves to secure kills. For example, when playing Nine-Tailed Fox, he’ll use Flash to dash into the middle of the enemy heroes, sacrificing himself to break up their formation. His attacks are unpredictable and hard to dodge, but if you can dodge them, it’s easy to counter-kill him.”

“Cui Rao, the jungler, has solid skills but struggles with pacing. He’s always rushing in before the enemy’s health is low enough. But that’s exactly why his ganks feel so sudden—if your reaction is even a split second too slow, he’ll get the kill. Like Yu He, he mainly plays assassin-type junglers, though he can play warrior-type ones too, just not very often.” After a brief pause, Ling Suyu added, “Oh, and his Blade Soul is terrible.”

“Haha.” Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but laugh, and Dou Jiayan also looked thoroughly entertained.

Listening to Ling Suyu trash-talk UH’s players was just so satisfying.

Mo Xiaoyun even chimed in with a quip: “It sounds like everyone on UH is crazy. Blade Soul requires a cool head, definitely not something they;d be cut out for.”

Yu He gave a soft “Hmm,” a hint of pride visible on his face.

“Next up are Jiang Hao and Wen Xu.” When mentioning these two, Ling Suyu paused briefly before continuing, “Although UH’s style is wild—and they’re all pretty wild—these two are more level-headed. Jiang Hao has the ability to calmly unite the entire team, turning each member into an asset rather than a liability. As for Wen Xu, he’s basically just a shield for Jiang Hao, and doesn’t really have a style of his own.”

“Got it,” Tang Yu’an summarized casually. “Although UH, like us, is marksman-centric, we’re a team where everyone supports the marksman, while they’re a team where everyone is dominated and exploited by the marksman.”

“One person leading four mad dogs?” Chen Jie snorted derisively, “I really don’t want to lose to such a twisted team.”

“We won’t. We’re a pack of five wolves, what do we have to fear from mad dogs?” Tang Yu’an said.

Four mad dogs…

Ling Suyu paused slightly.

Such a comparison was really…

So, was the reason he couldn’t fit in at UH because he couldn’t rein in the mad dogs?

Wait a minute, that thought was getting a bit dangerous…

Stop it! Stop it right now!

“I can’t believe Yumao stayed in such a crazy team for so long,” Dou Jiayan sighed. “If only I’d known sooner—if I’d known sooner, even if it meant paying a huge penalty, I would’ve poached you from UH.”

Chen Jie glared at him: “You’d have loved for me to retire sooner, wouldn’t you?”

Dou Jiayan only then realized he’d said the wrong thing and immediately raised both hands in surrender: “No! No! That’s not what I meant!”

“He was thinking of paying the penalty clause—” Tang Yu’an chuckled, “It’s not too late to pay now. How much was UH’s penalty clause, anyway?” he asked Ling Suyu.

Ling Suyu answered obediently: “All the money I’ve earned at UH, plus 100,000 on top of that. I didn’t make much at UH, so…around 500,000?”

500,000—that was no small sum. But it represented five years of Ling Suyu’s life.

Dou Jiayan was stunned on the spot: “Were you that cheap?”

He’d probably made well over 500,000 in just two years at Fight…

Ling Suyu blinked: “Cheap?”

Come to think of it, his salary at Fight really was in a league of its own compared to UH—it was way higher…

But he was a starter at Fight and a benchwarmer at UH, which made all the difference.

“F*ck! UH is so damn inhumane!” Dou Jiayan’s impression of UH was getting worse and worse. “500,000! I’d spend that 500,000 straight up on a missile to blow their club to bits!”

“You’re getting ahead of yourself. Do you think you can just buy a missile with money? Especially with a measly 500,000?” Compared to him, Tang Yu’an, who had ‘lured’ Ling Suyu into Fight, remained unusually calm. “Keep your anger in check and focus on the game. As for that 500,000, save it to treat us to a meal after the match!”

“I don’t even have 500,000 in my savings account…” Dou Jiayan scratched his head, feeling a bit guilty.

Tang Yu’an: “Fun fact: a meal doesn’t cost 500,000.”

“Haha—” Ling Suyu couldn’t help but laugh.

At 19 years old, Yanzi clearly hadn’t yet stepped into the ‘real world.’ Fight gave him a chance to unleash his youth while preserving his innocence.

It felt like Fight was more of a home than just a club.

Afterward, Chen Jie got Fight’s members to watch a few of UH’s matches, then they arrived at the venue for the Spring Quarterfinals before 12:00.

There was still over an hour until the match began, but the venue was already packed with people.

Finally, after the venue’s technical staff inspected all the equipment brought by both teams, the match officially kicked off.

Just by standing on stage, the two teams already exuded an intense feeling of tension.

On Fight’s side, their red-and-black team uniforms—vibrant and commanding—resembled a raging fire blazing in the darkness, or a demon lord standing tall amidst the flames of hell.

On UH’s side, their blue uniforms accented with white resembled a sky dotted with wispy clouds, or angels descended to Earth.

If you didn’t know what UH had done, seeing the two teams standing there would give you the distinct impression of angels versus demons.

But who was the true angel angel?

The true angel was one who transformed into a spotless feather, drifting down to the earth—which appeared scorching yet was actually warm—and unleashed the fiery wrath of vengeance upon the heavens, where angels no longer exist.
