You guys aren’t still going to treat Feather as UH’s substitute, are you?

Upon hearing this, YJ’s players all froze for a moment before realizing—yes, in their minds, Feather was still UH’s substitute, that same “benched” substitute who would almost never get a chance to play unless something unexpected happened to the starters.

Although many substitutes are just as skilled as the starters, they were often benched primarily to maintain the team’s overall balance.

But to put it bluntly, substitutes were substitutes precisely because they couldn’t help the team perform better than when the starters were on the field.

So, as YJ’s starters, the players subconsciously believed that Feather wasn’t as good as they were.

The reason Ma Mingxi repeatedly set himself up for failure and got thoroughly humiliated was based on this same mindset.

Feather was now a starter for Fight, and they all clearly knew that Feather’s negative reputation was the result of UH’s deliberate manipulation.

Yet they still let their guard down after Feather joined Fight.

They assumed that while Fight wouldn’t necessarily get weaker by suddenly switching marksmen, they certainly wouldn’t become stronger than before.

The reality, however, was that their team, known as the “best at early-game play”, was completely overwhelmed by Fight in the early stages.

They even nearly got snowballed.

In the end, Fight couldn’t push down YJ’s main tower in one go because their minions had run out, and the next wave hadn’t arrived yet.

Even so, at this stage of the game, unless YJ could defend their main tower, farm up, and win a team fight, it was nearly impossible to stage a comeback against these odds.

“This match is over. Let’s play well in the next one.” YJ’s mid laner had already given up.

“What kind of talk is that!” Ma Mingxi was frustrated with his teammate’s lack of resolve. “The match isn’t over yet, we still have a chance!”

“Ruicong, look at Mingxi. He’s all swollen from all the face-slapping, yet he hasn’t given up.

How could you give up?” Captain Cheng Cheng praised Ma Mingxi while casually taking a dig at him.

Ma Mingxi: “……”

“Exactly, how could we give up!” said jungler Xiao Hong. “We agreed to treat today’s practice match like an actual competitive one. Are you going to quit mid-game during the competition too? Take a cue from Ma Mingxi!”

Alright, another jab.

Ma Mingxi let out a curse and could only turn his anger into a bold declaration: “Once I respawn, I’ll take down Feather right in front of you all!”

“Enough, shut it,” top laner Lin Hao couldn’t hold back anymore. “If you keep making bold claims like that, your face won’t just be swollen—it’ll look like a pig’s head.”

“Let me translate for Mingxi,” said captain Cheng Cheng. “If one’s left cheek is already swollen, your right cheek might as well swell up too, right, Ximing Ma?”

“F*ck, f*ck, f*ck! You’re all ganging up on me!” Ma Mingxi exploded.

“Alright, let’s get serious. ” Cheng Cheng cut in at the right moment, “The purpose of practice matches, to put it simply, is to ensure our win rate in the league. So, don’t take the wins and losses too seriously, but don’t give up easily either. I’m talking to you, Ruicong. I know this game will be tough for us, but if we can get through it, we won’t be so intimidated when we face the same situation during an actual competition. On the other hand, if we can’t get past this hurdle, then we’ll really be done for.”

“Got it.” YJ’s mid laner, Gu Ruicong, replied, taking a deep breath to steady his nerves and prepare to farm safely in preparation for the late game.

However, Fight wasn’t about to give them that chance.

“Push the mid and bottom lanes all the way and go for a decisive push!”

After the entire Fight team returned to base to finish up their builds, they split into two groups under Tang Yu’an’s command and charged toward YJ’s main tower.

At this point, YJ had only one tower left on the top lane; all their other towers had already been taken down.

Once the entire YJ team had respawned, facing Fight’s relentless onslaught, as if they were met with a pack of ravenous wolves.

“Clear the minions first! ” Cheng Cheng shouted a reminder.

However, before his words had even finished, Dou Jiayan’s Phoenix Soul flew in, bringing with him searing flames and devastating damage!

The entire Fight team followed him into the tower. Mo Xiaoyun stunned the top laner with a “Heavy Bone Hammer”, while Tang Yu’an locked down the mid laner with “Patrol.”

Yu He’s Double Phantom activated his ultimate, “Phantom Transformation”, followed by “Shadow Displacement” and “Double Stab”. A transparent figure flashed across the screen several times, and one by one, YJ’s fragile heroes fell!

【System】Double Phantom, one kill!

【System】The enemy Wind Guardian has been slain!

【System】Double Phantom, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

Ma Mingxi died for the fourth time. This time it wasn’t Feather who killed him, but he felt worse than ever.

Because he’d been so focused on Feather, he’d completely forgotten that the other members of Fight weren’t pushovers either!

YJ had originally planned to clear the minions first, but suddenly realized that if things continued this way, they’d be wiped out again before the minions were even cleared. They had no choice but to fight back!

As soon as the top laner recovered from the stun, he immediately used “Chains of Darkness”

to counter-control Mo Xiaoyun, then charged straight at Ling Suyu, who was standing behind Tang Yu'an, with his ultimate!

Ling Suyu expertly fired an “Explosive Shell”, darting from one side of Tang Yu'an to the other. After dropping a “Hunter's Trap” to slow Black-Armoured Scythe’s movement speed, he immediately activated his ultimate, “Roar of the Desert”, and unleashed a series of auto-attacks at him from across Tang Yu'an!

Once Desert Hunter’s attack speed kicked in, bullets rained down one after another without pause. Combined with the buff from his ultimate, YJ’s top laner watched helplessly as his health bar plummeted at an alarming rate. He instinctively tapped Flash, but it was too late.

He too went down.

In fact, the cooldown on his Flash began the moment he died, proving he had used it, but the Desert Hunter’s bullets had struck him first.

What terrifying attack speed!

【System】Desert Hunter, one kill! Swift as lightning!

【System】The enemy Black-Armoured Scythe has been slain!

After taking down the top laner, Ling Suyu didn’t even spare a glance at his fallen body; he immediately used “Hound’s Charge” to rush toward the enemy jungler.

His teammates had exhausted all their available abilities. Now, it was time for the AD to shine!

Come on, time to go on a killing spree! Biu biu biu— 【System】Desert Hunter, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Blade Soul has been slain!

【System】Desert Hunter, triple kill! Unstoppable!

【System】The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain!

【System】The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us!

“So cool!” Dou Jiayan, having taken two tower hits, used his ultimate to land safely outside the tower. As he followed the remaining minions into the tower to push it down, he continued to praise him, “Feather that was so cool! It gave me goosebumps!”

“Hunter’s damage in the late game is pretty insane,” Mo Xiaoyun remarked as he returned to the tower, having taken all the remaining tower damage. “To be honest, top laners would be genuinely scared facing a marksman like that. Thank goodness Feather is on our team.”

“So it wasn’t in vain, sacrificing my precious life for Feather’s 0-death streak?” Yu He raised an eyebrow.

“Yu He, you really make someone want to punch you!” Tang Yu’an teased. “You’re just taking advantage of Feather not knowing you well enough. You block blows for your teammates all the time just to have an excuse to rest your hands.”

Ling Suyu paused in surprise: “Is that even possible?”

He’d been surprised when he’d heard about it before. Would a player really take a hit for a teammate just to give his own hands a rest?

“That’s just how HeHe is!” Dou Jiayan confirmed. “He’s taken hits for me too!”

“It’s not really to rest his hands, more of a bad habit he picked up when we used to play together before the team was even formed,” Mo Xiaoyun explained, “Back then, Yu He was just getting into Glorious and wasn’t very good at it. After we teased him a lot, he started ‘trolling’. He’d die for his teammates whenever he could, leaving the rest to them so he could take it easy. Once it became a habit, whenever he saw a teammate about to die, he’d instinctively step in to block the attack. He probably doesn’t even realize it himself as it happens, it’s practically muscle memory.”

“But being able to block attacks for teammates with such precision is pretty impressive…”

Ling Suyu marveled sincerely.

That took real skill!

“HeHe is just that good!” Dou Jiayan praised, “Honestly, his skills aren’t any worse than the enemy jungler’s, right?”

“No need to flatter me to death,” Yu He countered. “I’m just playing casually.”

“Here we go again. Look at this poser.” Tang Yu’an said, glancing sideways at Ling Suyu beside him. “I told you, he’s just asking to be punched!”

Ling Suyu blinked, suddenly feeling curious. “When did you guys first meet?”

Mo Xiaoyun answered, “Captain Tang and Jie-ge met first. Back then, they’d play any position when duo queuing. Later, they were randomly matched with me. They thought I played pretty well, so they added me as a friend and we started trio-queuing. Since I only played top lane, Captain Tang started playing mid and support. Later, I wanted to go pro, and so did Captain Tang. After analyzing a bunch of teams, Captain Tang suddenly said, ‘Let’s start our own team’.”

Ling Suyu: ??

Damn, was it really that “random”?

Mo Xiaoyun continued, “But a team with just the three of us obviously wouldn’t work. We needed at least a starting lineup. So Captain Tang personally wrote a recruitment post and posted it on Weibo and the official forums. A week later, that idiot Yanzi came knocking on our door.”

Dou Jiayan: “Hey!”

Mo Xiaoyun: “I didn’t say anything wrong. To actually go for an ad that looked so much like a scam, if that’s not being an idiot, what is?”

Dou Jiayan pondered for a moment: “To be fair, when I got there and saw there were only the three of you, I did think it was pretty sketchy. But since I was already there… I just went along with it. What else could I do?”

Ling Suyu: “……”

You’re also pretty random!

By this point, YJ’s main tower had already been taken down, and the word “WIN” appeared on the screen, followed by Desert Hunter’s MVP highlight reel.

However, Ling Suyu’s mind was already elsewhere; he’d been piqued by Fight’s “founding history.”

“Then what?” he asked impatiently.

“Well… we were still missing one person, right?” Tang Yu’an opened his mouth, “So I just went to a nearby internet café and picked someone at random, asking if they wanted to go pro. I asked about a dozen people, and one of them said he’d give it a shot, so I brought him to the club. That guy was Yu He.”

Ling Suyu: “……”

Oh my god! It doesn’t get any more random than this!

Wait, Fight was clearly really strong, right? It was just too absurd for a group of randomly-thrown-together people to be this good!

As if he knew what Ling Suyu was thinking, Tang Yu’an chuckled softly and said, “Feather, do you believe in fate?”

“No.” Ling Suyu didn’t give him any face.

Tang Yu’an didn’t seem to mind and continued, “I met both Chen Jie and Mo Xiaoyun while climbing to the Glorious tier. Their skills were guaranteed. As for that weirdo Yanzi, after we found him, we checked his rank, and he was also in Glorious. Anyone who could solo-queue their way to Glorious has some real talent. But Yu He? I really just picked him up on a whim.

When I found him, he had pretty much never played Glorious before. In fact, he was playing a different MOBA. We used alt accounts to carry him all the way from Novice to Glorious.

When he first reached Glorious, he knew fewer than five champions. But look at his skill now —even if he’s not among the top players in his position, he’s at least upper-middle tier, right?

Back then, I thought to myself, maybe it was destined from the start. That a team named Fight was destined to emerge in the BPL, a team destined for greatness, destined to win the BPL championship!”

Ling Suyu listened, completely dumbfounded.

Tang Yu’an continued, “Then came the White Fox Cup. Chen Jie had a hand injury, Jiang Hao was suspended, and you suddenly appeared on our radar.”

“Um…” As Ling Suyu listened, he actually found himself somewhat convinced. “Plus, my contract just happened to expire this month…”

“That’s right, you just happened to part ways with UH right before the Spring Seasonals,”

Tang Yu’an said, a smile tugging at the corners of her lips. “See? Isn’t this fate?”

Ling Suyu fell deep in thought; However, his teammates couldn’t take it anymore.

Mo Xiaoyun: “Bullshit. It’s just a coincidence. But we’re definitely going to win the championship.”

Dou Jiayan: “Even though HeHe hadn’t played Glorious before, he was top ten server-wide in another MOBA. Don’t make him sound like a total noob.”

Yu He: “It’s fine, I’m a noob, carry me.”

Ling Suyu: “……”

Tang Yu’an shrugged: “Say what you will, but my encounter with Feather was definitely fate.”

Ling Suyu opened his mouth, hesitated, and ultimately said nothing, though the corners of his lips turned up slightly.

Whether it was coincidence or destiny, he was grateful that Tang Yu’an had noticed him.

His appearance at the White Fox Cup was indeed an unexpected coincidence, but the one who saw and wanted him was none other than Tang Yu’an.

So, whatever Tang Yu’an said, that’s what it was— If this really was fate, then let them go with the flow and lead Fight to the pinnacle!

If this was merely a wonderful coincidence, then let them join hands and, in tears and sweat, forge the most dazzling, most brilliant championship trophy for Fight!

The practice match had ended, but neither Fight’s members nor YJ’s starting lineup had left; they all chose “Play Again”, returning to the room where the previous game had begun.

It was just practice matches. They could play as many rounds as they wanted. When you get the chance to spar with a top-four team, you’ve got to take every opportunity you can.

But Tang Yu’an wasn’t in a hurry to start. Instead, he teased his opponent first.

【Ally】Fight-Yuan: Ximing Ma?

【Enemy】YJ-Neigh: F*ck!

【Ally】Fight-Swallow: Hahaha, Ximing Ma!

【Enemy】YJ-Neigh: Shut up! I’m changing my Weibo handle as soon as practice ends

today!

【Ally】Fight-Yuan: What are you going to change it to? Send it here first so we can

give you some feedback?

【Enemy】YJ-Neigh: Cut the crap! Let’s go again!

【Ally】Fight-Yuan: Not setting any flags this time?

【Enemy】YJ-Neigh: What flag? Esports is played with your hands, let your skills do

the talking!

Upon seeing this, Ling Suyu raised an eyebrow, while Tang Yu’an let out a “Whoa”.

【Ally】Fight-Yuan: Why are you copying our Feather’s way of talking?

Only then did Ma Mingxi remember: “Esports is played with your hands”—those were the exact words Ling Suyu had thrown at him during the White Fox Cup.

No wonder it felt so familiar when he typed it out…

Damn it, could he take that back?

Just as he was frowning, wishing he could find a hole to crawl into, Ling Suyu, who had been silent the whole time, spoke up.

【Ally】Fight-Feather: Thanks for speaking up for me when UH posted that

announcement. It’s great to have the chance to play against you again. Good luck in the Spring Seasonals.

Ma Mingxi froze.

The rest of YJ was stunned too.

Did Feather really just say that?

The same Feather who wouldn’t even blink when calling someone out, and who would dismissively type “Got it” in response to a challenge sent his way?

Over on Fight’s side, his teammates, who’d come to know Ling Suyu to a certain degree, upon seeing this message, also froze.

“Haah, Feather, you’re way too gentle,” Dou Jiayan sighed. “It’s exactly because you’re so gentle that UH was able to bully you.”

“Ma Mingxi spoke up for you?” Mo Xiaoyun, who clearly didn’t have a habit of checking Weibo, asked, “That kid doesn’t think before he speaks, but his character seems decent enough?”

Tang Yu’an sucked air in through his teeth: “Are you… happy because you enjoyed competing against Ma Mingxi, or because you had so much fun crushing him?”

Ling Suyu had been lost in the sentiment of “putting the past behind him,” but when he suddenly heard Tang Yu’an’s remark, he couldn’t help but burst out laughing.

Then, mimicking Tang Yu'an’s tone, he replied, “Of course it’s the latter—but mostly, it’s just that I really enjoy playing with Fight!”

“Got it, you mean you enjoy playing with me!” Tang Yu’an effectively excluded everyone else from “Fight”.

“Hey, hey, hey, watch it!” Dou Jiayan warned.

“There you go again, picking on Feather,” Yu He muttered under his breath.

“Stop messing around, get ready! We’re just waiting for you!” Mo Xiaoyun urged.

“Alright, alright.” Tang Yu’an clicked “Ready”.

Soon, the second practice match between Fight and YJ began.

In this game, YJ tried banning Phoenix Soul and snagging Harpist, but Dou Jiayan picked Deepsea Faerie and managed to prevent the Harpist from gaining an advantage in the lane.

Tang Yu’an wanted Ling Suyu to “hide his true strength”, so Ling Suyu resisted the urge to pick Cat Spirit and locked in Desert Hunter instead. He barely managed to hold the tower in the early game, dragged the match into the late game, and wiped out YJ completely!

In the third game, YJ had had enough and finally banned Desert Hunter. Dou Jiayan got Harpist, while YJ took Deepsea Faerie.

Everyone thought this would turn the tide in the mid lane, but to everyone’s surprise, when Dou Jiayan’s Harpist realized the mid lane was unsustainable, he simply stopped defending it. He left the mess to his own top laner and jungler, went to the bot lane to go on a rampage, secured a triple kill, and then carried the team all the way to victory, “stealing” the MVP from Ling Suyu.

YJ was stunned!

Ling Suyu was stunned!

Dou Jiayan was stunned too: “I’m so strong!”

Mo Xiaoyun and Yu He, who had spent nearly the entire game farming and pushing lanes:

“……”

Never mind, never mind. If they wanted to beat someone up, they could wait until after practice.

By the way, Ling Suyu picked Netherworld Shadow for this game.

Just because he doesn’t like burst-damage marksmen didn’t mean he can’t play them.

In the fourth game, with three ban slots, YJ took a full half-minute to decide, ultimately banning Phoenix Soul, Harpist, and Desert Hunter.

On the Fight side, they banned Tempest Blade, Realm GOd, and Beast Master while picking up Twin Demonblades.

Then, Yu He let YJ’s more fragile heroes experience firsthand the terror of being dominated by Twin Demonblades!

In a 1v1, he’d one-shot you even at full health!

After losing four games in a row, YJ’s players started questioning their very existence. So much so that even after winning the fifth game, they didn’t feel like they’d actually won, and instead felt like Fight had let them win.

Fight on the other hand really hadn’t been holding back. They were simply… tired.

Dou Jiayan was playing Nine-Tailed Fox, but halfway through the game, he suddenly had the illusion that he was playing Deepsea Faerie. After using all three of his skills, he felt like his “stacks were maxed out”, so he immediately activated his ultimate, only to hit the nearby jungle monster instead, catching himself by surprise.

Later, when YJ pushed forward, Ling Suyu just stood motionless in the fountain. After Yu He respawned, he didn’t move either. The two stood in the fountain, staring at each other, quietly waiting for the other side to finish their push. They even wanted to urge them to hurry up.

Ling Suyu couldn’t leave the tower, as stepping out meant certain death.

Yu He clearly had mobility and a dash ability, yet he was too lazy to even flash over just for show.

Noticing their exhaustion, Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow and suggested to YJ, “Let’s take a break and continue later?”

YJ had no objections, because they were tired too. Although they’d won this game, it felt like they’d played it in a daze. They had absolutely no idea how they’d managed to pull it off.

So, after finishing five games, Fight and YJ went their separate ways, some to the bathroom, some to wash their faces, some to get a drink, planning to rest for half an hour before resuming the battle.

Of course, they weren’t going to just lie around doing nothing for that half-hour.

Once everyone had finished stretching, Tang Yu’an said, “Let’s review the game.”

Dou Jiayan covered his face: “I’ll throw it out there first, I’m bad.”

Mo Xiaoyun: “Wow, what happened to all that confidence you had before the match, saying you could carry the entire team?”

Ling Suyu sat down obediently, waiting for the post-game analysis.

Over on YJ’s side, back in his room, Ma Mingxi angrily grabbed his phone, changed his Weibo username, tossed the phone onto the bed, and then flopped down onto the bed himself.

And so, YJ’s fans, especially Ma Mingxi’s, suddenly noticed that his username had changed yet again.

He’d changed it once after the White Fox Cup to “Ma Mingxi is a jerk”, and now it read, “If I ever blindly set another flag then I, Ximing Ma, will write my name backwards!”
