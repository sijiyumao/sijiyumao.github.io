The week-long “White Fox Cup” soon came to an end, and the final champion was Winne, with YJ as the runner-up.

This result wasn’t surprising, as during the world finals in October, two of China had two teams that got the right to compete. One of them was YJ, and the other one was Winne.

However, unlike YJ, who only made it to the quarterfinals, Winne not only entered the final match, but also won 3-2 against their opponent. In the midst of cheers back home, they won the world championship.

Yet, what really surprised people was UH, who everyone thought would be able to get third place in the “White Fox Cup”. In the end, they only ranked second-to-last, and the only opponent they won against was a team that lost during the group stage of last June’s Summer Seasonal tournament, a team that wasn’t even in the top 8.

Ling Suyu didn’t have the habit of scrolling the forums, so he had no idea what was going on.

He only knew that in a few days, he would have to officially say goodbye to UH.

As soon as the “White Fox Cup” concluded, pretty much as soon as he stepped foot into the club, he started packing his luggage.

During this time, the manager found him again and spent a long time trying to painstakingly convince him to stay, saying “with your strength, even if you found a new team, you would only be a substitute, and your salary will only decrease rather than increase, blah blah blah”

Ling Suyu just packed his things without listening to what the other was blabbing about, and after the manager finished speaking, he replied faintly: “I’m good.”

Although he didn’t like speaking, and kept his silence on many matters, preferring to go with the flow, he had his own persistence. Once he’d made a decision, he would stick to it and not be swayed.

The manager had spoken until his mouth ran dry and his throat felt like it was on fire. Once he heard that reply, he almost fainted from anger.

So everything he just said was all for nothing? It felt like he was punching cotton; not to mention creating a splash, not even a single sound was made.

“Fine, go! You’ll definitely regret this!”

In the end, the manager spat out these words and left Ling Suyu’s room.

A few days later, after getting the manager and coach’s permission, Ling Suyu took his leave in advance, dragging his long-packed suitcase behind him as he left UH’s headquarters.

As he passed the training room, he subconsciously looked back.

Through the transparent glass door, he could see the teammates he’d fought shoulder-to￾shoulder with. Suddenly, he felt overcome with emotion, unable to believe that he was about to say goodbye to this place for good.

At this moment, the jungler Cui Rao stood up to leave the training room. As he pushed open the door, he saw Ling Suyu with his suitcase and paused for a moment. But he didn’t say anything, only brushed past him and headed towards the washroom.

Ling Suyu also pretended that he didn’t see the other, averting his gaze and determinedly walking towards the main doors.

In the winter noon, the sun shone brightly, but there was no warmth. The sunlight spilled through the large glass doors, illuminating Ling Suyu’s overly youthful and gentle face, dying his long lashes golden.

Ling Suyu raised his hands, pushed open the doors, and walked out of the building, allowing the doors to close behind him without looking back.

This was B City. Ling Suyu wasn’t a local; he’d come from S City.

At 16, he was picked up by UH and, in order to better facilitate training and fulfill the details on his contract, he came alone to B City and directly moved into UH’s player dormitory.

In the past five years, aside from long holidays, he’d pretty much never left the dorms, treating them as his home.

As such, after leaving UH, he suddenly didn’t know where to go.

Ling Suyu dragged his suitcase around as he wandered the streets of B City, before stopping in front of an internet café.

He hesitated for a moment, before ultimately choosing to go inside.

He’d walked this path many times before, but as expected, the most familiar place to him was the internet café.

Similarly to the past, he booked a small private room, locking the door behind him once he’d entered. He turned on the PC, adjusted the chair’s height, smoothly double￾clicked a certain icon titled 《Glorious Heroes》, logged into the game, proficiently clicked on “ranked match”, and waited for the match-making…

Wait a minute?!

Ling Suyu stared dumbly at the “Match Successful!” displayed on his screen, his brain blanking out for a second.

Come on! Why was he so smooth at this!

He should have been looking for other teams’ recruitment information, not gaming!

How did this happen!

Screaming_hedgehog.gif However, things had already progressed to this point, so it was no use no matter how much he screamed internally. On his screen, the ban phase had already started.

He didn’t want to be reported for afk-ing, so he could only quickly put on his headphones and enter gaming mode.

But before he could even make out which hero the enemy had banned, he spotted four consecutive messages in the game’s chat.

【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler: Feather??

Ling Suyu eyed his own ID.

UH-feather Ling Suyu facepalmed.

It’s over, he logged into the wrong account!

He accidentally logged into his professional account!

How could he be this careless? No wonder UH hated him!

But things had gotten to this point, so he could only…..

【Ally】UH-feather: yeah, it’s me…I logged into the wrong account QAQ 【Ally】tangyuan with sesame filling: pfft.

【Ally】can die but no need: damn, are you tryna be cute?

【Ally】if I have feet I can run: screenshot for the record!

【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler: hope you can carry this round!

Ling Suyu let out a “Ss——”.

Don’t say that, now he had a lot of pressure, 《Glorious Heroes》wasn’t the type of game that could be won with just one person carrying.

At this moment “tangyuan with sesame filling”’s avatar flashed with the speaker and microphone icons.

At the same time, a message was sent in the chat.

【Ally】tangyuan with sesame filling: Feather, can you voice chat?

Ling Suyu didn’t reply to the message but directly opened his speaker and mic: “Can you hear me?”

“Yeah.”

A young male voice sounded from his headphones, bright and magnetic with three parts teasing and seven parts frivolity. Yet it didn’t sound annoying but rather filled with charisma.

Hearing this voice, Ling Suyu froze for a moment before reacting, instinctively replying:

“That’s good.”

“Which hero do you want? I’ll get it for you.” The other spoke again, and as he spoke more, he couldn’t mask the delight in his voice.

Ling Suyu didn’t think too much about it, only assuming that the other was a regular player who was excited to be matched with a professional.

He hesitated for a moment, before finally listening to his inner voice: “Is Cat Spirit ok?”

“Ah, the kitty, of course it’s ok.” The other side replied without hesitation, “Then I’ll use the spider, which is fast enough to keep up with your movement speed, and can increase your basic attack damage, perfect.”

Ling Suyu blinked, only then realizing that the player voice-chatting with him planned to play support. Furthermore, from the sounds of it, he was quite experienced in the role.

“I’ll go in and control them, you go ‘biu biu biu’ and they’re done for.” The support said, “No worries, with my protection, even if the top, mid, and jungler all died, you wouldn’t die.”

Top, Mid, Jungler: ?

At these words, Ling Suyu couldn’t help but let out a “pfft” in laughter.

Although he didn’t know how strong this support was, he liked his personality.

Hearing the other’s laughter, Tang Yu’an also couldn’t resist raising the corner of his lips.

That’s right, “tangyuan with sesame paste” was Fight’s captain Tang Yu’an’s alt account.

As New Year’s was coming up, no one in the club was in the mood to practice. Tang Yu simply let all of Fight’s members and staff go early for a few extra days of vacation.

What should he do during the break?

Before he even came up with an answer in his brain, his hands had already betrayed him and opened 《Glorious Heroes》's application and clicked on ranked matches.

This was really…..

Though 《Glorious Heroes》has hurt me a thousand times, I still treat him as my first love…..

Never in a thousand years did he think that in his first match, he would match with UH’s substitute marksman—Feather.

No matter who Tang Yu’an forgot, he would never forget Feather and the deep impression his battle records left.

Forever 0 deaths, forever the losing side’s MVP.

Originally, Tang Yu’an had planned to rest for two days before considering whether or not to poach Feather into Fight, and how exactly to go about poaching, but he didn’t expect that the person would appear right in front of him.

Perhaps the heavens knew he was hesitating, and wanted to urge him to make a decision.

This was pretty good.

Whether or not Feather was worth poaching, after his match, he trusted that he would have his answer.
