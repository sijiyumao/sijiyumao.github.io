As the distance between himself and Fight’s headquarters drew closer, the familiar voice of UH’s manager sounded from the phone: “Where are you right now?”

Ling Suyu didn’t wish to reveal any information before he and Fight had officially signed the contract, so he ignored the question and asked back: “Why are you looking for me?”

The manager understood that he didn’t want to answer and didn’t pursue it, deciding to cut to the chase: “What’s the meaning behind Fight’s Weibo post?”

Ling Suyu: ?

After checking Weibo, Ling Suyu naturally knew which post the manager was referring to, but…why ask him?

In fact, he hadn’t even asked what UH’s Weibo meant yet!

“What Weibo? What does Fight posting on Weibo have to do with me?”

After knowing there was a team willing to take him in, Ling Suyu grew more confident. As he spoke, his words were filled with guardedness and detachment.

He didn’t enjoy insulting others, but that didn’t mean he wouldn’t.

If it weren’t for the fact that he’d stayed at UH for five whole years, he would have long started cursing as soon as he picked up— I’d always wholeheartedly devoted myself to UH, yet this is how you treat me?

Ling Suyu acknowledged that Jiang Hao had strengths that he himself lacked, so when UH nominated Jiang Hao as a starter and relegated him to the role of substitute, while there was unwillingness in his heart, he hadn’t uttered a single complaint.

But for UH to imply that their poor performance during the White Fox Cup was his fault?

Not a chance!

Yet the manager continued to be unreasonable, bringing up one sore topic after another:

“Stop pretending! Did you make an agreement with Fight early on? Didn’t you purposefully put on an act during the White Fox Cup? You never played so poorly during the regular practice matches! Explain yourself, haven’t you long been in cahoots with Fight? Did you also get Fight to send that repost?”

With a “snap”, Ling Suyu felt himself lose the last bit of rationality he had.

“I played poorly? Say that again, I played poorly?” Ling Suyu’s tone had never been so cold.

Despite his voice being naturally gentle, giving others a soft impression whenever he spoke, it was currently cold as a solidified layer of frost. “I never had any agreement with Fight, but that’s not important. The main point is—do you at UH genuinely believe that I was the one with the poor performance? Do you really believe that? Even though MVP doesn’t mean much, I fought 17 matches in the White Fox Cup, and got MVP 17 times. If I missed getting MVP even once, then you could say I played poorly! But in actuality? If you think you can perform better than I did, then by all means! I wish you success! Goodbye! See you never!”

After that, Ling Suyu didn’t bother giving the other a chance to reply, immediately hanging up and blocking the number.

Unbelievable, just because the cat never showed his claws, they thought he was easy to bully!

By this time, Tang Yu’an had already parked the car and removed the keys.

Though Ling Suyu had not put the phone on speaker, the two had been close together, and Tang Yu’an hearing was quite good. He’d heard the UH manager’s spiel pretty much word-for-word.

He could imagine just how angry Ling Suyu had to be after hearing all that, so he fully supported the other firing back, but…

“If you think you can perform better than I did, then by all means! I wish you success!”

“Goodbye! See you never!”

—How could this little penguin be so cute?

Tang Yu’an bit down on his laughter, pressing the unlock button on his keys and opening the car door: “You should’ve insulted them back a long time ago. You must’ve held those words back for a long time, right? Don’t you feel better after letting them out? That manager of yours is pretty impressive, offering his head on a silver platter for you to hit.”

Actually, Ling Suyu felt rather sorry after insulting UH’s manager. Of course, he wasn’t feeling sorry towards the manager, but instead Tang Yu’an.

He didn’t want Tang Yu’an to see his “bad” side, much less make him wait this long…

And yet, before he could even apologize, he heard Tang Yu’an’s words. The guilt and unease in his heart instantly disappeared, and he even wanted to laugh.

Ling Suyu glanced at Tang Yu’an in exasperation, correcting him in a small voice: “He isn’t ‘my manager’ anymore.”

“That’s true, UH has nothing to do with you anymore,” Tang Yu’an immediately corrected, before chuckling, “Alright, let’s get off. Allow ‘your captain’ to give you a tour around your new home.”

“Okay~” Ling Suyu was infected by his smile, the corners of his lips raising uncontrollably, and he near-impatiently opened the car door and got out.

The Fight eSports Club looked truly magnificent from a distance, and from close-up, it looked less like a regular club and more like a competition venue.

The neon red sign that said “Fight eSports Club” was quite high up, and one couldn’t see it without craning their necks if they were close.

Fight’s logo was also pasted on the walls beside the front doors. It was incredibly large, pretty much covering the entire wall, and was a bright red just like the neon sign.

Upon first glance, this logo appeared to be a mass of flames. Only upon a closer look would you notice that said flames were made up of a combination of the letters in “Fight”.

UH’s full name was Under Heaven. Translated into Chinese, it would probably be “Tianxia”, similar to “All Under the Heavens”. So be it their logo or their team uniform, it was all sky-blue.

Ling Suyu liked blue, so even if UH’s image in his heart had completely collapsed, he could never hate blue because of UH.

But in eSports, perhaps one should be more passionate?

As Ling Suyu approached the door, he couldn’t help but reach out his hand, feeling the logo on the wall.

Despite it being an ardent red, it felt cool to the touch.

After all, it was winter right now.

However, not only did Ling Suyu not find it cold, he even enjoyed the smooth feeling.

Standing beside him, Tang Yu’an gently tapped him on the back: “Do you like it? In the future, you can wear it on your clothes, on your head, or even pin it to your chest. Come on, let’s head inside. If you catch a cold and get sent to the hospital to keep Chen Jie company, not only will he not thank you, he’ll also kill me.”

Ling Suyu couldn’t hold back a laugh: “I don’t catch colds that easily.”

“Don’t jinx yourself, take that back.”

“Oh, I take it back.”

“Good boy~” Tang Yu’an said as he lifted the hand still holding the keys and unlocked the door.

Ling Suyu stood quietly by his side and watched, only coming to a realization after the door was opened: “Is there no one inside the club right now?”

“Didn’t I mention it already? Fight’s on break, much less a five-stack, I have trouble even finding a duo. It’s a good thing you’re here.” Tang Yu’an flipped the switch, and the lights in the hallway turned on one-by-one, the dim lobby immediately brightening.

Yet perhaps because the walls and the floors were black, there was a subtle hint of danger in the light.

How should he put it? Didn’t this have an even greater gaming atmosphere compared to UH’s bright and airy environment?

Either way, Ling Suyu’s heart was already with Fight. Even if Fight’s club had turned out to be a dog’s nest, it would still be a million times better than UH!

So when Tang Yu’an asked, “How is it? This isn’t worse than UH, right?”, Ling Suyu blurted out: “It’s great, I like it here.”

Tang Yu’an froze before letting out a chuckle: “So fast to respond? Looks like you really like it. Come on, I’ll show you the dorms first.”

As he spoke, he carried Ling Suyu’s suitcase with him to the second floor.

Yep, Ling Suyu’s suitcase was still in his hands.

The first thing he did after he got out of the car was to retrieve Ling Suyu’s suitcase from the trunk. Originally, Ling Suyu had reached out to receive it, but he was stopped: “Shoo.”

Ling Suyu, seeing the other’s persistence, let him do as he pleased, but he still felt bad about it.

To him, Fight’s willingness to sign with him was like being gifted hot coals in the winter; you could even say they saved his life.

After all, he had been in the eSports circle for 5 years and still didn’t have any notable battle records. The timing of his termination was equally awkward, and it was as if he’d been standing on the edge of a cliff, about to fall down at any moment.

At the most difficult time in his life, Fight reached out their hand. By all accounts, he should be grateful, should reciprocate, should dedicate everything to Fight.

But what was this situation?

With the way Fight’s captain was treating him, he nearly misunderstood himself to be some impressive player, one that Fight had spent a lot of effort poaching from some amazing team, not one that had been picked up after they coincidentally played a match together.

As Ling Suyu followed Tang Yu’an upstairs, walking down a long hallway and passing by room after room, he couldn’t help but ask: “Doesn’t Fight have any intentions to sign a substitute?”

Did they trust his strength that much?

“We do,” Tang Yu’an answered without even turning his head back, “But a match that you don’t win with your own hands feels meaningless, don’t you think?”

“I…” Ling Suyu didn’t know how to answer.

He indeed felt that way, but fighting in a competition wasn’t about him alone. If by any chance he couldn’t help Fight win, and in fact dragged them down…

As though he knew what he was thinking, Tang Yu’an suddenly stopped, adding: “A lot of people have said that you’re just afraid of dying. But I can tell that you want to win. In that case, I’ll help you win. Not Fight, but you.”

Ling Suyu froze.

Tang Yu’an turned around, smiling at him: “I’ll say those words again. A support helping their marksman is heaven’s law and earth’s principle! Though, in return, you’ll bring back a trophy for me, right?”

“I’ll try my best! No, I’ll do it!” Ling Suyu replied seriously, even raising his hands and curling them into fists.

Tang Yu’an couldn’t help himself and let out a “pfft—”.

Not good, how could he be this cute?

Even falling for such an obvious trick…

“Alright, alright, let’s talk about competitions after the break, just rest up these next few days,” Tang Yu’an said as he opened the door in front of him.

Ling Suyu subconsciously followed his line of sight towards the room. Before he could even react, he heard Tang Yu’an’s next words: “From now on, this will be your room, exclusively yours.

…

The translator has something to say:

Although he knows his own strength to some extent, feather’s time at UH has still left its mark on his self-esteem…

Here’s to our little penguin’s new beginning!
