Those who shared the same level of gaming awareness always carried a certain kind of chemistry.

Often, Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu could pick up on each other’s thoughts without even speaking—or, as Tang Yu’an put it, they had a ‘tacit understanding.’

For example, when it came to predicting where the enemy jungler might appear, oftentimes as soon as Ling Suyu glanced at a specific spot on the minimap, Tang Yu’an would immediately remind their other teammates to be on the lookout.

At other times, as soon as Tang Yu’an had the intention of taking his marksman to farm in the jungle, he’d see Ling Suyu already on his way there.

They were constantly amazed by each others’ plays which perfectly aligned with their own intentions, and they admired one another from the bottoms of their hearts.

But occasionally, their “brainwaves” would clash.

Take this current game against XY, for instance.

“The enemy will most likely go top. Let’s counter-jungle from the start,” Tang Yu’an suggested.

Ling Suyu furrowed his brow slightly: “We can’t rule out the possibility of them going bot.”

Tang Yu’an: “As long as we steal the small dragon, we’re good.”

Ling Suyu: “It’ll be a huge loss if we can’t.”

Tang Yu’an: “But a top lane push is more likely.”

Ling Suyu: “Still too risky.”

Their other teammates trembled in fear, not daring to speak.

Having spent so much time with Tang Yu’an, they knew him all too well. Although he seemed easygoing, and in fact was generally easygoing, gentle, and considerate, able to instantly identify the problems troubling his teammates and offer solutions, never acting like a captain should, appearing laid-back yet reliable—when it came to crucial decisions, he wouldn’t give anyone room to challenge him.

Take the decision to sign Ling Suyu to Fight, for instance. He never seriously discussed it with any of his teammates, and simply made the decision on his own.

A matter of such magnitude, one that determined Fight’s future direction, certainly qualified as a crucial decision.

Similarly, decisions like whether to counter-jungle at the start, which could affect the entire round’s economy, were also crucial.

Because they clearly understood where their captain’s bottom line lay, even though Fight’s teammates took great pleasure in seeing their captain look frustrated and constantly sought opportunities to push back against him, when it came to the commands he issued during a match, they’d choose to set aside their own thoughts immediately and execute them without hesitation.

But as it happened, Fight’s new marksman refuses to “set aside his thoughts.”

Didn’t he know Tang Yu’an’s limits?

No, that wasn’t it.

Ling Suyu, being perceptive as he was, already understood what kind of person Tang Yu’an was after spending just a few days with him.

A trustworthy, gentle leader who could be relied upon, one who was only domineering in certain matters.

At the same time, however, Ling Suyu could sense Tang Yu’an’s overprotectiveness and indulgence toward him.

So, although he was typically the type to say as little as possible, speaking only when absolutely necessary, Tang Yu’an’s indulgence gradually emboldened him to voice his own thoughts.

He knew his limits and wouldn’t interfere with other roles, but if a teammate’s issues involved the marksman or the bot lane, he wouldn’t wait for someone else to answer as he used to. Instead, he’d speak up proactively or add his own insights after others had responded.

Dou Jiayan asked what to watch out for when laning against Mobile Artillery or Barrier Sorceress—that was right up his alley.

Dou Jiayan asked if the mid and bot should swap lanes—no, he refused.

Tang Yu’an suggested they start with a counter-jungle—specifically, in the bottom jungle—a matter requiring his participation, but…

A scene of White Cocoon, which he was unable to damage, crushing his teammates while he could only watch on helplessly as he ran away without looking back, emerged in his mind.

If this were UH, upon hearing this suggestion, Ling Suyu would never have said a single extra word, merely thinking to himself: I’ll just quietly watch you guys screw yourselves over, wait until I’ve leveled up to win the game, and avenge you in the process.

But in Fight, with Tang Yu’an’s indulgence, despite knowing it might lead to conflict, Ling Suyu still spoke up to express his thoughts: “That’s too risky.” He paused for a moment, then added, “I don’t want to do it.”

If his earlier words could still be considered an attempt to persuade Tang Yu’an to stay calm, then that last sentence was him being rather willful.

Their teammates held their breaths, expecting Tang Yu’an to immediately respond in an icy tone—My mind is made up. Take it or leave it. If you don’t come along, you’ll be held responsible for the failed counter-jungle.

It wasn’t like he hadn’t said things like that before. It was just that his teammates had been “well-behaved” for so long that they’d gradually forgotten.

To everyone’s surprise, when Tang Yu’an heard Ling Suyu say, “I don’t want to,” he didn’t get angry at all. Instead, he chuckled softly: “Why are you throwing a little tantrum all of a sudden? Come on, let’s try a counter-jungle. If we succeed, I’ll treat you to dessert; if we fail, I’ll treat you anyway. Do you prefer chocolate or ice cream? Matcha is pretty good too~”

With that, he dashed toward the enemy jungle: “Yu He, what are you spacing out for? Keep up!”

Yu He silently followed Tang Yu’an, then watched as their carry—who had just said “I don’t want to”—caught up to Tang Yu’an even faster than he had.

Yu He: ?

“I want chocolate tiramisu and cheesecake.”

Ling Suyu sighed.

It wasn’t like he wanted to eat sweets, it was just… Even though he was scared of dying, in a situation where he felt like he could escape, and his teammates went to counter-jungle, there was no reason for him, the marksman, not to follow.

Tang Yu’an had him figured out long ago. He knew Ling Suyu would definitely follow, so he wasn’t surprised at all.

As for the dessert?

Even if they didn’t counter-jungle, if Ling Suyu wanted some, would Tang Yu’an really not buy it for him?

Ever since discovering Ling Suyu’s sweet tooth, Tang Yu’an had been eager to find more opportunities to treat him.

As the saying goes, the way to a man’s heart was through his stomach! (something’s off here)

Thirty seconds into the game, a small dragon spawned in each of the top and bottom jungle areas.

“Top opener” referred to the jungler taking the top jungle camp at the start of the game.

Similarly, “bottom opener” means the jungler takes the bottom jungle camp at the start.

After analyzing XY’s match footage, Chen Jie concluded that XY’s probability of using “top opener” was as high as 70%. This was primarily because XY preferred to play tank junglers; many tanks lacked mobility before level 6, making ganking difficult. They were essentially “herbivore” junglers who cleared the top jungle first, then the bottom jungle, and waited until they reached level 6 and unlocked their ultimate before joining team fights.

This was in contrast to “carnivore” junglers like Yu He, who preferred to gank as much as possible in the early game.

In this match, XY picked White Cocoon as their jungler—an “herbivore” jungler who, like White Cocoon, lacks mobility before level 6 and excels at tanking damage in team fights late-game rather than ganking.

Tang Yu’an thought there was a very good chance XY would open top, so he figured they could emulate their opponents, igame, by counter-jungling right from the start—widening the gold gap between the two teams early on for a quick and stylish victory.

As for Ling Suyu, rather than worrying that XY might do a bottom opener, he was actually more opposed to the idea of “counter-jungling at the start” itself; he didn’t like taking risks.

Tang Yu’an decided to take him out to “broaden his horizons”—Don’t be afraid, you’ll get used to it after a few tries~

And so, under the stunned gazes of their teammates, Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu reached a “reconciliation” through desserts.

It wasn’t until the three of them had successfully sneaked into the enemy’s bottom jungle that Dou Jiayan finally plucked up the courage to speak up: “Should I come along too?”

Tang Yu’an: “It’s none of your business, just focus on your lane.”

Ling Suyu: “No need.”

Yu He: “……”

Dou Jiayan: “Okay, never mind. Have fun ganking the enemy jungle.”

As it turned out, Tang Yu’an’s judgment was spot-on; XY went for a top opener this game.

With no interference, the three members of Fight secured the small dragon in XY’s bottom jungle—a speed-boosting green dragon.

Even as they were fighting it, XY’s bottom lane was obliviously clearing minions on their lane.

When the first wave of minions arrived and there was still no sign of Fight’s bottom lane, every hair on XY’s bodies stood on end.

“The bottom jungle…”

“No way…”

“Have the marksman check… Nevermind, no need to go—they’ve already finished.”

Seeing Fight’s marksman, Candy Witch, suddenly jump ahead in gold, XY would have been foolish not to catch on immediately.

“Fight’s counter-jungle at the start was a huge success!” commentator Remi exclaimed excitedly. “Fight is so bold! Wasn’t XY’s reaction a little slow?”

“It was indeed slow,” analyst Liang Fan noted. “In this game, XY’s bot lane consists of Deepsea Faerie and Luminous Saintess. They can’t secure the bot jungle with just those two. XY should have opened in the bottom, but for some reason, they still chose to open top.”

Little did they know that XY’s reasoning was—

“Our composition is better suited for a bottom-lane start. Fight might go for a top counter-jungle, so we might as well start at the top like we usually do.”

Meanwhile, Tang Yu’an’s reasoning was—

“XY’s jungler is used to starting at the top. He’s convinced we won’t dare to counter-jungle the bottom, so he’ll likely start there, which gives us the perfect opportunity to counter-jungle the bottom.”

A classic case of “I predicted your prediction.”

In response, Ling Suyu wondered—

“What if they predict that we’ll predict they’ll go top and then counter-jungle bottom, so they go bottom to lure us into a trap?”

Ling Suyu took the green dragon from Yu He and asked this while returning to his lane to farm.

Before Tang Yu’an could respond, their teammates couldn’t help but retort:

Mo Xiaoyun: “What are you two this-and-that-ing for?”

Dou Jiayan: “No nesting dolls[^1]!”

Yu He: “Ugh, my head’s already starting to hurt.”

Tang Yu’an chuckled, “Then let’s cut our losses and just reclaim our advantage on the lane. You’re playing Candy Witch, not Desert Hunter—do you really think I’d worry about you not being able to get back up?”

Ling Suyu blinked, somewhat convinced: “That makes sense.”

Tang Yu’an: “Right? The benefits of counter-jungling outweigh the risks, so why not try it?”

Ling Suyu stiffened and, with the green dragon buff active, retorted, “We can win even without counter-jungling.”

Tang Yu’an chuckled and didn’t argue with him any further.

In the end, they successfully counter-jungled—nothing else mattered.

Tang Yu’an was a results-oriented person; as long as the outcome met his expectations, the process didn’t matter. He was perfectly willing to accept a little grumbling from his sweetheart once the goal was achieved.

As for Ling Suyu, although he cared about the process, he wouldn’t dwell on a process whose outcome had already been decided.

Since the jungle had already been invaded, the question of “whether or not to counter-jungle” was now a thing of the past for him; he didn’t give it a second thought.

Little did he know that it was precisely because he was this kind of person that Tang Yu’an had him wrapped around his finger.

Tang Yu’an would definitely do it again next time!

After Ling Suyu picked up the green dragon’s buff, both his movement speed and attack speed increased. Even before his first item was fully built, he was already putting XY under such pressure in the bot lane that they had no way to fight back.

Originally, XY had picked Deepsea Faerie to lane against Candy Witch precisely because Deepsea Faerie had crowd control—it could both poke and heal. Even if they couldn’t kill Candy Witch, they could hold out for a while. But they never expected that Candy Witch, who was already skilled at positioning, would take the green dragon’s speed buff. With flawless footwork, she not only dodged the crowd control but also evaded all their other skills, then hounded them with a barrage of auto-attacks, leaving them completely helpless.

“No way!”

The game had only been going for 3 minutes, and XY’s bottom lane already sensed trouble: “We can’t hold on! The bot lane is about to collapse!”

XY’s jungler glanced at his own gold, then at Fight’s gold, paused for a moment, and said, “Can’t help you now. Best of luck.”

The marksman, who was laning against Dou Jiayan in the mid lane, looked at his health bar, now down to half, and the opponent’s nearly full health bar. He felt like crying but couldn’t: “Can’t help you. I’m in enough trouble myself.”

So, three minutes later, Tang Yu’an, who had just reached level 6, immediately activated his ultimate and charged into the tower. Ling Suyu followed up with damage, securing a double kill without batting an eye!

【System】 Candy Witch, first blood!

【System】The enemy Deepsea Faerie has been slain!

【System】 Candy Witch, double kill! Peerless!

【System】 The enemy Luminous Saintess has been slain!

【Fight’s bottom lane duo is the first to reach level 6! Mandara Vines activates her ultimate “Vine Protection” and pushes straight into XY’s bottom lane first tower, with Candy Witch following up with damage! First kill! Double kill! Mandara Vines tanked the tower once, then twice, and broke out! Barely surviving with low health! Candy Witch teleported out of the tower almost simultaneously! Amazing, Fight went straight through the tower to secure a double kill!】

Liang Fan provided real-time commentary on the entire sequence of Ling Suyu’s double kill, his voice brimming with awe: “Fight’s timing for initiating team fights is still so precise! XY’s bottom lane duo was sent back to base before even reaching Level 6, further widening the gold gap between the two teams. XY is now completely at Fight’s mercy—it’s Fight who’s dictating the pace of this match!”

Mandara Vines’s ultimate ability, “Vine Protection,” created a circular area with vines. All allied targets within this area gained 30% damage reduction.

Mandara Vines wasn’t naturally a hero with high health or defense, but thanks to this ultimate’s damage reduction effect, her ability to tank under a tower rivalled that of a tank.

Early on, she could already push the tower with the marksman to secure kills; late game, with a full set of defensive items, it made no difference to XY whether they stayed under the tower or not.

Although this champion could also build magic damage to play as a poke-oriented support, as long as Ling Suyu’s damage was sufficient, Tang Yu’an wouldn’t want to spoil the mood by stealing his spotlight.

At this point, the game had only reached the 7-minute mark, but the gold gap between the two teams had already widened significantly, and XY’s bottom lane first tower was on the brink of falling.

If this were any other team, negative emotions would likely be starting to surface by now, but for XY, the harder they got beaten, the higher their fighting spirit rose.

“Counter-jungling right from the start, then diving the tower for a double kill as soon as they got their ultimates—Fight’s being way too cocky!”

“Just wait until we’ve leveled up, we’ll make them pay!”

Dou Jiayan, who had been trading minions with Barrier Sorceress in the mid lane, suddenly noticed that his opponent’s positioning had become increasingly agile. Previously, she’d been unable to dodge his first ability on several occasions, forcing her to recall to replenish her health after being worn down by his harassment. Now, however, it was as if she were being controlled by a completely different player—she managed to dodge his lock-on circle just in time after being caught by his first ability several times, causing his skills to miss.

Indeed, even lock-on skills could be dodged, as long as they weren’t insta-cast. Even the slightest delay gave you a chance to break free from the lock-on range and make the skill miss.

At first, after hearing what Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu had said, Dou Jiayan hadn’t taken the opposing Barrier Sorceress seriously at all. He’d even been willing to take her skills head-on just to land his first skill on her, dominating the lane in an almost overbearing manner. But gradually, he realized this tactic wasn’t working anymore.

Barrier Sorceress’s skills didn’t deal much damage, but she could auto-attack.

After a wave of minions was traded, the enemy Barrier Sorceress dodged two of his skills with her positioning, then suddenly plucked up the courage to close in on him, lock onto him, and start auto-attacking!

The enemy’s sudden shift in strategy caught Dou Jiayan off guard. He foolishly traded auto-attacks with her twice. Afterward, seeing his health bar drop significantly while hers remained virtually untouched, he panicked and hurriedly retreated: “Holy shit! Mobile Artillery’s auto-attacks don’t do any damage!”

Tang Yu’an: “Is there a possibility—and I mean, this is just a guess, not necessarily true—that you built skill-damage items?”

Ling Suyu: “Pffft.”

Dou Jiayan: “……”

Ugh, these two are heartless!

No sooner had he finished ranting inwardly than Ling Suyu, having finished laughing, spoke up: “Don’t trade blows with her—you can’t outdamage her. Clear the minions and come down to help me push the tower. Once we take the bottom tower, I’ll go help you.”

Dou Jiayan was touched: “Okay!” He then added with a sigh, “Feather’s the best. Captain just taunts me all the time.”

Tang Yu'an: ?

Wasn’t there a possibility—and I mean, this was just a guess, not necessarily true—that Yumao rejected you?

To him, what Ling Suyu said sounded more like, “Sigh, you’re no good—I’ll have to do it myself.”

But it’s best not to call out something like that.

At the 8-minute mark, with Dou Jiayan’s help, Ling Suyu quickly took down the enemy bottom lane’s first tower.

XY’s bottom lane duo watched from a distance at the second tower, too afraid to get any closer. They could almost imagine what would happen if they did—it would likely end in another double kill.

XY’s bot lane gold count was already a complete disaster, and the mid-lane Barrier Sorceress—since her laning partner, Dou Jiayan, had gone to the bot lane—was eager to take advantage of the opportunity to push towers. She took a risk by getting a little too close to Fight’s mid tower, only to be instantly taken down by a combo from Yu He, who appeared out of nowhere.

In Yu He’s hands, Tempest Blade was in no way inferior to igame’s jungler.

Though his first choice was Twin Demonblades, that was only because Demonblades was so overpowered it was unstoppable.

Unlike Ling Suyu, Yu He didn’t have a signature champion he could play to perfection, nor did he have any particular favorites. However, he did have champions that felt best in his hands—and the one that felt best of all was Tempest Blade.

XY’s marksman dared to solo lane while he was jungling with Tempest Blade?

He admired their courage.

“Heh, a corpse is trying to solo-carry,” he said before charging in. The next moment, a corpse appeared in the mid lane.

【System】 Tempest Blade, one kill! Swift as lightning!

【System】 The enemy Barrier Sorceress has been slain!

Yu He secured the kill with Tempest Blade, then teleported away without looking back.

Meanwhile, upon hearing that sudden, over-the-top remark, Ling Suyu found himself inexplicably recalling Tang Yu’an’s introduction of Fight’s members just before the New Year’s holiday ended.

He remembered that when Tang Yu’an was talking about Yu He, he said, “One word: pretentious. A few more: needs a beating.”

When he first met Yu He, Ling Suyu thought he’d been tricked by Tang Yu’an, but now…

Hmm, he really was a show-off.

And he really did deserve a punch, at least from XY’s perspective.

But there was also something inexplicably cute about him?

As for Yu He’s occasional showboating, the rest of the Fight team had long since gotten used to it—they didn’t even bother to call him out on it anymore.

Yu He took it a step further. Before ganking the top laner, he said, “Death is coming.”

Tempest Blade dashed into the brush, unleashed two skills in quick succession, and the enemy top laner, whose health was already precarious, was instantly reduced to a sliver of health. Then, without waiting for Mo Xiaoyun to follow up, Yu He finished off the enemy top laner, Skeletal Empress, with a single auto-attack.

Next, he waited for her passive to trigger.

Yu He stood coldly beside Skeletal Empress’s corpse: “This is taking forever.”

Three seconds later, Skeletal Empress resurrected. Mo Xiaoyun quickly cast “Chains of Darkness” to immobilize her, and Yu He unleashed two more skills in rapid succession, securing the kill.

【System】 Tempest Blade, double kill! Peerless!

【System】 The enemy Skeletal Empress has been slain!

Yu He: “Thanks for the hospitality.”

Tempest Blade dashed away, returning to the jungle he knew so well.

Ling Suyu pondered.

Have you only started experiencing these symptoms recently, or…

But he wouldn’t dream of criticizing his teammates—he wasn’t that kind of person.

No sooner had he thought this—

“Yanzi, don’t push so deep.” Tang Yu’an saw that Dou Jiayan was trying to clear minions, push the lane, and take down the tower just like the enemy mid laner, but since he and Ling Suyu were in their own bottom jungle collecting the lightning strike, they couldn’t get there in time. He hurriedly warned him, but it was ultimately too late.

The enemy jungler, White Cocoon, had reached level 6 and unlocked his ultimate. He immediately activated it, pulling himself right next to Dou Jiayan, then unleashed a full combo. Dou Jiayan’s health instantly dropped to zero, and he entered self-repair mode.

“Oh crap!” Dou Jiayan was startled.

He actually knew the enemy jungler was nearby, but wasn’t the enemy jungler a tank? How could his damage be so high!

After respawning, he hurriedly used Flash to retreat to his own tower, but to his surprise, White Cocoon didn’t hesitate to Flash right into the tower’s range and unleashed another combo.

【System】 White Cocoon, one kill! Dominating!

【System】 Ally Mobile Artillery has been slain!

But immediately afterward—

【System】 The enemy’s tower has been destroyed!

Although Dou Jiayan had been taken down, he’d successfully led his minions into the enemy turret, allowing them to finish off the tower’s remaining health.

Seeing this system notification, Dou Jiayan breathed a sigh of relief: “One kill for a tower—not a bad trade, right?”

“If you’d retreated just two seconds earlier, not only would the tower have been destroyed, but you’d have kept your life too,” Tang Yu’an retorted bluntly, though he quickly added, “It’s my fault for not warning you sooner.” He then took another dig at Dou Jiayan: “I actually thought that with your gaming sense, you wouldn’t need me to remind you—I clearly overestimated you.”

“Hey!!” Dou Jiayan felt offended and quickly defended his gaming instincts. “I knew White Cocoon was nearby! I was just trying to take down the tower!” Then he suddenly adopted a solemn tone. “Kills don’t matter. Glorious is a tower-pushing game.”

“But our gold…” Ling Suyu glanced at Dou Jiayan’s gold count—it actually wasn’t that far behind the enemy’s—but he still feigned a sigh of despair.

He had to say, teasing Yanzi was so much fun. He was starting to understand why everyone on the Fight team loved to tease him.

At that moment, Ling Suyu had completely forgotten that just a few seconds earlier, he’d assured himself that he wasn’t the type to make fun of his teammates.

Dou Jiayan: “……”

Tang Yu’an: “Messing with the carry’s mindset—that’s an even bigger offense.”

Dou Jiayan: “……”

You two, that’s enough.
