【System】The enemy’s tower has been destroyed!

After wrapping up that 2-for-2 exchange, Ling Suyu and Dou Jiayan immediately pushed the bottom lane minions into the enemy’s bottom lane first tower and took it down.

At this moment, the commentators were still providing additional analysis of the previous 2-for-2 trade based on slow-motion replays, and igame’s jungler Ye Xunjie and Ling Suyu’s ‘wave-like subtle steps’ had undoubtedly become the focal point of their discussion.

“Immaculate precision! Both players’ execution in this exchange was incredibly precise!” Ah-Mu couldn’t help but exclaim.

【Xunjie’s Double Phantom truly pulled off a shadow-like displacement! But Feather’s Cat Spirit not only matched his reaction speed, it even outdid him!】 ZhiZhi chimed in with her own exclamation, “Both showcased amazing technique! That was just so cool!”

What made professional matches so compelling? It was precisely those “god-tier plays” that set pro players apart from casual gamers.

Many casual players played games with their fingers tapping away at the keyboard, driven wholly by emotion rather than skill. They’d chase enemies when low on health, cast spells the moment they see the opponent’s health bar turn red, and often get carried away. Occasionally, they’d pull off some “god-tier plays” without even knowing how they did it, and when they watched the replay, they couldn't help but exclaim, “I’m so good!”

But professional players were experts—their reaction times and game awareness far exceeded those of ordinary people. Many of their “god-tier plays” were genuinely executed; they just didn’t always have the chance to show them off, since they often faced other pros.

Take the recent exchange between Cat Spirit and Double Phantom, where they traded dashes and Flashs, swapping positions twice in a row. In slow motion, you could see the entire sequence clearly, but in the actual match, it happened in the blink of an eye.

“Ah-Mu, you usually play jungler or marksman, right? If you were Cat Spirit, do you think you could’ve escaped?” ZhiZhi asked pointedly.

Ah-Mu cleared his throat before responding: “If I were Cat Spirit, I’d have been killed by Xunjie’s Double Phantom before I could even use Flash; if I were Double Phantom, I’d have been counter-killed by Feather’s Cat Spirit before I could use Flash. Although Xunjie’s Double Phantom was ultimately killed by Fight’s jungler Crane, Yu He, if Yu He hadn’t been right there, he might actually have escaped!”

“But igame’s Xunjie has always been strong; it’s Feather who’s really impressed me!” ZhiZhi said. “During the White Fox Cup, I thought he just had exceptional game sense—even with his eyes closed, he could pinpoint which bushes had enemies, and he never let any enemy hero cross his path when the support wasn’t around. I didn’t expect his reaction speed to be this fast, too! Maybe Feather would be better suited to playing jungler?”

“Feather definitely prefers playing marksmen!” Ah-Mu said with absolute certainty.

“Huh?” ZhiZhi looked surprised. “Are you that sure?”

Ah-Mu: “You can tell just by the type of marksmen he plays! Rather than instantly taking down enemies for kills, he prefers sustained damage—dominating the battlefield with auto-attacks!”

ZhiZhi: “Oh, right!”

Ah-Mu: “The next patch update’s in April. I wonder what changes they’ll make. Who knows, maybe they’ll even revert the basic attack damage!”

And just like that, without even realizing it, Ah-Mu became a prophet.

The match continued. After all the heroes on both teams had respawned, they gathered in the bottom river, eyeing the Divine Sunbird with intense focus.

At this point, Fight had a slightly higher gold lead than igame, but neither team’s composition held a clear advantage. Essentially, whoever initiated the team fight first—and executed it well—would gain the upper hand.

On the opposing team, White Cocoon, Curse Master, and Guardian Spirit all had crowd control abilities, but it was still more likely that the White Cocoon would be the one initiating the fight.

On Fight’s side, only Skeletal Empress and Deepsea Faerie had crowd control, so Skeletal Empress would definitely initiate the fight.

The two teams, separated by the Divine Sunbird, were faintly visible through the mist as they faced off against each other, careful to neither spread out too much nor cluster too closely.

If they were too spread out, they wouldn’t be able to react in time when the fight started.

If they were too clustered, they’d be easy targets for the enemy to initiate on.

Knowing that an all-out team fight was about to erupt, both the commentators and the audience held their breaths in anticipation.

“Don’t worry everyone, take it easy,” Tang Yu’an said casually, brazenly maneuvering his Spider Queen closer to the Divine Sunbird, then added, “Yumao, stick with me, we’ll bait them. Once they initiate, we’ll strike back. Everyone else, keep your eyes on me. As long as Yumao stays near me, White Cocoon will definitely try to wrap around me to control him. When that happens, just unleash your skills on me.”

His teammates all gave their agreement. Ling Suyu hesitated for a moment, but then gave a soft “Mm-hmm” and followed Tang Yu’an.

He knew his own reaction speed well—it was indeed fast.

He could position himself to dodge most of the enemy heroes’ skills, and react quickly enough to use Flash to dodge when the enemy jungler moved to initiate. However, this required a great deal of mental energy.

The longer the game went on, the more energy he expended, and the easier it was for his focus to waver.

If he were ambushed while his attention was wandering, there would be certain moves he could have reacted to, but wouldn’t be able to execute corresponding actions immediately.

In Ling Suyu’s view, this kind of “mistake,” whether it actually counted as one or not, was just as fatal as any other.

So, aside from luring enemies while laning, he didn’t particularly like lingering in areas where he knew danger lurked.

But Tang Yu’an was there.

Tang Yu’an would protect him.

His other teammates would protect him, too.

Ling Suyu obediently ran behind Tang Yu’an, and immediately saw a white thread extend from White Cocoon’s hand, connecting to Tang Yu’an.

Reacting in the blink of an eye, Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit used “Shadow Steps” to dash away, and almost simultaneously, Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen activated her ultimate: all friendly targets within the ultimate’s range gained a speed boost, while enemy targets were slowed!

The next moment, the young girl—her body completely wrapped in white silk, with only one red eye visible—used the white thread to pull herself next to Tang Yu’an and unleashed a combo of her first, second, and third skills!

Her ultimate ability, “Pull,” involved casting a white thread to entangle an enemy target and immediately pulling herself towards them, dealing damage while refreshing all her skills.

Her first skill, “Twisted Threads,” used threads to entangle an enemy target, immobilizing them in place.

Her second skill, “Cocoon,” encased herself in a cocoon and, whilst disabling her movement, granted her temporary extra health equal to 30% of her maximum health for 2 seconds.

Her third skill, “Unravelling,” released countless threads while she spun, lashing out at all nearby targets with the threads to deal area-of-effect damage.

All of his skills struck Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen, failing to harm Ling Suyu in the slightest.

Failing to control the marksman meant igame’s team fight initiation was a failure.

However, there were no do-overs in team fights, so igame had no choice but to sacrifice White Cocoon and abandon the Divine Sunbird, or push forward regardless.

Unfortunately, they had very little time to make a decision—hesitation meant defeat, so they chose to act decisively!

The enemy mid-laner Curse Master instantly teleported in and applied a crowd control effect, unleashing a full skill combo on Tang Yu’an before activating his ultimate to finish him off!

Tang Yu’an activated his shield, while Mo Xiaoyun, who had been prepared all along, also used Flash to follow up with a stun, slamming down with a leaping hammer, sending both White Cocoon and Curse Master flying into the air!

With only a sliver of health remaining, Tang Yu’an decisively used Flash to retreat. Ling Suyu locked onto Curse Master, marked him with “Spiritual Mark,” and immediately landed a series of basic attacks!

Before his teammates could reach the river, Ling Suyu, with “assistance” from Dou Jiayan, had already picked up the lightning strike in his team’s bottom jungle. Now, his basic attacks not only triggered Cat Spirit’s passive but also the lightning strike. Combined with Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen’s passive, the enemy Curse Master’s health bar was rapidly drained before he even hit the ground!

【System】 Cat Spirit, one kill!

【System】 The enemy Curse Master has been slain!

“Unbelievable!” were Curse Master’s last words.

Before Curse Master died, Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress used “Sea of Corpses,” laying out a field of eternal slumber for him.

This ability made the enemy marksman and support hesitate for a moment, but in the end, they steeled themselves and stepped in.

The enemy support, Abyssal Lord, activated his shield while protecting their marksman and top laner.

The enemy marksman, Guardian Spirit, unleashed a full combo on Skeletal Empress, even using his ultimate.

“Go for the backline!” igame’s jungler and captain paced anxiously, seeing that Cat Spirit had only lost a sliver of health.

He didn’t want to admit it, but this team fight had actually been lost the moment White Cocoon initiated and failed to lock down Cat Spirit.

He had originally planned that once White Cocoon had locked down Cat Spirit, he would step in and finish it off with a combo. He never expected Feather’s reaction to be this fast. To be able to break free from White Cocoon control in an instant, as if he’d long seen it coming…

Wait—as if he’d seen it coming?

Upon hearing their captain’s words, igame’s marksman and support looked at Cat Spirit, far behind Skeletal Empress, and both wore troubled expressions.

Fortunately, the captain immediately added, “Forget it, don’t fight anymore. You’ve already used your abilities; we can’t win this. Let’s retreat!”

He could almost imagine what would happen if Guardian Spirit and Cat Spirit traded blows.

However, for igame to retreat, Fight would have to allow it first.

Almost as soon as Guardian Spirit finished casting its ultimate, Dou Jiayan’s Deepsea Faerie used “Patrol” to immobilize him, then immediately followed up with “Water Lotus” and “Energy Absorption,” restoring his own health while unleashing his ultimate, “Deepsea Ballad”!

Unlike Cat Spirit and Curse Master, who stacked marks on enemy targets, Deepsea Faerie stacked marks on itself—no matter which target the skill hit, as long as it connected, the mark stacked.

He unleashed this ultimate with the mark-stack at maximum, so the damage was unimaginable.

Accompanied by a dreamlike siren song, blue rippling effects lit up and transformed into two beautiful musical notes that rushed towards Guardian Spirit!

Enemy targets hit by “Deepsea Ballad” lost a large amount of health and had their movement speed reduced by 50%.

The immobilized Guardian Spirit took the full brunt of this ultimate. Even after finally breaking free, he couldn’t escape and could only watch helplessly as the Cat Spirit strutted right up to him and hurled a green shadow blade at him.

【System】Cat Spirit, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

While the battle raged, on the other side, Tang Yu’an used the health regeneration provided by his gear.

Even so, his health still remained critically low—a tempting target.

He deliberately returned to Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit and said casually, “Let’s bait them one more time~”

Sure enough, as his health dropped, a phantom materialized out of thin air behind him—igame’s jungler had struck!

Tang Yu’an, who had been prepared all along, immediately countered with a crowd control ability, “Spidersilk Trap,” hitting the enemy jungler’s Double Phantom dead-on!

Yu He, who had been biding his time, saw Double Phantom and, as if receiving a signal, had his Venomous Scorpion dash out of the brush with a movement skill, immediately activated his ultimate, and landed a “Fatal Venom Spike” on Double Phantom!

It was only the 8th minute of the game; everyone’s gold was barely enough to boost their damage, let alone buy CC removal, so Double Phantom had no choice but to stand there and take the hit.

How could he have forgotten? Fight’s support was just as ruthless as the rest.

With reaction speeds like that, what role couldn’t he play? Why did he have to pick support?

Ling Suyu had been keeping a close eye on Tang Yu’an’s health. When he saw Double Phantom closing in for the kill, he’d already moved his mouse cursor over White Cocoon—but he quickly swerved and clicked on Double Phantom instead.

And in the next instant:

【System】 Cat Spirit, triple kill! Unstoppable!

【System】 The enemy Double Phantom has been slain!

【System】 Cat Spirit, quadra kill! Godlike!

【System】 The enemy White Cocoon has been slain!

【System】 Cat Spirit is dominating!

igame’s support realized they couldn’t win this team fight and used Flash the moment their marksman was killed, attempting to escape.

Dou Jiayan’s Deepsea Faerie used Flash to give chase, pursuing him across nearly half the map. Relying on his movement speed, which was faster than the Abyssal Lord’s, he used his refreshed skills to whittle him down to a sliver of health.

But in the end, he still managed to escape into the enemy turret, and he couldn’t keep up.

Then, unsurprisingly, he was met with a new round of teasing:

Tang Yu’an, who was taking advantage of the chaos near the Divine Sunbird: “Yanzi, why are you chasing him? Just take the bird.”

Mo Xiaoyun, who was tanking damage in front of the Divine Sunbird: “It’s one thing for casual players to get carried away, but why are you getting carried away too?”

“I thought I could get the kill!” Dou Jiayan teleported back to base, clearly unwilling to admit defeat.

Tang Yu’an: “One of life’s three great illusions: ‘I can kill!’”

Dou Jiayan: “What are the other two?”

Tang Yu’an: “I don’t know, I just made that up.”

Dou Jiayan: “……”

Ling Suyu stood behind Mo Xiaoyun, standing still and firing at the Divine Sunbird while clearing his mind, giving both his brain and hands a moment’s rest. He didn’t pay attention to what his teammates were saying.

【System】Cat Spirit has successfully conquered the Divine Sunbird!

Was it over?

Then let’s head back.

Ling Suyu activated his Recall and continued to zone out and relax.

Huh? Wait a minute?

“Yu He, where’s your Smite?” Tang Yu’an asked on his behalf as he stepped into the bird pit to pick up the token.

“No one on the enemy team can come steal it anyway,” Yu He replied nonchalantly, teleporting to the mid lane to push the lane.

Tang Yu’an: “No, Yumao’s already leveled up. It’s your turn now.”

Yu He: “Oh, why didn’t you say so sooner?”

Tang Yu’an was speechless for a moment, then burst out laughing.

These teammates really were…

When he told them to protect Feather, they truly protected him at all costs.

When he told them to help Feather farm, they really gave up every bit of gold they could spare.

Though he knew they did so because they trusted his judgment.

But he still felt a bit happy for Ling Suyu.

How should he put it? It felt like, “I wanted to spoil you, but it turns out the whole world’s also helping me spoil you.”

Still, he had to stay rational during the match: “Yumao’s gold lead is already solid. He’s playing Cat in this game—not Desert Hunter, and definitely not Candy Witch—so it’s not enough for just him to be ahead in eco. We need to get ahead too; only then can we protect him better.”

Desert Hunter had plenty of mobility and strong survival skills, while Candy Witch packed enough early-game damage. When Ling Suyu picked either of these heroes and had an economic advantage, he could easily pull off a 1v5.

But Cat Spirit still needed some protection.

His teammates all nodded in agreement, and Ling Suyu instinctively murmured “Mm-hmm,” then couldn’t help but let the corners of his mouth curl upward.

It truly felt strange.

Even though he’d been in the scene for five years, it felt like his very first time playing esports.

It was as if he’d never truly known what esports was all about during these past five years.

The passion that had been worn away over the course of five years, Fight helped him reclaim it in just half a month.

It had taken Fight only half a month to rectify his impressions of what coaches and teammates really meant.

All members united, working together to win the championship.

This was what esports was truly all about.

Fight—he loved them so much.

And Tang Yu’an, who had presented all of this to him as a gift, he loved even more.

In contrast to the ease on Fight’s side, igame was having a really tough time right now.

“I got carried away,” the captain admitted first. “I thought I could take out the support, but I didn’t realize he still had his abilities left.”

“He was saving them just to take you down,” the mid laner replied. “By the way, how is Cat Spirit’s damage so high? It’s actually ridiculous.”

“He built lifesteal and attack speed, plus a pair of attack speed boots,” the support said. “His build isn’t anything special—the key is that he picked up lightning strike, and the support boosted his speed too.”

“That Cat Spirit and Spider bot lane duo is just ridiculous!” the marksman said with utter disgust. “Just as ridiculous as the captain’s Blade Soul!”

Captain: ?

I get what you mean, but there’s no need to take out your frustration on your teammates.

“Weren’t auto-attacks nerfed? Why is Cat Spirit still so strong? Should we practice Cat Spirit too?” the top laner asked in confusion.

A moment of silence.

Then the marksman whispered, “My Cat Spirit has a 40% win rate this season.”

The support added quietly, “My Spider has a 20% win rate this season.”

Top laner: “Oh, well, nevermind then.”

On Fight’s side, they said they’d all focus on farming, but by the time everyone was levels 13–15, Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit was already level 18 and had the highest gold count on the map.

His lightning strikes were practically non-stop; after clearing his own jungle, he moved into igame’s jungle to farm, and igame had absolutely no way to stop him.

Then, at the 19-minute mark, igame’s jungler, Double Phantom, secured a 1v1 kill on Yu He, who was solo-pushing the top lane, and immediately rushed to take the Dragon.

Fight sensed they were fighting the Dragon and rushed over to steal it.

As a result, despite being a man down in the team fight, Fight managed to wipe out all of igame and claim the Dragon Lord just as it had only a few drops of health left!

Although Ling Suyu didn’t get a pentakill, he dealt almost all the damage—anyone who got close to him died.

igame was already on the defensive after having their Dragon stolen; Mo Xiaoyun’s leaping hammer left them completely helpless. Before they could even mount a counterattack, Ling Suyu’s Spirit Cat came in and took them out with a flurry of “biu biu biu,” leaving them furious.

From their first match against Fight, it was clear that they were a team that dared to take risks and was willing to gamble big.

They knew that the longer this match dragged on, the slimmer their chances of winning would become, so they decided to take a gamble—betting that they could snatch the Dragon in the blink of an eye before Fight arrived, and use the three waves of the divine dragon legion to turn the tide.

Win the bet, and they’d stage a comeback against the odds.

Lose the bet, and they’d be crushed.

In the end, they lost the gamble—and lost completely.

【System】 Cat Spirit has successfully conquered the Dragon God and summoned the Divine Dragon Legion!

【System】 The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us!

Accompanied by these two system notifications, Tang Yu’an immediately led Ling Suyu down toward the river: “Come on, Yumao, grab the Hero’s Chalice~”

Ling Suyu gave a soft “Mm-hmm” and obediently followed him.

Dou Jiayan, who had been killed in the team fight: “Whoa, you guys are kidding, right? Just push the base and be done with it—why bother with the Chalice?”

Yu He, who had been solo-killed by the enemy jungler and hadn’t respawned yet: “You should be asking, ‘With Yumao’s kill rate, is there even a point in going for the Chalice?’”

In the lower river of 《Glorious Heroes》's battlefield, the first Divine Sunbird spawned at the 5-minute mark. After it was killed, the second one spawned 5 minutes later.

Regardless of whether the second Divine Sunbird had been killed, at the 20-minute mark, all jungle monsters in the lower river were replaced with “Hero’s Statues.”

Hero’s Statues were stone statues, activated after one stood in front of one for 3 seconds.

When an activated statue is destroyed, it shatters and vanishes, and the team that destroyed it gains doubled health and mana regeneration for all heroes for a certain period afterward.

At the same time, it dropped an item called the “Hero’s Chalice”

Only one player could hold up the “Hero’s Chalice,” and the player who did so would gain a second life for a set period afterwards.

In other words, if they were killed by an enemy hero within a certain period after picking up the “Hero’s Chalice,” they would immediately respawn at that spot with full health.

【System】Spider Queen has activated the Hero’s Statue!

【System】Cat Spirit has defeated the Hero's Statue and earned the recognition of the God of Heroes!

【System】Cat Spirit has picked up the Hero’s Chalice and gained the power of immortality!

Although igame had suspected as early as when they were wiped out by Fight that the latter might go for the Chalice, seeing the system notification in person still left them utterly gobsmacked.

“F*ck, come on, really? It wasn’t enough to take the Dragon; now they’re even going after the Chalice? Do they think we haven’t lost thoroughly enough already?”

“Look on the bright side—they’re acknowledging our strength and don’t want to give us any chance to make a comeback.”

“They even gave the Chalice to Feather, I can’t f*cking believe it…”

“Come to think of it, have we ever actually killed Feather? Even in the first two games?”

“Uh…” The entirety of igame fell silent, staring at Ling Suyu’s stats: 16 kills, 0 deaths, and 5 assists.

Commentator Ah-Mu couldn’t help but chuckle when he saw Fight hand the Chalice to Ling Suyu: “Wow, it’s not enough that the whole team is protecting him—now he’s got a second life, too. I genuinely feel for igame! If they want to win this game, they’ll have to take Feather down right at the start of team fights. That was already tough, but now it’s even harder!”

“It’s not just hard—it’s practically impossible, right?” ZhiZhi corrected, then couldn’t help but sigh, “Feather is truly Fight’s team darling. No matter who goes hungry on Fight, it won’t be Feather.”

Upon hearing the commentator’s remark, several chat messages flashed across the livestream:

【Hahaha, ZhiZhi, that’s so adorably random!】

【Feeding Feather until he’s stuffed—】

【Haha, for three games in a row, igame hasn’t managed to get a single kill on Feather, yet Fight still gives the Chalice to Feather.】

【Seeing how much Fight spoils Feather puts my mind at ease!】

【Is Fight about to win? Does igame have no chance left? Can I pop the champagne early to celebrate?】

【Replying to the previous comment: Yes!】

Much less the commentators, even the audience could tell that igame had absolutely no chance left in this push.

Sure enough, capitalizing on three waves of the dragon legion, Fight pushed down all of igame’s towers in one go, drove all three lanes into their main tower, and launched a full-on assault.

After igame’s heroes respawned, they knew full well it was too late to turn the tides, but they still struggled to mount a counterattack against Fight, only to end up as mere numbers on Ling Suyu and Cat Spirit’s kill counts.

In the end, after 23 minutes, Fight secured the victory in the final game and advanced to the quarterfinals as the top team in Group D!

Before the match against igame began, they said that this time, they would take down igame’s main tower with their own hands!

And they did it!

Just as the word “WIN” appeared on Fight’s screen, the word “LOSE” appeared on igame’s screen.

igame’s players looked at the words on the screen and all let out a long sigh.

Then the captain pursed his lips and said, “Fight is getting stronger and stronger, so we have to keep pushing ourselves. Defeat isn’t the end—the championship is. We’ll win it back in the quarterfinals!”

Over on Fight’s side, as the referee once again awarded him the MVP, Ling Suyu flashed a satisfied smile, completely oblivious to Tang Yu’an standing beside him, smiling just as contentedly.

It wasn’t until Chen Jie’s voice rang out from behind them that they snapped out of it: “What are you two looking at? The host is waiting for you guys to do the post-match interview.”

“Both of us?” Tang Yu’an looked up first, raising an eyebrow in surprise.

Meeting his gaze, Chen Jie nodded firmly: “Yes, both of you. You go with Feather.”
