“We underestimated Feather as a player.”

“What do you mean?”

Hearing his top laner say this, S.MG’s marksman froze for a moment and instinctively asked.

However, S.MG’s top laner only had a vague feeling and couldn’t give a clear answer, so he replied, “I can’t really put my finger on it, but I just feel like Feather’s game sense is way better than I expected. We’ll probably have to analyze it during the post-game review.”

“Alright, let’s review after this game,” the mid laner and team captain responded.

Meanwhile, over on Fight’s side— “Let’s take the dragon!”

After securing the team kill, Dou Jiayan exclaimed excitedly, dashing toward the Dragon God’s pit without waiting for Tang Yu’an’s reply.

However, Ling Suyu didn’t follow. Instead, he began clearing minions in the mid lane. Tang Yu’an decisively joined Ling Suyu while responding to Dou Jiayan: “Why are you going for the dragon, come push the towers! If we go for the dragon now, aren’t we just asking for Blade Soul to steal it?”

“Fine…” Dou Jiayan reluctantly swam back, helped Ling Suyu clear minions, then followed as they pushed into the enemy mid lane’s third tower 【System】The enemy’s tower has been destroyed!

“I’m going back to replenish my health.” Mo Xiaoyun said after taking down the tower, immediately recalling his low-health Skeletal Empress right where he stood.

The others followed suit, recalling to replenish their health and buy items.

Jungler Yu He went to the enemy’s bottom jungle to steal a small monster, then pushed the bottom lane for a bit before recalling.

SMG’s top laner, Blade Soul, seeing that they weren’t fighting the Dragon God, similarly recalled before clearing minions.

However, as he was clearing, he sensed that something was off and headed back to the dragon pit.

Just as he arrived at the bottom river, before the fog in his field of vision even cleared, he heard the sounds of a dragon fight.

“Damn, they’re going for the dragon. Should I steal it?” Blade Soul hesitated.

“Go for it, don’t chicken out. If you die, it’s on me!” the captain urged.

“Alright, I’m going in!” Blade Soul gritted his teeth, using “Flash of a Blade” to leap over the pit wall and dash into the dragon pit before immediately activating “Absolute Defense”!

The damage-absorption skill absorbed a massive amount of damage for him. He took the opportunity to glance at the Dragon God’s health bar—it was still well above half, far from something he could finish off with a single strike.

“Alright, nevermind.” Blade Soul unleashed his ultimate ability, randomly dealing damage to Fight’s members. He then gave one final struggle, using “Instant Step” to dodge an attack, landing a single strike on the Divine Dragon, and going down with dignity.

【System】Twin Demonblades, one kill!

【System】The enemy Blade Soul has been slain!

【System】Twin Demonblades has conquered the Dragon God and summoned the Divine Dragon Legion!

By this point, the rest of SMG’s heroes had respawned, but with Blade Soul down and the Dragon God taken by Fight, the outcome of this match was practically decided.

“Why didn’t you check the health bar before diving in?” their jungler couldn’t help but complain.

Blade Soul, lying on the ground: “I panicked.”

He had spent too much time clearing minions and, worried he’d arrive too late to claim the dragon, had simply jumped over the wall. As it turned out, he had overestimated how quickly Fight could take down the dragon.

It made sense—the game had only been going for a little over ten minutes, and neither team had finished building up their gear sets yet; they couldn’t possibly take down the dragon that quickly.

In Glorious Heroes, the Dragon God—a key jungle monster capable of deciding the outcome of a match—didn’t spawn until the 10-minute mark.

It was located in the top half of the river, surrounded by a curved wall. This enclosed area was known as the “Dragon Pit.”

Players outside the wall couldn’t see what was happening inside the dragon pit; they could only tell if someone was fighting the dragon by the sounds.

To check the dragon’s remaining health, you’d either have to rush into the pit and take a gamble, or circle around to a gap in the wall to get a close-up view.

The latter was safer, but came with its own risks.

It was easy to get spotted by enemy heroes who were already fighting the Dragon, and you might get killed by them before you even have a chance to get close to the Dragon.

After hearing Blade Soul’s explanation, the members of SMG didn’t say much more.

The mid laner, and also their captain, looked at the mini-map showing all three lanes in complete disarray and sighed, “Good thing the coach isn’t here. Otherwise, we’d get chewed out after this game…”

“Should we even keep playing?” the marksman, who hadn’t managed to farm at all, wasn’t really in the mood to continue. “We’re way behind in gold. With our top laner down, they’ll just tower-dive us and run us over.”

“But we’re using our professional accounts, so surrendering would be pretty embarrassing,” said the support.

“Then let’s just chat,” the mid laner captain cheerfully decided.

Teammates: ?

So, just as the Fight team was seriously battling the Divine Sunbird, planning to push all the way through with both the Divine Dragon Legion and the Divine Sunbird, a new message popped up in the chat window.

【Enemy】S.MG-FOX (Nine-tailed Fox): You guys are so unfair. You said you were just messing around, but you’re actually playing seriously. If we’d known, we would’ve played seriously too.

“Huh?” Ling Suyu froze for a moment when he saw the message. “They weren’t taking it seriously?”

And to think he’d thought he and Fight were coordinating pretty well…

“No way you actually believed that! ” Tang Yu’an didn’t know what to say to Ling Suyu.

“That’s just [what the hell]’s style—even when they’re serious, it looks like they’re not. They know you’ll be playing as a starter in the Spring Seasonals, so to gauge your strength, there’s no way they wouldn’t take this match seriously.”

“If they weren’t serious, would they have picked Guardian Spirit and Abyssal Lord?” Mo Xiaoyun cut straight to the point, “They’re just putting on a brave face right now.”

“Is that so?” Ling Suyu let out a silent sigh of relief.

So, did they really beat S.MG? And that easily?

Though he thought this to himself, Ling Suyu’s auto-attacks never stopped.

【System】Cat Spirit has successfully conquered the Divine Sunbird!

Meanwhile, Tang Yu’an typed a reply to the opposing team while continuing to play the game without pause.

【Ally】Fight-Yuan (Spider Queen): “Wow, despite us deliberately going easy on you with Cat Spirit, you still called us ‘serious.’ Just imagine what’ll happen when we actually get serious—you’ll be crushed.”

【System】Spider Queen has picked up the Divine Sunbird’s token!

The SMG captain nearly spat out a mouthful of blood when he saw Tang Yu’an’s reply.

Marksman: “He’s lying, right?”

Support: “Definitely.”

Jungler: “We’ll see if we check Feather’s mains after this match.”

As for Feather himself, upon seeing Tang Yu’an’s reply, his reaction was: “I wasn’t holding back! Cat Spirit is my main.”

Tang Yu’an raised a hand to cover his eyes: “I was just messing with them. Don’t believe you go believing in it…”

“Pfft—” Dou Jiayan couldn’t help but laugh. “Feather, you’re so serious!”

Ling Suyu: 0.0; “But I don’t mind people who take things seriously!” Dou Jiayan added immediately, “Jie-ge is super serious too—”

“Your Jie-ge isn’t ‘serious’, he’s literally going all out!” Tang Yu’an said, glancing sideways at Ling Suyu beside him. Seeing the neutral expression on his face, he quietly looked away.

Tang Yu’an could sense that, likely due to being constantly overshadowed by Jiang Hao at UH, Ling Suyu was very sensitive to any topic related to Chen Jie.

Of course, he didn’t have anything against Chen Jie personally; rather, he was worried he wouldn’t be able to hold onto the starter position.

Once Chen Jie’s hand healed, deciding on a starter would indeed become an issue.

Tang Yu’an wouldn’t favor Ling Suyu out of sympathy for him, nor would he favor Chen Jie because of their friendship.

Ultimately, the starting position would have to be decided based on ability.

However…

Tang Yu’an glanced at Ling Suyu beside him again.

From his own perspective as a support player, compared to Chen Jie, who relied entirely on his commands, Ling Suyu, with his unique gaming instincts and ideas, was a cut above in every aspect where efficiency was concerned.

But Tang Yu’an couldn’t say for sure that this feeling wasn’t tainted by personal bias.

He could only say that, gaming skills aside, his heart truly leaned toward Ling Suyu.

Although he felt a bit guilty toward Chen Jie, he had to admit that someone who was both good-looking and gentle really appealed to him. He would even say Ling Suyu was his ideal type.

Playing games with someone he favored made his mood, and even his gameplay, much more relaxed.

【System】Spider Queen has summoned the Divine Sunbird!

Tang Yu’an placed the bird in the bottom lane.

After securing the lightning strike in his own bottom jungle, Ling Suyu immediately used “Shadow Steps” to rush to the bottom lane and meet up with Tang Yu’an.

Dou Jiayan had been following Ling Suyu the whole time, so when Ling Suyu headed to the bottom lane, he naturally followed suit.

Mo Xiaoyun also chose to stick close to Ling Suyu, leaving only the jungler, Yu He, to go to the top lane to push the lane.

On the SMG side, aside from Blade Soul, who hadn’t respawned yet, everyone else had gathered at the bottom lane, determined to defend their last tower there.

However, as a sustained-damage marksman who relied primarily on attack speed rather than critical strikes, Cat Spirit’s greatest strength lay in her ability to push towers quickly.

After taking down the bottom lane’s second tower, Mo Xiaoyun led the push towards the third tower, with Dou Jiayan close behind. He then unleashed a combo that landed squarely on the enemy mid laner!

SMG’s Nine-Tailed Fox was practically insta-killed.

【System】Deepsea Faerie, one kill! Swift as lightning!

【System】The enemy Nine-tailed Fox has been slain!

Tang Yu'an activated his ultimate, while Mo Xiaoyun sped up to catch the escaping enemy support. After casting a speed debuff, he immediately activated his ultimate to close in, draining the enemy's health while chasing him down and chipping away at his back!

Ling Suyu used “Shadow Steps” to catch up to his teammate, but instead of attacking the enemy, he focused on attacking the tower.

Before long, SMG’s tower defender hadn’t even died yet, but the tower itself had already fallen.

【System】 The enemy’s tower has been destroyed After taking down the tower, Ling Suyu immediately marked the enemy jungler, who was trying to take out their mid laner, and unleashed his ultimate, “Spiritual Explosion”!

The enemy jungler, Venomous Scorpion, lost more than half his health in the explosion.

Spooked, he abandoned the low-health Deepsea Faerie and used a Flash to dash back towards their main tower.

Tang Yu’an followed with a Flash of his own, and upon seeing this, Ling Suyu immediately Flash’ed in after him, landing two auto-attacks.

【System】Cat Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Venomous Scorpion has been slain!

After taking down Venomous Scorpion, Ling Suyu immediately left the tower. Tang Yu’an, who had taken a tower hit for him, followed suit, simultaneously calling out to his teammates:

“Xiaoyun, stop harassing their support, focus on pushing the lane and taking down their towers! Yanzi, why are you back at base? Push the tower! Let’s finish this!”

Upon hearing the command, Mo Xiaoyun decisively abandoned his pursuit of the support.

After helping his teammates clear the enemy minions, he began going for the main tower.

With only a sliver of health remaining, Dou Jiayan also paused his recall, hiding behind the top laner and support while helping them take down the tower.

Meanwhile, Yu He swept up all the minions from the top and mid lanes, used a dash to flash right into the enemy’s main tower, and unleashed a barrage of auto-attacks on it.

Ling Suyu himself remained behind Tang Yu'an, directing his Spirit Cat to fire away at the tower!

The enemy’s main tower’s health was dropping at an alarming rate.

The enemy marksman and support had just finished healing at the fountain and taken two steps out when the screen froze.

The enemy top laner fared even worse—he had just respawned and hadn’t even had a chance to react before it was all over.

On the Fight side, the word “WIN” appeared simultaneously on all five players’ screens.

Immediately following that was the Cat Spirit’s exclusive victory animation.

—Once again, Ling Suyu was given MVP!

“Awesome!” Dou Jiayan took his hands off the keyboard and mouse, raised his fist in celebration, and exclaimed, “Just got back from New Year’s break and we already crushed [what the hell] into the ground—feels great!”

“Yanzi.” Mo Xiaoyun, sitting right to his right, called out softly.

Dou Jiayan immediately shrunk back: “Mo-ge, let’s talk this out. Don’t get worked up… but don’t you think I played pretty well in the late game?”

Jungler Yu He took off his headset and ran a hand through his hair: “I saved this game’s recording, want to review it tomorrow?”

“Sure,” Tang Yu’an replied, taking off his headset as well.

Ling Suyu followed suit, taking off his headphones. As he looked at his MVP title and the string of golden badges on the screen, he felt a wave of relief wash over him.

Highest gold, most kills, highest damage, most towers destroyed, highest team participation rate, triple kills, quadra kills, pentakills.

I played pretty well this game, right?

I didn’t hold Fight back, did I?

Just as these thoughts crossed his mind, Tang Yu’an’s voice came from beside him:

“Feather, how did this game feel?”

“Hm?” Ling Suyu turned to look at him, blinking.

“Our team fights weren’t any worse than UH’s, were they?” Tang Yu’an asked.

Ling Suyu thought about it seriously, then nodded. “The overall rhythm was much better, and we made far fewer mistakes.”

Perhaps not expecting such a serious response, Tang Yu’an burst out laughing, then said with a smile, “Of course. After all, I’m the one calling the shots. Though I didn’t call many this game, so the team fights mostly came down to our chemistry.”

Indeed, compared to their previous duo queues, Tang Yu’an had given far fewer commands in this five-man squad match. Though there was certainly no shortage of meaningless chatter…

But precisely because of that, Ling Suyu could feel the bond between Fight’s members.

Even when Tang Yu’an didn’t call for a regroup, they’d instinctively come together when the time came.

And compared to UH, Fight offered much more comprehensive protection for their marksman.

Recalling the feeling of being shielded and pampered by the entire team, Ling Suyu’s lips curled upward, and he said from the bottom of his heart: “I like Fight.”

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow.

After spending some time with Ling Suyu, he knew that Ling Suyu didn’t like to say things he didn’t mean, nor did he like to do things against his will.

When faced with questions from others, he would usually either not answer at all, or answer with exactly what he was thinking.

Simply put, unlike Tang Yu’an, who was always talking nonsense, Ling Suyu always spoke his truest, most sincere thoughts.

He liked Fight?

Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but laugh. “That’s good. But… is it really Fight you like, or is it me?”

Ling Suyu: ?

The rest of the team: !

Yu He, sitting right next to the two of them, tucked a strand of hair behind his ear, directing it towards them.

Dou Jiayan faced the pair, hands pressed against the back of his ears, adopting a posture that suggested he was listening intently.

Even Mo Xiaoyun, who usually came across as the most level-headed, shifted slightly to one side upon hearing those words, his gaze passing through his other two teammates and landing directly on Ling Suyu.

Unaware of his teammates’ reactions, Ling Suyu stared blankly at Tang Yu’an for a moment before giving an answer that wouldn’t offend anyone: “Um… I like both. I like your support plays, and my teammates are all really strong too. I’ll keep doing my best!”

“Yeah, you’re also strong, let’s all do our best—” Tang Yu’an said, reaching up to ruffle Ling Suyu’s hair. He shot a glare at the three teammates behind him, trying to shoo them away with his eyes—What are you looking at? Still staring?

Yu He silently put his stray hairs back in place.

Dou Jiayan let out a dismissive “Tsk—” and turned his gaze back to his computer screen, casually clicking “Play Again” to enter the team lobby: “Want to play another round?”

Mo Xiaoyun glanced at the time in the bottom right corner of his screen: “It’s pretty late. How about tomorrow? We’ll review the game first thing tomorrow, then practice our heroes in solo queue. I barely played over New Year’s, and though I just had a match and my movements feel okay, I still don’t feel quite confident.”

“Alright then, it’s settled,” Tang Yu’an cut straight to the chase upon hearing that, “We’re done for today. Whoever needs to shower, go shower; whoever needs to sleep, go to sleep.

Get up before noon tomorrow, and after lunch, we’ll review the game, then practice heroes solo on our alt accounts. In the evening, we’ll team up for a five-man game to sync up more with Feather. And the day after tomorrow, we’ll give YJ a little surprise!”

“OK—” Dou Jiayan flashed an “OK” sign.

“Alright,” Yu He nodded.

“I’m outta here,” Mo Xiaoyun said as he shut down his computer, stood up, and stretched.

As he left the training room, he passed behind Ling Suyu. He glanced at Ling Suyu, who was still analyzing the post-match data, said nothing, reached out to pat his shoulder, and then walked away.

Startled by the sudden tap on his shoulder, Ling Suyu flinched like a startled cat, shrinking back into his gaming chair while instinctively turning to see Mo Xiaoyun’s back as he walked away with a casual stride.

He tilted his head in confusion, unsure what the gesture meant.

Tang Yu’an watched his reaction with amusement and explained on Mo Xiaoyun’s behalf, “He’s acknowledging you.”

“Is that so?” Ling Suyu relaxed slightly.

In truth, even if Mo Xiaoyun hadn’t said anything, Ling Suyu could sense his approval.

In that last game, Mo Xiaoyun kept standing in front of him to tank any damage coming towards him, a clear sign of his trust in him.

Skeletal Empress was a tank. She didn’t deal much damage, but her defense was top-notch.

If she didn’t have high-damage teammates behind her to provide firepower, charging in with this hero was just asking to get beaten up.

“See, like I said, dreams are often the opposite of reality. Your teammates on Fight are all pretty good,” Tang Yu’an suddenly said.

Ling Suyu froze for a moment before finally realizing, “You still remember that dream…”

He’d almost forgotten it himself.

Indeed, after playing a match with his new teammates at Fight, that nightmare, which had once felt so painfully real, suddenly seemed distant.

The solid, satisfying sensation of each auto-attack landing on an enemy lingered on his fingertips, filling Ling Suyu with a profound sense of peace.

“That’s—I’ve always had a great memory,” Tang Yu’an drifted off-topic as he spoke, “When I was a kid, I could recite classical poems word-for-word after just one read-through. My Chinese teacher was always pestering me to be the class representative.”

“Wow!” Ling Suyu’s eyes lit up.

However, the passer-by Yu He: “Ugh—he’s just bragging.”

Dou Jiayan, also passing by: “I can already guess what the captain’s next line is going to be…”

Dou Jiayan: “Just kidding.”

“Just kidding.”

Tang Yu’an and Dou Jiayan spoke almost simultaneously, uttering the exact same words.

Ling Suyu: ??

Dou Jiayan slipped away silently, while Tang Yu’an nonchalantly patted Ling Suyu on the shoulder: “You… Don’t just believe everything you hear from now on. Actually, the Chinese teacher kept pestering me to be the class representative because I was good at writing essays!”

Ling Suyu stared at Tang Yu’an suspiciously for a moment, then asked tentatively, “Are you lying to me about that too?”

“Guess—” Tang Yu’an didn’t seem inclined to reveal the answer. After saying those two words, he stood up and left his seat. “Alright, time to wash up and go to bed. Good night.”

Ling Suyu: ??

Wait! Just make yourself clear!

How the hell was he supposed to sleep now!

“Wait!” Ling Suyu hesitated for a moment, then stood up and grabbed Tang Yu’an’s wrist.

Tang Yu’an blinked.

Ling Suyu cleared his throat. Even though he felt the atmosphere had grown a bit awkward, he still… steeled himself and pressed on: “So, why exactly did the Chinese teacher make you class representative?”

“Pfft—hahaha!” Dou Jiayan, who had already stepped one foot out of the training room, couldn’t help but burst out laughing upon hearing that.

Even Yu He, who was usually cold and aloof, couldn’t stop the corners of his mouth from twitching.

Ling Suyu looked at them in confusion.

Finally, Dou Jiayan couldn’t hold back anymore: “Feather, you’re just too cute! Truth is, the whole ‘Chinese teacher made him class representative’ thing was a lie from the start! Nine out of ten things the captain says are lies, don’t believe him!”

“Come on, I really was one, back in elementary school. Isn’t that good enough?” Tang Yu’an protested.

“You’ve got some nerve,” Yu He couldn’t stand it anymore. “We finally got an innocent newbie, so you’re picking on him, aren’t you?”

“What do you guys know? I’m teaching him by example to not believe everything people say!” Tang Yu’an said, casually placing a hand on Ling Suyu’s back. “Feather, don’t pay them any mind. Let’s go.”

“Oh.” Ling Suyu let Tang Yu’an lead him along for a bit before realizing something was off.

“Wait, I haven’t shut down my computer yet!”

“Yanzi, help him shut it down.”

After giving the instruction, Tang Yu’an immediately pulled Ling Suyu upstairs.

Dou Jiayan watched their retreating figures, his mouth forming an “O”. It took him a moment to snap out of it: “He-He, do you think the captain might be…”

Yu He raised an eyebrow: “Don’t ask me. I don’t care about the captain’s personal life.”

That said, he did think Dou Jiayan’s guess made sense.

Although the captain usually talked nonsense all the time, this was the first time he’d been caught really going all out with someone.

And that gesture of raising his hand to rest on the other person’s back was just a little too natural…

Ugh—better not let his imagination run wild. As long as it didn’t affect their matches, they could do whatever they wanted.

After Dou Jiayan shut down Ling Suyu’s computer, he headed upstairs with Yu He.

Meanwhile, over at SMG, the team was still seriously reviewing the match they’d just played.

They first logged into Ling Suyu’s pro account and took a look at his frequently played heroes.

His most commonly used was Guardian Spirit, with a win rate of 81%; Next was Netherworld Shadow, with a win rate of 77%; Third was Cat Spirit, with a win rate of 79%; Fourth was Desert Hunter, with a win rate of 76%; However, these were his total statistics for all seasons combined.

They then looked at his data for this season—specifically, the matches played after New Year’s Day—and found that Cat Spirit ranked first with a 100% win rate!

Second was Desert Hunter, with a 95% win rate!

“Damn!” the SMG players couldn’t help but curse.

“How did he manage to achieve a 100% win rate with a dogwater hero? He’s played quite a few games, too. It can’t be that they’ve been training nonstop over New Year’s, can it?”

“No.” The mid-laner captain immediately checked the number of games the other members of Fight had played this season—most were in the single digits. As much as he didn’t want to admit it, he had to state the harsh truth: “They haven’t been training. The game we played against them today might be the first one they’ve played since New Year’s.”
