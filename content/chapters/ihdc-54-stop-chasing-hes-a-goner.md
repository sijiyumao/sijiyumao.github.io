Just as Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit killed Guardian Spirit, the livestream camera happened to focus on the bottom lane, as both the commentators and the audience were curious to see how Cat Spirit would lane against Guardian Spirit.

While watching the match, the two commentators drew on their own insights to describe to the audience the entire process—from the Cat Spirit leaving its tower to killing Guardian Spirit:

Ah-Mu: “Guardian Spirit is pushed out really deep right now! Both junglers are in the top jungle, is it really okay for him to be pushing this far? But Cat Spirit probably wouldn’t dare leave the tower, since Guardian Spirit has all its abilities charged except for its ultimate. A full combo could instantly kill someone… Fight’s support has left the tower! What’s he up to?”

ZhiZhi: “Cat Spirit followed him out too! Is Fight planning to go head-to-head with igame? As expected, Fight has been keeping a close eye on the enemy jungler’s position this whole time!”

Ah-Mu: “Guardian Spirit strikes first! Oh, it missed! Cat Spirit dodged his CC with a dash, applied a mark, and started auto-attacking!”

ZhiZhi: “igame’s support cast their shield this early?”

Ah-Mu: “Guardian Spirit’s first skill was dodged by Cat Spirit! Cat Spirit maneuvered to avoid the Arrows of Light! Cat Spirit keeps attacking! Guardian Spirit should pull back right about now! Wow, Fight’s support just landed a crowd control! Guardian Spirit is locked down!”

ZhiZhi: “That crowd control was timed perfectly!”

ZhiZhi was cut off mid-exclamation by Ah-Mu, who switched to rapid-fire real-time commentary: “igame’s support used his shield, but Cat Spirit is still auto-attacking! Cat Spirit is now blocking the path to igame’s bottom lane first tower—Guardian Spirit has no way out! Guardian Spirit used Flash in the direction of the river, but Cat Spirit Flashes right after it and keeps attacking! Hmm?”

When he saw Cat Spirit turn around, Ah-Mu froze for a moment, but immediately afterward, he saw Guardian Spirit fall to the ground, along with the system notification confirming its death.

“Cat Spirit gets first blood!” Ah-Mu quickly realized what had happened. “Cat Spirit landed one final auto-attack, then turned and walked away—that was pretty cool!”

“So cool! That’s the vibe of a hero who never looks back at the explosion!” ZhiZhi finally found a chance to speak. 【You can tell Feather had a clear assessment of the damage he was dealing, that he knew he could definitely secure the kill on Guardian Spirit!】

“Wait, could you please show the replay?” Ah-Mu noticed something off—Guardian Spirit’s remaining health wasn’t enough for Cat Spirit to finish off with a basic attack.

Sure enough, after watching the replay, he confirmed it: Cat Spirit’s final basic attack had triggered her passive!

And the reason Ling Suyu turned away so decisively was that he clearly knew the passive would trigger—had he been counting his basic attacks the whole time?!

Even the commentators only realized this after watching the replay, and many viewers didn’t catch it until the commentators pointed it out, causing them to all let out a collective gasp.

The livestream chat had originally been filled with 【666666】, 【Feather is awesome!】, and 【Awesome!】

After the commentator pointed out that Ling Suyu had finished off Guardian Spirit’s low health only after triggering the passive with that final hit, the chat then turned to:

【Holy sh*t! That’s way too detailed! I’d never count my own auto-attacks, I just chase people down and attack until it’s over!】

【To the person above: Can you really compare yourself to a pro player?】

【Do pro players really count their autos?】

【Well, it’s not like you need to be counting consecutively. Just count to three to trigger the passive, then repeat ← explanation by a Cat Spirit player】

【As someone who hates hassle, I’ll probably never get the hang of Cat Spirit my whole life, orz】

【Who in their right mind would play Cat Spirit?! Feather isn’t human, he’s a god!】

After Ling Suyu took down Guardian Spirit and returned to the lane, he continued last-hitting minions with Tang Yu’an’s help.

Tang Yu’an deliberately targeted minions at full health, whittling them down to low health so Ling Suyu could finish them off. Ling Suyu, for his part, was having a blast.

The enemy support, Abyssal Lord, existed solely to protect his teammates. With no one left to protect, apart from being tough to kill due to his high health and defense, he may as well have been invisible.

So, after Ling Suyu and Tang Yu’an pushed the minion wave all the way to the tower, they went straight for the tower, completely ignoring Abyssal Lord, who was frantically pacing back and forth inside it.

Tang Yu’an even quipped, “If you’re playing Abyssal Lord, letting the marksman die before you is a dereliction of duty.”

Upon hearing this, Ling Suyu couldn’t help but add, “If I were playing Guardian Spirit, I’d use Flash to ambush, not to escape.”

Skill damage could be dodged with proper positioning. Professional players all had quick reflexes, especially during the laning phase. When they didn’t have to worry about other heroes and focused solely on the enemy marksman, they could anticipate when the opponent would use a skill. As soon as they saw the cast animation, they could reposition to avoid it, making dodging quite easy—Or at least, Ling Suyu found it easy.

So, if Ling Suyu were playing Guardian Spirit, he wouldn’t dare push so deep. But once he decided to strike, he’d Flash right in, lock them down, and unleash his damage, taking them out in one combo.

—That was exactly how he’d killed Ma Mingxi during the White Fox Cup.

The reason Ling Suyu didn’t like playing Guardian Spirit was that, in the early game, she played more like an assassin than a marksman—and an assassin without any mobility to boot.

The cooldown on Flash was much longer than the ones on any one of her skills, so she couldn’t use it willy-nilly.

He doesn’t mind using movement skills. In those cases, he could calculate the cooldowns, leaving himself enough time to escape.

But when it comes to using Flash, he really did feel a sting in his heart.

“The jungler’s coming, let’s pull back.”

By the time Tang Yu’an said this, Ling Suyu had already stopped attacking the tower and was running back.

Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but chuckle: “True enough, you don’t need me to remind you of that. As expected of my marksman, so reassuring~”

Ling Suyu: “……”

Guardian Spirit met its end at the hands of Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit before even reaching level 6, and SMG’s players were undoubtedly the happiest about it.

They were all acting like they were high right now: “Yeah! Let igame get a taste of the fear of being dominated by the Cat Spirit!”

“What did I tell you? If Feather picks Cat Spirit, igame will definitely lose even worse than we did!”

“igame’s no good! At least we held out until level 6, but they got taken out by Cat Spirit before they even hit that point!”

“Feather’s Cat Spirit is truly insane—beautiful!”

As another team that had faced off against Fight, YJ didn’t have much to say about this clash between Fight and igame just yet. After all, the match had only been going for less than five minutes, so it was still too early to tell.

But they all felt a vague sense of unease.

—Could it be that… Cat Spirit is actually Feather’s first-pick? Did Fight hold back when they played us in the practice match?

It was normal to hold back in practice matches. If they went all out in practice, wouldn’t they just be asking for their opponents to completely analyze their tactics and crush them in the official matches?

But in order to force Fight to reveal their true strength, YJ had gone all out during the practice match…

Even if they didn’t take the results of the practice matches too seriously, they’d definitely played with full commitment.

As it turned out, even when they gave it their all, Fight still crushed them without giving them a chance to fight back.

And now they were being told that Fight might’ve been holding back.

That was just too demoralizing!

However, there was also another possibility: maybe it was precisely because they’d discovered issues during those practice matches that Feather decided to practice Cat Spirit as well.

While this wasn’t as crushing, it gave the entire YJ team a strong sense of crisis—they’d finally figured out a way to counter Desert Hunter, but if Feather’s Cat Spirit became even stronger than his Desert Hunter, then…

While they were pondering this, igame’s jungler arrived in the bottom jungle. Realizing he couldn’t kill Cat Spirit at that moment, he skipped the bottom lane entirely and continued jungling in the bottom jungle instead, while advising his marksman: “You can’t out-damage him early on, so don’t push too deep. Focusing on poking from near the tower.”

Guardian Spirit had already respawned and, hearing this on his way back to the lane, couldn’t help but sigh: “If I can’t out-damage him early on, I definitely won’t be able to do it later. I’ll still have to find a chance to insta-kill him with a combo. I shouldn’t have cast my ability right away just now; I should’ve Flashed behind him first.”

“You’re overthinking it. He has a movement skill—if you Flash towards him, he’ll dash away.”

“That depends on his reaction time,” igame’s marksman said, though he knew full well that if the enemy could dodge his abilities with proper positioning, his reaction time must be sharp. Their captain was right—if he actually Flashed in to apply CC, he’d just die even faster.

It was all the opponent’s Cat Spirit’s fault for staying tucked under the tower the whole time, even getting hit by one of his skills, which had lulled him into a false sense of security. He’d assumed he could finish him off no matter what, given his health. He’d never expected to miss two skills in a row, only to get hit by the enemy support’s crowd control instead, giving Cat Spirit the chance to stand still and auto-attack.

Facing a late-game marksman, getting the chance to land so many auto-attacks for a kill this early on—Cat Spirit must be having a great time just about now?

Indeed, Ling Suyu was on cloud nine—

He felt as if, playing Cat Spirit, he’d been coated in gold—shining brightly and unstoppable!

And his own support was incredibly strong too—the timing of the crowd control was spot-on!

If it had been cast too early, Guardian Spirit would’ve had enough health left to escape once the CC wore off.

If it had been cast too late, Guardian Spirit would’ve definitely used Flash to escape towards the tower.

Although they’d still have a chance to Flash in and finish her off, a Spider Queen at this level couldn’t survive more than two hits from the tower.

Casting the CC right when Guardian Spirit was about to run but hadn’t quite made it yet was absolutely brilliant!

Ling Suyu cleared a wave of minions in high spirits, then used a dash to head straight into his team’s bottom jungle.

Tang Yu’an knew what he was up to and hurried to catch up, helping him tank the jungle monsters’ damage. Then he suddenly found the whole situation a bit Ah-Musing, and the corners of his lips curled up involuntarily.

He remembered when Ling Suyu had first joined Fight and they were all practicing in a five-stack. In those games where they failed to secure an early lead, Ling Suyu would always stand on the lane, staring longingly at the jungle camps until he finally called out, “Come on, Yumao, take this camp over here~”

Later on, as Ling Suyu started stealing more of Yu He’s jungle camps, he’d ask when he needed gold: “Can I take this camp?”

Yu He would always just reply with a “Mm-hmm,” not giving it a second thought.

After all, the captain had said that the marksman was the carry, and carries were supposed to take jungle camps anyway. If the carry couldn’t even level up, then what was the point of playing at all!

After getting Yu He’s permission, Ling Suyu felt completely at ease taking the jungle camps.

Later on, Ling Suyu stopped asking altogether. Whenever he wanted to take a camp, he’d just go for it. After all, his teammates had all given him the green light.

Just like in this game.

Tang Yu’an noticed the change in Ling Suyu and thought he was so adorable that he couldn’t help but smile.

But when he realized that the sole reason Ling Suyu had even gone through this change in behaviour was because UH hadn’t let him take any jungle monsters, he sighed quietly to himself.

Ling Suyu was like a small wounded animal. Only after receiving permission did he dare to slowly and cautiously try touching everything that had once hurt him, in an effort to change his perception of it all.

All Tang Yuan could do was protect him well and make sure he didn’t get hurt again.

After igame’s marksman was killed once, he became noticeably more cautious.

To put it bluntly, he chickened out.

He had underestimated Cat Spirit’s damage and, even more so, Feather’s skill; after taking a beating, he didn’t dare to take any more risks.

But while he chickened out, Ling Suyu was even more “chicken-hearted” than him.

Even after killing Guardian Spirit once, Ling Suyu didn’t let it get to his head and never made any attempts to push the enemy down to their tower. Instead, he continued to hover very close to his own tower, seemingly frustrated as he last-hit minions.

He knew full well that at this point, he wasn’t strong enough to pressure the enemy. It took him a long time to kill Guardian Spirit with basic attacks, whereas Guardian Spirit could kill him in an instant if all its skills landed.

While other players using sustained-damage marksmen would panic when facing Guardian Spirit, he remained unusually calm, because the one thing he had in abundance was experience laning against Guardian Spirit.

He knew full well that as long as he remained cautious and patient, not only would Guardian Spirit fail to kill him, but he’d also find an opportunity to counter-kill.

In the early game, however, his teammates needed to create that opportunity for him.

At the 6-minute mark, Yu He coordinated with Dou Jiayan to gank the mid lane. Curse Master in mid lane escaped with low health; knowing he couldn’t catch up, Yu He didn’t pursue. Instead, he returned to base with Dou Jiayan and then cut straight through the jungle to the bottom lane.

At the 7-minute mark, with all his teammates ready, Tang Yu’an instantly teleported into the tower, casting “Spidersilk Trap” to immobilize Guardian Spirit. Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit followed up with “Shadow Steps,” locking onto Guardian Spirit with lightning-fast reflexes. After marking her with “Spiritual Mark,” he started firing away—biu biu biu!

At the same time, Dou Jiayan used a Flash to catch up and cast “Patrol” on the immobilized Guardian Spirit. The jellyfish he had thrown returned and struck Guardian Spirit right on target!

The poor Guardian Spirit hadn’t even recovered from the previous CC when it was immobilized again, left standing in place.

Yu He’s Venomous Scorpion used the movement skill “Poison Fog’s Shadow” to transform into a cloud of purple smoke and appear behind Guardian Spirit, immediately following up with “Poison Claw”!

The half-human, half-scorpion girl extended a pale hand from within the mist, her sharp nails tearing viciously across Guardian Spirit’s body!

Guardian Spirit’s health was dropping rapidly. Even with Abyssal Lord’s shield protecting her, her health fell below a quarter under Fight’s combined assault.

After breaking free from the effects of the control, she immediately abandoned any attempt to counterattack and used Flash to escape.

Tang Yu’an spoke up: “Don’t chase him, he’s done for.”

The next instant, Ling Suyu tapped the R key, and Cat Spirit unleashed her ultimate ability, “Spiritual Explosion”!

Having barely escaped the onslaught and believing himself safe, Guardian Spirit was instantly engulfed by a green explosion and collapsed lifelessly to the ground.

【System】Cat Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

Indeed, Cat Spirit’s ultimate ability, “Spirit Blast,” ignored distance and targeted only enemies marked by “Spirit Mark.”

As long as the mark remained, forget about escaping a short distance—it’d blow you up even if you’d retreated to base!

However, the mark lasts only a limited time and won’t hold out long enough for Guardian Spirit to return all the way to their base.

“Everyone, get out of the tower! Protect me!” Tang Yu’an shouted as he activated his ultimate!

Boosted by Spider Queen’s “Neurotoxin” buff, the entire team sped out of the tower. Immediately afterward, a phantom flashed past their field of vision, appearing almost out of thin air behind Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen.

Tang Yu’an decisively activated his shield, blocking the opponent’s “Double Stab.”

The phantom was the enemy jungler, Double Phantom.

After Double Phantom activated her ultimate, “Phantom Transformation,” she became invisible to friendly targets, while to enemy targets, she was virtually invisible and could not be detected until she launched an attack.

Tang Yu’an knew the enemy jungler was nearby. Although he didn’t know his exact location, he knew he would prioritize killing him since he was low on health, so he activated his ultimate without hesitation and deployed his shield the moment he spotted him.

But Double Phantom’s burst damage was too high; “Double Stab” not only shattered his shield but also dealt a significant chunk of damage, leaving him with just a sliver of health.

Before he could be finished off with a basic attack, Tang Yu’an only had time to say, “Protect Yumao!”

【System】Double Phantom, one kill!

【System】Ally Spider Queen has been slain!

Before he died, Ling Suyu had already locked onto Double Phantom and started auto-attacking!

After Double Phantom killed Tang Yuan, it immediately targeted him and used “Shadow Displacement” to appear right beside him.

At that moment, Ling Suyu’s movement skill was still on cooldown, so he decisively used Flash to dodge Double Phantom’s movement damage. Immediately afterward, his movement skill refreshed. Seizing the moment Double Phantom used Flash to pursue him, he used “Shadow Steps” to swap positions with it and continued his basic attacks!

The two traded Flashs and movement skills, swapping positions twice in a row, yet neither managed to even brush against the other’s hem.

During the fight, Dou Jiayan tossed a “Water Lotus” onto the ground, but it missed.

Immediately after, he used “Energy Drain,” and came up empty-handed again.

Damn it! Was igame’s jungler some kind of loach?! He’s way too hard to hit!

—Completely oblivious to the fact that his own marksman was just as slippery.

Double Phantom couldn’t land a hit on Cat Spirit, and instead, he kept getting hit by Cat Spirit’s auto-attacks; his health was already in dire straits.

Though he was reluctant to admit it, he knew that continuing to trade blows would mean certain death, so after using Flash he stopped chasing Cat Spirit and decisively ran back to his own tower.

But Yu He, standing right nearby, wasn’t about to give him the chance to survive. He instantly dashed into the enemy bottom lane’s first tower and unleashed his ultimate, “Fatal Venom Spike”!

Venomous Scorpion’s ultimate, like Cat Spirit’s, dealt single-target damage and could only be used on one enemy target.

The downside was that its damage was limited in team fights, but the upside was that it dealt high single-target damage. If timed perfectly, it could instantly kill an enemy!

The enemy Double Phantom had already been severely weakened by Cat Spirit, and after taking Yu He’s ultimate, he was left with just a sliver of health.

Just as it looked like Yu He could finish him off with a single auto-attack, suddenly, the enemy mid-laner, Curse Master, activated his ultimate!

Curse Master’s ultimate, “Curse of Evil,” dealt a set amount of damage to all enemy heroes regardless of distance and applied “Cursed Mark.”

Having already taken tower damage, Yu He was whittled down to a sliver of health by this ultimate. In his panic, he didn’t use a basic attack but instead cast his first skill, “Poison Claw”—only to have the enemy jungler dodge it with a quick flick.

In the next instant, Curse Master finished him off with “Exploding Curse.”

Exploding Curse—Consume all marks to deal damage to the marked target.

This ability shared similarities with Cat Spirit’s “Spirit Burst,” but with one key difference: Cat Spirit’s “Spirit Mark” could only mark a single target, whereas the Curse Master’s “Curse Mark” was applied by nearly all of his abilities and could stack.

Skill 1, “Malicious Infection,” added one layer; Skill 2, “Hand of Hexes,” added another; and its ultimate, “Curse of Evil,” added yet another. Then, by using Skill 3, “Exploding Curse,” which consumed three layers of marks, Curse Master could deal explosive damage.

—This was the basic playstyle for Curse Master, a mid-lane hero.

This hero wasn’t exactly strong, but he wasn’t weak either.

His strength lay in his marks, as well as his weakness

As long as his marks were fully stacked, he could one-shot squishy targets with a single combo.

But if the marks weren’t fully stacked, his damage was very limited—about the same as Cat Spirit’s early-game auto-attacks, which barely made a scratch.

Just like moments ago; though he’d secured Yu He’s kill from a distance, Yu He had already taken tower damage and was already low on health; essentially, he finished off Yu He’s remaining health with his ultimate and third skill.

Moreover, it was clear that he carried the “Increase Damage Against Low-Health Targets” talent. That was why he was able to finish off Yu He’s low health, while to the other members of Fight, including the similarly fragile Cat Spirit, he only managed to deal a single layer of negligible damage.

“Is igame’s jungler about to escape?!” Commentator Ah-Mu watched this team fight with bated breath, as it would directly determine which side would secure the first Divine Sunbird.

Just as he said that, igame’s jungler Double Phantom fell, killed by the poison from Yu He’s Venomous Scorpion.

【System】Venomous Scorpion, one kill!

【System】The enemy Double Phantom has been slain!

All of Venomous Scorpion’s attacks inflicted slow-acting poison that dealt damage over time.

So, even though Yu He’s first skill missed Double Phantom, the poison applied by his ultimate was enough to take him down.

One could use “Heal” to restore health and reduce damage taken.

“Purify” could similarly remove all negative effects from onself.

However, each hero could only bring two talents into battle; Double Phantom had “Flash” and “Smite.”

Faced with his rapidly depleting health and a jungle far out of reach, he was powerless to act and could only watch helplessly as the poison killed him.

And so, this team fight in the bottom lane ended in a 2-for-2 trade.

On Fight’s side, the support died, along with the jungler, who hadn’t fully leveled up yet and had ceded most of his gold to the carry.

On igame’s side, the marksman died, along with their carry—their highest-gold jungler.

Comparing the two sides, even though it was a 2-for-2 trade, Fight came out on top!

But instead of celebrating, Fight broke into an impromptu rant session.

“Wow, Yanzi, what’s wrong with you!” After Tang Yu’an died, he kept a close eye on the bottom lane. Seeing Dou Jiayan miss two skills in a row, he was practically itching to punch him. “I told you to protect Yumao, and this is what you give me? Thank goodness our Yumao is so agile, that ‘wave-like subtle steps’ [^1] was absolutely masterful. He didn’t even let the enemy jungler catch the corner of his clothes. Otherwise, if we’d lost this game, you’d have to answer for it!”

“Tsk,” Yu He followed up, “If even one of Yanzi’s skills had hit, I wouldn’t have died. The enemy mid laner didn’t even come to the bot lane, but he still made a difference. Did you just come down here to sightsee?”

“Hey hey hey! If I hadn’t CC’d Guardian Spirit, would you guys have been able to kill it? I did play a part!” Dou Jiayan retorted, “Besides, the enemy jungler’s clearly also perfected the ‘wave-like subtle steps’—it was nothing short of divine, leaving us mere mortals in awe!”

Tang Yu’an: “Don’t just stand there marveling, learn from it! If you can’t master ‘wave-like subtle steps,’ you’ve got to learn at least one of ‘cloud ladder ascent’ or ‘flight on water’ [^2]!”

Dou Jiayan: “Okay, okay, okay. After this game, I’ll have Yumao teach me!”

Ling Suyu, who was pushing the tower under Dou Jiayan’s protection, blinked—was he really playing in a league match? Or had he accidentally wandered into some kind of martial arts tournament?
