Before the match against igame began, Ling Suyu truly believed that Tang Yu’an’s habit of making off-topic comments during matches was just part of his routine.

He had already gotten used to it and even thought it was a good thing. After all, it helped ease his teammates’ nerves and kept everyone from getting too tense during the match.

However, once the match against igame began—especially after entering the battlefield—Tang Yu’an displayed a level of seriousness completely different from before. Not only was his tone much more serious, but his commands were also brief and clear: “Yumao takes the bottom dragon, Yu He takes the top dragon, and everyone in the top and mid lanes go help.”

Immediately after, he added: “Yumao, let’s play it safe. The enemy might counter-jungle right from the start.”

A counter-jungle right at the start?

That’s just too bold!

igame didn’t do this when they played against GQ or OsO, did they?

Still, if you had to ask whether it was  possible, well, it actually was.

So Ling Suyu obediently replied, “Okay.”

He followed Tang Yu’an to the lower jungle’s dragon pit and waited for a while. At the 30-second mark, the dragon spawned, one blue dragon in the top jungle and a red dragon in the bottom jungle. Their luck wasn’t too bad.

In the early laning phase, the goal is to secure the red dragon, which accelerated health regeneration, reducing the number of times you had to return to base to replenish your stats and ensuring steady gold accumulation. Then, once you reached the appropriate level and had enough gold, you could return to base to fully replenish your stats in one go.

After the red dragon spawned, Tang Yu’an’s Abyssal Lord went in first to build up aggro and lure it into the brush at the edge of the pit.

Then Ling Suyu, who was hiding in the brush with him, started firing away with Desert Hunter—biu biu biu!

He hadn’t activated any of his skills yet, because Tang Yu’an was right—what if the enemy came to counter-jungle?

If the enemy did come to counter-jungle, he’d immediately unlock his second skill, “Explosive Shell.” If they didn’t, he should actually have unlocked “Hunter’s Trap” to poke them at this point.

But Ling Suyu felt that, facing a powerhouse team like Fight, which had secured a championship spot last season and had been on a dominating streak this season, crushing every team they’d faced, the likelihood of igame counter-jungling was negligible, probably around 10%?

Although Ling Suyu was highly sensitive to danger, he had a rough estimate in his mind. For example, if he judged the probability of the enemy jungler coming to the bottom lane to be over 50%, he would decisively retreat unless his teammates were fully prepared to engage.

Conversely, if he judged the probability of the enemy jungler coming to the bottom lane to be less than 5%, even though the possibility still existed, he would still dare to push forward.

That awkward 10% probability left him feeling that the chance of the enemy coming was very low, yet he still had to remain on high alert.

But even with that mental preparation, Ling Suyu was still startled when the enemy jungler, Child of Frost, used his first skill, “Frost Spike,” to dash straight into the pit—he really came?!

On igame’s side, the coach’s last words before taking off his headset were: “The longer the game drags on, the more disadvantageous it is for us. Even if we have to take risks, we should try to establish an early lead.”

So once the game began, igame’s captain and jungler, Ye Xunjie, spoke up: “Bot lane, follow me to counter-jungle. Mid laner, come with us too.”

And so, on the livestream feed with its bird’s-eye view, igame’s jungler, marksman, support, and mid laner all surged into Fight’s bottom jungle!

“igame is counter-jungling right from the start!” Ah-Mu exclaimed excitedly. “igame is so bold! But the greater the risk, the greater the reward. If they actually manage to steal this camp, Fight is going to have a rough time early on!”

Fight had also noticed igame’s high-profile counter-jungle attempt.

“Something’s off. I’ve got no one on my lane.” After feigning a few auto-attacks to help Yu He, Dou Jiayan immediately rushed back to his lane to farm minions.

The mid lane minions arrived first; if he was even a split second late, he’d miss the first one. For a mid laner who relied on gold to deal damage, missing even a tiny bit of gold felt like losing a hundred million.

But his lane was completely empty, not a soul in sight.

He watched helplessly as his first minion was killed by the enemy’s minions, and even felt a twinge of sympathy for the enemy mid laner over the gold he’d missed out on.

But immediately afterward, he realized something was off. He called out to warn his teammates while rushing toward his team’s bottom jungle.

owever, by the time he made a round trip to the lane and back, it was already too late.

Just then, the enemy jungler, Child of Frost, used his first ability, “Frost Spike,” to dash into the dragon pit in Fight’s bottom jungle, then brazenly stepped into the jungle next to the pit.

Then: “Hey, how’s it going—”

Seeing Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu crouched in the brush alongside the half-killed red dragon, igame’s jungler Ye Xunjie couldn’t help but call out to them.

Of course, only his teammates on the same voice chat could hear this greeting; Fight couldn’t hear it.

Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu had spotted him the moment he teleported into the pit. They knew he’d come to check the bushes, but they never expected him to just stride right in so brazenly.

Truly…commendable courage.

Tang Yu’an immediately activated his second skill, “Touch of the Abyss,” lashing out with a tentacle to slow Child of Frost’s movement speed.

Ling Suyu, meanwhile, stayed hidden behind Tang Yu’an, continuing to farm the red dragon while activating his second skill, “Explosive Shell,” ready to make a run for it at any moment.

Just then, the enemy marksman and support rushed over. The support cast a heal on the jungler, and the marksman’s first skill, “Entangling Vines,” hit Abyssal Lord right in the brush, trapping Tang Yu’an.

Immediately afterward, the enemy mid-laner, Curse Master, used his first skill, “Malicious Infection.” Black smoke enveloped Abyssal Lord, quickly spreading to Desert Hunter and dealing damage to both of them.

Tang Yu’an was taking damage from both the enemy heroes and the red dragon, and his health was critically low.

Seeing that the time was right, Ling Suyu fired an Explosive Round, hitting the red dragon, which was already low on health, dead center.

At the same time, the enemy jungler, Child of Frost, cast “Smite” on the red dragon.

Witnessing this, the live audience couldn’t help but hold their breath.

Both players activated their abilities or talents almost simultaneously; the timing couldn’t have been better. Even if there was a difference, it was within 0.1 seconds, too brief for the naked eye to detect, leaving the system to make the call.

And the system’s verdict was—

A small red marker, signifying the red dragon’s buff, appeared above Child of Frost’s head, and all the experience and gold from the red dragon went to Child of Frost.

Tang Yu’an gave a quiet sigh: “Yanzi, don’t come over.”

The very next moment—

【System】Child of Frost, first blood!

【System】Ally Abyssal Lord has been slain!

“igame snatched it! igame successfully counter-jungled and took the red dragon! On Fight’s side, the support is down, and the marksman has escaped!” Ah-Mu commented.

“So intense! As expected of a match between quarterfinalists, it’s already this exciting right from the start! Less than two minutes into the game, igame has already secured a kill on Fight. Fight is going to have a rough time in the early game!” ZhiZhi added.

Ling Suyu returned to base to fully restore his health bar, which had been reduced to a sliver, and waited for Tang Yu’an to respawn so they could head to the bottom lane together.

The longer the match went on, the longer it took to respawn.

Right now, the match had only been going for less than two minutes. Almost as soon as Ling Suyu returned to base, Tang Yu’an respawned as if nothing had happened.

But the 0-1 displayed at the top of the screen, along with the enemy jungler’s game-high gold count, served as constant reminders to Ling Suyu of what had just happened.

“I’m sorry.” He muttered in a barely audible voice.

Hearing those words, not only did Tang Yu’an pause for a moment, but his other teammates did as well.

However, while the other teammates were merely puzzled and said nothing, Tang Yu’an spoke up with a hint of resignation: “What are you apologizing for? You didn’t do anything wrong. Just farm safely and catch up on gold.”

Ling Suyu gave a soft “Mm.”

He knew he hadn’t done anything wrong. In that previous engagement, Tang Yu’an was bound to die—the only difference was whether he could steal the red dragon.

If he had stolen it, Tang Yu’an’s death wouldn’t have been in vain, and igame’s failed jungle invasion would have cost them an economic disadvantage.

But the problem was, he didn’t steal it. So not only did Tang Yu’an die in vain, but igame’s economic lead over Fight also widened.

He’d used his ability at the perfect moment, but the enemy jungler still beat him to it.

If only he’d been a little faster, just 0.1 seconds faster…

Back when he was on UH, Ling Suyu would never have dwelled on something like this. He knew full well that such a difference of a few tenths of a second depended not only on the players’ skills but also on how the game registered for both teams—sometimes, it really did come down to luck.

If he couldn’t steal it, he couldn’t steal it. Just as Tang Yu’an had said, he’d simply have to make up the gold later.

But for some reason, thinking about how Tang Yu’an had died for him, and that he hadn’t even managed to steal the red dragon, Ling Suyu, though he ran off just as decisively as ever, without even looking back, felt a vague sense of guilt he couldn’t quite put his finger on.

Perhaps sensing his emotional turmoil, Tang Yu’an, who had been on edge the entire time since the match began, suddenly reverted to his usual laid-back self and started cracking jokes to lighten the mood: “igame must be gloating like crazy right now, huh? Let them gloat a little longer! The more they’re gloating now, the harder they’ll cry once we win this match! Yumao, just focus on leveling up for now. Once you’re ready, we’re going to take them down! Especially that jungler—we won’t be satisfied until we’ve killed him a hundred times!”

“You’re right!” Dou Jiayan was the first to chime in. “Killing our captain right at the start, that’s a total lack of respect! We’re gonna have to hunt him down the whole game, aren’t we?!”

Mo Xiaoyun: “igame’s really getting out of hand. We’ll make them pay in the late game!”

Yu He nodded: “Let’s avenge the captain.”

Hearing his teammates’ words, Ling Suyu let out a sigh of relief and murmured, “Mm-hmm.” “I’m going to farm first.”

After he and Tang Yu’an arrived at their lane, they began farming minions safely under the tower.

However, at the 6-minute mark, the enemy jungler Child of Frost, who had reached level 6 first, tower-dived and instantly killed Nine-Tailed Fox, who was still at level 5 and cowering under the mid lane tower.

Dou Jiayan, who was usually loud but rarely swore, blurted out, “Damn it!” on the spot.

At the 7-minute mark, that same Child of Frost, coordinating with Curse Master who had rushed to support the top lane, and Skeletal Empress, who had just respawned after her passive was triggered by Mo Xiaoyun, tower-dived and surrounded Mo Xiaoyun’s Black-Armoured Scythe, killing him.

Yu He activated his Double Phantom’s ultimate and charged into the tower. After killing Skeletal Empress, he was counter-killed by Child of Frost, but immediately afterward, Child of Frost was also killed by the tower. Only igame’s mid laner Curse Master managed to escape successfully and cleared a wave of minions on the top lane.

It was a 2-for-2 exchange.

At the 9-minute mark, the enemy bottom lane launched an attack. Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu sensed danger and immediately retreated along the wall.

Sure enough, the enemy jungler Child of Frost used the minion wave to dash into the tower and, in coordination with the enemy bot lane marksman and support, took down Fight’s bottom lane’s first tower.

“Fight’s bottom lane first tower has been taken! igame’s gold has completely suppressed Fight!” Ah-Mu commented.

“igame has a great chance this game!” ZhiZhi said, “Desert Hunter is a champion who can’t deal damage without early-game gold. Fight’s jungler gave all the bottom jungle camps to Desert Hunter, leaving himself unable to farm. If Desert Hunter still can’t farm up under these conditions, then during team fights, it’s as if Fight is missing two teammates at once!”

“Actually, I was really looking forward to seeing Desert Hunter dominate in the quarterfinals.” Ah-Mu said, “But against igame, Desert Hunter is indeed a bit out of his depth.”

UH’s match was already over. Today was the 18th, and the quarterfinal points round was on the 27th, leaving nine days left before it began. So instead of rushing into practice, they turned their attention to the matches of the other quarterfinal teams, including the upcoming Fight vs. igame match that would follow theirs.

Before watching this match, they all had mixed feelings, because Fight’s current starting carry was Ling Suyu—a substitute marksman they had once “benched”—whom they had never taken seriously before.

They didn’t want to see a substitute like this dominating the battlefield, because that would mean that back when Ling Suyu was still at UH, they were not only blind but also overly conceited. Not only had they mistaken a dazzling gem for an ordinary stone, but they also believed themselves to be far more brilliant than that stone, and thus were unwilling to make any sacrifices to polish it.

Perhaps the heavens heard their inner thoughts. The scene they’d imagined, where Ling Suyu single-handedly carried Fight to victory over igame, never materialized. Instead, Fight was being crushed by igame to the point of suffocation.

UH’s starters all breathed a sigh of relief and began to gloat: “Who let him use Desert Hunter! If he’d just properly stolen Guardian Spirit, would he be playing so miserably?”

“How many times has the support died for him? The jungler gave him all his monsters, and he even went to farm mid-lane minions. If this were any other marksman, being pampered like this by his teammates, wouldn’t a pentakill be the least he could do?”

“Fight’s actually treating him like something precious? For the whole team to be spoiling him, it’s ridiculous!”

“Ever heard the fable of the farmer and the snake [^1]? If Fight keeps this up, they’ll get bitten by that snake sooner or later!”

It wasn’t just UH’s starters bragging. Once UH’s fans found out how hard Fight was struggling against igame, they started bragging too:

【Is this the ‘Feather’ you guys have been hyping up, the one who can carry four and get a pentakill?】

【The commentator even called him Glorious’s number one Desert Hunter—that’s hilarious!】

【He can’t even beat igame, and he thinks he can beat us, UH?】

These comments undoubtedly drew a backlash from Fight’s fans, but at this point, most people were focused on the match itself and didn’t have time to pay attention to the online buzz.

Although Fight was clearly at a disadvantage, the match wasn’t over yet, so it was too early to count them out.

While UH and their fans were gloating, igame easily took down the Divine Sunbird and placed it in the mid lane.

Dou Jiayan tried to defend it but was stopped by Tang Yu’an: “We can’t hold it. Retreat and follow me.”

Dou Jiayan had no choice but to fall back to the second tower, watching helplessly as igame took down their mid-lane first tower, then teamed up with Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu to kill the Divine Sunbird and clear the minion wave.

Meanwhile, Yu He, knowing that the enemy heroes were all in the mid lane, took the opportunity to team up with Mo Xiaoyun to take down the enemy’s top lane first tower, finally preventing igame from gaining an advantage on all three lanes.

At the 10-minute mark, the Dragon God spawned.

The entirety of Fight returned to base—some to replenish their health, others to buy items.

“Do they dare to fight it?” Yu He said as he rushed toward the dragon pit. With the fastest movement speed and a dash ability, he could reach the dragon pit before his teammates.

But as a jungler with “Smite,” he had to ensure he could secure the Dragon God. Even if all his teammates died, he couldn’t afford to die.

So when he arrived, he dared only to hide in the bushes a short distance from the river and didn’t move in at all.

Tang Yu’an thought for a moment: “If it were igame, they’d actually dare to do it.”

When was the safest time to take the Dragon God?

Of course, it was when the enemy team was wiped out.

So, when the Dragon God spawned, it meant a team fight was coming.

Whether a team had an economic advantage or disadvantage, both sides had to be prepared for a team fight once the Dragon God spawned.

And to ensure they could secure the Dragon God, either side might initiate the team fight first.

However, since the goal of the team fight was to secure the Dragon—and ultimately, it was all about the Dragon—it was also possible to skip the team fight and go straight for the Dragon, provided they can ensure the enemy wouldn't contest it or that the probability of them securing it was very low.

Currently, igame held an advantage in the mid and bot lanes. In particular, their lead in the mid lane, combined with the presence of their mid-lane first tower, had placed Fight in a very disadvantageous position.

Whether it was taking the Divine Sunbird, fighting for the Dragon, or engaging in a team fight near the river, igame held an absolute advantage.

Under these circumstances, it’s entirely possible for them to skip the team fight and go straight for the Dragon.

Faced with this situation, Fight had only two options.

Either they sent only the jungler in to take a gamble—if they stole the Dragon, that was a win; if they failed, the jungler would be in a much tougher spot.

Or, the entire team charged in for a team fight—if they won, they could turn the tide of the game; if they lost, the whole team suffers, the economic gap widens further, and they might even get swept by igame.

The higher the reward, the greater the risk, fair and realistic.

In response, Tang Yu’an’s decision was: “Let’s go for a team fight. Xiaoyun, initiate.”

At this point, Fight wasn’t sure if igame was fighting the Dragon, but from the god’s-eye view on the livestream, it was clear that igame had already started fighting the Dragon.

Ah-Mu: “igame’s jungler hit the Dragon! Are they planning to take it right away? Or are they just testing the waters?”

ZhiZhi: “He hit it once and ran away—it was probably just a probe…Huh? He’s gone back to keep attacking it! The rest of igame is coming over too!”

Ah-Mu: “igame is actually going for the Dragon right away! They’re so bold! Will Fight come to steal it?”

ZhiZhi: “They definitely will! I just don’t know if they’ll send one person to steal it or the whole team!”

Ah-Mu: “The entire Fight team is rushing to the dragon pit! A team fight is about to break out!”

Almost as soon as Ah-Mu finished speaking, Mo Xiaoyun unleashed his ultimate into the dragon pit!

He and jungler Yu He flanked around the back and, from behind the pit wall, heard the sounds of the Dragon being attacked, confirming that the enemy was indeed fighting.

At the same time, Tang Yu’an, accompanied by Ling Suyu and Dou Jiayan, charged straight in from the front.

Just before they actually broke through, Tang Yu’an called out, “Xiaoyun, now’s the time!”

Hearing the command, Mo Xiaoyun didn’t even pause to think—he unleashed his ultimate straight into the Dragon Pit!

The ultimate ability of the Black-Armoured Scythe, “Scythe Armour,” was a two-stage skill. The first stage hurled the massive scythe in his hand, dealing damage to enemy targets within the area where it landed. The second stage used the chain attached to the scythe’s tail to pull him toward it, temporarily boosting his defense.

After pulling himself into the pit, Mo Xiaoyun immediately used his second ability, “Chains of Darkness,” to precisely latch onto the enemy jungler, Child of Frost. To steal the Dragon, you had to take out the jungler first!

igame had been fairly decisive in taking the Dragon, so by this point, the Dragon God’s health was already very low.

As soon as Mo Xiaoyun entered the pit, the Dragon God’s health bar appeared in Fight’s field of view. Yu He saw it and thought, “Perfect timing—I can finish him off.” He immediately used “Shadow Displacement” to enter the pit, followed by “Smite”!

【System】Double Phantom has successfully conquered the Dragon God and summoned the divine dragon legion!

“Fight has snatched the Dragon God!!” Ah-Mu shouted in disbelief!

Just as he let out his cry, Dou Jiayan’s Nine-Tailed Fox unleashed its ultimate ability, “Devouring Flames,” landing a direct hit in the dragon pit, which instantly turned into a sea of fire!

Amid this searing sea of flames, Tang Yu’an shielded Ling Suyu as they charged head-on into the fray. Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter activated his ultimate, “Roar of the Desert,” boosting all his damage, then immediately locked onto the enemy marksman and unleashed a barrage of attacks!

Desert Hunter’s first skill, “Hunter’s Trap,” was a speed debuff that dealt minimal damage. Ling Suyu usually only used it during the laning phase. In team fights, he’d treat this skill as nonexistent, auto-attacking whenever possible, landing every extra hit he could!

His teammates were falling behind in both level and gold, but thanks to their sacrifices, his Desert Hunter’s level and gold weren’t any lower than the enemy’s. Without his ultimate, he had no advantage trading auto-attacks with Guardian Spirit, but once his ultimate was active, every auto-attack felt like a skill—the damage was nothing to scoff at!

The enemy marksman, Guardian Spirit, was rapidly losing health, but because his teammates were CC’d, he didn’t choose to Flash away. Instead, he immediately activated his ultimate to counterattack, determined to win this team fight!

Tang Yu’an used his ability shield and talent shield in quick succession to tank his attacks, while Ling Suyu continued to auto-attack!

The enemy support had already exhausted every available ability and shield, but couldn’t save their marksman’s health.

In the end, Guardian Spirit didn’t even have time to use Flash before falling to Desert Hunter’s relentless barrage of bullets!

【System】Desert Hunter, one kill!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

Seeing their marksman fall and with his own health already significantly depleted by the Nine-Tailed Fox’s ultimate, the enemy support immediately used Flash to flee.

However, Dou Jiayan, who had been positioned slightly higher up to begin with, immediately followed with a Flash of his own. A “Flame Wave” knocked him back, sending him straight into Ling Suyu’s face.

【System】Desert Hunter, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Saintess of Light has been slain!

The enemy jungler’s health had been whittled down by more than half by Mo Xiaoyun, but the enemy support saved him with a last-ditch ultimate before dying. After breaking free from the crowd control, he immediately used his first skill, “Frost Spike,” to charge at Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter.

Ling Suyu knew Child of Frost would prioritize killing him once he broke free, so he had deliberately saved his dash ability. The moment Child of Frost closed in, he activated it and fired an Explosive Shell straight into his face!

Child of Frost didn’t give up; he used Flash to catch up to Ling Suyu and immediately unleashed his ultimate!

Ling Suyu dodged it once again with “Hound’s Charge,” then locked onto him and auto-attacked! Auto-attacked! And auto-attacked again!

Child of Frost’s ultimate struck Tang Yu’an, who was standing close to him, with full force.

Having grown accustomed to this, Tang Yu’an silently absorbed the damage, watching Ling Suyu’s performance from where he stood.

Sure enough, Ling Suyu didn’t let him down.

Ling Suyu had never let him down.

【System】 Desert Hunter, triple kill! Unstoppable!

【System】 The enemy Child of Frost has been slain!

【Feather killed igame’s jungler and saved the low-health support at the same time! Feather got a triple kill!】

Ah-Mu commentated excitedly as the chat flooded with “666,” while over on UH’s side, the team stared blankly at the battle unfolding on the livestream, suddenly at a loss for words.

Just as Ling Suyu secured a triple kill, Yu He’s Double Phantom had just snatched the Dragon God when he was CC’d by the enemy mid-laner, Curse Master, using his second skill, “Hand of Hexes.” This was immediately followed by his first skill, “Malicious Infection,” his third skill, “Exploding Curse,” and his ultimate, “Curse of Evil”!

That burst of damage instantly wiped out Yu He’s Double Phantom’s health bar. He didn’t even have time to use his ultimate or Flash before he fell to the ground cleanly and decisively.

However, knowing that he had secured the Dragon God for his team just before his death, he lay there in perfect peace. There wasn’t a trace of regret in his gaze as he looked at the screen; he even leaned back slightly, rubbed his wrist with a look of contentment, and relaxed completely, free of any burden.

Since Curse Master had used all his skills to take him down in an instant, that meant he now had no skills left to use.

Dou Jiayan’s Nine-Tailed Fox immobilized him with “Fire of Imprisonment,” then stood ‘peacefully’ motionless in place.

After all, someone else would claim this kill.

【System】 Desert Hunter, quadra kill! Godlike!

【System】 The enemy Curse Master has been slain!

Now, on igame’s side, only the top laner, Skeletal Empress, remained alive.

After he finished off Mo Xiaoyun’s Black-Armoured Scythe, who was down to low health, his own health bar was completely wiped out by Black-Armoured Scythe’s passive, “Double-Edged Blade.”

But he still had his passive—he could come back to life one more time.

That is, provided his teammates were still alive.

If all his teammates were dead, then even if he revived, there would be no one to cover his retreat, and only one outcome would await him.

【System】 Desert Hunter, penta kill! Even the gods bow down!

【System】 The enemy Skeletal Empress has been slain!

【System】The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us!

【System】 No one can stop Desert Hunter now!

“Fight! Not only did they steal the Dragon God in this team fight, but they also pulled off a 2-for-5 trade! Feather’s Desert Hunter got a pentakill! A pentakill!!” Ah-Mu shouted excitedly, then choked on his own spit and coughed violently.

ZhiZhi quickly added, “With that team fight, Fight not only closed the gold gap but also regained lane control, pulling off a stunning comeback! Feather’s Desert Hunter is so awesome! He’s undoubtedly the best Desert Hunter in Glorious!”

After finishing his coughing fit, Ah-Mu heard this and quickly agreed: “That’s right! Feather is undoubtedly the best Desert Hunter in Glorious! His Desert Hunter is absolutely on fire right now! Let’s wait and see if igame can reclaim the advantage from Fight!”
