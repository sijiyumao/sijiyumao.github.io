【Through blood and sweat, who will be victorious?! The 12th “White Fox Cup” Professional Invitational Match has officially begun!】

A thunderous round of applause followed the announcer’s passionate voice. 

Ling Suyu lifted his gaze. He felt the venue was abnormally bright, to the point of hurting his eyes, yet he couldn’t bear to look away.

It had been a long time since he had officially competed as a member of UH. He never thought that he would have a chance to stand here before his contract ended, to stand on the stage as a starting member. 

Behind him, and behind the four teammates standing by his side, was a large screen. On it, the UH team’s blue logo was displayed, the same colour as their team’s uniform. 

Ling Suyu signed with UH at 16 as the team’s marksman. 

He gave five years and his youth to UH. 

Now, the 20-year-old Ling Suyu would soon arrive at the end of his contract  with UH in less than a month. This could very well be the last time he fought for UH. 

As the applause faded, the announcer once again started: 【The upcoming match is between UH and YJ! Both teams are popular candidates for this competition’s championship, and have traded blows on countless other occasions! Let us wait and see, will UH be able to avenge themselves today, or will YJ’s legend continue!】

【Well then, before we begin today’s match, let us first welcome members from both teams to share their current thoughts with us!】

As the announcer said this, he turned and walked towards YJ. 

Although on paper it was only sharing some thoughts, those who paid attention to 《Glorious Heroes》 were aware that this was actually a trash-talk section. 

As for why YJ got to go first, it was likely because they were more popular. After all, they were a team that once had the chance to compete in the international finals. 

As Ling Suyu glanced towards the direction the announcer was heading, he spotted the members of YJ sharing a look among each other. In the end, their captain, the support Cheng Cheng, was the one who took the microphone: \[Um…..We’re pretty happy right now. How should I say this? Last month, we just got back from overseas, and just last week, we were competing in the All-Stars tournament, so we were pretty tired today. We didn’t want too tiring of a match, but since we met UH in the first match, our pressure has decreased a lot.]

As soon as he said this, there were both boos and roars from the crowd. 

Ling Suyu raised his eyebrows. He didn’t expect that, although YJ’s captain looked steady and reliable, the words from his mouth were quite harsh. 

Even more unexpectedly, as soon as he finished speaking, the microphone in his hand was snatched by the marksman Ma Mingxi beside him. He held the mic to his mouth and shouted at Ling Suyu: \[Feather, long time no see! If it weren’t for your team’s marksman being banned from the competition last night, you wouldn’t even be here today, right? After so many years of not competing, I hope you haven’t gotten rusty. Beware of dying at my hands before even reaching level 6!]

As these words fell, an even louder round of boos sounded, but some also whistled. 

Though this was a trash-talk section, in the gaming spirit of “friendship first, competition second”, most would control the trash-talk within a reasonable range, to the point that it could be brushed off as a joke.

But the words from YJ’s marksman didn’t sound like a joke at all. They were targeted and aggressive, almost to the point of being a personal attack.

The announcer looked rather awkward, but after he got the microphone back, he professionally controlled the scene: 【Oh boy, YJ’s Mingxi is brave with his words! In that case, does UH’s Feather also plan to respond strongly?】

As he said this, he made his way over to Ling Suyu, handing him the microphone.

Ling Suyu took the microphone, not bothering to look at the expressions of the teammates behind him, and didn’t notice the look that his coach gave him.

He calmly faced his opponent, responding in a fresh and unruffled tone: 【e-Sports is played with your hands, not your mouth. As for who'll die in whose hands before level 6, we’ll have to see in the actual match.】

After saying this, he unhurriedly handed the microphone back to the announcer.

Measuring around 180cm, with a skinny build and gentle features, this vulnerable-looking fair-skinned youth had a voice that was equally as soft, but his tone was defiant.

 “Good!” It was unknown who shouted this, but what followed was a round of cheers and applause. 

No matter how strong each respective team was, in this trash-talk segment, UH had won. 

It wasn’t that Ling Suyu’s response was brilliant, but rather that YJ’s unrestrained words disturbed the audience. In comparison, Ling Suyu’s response was composed and cathartic. 

Nevertheless, this was an e-sports competition, and in the end everything relied on strength.

【Through blood and sweat, who will be victorious! Welcome to the professional competition livestream of “Glorious Heroes”’s White Fox Cup! I’m commentator Ah-Mu!】

【I’m your commentator ZhiZhi~】

On various livestreaming platforms, the commentators appeared seated side-by-side.

Ah-Mu was a male commentator, and ZhiZhi was a female commentator. Both were good-looking, and were particularly eye-catching as they sat together.

【The upcoming match is between YJ and UH!】Ah-Mu said, 【We all know YJ, who competed in the last global competition. Although they lost in the quarter-finals, losing the chance to compete in the championship, their strength is nothing to scoff at!】

【Indeed.】ZhiZhi said, 【And UH, though they didn’t manage to enter the last global competition, has always been a popular team in competing for the chance, as was the Spring Tournament runner-up for two consecutive seasons. With YJ, they’re evenly matched, and also old enemies~】

Ah-Mu: 【Unfortunately, UH’s starter marksman was banned from competing today due to some technical difficulties last night. The one appearing today is their substitute marksman—Feather.】

ZhiZhi: 【Changing their main damage dealer right before the competition will likely affect UH’s performance today, it’s a bit worrying!】

Ah-Mu: 【The match is about to begin, let’s not delay any longer, and see how this plays out!】

Alongside the commentators’ words, the livestream screen switched to the game menu. “The first match of the White Fox Cup is about to begin!”

Ling Suyu sat in front of the computer with his headphones on, stared at the game menu in front of him, and took a deep breath.

Although his contract with UH was about to end in less than a month, he would still take this competition seriously. 

Not only for his future e-sports career, but also because this was a part of his professionalism.

At the moment, both sides had picked their respective three bans, and it was UH’s turn to pick a hero. 

“Feather, who do you want?” His teammate’s voice sounded through the headset. 

“Cat Spirit.” He didn’t hesitate.

As soon as he said this, his coach scolded him: “Cat Spirit again! I won’t allow it! Choose the Guardian!”

Ling Suyu opened his mouth, but before he could speak, his teammate had already locked in Guardian Spirit. 

Ling Suyu accepted reality: “Okay, sure.”

Cat Spirit had high base damage, quick movement speed, and most importantly movement and live-saving skills. 

Although this type of hero was frail enough that even two basic attacks from a support could kill them, Ling Suyu was still more confident moving in with them.

Guardian Spirit was different. She was a marksman that dealt explosive damage, with the advantage that if she were able to unleash all her skills, she could wipe out the enemy marksman in one go. The disadvantage was that she had no movement skills, with low sustainability, and her damage was front-loaded.

Although both marksmen relied on their teammates, with Cat Spirit, Ling Suyu could continue to deal damage even behind the frontlines, and if the situation turned sour he could sacrifice his teammates and run. 

But with Guardian Spirit, he could only steel himself and hope to kill the enemy, otherwise he’d be the one dying. There was no chance to escape at all. 

Unless his support had the ability to protect him. 

【UH’s first hero is Guardian Spirit, let’s see what YJ chooses!】Just as AhMu said this, YJ immediately locked onto Abyssal Lord. 

【Oh my, YJ’s first choice is a support!】ZhiZhi exclaimed in surprise, but quickly reacted, 【Do they not want UH to take this support hero?】

【Abyssal Lord’s shield synergizes well with Guardian Spirit’s explosive damage, and would indeed be hard to deal with.】 AhMu clarified YJ’s strategy.

Ling Suyu pursed his lips when he saw what the other side chose, but didn’t say anything. 

In this end, the support UH chose was Luminous Saintess, and the marksman YJ chose was the Netherworld Shadow. 

Once everyone had chosen their respective heroes and handed them to the corresponding people, the countdown soon ended, and they entered the battlefield!

Ling Suyu moved his mouse, directing the Guardian Spirit on his screen towards the bottom lane. He glanced at the Luminous Saintess behind him, before putting his skill points into his second skill “Arrows of Light”.

“Support, buff my damage, we’re stealing level 2.”

UH’s support Wen Xu hesitated, but after hearing no reaction from the other team members, responded with an “Mm” and did as he said.

Soon, Ling Suyu and Wen Xu made their way into the bottom lane. At the same time, the other side’s marksman and support appeared within their line of sight, and the two sides began laning. 

【As the match begins, let’s first take a look at the bottom lane, as that’s what everyone’s curious about!】

As AhMu said this, the director turned the livestream perspective to the bottom lane, only to see Ling Suyu not being stingy with his skills as he cleared minions, with his support also using skills, leaving the last blow for Ling Suyu.

【Um? UH’s support’s first skill was a damage buff and not a heal, do they want to compete for level 2?】

AhMu, as a professional commentator, immediately recognized Ling Suyu’s strategy. 

【YJ’s Mingxi is being very aggressive today, continuously using skills to chip away at the opponent’s heroes. Nevertheless, Feather’s moves aren’t bad, he’s dodged them all…oh f*ck!】

ZhiZhi’s words had barely left her mouth before Ling Suyu and Wen Xu both dashed forward, causing her to swear on the spot.

In the next instance, a system announcement appeared clearly on the top of the screen. 

【System】Guardian Spirit, first blood!

Soon after Ling Suyu cleared the last minion and reached level 2, he immediately unlocked his first skill, Entangling Vines. While shouting “Follow me!”, he used his dash to directly put himself behind the enemy’s marksman Netherworld Shadow, swiftly tapping twice on his keyboard. 

On the screen, the fair elven archer reached out and unleashed a bunch of vines, accurately entangling Netherworld Shadow, controlling him. This was then swiftly followed by three arrows of light, immediately leaving Netherworld Shadow at half-health!

Wen Xu, as UH’s support, wasn’t slow in reacting, and in the moment Ling Suyu dashed out immediately followed. As Ling Suyu unleashed his two skills, his Luminous Saintess also used the damage skill Radiant Light, taking a chunk of the opponent’s health.

The other side’s support hurriedly opened their shield, and Netherworld Shadow also swiftly retreated underneath his tower. But it was already too late. Ling Suyu decisively chased him under the tower and, with two basic attacks, destroyed his shield and took the remainder of his health!

【System】Guardian Spirit, first blood!

【System】The enemy Netherworld Shadow has been slain!

Everything happened in the blink of an eye, without giving anyone the time to react.

Ling Suyu’s Guardian Spirit took a hit from the tower, losing more than half its blood, but still successfully exited the enemy tower and re-entered under his own, preserving his life and casting Recall to return to his base. 

Ah-Mu didn’t expect that within two minutes of the first match of the “White Fox Cup”, the battle would become this thrilling. His tone was equally excited: 【UH’s Feather grabbed level 2 before decisively dashing behind YJ’s Mingxi, disrupting his opponent’s ability to escape. With a combo of Entangling Vines and Arrow of Light, he instantly cleared more than two-thirds of his opponent’s health! Mingxi chose to dash and escape to the safety of his tower, but it was already too late! Feather chased him under the tower and claimed the kill! UH got first blood!】

【Oh my god! Was Feather this aggressive?】ZhiZhi was so shocked she forgot to control her expression. 【Although he got to level 2, instantly using Flash was incredibly risky! I thought that with a substitute, they would play more conservatively…..is he really UH’s substitute member?】

Forget the commentators, even the audience found it hard to believe. 

UH matches with YJ consisted of mostly losses, and due to complications with their original marksman, they had to use a substitute. Originally, everyone thought there wouldn’t be much suspense regarding the outcome of this match. They never expected that within two minutes of the match’s beginning the points would change, and the one changing it would be the substitute that no one put any faith into.

Ling Suyu was naturally unaware of the commentators’ and audience’s reactions, and could only hear his teammates’ voices.

The mid-laner spoke as he was recalling: “Bottom lane, steady yourself.”

He pretended he didn’t hear that.

Those who really understood him knew that he never started a fight he couldn’t control. 

Just now, he had calculated that the enemy’s marksman couldn’t escape from his position before dashing forwards. 

As the match continued, Ma Mingxi’s Netherworld Shadow revived and quickly returned to his lane, and Ling Suyu’s Guardian Spirit also finished healing and returned to the frontlines. 

In Fight’s team practice room, Fight’s mid-laner Dou Jiayan left his seat to get some water. As he passed by his captain’s seat, he saw the screen out of the corner of his eye and couldn’t help asking: “Are you watching the White Fox Cup too?”

Fight’s captain, professional support Tang Yu’an, removed his headset upon hearing these words. He turned around to stare Dou Jiayan in the eyes and raised an eyebrow: “Too?”

Dou Jiayan coughed lightly: “I definitely did *not* slack off, I’m just…researching the opponent! Yes, researching the opponent! As they say, know thyself and know thy enemy, and you can win a hundred battles!”

“If your hands were as agile as your tongue, would we still miss winning the championship?” Tang Yu’an said before turning his gaze back to his computer screen. 

Indeed, he was watching the White Fox Cup livestream, originally with the intention of observing how UH would be destroyed by YJ, but he hadn’t imagined…

UH-feather, Feather?

This substitute was quite interesting.

“So, captain, did you see anything?” Dou Jiayan asked curiously.

“No.” Tang Yu’an responded without glancing back, “Hasn’t the match just begun? I wouldn’t dare comment carelessly only to receive a slap in the face like Ma Mingxi.”

“Huh? What happened to Ma Mingxi?”

Tang Yu’an smiled: “You’ll know if you open the comments.”
