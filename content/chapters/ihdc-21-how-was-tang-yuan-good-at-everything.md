In the future, let me protect you.

—Referred to a support protecting their marksman.

However, it was unknown how Ling Suyu interpreted these words, but after hearing them, he started crying even harder.

After a while, he stopped crying and lay in front of his computer, feeling deeply regretful— aaaaaaaahhhhh! What had he done! As a new member of Fight that hadn’t even signed the contract yet, he hugged the captain while bawling his eyes out!

Too ashamed to show his face QAQ Tang Yu’an left the training room to pour Ling Suyu a cup of warm water, and once he returned, he saw Ling Suyu laying wilted on the computer desk with a defeated expression.

He looked like a blob of melting ice cream, and he almost laughed out loud.

It was a good thing he managed to hold back, as Ling Suyu slowly perked up from the table and took the warm water, murmuring a “thank you”, before holding the cup with both hands and taking a sip.

Throughout the whole process, his head was bowed as if he was too ashamed to show his face.

Tang Yu’an sat down by his side, holding his head in one hand and appraising him amusedly: “Why are you keeping your head down? Are your eyes puffy? Afraid I’ll dislike you?”

Ling Suyu lowered his head even more, looking like he wanted to find a hole to crawl into.

Tang Yu’an ultimately couldn’t hold back and let out a couple chuckles, before saying: “How did you reply to that Weibo post?”

“Which post? Oh…the one UH sent?” Ling Suyu put the cup down on the table, holding it with two hands and feeling the soft heat radiating out of it. He watched as steam continued to rise out of the cup, briefly spacing out. Only after a while did he finally react to Tang Yu’an question, and responded with only three words: “I didn’t respond.”

Tang Yu’an raised his eyebrows.

He’d thought that Ling Suyu, after seeing that post, wouldn’t reply with many words, probably just a simple “Ok, I accept”, or perhaps he’d reveal that he’d already joined a new team with a “See you in the Spring Seasonal”.

He didn’t think that he actually wouldn’t reply?

Was it that he didn’t want to accept UH’s apology?

“I don’t know how to reply…” Ling Suyu said in a small voice, “It’s not that I don’t want to accept UH’s apology, it’s just…”

“If apologies could cut it, what good are the police?” Tang Yu’an answered for him.

Ling Suyu let out a burst of laughter, finally raising his head to glance at Tang Yu’an, his gaze both slightly complaining and exasperated—Just as I’m feeling extremely conflicted, why are you butting in?

It was unknown whether he read the admonishment in Ling Suyu’s eyes, but Tang Yu’an immediately threw up both his arms in surrender: “Alright alright, I won’t interrupt you, go on, why don’t you want to reply?”

“I’ve seen a lot of people say that UH was manipulating me,” Ling Suyu continued to lower his head and respond, “Actually, I don’t think that’s the case. Regardless of what kind of person Jiang Hao is, he does indeed have skills, and compared to me, he suits UH better.

That’s the truth. So, even though I’m frustrated constantly being a substitute, I don’t think there’s anything wrong with UH’s arrangement. It’s the same thing with the contract. 5 years was written in black ink on white paper when I signed with UH. If I were to break the contract early, that would be a violation, and I wouldn’t be able to pay the fee.” He paused, as if afraid that Tang Yu’an might misunderstand, before raising his voice and adding, “I’m not trying to whitewash UH, I’m just saying what I think.”

“Hm, I understand,” Tang Yu’an replied faintly, signalling that he was still listening.

“If you want to pinpoint what UH did wrong, it’s sacrificing me for the sake of developing the club,” Ling Suyu continued, “I’ve suddenly come to a realization in these past couple of days.

When they said that I played badly, it wasn’t that I really played that horribly, but rather they needed me as a target to divert the focus of the criticism. Otherwise, if the one getting scolded isn’t me, it would be the other members of UH, and if this went on to affect UH’s matches later on, they’d enter a vicious cycle.”

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow, a bit surprised.

Though he wasn’t surprised at the fact that he’d realized these points.

Rather…”I’ve suddenly come to a realization in these past couple of days”, meaning that in the past, you didn’t notice this at all?

“With UH’s apology letter, instead of saying it was written for me, it’s more like it was written for their fans, and it’s fundamentally meant to salvage their reputation. So if I reply, who would my reply be for?”

Hearing this, Tang Yu’an generally understood what Ling Suyu was conflicted about, and he asked probingly: “Then if you were to respond in private, what would you say?”

Ling Suyu thought seriously for a moment: “It would probably be…Don’t kid me! You think I don’t know your intentions? You really think this apology is sincere? Since you’ve always treated me as expendable, why not continue!”

Tang Yu’an: “pfft—”

Ling Suyu: ?

“My bad.” Tang Yu’an covered his eyes with his hand.

Although he’d never received professional training, he wouldn't laugh in situations where he shouldn’t, unless he really couldn’t hold back.

This response had to be the most honest one in Ling Suyu’s heart.

How could this little feather be so cute!

Ling Suyu didn’t mind Tang Yu’an reaction, continuing: “If I responded like that on Weibo, things would probably get complicated, right? But if I just reply with ‘Ok, I accept’, I…”

“Mm, I get what you mean,” Tang Yu’an said while holding back a chuckle.

To turn this question into something so complicated, as expected of Feather.

If it were him, he wouldn’t even put that much thought into it, simply responding with a “Read” and ending it there.

Hm? Wait a minute, now that he thought about it…

“Since you’re not sure how to respond, why not let Fight do it for you,” Tang Yu’an suddenly said.

Hearing this, Ling Suyu raised his head and blinked, slightly confused, unsure of what he planned to do.

However, after Tang Yu’an spoke, he didn’t even wait for Ling Suyu’s response before picking up his phone, logging into Fight’s official account, and drafting a new post.

After he finished editing, he didn’t immediately post it, but instead showed it to Ling Suyu:

“What do you think?”

Ling Suyu cradled the phone with both hands and read the Weibo post Tang Yu’an wrote word for word, and after going over it twice in detail, let out a breath he’d been holding and nodded with no complaints.

Thus Tang Yu’an relaxedly hit “Send”.

Fight_eSports_Club:

Due to the severity of player Chen Jie @FightChenJie’s hand injury, he will be unable to participate in the Spring Seasonal beginning on January 10th. The Fight eSports Club has decided to sign with Feather @GloriousHeroesFeather, with the contract effective starting January 7th. At that time, Feather will compete as the marksman in Fight’s starting lineup.

Additionally, we would like to respond in Feather’s place to UH’s apology letter. Feather himself has read the contents of the letter and has expressed his understanding towards UH’s arrangements. As he personally does not often use Weibo, he has only just seen the message and has not yet found the time to respond, and hopes UH will excuse him.

Finally, thanks to Fight’s fans for continuing to support Fight, we will see you in the Spring Seasonals!

This Weibo post not only officially announced the signing of the contract between Feather and Fight, but also replied to the apology letter he was unsure of how to respond to in his place, getting two birds with one stone.

Most importantly, this reply perfectly evaded the question of whether or not Ling Suyu accepted UH’s apology, and additionally expressed his own thoughts—that he’d understood the UH club’s arrangements.

Yes, Ling Suyu understood UH’s arrangements; however, he didn’t accept them.

The nice thing about this reply was that it gave UH the opportunity to step back while also not making it seem as if Ling Suyu had been forced to yield. This was simply…too smart!

Ling Suyu raised his gaze and made eye contact with Tang Yu’an, his eyes that were still slightly red filled with surprise and admiration.

He was willing to bet that Fight’s official Weibo had always been managed by Tang Yu’an, and that he’d also written the previous repost.

How was Tang Yu’an good at everything!

Seeing the worship towards himself in Ling Suyu’s eyes, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but laugh:

“What? I thought you’d long discovered that my language grades were twice that of my math grades!”

Ling Suyu immediately cracked a smile, even laughing out the remaining tears in his eyes, instinctively raising a hand to rub at them.

He couldn’t have imagined that at the same time that he raised his hand, Tang Yu’an also reached over. He reached his face first, using his thumb to gently wipe away the tears in the corners of his eyes, before relaxing his tone and softly saying: “Stupid, I was lying to you, my math grades were also excellent.”

Ling Suyu froze and stared at him, feeling the slightly cool temperature of his fingers.

However, not only did he not feel any chills, but his body instead heated up, and he even momentarily lost focus.

It was a good thing that Tang Yu’an was simply casually wiping his face, and retracted his hand: “Alright, since we’ve even declared it on the official Weibo, might as well sign the contract now.”

“En.” Ling Suyu didn’t even hear what he said before instinctively nodding.

Even if Tang Yu’an sold him off right now, he probably wouldn’t even hesitate before nodding, and he only realized afterwards…huh? What?

Sign the contract?

Right now?

Before he could even react, Tang Yu’an had already sent a link to him through the messaging app: “You’ve brought your ID, right? Download an app called Scanner King, scan it, and upload it, both the front and back.”

“Okay, my ID is in my room, I’ll go get it!” Ling Suyu finally snapped back to his senses, and after saying this, left the training room with his phone. He ran up the stairs, steps as swift as a rabbit.

Tang Yu’an watched his departing back and couldn’t help but chuckle, before relaxedly laying back in his gaming chair and flipping through the chat logs.

Dou Jiayan: Why did Feather disappear?

Dou Jiayan: Did the captain also disappear?

Ye Xiran: Feather went to check Weibo, Captain Tang’s probably checking with him?

Dou Jiayan: Ah, I saw the Weibo captain sent! Why didn’t you inform the group chat before sending it?

Ye Xiran: They’re probably busy.

Dou Jiayan: Busy with what?

Ye Xiran: How would I know? What if it’s something that can’t be said in the group chat?

Tang Yu’an: ???

“Ye Xiran, you f…”

Tang Yu’an’s fist hardened.

Tang Yu’an: Ye Xiran, is your skin itching?

【Ye Xiran has recalled a message.】

Ye Xiran: Hm? What’s up?

Ye Xiran: Where’s Feather?

Tang Yu’an was just thinking of reprimanding him as a private message suddenly popped out in the corner of the messaging interface.

Feather: I’m done, please check it~ ?

Tang Yu’an froze, only realizing after a moment that Ling Suyu was referring to the contract.

He’d already…filled in the contract?

He probably didn’t even read the contents!

Tang Yu’an raised his hand to his eyes, letting out a helpless sigh, before similarly leaving the training room and heading upstairs.
