Ling Suyu hadn’t shut the door to his room. Tang Yu’an quietly walked to the entrance and looked inside, only to see the innocent penguin cupping his phone with both hands. He was sitting on his bed, intently waiting for a response.

The temperature inside the club was much higher than outside, so Ling Suyu had taken off his white puffer jacket soon after they’d entered, leaving only a black thermal long-sleeve.

The form-fitting undergarment emphasized his slim body, making him appear akin to a lithe black cat.

In addition to his tight black leggings…

Tang Yu’an instinctively glanced downwards, before quickly realizing something, retracting his gaze and knocking twice on the door.

Hearing the sound of knocking, Ling Suyu was startled, reflexively turning his head towards the door. He only then realized that Tang Yu’an had come upstairs at some point, instantly stuttering out of nervousness: “Huh? Um…that, contract…”

“I came up to talk about the contract,” Tang Yu’an looked at him helplessly, “Although I’m happy that you signed it so decisively, but to be honest, you didn’t check the contents at all, did you?”

“I checked it…a bit,” Ling Suyu rebutted quietly, “I checked the duration, 3 years, right?”

“What about the salary?”

“Um…”

Tang Yu’an facepalmed.

It’s a miracle that he managed to survive in UH until now!

No, if he’d been even a bit more clever, he wouldn’t have been tricked by UH to this extent!

It’s a good thing that he was the one that picked him up. If it were someone else…Tang Yu’an didn’t even want to imagine it.

“You know what, I’ll look over the contract with you. Let me know if you find any problems, and we can fix it. Once everything’s good you can sign it and I’ll review it, ok?”

“Ok.” Obedient Ling Suyu.jpg Tang Yu’an watched his cooperative demeanour and couldn’t help but chuckle.

When signing those other guys in his club, each one had more conditions than the next. Ye Xiran even requested that he be allowed to work from home, and he nearly kicked him out the window on the spot.

It was a rare occasion that he met anyone as “obedient“ as Ling Suyu, and it conversely made him feel more worried.

As a result, Tang Yu’an patiently helped Ling Suyu comb through the contract from start to finish, explaining all the clauses he had questions about. After confirming that there were no issues, he watched as he uploaded his ID and signed his name.

Afterwards, Tang Yu’an re-checked everything on his end, filling in the date and stamping the company’s seal: “All done, I’ve sent you the link, go download it yourself. If you want to have it printed out, there’s a printer on the first floor.”

“Okay!” Ling Suyu replied happily, unable to wait before clicking the link, downloading the contract as a pdf on his phone. He completely didn’t realize anything was wrong. For instance, why did Tang Yu’an, as Fight’s captain, have Fight’s official seal?

He opened the successfully-downloaded electronic contract and couldn’t help but sigh:

“Signing contracts is so convenient now. When I signed with UH, we were still using paper contracts.”

“Actually, we can sign a physical one too, aren’t I just trying to show off?” Tang Yu’an blurted out, “In comparison, doesn’t it make us at Fight seem more high-end?”

Ling Suyu let out a “pfft—” in laughter.

This guy always knew how to make him laugh out of the blue.

In the evening, the two once again went out for a meal.

Due to it being the second time they’d eaten together alone, Ling Suyu was a lot more open, and was even willing to participate in ordering.

Although the only thing he decided on was the drinks orz Tang Yu’an was rather surprised. He didn’t realize he was this bad at decision-making and asked curiously: “Do you usually follow others when you go out?”

“Pretty much?” Ling Suyu thought back to the past 20 years of his life. His experiences that could count as “going out with others” seemed to have stopped around middle school?

At the time, it was true that he wasn’t very opinionated, going along with whatever his friends wanted.

Actually, rather than saying he wasn’t opinionated, it would be more accurate to say that he didn’t have much interest in “going out” in the first place, so he was alright with wherever he went.

Tang Yu’an, upon hearing his reply, only got more curious: “UH has always rendered your 0 deaths record into being afraid of death. Have you ever thought of changing that?”

“Huh?” Ling Suyu froze, as if he’d never thought that Tang Yu’an would ask a question like that.

Tang Yu’an knew what he was worried about and added: “I’m not planning to make you change. You’re fine with your current style, I’m just a bit curious.”

A bit curious about—for someone so unused to decision-making, why you are so adamant about your gaming style.

He could have never imagined that Ling Suyu’s reply would be: “My style? Do I have one? I haven’t really thought that much about it, I just want to win. And in order to win, I can’t die.”

—In order to win, I can’t die.

Is it strange to not want to die when playing a game?

It wasn’t strange at all!

In fact, pretty much everyone thought the same way.

However, how many could actually do it?

Tang Yu’an suddenly understood.

He himself had also been brainwashed by UH. What personal style, Ling Suyu purely had good game sense. He had a solid grasp on when to retreat, and additionally predicted the outcome of every team battle, making what he thought was the best possible decision. That was all.

He wasn’t stubborn.

He was a genius!

Tang Yu’an let out a few low chuckles, before giving Ling Suyu a thumbs-up under his confused gaze: “Yeah, you’re right. I love marksmen like you who don’t feed; it makes me feel reassured as a support.”

Just as he said to Mo Xiaoyun, in comparison to a marksman who is afraid of death, he was more worried about one who wasn’t.

A support’s greatest duty was protecting their marksman, but they weren’t almighty. They couldn’t be protecting the marksman at all times.

A marksman who was afraid of death could escape on their own, lessening the pressure on the support.

On the other hand, a marksman who was unafraid of death wouldn’t escape even when they were dying, and would ultimately drag the support down with them.

Tang Yu’an wasn’t afraid of being killed. Every time he initiated a team fight, he was already prepared to die.

But he liked marksmen who were afraid of being killed.

Put more specifically, he liked marksmen who knew when to run.

Although Ling Suyu’s predictions weren’t always correct, what did it matter?

Giving Ling Suyu a sufficient feeling of safety and guiding him towards actions that would benefit the team when his predictions were incorrect; wasn’t that what he should do as not only a support but also a commander?

“I think we can win during this Spring Seasonal.”

Just like that, he set up a flag, although Tang Yu’an didn’t believe it would fall over.

By contrast, Ling Suyu was the one who got worried: “Don’t do that, you’re scaring me.”

“That’s true, I shouldn't be pressuring you,” Tang Yu’an reflected, “Although, ever since I was a kid I’ve believed in one truth, and it’s that, when someone truly desires something, they’ll definitely obtain it.”

“When someone truly desires something, they’ll definitely obtain it?” Ling Suyu repeated the words, memorizing them.

He was an atheist who didn’t believe in mysticism, but for some reason unbeknownst to himself, when Tang Yu’an said those words, he instinctively believed them.

Even if this person always came up with some nonsense to get him to laugh, making him appear rather unreliable, that feeling of being fully protected in the game still remained on Ling Suyu’s fingertips.

No matter how tall Tang Yu’an was in real life, in-game he was as tall as 2.8 meters!

At that, Ling Suyu suddenly felt a bit curious: “Captain, how tall are you?”

Hearing the title “captain,” Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow. Then he reacted to the question and let out an expression of amusement: “1.86 meters, why do you ask? Should I also give you my zodiac and blood type?”

“Umm…no need, I’m just asking casually.”

Though Ling Suyu said that, his face fully conveyed an expression of wanting-to-know.

Tang Yu’an propped his head up with one hand, looking over him with great interest No way…right?

Those guys at Fight, after finding out about his sexuality, would always unintentionally tease him, asking if he cared about the appearances of various eSports players, or immediately running over and poking him whenever they saw a particularly handsome player: “Hey, captain, do you think that player looks handsome? Is he your type?”

There was also that bastard Ye Xiran, who was clearly implying something with that message he recalled.

However, Ling Suyu had just joined Fight and didn’t know his secret, so…

Thinking of the upcoming Spring Seasonals, Tang Yu’an ultimately didn’t say anything.

It wouldn’t be good if he accidentally misunderstood and affected Ling Suyu’s performance during the competition.

*

The two duo-queued for a few more rounds after returning from dinner.

It was unknown whether they had become tired after playing in the afternoon, but they didn’t win all the rounds they played that night, though they still won more than they lost.

Also, Ling Suyu’s battle records remained consistent, unchangingly 0 deaths, unchangingly MVP.

After the last round, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but reflect: “I understand now why UH didn’t like you. Before you arrived, I could still get a few MVPs, but now they’re all yours.”

Ling Suyu felt panicked.

Seeing his reaction in his peripheral vision, Tang Yu’an let out a laugh: “I’m complimenting you. If I, as a support, got MVP, what does that mean? It means that us being able to win is all thanks to the opponents being bad!”

Ling Suyu let out a breath in relief: “Again?”

Tang Yu’an glanced at the time: “It’s almost past twelve. Let’s go to bed, it’s not good to stay up too late, yeah?”

“Okay!”

And so, Ling Suyu took a shower and went to bed.

Just after he finished showering, blew his hair dry, and got into bed, he didn’t know whether it was because he hadn’t felt this good playing in a long time, but he suddenly felt extremely energetic, as if he could go for another 500 rounds.

So, before he went to sleep, he decided to scroll through Weibo on his phone.

The post that Tang Yu’an had sent through Fight’s official account had already amassed 3000 likes, and the comments were full of support.

【Congratulations Feather! Good luck in the Spring Seasonals!】

【Go Fight! Go Feather!】

【Grab a gun and follow me (a famous quote from the movie 让子弹飞, which later turned into a meme), with Feather, we can win the championship! 】

Seeing this, Ling Suyu couldn’t stop the corners of his lips from rising.

However, he thought of his own loss of composure after seeing UH’s apology letter…

Aaaahhhhh! He really died a social death!

It was a good thing that Fight’s break started early and Tang Yu’an was the only one in the club.

It was a good thing that Tang Yu’an was gentle, kindly accepting everything.

When he angrily insulted UH, when he broke down and cried…Tang Yu’an had witnessed all of it.

Yet he was still willing to smile at him gently.

Tang Yu’an was really good.

Ling Suyu felt his eyelids grow heavier.

Right before he fully lost consciousness and drifted off into dreamworld, one final thought surfaced.

He liked him.

Be it his style of support, or him as a person.

He liked all of it…
