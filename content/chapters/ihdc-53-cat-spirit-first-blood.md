Fight’s loss in the second game against igame undoubtedly came as a relief to UH and their fans.

But thanks to their comeback victory in the first game, even though Fight lost the second game, there wasn’t much online criticism directed at Fight or Ling Suyu, as people were afraid of being proven wrong again.

So, for a moment, the cheers for Fight took the upper hand:

【Go Fight! Go Feather!】

【Go for it, Fight! The championship is within reach!】

【Keep soaring, Feather! May your glory endure forever, and may honor be with you!】

Naturally, there were plenty of cheers for igame as well. Fans from both sides firmly supported their own teams without any online feuds, and the atmosphere was harmonious.

However, unlike the excited fans, and unlike UH, whose mood was mixed, the other quarterfinalists watching the match didn’t have much to say about the current 1-1 tie.

From their perspectives, Fight and igame were two teams of very similar strength.

In last season’s Summer Seasonals, one finished fourth and the other fifth.

In last season’s overall annual rankings, it was the same—one fourth and one fifth.

Furthermore, their last game was a close matchup. Although Fight won, it was a narrow victory. Had things gone slightly differently, it wouldn’t have been them advancing, but igame.

So, in their view, a full three matches between Fight and igame was normal. If Fight were to actually defeat igame 2-0, then Ling Suyu would undoubtedly be a true gem who could single-handedly transform an entire team, and would require close attention and vigilance.

However, among these teams, two had a different perspective.

Namely, SMG and YJ—teams that had already faced off against Fight.

S.MG: “How much longer does Fight plan to hide their true strength?”

“Feather, switch to Cat Spirit already! We can’t be the only ones getting slaughtered by Cat Spirit! Let igame get a taste of the ‘joy’ of being pinned to the ground and pressed into the dirt by Cat Spirit!”

“I’ll bet that if Feather had used Cat Spirit in that last game, igame would’ve lost even worse than we did!”

“It wouldn’t have been quite that bad… but Cat Spirit deals more damage than Desert Hunter early on and levels up faster. If Feather had used Cat Spirit in the last game, they might not have lost that final team fight.”

—After watching the second match between Fight and igame, SMG was certain: Desert Hunter was definitely not Ling Suyu’s first choice; he must have held back Cat Spirit to hide his true strength.

It was simple: setting everything else aside, if they’d just swapped Desert Hunter for Cat Spirit, Fight wouldn’t have lost that quickly.

Though both Cat Spirit and Desert Hunter were late-game heroes, one needed to take a closer look at their skills:

Cat Spirit’s first skill, “Spirit Mark,” marked enemy heroes to increase damage. Her passive, “Shadow Strike,” dealt a fixed amount of bonus damage upon landing three basic attacks on a target marked by “Spirit Mark.”

—This ensured a certain level of basic attack damage even before he built up his gold.

Now look at Desert Hunter: his first skill, “Hunter’s Trap,” was a slowing ability with little damage; his second skill, “Explosive Shell,” functioned more as a movement ability than a damage-dealing one; and his third skill, “Hound’s Charge,” was purely a movement ability.

So, while both were sustained-damage marksmen that rely on stacking attack speed, Cat Spirit gained the ability to deal damage early on. Nevertheless, her skills were designed to support auto-attacks, and she relied entirely on auto-attacks for damage. Thus, for now, she couldn’t compete with burst-damage marksmen who relied primarily on skills.

As for Desert Hunter, he was useless until he stacked up his attack speed and unlocked his ultimate.

When playing ranked matches, players might just frown a bit if they saw a teammate picking Cat Spirit as their marksman, but if they saw Desert Hunter, they would definitely vent their frustration.

“You trolling?”

“We’re done for, this game’s lost.”

“Starting the game with one less teammate, feels bad man.”

That was why the commentators got so excited when they saw Desert Hunter appear in professional matches.

It really was an incredible sight.

In pro play, you might not have to pick meta heroes, but what was the deal with picking a bottom-tier one?

What? A “bottom-tier” hero carried the team to victory?

Awesome! Best in Glorious!

Even casual players knew that among bottom-tier heroes, Cat Spirit was still a bit stronger than Desert Hunter, so how could SMG not know that?

But since the start of this season, Ling Suyu had been playing either Desert Hunter or Candy Witch, which has left them a bit unsure.

But now that Fight lost to igame while using Desert Hunter, with the main reason being the hero itself, there was no way they’d stick with Desert Hunter in the next game.

SMG was buzzing—so, was Cat Spirit about to make an appearance? She’s gotta be coming in, right!

Meanwhile, YJ was a bit shocked and frustrated: “Damn! Fight lost to igame with Desert Hunter?”

—YJ, a team that focused on the early game, couldn’t even beat Desert Hunter, yet igame actually managed to win?!

“igame’s been so strong this season! Especially their captain—he’s been in incredible form! I watched their match against OsO. OsO banned every jungler they could, but igame still carried the game with their jungler!”

“Winne and Fight alone are enough to give us a hard time—I didn’t expect igame to come along too. Are they trying to kill us this season? Are we even going to get third place?”

“It’s not that bad, it’s not that bad. Just train hard and play seriously—that’s all it takes!”

“So, what’s Fight planning to pick for the next game?”

“If Feather specializes in sustained-damage marksmen, he’ll probably go with Cat Spirit or Dark Raven?”

Those were the only two sustained-damage marksmen stronger than Desert Hunter.

Soon, the third game between Fight and igame, and also their final match of the group stages, began.

With the score tied at 1-1, neither side could afford to relax; both were determined to secure the victory in this final match.

In the previous game, igame had won. Since it was a one-sided match, it looked relatively easy, but only they knew just how hard they’d had to work to limit Fight’s gold.

“Desert Hunter is even harder to counter than I thought,” said the captain, the jungler. “Good thing we won early on; otherwise, if it had dragged on, we would’ve lost for sure.”

“But that should be the only movement-based marksman pick they have out of all of them, right?” a teammate tried to reassure him. “Our performance in the last game should have intimidated Fight a bit, so it’s unlikely they’ll pick Desert Hunter again.”

“I don’t want them to pick Desert Hunter again, but if they bring out an even stronger marksman… ”

“Hey, no pre-game intimidation!”

“But Fight is definitely strong this season. We might have to consider playing our trump card early in this game.”

Since igame won the previous game, they had the first ban in this match, while Fight had the first pick.

The bans selected by both teams were almost identical to the first game, with only igame’s final ban differing.

igame’s three bans were Twin Demonblades, Tempest Blade, and Phoenix Soul;

Fight’s three bans were Blade Soul, Dark Angel, and Mandara Vines;

igame’s decision to ban Phoenix Soul caught both Fight and the commentators by surprise. After all, in the previous two games, when Phoenix Soul wasn’t on the ban list, Fight hadn’t picked that hero at all.

“They suddenly banned my Phoenix Soul—what’s the enemy mid laner planning to pick?” Dou Jiayan asked warily.

Tang Yu’an shrugged nonchalantly: “Who knows? They’re picking second, so they’ll probably save their mid laner for last. If you pick Harpist, they’ll pick Curse Master; if you pick Wind Guardian, they might just pick Harpist.”

“Damn it!” Dou Jiayan couldn’t help but swear again. Playing against igame made his emotions run higher than usual, but he quickly came up with a solution. “In that case, I’ll take River Deity. I’ll help Feather build an advantage as fast as possible—I’m not worrying about mid lane!”

Mo Xiaoyun: !

Yu He: !

“Hey, calm down!” Mo Xiaoyun got a headache just thinking about having to manage two lanes at once.

“Calm down.” Yu He liked solo carrying, but only when he chose to do it—he hated being forced to clear lanes. “If you dare pick River Deity, don’t count on me ganking mid.”

However, after thinking it over, Tang Yu’an decided it wasn’t a bad idea: “Let’s snag Skeletal Empress first and see what they pick. If they really leave the mid laner for last, then Yanzi, you can go with River Deity.”

“Awesome!” Dou Jiayan cheered.

Mo Xiaoyun and Yu He were seething, but since Tang Yu’an had spoken up, they didn’t say anything else.

“Fight took Skeletal Empress as their first pick!” Ah-Mu commented. “Fight’s top laner is definitely better at playing Skeletal Empress than Black-Armoured Scythe. Having igame snatch Skeletal Empress from him two games in a row must be pretty rough for him, right?”

【But with that, igame will snag Abyssal Lord as their support champion.】

No sooner had ZhiZhi finished speaking than igame instantly locked in Guardian Spirit and Abyssal Lord.

“As expected,” ZhiZhi said, not at all surprised. “Guardian Spirit lacks mobility; while it has high burst damage, its self-preservation abilities are limited. It’s definitely better to use a support champion with strong team-protection capabilities.”

Fight’s wasn’t surprised by this at all.

“We don’t need Abyssal Lord this game, so we weren’t planning on taking him at all—igame always picks up the heroes we don’t want.” After throwing out that cheeky remark, Tang Yu’an continued, “Come on, Feather, let’s show them our bot lane lineup—”

“Mm-hmm—” Ling Suyu replied, happily selecting his champion—Cat Spirit!

He’d been assigned to the second floor for this game, and it was now his turn to pick.

Even though his teammates would help with the selection and the champion would eventually be passed to him, the feeling of picking it himself was still different.

So, under the watchful eyes of everyone in the venue and all the viewers watching the livestream, Fight selected their “brand-new” bot lane lineup for this season—Cat Spirit and Spider Queen!

“Fight picked Cat Spirit and Spider Queen?!” Ah-Mu was taken aback; this bottom lane pairing was clearly unexpected. “Is this just a bluff to throw the enemy off, or…”

Before he could finish his sentence, Fight had already locked in both heroes.

“Locked! Fight’s bottom laners for this game are definitely Cat Spirit and Spider Queen!” ZhiZhi exclaimed with delight. “It’s been ages since we’ve seen this bot lane duo! The last time I saw it was…well, the last time!”

“Ha, what a statement you just stated!” Ah-Mu couldn’t help but quip, but he also wore a look of surprise. “This game’s going to be interesting! I was just wondering if Fight would pick Candy Witch, but I never expected them to go with Cat Spirit! Cat Spirit and Spider Queen are both rarely played these days—Cat Spirit has low early-game damage, and Spider Queen’s ability to protect allies is weak. They’re a duo that’s clearly designed for the late game, but they’re actually better suited for games where you’re already ahead.”

ZhiZhi: “That’s right. Spider Queen is an offense-focused support hero. Her ultimate doesn’t grant a shield or health, it boosts attack speed and movement speed. But don’t forget she has a passive that increases nearby allies’ basic attack damage. With her on the team, Cat Spirit’s early-game damage is guaranteed!”

Ah-Mu: “After being crowned the ‘Number One Desert Hunter’ in Glorious, is Feather about to claim the title of ‘Number One Cat Spirit’ in Glorious as well?”

“Hahaha, the match hasn’t even started yet, so we won’t know for sure!” ZhiZhi replied, “But I’m really looking forward to this game!”

igame clearly wasn’t ready for Fight’s picks of Spirit Cat and Spider Queen.

As a result, they took an exceptionally long time to select their third and fourth heroes, finalizing their picks almost exactly one second before the countdown ended.

They selected Double Phantom for the jungle and White Cocoon for the top lane.

Originally, they had planned to pick Child of Frost, just like in the previous game, and then use Black-Armoured Scythe to lane against Skeletal Empress.

But upon seeing Cat Spirit and Spider Queen, the entire team fell silent, followed by utter disbelief!

“Cat Spirit and Spider Queen?! A common lineup from two seasons ago, and I’m actually seeing it this season? Am I dreaming?!”

“Though it’s not entirely surprising? If someone’s good at Desert Hunter, maybe they can pull off Cat Spirit?”

Although Cat Spirit was stronger than Desert Hunter this season, Desert Hunter was actually much harder to play, a hero that was truly both difficult and underwhelming.

“I’m not surprised that Feather is using Cat Spirit, but…they’re using this lineup in this game? Is this a practice run or what?”

Did Fight decide to switch to Cat Spirit on the fly after realizing Desert Hunter was too hard to get going, or did they pull out their trump card, intending to win this match for real?

The answer to this question was crucial—it directly affected whether igame should also play their trump card.

Although they had originally planned to use it anyway.

Rather than the risk of revealing their trump card early and giving other teams more time to study it, they felt much more strongly about not losing to Fight.

In the end, igame’s coach made the call: “The captain will take Double Phantom. Fight’s jungler only plays assassins, so we’ll shut him down. Top lane should pick a champion with plenty of mobility but not too squishy—let’s go with White Cocoon. If they pick Cat Spirit, our tactics from the last game won’t work; we have to target Cat Spirit! In team fights, take him down first!”

If YJ were to hear this exchange from the igame coach, they’d probably find it quite poignant.

They’d used a similar tactic before, focusing entirely on Feather and mobilizing the entire team to take him down, only to end up being completely outmaneuvered by him.

They weren’t sure how Fight treated Feather in real life, but in-game, he was definitely the team’s darling. Targeting him was like targeting the entirety of Fight—it was just too difficult.

However, in the practice matches against YJ, Ling Suyu had only played Desert Hunter and never used Cat Spirit, so no one knew what would happen if he were targeted by the entire enemy team while playing Cat Spirit.

Seeing the champions igame had picked, Fight knew they were targeting Feather, so they decided to protect their own little Yumao.

“Yanzi, take Deepsea Faerie. ” Tang Yu’an decided on Dou Jiayan’s behalf, “If you can’t secure an advantage in the early laning phase, help out more. In the late game, stick with Yumao and protect him.”

“OK—” Dou Jiayan had no objections to this decision. “Don’t worry Yumao! I’ve got a real knack for playing support!”

“Mm.” Ling Suyu replied very reluctantly.

He still preferred Tang Yu’an as his support!

“What do you want to play, Hezi?” Tang Yu’an asked.

Yu He glanced at his usual heroes—most of which were either banned or already picked by igame—and shrugged, “Whatever. I’ll just be carried.”

Tang Yu’an smiled and made the decision for him: “Venomous Scorpion.”

And just like that, Fight’s lineup was set:

Top Lane: Skeletal Empress;

Mid Lane: Deepsea Faerie;

Marksman: Cat Spirit;

Support: Spider Queen;

Jungle: Venomous Scorpion.

And igame’s final slot: Curse Master.

igame’s lineup was also set:

Top Lane: White Cocoon;

Mid: Curse Master;

Marksman: Guardian Spirit;

Support: Abyssal Lord;

Jungle: Double Phantom.

“It’s still Curse Master!” Ah-Mu was taken aback. “When igame used their final ban slot on Phoenix Soul, I thought they were going to switch their mid laner, but it turned out to be Curse Master after all!”

“Maybe they originally planned to switch, but changed their minds after seeing Fight’s lineup?” ZhiZhi figured it out.

Indeed, igame’s so-called “trump card” was actually that, in addition to the Curse Master, their mid laner had practiced another champion that was dominant in this patch, one powerful enough to carry the entire team single-handedly.

Apart from heroes like Phoenix Soul and Harp Demon, who possessed both mobility and high damage, this meta-dominant champion fears one hero above all others: Deepsea Faerie.

If Phoenix Soul and Harp Demon were on equal footing against Deepsea Faerie, then this meta-dominant champion could very well be outplayed by her.

So, upon seeing Fight’s picks, igame had no choice but to fall back on Curse Master.

“They didn’t guess what we were going to pick, did they?”

“No, it was probably just a coincidence.”

“That’s true. Deepsea Faerie has a pretty high pick rate this season. Fight probably figured out we were trying to counter Feather, so they picked Deepsea Faerie to protect him.”

“Fight really spoils Feather,” igame’s captain and jungler couldn’t help but remark.

In any case, both teams’ lineups were now set.

SMG’s starters craned their necks, eagerly awaiting the start of the match.

As for the other teams watching the match, the general consensus was: “The early-game advantage isn’t too different between the two sides in this game. Although Fight’s bot lane is still a bit weak, it’s still better than Desert Hunter.”

“It’s been a while since we’ve seen the Cat Spirit and Spider Queen duo in the bot lane. I wonder how well they’ll hold up against the meta’s dominant compositions.”

—Now that’s what we call serious and professional.

As for SMG, this is what their commentary looked like while watching the game:

“Guardian Spirit, step out a little more! Just a little more! Hey—that’s it! Love from Cat Spirit is on its way!”

“What level was I when Cat Spirit took me out last time? Level 6? Go, Feather! You’ve gotta get a kill using Cat Spirit before level 6!”

“Wow, we’re so mean. If igame actually loses this game, are we going to be held responsible?”

“Mysterious energy from SMG?”

SMG’s mindset at this moment was the exact opposite of UH’s.

If UH wanted Fight to lose as badly as possible, then SMG wanted Fight to win as decisively as possible.

UH didn’t want Ling Suyu to keep showing off. The more he shone, the more it would make UH, who had forced him out, look either stupid or evil.

SMG, on the other hand, wanted Fight’s skill to be recognized. That way, their crushing defeat at Fight’s hands would have a reasonable explanation.

Although they would inevitably face each other again after advancing to the quarterfinals, until then, SMG wouldn’t let their crushing loss to Fight affect their mindset when facing any other team.

Come on! Admit it! Fight is strong!

Whether it was the “mysterious energy from SMG” truly coming into play or igame underestimating their opponent, igame’s bottom lane duo, relying on their higher early-game damage and the protection of the Abyssal Lord, attempted to push Fight’s bot lane back under their tower, just like in the previous game.

On Fight’s side, Ling Suyu quietly hid under the tower, farming minions and avoiding confrontation—but the claws he hid behind his back were already sharpened.

Soon, both sides’ bottom lane champions reached Level 3, unlocking all their abilities except their ultimates.

“Should we take them down?” Tang Yu’an asked for his opinion.

“Mm-hmm,” Ling Suyu replied confidently.

“Alright, then—let’s do this!” Tang Yu’an said, immediately sending his Spider Queen charging forward to initiate the team fight!

Suddenly, on the livestream, Spider Queen and Cat Spirit, who had seemingly been miserably cowering under the tower farming minions since the start of the game, shifted gears and both charged toward Guardian Spirit and Abyssal Lord.

igame’s Guardian Spirit and Abyssal Lord hadn’t yet realized the danger. Seeing the two charging straight at them, they didn’t even try to dodge—instead, they grew excited, thinking their chance had finally come.

Guardian Spirit cast “Entangling Vines” at Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit!

Without even batting an eye, Ling Suyu dodged it with “Shadow Steps,” slammed right into Guardian Spirit’s face, marked him with “Spirit Mark,” and started firing away—biu biu biu!

Green shadow blades struck Guardian Spirit one after another. Guardian Spirit hurriedly cast “Spiritual Shield,” then fired three light arrows in return!

His Spirit Shield shattered, and the counterattack hit Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen, dealing just a sliver of damage.

As for his Arrows of Light, Ling Suyu, who was already prepared, easily dodged them with a simple flick.

Auto-attacks are auto-targeted, but skills aren’t.

So, you can dodge skills by moving, but not auto-attacks.

While Guardian Spirit was firing its Arrows of Light, Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit landed three auto-attacks in a row, triggering its passive ability—Guardian Spirit’s health bar suddenly dropped significantly!

At the start of the clash, Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit wasn’t at full health, while Guardian Spirit was.

After both sides used up their skills, Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit’s health clearly exceeded that of Guardian Spirit!

Guardian Spirit’s early-game advantage lay in its skill damage. After missing two skills in a row and realizing he couldn’t outdamage the Cat Spirit with basic attacks, he attempted to use Flash to flee back to his own tower.

However, just as it was thinking this, Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen wrapped it in a “Spidersilk Trap,” followed immediately by a “Web of Constraint” to slow its movement speed.

Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit moved toward the enemy bottom lane’s first tower while firing off a flurry of shots: biu biu biu!

Green shadow blades rained down one after another. While the damage from each hit wasn’t particularly high, they frequently triggered the passives of both Cat Spirit and Spider Queen, suddenly dealing a massive chunk of damage to Guardian Spirit.

Abyssal Lord had already used up all his shields and was watching helplessly as his marksman’s health dwindled, pacing frantically.

His only skill for poking, “Touch of the Abyss,” was blocked by Tang Yu’an, leaving him no choice but to chase Cat Spirit and auto-attack. But as a mage-support, how much damage could his auto-attacks possibly do?

If the Cat Spirit’s early-game auto-attacks were like a light massage, then his were truly harmless.

Not to mention, while he was auto-attacking Cat Spirit, Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen was also attacking Guardian Spirit.

After breaking free from the crowd control, Guardian Spirit had less than one fifth of its max health remaining. Realizing that continuing to flee toward the tower as originally planned would mean certain death, it decisively changed its escape route and used Flash to dash down the river!

Ling Suyu decisively used Flash to catch up, landed a basic attack, and then, without even looking back, turned and walked away.

That final basic attack directly triggered his passive, dealing “Shadow Strike” damage and finishing off Guardian Spirit’s last sliver of health.

Had he not triggered that passive, Guardian Spirit would still have been clinging to life.

But he clearly knew the passive would trigger, which is why he walked away with such decisive ease.

In fact, even before the shadow blade Cat Spirit had thrown hit Guardian Spirit, he had already turned away, letting Guardian Spirit fall backward behind him.

【System】Cat Spirit, first blood!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!
