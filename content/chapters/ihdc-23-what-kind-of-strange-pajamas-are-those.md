After Ling Suyu fell asleep, he dreamt.

Within his dream, the Spring Seasonals had begun, and it was his first time competing for Fight as their starting marksman. However, he couldn’t keep up with his teammates’ rhythm at all, continuously missing the team fights, and could only watch helplessly as his team was wiped out.

Ultimately, Fight ended up second-to-last in the top 8.

Strange, blurry figures pointed their fingers at him, doubting his strength.

Tang Yu’an stood in front of him, protecting him, nearly getting into a fight with his long-time teammates for his sake.

“Coward!”

“You shouldn’t have let him play as a starter!”

“He’s cursed! So long as he’s here, we can’t win!”

Ling Suyu was shocked awake.

At that moment, the angry roars of the unknown teammates in his dream still echoed in his head: “Don’t let him compete!”

An unfamiliar ceiling filled his vision, and Ling Suyu stared frozen at it for a while before remembering that this was Fight headquarter’s player dormitory.

And the Spring Seasonals had yet to begin.

Ling Suyu breathed in deeply, glancing at the curtain-covered window.

The sky had yet to brighten outside, but he no longer felt sleepy.

After laying in bed for a while longer, he decided to get up and use the washroom.

He threw on his fluffy white rabbit pajamas and lightly opened the door, blindly heading downstairs in the dark.

Although there were washrooms on the second floor, he was afraid that the flushing sounds might wake Tang Yu’an, and thus decided to head to the first-floor washrooms.

However, what he didn’t know was that Tang Yu’an was actually on the first floor.

The club had gained a new member, a marksman. A good-looking, slender, and cute marksman.

They were the only two in the building.

Tang Yu’an tried to rationally control his impulses, but his body was honest.

Desire was one thing, but more importantly…he couldn't sleep!

It was unknown whether he was still running on the high of the game or if Ling Suyu’s charm was just that strong, but he felt strangely energized while laying in bed. He was still wide awake after counting more than 300 sheep, so he decided to scroll through Weibo for a bit, before also scrolling through a couple forums, looking through Ling Suyu’s previous competition recordings.

And then…he felt even less sleepy!

Looking at the time, it was 3 in the morning. He didn’t know whether time was passing too quickly or too slowly.

He felt a bit thirsty, so Tang Yu’an got up and went downstairs to get water.

Just as he finished pouring himself a cup and sat down on the sofain the first-floor lobby, he saw a little rabbit sneaking down the stairs.

Tang Yu’an’s movements froze, and he stared blankly as Ling Suyu, with two fluffy bunny ears on his head, entered the washroom, almost wondering if he was hallucinating.

That lasted until he heard the sound of water flushing, and the rabbit walked out of the washroom.

Tang Yu’an endured for a moment, ultimately giving up and opening his mouth to call:

“Feather?”

Ling Suyu was startled, his entire body shrinking towards the wall, before recognizing Tang Yu’an’s voice and letting out a sigh of relief. Looking at Tang Yu’an sitting in the darkness, he let out a confused expression: “Why aren’t you asleep?”

“Couldn’t fall asleep, you?” Tang Yu’an asked, sitting on the sofa.

Ling Suyu instinctually walked to the sofa next to him and sat down, saying honestly: “I had a bad dream and was shocked awake.”

He wasn’t wearing any pants, only the fluffy rabbit pajamas, exposing a pair of pale and slender legs.

Tang Yu’an looked at him helplessly for a moment, before picking up the blanket on the sofa and laying it over his legs: “What kind of bad dream did you have?”

Ling Suyu’s pajamas had a hood attached to them, and while his legs were exposed, the hood was properly on.

On top of the fuzzy hood was a pair of rabbit ears. They weren’t super long, with one standing upright and the other folded, appearing a bit…cute, making him want to ruffle them.

Ling Suyu didn’t notice that Tang Yu’an’s line of sight had fallen on his rabbit ears, briefly describing the nightmare he just had.

Actually, he didn’t recall much of the details. The only thing he remembered was that he caused Fight to lose in the competitions, before facing doubts from his teammates.

In addition to the words they shouted—Don’t let him compete!

Tang Yu’an knew immediately after hearing this that he was worried about the upcoming Spring Seasonals.

It appears that just relying on his own assurance wasn’t enough; he still needed a sense of safety from his other teammates.

Though it wasn’t a huge issue. After all, they still had a week left after the New Year’s break, enough for them to go for a few practice matches.

Thinking of this, Tang Yu’an rather unseriously tugged on the rabbit ears on top of Ling Suyu’s hood: “You’re overthinking it. We at Fight wouldn’t say things like that. You’ll know once you get to meet them. Speaking of which, what’s with your pajamas?”

“They’re just pajamas.” Ling Suyu raised his hands to save his ‘ears’, his voice growing smaller, “it’s not like I’ll wear them outside…”

“Did you also dress like this in UH?”

Just thinking about it made Tang Yu’an uncomfortable.

“Not really,” Ling Suyu replied, “UH’s washrooms were right next to my room, so I didn’t need to wear a jacket when going at night.”

“What about over the breaks?” Tang Yu’an asked curiously.

“Ah?” Ling Suyu didn’t understand why Tang Yu’an was asking about this, but still replied, “Over the break they would all return home, and I’d be the only one in the club.”

“They were alright with letting you stay in the club alone?” Tang Yu’an questioned. After thinking it over, he’d also feel okay with leaving Ling Suyu in Fight. Even if he were to be worried, it would be about Ling Suyu’s safety, rather than being afraid that he’d mess around.

He really…Ling Suyu really gave people the sense that he was too obedient.

However, Tang Yu’an hadn’t considered that Ling Suyu wasn’t exactly seen by everyone as so “obedient”.

“Of course they worried,” Ling Suyu responded, “So before they left, they’d lock up all the areas including the training rooms. I’d only have access to my own room and the lobby. If I wanted to play games, I’d go to an internet café.”

Tang Yu’an: ???

Was UH thoroughly a bunch of jerks?

At that moment, Ling Suyu yawned, and Tang Yu’an glanced at the time: “It’s half past 3, go sleep for a bit longer. Don’t set any alarms, just wake up naturally.”

Ling Suyu responded with an “en”, before lifting the blanket off his legs and standing up to leave the sofa.

He’d just taken a few steps towards the stairs before realizing something and turning back around: “Aren’t you going to sleep?”

Tang Yu’an raised the cup of water in his hands: “I’ll go after I’m done drinking.”

“Ok.” Ling Suyu didn’t think too much about it, heading up to sleep with the rabbit ears still on his head.

Originally he’d been scared awake by that nightmare and thought he wouldn’t be able to go back to sleep. However, after chatting a bit with Tang Yu’an, perhaps because he was now a lot more relaxed, he suddenly felt sleepy.

As he headed up the stairs, Tang Yu’an realized that his pajamas not only came with rabbit ears, but also a rabbit tail.

Staring at the fluffy little ball on the back of his clothes, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but chuckle —what kind of strange pajamas were those?

This guy, how were even his clothes so cute?

Ling Suyu returned to his bed, took a few deep breaths, and relaxedly fell asleep.

This time, he didn’t dream.

Even if he did, he didn’t remember them by the time he woke up.

He’d slept all the way until noon, and right after he finished washing up he was dragged outside by Tang Yu’an to have breakfast. On the way back, they picked up a few snacks— with Tang Yu’an paying for everything.

Once or twice was fine, but as it happened every single time, Ling Suyu felt a bit uneasy:

“How about I pay next time?”

Tang Yu’an wasn’t pushy about it: “Sure, how about you treat me during New Years?”

“Alright!” Ling Suyu instantly agreed, not realizing anything was wrong at all.

As such, all the way until New Years, Tang Yu’an was the one paying. On New Years, Ling Suyu finally treated Tang Yu’an to a meal, and only after he paid did realize—Huh? Although he’d paid, why did it feel like he’d taken more advantage of Tang Yu’an?

However, after New Years, Ling Suyu didn’t get the chance to treat him again, because the other members of Fight had begun coming back one by one, among them the chefs that were in charge of Fight’s meals.

Hearing that his teammates were coming, Ling Suyu, who’d finally managed to open up, once again tensed up. It was as if he’d been soaking in the arctic oceans for two days, his soft cuteness vanishing and his cold demeanour returning.

Yet, the first to arrive was Dou Jiayan. Right after he entered, he asked “Where’s Feather?”, before spotting Ling Suyu and immediately jumping him, giving him a big bear hug!

Ling Suyu looked incredibly confused.

“Aaahhh it’s Feather! A living, breathing Feather! Captain, you actually poached Feather!”

Dou Jiayan exclaimed while holding onto Feather and looking at his captain.

Tang Yu’an looked at him with an expression full of distaste: “How many times do I have to say it, I signed him, I didn’t poach him! Also, don’t act like Tarzan seeing another human for the first time, if Ling Suyu doesn’t perform well in the Spring Seasonals, it’ll be because you scared him!”

Ling Suyu was still in confusion before suddenly hearing Tang Yu’an’s words, unable to keep the corners of his lips from rising.

Before he got the chance to react, the person attached to him let go, introducing himself:

“Feather, nice to meet you! It should be our first meeting, right? I’m Dou Jiayan, Swallow, Yanzi!”

“The midlaner?” Ling Suyu remembered this ID.

“Yeah yeah! You remember me?” Dou Jiayan smiled happily.

He was slightly shorter than Ling Suyu, with a head of slightly curly brown hair. He looked full of energy, like a small sun.

Ling Suyu, apart from his parents, had only ever hugged Tang Yu’an, and his cool couldn't withstand being hugged like that, his cold demeanour instantly shattering. Seeing how happily the other was smiling, he couldn’t resist giving his own slight smile: “En, hello, um…

let’s work hard together?”

To the side, Tang Yu’an heard his reply and couldn't help chuckling.

He could tell that Ling Suyu wasn’t good at socializing.

It was good that each member of Fight was more enthusiastic than the next, and they weren’t lacking in a lively atmosphere.

The second to arrive was Ye Xiran. He didn’t hug Ling Suyu, but instead shook his hand for half a minute, smiling as he spoke: “Let me see, is this the marksman Captain Tang personally poached? Captain Tang’s vision isn’t half bad~”

Ling Suyu instinctively let out a noise in response, his face full of confusion.

Was this Fight’s manager?

Saying that Tang Yu’an’s vision wasn’t bad…had he also watched his matches?

Tang Yu’an facepalmed, resisting the impulse to kick Ye Xiran out the front door: “Why do you have so much nonsense to say…what about the stuff you actually need to do?”

“Are you talking about the practice matches? The date’s set~” Ye Xiran only let go of Ling Suyu’s hand upon hearing Tang Yu’an’s voice, turning to him.

Ye Xiran was taller than Ling Suyu but slightly shorter than Tang Yu’an. When facing Tang Yu’an, his whole aura weakened by half, though his tone was like he was asking for a beating: “When it comes to the business you assign me, as long as I’ve accepted it, when have I ever let you down? January 5th, 1pm, with YJ, how about it?”

“YJ?” Dou Jiayan curiously cut in, “Ma Mingxi?”

“Yep,” Ye Xiran nodded in affirmation, turning to look at Ling Suyu, “Feather, are you happy with that?”

Ling Suyu blinked, not understanding.

“Revenge for the White Fox Cup! Aren’t you going to take it?” Ye Xiran couldn’t help but remind him.

He’d specifically sought out YJ so that Feather could take revenge!

Ling Suyu thought of the Weibo posts that Ma Mingxi had sent and couldn’t muster up an ounce of hate towards him.

Compared to UH, he probably just had a potty mouth in addition to being too genuine, no?

Though he thought that, he nodded solemnly: “I’ll take my revenge! I’ll defeat them for Fight!”

Hearing this, Tang Yu’an couldn’t resist raising his hand and patting his head, laughing: “It’s not for Fight, but for yourself. I’ll let you do whatever you desire.”

Ling Suyu enjoyed the feeling of his palm, nodding agreeably: “En.”
