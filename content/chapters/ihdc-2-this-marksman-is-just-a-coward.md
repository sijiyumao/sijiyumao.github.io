You’ll know if you open the comments?

Intrigued by his captain’s words, Dou Jiayan quickly finished getting his water and returned to his computer. Opening the live comments, he was greeted with a flood of 【666】as well as—

【Does Ma Mingxi’s face hurt?】

【He really died before reaching level 6, is this was they call a prophecy coming true?】

【Moral of the story: as a person, you can’t be too unrestrained!】

Dou Jiayan was extremely confused and couldn’t help but scroll below the livestream’s video to check the comment section, before realizing what the livestream comments were saying. 

【Ma Mingxi told Feather to beware of dying at his hands before reaching level 6, but ended up being killed by Feather at level 1. He really did lift a rock only to smash it on his own feet!】

【For the record, I’m not one of Feather’s fans, but Ma Mingxi’s comments today were really too much, Feather’s kill was deserved!】

“Something like that happened?” Dou Jiayan decided that once the livestream was over he’d check the replay.

If he had known that even the trash-talk segment of the White Fox Cup would be this thrilling, he would have entered the livestream earlier. 

Due to Feather getting first blood, along with the stuff that happened before, the livestream’s camera pretty much became glued to his perspective, and the two commentators followed him word-for-word.

Ah-Mu: 【UH got a nice headstart on their economy, what a great success for Feather!】

ZhiZhi: 【Feather’s really brave! Although his play was risky, now the entire team’s economy has been pulled up. Maybe he’s actually UH’s secret weapon?】

Ah-Mu: 【You have to admit though, just now Ma Mingxi’s positioning was pretty dangerous.】

ZhiZhi: 【You mean him being too far from the tower?】

Ah-Mu: 【Yeah, standing that far from his tower while clearly being unable to compete for level 2, he must have never thought that the enemy would be so aggressive after reaching level 2.】

【He might have underestimated his opponent.】ZhiZhi accidentally spoke the truth, 【But regardless, who would have thought that UH’s substitute would be so intense? Nevertheless, the match has only just begun, and YJ still has hope, so let’s see if Mingxi can adjust his condition from here on out!】

ZhiZhi’s words hit the bullseye. Ma Mingxi was indeed feeling a bit uneasy. 

Actually, not only Ma Mingxi, but the entirety of YJ’s team was caught off-guard.

Although the White Fox Cup was an official competition, its outcomes only affected the players’ personal rankings, and wouldn’t influence whether or not a team qualified for the world championship. 

As a result, having just finished the All-Stars competition, they went into this match with the mindset of playing an entertainment match. In their fight with UH, who would have thought that after trash-talking, less than 2 minutes in, they’d be loudly slapped in the face? Who’s mentality wouldn’t be affected?

Thankfully, YJ were nevertheless seasoned players, and as soon as Ma Mingxi was killed, YJ’s captain Cheng Cheng reminded him: “Mingxi, don’t be hasty, watch yourself in the early game. Same goes for the rest.”

To Ma Mingxi’s relief, his tone wasn’t accusatory. But thinking of his pre-match trash-talk, he couldn’t help but feel like slapping himself in the face, his eyebrows furrowing.

At this moment, Ling Suyu had already finished with the third wave of minions and reached level 3, unlocking Guardian Spirit’s third skill “Spiritual Shield”.

This shield needed to be manually activated and only lasted a short amount of time. But the moment the shield broke, it would unleash damage on surrounding enemies. 

Ling Suyu continued to clear the 4th wave of minions while keeping an eye on the enemy marksman’s movements. Ma Mingxi’s Netherworld Shadow has clearly become more cautious after being killed once, staying under his tower as he cleared minions. 

Similarly to Guardian Spirit, Netherworld Shadow was also a marksman that dealt explosive damage. 

The base damage in this version had been scaled down, and the usage of explosive-type marksmen noticeably increased. However, for Ling Suyu, relying on skills was the playstyle of a mid-laner. He played marksman because he wanted to enjoy the thrill of continuously dealing damage and killing. 

Unfortunately, his coach wouldn’t let him play Cat Spirit…..

Nevermind, Guardian Spirit was fine, any marksman was fine, he’d practiced with them all anyways. 

Soon, the 6th wave of minions arrived, and after clearing out this wave, he would reach level 6 and unlock his ultimate. 

Looking at his own jungler’s position, he suddenly felt a bit of unease, returning to his tower before he finished clearing the minions. At the same time, he asked his teammate: “What’s the enemy jungler’s location?”

“Probably grabbing the small dragon in the bottom jungle?” The one responding was their mid-laner.

Soon after, their jungler also spoke: “I haven’t even finished clearing the jungle, why are you so nervous? I’ll head over once I’m done with the drake here.”

Ling Suyu furrowed his eyebrows, unwilling to re-enter the lane: “Support, check the undergrowth.”

“Okay.” Wen Xu obediently let Luminous Saintess throw a skill into the undergrowth near the tower. Nothing happened.

Then, as he entered the undergrowth, he suddenly heard a “pa-pa” sound before two skills from the opposing jungler landed directly onto Luminous Saintess, immediately taking away more than half her blood!

The enemy jungler was actually in the undergrowth!

Luminous Saintess’s dash and shield were both on cooldown, and she could only furiously run back to her tower. But on the other side, Ma Mingxi’s Netherworld Shadow, upon noticing his side’s jungler, instantly used his second skill Windchaser to hurry over, before using Soul Suppression on Luminous Saintess, followed by a series of basic attacks!

At the same time, the jungler also chased her under the tower and unleashed a basic attack!

【System】Gale Blade, one kill!

【System】Ally Luminous Saintess has been slain!

Ling Suyu didn’t bother with the support, using the fastest speed to lock on to the jungler Gale Blade. Comboing his first and second skills, he controlled him under the tower and shot three arrows, also following up with a series of basic attacks!

【System】Guardian Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Gale Blade has been slain!

【Luminous Saintess entered the undergrowth to scout, and Gale Blade chose to attack! Mingxi’s Netherworld Shadow rushed over! Nice! Luminous Saintess couldn’t escape, and Gale Blade got the kill! But Gale Blade got hit by the tower, can he escape? He can’t! Gale Blade has been slain!】

Commentator Ah-Mu’s speech became faster and faster: 【Feather got a second kill! Things are looking up for UH!】

【A jungler for a support, this round was a loss for YJ!】ZhiZhi said, 【Gale Blade hadn’t even entered the undergrowth, and UH had already begun to retreat, they’re pretty cautious! Actually, once Feather retreated underneath his tower, Gale BLade should have left. But after eating one of Luminous Saintess’s skills, he threw two back in revenge. Using one round of skills isn’t a problem, but chasing her under the tower was too reckless, underestimating Feather’s Guardian Spirit!】

Ah-Mu: 【It’s still understandable, after all, Netherworld Shadow had already rushed over, and Luminous Saintess only had a bit of health left, not killing would be a shame!】

ZhiZhi: 【They were mesmerized by the kill just within their reach?】

Ah-Mu: 【Hahaha, you could say that!】

ZhiZhi: 【But isn’t UH being too cautious? What if the enemy jungler wasn’t in the undergrowth? Were they going to give up on competing for level 6?】

Ah-Mu: 【It’s good to be careful, as Guardian Spirit is a marksman with no movement skill, and her dash was still on cooldown.】

Indeed, because his dash was still on cooldown, Ling Suyu refused to take the risk of being ganked by the opposing jungler just to clear the minions in his lane. 

And reality proved that he was right.

Wen Xu’s Luminous Saintess revived, and without a word, he solemnly returned to the bottom lane, to Ling Suyu’s side. 

UH’s captain was a marksman, and he was used to listening to the marksman’s commands. So, even though their temporary captain was a mid-laner, as long as Ling Suyu commanded, he would instinctively listen. 

Ling Suyu didn’t hate supports that listened, but he still thought that with something like checking the undergrowth, the support should do that on their own initiative instead of waiting for his direction. 

In addition, keeping track of the enemy jungler’s location was also the support’s duty. 

After all, supports didn’t really have much laning pressure, and could place more of their focus onto the mini-map.

However, the match had already begun, and it didn’t seem proper to say these things during a match. 

Ling Suyu could only fight while looking at the mini-map, trying to predict the jungler’s position.

【Currently, Feather has the highest economy on the battlefield! I’m looking forward to this match’s result more and more!】ZhiZhi said, 【Although it’s evident that YJ’s condition today isn’t super good, Feather’s performance is nevertheless impressive! Ah-Mu, how do you think he compares to Jiang Hao?】

【Ah? Are you sure you want to ask that?】Ah-Mu responded in shock.

Jiang Hao, UH’s original and starter marksman. 

Due to getting physical in a dispute the night before the competition, he was banned from competing in the White Fox Cup. If not for that, Ling Suyu would have never had the chance to appear in the White Fox Cup, much less get two kills in less than 2 minutes. 

In other words, Ling Suyu being able to compete was fully due to Jiang Hao being banned. 

Directly asking for a strength comparison between Ling Suyu and Jiang Hao?

Can this question even be answered?

In the end, Ah-Mu responded to ZhiZhi: 【The match has just begun, so we can’t tell much for now. But regardless, it’s difficult to judge the strength of players who play the same position. After all, aside from personal strength, there’s also critical factors like cooperation with teammates that can affect the chance of victory.】

【Indeed~】ZhiZhi tacitly understood Ah-Mu’s answer, continuing her commentary on the match between UH and YJ, 【Currently, members of both teams have reached level 6 and unlocked their ultimates. UH’s jungler is heading towards the bottom lane; is he planning to gank?】

Ling Suyu raised his eyebrow as he saw his team’s jungler hiding in the undergrowth beside the tower. 

In that fight just now, Ling Suyu intuited that the enemy jungler had come, but Cui Rao refused to believe him. As a result, he was slapped in the face, and stayed quiet after that. Ling Suyu almost thought he wouldn’t come to the bottom lane, but in the end he still came. 

It made sense. Not liking him was one thing, but whether or not they could win the match was another.

If not liking him was a reason to not play well in the competition, then UH would have long left the competition immediately after Jiang Hao was banned.

UH’s starters, aside from Jiang Hao who shared his position, had all played matches with Ling Suyu before, and all deeply detested him.

They all held the same impression of him, which could be summed up in four words—cowardly as a mouse. 

To be more specific—in order to survive, he would constantly sacrifice his teammates.

These comments might be shocking to the audience and the commentators, but it was a perfect descriptor of Ling Suyu’s personal playstyle.

This marksman was a complete chicken!

Have you ever seen a marksman who, in a team fight, not only did not go up and deal damage, but actually turned tail and ran?

No need to wonder, this was something Ling Suyu had actually done before.

His teammates didn’t know what his problem was. His movements were top-notch, his sense of attack timing was incredible, and his game sense was far above other professional players. But he refused to take even the slightest risk of death; no matter if the team fight could be won or not, as long as he would die, he wouldn’t go. 

If they could, UH would have refused to play with Ling Suyu, and let him spend the rest of his time as a substitute!

But this time, it felt like they had a chance!

“Feather, I’ll trust you just this once!” UH’s jungler Cui Rao said, “You bait, I’ll catch them. If you dare play around with me, once this match ends, I’ll beat you up in the bathroom!”

Ling Suyu thought for half a second, and in the end…..he grit his teeth and replied: “Okay, if I won’t die.”
