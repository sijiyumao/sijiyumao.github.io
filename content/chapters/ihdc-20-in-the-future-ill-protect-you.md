Afterwards, the two duo-queued for five more rounds, and Ling Suyu continuously got MVP for five rounds.

He played the marksman positions during all five rounds, pretty much going through all of the marksmen he was most familiar with as well as the current version meta. His support on the other hand…

In the first round, Tang Yu’an’s support position was stolen again. He took Nine-Tailed Fox and went mid, helping the bottom lane whenever he got the chance in the early game. In the late game, he directly played support and closely followed the marksman. Every time the enemy jungler tried to gank Ling Suyu, they’d be launched away by his skills and killed by a combo. Near the end, the enemy jungler directly started trolling in the fountain, spamming surrender.

In the second round, the version meta mid-laner Realm God was released, but their allied mid-laner didn’t know how to use it. Tang Yu’an insta-locked it as the first to pick, trading his Shield for Heal in order to support. In the early game, his explosive damage helped Ling Suyu collapse the enemy bottom lane, and once their side got the economic advantage they violently pushed. Within 10 minutes, the other side erupted into internal strife and surrendered, the chat box filled with beautiful words from the Chinese language.

In the third round, their teammate didn’t want to play jungler, so Tang Yu’an “regretfully” took the top-lane hero White Cocoon to play jungler. During the early game, he repeatedly ganked the bottom lane to the point of collapsing the enemy bottom lane. In the late game he basically turned the jungler into a top-laner, tanking all of the damage for Ling Suyu, not letting the enemy marksman touch a hair on Ling Suyu’s body.

In the fourth round, Tang Yu’an finally got the support position, but used the top-lane hero Black-Armoured Scythe to support. In the early game, he pressured the enemy bottom lane into being unable to leave the safety of their tower, and in the late game brought Ling Suyu to directly kill under the tower in all sorts of ways. Before the enemy towers were even destroyed, the enemy heroes had already all died.

In the fifth round, Tang Yu’an support position was stolen for the nth time, their teammates only leaving a top-lane position for him, with the mid-laner being unwilling to switch positions.

He took the top-lane hero Dark Angel to the top-lane and solo-killed the enemy top-laner as soon as the match began and ascended to level 3. He then directly went down to gank the mid-lane, and after collapsing the enemy mid-lane, went with their jungler to run amok in the enemy’s jungler. In the late game, with both tankiness and damage, the enemy top-lane turned around and ran away as soon as they saw him.

After finishing these five rounds, Ling Suyu, seeing the series of Victor’s MVPs in his battle records, had this expression: 0w0 Then, seeing the heroes his support picked, his expression turned into this: 0.0 How did Tang Yu’an know how to play every position!

Actually, it wasn’t every position.

Ling Suyu noticed that no matter which position he played, the heroes Tang Yu’an picked were similar—they either had high health and defense, or control skills.

“You used to play top-lane?” Ling Suyu asked curiously.

“Nope, I’m a professional support,” Tang Yu’an blurted out, but quickly followed up with an explanation, “I used to play all sorts of positions before I went professional. After becoming a professional player, to make it more convenient to command, I only play support.”

“Have you also played marksman before?” Ling Suyu asked.

Hearing this question, Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow. He understood what Ling Suyu really wanted to ask, so he replied: “I’ve definitely played before, but whenever I play marksman, my teammates get on their knees and beg me, which makes me feel quite embarrassed.”

Ling Suyu: “Ah?”

Tang Yu’an: “They get on their knees and beg me to log off, saying that they can definitely win.”

Ling Suyu took a second to react, and in the next, he understood what Tang Yu’an was saying and started laughing with a “pfft—”

“What,” Tang Yu’an actually seemed pretty aggrieved, “I’ve played so much top lane so I like getting up close and attacking, but if a marksman gets close they’ll be instakilled. I’m also pretty helpless.”

“Hahahahaha!” Ling Suyu couldn’t stop laughing.

Indeed, the playstyles of a top-laner and a marksman were quite different. Top-laners were frontliners, whereas marksmen belonged to the rear. Top-laners were in charge of tanking damage in the front, while marksman hid in the back and dealt damage. Upon switching to marksman after playing a lot of top-lane, it was easy to overestimate your health and underestimate the opponent’s damage.

For himself too, after playing marksman for so long, he found it hard to play top-laner. The close range didn’t give him any sense of security, and he always worried about being instakilled and thus was afraid to charge forwards. In the end, his battle records always showed three 0s, pretty much no difference from just going offline.

Put this way, the two of them complemented each other pretty well.

“Look, I don’t play assassin-type junglers, and I’m always the one initiating. I can’t hold back and join in after the fight’s already started, but if I initiate the fight then I’ll be instantly killed,”

Tang Yu’an continued, “So, although I can play all other positions aside from marksman, support still suits me best. The heroes I’m practicing right now, be they top or mid lakers, can all be used to support.”

“As expected, you’ve practiced before!” Ling Suyu didn’t let the details in his words slide, “You said you never practiced Skeletal Empress before!”

He knew it, how could he play so well if he’d never practiced before! If he was really that much of a genius, how could other professional players even survive?

“No way~ what I said was that I’ve been in support training for a hundred years!” Tang Yu’an refused to admit it.

“You’ve definitely said it before, the camera can prove it!” Ling Suyu lifted his hand and pointed at the security camera in the top corner of the training room.

Tang Yu’an raised his head and glanced in the direction he was pointing, before turning back and smiling mischievously: “The club’s on break, and so are the security cameras, surprised?”

Ling Suyu: “.....”

Ah, how annoying.

But…it wasn’t really that annoying.

Tang Yu’an saw that Ling Suyu had stopped arguing and offered a compromise: “Alright, I’ll admit I was messing with you at the start. All the heroes I used were ones I’ve been training with. Since I asked you to show me the marksmen you knew, how could I troll in response?”

As he said that, he stood up from his gaming chair and stretched his body, before suddenly thinking of something: “Right, I haven’t pulled you into Fight’s group chat yet, have I?”

Ling Suyu blinked.

Before he could even react, Tang Yu’an had placed his phone in front of him: “Come on, friend me first.”

Ling Suyu obediently picked up his own phone and scanned the QR code Tang Yu’an showed to him, adding him as a friend. Soon, he was pulled into a group chat titled “Everybody Fighting!”.

“Change your username to your real name,” Tang Yu’an reminded.

Ling Suyu did as he was told, before suddenly thinking of a problem: “I haven’t even signed with Fight yet, and you’re already adding me to the group chat?”

“What kind of dumb question is that? You’re already a part of Fight.” Tang Yu’an lifted his hand and patted his head, before realizing the unease in his tone and immediately understanding. “Oh, we could also sign it earlier. I’ll send you the contract later.”

“Okay!” Ling Suyu felt relieved.

Although his and UH’s contract hadn’t expired yet, the entirety of Weibo now knew that the contract had been terminated. He could sign with Fight at any time, as long as the written date on Fight’s contract was later than his original contract’s expiration date.

Signing sooner meant feeling reassured sooner, to avoid feeling like a kite with the snapped string, drifting through the wind.

After Tang Yu’an added Ling Suyu into the group, he @’d all the members.

Tang Yu’an: @Everyone stop playing dead, come welcome our newest member!

A single stone resulted in a thousand waves, and the originally-silent group chat suddenly became bustling with activity.

Dou Jiayan: Ah! Let me guess, is it Feather?

Ling Suyu: Hello everyone 0w0 Ye Xiran: Welcome, welcome! I was just planning to bug Captain Tang to see when he was planning to add you!

Mo Xiaoyun: Feather? The one from UH?

Ling Suyu: Yep orz Dou Jiayan: Sending hugs!

Yu He: Welcome.

Chen Jie: Welcome to the Fight family!

Seeing the name “Chen Jie”, Ling Suyu suddenly froze. He thought of UH’s marksman, Jiang Hao, and felt a bunch of mixed emotions.

Although Chen Jie’s hand injury meant he couldn’t compete in the upcoming Spring Seasonal, there would eventually be the day where his hand recovered, and at the time…

Just as his thoughts were wandering, he saw Tang Yu’an reply to Chen Jie.

Tang Yu’an: @Chen Jie why are you on your phone? Don’t want your hands anymore? Put your phone down and go rest!

Chen Jie: My left hand’s fine! What if I send voice messages?

Right after sending that, without waiting for anyone to reply, he directly sent a voice message.

Ling Suyu tapped on it with a slight anxiety, and from the phone came a mature and gentle voice: “Welcome, little Feather. With you here, I’m reassured. Good luck on the Spring Seasonals.”

Like Tang Yu’an, it was a magnetic voice full of charisma, though it lacked a bit of the playfulness and frivolity, leaning more towards mature and steady, giving Ling Suyu the impression of an older brother.

Hearing his voice, Ling Suyu instantly calmed down. Suddenly, his nose felt sour, and he felt a bit like crying, although he only felt it and didn’t actually cry.

Tang Yu’an observed the expression on his face and felt the fluctuations in his emotions, the corners of his lips lifting helplessly.

Ling Suyu: I’ll do my best!

Tang Yu’an: Jeez, don’t pressure him, our little Feather’s super nice.

Dou Jiayan: So where’s the photo? Newcomers should show a picture in the chat!

Tang Yu’an: What picture? You’ll see the real deal once you’re back from break.

Ye Xiran: Tsk tsk, Captain Tang’s attitude towards this newcomer is quite extraordinary.

Tang Yu’an: What the hell are you saying?

Ye Xiran: Feather, have you checked Weibo?

Ling Suyu: Saw it.

Ye Xiran: That was all Captain Tang.

Ling Suyu: Yeah! Captain’s the best!

Ye Xiran: Damn, as expected of you captain, I also did a lot, but you took all the credit.

Ling Suyu: Huh? Were you the one who posted on Weibo?

Ye Xiran: ?

Ling Suyu hadn’t checked Weibo afterwards, and thought Ye Xiran was talking about Fight’s repost.

At the same moment, Tang Yu’an also didn’t have time to explain, as one of the team members was privately messaging him. It was Fight’s top-laner, Mo Xiaoyun.

straight to the clouds: You’re planning to put Feather as a starter for the Spring Seasonals?

tangyuan: Is there a problem?

straight to the clouds: although feather’s pretty pitiful, being gaslit by UH for so long, but don’t tell me you haven’t seen that he also has his own issues. At some points, even though he could have joined in, and it wouldn’t be a loss even if he was killed, he just stood by and watched as his teammates died. To be honest, as a top-laner, I really hate these types of marksmen.

straight to the clouds: don’t get me wrong, I’m not against you signing him. But there’s a reason he spent so many years as a substitute, so I think you should consider signing a second marksman, just in case.

tangyuan: ok, I get what you mean. But from my point of view, since he doesn’t want to die, I’ll just protect him so that he doesn’t. Who doesn’t hate marksmen who sell out their own teammates? But for me as a support, in comparison to a marksman that’s afraid of death, I’m more afraid of those who aren’t. To put it bluntly, you’re doubting his strength, but that’s easy to solve. Just wait until after the break, we’ll have the manager make an appointment for a practice match, and you’ll know after going a few rounds.

straight to the clouds: ok.

With Mo Xiaoyun soothed, Tang Yu’an let out a breath he’d been holding and exited the private chatting interface. Lifting his head to see Ling Suyu’s complicated expression, he couldn’t help but pause: “What’s wrong?”

In front of Tang Yu’an confused gaze, Ling Suyu faced his phone towards him, the screen startlingly displaying the long apology message sent by UH.

“This…” as Ling Suyu spoke, he suddenly felt choked up.

After Tang Yu’an managed to see what was displayed on his phone, he immediately understood.

—Ye Xiran that bastard, he leaked the fact that he forced UH to apologize?

“Thank you…” Ling Suyu squeezed the two words out in a small voice. He suddenly dropped his arm, his grip loosening and the phone practically falling onto the table.

Then, he lowered his head, and asked in an even quieter voice: “Can I hug you? Just…for a moment?”

Tang Yu’an looked at him and, without saying anything, also placed his phone on the table, before opening his arms towards Ling Suyu.

In the next second, Ling Suyu leapt into his embrace, sobbing.

Ling Suyu clutched him tightly, like a drowning person hanging on to a piece of driftwood.

Tang Yu’an let him, feeling his surging emotions and the forceful hug, and felt momentarily lost.

Ling Suyu wasn’t good at showing his emotions outwardly. For the majority of the time, he was expressionless, making it easy for others to misunderstand him as cold and unfeeling.

Add that onto his near-pathological determination to stick to his own playstyle, and outsiders’

impressions of him could only get worse, thinking of him as a cold-hearted and stubborn tyrant.

To be honest, before interacting with Ling Suyu, Tang Yu’an had also held similar misconceptions towards him.

But now…

Ling Suyu was much more sensitive and fragile than he’d thought.

On many occasions, he only refused to verbalize his thoughts, and instead they built up in his heart, waiting for the chance to explode.

He must’ve been waiting for this apology from UH for quite a long time, no?

“Cry, you’ll feel better once you let it out. There’s only the two of us here, and the cameras are also on break. You can cry as much as you want,” Tang Yu’an said, gently patting Ling Suyu’s back. He rubbed his head, before lowering his own and softly adding, “Feather, welcome to Fight. In the future, let me protect you.”
