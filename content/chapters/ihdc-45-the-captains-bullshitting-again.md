The match between Fight and GQ was officially underway!

Here were the lineups for both teams:

Fight: Skeletal Empress, Deepsea Faerie, Desert Hunter, Abyssal Lord, Twin Demonblades.

GQ: Dark Angel, Nine-Tailed Fox, Netherworld Shadow, Mandara Vines, Double Phantom.

Both teams focused their bans primarily on the mid lane. Not only could Fight use Desert Hunter, but they also secured Twin Demonblades, giving them a significant advantage in this first round.

However, GQ’s lineup was also quite challenging.

“I don’t think I’ll be able to secure an early lead on the top lane, so I’ll be counting on you guys,” said Mo Xiaoyun.

Skeletal Empress excelled in team fights, whereas Dark Angel was a laning champion, extremely strong in 1v1s but with limited impact in team fights.

GQ’s composition clearly aimed to secure an advantage on the top lane.

Looking at the bottom lane, with a support specializing in heavy crowd control and a burst-damage marksman with mobility, they were perfectly suited for instant kills.

Tang Yu’an picked Abyssal Lord in this game specifically to prevent his team from getting instantly killed, but as far as Ling Suyu could recall, he rarely picked Abyssal Lord. After all, while playing other support heroes, he could still control an enemy to initiate a team fight or even deal some damage, but Abyssal Lord was purely for protecting teammates—his skills were nothing but shields..

As Ling Suyu entered the map, he glanced sideways at Tang Yu’an but said nothing.

As for Tang Yu’an, whether he caught it out of the corner of his eye or just sensed something, even though he wasn’t looking at Ling Suyu, he seemed to have picked up on that glance and asked, “What’s up? Do you think it’s weird that I picked Abyssal Lord?”

“A little?” Ling Suyu wasn’t quite sure.

After all… it was perfectly normal for a support to pick Abyssal Lord. This hero was super useful in team fights!

But for some reason, seeing Tang Yu’an pick Abyssal Lord just felt a little off.

On the other hand, whenever he picked Skeletal Empress, a top lane champion that almost no one would ever use as a support, Ling Suyu found it perfectly normal.

During their duo practice, Ling Suyu even forgot for a moment that Tang Yu’an was playing support; he’d just assumed he was a top laner.

Hearing Ling Suyu’s response, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but chuckle. “Consider this compensation for casting the shield too late yesterday. I’ll give you plenty of shields today.”

Ling Suyu didn’t reply, but the corners of his mouth curled up involuntarily.

Which marksman didn’t love a support with strong protective abilities?

“Actually,” Tang Yu’an added, “I don’t really like using this type of support. After all, with my skills, how would I show off my skills with a support that’s good at riding coattails?”

Ling Suyu left out a “pfft” in laughter.

The others: “……”

Here we go, the captain’s bullsh*tting again!

Tang Yu’an paid no attention to his teammates’ reactions and continued, “But since you’re the marksman, I don’t think I need to steal that much of the spotlight. As long as you get the glory, that’s enough for me.”

Ling Suyu was slightly taken aback.

“A good support doesn’t need a marksman with exceptional skills, and the same goes the other way around—a good marksman doesn’t need a support who does too much,” Tang Yu’an said. “I really don’t like playing Abyssal Lord, but at my core, it’s because I hate losing.

But with you on the team, I don’t think I’ll lose.”

“Mm-hmm,” Ling Suyu replied, somewhat curt, but his heart felt warm.

Since joining Fight, almost every day, he’d received acknowledgement of his skills from Tang Yu’an, something he’d never even dared to dream of back when he was with UH.

All the doubts he’d faced back then had vanished into thin air thanks to Tang Yu’an’s current recognition, leaving Ling Suyu feeling as though he’d been given a new lease on life.

The rest of the Fight members were silently rolling their eyes. They agreed with Tang Yu’an’s words—what they took issue with was the hidden agenda behind them. Was he implying that Ling Suyu didn’t cling to him enough?!

“The captain really does go out of his way every day to curry favor with Yumao,” Dou Jiayan couldn’t help but quip.

“What do you mean, ‘curry favor’?” Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but chuckle at being called out.

“As captain, isn’t it my duty to encourage my teammates?”

Dou Jiayan: “Then why don’t you encourage me, too?”

Tang Yu'an: “Good luck?”

Dou Jiayan: “Look at that! So half-hearted! I’m reporting the captain for favoritism!”

Tang Yu'an cleared his throat nervously: “I’m the support—of course I prioritize protecting the marksman.”

Dou Jiayan: “Hmph.”

By this point, everyone had arrived at their lanes. Ling Suyu had followed Tang Yu’an to the bottom lane and began laning against the enemy Netherworld Shadow.

This game, he was playing Desert Hunter instead of Candy Witch. As a pure support, Tang Yu’an couldn’t deal much damage to the enemy, so not only were they unable to pressure the opponent, but they were actually being pressured themselves.

The opposing team had clearly studied the Desert Hunter; although they were pressuring Fight, they didn’t dare push too deep. They played both aggressively and conservatively, maintaining a very stable balance between both teams’ gold.

Spectators who had originally expected Fight to build an early lead and push relentlessly, as they had in the qualifiers, were surprised. By the 6-minute mark, not only had Fight failed to secure a gold lead, but GQ had actually taken the lead instead.

“As we can see now, Fight is trailing GQ across the board in gold. Fight’s top and bottom lanes are both being pushed back, and the mid lane is suffering as a result,” Liang Fan explained. “Desert Hunter is a champion who can’t deal damage without a gold lead. I wonder how Fight plans to resolve this situation?”

How would they resolve it?

Of course, by lane-swapping!

Just as Ling Suyu was halfway through last-hitting his seventh wave of minions, and both he and Tang Yuan had reached level 6, Tang Yuan immediately called out: “Xiaoyun, recall and swap lanes with us! Yu He, get ready to gank top!”

At the same time, GQ was also itching to strike: “It’s about time to gank. Let’s send the jungler down to gank and crush their bottom lane!”

So, while Yu He’s Twin Demonblades rushed toward the top lane, GQ’s jungler, Double Phantom, lay in wait in the bottom lane bushes.

GQ had no idea what was about to happen, but the commentators, with their god’s eye view, had already seen right through it all.

Liang Fan: “GQ is preparing to gank the bottom lane, but Fight’s top and bottom lanes suddenly swapped lanes—GQ’s tactics have been figured out!”

Remi: “Fight’s jungler has gone to the top lane—he’s likely preparing to gank there. Let’s see if Fight can use this play to turn the economic tide!”

After Ling Suyu and Tang Yuan arrived at the top lane, they completely ignored the fierce battle at the lane and charged straight at the enemy top laner, Dark Angel. Tang Yu’an activated his shield and charged into the tower, lashing out with “Touch of the Abyss” to slow the Dark Angel’s movement speed. Ling Suyu followed up with “Hound’s Charge,” then activated his ultimate “Roar of the Desert” to boost all his damage, immediately unleashing a barrage of auto-attacks!

At the same time, Yu He, who had flanked from behind early on, chained his first, second, and third skills: Whirling Blade, Catch the Blade, and Double Slash, executing the combo flawlessly and instantly whittling Dark Angel’s health down by half!

Probably not expecting Fight to suddenly gank the top lane—and with such decisiveness and aggression—Dark Angel was caught off guard. It wasn’t until Yu He finished unleashing his wave of damage that he finally reacted, activating his ultimate “Six-Winged Seraph” and transforming into his six-winged form, which increased his damage while reducing the cooldowns on all his skills.

The next second after he activated his ultimate, Yu He also unleashed his ultimate, “Cries of Conflict,” chipping away another chunk of his health bar.

He immediately turned his attention to Twin Demonblades, unleashing a combo of Skills 1, 2, and 3. But almost simultaneously, Tang Yu’an activated his ultimate, “Ancient God’s Prohibition,” blocking all the damage from his skills. Yu He seized the opportunity to use his Double Slash again, followed by a Whirling Blade, then dashed out of the tower, ceding the kill to his team’s marksman.

Ling Suyu didn’t hesitate to finish him off with an “Explosive Shell”!

【System】Desert Hunter, first blood!

【System】The enemy Dark Angel has been slain!

“GQ’s top laner Dark Angel is down! Fight’s marksman, Desert Hunter, has secured the first kill!” Remi couldn’t help but exclaim, “That was so fast! Blink and you could’ve missed it!”

“That’s right! Fight executed that gank way too quickly! GQ’s top laner was completely caught off guard!” Liang Fan exclaimed excitedly, “Fight gave that kill to Feather’s Desert Hunter—it seems they have a lot of faith in Feather’s skills!”

Yu He had Flash available. With Flash, he could have easily used a dash ability to secure the kill and then Flashed out of the tower, but instead, he chose to finish his Double Strike combo and immediately use an ability to exit the tower—clearly, he had intentionally let the kill go.

Not only did the commentators spot it right away, but even the viewers could tell.

Ling Suyu realized it the moment he claimed the kill: “Thanks.”

Yu He replied casually, “You’re welcome.”

“Man, I took all the damage, and you got to take all the credit,” Tang Yu’an grumbled as he walked back to base with barely any health left after leaving the tower.

After Ling Suyu pushed the minion wave, he used “Hound’s Charge” to catch up to Tang Yu’an and returned to base with him: “Thanks to you, too~”

Tang Yu’an: “You’re welcome~”

Yu He: “……”

At that moment, GQ’s jungler was in the bottom lane. Despite knowing full well that Tang Yu’an was down to a sliver of health, he simply couldn’t make it in time and could only sigh in frustration.

GQ’s mid laner scouted the top lane bushes but couldn’t find anyone to take down. Not daring to venture too deep, he cleared a wave of minions and returned to mid lane.

Fortunately, they didn’t let this throw them off their game.

“Their mid laner’s been baited up top—quick, gank the bot lane!”

However, Fight had anticipated this all along.

Tang Yu’an: “Xiaoyun, watch out, the enemy jungler’s in the bot lane. Be careful not to get ganked.”

Mo Xiaoyun: “It’s fine, I can take him down!”
