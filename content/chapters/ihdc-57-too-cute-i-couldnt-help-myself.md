Fight partied all night at the KTV and didn’t return to the club until just after 5 a.m., then slept straight through until the afternoon.

Ling Suyu woke up naturally around 2 p.m. and headed to the bathroom on the first floor to take a shower.

When he got up, the club was very quiet. It seemed like everyone was still asleep.

But when he came out of the shower, he found that Tang Yu’an was already up, sitting on the sofa in the lobby. Not only that, but there was also a spread of breakfast items on the coffee table.

Seeing Ling Suyu emerge from the bathroom in his pajamas, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but laugh. “Good morning, little bunny.”

Ling Suyu instinctively replied, “Morning,” before realizing, “Wait, it’s already afternoon.”

He didn’t pay the slightest attention to the nickname Tang Yu’an had given him.

“Whenever I wake up, it’s morning,” Tang Yu’an said playfully, patting the spot next to him. “Come on, sit. You can eat whatever you’d like!”

“Did you buy all this? ” Ling Suyu leaned in to take a closer look at the coffee table, which was laden with steamed buns, xiaolongbao, and congee.

“Yeah, we ate pretty lavishly yesterday, so let’s keep it light today,” Tang Yu’an explained. Following Ling Suyu’s gaze, he spotted a carton of chocolate-flavored soy milk on the table and couldn’t help but smile. He reached out, picked it up, and handed it to him. “This one tastes pretty good, want to try?”

“Sure, thanks.” Ling Suyu reached out to take it, then sat down contentedly beside Tang Yu’an.

As he twisted open the small cap on the soy milk, he asked, “When did you get up? Where’s everyone else?”

“I just got up a little while ago. Everyone else is still asleep, but I figured it was about time—if I slept any longer, I’d turn into a pig—so I went out to buy breakfast nearby. As the team captain, I can’t let my members go hungry, right?” Tang Yu’an said. Seeing that Ling Suyu had been struggling to unscrew the cap for a while, he sighed helplessly, snatched the soy milk from his hand, unscrewed the cap, and handed it back to him.

“Thanks~” Ling Suyu said as he took a sip of the soy milk. The rich sweetness spread across his tongue, warming his heart.

The corners of his lips curled up slightly. Tang Yu’an turned his head to look at his profile and couldn’t help but smile too.

Sure enough, his little cutie loved sweets.

Looked like they’d better stock up on sugar at home from now on.

Just then, a noise came from the top of the stairs. Ling Suyu and Tang Yu’an instinctively looked up and saw Dou Jiayan, also in his pajamas, rubbing his eyes as he came down the stairs.

Compared to Ling Suyu’s white rabbit pajamas, Dou Jiayan’s pajamas were much more “normal”—a gray flannel robe.

As he came down the stairs, he noticed people in the living room and tentatively asked, “How are the other teams doing?”

Hearing this, Tang Yu’an burst out laughing on the spot, and Ling Suyu also found it hard to hold back a chuckle.

“Wow, you wake up and the first thing you ask about is game results? Did you dream you were playing in the matches yourself?” Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but tease.

“Actually, I did,” Dou Jiayan replied, feeling a bit more awake as he leaned on the stair railing after coming downstairs. “Let me tell you, I dreamt last night that we were playing against UH. You know how Yumao’s a marksman? Well, in the middle of shooting, he suddenly grew furious, turned his bow and arrow into a club, and spent the whole time chasing down UH’s players and beating them up. It was so cool!”

Ling Suyu: “……”

“Hahaha, I can totally picture it!” Tang Yu’an couldn’t stop laughing. “Only you could have a dream that outlandish! But as they say, what you think about during the day shows up in your dreams at night. I can tell you can’t wait to help Yumao beat up UH.”

“Yeah, I scrolled Weibo before bed last night, and it almost drove me crazy!” Dou Jiayan was fully awake now. He took a few steps over and sat down on the sofa next to them. “How can UH’s fans be so shameless? Several clubs have already spoken up for Yumao, and they’re still pretending to be blind!”

“You went straight to the comments under UH’s Weibo post, didn’t you?” Tang Yu’an guessed. “Isn’t it pretty normal to blindly gush over UH in the comments under their own posts?”

“What’s so great about UH anyway?” Dou Jiayan asked, puzzled. Then he noticed the fluffy little ears peeking out from behind Ling Suyu’s hood and couldn’t resist reaching out to give them a little tug.

Ling Suyu: ?

Tang Yu’an reached over Yumao and slapped Dou Jiayan’s hand away: “They just love being UH’s fans, so what? Stop bullsh*tting, did you brush your teeth? Did you wash your face? If you want breakfast, go wash up first!”

Dou Jiayan rubbed the back of his hand, which stung from the slap, and glanced at the breakfast on the coffee table. Although he really wanted to retort, “I’m not even hungry,” he ultimately couldn’t resist the tempting aroma of breakfast and said, “Fine, fine, I’ll go right now,” before getting up and heading to the bathroom.

After he left, Ling Suyu instinctively watched the direction he’d gone. Before he could even process what was happening, he suddenly felt someone tug on the ears of his hood again.

Surprised, he turned to look at Tang Yu’an beside him.

Tang Yu’an guiltily pulled his hand back: “You’re just too cute, I couldn’t help myself.”

Ling Suyu couldn’t hold back a giggle: “It’s fine, go ahead and tug.” He paused. “But I haven’t blow-dried my hair yet. Should I go do that first, then change my clothes?”

Although he didn’t mind having his “ears” tugged, if everyone started doing it…

He didn’t like drawing so much attention to himself.

Tang Yu’an gave a soft “Mm.”

Hearing his response, Ling Suyu immediately stood up. As he rose, he was still holding the soy milk carton; he hesitated for a moment, set it back on the coffee table, but then picked it up again a moment later, looking as if he didn’t know what to do with it.

Tang Yu’an watched his hesitant movements, stifling a laugh as he said, “Go ahead. I’ll keep an eye on it for you. No one’s going to touch your soy milk.”

Ling Suyu cleared his throat and set the soy milk down with a sigh of relief. “I’ll be right back.”

Tang Yu’an watched his fluffy silhouette and felt quite pleased.

Who would’ve thought this little bunny was so protective of his food?

After blow-drying his hair, Ling Suyu took off his pajamas and put on a jacket.

As Dou Jiayan finished his morning routine, the other members of the Fight Club began waking up one by one.

Chen Jie sipped preserved egg and lean pork congee from a plastic cup as he scrolled through his phone. “Team A’s Winne beat da diao 2-0. It was pretty much a foregone conclusion, Winne’s always been a powerhouse.”

“Da diao [^1]?” Ling Suyu, as usual, didn’t understand the nicknames Fight’s members used for the other teams.

“Big.C.” Tang Yu’an explained while munching on a steamed bun, “The ‘C’ stands for ‘Condor,’ meaning ‘divine eagle.’ The team’s official Chinese name is ‘Da Diao,’ something they chose on purpose. I remember that when the team first started, they used their full name, ‘Big Condor.’ The commentators were too lazy to say the full name, so they called them ‘BC’ during live streams. The team didn’t like that—they didn’t want to be just one letter away from the infamous fake news outlet BBC—so they shortened their name to ‘Big.C.’ ”

“Only Chinese people would get the ‘Big Condor’ joke,” Chen Jie said. “Actually, I’m really hoping they make it to the World Championship. When foreigners inevitably ask them what their team name means and how they came up with it, I’m kind of curious to see how they’ll respond.”

“Big Condor…what’s wrong with that?” Ling Suyu didn’t understand why this was such a joke.

Upon hearing this, Tang Yu’an and Chen Jie suddenly stopped eating their breakfast. Mo Xiaoyun and Yu He looked at Ling Suyu; the former hesitated as if about to speak, while the latter wore a look of bewilderment.

“Well…” Dou Jiayan tried to explain, but Tang Yu’an immediately covered his mouth.

“Oh, I see,” Tang Yu’an stepped in to save the situation. “Feather, do you know what the English title of 《射雕英雄传 [^2]》is?”

Ling Suyu shook his head.

Tang Yu’an continued, “It’s 《Legends of the Condor Heroes》. They translated ‘diao’ (雕) as ‘Condor.’ Originally, the ‘diao’ in 《Legends of the Condor Heroes》 were closer to what we call ‘eagles,’ but the birds in the book are much larger than eagles. Fearing that foreign readers might not understand, they were translated as ‘condors.’ So yeah, that’s the joke.”

“I see!” Ling Suyu’s eyes lit up.

Tang Yu’an smiled.

Chen Jie’s lips twitched slightly, and he silently gave Tang Yu’an a mental thumbs-up—

Seriously, he actually managed to make such a tacky pun sound so highbrow.

Actually, Ling Suyu eventually caught on, realizing that big condor (da diao) = big diao. Tsk, didn’t the players on this team feel any shame?

But he didn’t feel like Tang Yu’an had tricked him; on the contrary, he thought Tang Yu’an was really impressive—he knew so much!

“In Group B, YJ defeated S.MG, though they let S.MG score one point,” Chen Jie continued, sharing the group stage results. “In Group C, UH also beat XY 2-1. All the teams that made it to the quarterfinals in last year’s Summer Seasonals have advanced this year, no dark horses.”

“That’s fine. I’m more afraid of dark horses than playing against these ‘old friends,’” Tang Yu’an said. “After all, there aren’t many game replays available to study for dark horses, and their true strength is difficult to gauge. We were able to make it all the way to the semifinals last year precisely because the quarterfinalists didn’t know us well enough. But this year… we’ve got Yumao~”

As he spoke, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but ruffle Ling Suyu’s hair.

Chen Jie, sitting directly across from him, couldn’t help but tut at the sight: “Don’t go rubbing his head all the time. Haven’t you noticed that Yumao’s getting fed up with you?”

“Yumao would never get fed up with me~” Tang Yu’an had already been about to stop, but upon hearing this, he pettily rubbed Ling Suyu’s head a little longer. “Right, Yumao?”

Ling Suyu silently sipped his soy milk, gave a soft “Mm-hmm” in response, and then, completely ignoring the hand ‘running rampant’ on his head, picked up a steamed bun with his chopsticks, popped it into his mouth, and began chewing.

Chen Jie: “……”

Alright, never mind.

After everyone finished breakfast, they began their post-game analysis. Once that was done, they started training to prepare for the quarterfinals coming up in a few days.

However, before the quarterfinals officially began, the organizers assigned each participating team a “task”: to shoot individual posters and record a team video.

This has become a BPL tradition—shooting and recording took place before every Top 8 Qualifier, with the goal of building hype for the event and attracting more viewers.

Fight had done this last year when they made it to the top 8 for the first time, so they’d gotten the hang of it by now.

However, Ling Suyu seemed a bit taken aback when he heard the news.

To make matters worse, Dou Jiayan rubbed salt in the wound: “Huh? Yumao, you’ve been playing esports for five years, but you’ve never done this before?”

Chen Jie smacked him right on the head: “You truly have a knack for bringing up the worst topics. Get out here and go practice!”

Although Ling Suyu had been stung by the remark, he took it in stride and calmly replied to Dou Jiayan: “No, back in my first year, none of this existed. All we had to do was show up for the matches. Later on…substitutes didn’t have to do photo shoots.”

“Why didn’t substitutes…” Dou Jiayan was about to take another stab at it when Yu He, standing nearby, quickly covered his mouth.

Then Tang Yu’an spoke up: “It’s okay, I’ll teach you how to do it. Don’t worry~”

Ling Suyu gave a soft “Mm.”

With those words from Tang Yu’an, he felt at ease.
