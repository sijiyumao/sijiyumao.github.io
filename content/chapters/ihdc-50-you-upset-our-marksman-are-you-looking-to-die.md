While Fight was studying igame, igame was also intensely studying Fight.

Facing Fight, igame was equally determined not to lose.

“Last season, we were on the verge of making it to the semifinals, but we were knocked out by Fight, that dark horse. We absolutely cannot repeat that mistake this season!”

“Our training up until now has been specifically geared toward Fight. I didn’t expect them to suddenly switch marksmen, but even with the change, their core gameplay hasn’t changed —the lineup we’ve been practicing will still work.”

“We can’t let them gain an early-game advantage, and we have to ensure our late-game damage output.”

“Also, don’t forget that tomorrow’s match is just the group stage. The quarterfinal points-based matches are where it really counts. So while we need to win tomorrow, we can’t reveal our strongest lineup too early and give other teams too much time to study us.”

“Yeah, let’s treat the first game tomorrow like a practice match and just feel each other out first.”

And so, without knowing, both teams adopted the exact same strategy.

Like GQ, igame also spent quite a bit of time analyzing Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter.

They guessed that Desert Hunter wasn’t Ling Suyu’s top pick, but since he was very likely to use it in tomorrow’s first game, they still took the time to study it.

However, unlike GQ, after finishing their analysis, igame didn’t even consider banning Desert Hunter.

“Isn’t it normal for an ADC to put pressure on the enemy once they’ve secured an economic advantage? It’s a good thing Feather picked Desert Hunter. If he’d picked Guardian Spirit, GQ would’ve lost even more badly.”

“But Desert Hunter is too versatile, and Feather’s mistake rate is low. He’s really hard to gank. We shouldn’t worry about taking him down in the early game and just focus on preventing him from gaining lane dominance.”

“As for the late game, we’ll need to secure more crowd control.”

The following afternoon, Fight and igame faced off at the venue.

Coincidentally, the previous match had been between UH and XY. UH, the team Ling Suyu had originally played for, defeated XY 2-1 and advanced to the quarterfinals as the top seed in Group C.

As the host and commentators congratulated UH, the live audience erupted in a chorus of boos.

After the match, when UH’s players took off their headsets, they could faintly hear the boos from across the room. They were all a bit bewildered, unsure of what had happened, but they could guess it had something to do with Ling Suyu.

Even though Ling Suyu was clearly just a minor substitute for UH, he had stolen the spotlight as Fight’s star starter during the Spring Seasonals, which must have subjected UH to considerable scrutiny.

UH’s starters had mixed feelings about this. Deep down, they vaguely realized they might have underestimated Ling Suyu’s skills in the past, but they were unwilling to admit it.

Instead, they could only say in a tone that clearly echoed the proverb “the fox who can’t reach the grapes insists that they’re sour” [^1]: “Fight’s really willing to protect a marksman like that—aren’t they afraid of getting backstabbed?”

“Our marksman is still better.”

“Of course. If I say Hao-ge is the best marksman in Glorious, no one would argue, right?”

Jiang Hao pursed his lips and said nothing.

This was merely an internal celebration within UH. Right now, everyone, both inside and out, who’d heard the gossip about UH and Ling Suyu was dying to see Fight pin UH to the ground and grind them into the dirt, preferably by crushing them in a 10-minute round and turning UH’s faces into pig’s heads on the spot.

But before that, Fight had to finish their match against igame first.

Ling Suyu stood shoulder-to-shoulder with his current teammates at the competition venue.

The bright lights shone on his dazzling red jersey, enveloping his entire figure in a warm glow.

【This match pits Fight against igame! Both teams made it to the quarterfinals of the

last BPL, so this is truly a clash of titans! Facing a powerhouse they’ve battled before, the players from both teams surely have a lot to say to each other!】

Before the match began, as usual, there was a trash-talking segment to warm up the crowd.

After the host finished his remarks, he suddenly turned towards Fight’s bench, his gaze landing on Ling Suyu, who was standing right in the center: “Feather, you faced igame back when you were with UH, right? UH lost 0-2 to igame in the White Fox Cup, allowing igame to take third place. Do you feel any lingering regrets about that, Feather?”

As he spoke, he walked up to Ling Suyu and handed him the microphone.

Now that he’d been called out, no matter how much Ling Suyu wanted to vanish right then and there, he didn’t turn to Tang Yu’an, who was standing right beside him, for help. Instead, he took the microphone with a blank expression and, after a brief silence, replied, “No matter who the opponent is, I’ll help Fight win.”

With that single sentence, he sidestepped every trap the host had set.

Ling Suyu didn’t care which team he’d played for in the past, nor which team had beaten him before.

From the moment he joined Fight, his destiny had changed.

He’d been given a new lease on life.

Now and in the future, he would play for Fight! Whether the opponent was igame or UH, he would do his best to win!

Probably thinking his answer wasn’t sensational enough, the host continued to probe: “So even if the opponent is UH, you’ll still win, right?”

Just as Ling Suyu was about to answer “Yes,” the microphone in his hand was suddenly snatched away by Tang Yu’an, who was standing beside him.

As the microphone was snatched, Ling Suyu instinctively clenched his grip, but the moment he realized it was Tang Yu’an who had taken it, he not only let go immediately, but even pushed the microphone toward Tang Yu’an’s chest before releasing it, and afterwards looked up at Tang Yu’an with a gaze full of trust and a touch of curiosity.

In front of him, and in the presence of the host and the entire live audience, Tang Yu’an spoke in a leisurely yet slightly flippant tone: “This is a match between Fight and igame.

igame’s players are standing right across from us, and mentioning other teams in front of them is disrespectful. But to igame, we have only one thing to say—in our final match last season, your main tower was pushed down by minions, and today, we’ll push down your main tower with our own hands!”

As soon as he finished speaking, the entire arena erupted, with applause and screams rising in waves!

Then, under Ling Suyu’s admiring gaze and the host’s slightly awkward stare, Tang Yu’an smiled as he handed the microphone back to the host. He tilted his head back slightly, a hint of displeasure flashing across his narrow eyes.

—Mentioning that unlucky team on purpose before the match to upset our marksman, are

you looking to die?

The host: “……”

“Ahem, that’s true—this match is indeed between Fight and igame…What’s igame’s response to Fight’s declaration?” The host, looking a bit sheepish, walked over to the igame players and held out the microphone toward them.

At that moment, the live stream’s chat and the comments below were buzzing:

【Tang Yu’an said it well! The host should apologize to igame!】

【I’m an igame fan. Even though Fight took a shot at igame, I’m not angry at all, and

in fact, I’ve developed a real fondness for Fight. Is this the pinnacle of trash-talking?】

【Exactly what you’d expect from Fight’s captain! He saved Feather while also

standing up for igame—I’m giving this comeback full marks!】

【Tang Yu’an really spoils Feather, AnYu szd!!】

In fact, Ling Suyu didn’t realize that Tang Yu’an was coming to his rescue. He didn’t even realize that the host was trying to stir up trouble, hoping to escalate the conflict between him and UH to boost the BPL’s ratings.

But he felt that Tang Yu’an’s response was indeed much more clever than his own, giving him a moment of epiphany—oh right, their opponent is igame. Asking them right in front of igame whether they’d beat UH was a huge slap in the face to igame.

igame was indeed offended, so when their jungler, as igame’s captain, took the mic, he didn’t immediately respond to Fight’s trash talk. Instead, he followed Fight’s lead and fired back at the host: “UH’s match was the last one. If the host cares so much about UH, I suggest rewinding to that game. This one is the fated showdown between us, igame, and Fight. As for Fight, we at igame have only one thing to say—either you take the championship trophy in one fell swoop, or you’ll obediently bow down at our feet and watch us claim the title!”

“Whoa!” Dou Jiayan couldn’t hold back any longer and started rolling up his sleeves. “That’s something we should be saying!”

“Calm down,” Tang Yu’an said, pressing him down with one hand. “Didn’t you hear what they said? Either that, or take the championship trophy in one go. igame’s really generous, giving us a choice, so we’re definitely going with the former—”

After igame made their statement, the venue erupted once again, and the livestream’s bullet comments and the comments below refreshed rapidly:

【This is getting intense!】

【igame’s statement is so cool!】

【You can tell both teams really respect each other’s skills!】

【I think igame has always had a bone to pick with Fight, not because they lost to

Fight, but because Fight didn’t win the championship after beating them.】

【It was such a shame for Fight last season. During the semifinals, it was obvious their

marksman’s hands were giving out.】

【Last season’s Fight was like a shooting star—a fleeting burst of brilliance that

quickly faded. I hope they can go even further this season!】

【I really love both igame and Fight! No matter who wins today, I’ll be okay with it!】

Since igame’s statement was so explosive, the host gave Fight another chance to respond.

But Fight simply replied with one word: “Okay.”

Either they’ll sweep the championship trophy in one go, or they’ll watch igame claim the title?

Of course that’s okay.

We’re going to sweep the championship in one go!

After the warm-up segment, the players from both teams headed to their respective match rooms.

The ban/pick order for the first game was determined by a dice roll, and the dice landed on igame.

This meant that in the first game, igame would ban first, and Fight would pick first.

igame used their first ban slot on Twin Demonblades.

“Huh?” Dou Jiayan looked surprised for a moment, then a look of disappointment crossed his face. “I’m not used to not seeing a mid-laner get banned first.”

“Doesn’t it feel like we aren’t being taken seriously?” Tang Yu’an chuckled. “Well, then let’s not ban their mid laners or their captain’s jungler—let’s ban a top laner instead: Dark Angel.”

“Got it,” replied Yu He, third on the list, banning Dark Angel.

Ah-Mu and ZhiZhi were once again handling commentary today, and both seemed incredibly excited to be casting for a match between quarterfinalists.

“It’s quite surprising that igame banned Twin Demonblades first!” said Ah-Mu. “After all, igame is a jungle-centric team, so every jungler they ban is one less for them to rely on. On the other hand, they don’t really need a mid laner, so they can ban that position freely.

However, since Fight gets to pick first, if they snag Twin Demonblades, it would definitely put igame at a disadvantage.”

“Fight used their first ban on Dark Angel!” ZhiZhi said, sounding even more surprised than Ah-Mu. “They didn’t ban a strong mid laner for this patch, nor did they ban according to igame’s core position. Instead, they banned a top laner who’s strong in the laning phase. Are they trying to prevent igame from building an early-game advantage?”

“That’s probably it,” Ah-Mu speculated. “Although Fight has basically won the early game in almost every match they’ve played this season, their marksman-centered playstyle means they’re a team that excels in the late game, so they need to make sure they can survive until the late game. Their risky decision to use Mobile Artillery support against OsO was also part of their preparation for the late game, though they still managed to win the early game in that match.”

“So when facing Fight, opponents still need to secure an early lead and win as quickly as possible.” ZhiZhi understood.

No sooner had she finished speaking than igame used their second ban slot on Tempest Blade.

“They’ve banned another jungler!” Ah-Mu exclaimed in surprise. “igame is self-banning their own junglers—it seems they really recognize the strength of Fight’s jungler, huh?”

Yu He, who had just seen two of his picks banned consecutively: “I don’t want this kind of respect. Just give it to Yanzi.”

“Banning junglers? If they ban, we’ll ban too,” Tang Yu’an said, eager to stir up trouble.

“Come on, Yumao, let’s ban his Blade Soul—”

“Okay!” Ling Suyu didn’t hesitate for a second and instantly banned Blade Soul!

“Fight has banned a jungler too!” ZhiZhi exclaimed in surprise. “Wow, the junglers are really being targeted hard in this game. Even though the pick phase hasn’t started yet, I’m already worried about igame!”

“I wonder who igame will ban third?” Ah-Mu asked curiously. “Judging by their banning pattern, they probably won’t ban Desert Hunter.”

Sure enough, igame didn’t use their third ban on Desert Hunter; instead, they banned a mid laner— Wind Guardian.

“Oh?” This unexpected ban left ZhiZhi stunned, but she quickly recovered. “They banned Wind Guardian—does that mean igame is going to pick Curse Master?”

Wind Guardian had the longest range of any hero, but if the opposing mid laner had mobility, they could still outplay her in lane—and the strong mid laners in this patch almost all had mobility.

Since they’d banned Wind Guardian, igame was definitely going to pick a mid laner without mobility to face off against Fight. Considering igame’s mid laner’s go-to heroes, it wasn’t hard to guess their pick.

If the commentator could figure it out, how could Fight, who’d specifically studied igame, possibly fail to see it coming?

“Curse Master again?” Tang Yu’an mused, “This mid-laner really never gets tired of playing that hero.”

“So should I just pick whatever feels comfortable and call it a day?” Dou Jiayan said, “But last season, when I used Phoenix Soul, he kept crowd-controlling me. Similar champions like Harpist and Array Sprite probably won’t be able to outplay him either. Beast Master and Realm God could outplay him, but in those cases I’m not confident I can carry the team.”

“Go with Nine-Tails,” Tang Yu’an decisively chose for him.

Dou Jiayan thought for a moment: “Sure, I’ll carry you this game!”

“What should we ban?” Mo Xiaoyun on the fifth floor was still waiting for Tang Yu’an to help him pick the final ban slot.

Tang Yu’an replied decisively: “Ban Mandara Vines and snatch Abyssal Lord.”

“Alright.” Mo Xiaoyun banned Mandara Vines.

And just like that, Fight and igame had finalized their bans, eliminating three junglers, one top laner, one mid laner, and one support.

“Today’s bans are very different from usual! It’s really refreshing!” said ZhiZhi. “But it also shows that Fight and igame really know each other inside and out!”

Indeed, precisely because they understood igame, and knew that their mid laner’s Curse Master currently had a “meta advantage” and thus they wouldn’t even bother competing for the actual meta picks, Fight didn’t even consider banning the mid laner.

As for igame, precisely because they understand Fight, and knew that their jungler, despite his unassuming appearance, was actually very steady and had a much lower rate of mistakes compared to their mid laner, they were more focused on limiting Fight’s jungler than their mid laner.

And sure enough, their bans successfully limited each other.

Fight’s original strategy was to ban one of the meta-dominant junglers and pick the other. If the opponent didn’t ban junglers, they’d ban Tempest Blade and snatch Twin Demonblades; if the opponent banned Twin Demonblades, they’d go for Tempest Blade. But to their utter surprise, the opponent resolutely banned both of these meta-dominant insta-kill junglers, leaving Fight unable to pick either.

Originally, igame planned to use Dark Angel to secure an early-game advantage and Mandara Vines to ensure a late-game edge. If their jungler couldn’t get Twin Demonblades or Tempest Blade, they’d go for Blade Soul. But to their complete surprise, Fight predicted their strategy perfectly and banned every champion they intended to pick, not letting a single one slip through.

However, it wasn’t a big deal. Such bans were expected on both sides. No matter what lineup they picked, the key would be how they executed it on the battlefield.

“Now let’s take a look at Fight’s picks. They’ve got first pick…” Before Ah-Mu could finish speaking, Fight instantly locked in Abyssal Lord. “Abyssal Lord! It’s rare to see someone pick a support first, but it makes sense. Fight’s core strategy is for the entire team to protect the marksman, and Abyssal Lord is the support with the strongest protective abilities. It looks like Fight plans to play it safe until the late game and secure an advantage through team fights. Will Feather pick Desert Hunter?”

Next up was igame, who got to pick two positions at once. They chose Child of Frost as their jungler and Skeletal Empress as their top laner.

ZhiZhi: “Neither team is in a hurry to pick a mid laner or marksman. igame snatched Fight’s top laner’s first pick. I wonder which champion Fight plans to pick for the top lane in this game?”

After a brief moment of hesitation, Fight selected Double Phantom as their jungler and Black-Armoured Scythe as their top laner.

Ah-Mu: “Fight still hasn’t picked a mid laner or marksman. Let’s see what igame chooses!”

Unable to force Fight to reveal their dual carry picks, igame was forced to pick first, selecting Guardian Spirit as their marksman and Saintess of Light as their support.

Then, on Fight’s side, unable to force igame to reveal their mid-lane pick, they hesitated no longer and locked in their final two positions almost instantly—Nine-Tailed Fox and Desert Hunter!

“Desert Hunter!” Ah-Mu exclaimed excitedly. “What did I say? As long as Feather dares to use Desert Hunter in a quarterfinalist match, I’ll go out on a limb and say he’s the best Desert Hunter in Glorious!”

“That’s assuming he can actually win with Desert Hunter!” ZhiZhi said. “But the fact that Fight is still willing to use Desert Hunter against igame shows just how confident they are in Feather’s Desert Hunter!”

“Now igame only has one slot left to fill. Do you think they’ll pick Curse Master?” Ah-Mu wondered. “igame’s mid-laner, Taozi, is widely recognized as the best Curse Master in Glorious. If he plays Curse Master again in this match, it’ll be a showdown between the best Curse Master and the best Desert Hunter!”

No sooner had he finished speaking than igame picked their mid laner—and it really was Curse Master!

With that, both teams’ lineups were set:

Fight: Black-Armoured Scythe, Nine-Tailed Fox, Desert Hunter, Abyssal Lord, Double Phantom; igame: Skeletal Empress, Curse Master, Guardian Spirit, Saintess of Light, Child of Frost; “Judging by the lineups, Fight’s bottom lane will face significant early-game pressure, but in the late game, both teams’ compositions are solid. There’s no clear advantage or disadvantage,” Ah-Mu explained. “I’m really curious to see how this match will play out!”

“Indeed!” ZhiZhi’s tone grew noticeably excited. “Will igame’s Curse Master achieve glory once again, or will Fight’s Desert Hunter work another miracle? Let’s wait and see!”

Both teams assigned their heroes to the corresponding players, adjusted their talent and item presets, and soon, the countdown ended, and the battlefield was readying.

Watching the loading progress bar on the screen, players from both Fight and igame took a deep breath, while their coaches nervously clenched their fists.

For both Fight and igame, this match marked their first official encounter with a top-eight team this season. The opponents’ strength was on a completely different level from their previous matches against OsO and GQ—the outcome was anyone’s guess.

However, since they were aiming for the championship, no matter who their opponent was, they had to win!

【Both teams have entered the battlefield, the match has officially begun!】
