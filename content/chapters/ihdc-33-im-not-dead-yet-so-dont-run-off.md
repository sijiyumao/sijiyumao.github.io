Once they secured an advantage in the bot lane, the pressure on the mid and top lanes eased significantly, as the marksman and support could now assist the other lanes.

Ling Suyu returned to their base to once again replenish his health and mana, then headed straight to the mid lane to clear minions and push the tower. Tang Yu’an naturally followed him.

At this point, YJ had only their top laner left alive. He was already overwhelmed just defending the top lane’s first tower and couldn’t spare a thought for the mid lane.

As a result, Ling Suyu pushed down the mid lane first tower with almost no resistance.

【System】The enemy’s tower has been destroyed!

With YJ’s first tower on the mid lane gone, their situation immediately became dire, especially since Fight hadn’t lost a single tower yet. This left them in a tight spot in their attempts to take down both the Divine Sunbird and the Dragon God.

It was safe to say the situation was extremely unfavorable for them.

Although they had planned to take this practice match against Fight seriously from the start, everyone had looked relatively relaxed at the beginning. Now, however, their expressions had turned grave.

“The state of this game is getting a bit dangerous…” After the top laner returned to base to replenish his health, he immediately headed to the bottom river but didn’t dare go in, instead lingering on the outskirts.

It was now the 8th minute of the game. The Divine Sunbird had spawned, but the Dragon hadn’t yet. Fight was likely trying to take the Sunbird, but since he was alone, he wouldn’t stand a chance trying to steal it.

“We’re coming. Go in, check the health bar, and get out. If push comes to shove, use your ultimate to escape,” said the captain, who had just respawned.

“Got it,” the top laner replied, plucking up his courage to approach the bird pit, practically hugging the walls as he crept in.

The fog hadn’t cleared yet, but he could already hear the sound of the Desert Hunter’s rapid-fire bullets through his headset.

When the fog cleared, sure enough, Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress was tanking damage up front, Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter was firing away behind him, and Tang Yuan’s Deepsea Faerie was standing guard nearby.

He checked the Sunbird’s health—it had only dropped by a fifth.

There was still a chance!

YJ’s top laner, Black-Armoured Scythe, confirmed the Sunbird’s health and turned to run.

Fight spotted him but knew he was just taking a quick look and had no intention of chasing.

Tang Yu’an urged, “Yu He, hurry! When the team fight comes, don’t rush in for the attack and keep your eyes on the bird!”

“On my way.” Yu He’s Double Phantom was already making its way over.

In the meantime, Dou Jiayan’s Phoenix Soul had also respawned. He set up flags as he caught up: “I’m going to redeem myself this time! I’ll make YJ remember the terror of being dominated by Phoenix Soul!”

Ling Suyu glanced at his ultimate, which had just come off cooldown. He silently saved his abilities, ready to start a killing spree at any moment!

Soon, players from both teams arrived at the bottom river.

Before that, Ling Suyu stopped attacking the bird and retreated from the pit, with Tang Yu’an following closely behind, leaving only Mo Xiaoyun in the pit to hold off the Divine Sunbird.

YJ’s side:

Cheng Cheng: “Top laner initiate! Kill the marksman first!”

Fight’s side:

Tang Yu’an: “Everyone save your Flash until Blade Soul uses his ultimate. Swallow, cast your skill directly where Feather is!”

“Got it!” Dou Jiayan didn’t even pause to think. After calculating the distance, he cast “Phoenix Feather” right where Ling Suyu was standing.

Almost simultaneously, the enemy top laner, Black-Armoured Scythe, swung his scythe at Ling Suyu and activated his ultimate, “Scythe Armour.”

The next second, Black-Armoured Scythe used its chain to teleport to Ling Suyu’s location, then immediately cast “Chains of Darkness” to try to immobilize him.

Ling Suyu countered with an “Explosive Shell” while deftly escaping.

Dou Jiayan chained his second and third skills, using “Rebirth from the Flames” followed by “Sky of Flaming Feathers”, dealing massive damage to Black-Armoured Scythe. The devastating damage shattered the shield granted by his ultimate and reduced his health to two-thirds.

At the same time, Tang Yu’an’s Deepsea Faerie cast “Patrol”, hitting Black-Armoured Scythe head-on and immobilizing him. He quickly followed up with “Water Lotus” and “Energy Drain”, and once the stacks were maxed out, he unleashed his ultimate!

Black-Armoured Scythe’s health was now down to one-third.

Ling Suyu didn’t even blink; he activated his ultimate and just stood there, firing away!

【System】Desert Hunter, one kill!

【System】The enemy Black-Armoured Scythe has been slain!

YJ’s top laner: Huh?

He, a tank with high health and defense, went from full health to zero in the blink of an eye?

Before he died, or in other words the moment Ling Suyu activated his ultimate, the enemy marksman and support directly flashed over and began dishing out damage.

Ma Mingxi’s Guardian Spirit’s ultimate finally hit at the perfect moment. In an instant, a massive tree burst from the ground, and countless vines lashed out in all directions, trapping Ling Suyu, Tang Yu’an, and Dou Jiayan within its range.

Ling Suyu didn’t dodge this ultimate, but Tang Yu’an activated his shield in time to absorb some of the damage for the entire team.

Then, the very moment the enemy Black-Armoured Scythe fell, Ling Suyu used “Hound’s Charge” to leap right over Ma Mingxi’s head!

Ma Mingxi’s Entangling Vines missed their mark. Fortunately, he’d learned his lesson and didn’t immediately follow up with Arrows of Light. Instead, he waited for Ling Suyu to use up his movement skills before firing.

Then, with a simple strand of positioning, Ling Suyu evaded it.

The most perfect evasion often requires the simplest maneuver.

Ma Mingxi cursed under his breath, then retreated behind his support while trading blows with Ling Suyu.

After all, Ling Suyu had taken a hit from his ultimate; his health wasn’t doing great, his attack-speed was only halfway built, and he’d already used his ultimate. Trading blows with him at this point didn’t give him much of an advantage.

But Fight’s team wasn’t just composed of Ling Suyu.

The hero Phoenix Soul immediately refreshed her first ability after securing a kill or assist.

And as long as she had her first ability, and as long as its on-field cooldown hadn’t expired, she could use her second ability.

Only her third skill was still on cooldown.

Dou Jiayan chained his first and second skills together, instantly turning the area where Ma Mingxi and his support were standing into a sea of fire, causing their health to instantly drop.

Then Ling Suyu landed a few more auto-attacks and easily secured the kill!

【System】Desert Hunter, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

The enemy support used Flash to escape. Almost simultaneously, the enemy mid laner arrived, then cast his second skill, “Windstorm”, unleashing a gale that swept Dou Jiayan away from the corpse of Ma Mingxi’s Guardian Spirit. Immediately after, he unleashed his ultimate, “Hurricane”!

This ultimate had a crowd control effect, but there was a delay before it took effect. Before he was caught, Dou Jiayan chained his first and second skills again, dodging the ultimate and dealing massive damage to Wind Guardian.

YJ’s mid laner didn’t panic, and he immediately cast “Wind Cage”.

This skill used a lock-on mechanism, so Dou Jiayan couldn’t dodge it, but he had his ultimate. He immediately activated the Phoenix Soul’s ultimate, “Soul Concealment”, just before the skill hit him.

“Heh—safe! Don’t think you can one-shot me a second time!” Dou Jiayan spun around triumphantly.

YJ’s mid laner’s lips twitched. He decisively locked onto Ling Suyu as his next target and cast “Wind Slash”, firing countless wind blades in his direction.

The skill had a wide area of effect. With both of Ling Suyu’s movement abilities on cooldown, he knew he couldn’t dodge it. Seizing the moment, he used “Heal”, using the restored health to absorb part of the damage, then immediately locked onto the enemy and started firing!

Wind Guardian had exhausted all his available skills and was out of options. All he could do was flee.

So, after using “Heal,” he fled with Ling Suyu and Tang Yu’an chasing behind him. Even Dou Jiayan, now transformed into a semi-transparent phoenix, was trailing slowly in pursuit.

The enemy Blade Soul, who had been waiting outside the wall for a chance to steal the Sunbird, noticed that the bird’s health hadn’t dropped for ages, and seeing the team outside the pit in such a sorry state, he couldn’t hold back any longer. He charged at Ling Suyu with a “Flash of a Blade”, followed by his first skill, “Blade Shadow Combo”.

He hadn’t used his ultimate yet, but Ling Suyu knew that if he didn’t dodge now, he was as good as dead, so he activated his Flash and instantly teleported behind Tang Yu’an.

YJ’s jungler Xiao Hong saw him use Flash and immediately used his own Flash to give chase, following up with his ultimate, “Nine-Segment Blade Flash”, determined to secure the kill!

However, before that could happen, Ling Suyu’s “Explosive Shell” had already refreshed. He immediately cast it, using the damage-dealing ability as a means of movement to dash out of the lock-on range as quickly as possible.

As a result, Blade Soul’s ultimate struck Tang Yu’an instead.

Tang Yu'an, who had been “sold out”, raised an eyebrow, casually used Flash to dodge the ultimate, and then, the moment Blade Soul was forced to land, threw a jellyfish, successfully locking him down!

Ling Suyu retaliated by charging back with “Hound's Charge”, unleashing a series of shots at Blade Soul!

【System】Desert Hunter, triple kill! Unstoppable!

【System】The enemy Blade Soul has been slain!

Almost simultaneously, Yu He, who had been crouching for so long his legs were nearly numb, activated his ultimate to accelerate and catch up to the fleeing enemy mid-laner, Wind Guardian, cleanly securing the kill before immediately dashing into the pit to use Smite!

【System】Double Phantom, one kill!

【System】The enemy Wind Guardian has been slain!

【System】Double Phantom has successfully conquered the Divine Sunbird!

The team fight ended with only the support from YJ’s side escaping. On Fight’s side, although everyone was low on health, the entire team survived!

Ling Suyu glanced at his health bar, which was barely in the triple digits, and felt a chill run down his spine.

That was close! If his Explosive Shell had refreshed just a moment later, Blade Soul would have killed him!

Tang Yu’an, whose health was only slightly higher than his, picked up the Divine Sunbird’s token and teased as he teleported back to base: “You’re pretty good at hiding behind me, aren’t you?”

Ling Suyu froze for a moment, belatedly realizing he’d drawn the full force of Blade Soul’s attack onto Tang Yu’an, and felt a bit embarrassed: “I…”

But before he could finish, Tang Yu’an cut him off: “It puts my mind at ease knowing my own marksman is such a pro at dodging. Remember, I’m your support. You can bait me all you want; I’d gladly die for you.”

Ling Suyu: “……”

“Damn!” Mo Xiaoyun couldn’t believe what he’d just heard. “Are you being serious? How’s Feather supposed to hide behind you after hearing you say something like that?”

“Why not?” Tang Yu’an retorted. “Feather just likes to hide in safe places. The fact that he hides behind me means that’s the safest spot, right, Feather?”

Ling Suyu didn’t know what to say.

Although that was indeed true.

But something about it just felt off.

However, Ling Suyu didn’t like to speak insincerely, and since it was Tang Yu’an who had asked, he didn’t want to pretend he hadn’t heard. So in the end, he simply gave a soft “Mm-hmm.”

“You hear that!” Tang Yu’an said triumphantly.

Mo Xiaoyun rolled his eyes. “Fine, forget I said anything.”

“Wow, I can practically see a peacock preening its feathers,” Dou Jiayan quipped, “Captain, be honest, are you in heat?”

“What nonsense are you talking about? I just wanted to tell Feather not to feel guilty when he baits me,” Tang Yu’an explained, then suddenly changed the subject. “Ma Mingxi said he’d kill Feather three times. Instead, he ended up getting killed by Feather three times—talk about ‘what goes around comes around’. Does he have some kind of ‘any flag he sets is bound to fall’ constitution or something?”

As Dou Jiayan was teasing Tang Yu’an about being in heat, Ling Suyu sensed something was off. But before he could think it through, his train of thought was interrupted by Tang Yu’an’s next words.

Huh? Had Ma Mingxi already been killed by him three times?

He didn’t even notice.

He wondered what kind of expression the other had right now?

Ma Mingxi’s current expression: ="= To put it simply: his eyebrows were furrowed so tightly they could crush a fly.

Why?

Because he just can’t kill that damned Feather!

It was bad enough that he can’t kill him himself, but even his teammates can’t kill him!

Before he could voice his frustration, the jungler, Xiao Hong, with a face just as grim as his own, said, “This isn’t working. We’re too spread out. We need to focus our fire and take down the marksman in one go!”

“His movement is too annoying!” the top laner said.

Normally, his Black-Armoured Scythe’s combo was a surefire way to initiate on marksmen.

As long as he could lock them down, the team fight was practically half-won.

But Desert Hunter was as slippery as an eel. If his reaction time was even a split-second too slow, he’d miss the grab.

He even suspected that the enemy knew he was targeting him before he even initiated the fight, firing “Explosive Shell” the very moment he used his ultimate to pull himself in.

But he had no proof, so it remained just a suspicion. He wouldn’t know for sure until the post-game analysis.

In any case, he felt that Fight’s reactions were too fast, so fast that he, the one initiating the fight, actually felt like he was the one being ganked.

He’d intended to catch the enemy off guard, but ended up being caught off guard himself. It was too bizarre.

“Was Fight always this strong?” Mid laner Gu Ruicong even began to doubt himself. “How did we used to play against Fight?”

Their composition wasn’t bad. In fact, it was more solid than the enemy’s. Yet they had lost not just one team fight but another, and it was a humiliating 4-for-0. It was simply absurd.

Faced with his question, the captain, who had been silent for a long time, finally spoke up again: “That’s exactly how we played against them. Fight’s mid laner is strong, but not very consistent. The core of their damage has always been their marksman, so when we faced them, we had to take down their marksman first.”

“And in the cases where we couldn’t?” the top laner asked, hitting the nail on the head.

“Then…” After a moment of silence, the captain replied with just four words: “We were done for.”

Fight secured the Sunbird. After recalling to replenish their health and buy items, everyone except the jungler Yu He pushed up to mid lane and took down the second mid tower.

Then Tang Yu'an and Ling Suyu strutted into the enemy jungle and picked up a lightning strike.

After picking up lightning strike and blue buff in his own jungle, Yu He pushed the bottom lane minions forward.

The rest of the team joined him at the bottom lane and together they took down the enemy bottom lane’s second tower.

After the enemy top laner respawned, he immediately used Teleport to rush to the top lane’s first tower, apparently hoping to defend it, but he was ultimately too late.

The enemy top lane’s first tower was instantly pushed down by their minions.

He cleared his wave and immediately rushed to the bottom lane, but he was once again too late.

The enemy’s last bottom tower also fell under Fight’s assault. YJ had no way to defend it.

“I’ll initiate this team fight,” said YJ’s mid laner, Wind Guardian. “As long as I can lock them down, if we win this team fight, we still have a chance to turn the tables!”

“Alright, let’s wait for Lin Hao to get here before we engage,” the captain agreed.

Just as YJ’s top laner, Black-Armoured Scythe, arrived at the bottom lane, YJ’s mid laner, Wind Guardian, locked onto Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress, cast “Wind Cage”, and immediately followed up with his ultimate!

Mo Xiaoyun was locked down. He managed to break free, but took only a single step before being caught by the ultimate again.

YJ seized the opportunity to launch a full-team assault! The marksman, protected by the support, forced his way in and activated his ultimate, while the top laner, mid laner, and jungler all locked onto Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter!

Ling Suyu: !

Realizing they were dead set on taking his head, Ling Suyu instinctively tried to flee, but just then…

“I’m not dead yet, so don’t run off! Yanzi, go for it! If we’re fast enough, we can turn the tables!” Tang Yu’an activated his shield and used “Patrol,” instantly immobilizing the enemy top and mid laners, while the enemy jungler blocked the attack with “Absolute Defense”.

“Here we go! Kill, kill, kill!” Dou Jiayan unleashed a three-skill combo. With a flash, the health of the three enemy heroes within his attack range dropped sharply!

Since Phoenix Soul’s ultimate dealt no damage, its base skills deal significantly more damage than those of other heroes.

It used to be even more powerful, enough to instantly kill an enemy with a single combo, but now it was only useful for harassing and finishing off low-health targets. Still, it was more than enough for team fights!

Ling Suyu glanced at both sides’ health bars and decisively concluded—he could kill!

After dodging several high-damage skills with “Explosive Shell” and “Hound’s Charge,” he ducked behind Tang Yu’an, closed his eyes, and fired away!

Of course, “closing his eyes” didn’t mean he actually shut them.

But to ensure his damage output, there were some attacks he saw but had to pretend not to see, forcing himself to tank them and trade health!

Tang Yu’an dodged some damage through positioning. After locking onto the enemy marksman, stacking up marks, and unleashing his ultimate, he too began to close his eyes and tank the damage.

Both players' health bars were plummeting, but in the end, the opponent's health bar gave out first.

【System】Desert Hunter, one kill!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

【System】Phoenix Soul, one kill!

【System】The enemy Wind Guardian has been slain!

【System】Phoenix Soul, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain!

【System】Blade Soul, one kill!

【System】Ally Deepsea Faerie has been slain!

The enemy marksman, support, and mid laner had been killed.

Tang Yu’an’s Deepsea Faerie had been killed, Dou Jiayan’s Phoenix Soul was on cooldown after using her ultimate to heal, and Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal was waiting to respawn. The only ones still in the fight were Ling Suyu and Yu He, but both were down to low health.

Facing the low-health Blade Soul, the equally low-health Ling Suyu dodged his “Flash of a Blade” with an “Explosive Shell”, then finished him off with a basic attack, avenging Tang Yu'an!

【System】Desert Hunter, one kill!

【System】The enemy Blade Soul has been slain!

With the enemy jungler Blade Soul down, YJ now had only their top laner Black-Armoured Scythe left alive.

Knowing full well that this team fight was unwinnable, he still gave it his all and charged at the low-health Ling Suyu!

With both his movement skills and Flash on cooldown, Ling Suyu could only watch helplessly as the other charged toward him. Aside from frantically auto-attacking, he was completely at a loss.

His pupils suddenly dilated as he realized he was about to die under this hero’s scythe.

Suddenly, Yu He’s Double Phantom used “Shape-Shift” to flash in front of him.

【System】Black-Armoured Scythe, one kill!

【System】Ally Double Phantom has been slain!

Ling Suyu froze, and while he was still processing the situation, Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress resurrected. Immediately after reviving, he used the Flash he hadn’t had time to use earlier to rush over, casting “Sea of Corpses” at Black-Armoured Scythe’s feet, slowing his movement speed.

Thus, Black-Armoured Scythe’s scythe never managed to touch Ling Suyu.

【System】Desert Hunter, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Black-Armoured Scythe has been slain!

【System】The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us!

Ling Suyu’s health began to recover from the double digits, quickly reaching the triple digits, but he was still hanging on by a thread.

He looked up. Before him lay the “corpse” of Yu He’s Double Phantom, and further ahead, the “corpse” of Tang Yu’an’s Deepsea Faerie.

He had grown accustomed to Tang Yu’an’s desperate protection, but he never expected Yu He to…

Yu He’s teleport had a controllable range. He could have teleported behind Black-Armour Scythe to pursue it, yet he teleported right in front of him, clearly to take that hit for him.

A jungler risking his life to block a blow for him—this was a type of treatment Ling Suyu had never experienced before. It took him a while to react: “Thanks.”

“Huh? Are you talking to me?” Yu He raised an eyebrow. “No need. Your attack speed is faster than mine. I bought you time so you could avenge me. If you died first, I wouldn’t survive either.”

Ling Suyu blinked—was he really that rational?

“Just keep pretending. Are you pretending that Yanzi and Xiaoyun don’t exist?” Tang Yu’an called him out. “You just wanted an excuse to rest your hands.”

“Say what you like,” Yu He didn’t even bother to argue. “Anyway, only one of us, Feather or me, could’ve survived.”

Ling Suyu tilted his head, not quite understanding Yu He’s reasoning, but he was still grateful that he’d saved his life.

If it weren’t for him, he really would’ve died this time!

Actually, he’d wanted to run the moment the group of enemies charged straight toward him.

With two movement skills and a teleport, he could’ve easily escaped.

But Tang Yu’an, be it because he sensed something or otherwise, suddenly shouted, “I’m not dead yet, so don’t run off!”

He froze for a moment, and that split-second hesitation cost him the perfect chance to escape, forcing him to join the fight.

So the question was: How did Tang Yu’an know he was thinking of running?

He hadn’t even acted on it yet…

Tang Yu’an knew, of course.

Because, based on his understanding of Ling Suyu, if Ling Suyu intended to engage, given his reaction speed, he would have immediately activated his ultimate the moment the group of enemies charged in.

The fact that he didn’t activate his ultimate right away meant he was considering running.

It was only natural for him to have that thought; after all, if he didn’t run, he’d be easily overwhelmed and killed.

But…

“Feather, you need to have more confidence. Look at that exchange—sure, I died, but with all your mobility, you totally had a chance to survive!” Tang Yu'an suddenly said. “Isn't a close-quarters brawl kind of fun, too?”

Ling Suyu: “...”

Whether it was fun or not was another matter, but it was definitely thrilling.

If he had dodged just one less ability, if he had made even the slightest misstep in his positioning, no matter how insignificant, he’d be dead right now.

Ling Suyu was still trying to figure out how to respond to Tang Yu’an’s comment.

But as soon as Tang Yu’an finished speaking, he started typing a chat message without waiting for his reply.

【Ally】Fight-Yuan (Deepsea Faerie): “Allow me to interview Mr. Ximing Ma from the

opposing team. How are you feeling right now?”

Seeing the words “Ximing Ma,” Ling Suyu couldn’t help but burst out laughing.

Dou Jiayan burst out laughing even more dramatically: “Hahaha, ‘Ximing Ma’ is crazy! It sounds like a horse breed! It fits perfectly!”

By now, he had already “revived”. Together with Ling Suyu and Mo Xiaoyun, who were both low on health, he pushed their minion wave towards the enemy’s main tower and began attacking it.

Upon seeing this message in the chat window, Ma Mingxi rolled his eyes and slumped back in his gaming chair, refusing to engage with the enemy team.

Xiao Hong voiced his thoughts for him: “I just don’t get it. Why can’t we kill Feather? What’s the deal? Is he blessed by the God of Immortality or something?”

“It’s obvious that Fight’s strategy is for the whole team to protect the marksman,” the captain pointed out. “This has always been Fight’s strategy—it didn’t start after Feather joined. But originally, their marksman was pretty much being passively protected by his teammates.

Now, Feather has strong escape abilities himself. To take him down, you not only have to get past his teammates but also keep up with his reaction speed. It’s definitely pretty tough.”

“And then they picked Phoenix Soul, who’s even harder to take down,” the mid laner sighed.

“Both of their carries are each tougher to kill than the other. How are we supposed to play against this?”

“Looks like we have no choice,” said team captain Cheng Cheng. “Either we don’t let them take the version-meta mid laner, or we ban Desert Hunter.”

“F*ck?”

Upon hearing the words “ban Desert Hunter,” the rest of the YJ members looked utterly incredulous.

Banning a hero for the sake of a specific player was common in professional matches, but banning a bottom-tier hero, one who was practically useless if he didn't get enough gold, just for Feather?

Was that really necessary?

As if reading his teammates’ minds, YJ’s captain, Cheng Cheng, paused for a moment before saying, “After this game, you guys aren’t still going to treat Feather as UH’s substitute, are you?”
