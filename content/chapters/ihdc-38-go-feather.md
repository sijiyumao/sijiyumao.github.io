Desert Hunter?

The four teams grouped with Fight all had the same immediate reaction the moment they saw this champion: they felt they’d been underestimated.

Only a few of the overpowered mid laners from the previous season had been nerfed this time around, and the nerfs on auto-attack damage hadn’t been rolled back yet. Was a marksman who relied on basic attack damage even viable?

Even though it was the qualifiers and a BO1 situation, it was still being livestreamed.

The commentators for today’s broadcast happened to be the same duo who casted the White Fox Cup matches—Ah-Mu and ZhiZhi.

They had just finished commentating on the match between UH and an obscure underdog team when they switched the broadcast feed to Fight’s side.

“Next up, let’s check out Fight’s match! Their opponent this time is LR! Both teams have finished their bans, and they’re almost done picking their champions… Huh?”

Ah-Mu realized halfway through his commentary that Fight had locked Desert Hunter and couldn’t help but let out a gasp: 【Desert Hunter? Is Fight really going with Desert Hunter?】

“Fight’s starting marksman right now is Feather,” ZhiZhi added. “Feather’s picking Desert Hunter in this game? Are they trying to hide their true strength?”

Indeed, Fight had specifically avoided picking Cat Spirit, who happened to be another unpopular marksman, precisely to conceal their true capabilities.

However, they were serious about picking Desert Hunter.

At that moment, the other top 8 teams were also giving it their all on their respective battlefields.

But while their coaches were watching their own matches, they couldn’t help but keep an eye on Fight as well.

After all, with a new starting marksman, Fight now felt like a half-new team. No one had any idea what kind of lineup they’d field or what style they’d play. For the other previous quarterfinalists, this was an unknown threat.

Then, under their watchful eyes, Fight locked in Desert Hunter.

Huh?

Even if they’re trying to hide their strength in the qualifiers, wasn’t picking Desert Hunter way too risky?

What was Fight thinking?

However, UH, who knew Feather well, along with SMG and YJ, who’d been soundly beaten by Feather before, weren’t the least bit surprised to see Desert Hunter.

It was just that their respective reactions were worlds apart.

UH: “Feather is still the same old Feather.”

“Fight actually let him use that thing?”

“It’s the qualifiers, so Feather can probably pull off Desert Hunter. Let’s see if he dares to keep using it in the group stages.”

S.MG: “F*ck, seeing Cat Spirit and Dark Raven at that time, we already couldn’t help but wonder if Desert Hunter was one of his picks too—and sure enough!”

“Good thing we’ve analyzed all the sustained-damage marksmen. Anyway, just don’t let Fight’s bot lane gain an early advantage!”

“We’re not the same team we were on New Year’s Day! Bring it on, Fight, we’re not afraid of you!”

YJ: “Desert Hunter right off the bat? Should we offer a moment of silence for Fight’s opponents in advance?”

“Fight is ruthless, going all-in with their strongest lineup right from the start. Aren’t they planning to stay their hand?”

“Good thing we scheduled a practice match with Fight. Otherwise, we’d probably still be wondering why they’d be so reckless as to pick Hunter.”

Then, under the puzzled gazes of the commentators and other teams, UH’s calm stare, and SMG and YJ’s pitying glances, Fight’s bot lane struck with lightning speed to secure kills and widen the gold gap. They steamrolled their way through the game, clinching victory in the blink of an eye!

Forced by Desert Hunter’s pressure, LR had no choice but to turn their attention to Feather.

Not only did they fail to take him down, but they were wiped out several times by Fight’s mid laner, Dou Jiayan, who landed his ultimate.

SMG could only shake their heads—as those who’d been there before will tell you, ignoring Fight’s Swallow was a terrifying mistake.

LR’s top laner and jungler both had dash abilities, but let alone killing Desert Hunter, they struggled to even land a single hit on him. Either he’d dodge with his dash, or Tang Yu’an’s crowd control would interrupt their attacks, or Tang Yu’an would simply tank the damage for him.

YJ sighed repeatedly—congratulations, you’ve now experienced the “thrill” of being led around in circles by Desert Hunter.

After being wiped out by Fight twice in a row, the entire LR team developed a deep-seated fear of Desert Hunter. When the jungler was solo laning and encountered Desert Hunter, he didn’t even dare to engage. He’d instantly dash away, terrified that a split-second delay would mean meeting his end at Desert Hunter’s rifle.

During team fights, marksmen were supposed to prioritize taking out the enemy marksman, but they’d rather engage Fight’s tanky top laner with his thick health and high defense than face off against Feather.

In the end, LR was swept away by Fight in a single, decisive push. To describe their performance throughout the entire match as “utterly helpless” would be spot on.

Not just LR, even the commentators were left speechless.

Ah-Mu: “Desert Hunter delivered devastating damage with the aura of an absolute champion! Faced with such a powerful offensive, LR was completely helpless to defend! A clean sweep! Alright, Fight has won! Fight’s Desert Hunter carried the entire game! That’s a true carry in every sense of the word!”

ZhiZhi: “Oh my god! That was absolutely breathtaking! It’s been ages since I’ve seen a marksman exert such overwhelming pressure! I could even feel LR’s desperation in the final moments!”

Ah-Mu: “Thank you, Feather, for bringing us such an exciting and thrilling match! Will this be Desert Hunter’s final moment of glory, or the beginning of a resurgence? I’m getting more and more excited for the rest of the Spring Seasonals!”

At this point, the other three teams grouped with Fight hadn’t yet realized the gravity of the situation.

After finishing their own matches and watching the replay of Fight vs. LR, their first reaction was—LR is so weak!

Their second reaction—We absolutely cannot let Fight’s bot lane gain an early-game gold lead!

However, while the ideal was beautiful, reality…

Fight’s second opponent in the qualifiers perfectly reenacted the nightmare LR had experienced, getting crushed without putting up a fight.

Feather used Desert Hunter in two consecutive games to secure an early-game gold lead and quickly clinch victory.

This utterly dominating performance sent everyone following him into a frenzy!

【Holy sh*t! That’s Feather?!】

【Feather’s Desert Hunter is way too strong!】

【Is that really Feather? It’s not another player with the same ID, is it?】

【Did you see the pre-match interview? It really is Feather!】

【So, how did UH manage to ruin such a hidden gem of a marksman and turn him

into that mess?】

In an instant, the internet was flooded with voices marveling at Feather. Not just fans of other teams, but even Fight’s own fans were blown away!

【Whoa! While I supported Feather joining Fight, I never imagined he’d be this

strong!】

【Suddenly, I feel like a total idiot for worrying about Fight all day yesterday. Keep it

up, Fight! Go all the way to the championship!】

【I apologize for ever doubting Feather. I underestimated him. He deserves to be

Fight’s center!】

Amid all this amazement, some level-headed people pointed out that this was just the qualifiers, and Fight’s opponents weren’t that strong.

But was everyone simply amazed by how strong Fight or Feather were?

No, they were marveling at a pearl that had spent four years as a substitute on UH, gathering dust all that time, and which, after being polished by Fight, was now, at this very moment, bursting into brilliant, dazzling light!

There was no story more beautiful or inspiring than this!

Feather’s fans even wrote this introduction in his personal super topic:

He once devoted himself wholeheartedly to UH, only to be rewarded with four years on the bench and four years of taking the blame.

That is, until Fight embraced him, wiped the mud from his body, and raised his hands holding the bow and arrow.

In that moment, sunlight streamed into his star-filled eyes, reflecting a radiance that pierced through all darkness!

He will finally secure that championship trophy, the symbol of victory, for Fight, claiming the glory that belongs to him and his team!

Go, Fight!

Go, Feather!

The first day of competition wrapped up smoothly. Fight played two matches, won both, and earned 2 points. With just one more point, they’d advance to the group stage, instantly easing the pressure.

But they had no time to celebrate; they were busy reviewing that day’s matches and studying their next opponent.

Meanwhile, their next opponent was also busy analyzing them.

Unfortunately, Fight had pushed through both matches today so quickly that, aside from calling it a “one-sided affair,” there was virtually nothing to analyze.

And since it was a BO1 match, there was no option to sacrifice the first game as a test run.

Ultimately, the team’s coach made this decision: “Don’t ban Desert Hunter. The jungler should focus on ganking the bot lane early on—just don’t let Fight’s bot lane farm up. The top laner and mid laner shouldn’t push too deep. The mid laner should take River Deity, and as soon as the minions reach the river, rush over to help the bot lane.”

“In short, don't give the Desert Hunter a chance to develop!”
