The match between UH and YJ continued. Although YJ had conquered the Dragon, their marksman and mid-laner had both been killed by Ling Suyu. Their top-laner, jungler, and support revived, but even after furiously attacking, they didn’t manage to take down a single one of UH’s towers.

But UH still lost this round.

After clearing three waves of minions, in order to get the Divine Sunbird in the lower river, both sides engaged in another team fight. But halfway through the fight, Ling Suyu’s Guardian Spirit suddenly flashed and escaped, and UH’s other members were completely wiped out. On the other hand, YJ didn’t lose a single hero.

Then YJ was able to conquer the Divine Sunbird without any obstacles. Once conquered, the Sunbird would fly under the enemy’s tower, tanking the damage dealt by the tower.

YJ used the Sunbird to demolish all of the towers on UH’s middle lane in one go, pushing all the way to UH’s base.

“Clear the minions!”

“Quick, the minions!”

His teammates' anxious shouts rang beside his ears, but Ling Suyu didn’t let Guardian Spirit take a single step out of the fountain, and instead let go of his mouse.

They couldn’t save the base now. If he stepped out, he would die, without a doubt.

【YJ’s momentum is unstoppable, and UH is powerless to counter! Nice! Let us congratulate YJ on achieving the first victory of the day!】

Although UH’s base hadn’t been fully destroyed yet, commentator AhMu had already announced this match’s result in advance Those who saw clearly could see that in that team fight, they had directly been defeated by UH. Unless 《Glorious Heroes》somehow bugged out, letting UH’s dead heroes all simultaneously revive on the spot, UH had no chance of winning at all.

Ling Suyu also told his teammates directly: “There’s no saving this round.”

But aside from him, no one else could accept this result.

Top-laner: “Why didn’t you help clear the minions?”

Mid-laner: “How would you know if you don’t try?”

Jungler: “As expected of you, Ling Suyu. I’d have to be stupid to trust you again!”

Support: “.....”

Ling Suyu pursed his lips and took off his headphones, allowing his ears a moment of peace, while softly saying a few words: “Let’s play well next round.”

However, his teammates’ rage did not dissipate in the next round. Their top-laner used all sorts of ways to cross into the enemy tower only to get killed, their mid-laner missed tons of skills, their jungler messed up their rhythm two consecutive times, dragging the support to their death as well. They messed up countless times, getting worse and worse.

Near the end, Ling Suyu couldn’t help but feel a bit nauseous.

These teammates of his were actually professional players?

They had been this way five years ago, and were still like this five years later.

Even though they could perform well, they’d show all sorts of problems whenever he went up to compete.

Truly, was he the problem?

As a marksman, as this team’s central position, the main damage dealer, wasn’t he supposed to value his own life?

How could you deal damage if you were dead?

With your eyes?

Or your will?

“UH lost…”

In Fight’s training room, Dou Jiayan finished watching these two matches between UH and YJ with complex feelings, not knowing what to say.

When he first realized his captain wanted to poach Feather over, he had hoped that UH would lose in order to stop his captain. That way, he could still compete together with Jie-ge.

But now that UH had actually lost, and lost two matches in a row, he was not only not happy, but also felt a bad taste in his mouth.

“Is UH doing this on purpose?” He even had such thoughts, “Did they take a bribe or something? This whole time, I’ve only seen Feather playing seriously.”

“Why would UH take money in a fight against YJ? Isn’t it normal for them to lose?” Two seats away, Tang Yu’an took off his headphones and yawned lazily. His tone made it sound as if he was talking about something incredibly mundane; those who didn’t know Chinese would think he was saying something along the lines of “Today’s weather is pretty nice, I should bring the clothes out to sun-dry.”

Though what he said was true. In the first place, UH tended to lose more than win to YJ.

Even without mentioning earlier competitions, in the Summer Seasonals this June, UH faced off against YJ in the group matches and lost 0-2. They struggled a lot and barely managed to get into the quarter-finals and, facing YJ again, once again lost 0-2.

YJ’s style of pressing the advantage was way too good at restraining UH.

You could say that with today’s match, even if Jiang Hao hadn’t been banned from competing, UH’s chances of losing were high.

By contrast, after their substitute was switched in, their aggressive early-game actually managed to stun YJ, even controlling them for a short while.

Unfortunately…

At this moment, the livestream was showing the personal battle records of both teams in the match just now.

The winner’s MVP was YJ’s Ma Mingzi, 11 kills, 3 deaths, 8 assists.

The loser’s MVP was UH’s Ling Suyu, 9 kills, 0 deaths, 2 assists.

And in the match before that, the victor’s MVP was YJ’s jungler Xiao Hong’s Tempest Blade, 7 kills, 2 deaths, 4 assists.

The loser’s MVP was UH’s Ling Suyu, 6 kills, 0 deaths, 1 assist.

A familiar 0 deaths, and a familiar loser’s MVP.

【UH appears to be underperforming today, but Feather had quite a few exemplary plays!】AhMu said.

【Looks like Jiang Hao’s ban caused quite a few issues for UH’s players!】ZhiZhi found the reason for UH’s subpar performance.

Tang Yu’an stared at the personal battle records on his screen while listening to the sounds coming from the headphones around his neck, lost in thought.

At this moment, Dou Jiayan rose and shuffled to his side, cautiously opened his mouth, and asked: “Um, captain, you…are you still planning to poach Feather?”

Upon hearing this question, Tang Yu’an raised his eyebrow, turning to look at him with an expression of interest: “What?”

Only to see the delicate 19-year-old youth scratch his head awkwardly: “I’ll take back what I said about you not caring about Jie-ge after getting a new marksman, so um, I think Feather’s pretty good, I wouldn’t be against it if you really want to bring him over.”

“I still haven’t decided.” Tang Yu’an replied casually, though he couldn’t stop the corner of his lips from raising.

Though his team’s mid-laner was a blabbermouth, and got up to a lot of nonsense, as if he would never grow up, he was loyal and faithful, kind and earnest.

Tang Yu’an knew as soon as he heard the other’s words that he felt bad for Feather, whose strength could carry four people but was still relegated to the position of substitute.

As for Feather…

“I’ll have to consider it a bit more.” Tang Yu’an said.

For these two matches of UH’s, aside from Feather, it was true that the others played extremely badly, and they even got worse as time went on.

Just looking at the battle records, Feather really was carrying the entire team with just his strength alone. Unfortunately, he couldn’t bring them to victory. But, was the situation actually like this?

Others may not be able to see the problem, but Tang Yu’an, as a professional support and the captain of a team, could see the problem at a glance—UH lacked a proper commander.

The problem may not seem glaring when laning one-on-one, but in a team fight, their top￾laner had no intention of protecting their rear, their mid-laner and jungler only paid attention to attacking the enemy heroes without realizing that they had drifted apart from the team, and their support played like a robot, following others around brainlessly and even losing sight of his own marksman.

Initially, when the first match had just begun, Tang Yu’an had thought to himself that with Feather’s strength, he shouldn’t be relegated to the position of substitute. What kind of grudges did he have with UH?

After watching two consecutive matches though, he ultimately realized why Feather had to be a substitute.

What UH needed was not a top-tier marksman, but a commander that could guide the team to victory.

Simply because this commander was Jiang Hao, simply because Jiang Hao played the marksman position, simply because Feather, who played the same position, didn’t have the ability to command.

That’s why UH’s marksman had to be Jiang Hao.

So, even though his skills weren’t bad, after Jiang Hao entered the team, Feather never again played an official match.

As such, you couldn’t say that Feather, as a player, was completely without issues.

Whether it was because he didn’t have the ability or didn’t have the desire to do so, either way, he wasn’t good at commanding. However, Tang Yu’an could tell that he had very clear thoughts of his own.

He knew when to attack and retreat, and when it came time to attack, he did so without hesitation. In an unfavourable situation, even if his teammates went, he wouldn’t follow, playing by his own rhythm from start to finish.

Regarding this type of player, put nicely, they had a strong personal style. Put negatively, they simply wouldn’t listen to commands.

Tang Yu’an couldn’t hear UH’s conversations, but he was willing to bet that Feather had definitely refused his captain’s commands before, and even got into conflict with his teammates. Otherwise, it would be difficult to explain why in the second match, his four other teammates played so wildly and messily, to the point where even someone who had never played MOBA games before would say “Even I can do that!”

If one wanted to bring a player with such a strong personal style into their team, then, in order to properly harness their strength, they couldn’t command them willy-nilly.

Of course, it’s not like his current commands were very random.

Rather, right now, he could completely control his team according to his own thoughts and plans, but if he wanted to bring Feather in, he would need to cooperate with the other, and give commands according to Feather’s thoughts and needs.

In other words, he wouldn’t be leading the rhythm, but rather entering into Feather’s rhythm.

This wasn’t difficult for a support, but wouldn’t be a small challenge for a commander.

And unfortunately, Tang Yu’an was Fight’s commander.

Thus, he needed to consider carefully before deciding whether or not to poach Feather into his team.

On the other side, Ling Suyu, who had just finished competing, walked expressionlessly into the washroom, before using cold water to wash his face.

He raised his head and looked at himself in the mirror, unable to stop his brows from furrowing.

His teammates’ words still echoed by his ears:

“Why didn’t you follow the team?!”

“What, you think you’re so precious? That you’re the only one who can’t die?”

“You can’t even compare to a single finger of Jiang Hao’s!”

Ling Suyu punched outwards, his fist lightly landing on the mirror.

Although he looked calm on the surface, various emotions brewed within him.

There was anger— how could his teammates play that poorly!

There was regret— why didn’t he have the courage to speak up and stop his teammates from engaging in fights that they couldn’t win in the first place?

There was unwillingness— he’d finally managed to compete in a match at long last, he wanted to win, really wanted to win, but why couldn’t he?

There was also questioning— was it really his fault they were unable to achieve victory? He never lost the rhythm, never missed his skills, so where did he go wrong?

He wanted to vent, but he couldn’t.

If he broke the competition venue’s mirror, he would have to pay for it.

And more importantly— he was afraid of pain.

So, his fist only lightly landed on the mirror, before he sighed, turning around and leaving the restroom.

But just as he left, he was blocked by another person.

It wasn’t the jungler Cui Rao, who said he would beat him up in the washroom, but rather…

UH’s manager.

“So this is where you were!” The manager grabbed Ling Suyu’s wrist, before realizing that his tone was probably too impatient. He coughed and pushed his glasses up before continuing, “That, how are your considerations regarding the contract renewal with UH going?”

Ling Suyu stared at him blankly, before finally reacting: “Uh, UH is still willing to renew my contract?”

And here he was thinking they couldn’t wait to kick him out…

“Look at what you’re saying.” The manager gave a friendly smile, “In UH, aside from Jiang Hao, you’re our best marksman. If you’re willing to renew your contract, then you’ll always be UH’s number one substitute for the marksman position.”

As soon as he heard the four words “UH’s number one substitute”, Ling Suyu’s gaze dimmed.

“If you think your fixed salary is too low, we can raise it. But as I’m sure you’re already aware, when it comes to e-sports, in the end, we’re looking to win, so…”

Regardless of whatever the manager said next, Ling Suyu was no longer listening.

He’d had enough of being a substitute.

He wanted to go onstage and compete, he wanted to participate, wanted to personally achieve victory.

So, even if he lost two consecutive matches today, even if he continued losing tomorrow, to the point where no team would ever want him, he still wanted to fight for it.

“No need.” Ling Suyu made the decision with great difficulty, breaking free of the manager’s grip, “I’m grateful that UH acknowledges my strength, but…I won’t renew my contract.”

“Why? Is it because those kids said some nonsense again?” The manager’s tone immediately grew panicked.

“No, this has nothing to do with them.” Ling Suyu said, pausing briefly, “UH isn’t suited for me, it’s as simple as that.”

After that, he turned and left, not waiting to see the manager’s reaction.
