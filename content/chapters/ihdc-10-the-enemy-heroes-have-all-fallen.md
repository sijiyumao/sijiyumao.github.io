Ling Suyu controlled Cat Spirit and followed the support Spider Queen into their side’s lower jungle.

Once Spider Queen had pulled the aggro of the purple dragon, he began to relaxedly ‘biu biu biu’~ After buying attack-speed gear, his damage and attack speed had both gone up. Spider Queen didn’t help him consume the dragon’s health; after pulling aggro, he simply played dead on the spot.

The purple dragon’s damage fully landed on Spider Queen, and the damage dealt to the purple dragon was all from Ling Suyu.

Cat Spirit’s basic attacks manifested as green, translucent phantom blades. After her attack speed was increased, the phantoms flashed out consecutively, appearing nimble and powerful.

The purple dragon’s health bar continued to decrease as if there was a leakage, and soon, it had been completely drained.

After getting the lightning strike, Ling Suyu used “Shadow Steps” and flashed back to the bottom lane. He destroyed the enemy’s first tower, also taking out another wave of minions along the way.

On the other hand, Tang Yu’an Spider Queen, a bit before the purple dragon was fully taken down, left ahead of time. He ran downstream towards the Divine Sunbird and, with two skills, began fighting the bird.

Soon, their mid-laner finished up the mid-lane’s minions and headed down, and their jungler also quickly arrived.

However, once they’d finished using their skills, their basic attack damage couldn’t do much against the Divine Sunbird.

That is, until Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit arrived and unleashed a flurry of attacks!

The Divine Sunbird’s health was quickly depleted, and the enemy heroes didn’t even get a chance to intervene.

【System】Cat Spirit has conquered the Divine Sunbird!

Once Ling Suyu was done with the Sunbird, he directly used Shadow Steps and went for the mid-lane’s towers.

Glowing with crimson flames, the Divine Sunbird’s main body disappeared after being conquered, leaving only a small batch of flames as a token.

Picking up the token allowed the player to, within a short timeframe, unleash the Sunbird to aid in destroying towers.

In regards to the token, as long as it was by someone from the team that conquered the Sunbird, anyone could pick it up; however, usually it was tacitly left for the most “idle”

member, the support.

Thus, after the Sunbird disappeared, Tang Yu’an conscientiously guided his Spider Queen towards the flames.

【System】Spider Queen has picked up the Divine Sunbird’s token!

After getting the token, he chased after his marksman while saying: “Everyone, gather towards the mid-lane and push the towers. Top-lane, you can leave your lane after bringing the minions over to the other side. Isn’t it boring being an orphan? Come down and prepare for a team fight.”

【Ally】can die but no need (Skeletal Empress): Okay~ At first, these solo queue’d teammates didn’t fully trust Tang Yu’an’s command abilities, and even said that his four assists were also thanks to Feather carrying. But now, they fully listened to Tang Yu’an’s directions.

Perhaps, before they’d even realized, they’d already been “tamed” by Tang Yu’an.

Ling Suyu, on the other hand, had a few thoughts about the situation.

While he was still in UH, upon close observation of the practice matches between UH and other teams, he’d found that among the starting players of UH, they all held an extreme amount of trust in Jiang Hao. Whatever Jiang Hao wanted them to do, they would do it, as if they’d been fully tamed by Jiang Hao.

Ling Suyu had never doubted his own technical skills, but he had to admit that Jiang Hao had a sort of charisma that he lacked, which resulted in others voluntarily following his lead.

Ling Suyu understood how important a good commander was for a team, but he himself didn’t have that ability.

As a result, rather than striving to become that type of person himself, he preferred to seek them out.

In leaving UH, he’d already decided: even if he could only be a substitute in another team, at the very least, he wanted a team where the commander wasn’t a marksman.

【System】The enemy’s tower has been destroyed!

The sudden system announcement shook Ling Suyu from his thoughts.

After the five players worked together to push down the mid-lane’s first tower, they pressed onwards with the remaining minions.

Once they got to the second tower, their remaining minions fell, and the enemy heroes had come to defend their tower.

“Stay calm, wait for me to initiate,” Tang Yu’an’s voice sounded from his headphones, less playful, taking on a more serious tone.

First, he unleashed the Sunbird from the rear, before immediately sending his Spider Queen to the frontlines.

As the Divine Sunbird landed on the enemy’s tower and began tanking the tower’s damage, he agilely dodged two skills from the enemy mid-laner’s skills before flashing under the tower. He quickly followed up with a “Spidersilk Trap”, accurately latching on to the mid￾laner.

“Now!” he said while using Spider Queen’s ultimate “Neurotoxin”, decreasing the movement speed of surrounding enemies while simultaneously raising the movement speed and attack speed of surrounding allies.

Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit entered the tower’s range without even needing to use Shadow Steps.

With the swiftest hand speed, he selected the enemy mid-laner, casting his first skill “Spirit Mark”. After three basic attacks, he followed up with his ultimate “Spiritual Explosion”!

Unlike Guardian Spirit’s ultimate, which caused AoE damage, Cat Spirit’s ultimate dealt single-target damage and could only be used against enemies tagged by a Spirit Mark.

However, if the damage dealt exceeded the target’s maximum health, it would deal spillover damage to other surrounding enemies.

The lightning strike’s effect on Cat Spirit had yet to dissipate, Spider Queen’s passive provided a further buff, and there was an additional damage buff from his own passive “Shadow Strike”. As such, before he’d even used his ultimate, the enemy mid-laner Deepsea Faerie’s health had already been depleted.

So, when Ling Suyu used his ultimate, Deepsea Faerie fell down on the spot, and at the same time, the nearby enemy top-laner and support were also dealt a massive amount of overflow damage!

【System】Cat Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Deepsea Faerie has been slain!

At this moment, their top-laner and mid-laner also entered the tower. With a “Heavy-Bone Hammer”, their top-laner Skeletal Empress knocked the enemy top-laner out cold, and their mid-laner Vampiric Progenitor used “Fetters of Blood” to hold back the enemy support.

Yet Ling Suyu paid no attention to the enemy top-laner and support. After killing the mid￾laner, he instantly rushed in front of the opposing marksman Guardian Spirit, selecting him and unleashing a frenzy of attacks!

And, as if he’d known that his next target would be the marksman, before he’d even started attacking, Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen had thrown a “Web of Constraint” onto the marksman!

Unlike the forceful control provided by “Spidersilk Trap”, “Web of Constraint” was a speed debuff that also consumed a certain amount of the enemy’s health.

Guardian Spirit did not have any built-in movement skills and, after trying to move around in the web with great difficulty, had a moment of realization and used his Flash.

Ling Suyu didn’t even hesitate, using his own Flash in pursuit and furiously attacking!

Guardian Spirit used its ultimate Wrath of the Spirits!

Ling Suyu used the talent Heal, tanking the hits under the effects of the damage reduction, expressionlessly continuing his assault!

【System】Cat Spirit, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

After Ling Suyu killed the marksman, only then did he turn around to finish off the top-laner and support. Simultaneously, he took note of the enemy jungler’s movements, ready to flee at any moment.

At that moment, Tang Yu’an spoke: “Beware, the enemy jungler’s nearby. Everyone leave the tower first.”

He said as he stepped out of the tower’s range.

By then, the Divine Sunbird had been depleted by the tower’s attacks, and the new line of minions hadn’t arrived yet. The tower had begun to randomly select “lucky” targets.

Ling Suyu had left the tower at pretty much the same time as the support, grabbing a kill along the way.

【System】Cat Spirit, triple kill! Rampage!

【System】The enemy Black-Armoured Scythe has been slain!

Their mid-laner had been slightly too slow. After using his first skill Bats of Blood to finish off the enemy support, he didn’t have enough time to use Flash to escape the tower’s damage and was thus killed by the tower, with the kill being counted towards the enemy top-laner.

【System】Vampiric Progenitor, one kill!

【System】The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain!

【System】Black-Armoured Scythe, one kill!

【System】Ally Vampiric Progenitor has been slain!

Tang Yu’an furrowed his brows, but didn’t say anything.

Their top-laner also retreated a step too late, but because their mid-laner had “helped” tank a hit from the tower, was able to escape alive.

At this moment, a “whoosh—” sounded.

He’d arrived, the enemy jungler Tempest Blade had arrived, riding on the wind!

Immediately following, as expected, was the ultimate “Shattered-Blade Whirlwind”!

Tang Yu’an decisively used his shield!

All their teammates, including himself, lit up with a golden shield.

Tempest Blade’s ultimate landed on the shield, the two cancelling each other out, and nothing happened.

Ling Suyu countered Tempest Blade with a Spirit Mark and his own attacks!

【System】Cat Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Tempest Blade has been slain!

【System】The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us!

Ling Suyu, seeing this system announcement, taking in the fact that his own health still remained at more than half while his support’s health was at a sliver, suddenly felt his heart rate uncontrollably quickening.

Of course, it wasn’t because he was moved by this support.

Well, actually, he couldn’t say he was completely unmoved.

But more importantly—he’d never participated in such a thrilling team fight before!

He’d actually wanted to retreat right after killing the enemy marksman.

The opposing jungler had yet to show themselves, he’d already used up his Flash, and his Shadow Steps were still on cooldown, meaning he was at risk of being instantly killed by the enemy jungler at any moment. In order to avoid such a situation, he needed to immediately run as far away as he could, and leave his teammates within the enemy’s field of vision to attract the fire.

Yet, as the thoughts crossed his mind, the support arrived by his side, and even reminded their teammates of the enemy jungler’s presence.

At that moment Ling Suyu suddenly remembered: it seemed the support had yet to use his shield?

In that split-second, he decided to stay.

In that split-second, he left his fate up to another person, in the hands of this support.

In front of his eyes, the enemy jungler flew over, and golden shields lit up the battlefield.

Finally, the enemy jungler was counter-killed, and the opposing heroes were completely wiped out!

Now, not only was Ling Suyu’s heart rate speeding up, he was overcome with a feeling of…

how could he describe it? Burning passion?

Yes, burning with passion!

It had been a long time since he’d experienced this feeling of excitement.

And that support, who’d practically orchestrated the entire situation, still stood there, speaking with a relaxed voice: “Tsk tsk, the Dragon God’s refreshed, but it’d be a bit awkward if anyone died. With our health levels it’d be better not to risk it. Just take down the second and return to the base to recuperate, and also buy some gear along the way.”

Pausing for a moment, it appeared as if he suddenly recalled something, giving a small “huh”.

Closely following, through his headphones, Ling Suyu seemed to hear a series of typing sounds, and he instinctively looked towards the game’s chat.

【Ally】tangyuan with sesame filling (Spider Queen): right, who was it just now that said they were going to kill Feather?

【Ally】tangyuan with sesame filling (Spider Queen): oh, looks like you were killed by Feather, that’s alright then.

Seeing these two messages, Ling Suyu couldn’t hold back his laughter: “You’re so mean.”

“You flatter me,” Tang Yu’an said with a smile, “As they say, great masters produce outstanding students, I’m just learning from you~”
