The second game between Fight and GQ had begun.

Coaches were only allowed to communicate with their players via headset during the ban/pick phase; once the match officially started, they had to remove their headsets.

Upon seeing the final lineups selected by both teams, GQ’s coach furrowed his brow. Before removing his headset, he just managed to shout, “Top and mid swap lanes!”

As a result, on Fight’s side, when Mo Xiaoyun’s Dark Angel arrived at the top lane, he didn’t see Black-Armoured Scythe—instead, he faced Deepsea Faerie.

Meanwhile, Dou Jiayan’s Vampire Progenitor encountered Black-Armoured Scythe in the mid lane.

Tang Yu’an wasn’t surprised at all: “Just as I expected.”

Scythe had a short range and can’t hold its own against Dark Angel, which had long range and high damage. However, as a mage, Deepsea Faerie could poke Dark Angel in lane, preventing itself from being pinned under the tower early on. With this lane swap, even if GQ’s top lane didn’t gain an advantage, it wouldn’t fall behind too quickly.

However, once Black-Armoured Scythe moved to mid lane, he wouldn’t fare well against Vampire Progenitor either, as the Vampire Progenitor could poke him.

Fortunately, he had more health than Dark Angel, so he could hold out for longer.

“Black-Armoured Scythe’s first item should be related to magic resistance,” Tang Yu’an said.

“Yanzi, don’t rush to push towers this game, just finish clearing your lane and come down to help.”

Upon hearing this, Dou Jiayan’s first reaction was: “Do you guys in the bot lane really need help?”

Fight’s bot lane: Candy Witch, a lane-dominator in the early game, and Skeletal Empress, a domineering support!

With this dominant duo, GQ’s Mobile Artillery and Abyssal Lord would be lucky if they were even left with a chance to survive, so did they really need help?

Tang Yu’an knew what he was thinking and couldn’t help but chuckle: “Just come down when I tell you to. Why all the nonsense? And don’t forget, Blade Soul is the jungler this game.”

GQ’s only advantage in this game is probably that Blade Soul, their jungler, wasn’t as easy to kill.

A level 6 Blade Soul could solo kill the Divine Sunbird.

But a level 6 Double Phantom would get utterly crushed if he tried to take on the Divine Sunbird alone.

“True. Let’s help Yumao level up sooner so we can end the game faster,” said Dou Jiayan.

“I’ll find a chance to come down, wait for me!”

So, just four minutes into the game, while Black-Armoured Scythe was clearing minions under the tower, Dou Jiayan rushed down to the bottom lane.

At the same time, Yu He, who was jungling in the bottom jungle, was also ready to gank; after finishing his jungle route, he went to lurk behind the enemy’s first bottom tower.

At that moment, Ling Suyu and Tang Yuan were pushing the two enemy bottom-lane heroes back against their tower.

When Dou Jiayan arrived, he cast “Bats of Blood.” The dark red bats flew past the enemy marksman and support, instantly chipping away a layer of their health.

Once he confirmed Yu He was ready, Tang Yu’an immediately used Flash followed by “Heavy Bone Hammer,” landing a jump-hammer combo and pushing into the tower!

The female warrior, her body entirely covered in bone armor, materialized out of thin air directly above the enemy marksman and support, and immediately brought her massive bone hammer crashing down!

With a “BOOM,” the enemy marksman and support were both sent flying into the air, and Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch followed up with a “Witch’s Clone” and a flurry of auto-attacks!

Dou Jiayan followed right behind him, using “Fetters of Blood” to precisely immobilize the enemy marksman’s Mobile Artillery!

Yu He’s Double Phantom also appeared behind the Mobile Artillery with “Shadow Displacement,” unleashing “Double Stab”!

Thanks to Fight’s well-coordinated, powerful damage output, Mobile Artillery’s health was nearly wiped out in an instant, causing it to enter self-repair mode.

The enemy support, Abyssal Lord, immediately activated his shield upon landing in an attempt to protect his marksman, but Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch shattered the shield with a single basic attack.

At that moment, GQ’s jungler, Blade Soul, arrived. He used “Flash of a Blade” to dash under the tower, blocked Dou Jiayan’s AoE damage with “Absolute Defense,” then unleashed “Blade Shadow Combo” on Double Phantom, who was nearest!

“Yu He, come over here!” Tang Yu’an commanded while taking tower damage and casting “Sea of Corpses.”

Yu He had been hesitating, but upon hearing the order, he immediately used Flash to escape behind Tang Yu’an’s Skeletal Empress.

Meanwhile, Ling Suyu had already dealt several consecutive auto-attacks, depleting the enemy marksman’s health bar—which had been fully restored by his self-healing mode.

【System】Candy Witch, first blood!

【System】The enemy Mobile Artillery has been slain!

“Alright, let’s get out of the tower!” Tang Yu’an said. After everyone had exited the tower, he stepped out himself, his health down to a sliver.

Blade Soul looked at his single sliver of health, hesitated for a long moment, but ultimately decided against engaging and instead began clearing minions under the tower.

“Fight’s mid laner rushed to the bottom lane, the jungler ambushed from behind, the support used his hammer to initiate the fight, and everyone followed up with damage—they took down GQ’s marksman in the blink of an eye!” Liang Fan commented, “Fight’s execution was lightning-fast! And so clean!”

“Fight’s ganks are always so crisp and decisive, such a pleasure to watch!” Remi added, “They gank fast and retreat just as fast. They got the kill on the marksman and pulled back immediately, without lingering!”

Liang Fan: “Actually, if they hadn’t retreated, they could’ve secured the support too, and even had a chance to take Blade Soul as well, but doing that would’ve likely cost Fight a few team members.”

Remi: “By the way, Fight’s support took tower damage and is down to a sliver of health— why didn’t GQ’s Blade Soul finish him off?”

Liang Fan: “Skeletal Empress’ passive is still active, and GQ’s support is already on low health. If Blade Soul went in, it would be one against four. Not only would he fail to kill Skeletal Empress, but he’d also end up dying himself. Still, having to watch the Skeletal Empress slip away right under his nose with just a sliver of health left—I bet GQ’s jungler is feeling pretty lousy right now.”

You bet.

GQ’s jungler, Blade Soul, watched as the opposing Skeletal Empress teleported back right under his nose, and he was so furious he nearly crushed his mouse.

Actually, if Skeletal Empress were a top laner, even if charging in would only trigger her passive without securing the kill, Blade Soul would still go for it.

But Skeletal Empress happened to be a support, and giving up his own kill just to trigger a support’s passive wasn’t worth it at all.

So, after securing the enemy marksman, Fight left satisfied. Some returned to base to replenish their health, some went back to their lanes to clear minions, and some headed back to the jungle to farm, as if nothing had happened.

However, this gank had clearly widened the gold gap between the two teams, especially for GQ’s marksman, whose gold was only slightly higher than their support’s.

At the 7-minute mark, Dou Jiayan’s Vampire Progenitor came to assist the bottom lane once again.

This time, the enemy top laner, Black-Armoured Scythe, followed suit.

The two teams clashed in a team fight on the bottom lane. Once again, Tang Yu’an’s Skeletal Empress initiated the fight by diving the tower, and everyone followed up with damage. Under the sustained damage and protection of her teammates, Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch secured a triple kill!

The enemy jungler, Blade Soul, used up his ultimate without securing a single kill and teleported away toward the second tower. Yu He used a dash to chase him down and claim the kill.

But immediately afterward, Yu He, already low on health, was killed by the tower.

“1-for-4, we profited!”

No sooner had Tang Yu’an finished saying that than— 【System】Dark Angel, one kill!

【System】The enemy Deepsea Faerie has been slain!

【System】The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us!

Dou Jiayan: “Damn!”

Yu He: “Not bad.”

Tang Yu’an: “Nice going—”

Ling Suyu: “Amazing!”

While they were team-fighting in the bottom lane, the enemy mid laner was forced to go to the mid lane to help Black-Armoured Scythe clear the wave. Mo Xiaoyun’s Dark Angel also ignored his minion wave and followed him to the mid lane, before using the minion wave advantage to push into the tower and activate his ultimate, solo-killing Deepsea Faerie!

Hearing his teammates’ generous praise, Mo Xiaoyun’s lips curled slightly: “Just basic stuff.

Sit down, everyone, sit down.”

“Fight pulls off a 1-for-4 trade in the bot lane! Dark Angel in the mid lane secures a 1v1 kill on Deepsea Faerie! The game’s only at the 8-minute mark, and GQ has already suffered a team wipe!” Liang Fan commented.

“That’s brutal!” Remi couldn’t help but exclaim. “GQ’s composition already struggles in the early laning phase. Now that Fight has secured such a massive advantage, how are they supposed to play from here?”

“With GQ’s comp, if they can somehow manage to regain their footing, they still have a chance in the late game,” Liang Fan said. “But whether they can turn things around depends on whether Fight will give them that chance. And this season, once Fight gains an advantage, they play with relentless ferocity. I think GQ’s chances of winning this game are very slim.”

“That’s right. Before starting today’s commentary, I watched a few of Fight’s matches from earlier in the season—they’ve been absolutely brutal! Even if other teams gain an economic advantage, they don’t necessarily have the upper hand in team fights. But once Fight secures an economic lead, it’s almost impossible for them to lose a team fight. It’s mainly because their carry, Feather, deals such massive damage that the opposing team simply can’t withstand it!” Remi pointed out.

“Feather’s damage output is truly devastating!” Liang Fan agreed. “Fight is a team whose core strategy revolves around a ‘four-protect-one’ setup. Whether they win or lose basically depends on whether they can protect their marksman and let him farm up. That’s why Fight is so strong in the late game. To beat them, you have to dominate the early game, but once Fight secures an early lead, there’s virtually no chance of a comeback!”

As if to prove Liang Fan right, GQ was powerless to stop Fight afterward, whether they were securing the Divine Sunbird or farming in GQ’s jungle.

Even when all five GQ players were grouped together and they spotted Fight’s heroes in their own jungle, they didn’t dare to engage.

Instead, Fight’s support, Tang Yu’an’s Skeletal Empress, initiated the team fight first, and the entire team followed up with damage, leaving GQ completely helpless!

Even though they knew they couldn’t win this team fight, GQ’s other heroes who had a chance to escape were forced to stay in the fray because their teammates were trapped, so they gritted their teeth and fought on. In the end, they only managed to kill Fight’s jungler.

This was only because Yu He, seeing Dou Jiayan down to low health, proactively dashed over to block an attack from the enemy marksman for him.

【System】All our heroes have fallen! Darkness has engulfed us.

—GQ’s players had lost count of how many times they’d seen this system message; right

now, they truly felt as if they were being swallowed by the abyss.

They watched helplessly as Fight pushed the minion wave all the way to their main tower.

They watched helplessly as Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch landed basic after basic, mercilessly dismantling their main tower.

They watched helplessly as their main tower’s health plummeted at a frantic pace.

Finally, the word “LOSE” appeared on their screens, signaling the end of the match.

One by one, they sighed and hung their heads, unsure whether they were frustrated by the loss or relieved—relieved that the torment was finally over.

The most agonizing part of esports wasn’t losing a match. It was playing a match you knew you couldn't win.

By the ten-minute mark, they’d realized there was no hope of a comeback, yet they had to keep playing, had to keep fighting, because this was a match. Even though they’d given up mentally, they didn’t want to truly give up physically; they were still fighting tooth and nail for that faint glimmer of hope, still clinging to the delusion of snatching a precious point back from Fight.

Unfortunately, just as they’d predicted in their pre-match analysis, even if the other members of Fight made mistakes, Fight’s carry wouldn’t give them a single moment to catch their breath.

Ling Suyu was exactly that kind of player—he could evade death with near-obsessive precision, and he could drive his opponents to their deaths with the same intensity.

One by one, GQ’s players took off their headsets and sat in their seats, letting out long sighs.

Then, someone—no one knew who—suddenly blurted out, “To bench a marksman like that —was UH out of their minds?”

“Alright! Fight has secured another decisive victory in Game 2!” Liang Fan exclaimed excitedly. “Let’s all congratulate Fight! And congratulations to them on advancing to the quarterfinals!”

Remi: “Both quarterfinalists in Group D, Fight and igame, have secured back-to-back 2-0 victories in the group stage, advancing once again with their overwhelming strength! I’m getting more and more excited for their matchup tomorrow!”

Liang Fan: “Since switching their marksman, Fight has shown unprecedented dominance in the Spring Seasonals so far! I wonder if they can maintain this momentum against igame— let us wait and see!”

The group stage was divided into four groups, with the top two teams from each group advancing to the quarterfinals.

Even though there was still a match against igame tomorrow, after consecutive wins over OsO and GQ, Fight’s spot in the quarterfinals was already a foregone conclusion.

Tomorrow’s match against igame might as well be treated as a practice game.

That said, Fight still aimed to secure another victory against igame. After all, their goal wasn’t just the quarterfinals, but the championship!

Just like yesterday’s match against OsO, there was a post-game interview following today’s match against GQ.

This time, the host chose to interview Fight’s jungler, Yu He. On one hand, this was because his performance today was also outstanding—his play where he took damage for Dou Jiayan had the crowd buzzing. On the other hand, he was quite handsome. Although he wasn’t as striking as Ling Suyu or as eye-catching as Tang Yuan, his looks were very pleasing to the eye and clean-cut, the kind that grew on you the more you looked at him.

The host’s first question was why he had taken damage for Dou Jiayan. Yu He replied, “I didn’t think much of it. I saw he was about to die, so I just stepped in to block it.”

What he didn’t mention was that he wouldn’t hesitate to step in to shield not just Dou Jiayan, but any of his teammates, especially the damage-dealers when they were low on health.

However, since Ling Suyu was already quite cautious on his own, and with Tang Yu’an providing seamless protection, Yu He had far fewer opportunities to shield Ling Suyu than he did to shield Dou Jiayan.

After hearing his answer, the host broke into a warm, maternal smile. He looked a little bewildered, but didn’t think much of it.

Immediately afterward, the host asked the second question: “What do you think of your new marksman, Feather? You can talk about him both in-game and in real life.”

“In-game, he’s pretty strong,” Yu He replied. “In real life, he’s a bit shy. Oh, and he’s really innocent and easily taken advantage of. Basically, he’s the kind of person you just want to protect.”

Hearing this, the host’s eyes lit up, and he asked curiously, “So would you protect him?”

“Sure, but with the captain around, that’s not my job,” Yu He replied.

Host: !!

Yu He swore that his answer really wasn’t hinting at anything;. He was just telling the truth— he was the jungler, the captain was the support, and when it came to protecting the marksman, the captain charged to the front, while he only stepped in when Ling Suyu needed him.

However, after this post-match interview was released, the fan group for the Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu ship quietly gained another wave of followers.

Even a fan group for him and Dou Jiayan had been created—no one knew when or by whom —called “Swallow and Crane in Love.”

People who didn’t know the context saw this group and assumed someone had conducted a cross-species breeding experiment with a swallow and a crane, thinking to themselves, “Is that even possible? They’re not the right size for each other (bushi [^1])”; Meanwhile, just as Yu He was giving his winner’s interview, GQ was also giving their loser’s interview.

Yes, the losing team also got a post-match interview which was posted by the league officials on their official platform, though it certainly didn’t attract as much attention as the winner’s interview.

OsO’s post-match interview went virtually unnoticed.

But GQ’s post-match interview suddenly went viral.

The reason was that GQ’s captain, during the interview, very courageously challenged UH, the team Ling Suyu had once played for:

“My biggest takeaway from the match? I just can’t figure out why UH kept Feather on the bench for a full four years. Did UH lose their minds?”

The host never expected such a response. He was taken aback for a moment before quickly following up: “Are you saying that Feather is better than UH’s starting marksman?”

GQ’s captain didn’t even bat an eye as he blurted out, “Isn’t it obvious? What’s Feather’s skill hit-rate? What’s Jiang Hao’s skill hit-rate? The records are all there in the pro-league’s system. Just look at their error rates—anyone with eyes can see which of the two has better technique. I’ll be blunt: UH can’t beat the current Fight. I’m not afraid of being proven wrong.

If I am… then you’re all right, and I’ll just lie back and take the mockery.”

The host was so taken aback by the captain’s courage and candor that he couldn’t come up with a follow-up question for quite a while.

But before the host could ask, GQ’s captain concluded: “One last thing: I hope Fight wins the championship this season. Keep that momentum from when you swept us and continue winning all the way to the end!”

After this interview was released, the entire esports community was abuzz, and Ling Suyu, who had already been a hot topic of discussion, was suddenly thrust into the spotlight.

At the same time, the entirety of GQ also found themselves in the thick of the controversy.

Fans supporting UH and Jiang Hao lashed out at GQ, but they were quickly countered by GQ’s fans, as well as fans of Ling Suyu and Fight.

Even casual observers came to Ling Suyu’s defense:

【I don’t play games much, but Fight’s performance in the Spring Seasonals speaks

for itself. UH forced out a talented player, that’s a fact. What’s there to argue about?】

【You UH fans are so strange. If you really love UH, you should be criticizing UH’s

decision-making! If they can force Feather out, they can force other players out too.

Who knows, maybe the player you’re rooting for won’t even be on UH’s roster next season!】

【Forget it. I think everyone at UH is to blame for forcing Feather to be a

benchwarmer for four years and then terminating his contract in such a messy way.

This trash team should just fold already, I definitely don’t want them to win the championship and represent our country.】

【I can’t wait for these two teams to face off in the quarterfinals. Feather, you’ve got

to give it your all! Take UH down and avenge yourself!】

Faced with this overwhelming wave of public outrage, UH’s management had gone into full denial mode. UH’s coach even confiscated all the players’ phones in an attempt to cover up the incident and keep them focused on training.

As for Fight, who had just finished their match against GQ, although they’d face igame, another previous quarterfinalist, tomorrow, they could breathe a slight sigh of relief now that they’d successfully advanced to the quarterfinals.

After returning to their nearby temporary lodging following the match, Tang Yu’an allowed everyone to rest for two hours and do whatever they wanted.

Then he picked up his phone and came across the video.

Damn?

“GQ’s captain is so bold!” Dou Jiayan had obviously seen the GQ interview video as well and immediately ran over to Tang Yu’an to show it to him. “Even we wouldn’t dare say that Yumao is stronger than Jiang Hao!”

“What’s there to be afraid of?” Tang Yu’an retorted on the spot. “Yumao is just really strong, ten thousand times stronger than Jiang Hao!”

Ling Suyu, who was sitting right next to them, cleared his throat awkwardly upon hearing this and muttered, “Don’t be like that. Jiang Hao is still pretty strong…”

“Strong my *ss.” Tang Yu’an turned to look at him. “Let’s not even talk about anything else.

Just in terms of technique, is he better than you?”

Ling Suyu fell silent.

Tang Yu’an blinked and pressed further, “Is he?”

Ling Suyu finally steeled himself and ground out two words through clenched teeth: “He isn’t.”

When it came purely to skill, Ling Suyu didn’t think he’d lose to anyone.

“That’s the spirit! Now let’s talk about his leadership skills, his ability as a captain. Is he better than me?” Tang Yu’an pressed on.

“No.” This time, Ling Suyu’s response was particularly decisive. It practically slipped out before he could stop himself.

“So he’s not a damn thing!” Tang Yu’an concluded. “He’s just a little kid compared to us!”

Ling Suyu thought for a moment.

Jiang Hao was one person, and they were two. Wasn’t it a bit unfair to compare the two of them to just one person?

In the end though, Ling Suyu only thought this to himself and didn’t voice it.

Nevermind—the captain is always right!

“Mm-hmm,” Ling Suyu nodded in agreement. “We’re way better than him!”

Hearing his response, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but smile and reached out to ruffle his hair.

He’s just too obedient. How can he be this well-behaved…?

Tang Yu’an suddenly couldn’t bear to tease Ling Suyu anymore, so he softened his voice and added earnestly, “Setting Jiang Hao’s individual skills aside for now, Glorious is a team-based game. In terms of team strength, there’s no way the current Fight will lose to UH.

People online only believe what they want to believe, so just take what they say with a grain of salt. We’ll prove to them with our performance that those who support us are right, and those who bash us are nothing but clowns.”

“Mm-hmm!” Ling Suyu agreed, finding Tang Yu’an’s words particularly convincing—after all, he felt the same way. “We just need to prove ourselves through our skills.”

So when Chen Jie stepped out of his room into the living room, laptop in hand, this was the scene he witnessed— Tang Yu’an was smiling gently at Ling Suyu, who looked up at him with a face full of joy and trust, and even a hint of admiration.

Chen Jie: “……”

What happened to “competition first, personal feelings second”? I was gone for just a little while, and you two are already at it again, aren’t you?

But…nevermind. As long as the two of you are happy.
