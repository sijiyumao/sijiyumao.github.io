“It’s fine, I can take him down!”

At Mo Xiaoyun’s words, Tang Yu’an checked both teams’ levels and gold with a swift glance, but didn’t say anything.

Ling Suyu raised an eyebrow.

After spending a few days together, Ling Suyu realized that although Tang Yu’an was gentle with everyone on Fight—and he didn’t mean gentle in speech, but rather that, as captain, he never acted like a captain around his teammates and could joke and play around with anyone in a friendly manner— As gentle and easygoing as Tang Yu’an is, he wouldn’t indulge anyone when it came to critical decisions.

So, if he judged that Mo Xiaoyun couldn’t pull it off, he would definitely stop him.

He’d say it in a casual tone that belied his firm resolve: “You can’t kill a damn thing—get out of there!”

Since he didn’t say that, that meant…

Mo Xiaoyun really could pull off the kill?

At this point, Mo Xiaoyun had just finished farming a wave of minions and reached level 6, while the enemy marksman and support were already at level 7, and the jungler was at level 6.

However, Mo Xiaoyun had just bought some items before rushing back to his lane, and the enemy marksman and support hadn’t returned to base after reaching level 7. So, although there was a significant gold gap between the two sides, the difference in item levels wasn’t that great.

A team fight between GQ and Fight’s top laner was about to erupt!

Just as when Fight ganked their top laner, GQ’s support was the first to push into the tower.

As he entered, he cast “Mandara Wreath” to immobilize Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress!

“Mandara Wreath” was a crowd control ability that dropped a wreath on the ground; anyone who touched the wreath was immobilized. However, as long as a player doesn’t touch the wreath, even if they were trapped in the center of it, they’d merely be unable to leave the area and wouldn't actually be CC’d.

The wreath trapped Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress in the center, but didn’t actually touch him.

Unrestricted, Mo Xiaoyun countered with his ultimate, latching onto the enemy support Mandara Vines!

Skeletal Empress’ ultimate, “Blood Link,” bound itself to a single enemy target, dealing sustained damage and providing lifesteal.

The bound enemy support was suddenly caught between a rock and a hard place.

Advance—he’d been locked on by the Skeletal Empress’ ultimate, and his health was dropping rapidly. At this rate, he simply couldn’t withstand the tower damage, and Skeletal Empress, who was keeping herself alive by virtue of himself, had become even harder to kill.

Retreat—his teammates had already followed his lead and pushed into the tower. If he pulled back now, the tower would immediately attack his teammates. But how could squishy marksmen and assassins with low health and armor possibly withstand the tower’s fire?

Seeing this, the commentators were also holding their breath for GQ.

Liang Fan: “Fight’s top laner isn’t fully CC’d! GQ’s support’s been caught in the link! This is a tough play to pull off!”

Remi: “GQ’s marksman and jungler still pushed in to deal damage. Can they secure the kill?

It feels a bit risky!”

After GQ’s support pushed into the tower, GQ’s marksman, Guardian Spirit, instantly teleported in to deal damage.

His first skill, Entangling Vines, hit! Skeletal Empress was finally CC’d!

Second skill, Arrows of Light, hit! Skeletal Empress’ health bar dropped noticeably, but thanks to the defense provided by her items and the lifesteal effect from her ultimate, the damage wasn’t too severe.

Ultimate, Spirit’s Wrath—hit! Skeletal Empress’ health bar finally showed a noticeable drop.

Almost simultaneously, the enemy jungler, Double Phantom, activated his ultimate “Phantom Transformation” to leap over the wall. While still some distance from the Skeletal Empress, he used “Shadow Displacement” to teleport behind her, then followed up with “Double Stab”

and basic attacks, dealing burst damage!

Under the combined assault of the two attackers and the support’s sustained damage, Mo Xiaoyun, a tank with high health and defense, went from full health to critically low in the span of just one second.

But he didn’t die.

Just then, the control effect from the Guardian Spirit wore off, and Mo Xiaoyun immediately countered with a “Heavy Bone Hammer”!

The female warrior, her body covered in bone armor, leaped into the air and brought her massive bone hammer crashing down!

With a thunderous “BOOM,” GQ’s support, marksman, and jungler were all sent flying into the air, temporarily incapacitated.

Mo Xiaoyun immediately followed up with his second skill, “Sea of Corpses,” spreading a layer of jagged bones beneath the tower before locking onto the enemy support and pounding away relentlessly!

Standing atop the bones, the Empress fiercely swung her bone hammer, striking Mandara Vines, who was connected to her via “Blood Link,” time and time again. Mandara Vines’

health rapidly dropped, while the Empress’ health quickly regenerated.

Once Mandara Vines hit the ground and regained mobility, he realized he had to get out from under the tower. Seizing the moment, he tapped “Flash” with lightning-fast reflexes, dodged the tower’s damage, and escaped alive, all while reminding his teammates to retreat.

To his utter surprise, Skeletal Empress immediately used “Flash” to chase him down, smashing a bone hammer onto his head and ruthlessly finishing him off while still on low health!

【System】Skeletal Empress, one kill!

【System】The enemy Mandara Vines has been slain!

After the enemy support left the tower, the turret attacked the jungler, who quickly used Flash to escape the tower and successfully dodged the tower’s damage.

But the tower immediately turned its fire on the marksman, who hadn’t yet left.

The enemy marksman, Guardian Spirit, had already used his Flash. He was forced to move out of the tower, but moving over the corpses placed by Skeletal Empress caused him to lose health. Even though he used Heal and had damage reduction, his health still dropped significantly.

Then, a turret attack struck, instantly wiping out half his health.

Guardian Spirit ultimately managed to escape the tower alive, but just as he stepped out, Skeletal Empress—who had just finished killing his fleeing support—blocked his path like a wall.

Knowing he couldn’t escape Skeletal Empress’s attacks, he had no choice but to auto-attack, trading health for health, in an attempt to secure a kill before dying.

Looking at their health bars, he had a quarter of his health remaining, while Skeletal Empress had less than a fifth left.

But looking at the actual numbers, his quarter of health was less than 500, whereas Skeletal Empress’s less than a fifth was closer to 1,000, at around 800 or 900.

He couldn’t kill.

He was screwed.

“Jungler!” He couldn’t help but call out to the jungler.

GQ’s jungler retreated from the tower and immediately circled around to his team’s marksman, but it was still a split second too late.

【System】Skeletal Empress, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

At this point, Skeletal Empress was down to a sliver of health. He’d used a Heal to barely stay alive and secure the kill on the enemy marksman, but he couldn’t hold on much longer.

When the enemy jungler, Double Phantom, finally arrived, he didn’t even need to use his Double Stab—a single slash was enough to finish off his low-health target.

But he hadn’t forgotten that Skeletal Empress still had a passive ability.

“Bone’s Rebirth”: After your health bar was depleted and you’d been down for 3 seconds, you’d revive in-place and regain 20% of your maximum health. Cooldown: 200 seconds.

GQ’s jungler thought he could take him down!

However, 3 seconds later, Mo Xiaoyun rose from the ground and delivered a hammer blow the moment he got back up.

“Heavy Bone Hammer” struck Double Phantom squarely, sending him flying into the air just as he was about to unleash his Double Stab combo. Immediately afterward, “Sea of Corpses” was cast, thoughtfully covering the spot where he fell with a layer of bones.

【System】Skeletal Empress, triple kill! Unstoppable!

【System】The enemy Double Phantom has been slain!

Having traded a passive ability for three kills, Mo Xiaoyun, with his health bar climbing from single digits, arrogantly teleported back to base right in front of GQ.

Dou Jiayan: “666666!!”

Yu He: “Awesome!”

Tang Yu’an: “Not bad—that play was a total profit!”

This time, even Ling Suyu, who rarely spoke during matches, couldn’t help but praise him:

“That was amazing!”

Hearing his teammates’ praise, Mo Xiaoyun felt happy inside, though his expression remained neutral.

But when he heard Ling Suyu’s compliment, he raised an eyebrow and touched his nose:

“It’s no big deal.”

Being praised by someone who rarely gave compliments actually made him feel a little happy.

Commentator Liang Fan usually had a deep, booming voice, but after witnessing Mo Xiaoyun’s incredible last-second counter-kill, his voice soared with excitement—it even cracked: “Fight’s top laner, Cloud, was surrounded by GQ while trailing in gold, but with just a sliver of health left, he pulled off a counter-kill and secured a triple kill!”

“That was amazing! Absolutely amazing!” For a moment, Remi couldn’t think of anything to say other than “amazing.”

Fortunately, Liang Fan still had his wits about him: “Let’s take a look at the replay! Cloud’s reaction time was incredibly fast on this play! After getting rooted, he immediately landed his ultimate on GQ’s support! That ultimate played a crucial role! Thanks to its lifesteal effect, Skeletal Empress managed to tank the damage from GQ’s marksman and jungler, and after breaking free from the crowd control, he targeted and killed GQ’s support, who was taking the tower’s damage!”

“Yes, Cloud’s reaction was lightning-fast!” Remi finally regained her composure. “It felt like Fight was mentally prepared for this gank! Just as they were able to take down GQ’s top laner this way, GQ could have done the same to Fight’s top laner. But Cloud didn’t choose to retreat; instead, he stayed under the tower—he must have been confident he could secure a counter-kill!”

Liang Fan: “ Fight’s game sense is simply breathtaking! Although Fight’s captain, Tang Yu’an, doesn’t have a very strong presence, but much of Fight’s predictions are thanks to him!”

Remi: “Following that nearly perfect gank and Cloud’s last-second counter-kill on the top laner, Fight has successfully reversed the economic gap! Will GQ crumble from this setback, or will they find another opportunity to regain the advantage? Let’s keep watching!”

After Mo Xiaoyun secured a triple kill with that last-second counter-kill, his gold count became the highest on the map, followed by Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter.

Once Mo Xiaoyun returned to base to replenish his health, Tang Yu’an ordered everyone to regroup at the mid lane, where they took down GQ’s mid lane first tower.

The enemy mid laner, Nine-Tailed Fox, landed a full skill combo on Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress but still failed to kill him.

Mo Xiaoyun led his entire team to push into the tower, with Ling Suyu following up to deal damage. With the efforts of the whole team, they secured the kill with ease.

Nine-Tailed Fox didn’t even have a chance to use her Flash before she was brutally crushed under the tower.

Fight was like a pack of hungry wolves, furiously pouncing and biting, leaving him absolutely no chance to escape.

After during that previous fight, GQ could still console themselves—it’s okay, at least we disabled Skeletal Empress’s passive, and we still have a chance to catch up in gold within three minutes— Now, with their own mid laner being ganked and killed under the tower by a pack of ravenous wolves, and their first mid lane tower brutally pushed down, a shadow of gloom inevitably fell over every GQ player’s face, and a heavy silence fell over the team voice chat.

Since they’d chosen to pick Dark Angel as their top laner, their plan was to dominate the early game.

As it turned out, not only did they fail to push down the enemy’s towers in the early game, but they even fell behind in gold. This meant that whether they could win in the late game no longer depended on them, but on how many mistakes Fight would make.

However, setting aside the rest of Fight’s roster, just look at their new recruit, Ling Suyu—his mistakes were alarmingly few!

Before any match, it was essential to study the opponent, and GQ had certainly done their homework on Fight. But over the past few days, most of their analysis had focused on Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter—and the conclusion they reached was this: his performance was scarily consistent, and his mistakes were truly few!

Even after UH released a public apology, many people online continued to mock Ling Suyu’s zero deaths, even giving him the nickname “Ghost Marksman.”

This wasn’t exactly a flattering nickname, because those who gave it to him intended to say that during team fights, he was as invisible as a ghost—as if he didn't exist. In the matches he played, his team was effectively one player short from the very start.

Replacing the second character of “ghost,” “Ling” (灵) with “Lin,” “Zero” (零), served two purposes: it highlighted his “zero deaths,” and also implied that he was a 0.

Although Ling Suyu had a cool, aloof demeanor, his features were on the softer side; compared to “handsome,” “beautiful” is a more fitting descriptor.

Even though his aloof attitude didn’t make him appear very effeminate, his haters still targeted his appearance, claiming he looks like a “bottom.”

That wasn’t the point. The point was that GQ spent so much time analyzing Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter playstyle, only to suddenly realize that the people bashing him online could find nothing to criticize except his “zero deaths” and “refusal to play as a team”—and when they ran out of things to criticize, they’d even resorted to attacking his appearance.

After scouring the entire internet, they couldn’t find a single person criticizing his gameplay— especially not his mistakes.

There was no particular reason for this. It was simply because this player’s error rate was incredibly low!

Forget about gameplay mistakes—he rarely even missed a skill.

Every skill he casted was clearly executed after anticipating his opponent’s movements, and every dash was used at the perfect moment, allowing him to land precisely where he needed to be, dodge all incoming skills, and launch a counterattack.

The more mobility and flexibility a hero had, the lower their margin for error, and the wider the gap between their best and worst performance.

Skilled players could turn these heroes into meta powerhouses, even dominating the true meta favorites.

Unskilled players, on the other hand, would feed in all sorts of ways—they might as well just AFK at the fountain.

Feather is the former.

Before actually analyzing his Desert Hunter, GQ had even joked that there was absolutely no need for OsO to waste a ban slot on Desert Hunter.

But after actually analyzing his Desert Hunter…

“Should we ban him after all?”

“We’ve spent so much time analyzing it—we even put together a specific lineup to practice against it—and our conclusion is just to ban it?”

“True. It feels like if we just ban it outright, all our hard work these past few days would be for nothing.”

“Then let’s give it a shot. Let’s use this lineup and secure the win early on!”

“But will they really use Desert Hunter in the group stage?”

“They’d better.”

—The above was GQ’s thought process leading up to this match.

They had indeed studied Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter.

They had also put together a lineup specifically to counter Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter and put in a lot of practice and effort.

But that didn’t mean they were confident they could win just because Ling Suyu picked Desert Hunter.

To put it bluntly, they simply didn’t want all their hard work over the past few days to go to waste.

When facing Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter, the only thing they could do was stay calm.

And precisely because they had studied Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter so thoroughly, every single one of them knew exactly how terrifying this champion became once he was fully developed. So, if they failed to secure an early lead as planned…

At the 7-minute mark, Fight secured the Divine Sunbird. GQ’s jungler was lurking nearby but completely missed the opportunity to steal it. And if that wasn’t bad enough, he made a gameplay error that gave away his position, allowing Fight to chase him down and secure a kill on the spot—essentially handing over a free kill.

At the 10-minute mark, the Dragon God spawned. Fight’s jungler, Yu He, was solo-pushing the top lane when he was intercepted by the enemy mid laner. He instantly secured a 1v1 kill, even snatching the lightning strike, and GQ inexplicably gave away another kill.

However, immediately afterward, GQ’s top laner and jungler arrived, attempting to defend the top lane’s second tower. Yu He counter-killed the jungler and fled across half the map, somehow evading the enemy top laner’s pursuit and surviving on low health, effectively meaning GQ’s jungler had once again needlessly gifted another kill.

After GQ’s jungler died, Fight completely ignored the enemy top laner who was loitering nearby and went straight for the Dragon!

After escaping the enemy top laner’s clutches, Yu He, the lone wolf, didn’t recall to base.

Instead, he circled the map and returned to his team while still low on health, crouching in the bushes near the dragon pit to wait for his chance.

Soon, the Dragon God was low on health. The enemy top laner, accompanied by their marksman and support, charged into the pit to steal it. Yu He dashed in, used his ultimate to secure the Dragon God, then fell to the ground without a hint of regret, leaving the rest to his teammates, passing peacefully.

Mo Xiaoyun landed his ultimate on the enemy top laner; Dou Jiayan locked him down; Tang Yuan activated his shield to block the enemy marksman’s ultimate damage for the entire team; Ling Suyu used his dash to dodge the enemy marksman’s crowd control, then immediately dashed back to deal damage!

One kill!

Double kill!

Triple kill!

【System】The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us!

Liang Fan: “Fight got the Dragon God! Feather secured a triple kill!”

Remi: “GQ not only failed to steal the Dragon God, but also got wiped out in a team fight!

They ultimately paid the price for underestimating Desert Hunter!”

In this team fight, only Fight’s jungler Yu He was killed; the rest of the team, capitalizing on GQ’s wipe, pushed lanes while clearing minions despite being low on health, and took down both GQ’s mid lane second and third towers in one go.

In the end, the match lasted just 17 minutes—it was decided long before it even reached the late game.

GQ, with an early-game composition, was completely dominated in the early stages by Fight’s late-game composition.

It was a crushing defeat.

After the game, the GQ players were utterly dejected.

Although they had prepared themselves before the match for Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter to become terrifying once he reached full power, they never imagined it would be this terrifying!

“Let’s ban Desert Hunter.”

This time, when one of them suggested it, no one objected.

However…

“If we ban Desert Hunter, won’t Feather pick Candy Witch?”

By now, every team knew Feather’s pick pattern. At this stage of the Spring Seasonals, he’d only used two marksmen: if Desert Hunter wasn’t banned, he’d pick Desert Hunter; if it was banned, he’d pick Candy Witch.

“How do you even play against Candy Witch?”

“Funny, we haven’t even looked into it.”

“Should we just ban her too?”

“We’re such a mess.”

GQ’s teammates had all been beaten into submission by Desert Hunter, resorting to all sorts of self-deprecating jokes.

However, their pre-match strategy this time was indeed flawed. They’d invested so much time and effort into Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter, only to end up completely overwhelmed by that very champion.

This reality dealt a far greater psychological blow to them than simply losing a single match.

Fortunately, GQ’s coach kept his cool: “If they pick Candy Witch, they’ll definitely take Mobile Artillery. We haven’t practiced Candy Witch, so we can’t steal her, but we can take Mobile Artillery. Losing this game means we get first pick in the next one. Since that’s the case, let’s ban Desert Hunter and steal Mobile Artillery!”

After that, he offered a word of encouragement: “ It’s okay! It’s a BO3—we still have a chance! If we can come back from a 1-0 against Fight, it’ll have been worth the trip!”

“Yeah!”

What they didn’t know was that while they were regrouping, the Fight team was also discussing their strategy for the next game.

Chen Jie: “In the next game, Desert Hunter will probably be banned, and Candy Witch is a long shot too.”

Dou Jiayan: “Does this mean Feather’s Cat Spirit will finally get a chance to take the stage and dominate?”

Tang Yu’an: “It’s not that likely. I bet they’ll go for Mobile Artillery.”

Although very few teams had practiced with either Candy Witch or Mobile Artillery.

However, unlike mages who rely on specific skills, most marksmen were played in a similar way—in the end, they all relied on basic attacks to deal damage.

So, even teams that hadn’t practiced with Mobile Artillery might still pick him.

“It’s possible.” Chen Jie, who played marksmen, felt that if he were GQ, regardless of whether he’d practiced Mobile Artillery or not, and given the precedent set by OsO, he’d have to secure Mobile Artillery no matter what. “In that case, there’s a high chance Candy Witch will be let out. Captain, let’s go with Skeletal Empress for the next game.”

Privately, Chen Jie always called Tang Yu’an “Tangyuan,” but in front of others, he would address him by his full name.

Yet this time, he called him “Captain.”

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow, a bit curious as to why he’d suddenly changed his form of address, but he didn’t ask directly; instead, he simply said, “Okay.”

Fight hadn’t mentioned that, after their match against OsO, they’d revisited their strategy during the post-game analysis and scrapped the plan to pick both Candy Witch and Mobile Artillery at the same time.

The reason was that Dou Jiayan’s Mobile Artillery was too weak, while Tang Yuan’s Mobile Artillery, though it boosted physical damage, couldn’t protect Ling Suyu effectively—thereby actually limiting Ling Suyu’s damage output to some extent.

To ensure Ling Suyu’s damage output, they really did need a support with high health, strong defense, and the ability to protect teammates.

After all, Ling Suyu was talented enough that, as long as he was protected and allowed to farm, his damage output alone could match that of an entire team.

And since the strategy of picking both Candy Witch and Mobile Artillery didn’t work, they naturally had to find another solution.

After some discussion between Fight, the solution they came up with was picking a top-lane champion as the support.

This way, they could both protect Ling Suyu and deal physical damage.

And the top-lane champion Tang Yu’an excelled at the most was Skeletal Empress.

Although she was also one of the top laners Mo Xiaoyun favoured, when Tang Yu’an expressed a desire to play her, Mo Xiaoyun gave in without a word of complaint.

“The captain’s Skeletal Empress—” Dou Jiayan drawled, dragging out the last syllable with interest.

Ling Suyu felt he’d only said half of what he meant, that there must be some unsaid words, so he looked at him intently.

But Dou Jiayan didn’t continue; instead, Tang Yu’an added on his own: “Every marksman I’ve supported as Skeletal Empress has said great things—right, Feather?”

Ling Suyu’s attention was instantly drawn to him, and the corners of his lips turned up. “Mm!”
