This was the first match Fight had played together since the New Year’s?

Hearing their captain’s words, the members of SMG all instinctively wanted to say, “No way, there’s definitely no way!”

However, after looking at the number of games played this season by the other members of Fight’s team besides Feather, they couldn’t come up with a single word to refute.

If Fight had been training with alt accounts, why did they suddenly play a full-stack game with their main accounts?

If the other members of Fight’s team besides Feather had been training with alt accounts, why wasn’t Feather using one?

None of it made sense.

Ling Suyu had hidden his match history, so SMG couldn’t see his previous games’ details and had no way of knowing that he hadn’t been solo queuing, but had instead played many games in a duo with a support player whose ID was “tangyuan with sesame filling”

They assumed Ling Suyu’s 100% win rate on Cat Spirit was achieved through solo queue, and a chill ran down their spines.

“Huh—so Feather specializes in sustained-damage marksmen, not burst-damage ones?”

SMG’s marksman asked hesitantly.

“Then what the hell was UH doing?” the mid laner captain frowned, “If he can make a bottom-tier hero that dominating, why bother picking a meta one?”

“Perhaps because Jiang Hao plays burst-damage marksmen?” the support speculated, “UH has adapted to Jiang Hao’s playstyle, but hasn’t had time to adjust to Feather’s, so instead they had Feather adapt to theirs?”

“UH’s coach must be out of his mind,” the jungler remarked bluntly. “It’s not like they don’t practice in solo queues. They play just fine when matched with random teammates who have different styles. Is it really necessary to make Feather play a marksman he’s not good at just to accommodate the rest of the team?”

“The thing is, Feather’s Guardian Spirit has a high win rate too,” the top laner pointed out.

A moment of silence fell.

Indeed, looking at the overall stats of Feather’s pro account, Guardian Spirit was the champion he used most frequently, with the highest win rate.

Never mind UH’s coach—even if coaches from other teams saw the stats on this account, they’d have their team’s members fight to lock in Guardian Spirit for him.

However, 《Glorious Heroes》was, in the end, a game that emphasized teamwork, so solo queues depended not only on skill but also on luck.

Only Ling Suyu himself knew how much of his 81% win rate with Guardian Spirit was inflated, and how much of his 79% win rate with Cat Spirit was due to being let down by his teammates.

In any case, judging by Ling Suyu’s performance in the White Fox Cup and their recent ranked match, he was clearly better suited to playing Cat Spirit.

To be precise, he was better suited to playing a sustained-damage marksman with mobility, attack-speed stacking, and the ability to chase down enemies.

“We should probably show the coach the replay from that last match,” said SMG’s captain with deep regret.

“Yeah.” His teammates had no objections.

Although it was just a five-man squad match they played out of boredom on New Year’s Day to kill time, they still took it seriously.

Losing so badly was shameful. Pretty much none of them had been in the zone, and no one would be able to escape the coach’s scolding. But if reviewing this match could improve their win rate against Fight in the Spring Seasonal, then even getting yelled at by the coach all the way until the Spring Seasonal began would be worth it.

And so, with heavy hearts, SMG began their post-game analysis.

They were simply too concerned about the reasons behind this loss. So, even though they’d already decided to show the coach the match footage, they still wanted to watch it themselves first before he arrived.

Meanwhile, over on Fight’s side, some players were showering, while others were sleeping.

The atmosphere couldn’t have been more relaxed.

After Dou Jiayan finished his shower, dried his hair, and lay down on his bed, he immediately shared the highlights of his day with Chen Jie.

yanzi: Jie-ge, Jie-ge, we just played a full-stacked match with Feather. Guess who we got matched with?

yanzi: We got matched with SMG!

yanzi: Then we absolutely crushed them!

yanzi: I think it took us less than 20 minutes total?

yanzi: I’ll log in tomorrow and send you a screenshot!

Chen Jie, Fight’s former marksman, was lying in bed at that moment, staring blankly at the ceiling.

His right hand was throbbing slightly. Although it no longer woke him up in the middle of the night like it had on that particular evening a few days ago, it still kept him from falling asleep.

Then, just as he was staring at the ceiling and pondering the meaning of life, a series of message notifications rang out from his phone on the nightstand.

He instinctively reached for it with his left hand. Bringing the phone up to his eyes, he saw the screen filled with messages from Dou Jiayan.

And the very first message mentioned Feather.

That damn yanzi really doesn’t think about how others feel at all when he speaks.

It was his own fault for being too lenient with him.

Chen Jie sighed, then unlocked his phone and carefully typed out a reply with one hand.

jie: How did Feather do?

yanzi: He was absolutely amazing! Can you believe he uses Cat Spirit? He carried the whole team in the late game, sending SMG running for the hills. His damage was even higher than mine!

jie: Oh, that’s great.

yanzi: Jie-ge, when are you coming back to the club? I’ve been missing you a bit.

jie: Probably in a few days, I’ll be back.

yanzi: OK, I’ll wait for you— After sending that message, Dou Jiayan tossed his phone onto the desk by his bed and drifted off to sleep.

Chen Jie stared blankly at the chat screen, which had stopped updating, then suddenly seemed to make up his mind. He pursed his lips, sat up in bed, found Tang Yu’an in his contacts, and dialed his number.

Tang Yu’an had just finished showering and was stepping out of the bathroom on the first floor, wearing a short-sleeved shirt and a bathrobe.

As soon as he stepped out, he heard his phone vibrating. Realizing it was his own phone ringing, he hurried over to the sofa in the living room, picked up the phone from the coffee table, saw “Ah-Jie” on the screen, and decisively swiped to answer: “Hello?”

“Are you free right now? I want to talk to you and discuss something.” Chen Jie’s familiar voice came through the phone, though it sounded more weary than usual.

Tang Yu’an hesitated for a moment before finally saying, “Sure, I’m free. Go ahead,” then dropped the towel, threw on a jacket, and headed to the empty training room.

On the other end of the line, Chen Jie paused for a moment before continuing, “The others don’t know the actual situation with our team. They think my hand injury’s due to the intensity of training and matches, but you know that besides the matches, I have to juggle so many other things: watching other teams’ matches, analyzing their styles and tactics, and researching lineups that can counter them.”

Tang Yu'an hesitated, but said nothing in the end. He simply hummed in acknowledgment and continued listening to him.

Chen Jie went on, “I’m not saying this to complain. We built Fight together, and I’m deeply attached to it, so I’m more than willing to do all this. But since I haven’t been able to play these past few days, I’ve had a lot of time on my hands and have been thinking. I’ve realized that for me, whether or not I get to play in the matches isn’t that important. What matters is that Fight wins the championship.”

Hearing this, Tang Yuan vaguely sensed something and froze in surprise.

Chen Jie continued, “I hear Feather is pretty skilled? He’s probably better than me, right?

Remember when I first thought I couldn’t make it as a pro? You told me, ‘No matter how terrible I am as a marksman, you won’t fall as long as I’m with you.’ As it turned out, you were right. With you leading the way, I didn’t even have to think. I just had to do whatever you told me to do. But doesn’t that mean it doesn’t matter to you who the marksman is? That even a complete newbie who’s never played a MOBA before could play professionally under your command?”

“Have you been drinking or doing drugs?” That was Tang Yu’an’s first reaction upon hearing this. “I’m human, not a god. I did say that no matter how terrible your plays were, I could save you. But no matter how bad you are, you’re still a pro player. If you actually gave me a complete newbie, do you need me to show you exactly how fast I’d fall apart?”

“Don’t interrupt. You know what I mean.” Chen Jie ignored Tang Yu’an’s “reassurance” and continued, “UH can’t handle Feather, but you can. With him on the team, Fight will definitely go further, and I’ll have it a lot easier. That way, I can confidently hand the matches over to him and focus on being Fight’s coach. Or do you think I’m not qualified to be a coach?”

Tang Yu’an: “……”

Tang Yu’an took a deep breath, then, in a rare moment of seriousness, carefully chose his words before replying: “I trust you completely as a coach. If you can focus on coaching, it’ll make things easier for me too. But… never mind. If that’s truly how you feel, I’ll support you wholeheartedly. Feather is indeed strong. He played a match against SMG today and almost dominated them, but SMG’s style is inherently unpredictable, so it’s hard to tell for sure yet.

Let’s wait and see how he does against YJ.”

Chen Jie gave a soft “Hmm.”

“Anyway, I’ll keep this under wraps for now. Think it through carefully. Once you’ve made up your mind, I’ll find a time to announce your decision to the rest of the club.”

Chen Jie gave another “Hmm,” then suddenly let out a soft chuckle.

Tang Yu’an: ?

“Tangyuan, you might not realize it, but you’re actually quite stubborn. Once you’ve made up your mind, no one can sway you,” Chen Jie said, “If you didn’t want me to retire, you would have done everything in your power to stop me. Since you didn’t stop me but instead told me to think it through, that means you already have a clear assessment of Feather’s and my abilities, right?”

Tang Yu’an froze, opened his mouth, but found himself at a loss for words Because he realized Chen Jie was right.

If anyone else had said this to him, be it Mo Xiaoyun, Yu He, Dou Jiayan—if any of them had wanted to retire early, he would have done everything in his power to talk them out of it.

But Chen Jie…

“I’m not blaming you. In esports, skill comes first, and if you want to win the championship, you have to be rational. I’m just a little curious—is Feather really that strong?” Chen Jie said, his tone not only not down but actually much more relaxed. “Looks like I can’t slack off anymore. I want to come watch your practice match against YJ in person.”

Tang Yu’an blinked, catching on almost immediately: “Are you coming to the club tomorrow?”

Chen Jie gave a soft “Mm-hmm”: “Tomorrow morning.”

Tang Yu’an: “OK. Let me know when you’re near the subway stop, and I’ll drive over to pick you up.”

“Alright,” Chen Jie replied, audibly letting out a sigh of relief. “I feel much better after talking to you. Thanks.”

“Don’t be so corny. You know I like guys.”

“So what? If you had even the slightest interest in me, you’d have come over on your own.

You wouldn’t need me to seduce you.”

“Uh…”

“Hahaha, just kidding. See you tomorrow.”

“Yeah, see you tomorrow,” Tang Yu’an replied. He waited for Chen Jie to hang up before ending the call himself. Then, looking at the empty training room, he lowered his head, hair still dripping wet from the shower, and let out a long sigh.

Chen Jie was right. Deep down, he already had a clear assessment of Chen Jie and Ling Suyu’s skills.

Setting aside their technical skills and hand speed, Ling Suyu was leagues ahead of Chen Jie in terms of strategic awareness alone.

But even so, even though he’d already made up his mind, hearing Chen Jie’s plans to retire and realizing he might never again have the chance to protect a marksman named Fight-Jie still left him feeling a sense of emptiness.

Still, even if Chen Jie retired, he had played a part in founding Fight, and he would always be a member of the team. In the future, he would help Fight win championships in his own way.

So, there was no need to feel down.

With that thought, Tang Yu’an quickly pulled himself together, left the empty training room, grabbed the towel he’d tossed on the sofa in the lobby, and headed upstairs to dry his hair.

Ling Suyu had just finished his shower and was lying down.

To make sure he didn’t oversleep, he set his alarm for 10am. the next morning.

However, he woke up naturally around 8:00am. He lay around in bed until 9:30, then, deciding it was about time, turned off the alarm early and got up to wash up.

He only wore his soft, cute white bunny pajamas when going to the bathroom in the dark of night. After waking up, he didn’t put them on but changed directly into the jacket he’d usually wear out.

When he came downstairs wearing a clean, crisp white tracksuit, he noticed there was already someone in the living room.

The person sitting on the sofa facing him was Tang Yu’an.

But who was the person sitting across from Tang Yu’an, with their back to him…?

Out of an instinctive wariness toward strangers, Ling Suyu paused in his tracks, then crept forward silently.

However, before he could successfully circle around to the side of the person standing with his back to him and catch a glimpse of his face, the other man heard the sound first and turned to look at him.

What met his eyes was a mature face, and though it was covered in a patch of unshaven stubble, perhaps because of his gentle facial features, he didn’t seem dangerous at all.

Rather, he appeared quite approachable.

Yet the sudden eye contact left Ling Suyu frozen in place. After a moment, he called out the man’s name uncertainly: “Chen Jie?”

Ling Suyu rarely appeared in official matches, but that didn’t mean he didn’t follow them closely.

Not to mention the Fight members he’d specifically looked up online; even when encountering players from other teams, there was a decent chance he’d recognize them.

Well…probably not every single player. After all, he’s socially anxious, and rather than the professional players’ appearances, he paid more attention to their IDs, go-to champions, and playstyles.

But the uncle big brother sitting on the couch with his back to him, sporting a friendly stubble, was definitely Fight’s former marksman, Chen Jie.

Sure enough, upon hearing the name “Chen Jie,” the other person reacted immediately:

“Yeah, that’s me. Good morning, Feather.”

“Good morning.” Ling Suyu replied curtly in his somewhat cold tone, his face almost expressionless, giving off an air of complete aloofness.

Chen Jie raised an eyebrow.

Although Tang Yu’an had mentioned in the car that this player came across as cold on the surface, actually interacting with him still took him by surprise.

Meanwhile, Tang Yu’an, standing nearby, seemed to be thoroughly enjoying the spectacle.

Without offering any explanation, he shifted to the side, making room for Ling Suyu between himself and Chen Jie: “Come on, Feather, come sit here—”

Hearing his voice, Ling Suyu didn’t even pause to think; he walked over expressionlessly and sat down beside him, behaving as obediently as could be.

Tang Yu’an nodded toward the breakfast spread on the coffee table. “Jie-ge bought this. Eat while it’s hot.”

Only then did Ling Suyu notice the coffee table piled high with all sorts of breakfast items:

steamed buns, xiaolongbao, pan-fried dumplings, potstickers, egg pancakes, potato pancakes, glutinous rice cakes… even soy milk!

No wonder it smelled so good when he came downstairs. But his attention had been completely absorbed by Chen Jie, and he hadn’t noticed what was on the coffee table at all.

“Usually, someone cooks lunch and dinner, and we occasionally order takeout, but breakfast has always been a free-for-all. Whoever wants to eat goes out and buys it themselves,”

Tang Yu’an explained. “Your Jie-ge has been absent from work for so many days, so it’s his turn to buy the guys something nice to make amends and apologize.”

“Huh?” Ling Suyu was confused. “But…”

But didn’t Chen Jie “skip work” because of his hand injury? Didn’t that count as a “work-related injury”? Did he still have to apologize?

He’d barely formed this thought when Chen Jie leaned forward, his arm sweeping past Ling Suyu to slap Tang Yu’an on the shoulder. “Can’t you just speak properly?”

Tang Yu’an pretended to dodge but couldn’t get out of the way, responding with a laugh, “Fight skipped out on All-Stars and the White Fox Cup because of you. You should feel a little more guilty!”

Chen Jie resisted the urge to roll his eyes and instinctively looked to drag the other person in the room in to scold their heartless captain. But when he turned his head, he found Ling Suyu completely dumbfounded, one hand hovering in the air, unsure of whether to step in to break up the fight.

Seeing him like this, Chen Jie couldn’t help but let out a laugh.

He shouldn’t have lashed out like that—now he’d scared the rookie.

But before he could say a word, Tang Yu’an beat him to it, playing the victim: “Look, you’ve scared Feather half to death. He already has coach PTSD, and now, before you’ve even officially taken office, you’ve given him psychological trauma. If he doesn’t perform well in the Spring Seasonals, it’ll be your fault!”

“Fine, fine, fine. I can never win an argument against you. I won't say anything, alright?”

Chen Jie conceded defeat, then scratched his head helplessly and explained to Ling Suyu, “Don't worry, Feather. I don't hit people for no reason. Really, your captain just really needs a beating!”

“Wait, coach?”

Even though he was a bit confused by their interaction, Ling Suyu still caught the gist of their conversation and thought he must have misheard: “What coach?”

Chen Jie and Tang Yu’an exchanged a glance. The latter tilted his chin, signaling for Chen Jie to speak. So before announcing it to the entire club, Chen Jie shared his thoughts with Ling Suyu first: “Fight currently doesn’t have a coach. Not being able to find a good one is only part of it. The main reason is that we wanted to take on that responsibility ourselves, but you saw how that turned out…” He raised his right hand, which was wrapped in a black wrist guard. “I guess that’s the price of arrogance, but I don’t regret it.” He paused. “Although I really wanted to do both, it turns out I’m not Superman. I don’t have that kind of ability. So now that you’re here, I’m thinking of retiring outright and focusing solely on coaching.”

Ling Suyu froze, then stared in disbelief.

He’d just arrived, and the team’s original marksman was already retiring?

He didn’t even know how to react.

Before joining Fight, he’d been nothing more than a substitute for UH. Did Fight really trust him that much?

As if reading his mind, Chen Jie added, “This is just an idea for now. Ultimately, it’ll depend on how things actually play out. If you can’t help Fight go far in the upcoming Spring Seasonals, I’ll definitely have to consider coming back. If that ends up happening, then you won’t have any complaints being a substitute, right?”

Ling Suyu fell silent for a moment before nodding.

Indeed, if he couldn’t play better than Chen Jie, then serving as Chen Jie’s substitute would be something he’d accept wholeheartedly.

“Is the practice match against YJ tomorrow?” Chen Jie suddenly asked Tang Yu’an.

Tang Yu’an nodded. “Tomorrow at 1:00pm.”

“Alright, then I’ll see for myself just how strong Feather really is,” Chen Jie said.

Hearing this, Ling Suyu suddenly felt nervous.

Although he went into every match determined to prove himself, this was the first time someone had actually looked forward to seeing him prove himself.

But just as he began to feel nervous, he heard Tang Yu’an’s voice beside him: “Don’t worry.

It’ll still be the same as before. I’ve got your back.”

His voice, slightly softer than usual, was filled with tenderness, like a warm, large hand gently caressing his heart. In an instant, it soothed all of Ling Suyu’s anxiety, allowing every cell in his body to relax and unwind.

He silently exhaled in relief and murmured, “Mm.” Not only had his nervousness vanished, but he now found himself looking forward to tomorrow’s match against YJ.

He would help Fight defeat YJ, show Chen Jie what he was capable of, and avenge his loss during the White Fox Cup loss while he was at it.

With Tang Yu’an by his side, he had every bit of confidence that he could do it!

Sensing his relaxation, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but relax as well. Smiling, he picked up a small steamed bun and held it to Ling Suyu’s lips: “Don’t overthink it. Let’s eat breakfast first.

Here, open wide—”

Ling Suyu instinctively opened his mouth to catch it, chewed, swallowed, and blinked.

Huh? What just happened?

Tang Yu’an: 0v0; Chen Jie: ?
