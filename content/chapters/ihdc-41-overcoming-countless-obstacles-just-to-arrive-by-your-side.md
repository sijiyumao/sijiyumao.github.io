Oh, right, before the organizers released any videos related to the team’s voice chat, they’d definitely contact the team to confirm first.

Ling Suyu secretly let out a sigh of relief, but only then did it dawn on him—wait a minute, why was he even nervous? He hadn’t said anything he shouldn’t have.

【Now let’s take a look at the mid lane!】

“Fight’s mid laner Swallow is playing very conservatively this game!” said ZhiZhi. “He’s been hiding under the tower, last-hitting minions, and even after clearing the wave, he refuses to leave the tower—that’s not like Swallow at all. But I guess there’s nothing he can do.

What? Auto-attacking?

That kind of thing was completely out of Dou Jiayan’s zone.

Everyone had noticed during practice matches against YJ: given Dou Jiayan’s positioning skills, the probability of him getting counter-killed while chasing down an enemy to auto-attack was over 50%.

He excelled at burst-damage mages, the type that unleashed combos and then retreated, only to return when the opportunity arose for another burst. Although he’d practiced a few skirmish mages like Deepsea Faerie, his approach was generally to stack burst damage to take down opponents as quickly as possible, avoiding prolonged battles. This was in contrast to most professional players, who not only dealt damage but also built up their health to ensure survivability and prepare for sustained engagements.

This was why, at the end of each match, Dou Jiayan’s damage output was always extremely high, but his death count was also staggering.

In response to Dou Jiayan’s playstyle, Chen Jie’s only requirement for his Mobile Artillery was: “Don’t feed.”

Specifically: “In the early game, stay under the tower to farm minions and defend it as much as possible. In the late game, stick with Tang Yu’an—wherever he goes, you go. In team fights, cast your ultimate, then attack whoever Yumao targets, don’t spam randomly. In short —leave everything else to your teammates; just stay alive.”

So now, Dou Jiayan was just playing it safe.

And he was doing it without any pretense, standing completely still.

As long as the enemy didn’t cast their first skill at him, he couldn’t even be bothered to move, almost as if he’d disconnected.

The enemy jungler even ran up to the tower to take a look at him, as if he were observing some rare animal.

“Wow, OsO’s jungler is really being provocative!” ZhiZhi chuckled at the scene. “He’d rather give away his position just to pull that, huh?”

Child of Frost could definitely tower-dive and one-shot once it reaches a high enough level, but Mobile Artillery’s passive is still active. Trying to gank it head-on right now is definitely a bad idea.”

Mobile Artillery’s passive ability, “Self-Repair,” triggering when its health dropped to zero, caused it to immediately switch to a defensive mode at its current location, fully restoring its health and increasing its defense by 20%. If enemy heroes failed to kill Mobile Artillery in this form within 3 seconds, it would regain mobility after 3 seconds and instantly gain 30% of its maximum health. The cooldown was 200 seconds.

After Dou Jiayan allocated his first skill point to his first ability, “Pursuit Cannon,” he spent his second skill point on his passive.

It’s just farming, right? He’d farm until the end of time…not.

“Okay, then you come down too. Just block the marksman later; our Yumao will handle the mid laner,” Tang Yu’an said.

Ling Suyu blinked—his Candy Witch was an early-game champion, and one of the strongest in the early game. If he were laning against her, he’d never run into her auto-attack range to court death. Yet, after the enemy marksman had already gifted them a kill by playing recklessly, was the enemy mid-laner about to come over and do the same?

Such good things actually existed?

By this point, Ling Suyu had already cleared the fifth wave of minions and was just a bit short of the experience needed to reach level six.

Candy Witch waved her hand, unleashing streaks of enchanting purple light. Red and purple damage indicators intertwined and floated up from the monsters’ heads, representing physical and magical damage respectively.

Auto-attacks dealt physical damage, but thanks to the Candy Witch’s passive ability and the damage bonuses from her items, the magical damage from her auto-attacks was more than double the physical damage. Just watching it looked painful.

Sure enough, the enemy mid laner Wind Guardian was there!

Ling Suyu seized the opportunity to throw a “Candy Bomb” into the bushes where the Wind Guardian was hiding.

“Candy Bomb” exploded automatically upon contact with an enemy hero, and any enemy hero doused in its syrup instantly had their movement speed reduced by 20%.

This ability didn’t deal much damage and was purely for slowing.

Realizing he’d been slowed, Wind Guardian simply stood his ground and countered with a “Wind Cage” to try to lock down Ling Suyu.

But why did Ling Suyu choose to play Candy Witch?

It was because she had a dash ability!
