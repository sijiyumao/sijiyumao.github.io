That night, both Fight and YJ reviewed their matches until late into the night.

After the review, Ling Suyu took a shower, dried his hair, and returned to his room. Looking at the stack of brand-new team uniforms on his desk, his face lit up with happiness.

Even though this wasn’t his first time joining a team, nor was it the first time he’d received team uniforms, he felt something special, like he’d finally found his true home.

For the umpteenth time, he felt incredibly grateful that Tang Yu’an had taken him in— Ling Suyu set his alarm for 11:00 a.m. the next day, but he slept straight through the night until a little past 10:50, waking up naturally just before the alarm went off. Another wonderful day had arrived!

However, as soon as he finished his morning routine and went downstairs, he saw a crowd of strangers in the club lobby. Startled, he froze in his tracks, standing motionless on the stairs.

Upon closer inspection, he realized the group was led by a female reporter dressed in a smart and near style with an ID badge around her neck and several photographers following behind her.

Ling Suyu had seen this scene before.

Before the Spring Seasonals began, to build hype for the tournament, the league organizers would send reporters to the most talked-about clubs to interview players.

As one of last season’s top four teams, Fight, having missed both the All-Star Games and the White Fox Cup, once again “re-emerged” during the Spring Seasonals. With such a high degree of public attention, it was only natural for the official league to send reporters to cover them.

However, what Ling Suyu hadn’t expected was that the female reporter, who had been chatting amiably with Tang Yu’an, would suddenly look up, spot him, and walk straight toward him: “Feather?”

In that moment, almost on instinct, Ling Suyu turned and ran, dashing back to his room!

Downstairs, Tang Yu’an watched his figure dart away like a rabbit and couldn’t help but burst out laughing.

That reaction was just too cute. Was he some kind of little rabbit startled by a hunting dog?

Then, under the female reporter’s somewhat bewildered gaze, Tang Yu’an sighed helplessly as he also headed upstairs, explaining as he went, “Mmm…Feather’s a bit shy. Please wait downstairs. I’ll bring him down after I’ve explained things to him.”

“Okay, thanks for your trouble,” the female reporter replied, returning to her seat by the coffee table.

Chen Jie poured her a glass of water, then asked the photographer behind her if he’d like some too.

The photographer didn’t say anything and simply shook his head.

Upstairs, Tang Yu’an knocked on Ling Suyu’s door.

As he knocked, he thought back to how Ling Suyu had just darted back into his room with a “whoosh” and couldn’t help but chuckle. Then, stifling his laughter, he said, “Feather, open up. It’s me.”

Recognizing Tang Yu’an’s voice, Ling Suyu opened a crack in the door and immediately glanced behind Tang Yu’an to make sure no one was there. Only then did he relax a little.

“What’s going on?”

“Haha…Sorry.” Seeing how he was checking to make sure the coast was clear, Tang Yu’an couldn’t hold back his laughter any longer. After a moment, he finally managed to stop laughing and explained, “Here’s the thing: the Spring Seasonals are about to start, and the league organizers sent someone over for an interview. But their space is limited, so each team can only choose one person for a feature interview, and they wanted to interview you.”

“Huh? Why?” Ling Suyu didn’t think there was anything particularly interesting about him to warrant an interview.

But Tang Yu’an didn’t see it that way: “Because you’re currently in the spotlight. Take the White Fox Cup, for example. Even though UH didn’t place very well, the buzz surrounding the event was off the charts, so much so that even people outside the gaming community know about a marksman named Feather who got bullied by them.”

Ling Suyu: “……”

Help! Couldn’t that awkward incident just stay within the gaming community?!

Noticing Ling Suyu’s embarrassment, Tang Yu’an gave him a light pat on the shoulder to reassure him: “Don’t be nervous. It’s just a few questions, just answer however you feel. If you can’t answer, just say ‘next’. The video will be edited during post-production, and the parts where you don’t answer won’t be included.”

Was that how it worked?

Ling Suyu gradually relaxed.

“Let’s go. The sooner we finish this interview, the sooner we can get rid of them. I’m still waiting to have lunch,” Tang Yu’an said, casually placing a hand on Ling Suyu’s back.

Ling Suyu was led out of the room in a daze. He watched as Tang Yu’an closed the door behind him, and it took him a moment to realize: “Wait, if I don’t want to be interviewed, can’t I just refuse?”

Tang Yu’an: “It’s not that you can’t, but she’s just trying to complete an assignment from her agency. She’s such a pretty young lady—how could you bear to put her in a difficult spot?”

Ling Suyu: “……”

That…he could bear it, but…nevermind.

Ling Suyu sighed and reluctantly accepted the arrangement: “All right, then I’ll make this quick.”

“Mm-hmm—”

A few minutes later, Ling Suyu and the reporter sent by the league officials were seated a short distance apart behind a coffee table in Fight’s club lobby.

With at least three cameras trained on the two of them, Ling Suyu was so nervous he didn’t know where to put his hands, eventually folding them and resting them on his knees.

Seeing that Ling Suyu was willing to cooperate, the reporter let out a quiet sigh of relief and began the interview: “Welcome to today’s pre-match interview! The contestant we’re interviewing today is, drumroll please—”

She raised both hands, spread them out, and “presented” Ling Suyu standing beside her.

“Feather from Fight!”

Ling Suyu kept a stiff expression on his face and nodded briefly at the camera, unable to even manage a full syllable.

Reporter: “Feather, you’re currently Fight’s starting marksman, right? The Spring Seasonals are about to begin—how are you feeling right now?”

How was he feeling?

Well…

“Pretty good,” Ling Suyu replied flatly with just those three words.

Tang Yu’an, who was “standing in the corner” of the hall as punishment, raised his hand to cover his mouth to keep from laughing out loud.

He could tell that Ling Suyu was now completely on high alert, as aloof as could be.

But when he thought of how soft and cute this guy usually was, the contrast was just too much, and he couldn’t help but chuckle.

Chen Jie, who was similarly “standing in the corner” next to him, raised an eyebrow.

Remembering how aloof Ling Suyu had been when they first met, he, too, found it hard to keep a straight face.

Feather really was shy.

The reporter, noticing Ling Suyu’s awkwardness with words, helped guide the conversation:

“Are you nervous?”

“I’m alright,” Ling Suyu replied again, offering just two words in a flat tone.

The reporter decisively skipped that question and moved on to another: “We all know that the timing of Feather’s contract termination with UH was a bit awkward, and the time you’ve had to train with Fight has been limited. I’m curious—in such a short time, how well do you think Feather and Fight have gelled? What’s your own take on it, Feather?”

Ling Suyu thought about it seriously for a moment before replying with just a few words: “I think it’s going okay.”

“Could you elaborate a bit?” the reporter pressed. “What do you think of your current teammates?”

“They’re all pretty good,” Ling Suyu said sincerely.

“And compared to your teammates at UH?”

Ling Suyu blinked. After a brief mental pause, he replied, “I’m with Fight now. I only have teammates from Fight and opponents from UH.”

Hearing this answer, Tang Yu’an resisted the urge to clap and instead gave Ling Suyu a thumbs-up, regardless of whether he could see it.

Just as one would expect from his Feather. Although he wasn’t good with words, he’d never let him down, whether it was giving someone a sharp retort or responding to others.

The reporter was also impressed by Ling Suyu’s reply. She smiled briefly before continuing, “It seems Feather is adapting well to Fight. So, you must be confident about winning the championship during the Spring Seasonals, right?”

“I am,” Ling Suyu replied, earnestly and solemnly.

“Haha—” Tang Yu’an finally couldn’t hold it in any longer. After bursting out laughing, he quickly turned to face the wall to reflect on his mistake.

Ling Suyu instinctively glanced in his direction, then tilted his head in confusion, as if he didn’t understand why Tang Yu’an was laughing.

The female reporter found his head-tilt adorable: “Haha, it seems that even though you’ve only been working together for a short time, it hasn’t dampened Fight’s enthusiasm for winning the championship. Speaking of which, Feather, has anyone ever complimented you on your good looks?”

Ling Suyu was completely baffled.

Huh? That was a pretty sudden shift in the line of questioning, and his brain almost couldn’t keep up.

“Well…I don’t know. I don’t think anyone’s ever said it to my face?”

The reporter: “Haha, so you think someone’s complimented you behind your back? Feather, you’re pretty confident in your looks. Honestly, it’s actually because you’re so good-looking and photogenic that we decided to interview you.”

“Huh?!” Ling Suyu exclaimed in surprise. “Is that really why?”

“Yes, yes,” the reporter nodded repeatedly.

Ling Suyu’s face was full of surprise. He actually believed her, but…

“But the captain is really good-looking too, isn’t he?”

“Hahaha, of course it’s not just about looks!”

The reporter had originally been worried that such an aloof player would be difficult to interview, but the more she talked to him, the more she found Ling Suyu kind of cute, or perhaps, adorably clueless?

“The next question is kind of an exclusive scoop—does Feather have a girlfriend?”

“No,” Ling Suyu replied bluntly.

“Then, what kind of girl does Feather like?”

Ling Suyu thought about it seriously for a moment.

He’d devoted himself entirely to esports and had never really given this question much thought. It always felt like something far removed from his reality.

A girlfriend, dating…he didn’t even dare to think about it. It felt like something that would never happen in his lifetime.

But if he ever did have the chance to date…

“First of all, she’d have to know how to play video games,” Ling Suyu stated firmly.

“Otherwise, I don’t think we’d have much in common.”

“And?” The reporter, who hadn’t expected such a serious answer, instantly got a sparkle in her eyes.

Ling Suyu continued to envision his ideal girlfriend: “If we go out to eat together, she’d need to know how and what to order, because I’m not very good at that.”

A memory flashed through his mind of when Tang Yu’an had treated him to a meal; ordering was basically his “job”, and he himself was happy to just sit back and relax.

“It’d be best if she were talkative, because I’m not much of a talker. If neither of us says anything, the atmosphere can get awkward pretty quickly.”

He pictured Tang Yu’an chattering away nonstop whenever they were together. Around him, he could simply relax and be a good listener.

“She should be decisive and good at making decisions, because I’m also not great at that. I usually just go along with whatever others suggest when we go out.”

He recalled those days he spent in a “world of two” with Tang Yu’an. Whenever they went out together, Tang Yu’an inevitably made all the decisions, be it small things like conversation topics to major plans like the day’s itinerary.

It might make Tang Yu’an seem domineering, but in reality, every decision he made took both of their thoughts and wishes into account, ensuring no one ever felt uncomfortable.

Ling Suyu really enjoyed the feeling of having everything in his life perfectly organized.

There were far fewer things he had to think about or worry over each day, and he felt that life had become much simpler and easier.

As he thought about it, he couldn’t help but blurt out, “Someone like the captain.”

Reporter: !!

Tang Yu’an: !

Chen Jie: ?!

As Ling Suyu said this, he didn’t realize anything was off at all. It wasn’t until he saw the reporter’s expression, which seemed to say, “Oh my god, I’ve stumbled upon the real deal”, that he froze for a moment and realized what had happened. He hurriedly explained, “Umm, I meant, like…someone like the captain means, a girl with a personality like the captain’s, someone who’s, who’s, uh, more decisive.”

However, the reporter was already tuning out his clarification: “Looks like the captain of Fight is quite the charmer! He’s already won Feather’s heart in just a few days—haha, just kidding, don’t take it seriously. Well, that wraps up today’s pre-match interview. See you next time!

Bye-bye—”

“Bye…” Ling Suyu raised his hand blankly and waved at the camera. Then, as soon as he lowered his hand from the three cameras pointed at him, he couldn’t help but cover his face with both hands.

Help! What on earth had he just said?!
