The host in charge of this time’s post-match interview for Fight was the same one who had previously gone all the way to Fight’s headquarters to conduct a pre-match interview with Ling Suyu—a pretty young woman.

She’d said she wanted to interview Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu together not out of personal interest, but rather because the official questions provided to her required answers from both of them.

However, having the chance to interview both the captain of Fight and the cute marksman who trusted said captain so deeply was exactly what she’d been hoping for.

Before they’d even walked in, she was already getting excited.

When Tang Yu’an walked in with Ling Suyu, she broke into a wide smile: “Hello there, it’s been a while! Feather, do you remember me?”

Ling Suyu stared at her for a second, then two, let out an “Ah,” and then looked up at Tang Yu’an.

Meeting his pleading gaze, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but burst out laughing.

Ling Suyu looked utterly embarrassed.

He remembered this person, but had simply forgotten her name.

“Hello, Mo Li, right? Thanks for your hard work during the pre-game interview,” Tang Yu’an said with a gentle smile, pulling Ling Suyu to sit down on the sofa.

“That’s right, you remember my name?” Mo Li smiled even more brightly.

Of course, “Mo Li” [^1] wasn’t her real name—it was a nickname she’d given herself as a reporter and host. Although she’d chosen it to make it easier for people to remember, she hadn’t expected anyone to actually remember it.

After all, whether you were a reporter or a host, you were just a “supporting role,” with the interviewee being the actual “star.” Once the interview video was released, although both the star and supporting role appeared on screen, most people would focus their attention on the star, thereby overlooking the supporting role.

Mo Li truly hadn’t expected Tang Yu’an to remember her name, but on second thought, perhaps that was exactly why Ling Suyu trusted his captain so much. He was truly both gentle and thoughtful.

“Then let’s not waste any more time and get going!” said Mo Li, eagerly asking her first question. “This match against igame was probably the first time you’ve gone the full three games since the start of the Spring Seasonals, right? What do you think of igame’s strength?”

“They’re very strong,” Tang Yu’an replied earnestly. “Although we won, it wasn’t easy, and I can’t help but feel they were holding back—they didn’t give it their all.”

At that moment, igame was also in another room giving their losers’ interview. Their captain, Ye Xunjie, was being interviewed, and the host asked a similar question: what they thought of Fight’s strength. Ye Xunjie replied, “Fight is very strong. Although we lost to them, we have no regrets because we gave it our all. We’ll keep working hard and aim to defeat them in the quarterfinals!”

Over on Fight’s side, Mo Li’s second question was: “I’m a little curious, did you pick Cat Spirit and Spider Queen on a whim, or had you practiced with them?”

“Of course we’d practiced them,” Tang Yu’an said earnestly. “Picking heroes you haven’t practiced in a professional match is disrespectful to your opponents!”

Ling Suyu glanced at him, pursed his lips, and said nothing—’I wonder who picked a Mobile Artillery they hadn’t practiced with during the match against OsO…’

Mo Li keenly picked up on Ling Suyu’s reaction and asked with a smile, “Feather, do you have anything to add?”

“Huh?” Ling Suyu, suddenly called out, was startled and shifted his gaze away. “The captain’s right.”

He never would have imagined that he, someone who never did or said anything dishonest, would one day lie to cover for someone.

But for someone who never lied, his demeanour was full of telltale signs: not only did his eyes keep darting away, but even his usually icy face flushed with guilt—he’d practically given himself away.

Mo Li fought back a laugh as she looked at Tang Yu’an: “Looks like the captain wasn’t telling the truth, huh?”

Tang Yu’an turned his head toward Ling Suyu, who cleared his throat and averted his gaze.

Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but chuckle: “I never lie. We’ve really been practicing Cat Spirit and Spider Queen as a bot lane duo.”

He was indeed telling the truth, but the truth was laced with lies.

They had indeed practiced Cat Spirit and Spider Queen. Aside from Dark Raven, Ling Suyu had practiced Cat Spirit the most, at least twice as much as Desert Hunter, while Candy Witch was something they’d crammed last-minute right before the match began.

But the statements “I won’t play heroes I haven’t practiced” and “I never lie.”

It was amazing Tang Yu’an could say such things without batting an eye.

Ling Suyu let out a soft sigh.

Both of them knew exactly what Ling Suyu was feeling guilty about, but to outsiders, it looked like something entirely different.

After the interview video was posted, the comments ran wild with speculation:

【No way! Such an amazing Cat Spirit and Spider Queen, and they didn’t practice before the match?!】

【Of course they’ve practiced! Feather must be feeling guilty about something else!】

【You all think Feather’s feeling guilty? He’s clearly just shy! Tang Yu’an, come clean! What did you do to our Feather?】

【AAAAH, a shy Feather is so cute! Oh my gosh, I’m supposed to be an esports fan, right? Why am I suddenly getting a sense of what it’s like to be a mom fan?】

【So we’re all assuming Feather’s reaction was because of shyness? By the way, why does it have to be Tang Yu’an that did something? Couldn’t it just be that he has a crush on the host?】

【If he had a crush on the host, he would’ve shown that during the pre-match interview!】

At that moment, Ling Suyu had no idea that his reaction would be misinterpreted in this way by netizens watching the video, causing the AnYu fan community to grow even larger.

He was currently trying his best to stay calm, not letting the host see that he was covering for Tang Yu’an.

Little did he know that his cover-up only made it more obvious.

Just as he was fidgeting nervously, Mo Li asked her third question: “Feather, do you prefer sustained-damage marksmen over burst-damage marksmen? If basic attack damage continues to be nerfed, will you still stick with sustained-damage marksmen?”

“No,” Ling Suyu replied, ignoring Mo Li’s stunned reaction, and continued, “They won’t keep nerfing basic attack damage. Because if they keep nerfing it, there’d be no point in playing marksmen anymore. I’ll try playing a mage in the bottom lane—Candy Witch is actually pretty good.”

Mo Li blinked.

So when Ling Suyu said “no,” he didn’t mean “I won’t stick with sustained-damage marksmen,” but rather “the nerfs won’t continue”?

As a reporter, she was far less knowledgeable about game balance than Ling Suyu, a professional player, so she didn’t argue. Instead, she quickly followed up with another question: “Feather, between Cat Spirit and Guardian Spirit, which marksman do you think is stronger?”

Ling Suyu thought about it seriously for a moment, then replied with five words: “Depends on who’s playing them.”

“Hahaha, that’s such a cool answer!” Mo Li was genuinely impressed. “Looks like Feather is pretty confident in his Cat Spirit!”

Ling Suyu: ?

He was just telling the truth—he wasn’t trying to be cocky! Listen to his explanation!

Tang Yu’an, standing nearby, couldn’t help but laugh again. He thought Ling Suyu was simply too adorable, with a kind of natural, unpretentious, and truly irresistible charm that radiated from within.

He couldn’t resist stirring the pot a little: “Feather’s Cat Spirit? I don’t think it’d lose to anyone or any hero.”

Ling Suyu gave an awkward cough—forget it, he wasn’t going to explain. Just give him a hole in the ground to crawl into!

Shouting “I can carry the team!” to your teammates was not the same as shouting it to the entire esports community!

Meanwhile, the host asked igame’s captain, Ye Xunjie, “What do you think of Feather’s Cat Spirit?”

“Very strong,” Ye Xunjie replied. “Or rather, a tough opponent—not very friendly to the jungler, mainly because he’s so hard to catch.”

Host: “Next time you face off against Fight, will igame consider banning Cat Spirit?”

“Maybe,” Ye Xunjie replied. “It depends on the post-game analysis, but if we’d played a fourth game afterwards, I’d ban at least one of Cat Spirit or Spider Queen.”

Host: “Haha, I can really feel how tricky Fight’s bot lane composition is. So, Xunjie, how would you evaluate Feather as a player?”

Ye Xunjie paused to think: “I know what you’re getting at, but I don’t want to drag my team into this, so I’ll answer this as an individual—UH wasted five precious years of a genius player’s career. It’s as simple as that.”

And just like that, following GQ, another club was added to UH fans’ “list of enemies.”

The next day, the post-match interview videos featuring Fight and igame were released one after the other by the BPL on various platforms, undoubtedly sparking another round of discussion.

【Right now, I’m truly both happy and sorry for Feather, mixed feelings.】

【Actually, the two aren’t mutually exclusive. Feather’s past hurts, but I’m happy to see the Feather everyone knows now. In the same sense, I just hope Feather can go even further from here!】

【He will! Feather and Fight bring out the best in each other; they’re sure to go even further!】

【But Feather is just so adorable. I’m so jealous of Tang Yu’an. Feather must have shown him even more of his true self, right?】

【To the person above: there’s something off about you.】

【Nope, you’re the one reading too much into it. Definitely.】

【By the way, are you all shipping AnYu? Doesn’t anyone ship the teams? I think Fight and igame make a great ship too!】

【They’re definitely a great ship. Even though the two teams are archrivals, they’re not as bitterly opposed as UH and YJ. They admire each other and strive to become better by using each other as benchmarks. Actually, I think it’s not just Fight that has a shot at the championship—igame’s really strong this season too. Who knows? Maybe both teams will make it to the finals together?】

【To the person above, you’re absolutely right, but I don’t want to see those two letters, UH, anymore.】

【That trash team UH should go to hell already!】

While everyone was discussing the interview video, the members of Fight and igame hadn’t even woken up yet.

igame’s members had stayed up late reviewing the match and had only just gone to sleep at 4 a.m.

As for Fight, to celebrate their team advancing to the quarterfinals as the top seed in their group, everyone went straight to a famous nearby restaurant for a meal after leaving the competition venue, then headed to a KTV to party hard.

Ling Suyu had never been to a KTV before, so he was completely bewildered when he was led inside.

It wasn’t that he couldn’t sing, but he was too shy to sing in front of others…

So, while Dou Jiayan belted out five songs in a row, singing so badly that Ye Xiran used going to the restroom as an excuse to escape, Mo Xiaoyun finally lost his patience and pinned him to the sofa. Chen Jie snatched the microphone from his hand, and Yu He quickly followed up by cutting off his next song. By then, Ling Suyu still hadn’t selected a single song.

However, not singing didn’t mean he wasn’t having fun.

Sitting on the sofa, he watched as the colorful lights flickered around the private room, munching on popcorn from the coffee table, sipping iced black tea, and listening to his teammates’ hit-or-miss singing. A faint smile involuntarily tugged at the corners of his lips.

Suddenly, Tang Yu’an, who was standing nearby, leaned in close to him and whispered, almost directly into his ear, “Aren’t you going to sing?”

Feeling his breath on his face, Ling Suyu froze. It took him a moment to process what he’d said. He looked at Tang Yu’an, shook his head, then asked, “What about you?”

Tang Yu’an blinked, as if he hadn’t heard him.

Ling Suyu hesitated for a moment, then had no choice but to lean in close and repeat his question, his voice barely above a whisper, “Why aren’t you singing either?”

Tang Yu’an felt a tickle in his ear and couldn’t help but smile as he replied, “Next time. Next time, I’ll sing just for you.”

Ling Suyu stared at him blankly, thinking he must have heard wrong.

But Tang Yu’an didn’t give him a chance to ask; he picked up his glass and brought it close to Ling Suyu’s: “Cheers~”

Ling Suyu’s attention was immediately diverted. He picked up his own glass and gently clinked it against Tang Yu’an’s: “Cheers!”
