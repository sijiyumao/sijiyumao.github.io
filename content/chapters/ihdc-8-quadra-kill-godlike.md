Just as Tang Yu’an said he wanted to steal the other’s monsters, he actually controlled his Spider Queen to enter into the enemy jungle.

Ling Suyu finished off the last minion and ascended to level 2, illuminating his second skill “Shadow Steps” before decisively following.

He now had a speed-boosting skill, and he still had his Flash. Even if they ran into a pincer attack from the enemy jungler and marksman, he could no doubt escape.

Although going for the other’s monsters at a time like this was a bit dishonourable…if you can steal monsters, why not steal 0w0 ?

As the two proceeded, the surrounding fog dissipated, revealing a small purple dragon.

In《Glorious Heroes》, each bottom jungle, be it their own or the enemy’s, had a dragon. The dragon would spawn at set times, but the dragon’s colour would be random. Killing different-coloured dragons would grant different buffs.

Red dragons restored health, blue dragons restored magic points, green dragons increased speed, and purple dragons increased basic attack damage, manifesting as an electric current that accompanied every third basic attack. Thus, getting the purple dragon was also called getting the lightning strike.

Typically, when the match began, both junglers would first go for the dragons. Since the dragon in the enemy’s bottom jungle was still there, that meant— “Top lane watch out, the jungler’s in the top jungle,” Tang Yu’an said while simultaneously attacking the purple dragon with his Spider Queen, pulling the aggro for his marksman while luring the purple dragon into the undergrowth.

Ling Suyu relaxedly attacked from behind Tang Yu’an, while also following him into the undergrowth.

Perhaps he’d sensed something, but the enemy jungler suddenly stopped sending messages.

The enemy marksman and support appeared in the top-left corner of Ling Suyu’s minimap.

After finishing off the first wave of minions, they didn’t wait for the second wave, instead immediately disappearing into the fog.

“They’re coming,” Tang Yu’an said, then paused before opening his mouth again, “We can make it.”

“They’re coming”— meant the enemy marksman and support were coming for them.

“We can make it”—meant that they could finish off the dragon before they arrived.

Ling Suyu also felt that they could make it, so he never stopped attacking. Nevertheless, he didn’t use his first skill, which had just finished its cooldown, saving it for the enemy marksman.

As expected, in the moment he killed the dragon and got the lightning strike, the enemy Guardian Spirit and Abyssal Lord appeared in his field of vision.

The moment the two approached, Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen’s “Spidersilk Trap” flew precisely past Abyssal Lord and landed on Guardian Spirit.

Once the enemy marksman was controlled, Ling Suyu’s fingers tapped swiftly on his keyboard, adding a “Spirit Mark” onto the controlled Guardian Spirit. Then, he ran towards the enemy’s second defense tower while shooting!

Spider Queen’s passive “Fear Deterrence” buffed the basic attack damage of herself and ally targets.

The purple dragon’s buff was also geared towards basic attack damage.

Additionally, Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit was currently at level 2 while the other side was still at level 1, not even having had the time to change their gear. Cat Spirit’s basic attack damage would pack quite a punch for Guardian Spirit.

Guardian Spirit was hit by three normal attacks plus a lightning strike, its health instantly dropping to less than half.

After recovering, the player didn’t dare to counterattack, using a Flash to escape under his second tower as quickly as possible.

Guardian Spirit’s speed was no match for Cat Spirit, so running directly towards their base would be suicide!

He wanted to live, and the only path to survival was under the second defense tower, borrowing the tower’s strength to counter-kill. The game had just begun, and although she was at level 2, at best, the already-fragile Cat Spirit would only be able to take one hit from the tower.

However, Ling Suyu had long guessed that the other wanted to escape towards the tower, and while he was attacking had already moved a short distance in the tower’s direction.

At the same time, his support Spider Queen had also moved in front of him in advance.

Thus, Guardian Spirit’s Flash couldn’t get him out of the two’s attack range, and only succeeded in blinking from one side to another.

Even after using Flash, the other didn’t hesitate, quickly heading under the tower.

“Follow.” Tang Yu’an said, guiding his Spider Queen to press the other under the tower.

Ling Suyu didn’t even stop to consider, following him into the tower and unleashing two basic attacks, cleanly getting the kill.

【System】Cat Spirit, first blood!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

At nearly the same time as he got the kill, his fingers lightly tapped ‘w’ while simultaneously dragging his mouse. Cat Spirit immediately turned into a phantom and flashed out of the tower, catching up to the escaping support.

In the next moment, Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen used Flash to evade the second hit of tower damage, following behind him with less than a third of his blood left.

The enemy Abyssal Lord, in a bid to survive, instinctively opened both Abyss Shield and the Shield from his talent. However, be it the third tower or their base, they were both too far away. Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit attacked the entire way, and he was powerless to defend, only able to watch as his health dropped at a despairing rate until it finally hit zero.

【System】Cat Spirit, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain!

Ling Suyu pressed ‘a’ one final time, turning around before Abyssal Lord’s body even fell.

Reuniting with Tang Yu’an Spider Queen in the undergrowth, the two both pressed the Recall button.

However, with a “whoosh—”, the enemy Tempest Blade emerged from the fog, unwaveringly using a displacement skill to appear in the undergrowth the two were situated in, interrupting their recall.

Perhaps because the other never expected the two of them to actually be in the undergrowth, Tempest Blade paused for a moment before finally reacting, quickly using Swift Blade.

Simultaneously, Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen used Shield, protecting himself and his marksman, perfectly offsetting the damage.

Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit used “Spirit Mark”, which had finished cooling down, on Tempest Blade and started attacking!

Tempest Blade’s health was even more fragile compared to Guardian Spirit. Pretty much at the same time he stepped out of the undergrowth, he’d already fallen to the ground.

【System】Cat Spirit, triple kill! Rampage!

【System】The enemy Tempest Blade has been slain!

Just as Tempest Blade had been attacking the two, their mid-laner “if I have feet I can run”’s Vampiric Progenitor sent an “Enemy Hero Missing” signal.

Evidently, the enemy mid-laner Deepsea Faerie had been attracted by the commotion in the bottom jungle.

Ling Suyu and Tang Yu’an waited silently in the bushes without recalling.

As expected, not long after, the enemy mid-laner Deepsea Faerie appeared in their field of vision, casting “Lotus in the Water” towards the undergrowth.

Ling Suyu evaded with a Flash, directly responding with a flurry of basic attacks!

Tang Yu’an tanked the damage, following after his marksman with a sliver of blood. With a single “Spidersilk Trap”, he controlled Deepsea Faerie, making her unable to escape.

The immobile Deepsea Faerie could only watch as, following Cat Spirit’s attacks, her health bar swiftly dropped to zero.

【System】Cat Spirit, quadra kill! Godlike!

【System】The enemy Deepsea Faerie has been slain!

【System】Cat Spirit is unstoppable!

After getting four kills in a row, Ling Suyu pressed the recall button in satisfaction.

Tang Yu’an similarly recalled at the same time.

Their respective recall bars intertwined, creating an inexplicably beautiful scene.

The enemy top-laner had originally been fiercely clashing with Skeletal Empress, but after seeing that series of system announcements, he shrank back under his tower and started typing.

【Enemy】haven’t pierced the enemy’s armour (Black-Armoured Scythe): what the f*ck?!

At the same time, Ling Suyu’s teammates happily typed a bunch of “666”s.

【Ally】if i have feet i can run (Vampiric Progenitor): 666666 【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler (Blade Soul): 666666666 【Ally】can die but no need (Skeletal Empress): bad*ss!

Seeing the messages in the chat, Ling Suyu couldn’t help but laugh.

Closely following, Tang Yu’an’s amused voice sounded in his headphones: “Happy? Isn’t having an awesome support super nice?”

Hearing this line, Ling Suyu choked on his laugh and entered a coughing fit.

However, to be perfectly honest, this support was quite good. If it weren’t for his precise crowd control, accurate shield timing, and help tanking the tower’s hits, Ling Suyu wouldn’t have been able to get a quadra kill this easily.

Most importantly, the support’s grasp of timing and positioning was the type of stuff in his ideals, and he couldn’t pick out a single flaw.

Don’t even mention being badass or not, this was simply the support of his dreams!

“It was good.” In the end, after Ling Suyu found his voice again, he responded with these three words.

After hearing this reply, Tang Yu’an lightly chuckled: “Why of course, all the marksmen I’ve supported have all said it’s good.”

【Ally】if i have feet i can run (Vampiric Progenitor): ?

【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler (Blade Soul): brother if you’re so shameless even when taking advantage of a professional player, you must not be ordinary!

【Ally】can die but no need (Skeletal Empress): this dude, are you taking advantage of Feather because he’s soft?

Ling Suyu: ???

Wait a minute, since when was he soft!

Just as Ling Suyu was about to retort, before he could open his mouth, Tang Yu’an had already cut in: “Haiya[^1], I’m not taking advantage of Feather. It’s clearly that we’re a power duo, two swords together[^2], joining forces to get a quadra kill. If you don’t believe me, you can ask Feather, right?”

Ling Suyu: “.....right.”

“See~” Tang Yu’an dragged the last vowel of the word, “Personally verified by a professional player!”

Their teammates responded with a series of “2333” and “hhhhh”.

Ling Suyu did not say anything, but he couldn’t help raising the corners of his lips.

He usually didn’t enjoy speaking, and also didn’t like talking while gaming, so he was easily drawn towards outspoken people with leadership abilities.

Yet at the same time, he was also rather egotistical. If he didn’t agree with a command, he wouldn’t follow it, so he would also hope that the person giving commands would cooperate with him.

This support was chatty and had leadership skills. Every command was something he agreed with, and every move was one he’d hoped for.

It could be said that in all of Ling Suyu’s years of playing 《Glorious Heroes》, he’d never met a support that had suited him more.

If, during the “White Fox Cup”.....

Ling Suyu froze, quickly shaking his head to rid himself of the idea.

What did it matter if the support had been switched? He was already no longer a part of UH.

While Ling Suyu was still reflecting, a red message suddenly appeared in the chat box, coming from the enemy team.

【Enemy】beefing with whoever steals marksman (Guardian Spirit): I just don’t believe you’ll be able to maintain that 0-death streak! Feather, just you wait, I’ll definitely kill you once this match!

Ling Suyu raised an eyebrow, suddenly interested, and thus typed a reply.

【Ally】UH-feather: ok, i’ll be cheering for you.
