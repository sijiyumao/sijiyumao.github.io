This Weibo post from UH was sent not too long ago, and so far had only been seen by fans, all echoing their support:

【You’ve got my support! Looking forward to seeing UH’s performance during the Spring Seasonal!】

【Go Jiang Hao! Go UH!】

【With a substitute as useless as that, his contract should’ve ended long ago. He’s been wasting UH’s resources for five years!】

【Could it be that talks about ending his contract had already begun before the White Fox Cup? No wonder they played so poorly!】

The users from the other Weibo post were still busy with their own situation, not realizing that the UH official account had posted such a misleadingly-worded Weibo.

Yet UH’s braindead fans took it as “proof that UH’s loss during the White Fox Cup was Feather’s fault”. They screenshotted the message and posted it, which obviously stirred up another storm!

OP: 【Look! The Feather you all say carried the other four members has been chased away by UH!】

【F*ck! UH is actually inhumane!】

【Could UH be even more disgusting!】

OP: 【?】

OP: 【Are you all blind? What UH means is that the main reason for their loss during the White Fox Cup was Feather!】

【I’m not the blind one, you are! Feather competed in 17 matches during the White Fox Cup and got MVP 17 times. Yet UH fires him but not the others? Isn’t that disgusting?】

【What the hell? Does this Weibo actually exist? It’s like they’re implying that UH actively terminated Feather’s contract, yet it took them five years before they decided to terminate it? What the hell kind of “active termination” is this?】

【Didn’t the post next door say that ending the contract was Feather’s own decision?

What’s UH trying to say?】

The one who posted the screenshot probably didn’t expect it to crash and burn so severely and directly went into hiding.

The replies insulting UH continued to pour in.

【Does UH really think that if you’re not a professional player then you can’t understand what goes on in a professional match?】

【Does UH still think they’re that same team from five years ago? That because no one’s paying attention to you, you can say whatever you want?】

【Feather’s way too tragic! Manipulated by such a trash team for a full five teams, and just when he’s managed to end his contract, he got blamed for such a huge mess!】

【Gonna go throw some insults at UH on Weibo!】

【Don’t, UH’s fans are all insane, they’ll return your insults tenfold!】

【F*ck, my comments were deleted and my account’s blocked! No wonder you can’t see a single negative comment, all our allies have been locked up!】

【Have the other teams reacted?】

【If the other teams react, they’ll just be attracting fire to themselves. Remaining silent is the logical move.】

【So we can only watch Feather be bullied by UH? I’m so pissed!】

“F*ck! UH is so shameless!”

Fight’s mid-laner Dou Jiayan, currently taking the subway back home, decided to browse Weibo to kill some time. He never thought that he’d come across such a shameless official announcement.

He even wanted to personally jump in, but was also afraid of dragging his own team into the storm. After much thought, he ultimately decided to call his captain.

At that moment, Tang Yu’an was driving, nearing the internet cafe where Ling Suyu was at.

Suddenly, he received the call from Dou Jiayan, and after a moment’s hesitation, decided to pick up.

Dou Jiayan’s enraged voice sounded from the in-vehicle display: “How could UH dare?! How could they be so shameless!”

Tang Yu’an blinked, face full of confusion: “Wait a minute, calm down, speak slowly, what did UH do?”

Dou Jiayan took a deep breath, then used his sub-par summarizing skills to sum up UH’s official Weibo post while adding his own embellishments: “...basically what they’re saying is their performance during the White Fox Cup was Feather’s fault, and Feather needs to take full responsibility. They had to punish Feather, so they kicked him out of the team. F*ck, did Feather eat their rice or something?! If any team dared to treat me like this, I’d f*cking…”

Tang Yu’an: “And what would you do?”

Dou Jiayan: “I’d pour oil all over the club that night and set it on fire!”

Tang Yu’an couldn’t help letting out a laugh.

Perhaps this was youthful recklessness.

Indeed, children’s speech knows no bounds.

“How could you laugh?” Dou Jiayan complained, “We’re all e-sports players, but you’re heartless!”

“Yes, yes, I’m the heartless one.” Tang Yu’an held back his laughter, “And your heartless captain is currently on his way to pick Feather up.”

Dou Jiayan: ???

“Wait, really?”

“Why are you so happy?” Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow, “Someone seemed quite against me poaching Feather in the beginning.”

“Eh, people change, it’s just that plans can’t keep up with the change…” Dou Jiayan guiltily scratched his head. But be it his demeanour or his tone, both were much more relaxed compared to before. “So, captain, did you successfully poach Feather? Does this mean that I can see him when I get back to the club tomorrow?”

Tang Yu’an: “What do you mean poach? Even UH said that Feather terminated his contract.

I'm just signing a new player…Wait, vacation just started, why the hell are you coming back?

Go home and enjoy the break.” After a brief pause, he didn’t give Dou Jiayan the chance to reply, swiftly starting another topic, “By the way, it’s a bit inconvenient for me right now because I’m driving. Help me log onto Fight’s official account and send a Weibo. Draft and send out exactly what I say, if you dare to even add half a word more, watch how I’ll deal with you when you get back!”

“Okay~” Dou Jiayan not only didn’t feel threatened, but even seemed eager to get started, “What’s the password again?”

Tang Yu’an felt reassured as he gave him the password.

Although Dou Jiayan appeared unreliable, he was prudent when the situation called for it. He had the tendency to verbally exaggerate, but wouldn’t actually mess around with the official account.

Dou Jiayan logged onto Fight’s official account and typed out the Weibo post Tang Yu’an wanted word-for-word. After hitting send, he logged out with satisfaction and started messing around again: “Captain, when you see Feather, can you secretly take a picture of him and send it to me?”

Tang Yu’an: ?

“Why are you being creepy?”

“No, no, don’t get me wrong, I just want to confirm that he’s actually coming to Fight,” Dou Jiayan explained in a panic, “If you really can’t, could you trick him into taking a photo with you and send that to me instead?”

Tang Yu’an rolled his eyes: “Just ride the damn subway, Yanzi.”

After that, he hung up.

As he finished his call, he’d also arrived at his destination.

Tang Yu’an patiently parked the car before getting off and walking towards the entrance of the internet cafe, giving Ling Suyu a phone call.

UH’s Weibo post was way too obviously misleading, as if they were taking people to be idiots.

After Tang Yu’an learned of it, there were no fluctuations in his heart. It wasn’t that he didn’t feel bad for Ling Suyu, but rather felt that UH’s words could only go so far as to trick their braindead fans. Any real fan of e-sports wouldn’t be swayed, and they’d soon reap what they sowed.

The Weibo post he got Dou Jiayan to send was just to push things along.

When Ling Suyu received Tang Yu’an’s call, his heart rate that he’d just managed to calm down sped up again, and he picked up in a fluster: “Hello?”

His voice, on the other hand, sounded quite calm, and Tang Yu’an didn’t realize his nervousness at all: “I’m here, at the door. You can come outside. Don’t rush, go at your own pace, don’t forget anything.”

“En,” Ling Suyu replied, one hand keeping his phone raised while the other logged out of his game account. After shutting off the computer, he hesitated for another moment before asking, “Then I’ll hang up first?”

“Sure.”

After Ling Suyu hung up the phone, he double checked the entire room to ensure he hadn’t left anything behind before taking a deep breath and stepping out with his suitcase in tow.

As he left the internet cafe, he couldn’t help but reminisce about the scene when he had first joined UH.

He’d already forgotten most of the details, including his feelings at the time. Had he been…

happy?

Probably. After all, it was then when he transformed from an ordinary player to a professional, and the first step he took towards his dream.

But that dream ultimately shattered.

Thankfully, he never gave up, and the heavens had yet to abandon him.

No matter how many insults and blame he had to shoulder, there was still someone willing to tear through the darkness above him. To see him for what he was worth, and reach out their hand.

Ling Suyu couldn’t stop the corners of his lips from lifting, and his footsteps similarly became lighter.

He couldn’t tell whether he was more nervous or excited.

But he knew that, at that moment, he was happy.

He felt that his future was full of hope.

Before he’d even exited the internet cafe, he spotted Tang Yu'an from afar, standing at the cafe’s door.

With a black windbreaker and a red scarf around his neck, he appeared handsome and eye-catching.

A girl passed by and, perhaps because she was blown away by his looks, covered her face and smiled at him.

Seeing this, he smiled and nodded in response before shifting his gaze away, gentle and respectful.

Just a few days ago, Ling Suyu had seen his pictures, had watched his videos, but today was his first time seeing him in person. He was even more handsome than he was on-screen, as if he’d walked straight out of a drama.

Ling Suyu’s footsteps suddenly slowed. A few short steps seemed to span an entire century.

Hearing the sound of the suitcase’s wheels, Tang Yu’an raised his head and glanced over.

Immediately, his gaze caught on the youth in a white puffer jacket.

Oh, right, he couldn’t be considered that young. His searches on Baidu had said the other was already 20 years old, only one year younger than himself.

But in comparison to his own height at 186cm, Ling Suyu, who didn’t even reach 180cm, appeared so small to Tang Yu’an. As he slowly made his way over, wrapped in a puffer jacket, he was almost like a little penguin(?) “Ahem.” Tang Yu’an put a stop to that chain of thought. Taking the initiative to greet the other, he lifted a hand towards the suitcase in Ling Suyu’s hands, “Need help with that?”

Good lord, now that they were closer, he appeared even smaller.

Ling Suyu raised his head and locked eyes with Tang Yu’an for two seconds before finally reacting: “No need…”

Before he could even finish, the suitcase in his hands had already been taken.

Ling Suyu: ?

Tang Yu’an, without another word, walked away while dragging Ling Suyu’s suitcase behind him.

Ling Suyu opened his mouth, but ultimately didn’t say anything, obediently following behind him. At the same time, he breathed a sigh of relief.

It really was Tang Yu’an!

Fight’s Tang Yu’an, the authentic one!

It wasn’t a scam!

Tang Yu’an, who didn’t know he almost became a scammer, took Ling Suyu to his car. After putting the suitcase in the trunk, he personally opened the door to the front passenger seat for him.

Ling Suyu couldn’t help but feel that something was off, but he still said “Thanks” before getting in the car. Then he watched as Tang Yu’an shut the door, circled back around to the driver’s seat, and opened the door.

Wait a moment?

Was he actually joining a team and not obtaining a new boyfriend?

Isn’t Fight’s captain being way too enthusiastic!

No, maybe he was reading too much into it…how could he have such thoughts at this moment?

Just as Ling Suyu was letting his thoughts run wild, Tang Yu’an, who had just got in, asked him a question: “Have you checked Weibo?”

Ling Suyu’s face was full of confusion: “No…what’s wrong?”

“Oh, nevermind then, no need to check,” Tang Yu’an said, starting the car as if nothing had happened.

Ling Suyu stared blankly at him for a moment before fishing his phone out of his pocket, clicking Weibo open.

What was there to see on Weibo? Should he also take a look?
