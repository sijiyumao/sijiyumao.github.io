After that, the hostess asked a few more questions about their tactics. Ling Suyu didn’t dare to answer, keeping his mouth closed the entire time, waiting until Tang Yu’an finished speaking before letting out a soft “Mm” as his response.

When the interview ended, Ling Suyu blinked in confusion at the host, who was beaming with a doting smile, not understanding why she was smiling so happily.

Tang Yu’an, who had guessed what the host was thinking, said nothing. He raised his hand very naturally and gently cupped Ling Suyu’s back with his palm: “We’re done. Let’s head back.”

Ling Suyu: “Mm—”

That evening, after the league officials released the video of that day’s match, they also included the post-match interview.

After watching Fight’s post-match interview, some were surprised, while others screamed.

Those who were surprised— 【How come Feather’s become so cute after joining Fight? Where’s the Feather Dad who snapped at Ma Mingxi during the White Fox Cup?】

【Before today, I’d always thought that Feather was cold, my bad, I was wrong.】

【Haah, Feather’s so obedient, I’m liking him more and more. To be honest though, I’d

rather he were a bit more stubborn. That way, he wouldn’t have been bullied so badly back in UH.】

【That’s ok, Feather’s in Fight now! I won’t even mention the others—at least Fight’s

captain treats him super well, no way he’ll be bullied again!】

The screamers— 【AAAAAA the way you two banter back and forth, how could you be such a perfect match!】

【Other teams only send one person up for interviews, so how come you guys are

coming up together?!】

【So sweet! I can’t stop smiling!】

Although Fight didn’t confiscate their players’ phones, during the tournament everyone was busy reviewing matches and training, so they didn’t have time to scroll through Weibo, forums, or Bilibili, and thus hadn’t realized that the buzz around Fight has been steadily rising these past few days.

Some fans were drawn in by Fight’s standout performance in the Spring Seasonals and believe the team had a strong chance of winning the championship.

Others were captivated by the pure “camaraderie” between Fight’s captain and their new marksman, hoping to see more interaction between them and wishing for the team to go far in the league.

The other Fight players either didn’t see these discussions or didn’t have time to read them, but Chen Jie did.

Though he saw it, he didn’t say a word.

Ever since Tang Yu’an assured him that he wouldn’t let personal feelings take precedence over competing, Chen Jie had stopped commenting on the “enthusiasm” Tang Yu’an showed toward Ling Suyu.

After the post-match review, he immediately led Fight in analyzing their next opponent, GQ.

Unlike OsO, GQ wasn’t some dark horse. They’d competed in the BPL during the previous seasons, advancing to the group stages, only to be eliminated in both the Spring and Summer Seasonals, failing to reach the quarterfinals.

However, last season, they were soundly defeated by Winne and SMG in the Spring Seasonals, and lost to YJ and UH in the Summer Seasonals.

In other words, they hadn’t faced Fight yet.

When it came to teams you’d never faced before, although there were plenty of match recordings you could use to analyze their style and tactics, without having actually played against them, no one could guarantee what would happen in a real match.

“In any case, we need to be prepared to field Cat Spirit and Spider.” Chen Jie said, “Although our goal is the championship, if we can’t even make it to the quarterfinals, nothing else matters. We need to win at least two matches in the group stage, so we must win both today’s and tomorrow’s matches. If we can also win tomorrow’s match 2-0, then when we face igame, we can use a different lineup to test the waters in one game, in preparation for the quarterfinal points-based matches.”

“So, have GQ and igame already played?” Dou Jiayan asked, “What was the result?”

Chen Jie: “2-0. igame won.”

Dou Jiayan: “Oh, that’s not really surprising.”

Chen Jie: “If we let our guard down because of that, it could be fatal.”

“Got it, got it,” Dou Jiayan replied half-heartedly, unable to resist muttering under his breath, “Jie-ge’s gotten more blabbery since he stopped competing.”

Chen Jie swatted Dou Jiayan’s head with the palm of his hand, but it barely grazed his hair —he couldn’t bring himself to actually hit him hard enough to hurt.

Still, Dou Jiayan let out an “Ouch!” and raised his hand to cover his head, sticking out his tongue.

Ling Suyu watched the two of them interact, blinked, and said nothing.

Chen Jie was always complaining that Tang Yu’an was too friendly with him, but from Ling Suyu’s perspective, weren’t Yanzi and Chen Jie pretty close based on their interactions too?

He just couldn’t figure it out.

The next evening, before the match between Fight and GQ began, the first game featured igame vs. OsO.

Perhaps because they’d been crushed by Fight the day before, OsO played exceptionally conservatively today, desperately defending their towers in the early game and not giving igame any opportunities to tower-dive or gank.

In the end, the first game lasted 36 minutes, and igame barely managed to win after securing two Sunbirds, two Dragons, and one Hero’s Chalice.

However, in the second game, which lasted 16 minutes, igame replicated the dominant performance Fight had shown against OsO.

It was clear that the players on OsO had their mentalities shaken. Perhaps due to their lack of tournament experience, they made significantly more mistakes in the second game after losing the first.

However, since Fight’s matches against OsO had already concluded, their focus wasn’t on OsO, but on igame.

“igame’s mid laner is actually still using Curse Master,” Chen Jie couldn’t help but remark.

“Huh?” Manager Ye Xiran, standing nearby, was puzzled. “Isn’t Feather still using Desert Hunter?”

“It’s different. Even though their auto-attack damage has been nerfed, burst-damage marksmen and sustained-damage marksmen still have their own distinct advantages. Burst-damage marksmen are definitely stronger in the early game, but in the late game, as long as they’re played well, both types can deal comparable damage. Mages, on the other hand, rely on their abilities—their effectiveness in team fights is already capped by their skillset. If their damage is nerfed any further, they’re basically useless.”

“I don’t really get it…” Although Ye Xiran also played Glorious, he just played for fun—he didn’t care about any of this technical stuff. Chen Jie’s explanation went in one ear and out the other, but he did catch a bit of it. “So Curse Master is basically unusable, right? Then why is igame’s mid-laner still using him?”

“Maybe he just loves the champion too much,” Chen Jie said. “Plus, his gameplay is good enough that he’s really pushed the hero to its limits. I just think that if he’d picked a meta-dominant champion, igame might already be champions by now.”

“That’s not necessarily true,” Ye Xiran replied absentmindedly as he took a bite of an apple.

“Maybe he’s the only one who can play Curse Master.”

Chen Jie shot him a helpless glance and said no more.

He didn’t believe igame’s mid-laner could only play Curse Master, but igame had indeed been using Curse Master consistently.

There was absolutely no need to ban Curse Master this season, so whether it was the qualifiers or yesterday’s and today’s matches, igame had picked Curse Master every time.

Would they pick Curse Master for tomorrow’s match against Fight as well?

Or was igame hiding something, just like how they had Ling Suyu play Desert Hunter to conceal his true strength?

Soon, the match between Fight and GQ began.

Today’s commentary was handled by two other commentators, Liang Fan and Remi.

Liang Fan was a male commentator with a deep, resonant voice: “Fight’s playing this match, which I’ve actually been pretty looking forward to. Their opponent, GQ, is a veteran team, so I’m sure this match will be incredibly exciting.”

Remi was a female commentator with a sweet, girlish voice: “Wow, Fight’s been getting a lot of attention lately. I’ve been hoping for a chance to commentate on their matches, so I’m really happy to get the chance today!”

Liang Fan: “Are you looking forward to their performance on the field, or to some sweet moments in the bot lane?”

Remi: “Oh no, did I get caught? Okay, actually, both! But what I’m most excited about is whether Desert Hunter will make an appearance in today’s match! It’s about time the BPL marksmen updated their champion pools!”

Liang Fan: “Both teams are starting the draft phase now. Let’s see if GQ will ban Desert Hunter. ”

Remi: “Mm-hmm! Fight’s captain explicitly said in yesterday’s post-match interview that Feather’s Desert Hunter was really strong, and he’s not wrong. I’m sure everyone who’s been following the qualifiers can see that Feather’s Desert Hunter is incredibly intimidating.

But compared to that, I still think his Candy Witch is even stronger. What do you think, Liang Fan?”

Liang Fan: “ Actually, it depends on the early and late game. In the early game, Candy Witch is definitely stronger, but in the late game, Desert Hunter has higher damage output.”

While the two spoke, GQ selected their first ban.

“GQ has banned Phoenix Soul first!” Remi commented, “It seems it’s not just Feather.

Fight’s mid-laner Swallow’s Phoenix Soul has also left a deep impression on everyone!”

【However, after Phoenix Soul was nerfed, its late-game impact is still fairly limited. I

wonder what Swallow will pick for this match?】

Just as Liang Fan said this, Fight placed Beast Master on the ban list.

Beast Master was a very strong hero, the kind that could truly carry a team, but unfortunately, he wasn’t a good fit for Dou Jiayan, so no matter who Fight picked, he wouldn’t be able to play this hero.

Immediately after, GQ banned Harpist, a hero with a playstyle similar to Phoenix Soul.

Fight quickly followed by banning Realm God.

Then, on GQ’s side, they used their final ban slot on Array Sprite.

In other words— “Desert Hunter is unbanned!” Liang Fan and Remi exclaimed almost simultaneously!

Liang Fan immediately added, “None of Feather’s go-to heroes, Desert Hunter nor Candy Witch, made it onto the ban list! It seems GQ is confident they can hold their ground against the pressure Feather is putting on them, huh?”

“I just wonder if Fight will actually pick it!” Remi pondered curiously.

“I think they will,” Liang Fan replied. “After all, both teams already exchanged trash talk before the match started!”

Just like in previous matches, there was a warm-up before this one—commonly known as the “trash-talking segment.”

GQ spoke first, taunting Fight: “If you’ve got the guts, use Desert Hunter against us too!”

From Fight’s side, Tang Yu’an replied, “Sure. If you dare to release him, we dare to pick him.”

Sure enough, after the champions for all other positions had been selected, Fight chose Desert Hunter for the final slot.

The moment this champion was revealed, the entire audience erupted!

Some spectators were reminded of Feather’s dominant performance in the qualifiers and hoped to witness something similar firsthand at today’s match.

Others were aware of Desert Hunter’s early-game weaknesses but admired Fight and Feather’s courage, eagerly anticipating how this match would unfold.

On Fight’s side, after handing Desert Hunter over to Ling Suyu, the entire team felt a sense of relief.

Even though this hero was practically useless if he couldn’t establish an early lead, because Ling Suyu was the one playing him, they weren’t worried at all. In fact, they were brimming with confidence about the match’s outcome.

However, on the GQ side, seeing Desert Hunter, the entire team also breathed a sigh of relief.

“They’re actually using Desert Hunter—our night-long hard work wasn’t for nothing.”

“You bet! We spent the whole night studying Desert Hunter.”

“We’ve got to crush Feather today!”
