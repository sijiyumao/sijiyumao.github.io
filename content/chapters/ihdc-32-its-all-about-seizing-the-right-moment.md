By the time Tang Yu'an returned to their base, he was down to his last bit of health, and Ling Suyu wasn’t in great shape either. The two of them sneaked their way over to a hidden corner before pressing recall, wary of enemy junglers coming to gank them.

As for the 0-for-1 exchange, here’s how their teammates reacted:

Mo Xiaoyun: “Not bad!”

Yu He: “That was satisfying.”

Dou Jiayan hadn’t even processed what had happened yet: “Huh? That fast? So the person who yells the loudest really is the weakest?”

Tang Yu’an: “Man, if Ma Mingxi heard you say that, he’d chase you down the entire game just to kill you, all while carrying the name ‘Ximing Ma’!”

Over on Chen Jie’s end, since he was watching from the spectator view, there was a 3–5 minute delay. When he saw Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu dash into the tower, he held his breath in anxiety. That was, until he saw Ling Suyu’s “Explosive Shell” land a critical hit, cleanly taking down Ma Mingxi, and only then did he breathe a sigh of relief.

Regardless of whether Tang Yu’an survived this exchange, as long as Ling Suyu secured a kill on Ma Mingxi and stayed alive, it was a win.

But even with Tang Yu’an’s play being so risky, Ling Suyu actually dared to follow him?

Although Chen Jie had reviewed the ranked match between Fight and SMG and gained a general understanding of Ling Suyu’s skill level, he felt that regardless of Ling Suyu’s unique style, his technical skills were at least superior to his own.

But watching his old match-footage, Chen Jie also felt that Ling Suyu was indeed overly cautious. On several occasions, he had unhesitatingly sacrificed his teammates out of fear of threats that didn’t even seem to exist.

The reason the same situation didn’t arise in that ranked match between Fight and SMG was entirely because Fight placed a high priority on protecting their marksman from the start.

On one hand, this was because their captain, Tang Yu’an, was always harping on things like, “The marksman is the carry”, “Without the marksman, what are we going to rely on for damage?”, and “Not only do I have to protect him, but you all have to protect him—the whole world has to protect him”. As a result, even during a season where basic attacks were nerfed and the marksman position’s very survival was under threat, the entire Fight roster remained influenced by this mindset, placing immense trust and reliance on the marksman’s damage output.

On the other hand, compared to other players in the same role, Chen Jie wasn’t particularly outstanding. If he ever found himself in a situation where he had to survive entirely on his own without any protection, it was safe to say Fight might not even have made it to the quarterfinals. This was purely a tactical consideration.

In short, it was precisely because of the protection provided by the entire Fight team that Ling Suyu was able to showcase his full potential in the ranked match against SMG.

But in that previous engagement, his only teammate was Tang Yu’an, and Tang Yu’an’s health wasn’t exactly in great shape—yet he actually dared to follow?

Chen Jie had these doubts solely because he didn’t understand Ling Suyu well enough.

From Ling Suyu’s perspective, whether to follow or not was easy to determine as long as he can clearly answer the following questions:

1. Where is the enemy jungler?

Answer: The enemy top laner arrived at the forefront of their lane too early, indicating that they were running a bottom-first strategy. The enemy jungler had already missed the optimal window to gank the bottom lane and was likely in the top or jungle right now. He wouldn’t be able to get down there anytime soon, so there’s no need to worry about getting ganked.

2. Will I die if I push the tower?

Answer: With Tang Yu’an tanking the tower’s damage, there was no need to worry about being killed by the tower. The enemy ADC had already used up his skills, so there was no risk of being combo-ed to death by him either. In conclusion, no, he wouldn’t die.

3. Could I secure the kill on Ma Mingxi?

Answer: After factoring in the enemy support casting his shield in time and the possibility that Tang Yu’an’s poke skills might miss, there was still a chance, as long as his second ability landed.

Now that these three questions were answered, was there still even a question about whether Ling Suyu should follow the play?

Ling Suyu himself would wonder why he shouldn’t follow.

He had no reason not to.

After all, the worst-case scenario was Tang Yu’an’s Deepsea Faerie dying. Even if it was a 1-for-1 trade, support for marksman, it was still a win.

Indeed, Ling Suyu’s could see that far ahead.

He chose to follow Tang Yu’an’s lead because he was fully aware of how the operation would play out.

As for Tang Yu’an, he chose to set that pace because he knew Ling Suyu could foresee things to that extent.

He knew Ling Suyu’s awareness and reaction speed could keep up with his own, so he didn’t even bother to warn him before using Flash to chase the enemy into the tower.

Reality proved him right.

His marksman not only didn’t let him down, but performed even better than he’d imagined!

Ling Suyu had calculated the enemy marksman’s health precisely. As he finished him off with an Explosive Shell, he used the bullet’s “recoil” to dash out of the tower, displaying a certain “cool guys never look back at explosions” type of nonchalance.

It was precisely because Ling Suyu exited the tower so quickly that Tang Yu’an was able to escape before the enemy turret could land its second hit, surviving with just a sliver of health.

Ma Mingxi was pretty much kicking himself at the moment. He shouldn’t have locked onto Ling Suyu. If he’d just attacked Tang Yu’an one more time—even if it meant trading a marksman for their support—that would’ve been a kill!

To make matters worse, his teammates were making fun of him.

Mid laner: “All I can say is… history has an interesting way of repeating itself, doesn’t it?”

Top laner: “Feather always plays it safe under his tower when laning against other marksmen. How come you’re the only one getting tower-dived and killed before level 6?”

“Damn it!” Ma Mingxi roared in frustration, making the entire YJ team adjust their headsets.

Captain Cheng Cheng turned his head helplessly to glance at the completely frazzled marksman beside him: “Mingxi, calm down. Our strength lies in team fights. We’ll show them what we’re made of in those. Just farm safely before level 6, don’t be reckless.”

Jungler Xiao Hong: “I’m going to gank bot lane right now!”

Cheng Cheng: “You calm down too!”

Both YJ’s marksman and jungler were overly impulsive, a trait with both pros and cons.

The upside was that they’d often charge forward at the most unlikely moments, then “unexpectedly” pull off a win, laying the groundwork for victory.

The downside was that they often charged headlong at inopportune moments and, unsurprisingly, got crushed by the enemy.

So, YJ didn’t excel at late-game plays. Or, to be precise, they weren’t good at handling games where they were falling behind.

YJ either steamrolled through a game while they were ahead or collapsed completely when they fell behind, never recovering.

So it was actually quite simple for Fight to beat YJ: just build a bigger lead than YJ in the early game.

But putting that into practice wasn’t so easy.

At the 6-minute mark:

【System】Wind Guardian, one kill! Swift as lightning!

【System】Ally Phoenix Soul has been slain!

Ling Suyu froze for a moment when he saw those two system notifications.

While laning against Ma Mingxi, he had been keeping a close eye on the minimap to track the enemy jungler’s position at all times, and had also glanced at his teammates’ health bars.

Dou Jiayan’s Phoenix Soul had been at full health one second, and the next, his health bar was completely wiped out.

The key point was that neither jungler was in the mid lane.

In other words, he had been instantly killed by the enemy mid laner during a 1v1 duel.

The “Swift as lightning” suffix in the system notification confirmed this—in《Glorious Heroes》, this suffix only appears when a target was killed upon being dealt damage exceeding 60% of their maximum health within an extremely short timeframe.

Otherwise, if the kill took too long, the suffix “Dominating” would appear.

“What happened?” Yu He asked as he used his Double Phantom’s dash to move to mid lane and clear the minions there.

“I hit level 6 a second later than him, so I didn’t have time to cast my ultimate,” Dou Jiayan explained, “It’s fine. I’ll come back and take him down once I respawn!”

“So the enemy mid-laner doesn’t have his ultimate right now, huh?” Yu He said, feeling a bit restless, but then glanced at his own level: level 5.

Alright, never mind.

At that moment, Ling Suyu’s Desert Hunter and Tang Yu’an’s Deepsea Faerie both reached level 6.

After clearing a wave of minions, they both returned to their base to replenish their health and buy items.

While shopping for items, Ling Suyu took a quick look at both teams’ gold counts.

He had killed Ma Mingxi once before reaching level 6, and since then had been completely dominating the enemy bottom laners, so Ma Mingxi and his support were running low on gold.

However, when it came to overall team gold, YJ actually had the advantage.

The highest gold earner was YJ’s mid laner, Wind Guardian, followed by the jungler, Blade Soul, and then the top laner, Black-Armoured Scythe.

Not only was Dou Jiayan’s gold outpaced by the enemy mid laner, but Yu He and Mo Xiaoyun’s gold was also outpaced by the enemy jungler and top laner respectively.

Looking back on the match against YJ when he was still with UH, the huge economic gap could have been attributed to his UH teammates making all sorts of mistakes and giving away kills.

But in this game, all he could say was that YJ was truly strong—they really lived up to their reputation as the second-ranked team in the entire country.

Ling Suyu pursed his lips, preparing to farm safely and focus on the late game.

However, Tang Yu’an had other ideas: “We’re falling behind in gold. We need to create a breakthrough. Yanzi, don’t go back to your lane after you respawn. Come straight to the bottom lane. Yu He, clear your minions and come down immediately. Xiaoyun, do your best to hold off the enemy top laner and don’t let him recall.”

His teammates all agreed, showing not a shred of doubt in Tang Yu’an’s commands.

Ling Suyu blinked.

Were they planning to... team fight in the bottom lane?

Soon, Dou Jiayan’s Phoenix Soul respawned and rushed to the bottom lane. Yu He, having cleared his minions and reached level 6, prepared his ultimate and lurked in the bushes near the enemy bottom lane’s first tower.

“Go for it, Swallow!”

Almost the moment Dou Jiayan arrived, Tang Yu’an urged him on while closing in on the enemy bottom lane’s tower.

In fact, the moment Dou Jiayan respawned, YJ had vaguely sensed that something was off, with their marksman and support both farming from beneath their tower with extreme caution.

But it wasn’t until Dou Jiayan’s Phoenix Soul appeared in the bottom lane that YJ knew something was wrong. YJ’s mid laner rushed down to help, but it was already too late— timing was everything in a fight!

Dou Jiayan unleashed a combo of his Phoenix Soul’s first, second, and third skills. With his first skill, “Phoenix Feather”, he fired a feather toward the enemy tower. Upon landing, it ignited into a ball of flame, dealing damage to both the enemy marksman and support.

His second skill, “Rebirth from the Flames”, allowed him to directly vanish from his current location and reappear amidst the flames at the spot where his first skill was cast. The moment he was “reborn”, he dealt another burst of fire damage to surrounding targets.

His third skill, “Sky of Flaming Feathers”, caused the red-haired girl to unfurl flaming wings from her back, firing countless feathers in all directions!

This combo happened in an instant, and was impossible to dodge. Even though Ma Mingxi activated his Spiritual Shield and used Flash with lightning-fast reflexes, he still took massive damage!

His support shielded him as he retreated, but by then, the rest of Fight’s team had followed Phoenix Soul and flooded the tower.

Tang Yu’an used “Patrol” to pin the support in place, while Ling Suyu used “Hound’s Charge”

to close in on Ma Mingxi. As he locked onto him with his cursor, he activated his ultimate, “Roar of the Desert”, boosting his damage for 5 seconds, and unleashed a barrage of shots!

Ma Mingxi knew he couldn’t escape. He stopped in his tracks and countered with Entangling Vines followed by Arrows of Light. Ling Suyu dodged it with a single “Explosive Shell”, then used Flash to charge back in, firing away!

“Damn it!” were Ma Mingxi’s last words.

This Desert Hunter hero was just too damn agile!

【System】Desert Hunter, one kill!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

Although Ma Mingxi died, he activated his ultimate before going down, thinking that even if he couldn’t kill the Desert Hunter, he could at least chip away at his health a bit, making him an easier target for their jungler.

Ling Suyu couldn’t dodge this ultimate, but Tang Yuan’s shield arrived just in time, absorbing most of the damage. Combined with Ling Suyu’s own healing, his health didn’t drop a single point.

Seeing this, Ma Mingxi nearly spat out a mouthful of blood.

At this moment, the damage on Fight’s side was being absorbed by Dou Jiayan’s Phoenix Soul. After taking a hit from the tower that nearly wiped out his health bar and drawing a second tower hit onto himself, he decisively used his ultimate ability, “Soul’s Concealment”, which he hadn’t had time to use during the laning phase.

The unique aspect of Phoenix Soul was that her ultimate wasn’t a damage-dealing ability—it was used for healing.

After using “Soul’s Concealment”, Phoenix Soul transformed into a semi-transparent phoenix that could not be targeted or attacked for 5 seconds, during which she restored 20% of her maximum health.

While in the concealed state, she could still move, though her movement speed was reduced by 20%.

Even so, five seconds was enough time for Dou Jiayan to escape the tower.

“Yes! I’m alive!”

As Dou Jiayan cheered, Tang Yu’an’s Deepsea Faerie stepped in to absorb the tower’s damage.

Ling Suyu confirmed he was steadily tanking the tower’s damage, finished off the support with a few hits, and then rushed out of the tower as fast as he could.

【System】Desert Hunter, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain!

Just as the enemy support fell, the enemy mid laner and jungler had already rushed to the bottom lane. However, before the enemy mid laner could join the fray, he was ambushed by Yu He, who had been lying in wait in the bushes, catching him completely off guard!

Assassin-type heroes relied on burst damage. Yu He unleashed a full combo, instantly crippling the enemy mid laner.

The enemy mid laner Wind Guardian used his second ability, “Windstorm”, to knock Yu He back, then unleashed a combo of his own.

Yu He reacted quickly, dodging with a dash, then activated his ultimate to increase his speed. He circled around the range of the enemy’s ultimate to catch up to the fleeing Wind Guardian and finished him off cleanly!

【System】Double Phantom, one kill! Swift as lightning!

【System】The enemy Wind Guardian has been slain!

But immediately afterward, he was instantly killed by Blade Soul, who burst out of the fog.

【System】Blade Soul, one kill!

【System】Ally Double Phantom has been slain!

After Blade Soul killed Yu He’s Double Phantom, he immediately used his newly-refreshed dash to enter the fray, locked onto Dou Jiayan’s low-health Phoenix Soul, and unleashed his ultimate!

“Save me!” Dou Jiayan frantically tapped Q and W, chaining his first and second skills together. Miraculously, he managed to dodge the ultimate using his skill’s passive even though his Flash was still on cooldown, but he was left with barely a sliver of health.

Blade Soul then caught up to him and finished him off with a single strike!

【System】Blade Soul, double kill! Peerless!

【System】Ally Phoenix Soul has been slain!

At that moment, Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu had both left their towers. Just as Blade Soul struck Dou Jiayan, Tang Yu’an activated his second skill “Patrol”. Deepsea Faerie released a jellyfish which struck Blade Soul upon its return, denying him the chance to use “Absolute Defense”.

Ling Suyu seized the opportunity to raise his gun and fire—bang, bang, bang!

After recovering from the crowd control, Blade Soul immediately used his refreshed dash ability.

Ling Suyu hid behind Tang Yu'an, attacking while maneuvering.

Unable to hit Ling Suyu, Blade Soul had to target Tang Yu'an instead. With his health critically low, Tang Yu'an cast “Energy Drain”, instantly restoring a small portion of his health.

He immediately followed up with “Water Lotus”, landing a precise hit on Blade Soul before unleashing his ultimate “Deepsea Ballad”!

The ultimate “Deepsea Ballad” dealt damage by stacking marks.

This also meant its damage wasn’t as heavily affected by gear.

Blade Soul watched his health plummet and wanted to shout that this was insane!

But before he could even finish his exclamation, Ling Suyu finished him off with two basic attacks.

【System】Desert Hunter, one kill!

【System】The enemy Blade Soul has been slain!

With that, the team fight in the bottom lane came to a complete end.

On Fight’s side, the jungler and mid laner were down.

On YJ’s side, only the top laner, who hadn’t participated in the fight, remained alive.

“Two for four, what a steal!” Tang Yu’an said casually, despite his health being reduced to the bare minimum.

Ling Suyu took a deep breath. His entire body was still tense from the pressure, and his heart was racing.

Realizing he couldn’t make it in time, the enemy top laner simply gave up on rushing back and remained in the top lane, continuing to duel Mo Xiaoyun.

With no real threat in sight, Tang Yu’an, still clinging to his last bit of health, joined Ling Suyu in pushing down the enemy bottom lane’s outer turret, then brazenly recalled right on the spot.

“I died. I’m not happy.” Dou Jiayan didn’t feel like he’d won this team fight at all. He’d died twice, only managed two assists, and didn’t even get a single kill. He felt like he’d lost a fortune.

But YJ was clearly feeling much worse than he was right now.

“Phoenix Soul is still so annoying even after being nerfed!” Ma Mingxi couldn’t help but complain.

“Ruicong, you should’ve come down after killing Phoenix Soul,” said team captain Cheng Cheng.

Ruicong was the name of YJ’s mid laner, with his full name being Gu Ruicong. Hearing his captain’s words, he felt like he had a million complaints he couldn't voice: “Bro, do you think it’s that easy to kill Phoenix Soul? He died, and I was left crippled. My recall speed wasn’t much faster than his respawn speed. The enemy jungler pushed the mid lane minions all the way up—was I supposed to ignore the wave and head straight to the bottom lane? This was clearly a premeditated team fight.”

“But we actually had a chance to win that team fight,” jungler Xiao Hong frowned as he looked at his screen. “Deepsea Faerie is just too insane. How can it still deal damage even through defensive items? And so much at that?”

“That’s why they say Deepsea Faerie is a 50-50 matchup against both Phoenix Soul and Beast Master,” mid laner Gu Ruicong sighed.

They’d given it their all in this team fight, and the fact they didn’t win was mostly down to their team composition.

After securing Guardian Spirit, they shouldn’t have gone for the Abyssal Lord. They should’ve gone straight for Deepsea Faerie.

The problem was, who could’ve predicted that Fight would pick Deepsea Faerie as a support?

Just how deep was their captain’s pool of playable heroes?

After Ling Suyu returned to base to replenish his health, he headed straight for the mid lane.

On the way, he glanced at both teams’ gold counts, realizing that Fight had completely surpassed YJ!

Thinking back to that team fight, the corners of his mouth curled upward.

Was this the benefit of having a good leader?

Tang Yu’an was the best!
