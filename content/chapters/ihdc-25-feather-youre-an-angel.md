“Cat Spirit?” Mo Xiaoyun thought he’d misheard.

But he only briefly voiced his confusion, and didn’t say anything else.

On the contrary, Dou Jiayan asked in surprise: “Huh? Feather, you want to play Cat Spirit?

Cat Spirit’s pretty weak in the current meta.”

“Just because the hero’s weak, doesn’t mean the player’s also weak,” Tang Yu’an retorted for Ling Suyu, “Just look at igame’s midlaner. Curse Master has been nerfed to hell and back, but doesn’t he still play him?”

“That’s true,” Dou Jiayan was convinced, returning his attention to the game, “So what do we ban? Tempest Blade?”

Yu He: “Yeah.”

Tang Yu’an: “Ban Tempest Blade, Feather help snatch Twin Demonblades.”

Dou Jiayan: “Got it——”

Ling Suyu: “Ok.”

And so, after Dou Jiayan banned Tempest Blade, the jungler with the highest use-rate, Ling Suyu immediately locked Twin Demonblades.

On SMG’s side, they saw them ban Tempest Blade and guessed that they would snatch Twin Demonblades, so they weren’t shocked.

But what surprised them instead was…

“Feather? That ID is Feather right?” SMG’s marksman thought his eyes were playing tricks on him.

“It’s Feather,” their mid laner captain confirmed, “Fight already officially announced that Feather is going to be playing for them during the Spring Seasonal, so it’s not like they can slap themselves in the face.”

“Then isn’t our luck pretty good? Randomly matching with Fight before the Spring Seasonals even begin will let us gauge their strength. Then they won’t catch us off guard during the competition,” their top laner said.

“Although, what kind of hero will Feather bring out?” their support asked curiously.

Mid laner: “Judging from what he took during the White Fox Cup, he should be used to explosive-type marksmen, so we should probably take Guardian Spirit?”

Marksman: “Sure.”

And so, SMG locked two heroes—Guardian Spirit and Abyssal Lord.

If it were a different team picking this combo, Fight wouldn’t be shocked at all.

But this was SMG!

This team, coined [What the hell] by Dou Jiayan, had all kinds of tricks up their sleeves. The things they’d pulled during competitions included but weren’t limited to their support playing top lane, going mid lane with an assassin, going double mages on the bottom lane…

By contrast, it felt abnormal for them to pick such a usual bottom lane combination!

“Did [what the hell] take the wrong medicine or something?” the jungler Yu He insulted first.

“Is this still the [what the hell] that I know?” Dou Jiayan followed.

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow: “In a certain sense, this is also an ‘unexpected’ pick.”

Only the ever-reliable Mo Xiaoyun spoke out with the truth: “Are they targeting Feather?”

Ling Suyu blinked, innocent and well-behaved.

They were trying to use the bottom lane duo he was most used to fighting against him?

SMG was such a kind team.

This time the system let the enemy side pick two heroes in a row, so next it came Fight’s turn to pick two. They first locked Deepsea Faerie and Skeletal Empress for their mid laner and top laner.

The pressure was once again on SMG.

They hesitated a moment before locking Blade Soul and Venomous Scorpion.

“Hm?” Yu He raised an eyebrow, “Blade Soul top lane?”

Both Blade Soul and Venomous Scorpion were jungling heroes. Since they picked both, one of them had to go to the top lane.

Blade Soul was a fighter-type jungler, while Venomous Scorpion was an assassin. If one had to go top, it had to be Blade Soul.

However, since they were facing SMG, nothing was for certain.

“They could be taking Blade Soul mid?” Dou Jiayan made a passing remark.

“Feather, you’re sure you want Cat Spirit?” Mo Xiaoyun asked in the midst of everything.

Ling Suyu paused for a moment, before resolutely making a sound of affirmation.

Although many marksmen were stronger than Cat Spirit this season, Cat Spirit would forever be the hero he was most familiar with, that he felt the best playing with, and that he felt the most confident winning with.

Although he couldn’t say for sure that he could carry, at the very least he wouldn’t disappoint too much.

And so, if he could take Cat Spirit, he would definitely go for it.

Hearing Ling Suyu’s reply, Mo Xiaoyun didn’t say anything else, locking Cat Spirit for him.

Then Tang Yu’an locked Spider Queen.

Thus, Fight’s team composition was decided.

Top lane: Skeletal Empress; Mid lane: Deepsea Faerie; Marksman: Cat Spirit; Support: Spider Queen; Jungler: Twin Demonblades.

SMG still had one empty position left.

Originally they’d been planning to crush the bottom lane with a high-movement lineup, but then Cat Spirit popped up. Huh?

Followed by Spider Queen, what?!

“Cat Spirit?!” SMG’s marksman thought he was hallucinating, “Are they trolling?”

“Cat Spirit, now that’s rare,” their captain was rather calm, “Maybe they’re trying to catch people off-guard by playing a low pick-rate hero?”

“That shouldn’t be necessary?” the top laner didn’t understand, “Early-game, trying to damage me with this hero is basically just scratching, apart from being good at running away, what else is it good for?”

“Good at running away,” their jungler realized, “I’d already thought about it during the White Fox Cup, but with Feather’s cowardly playstyle, it was strange he didn’t take any high mobility heroes. But here it is, no?”

“This guy,” their marksman also reacted, “He’s taking Cat Spirit in order to escape? Fight can withstand this?”

“They don’t really have a choice, isn’t Feather one of their starters now? Forcing him to play no-mobility marksmen during the White Fox Cup might have been UH’s downfall,” the support said.

Mid laner: “In that case, I’ll go bottom lane.”

Teammates: ???

Mid laner: “Coach-isn’t-home mode, activate!”

Teammates: Captain, reign it in.

And so, SMG in equal parts ease and unease locked in their final hero: Nine-tailed Fox.

When they saw this hero, Fight had yet to realize the problem.

After all, just looking at the composition, SMG’s lineup appeared much more normal compared to theirs.

Blade Soul could go top lane, Nine-tailed Fox was for sure a mid laner, the strong bottom lane combination of Guardian Spirit and Abyssal Lord, and Venomous Scorpion as jungler.

However, after they actually entered the game…

“Oho! What was I saying? Blade Soul mid lane!” as Dou Jiayan reached the front of his lane and he saw the opposing Blade Soul, he reacted like he’d seen a ghost, “So what in the world is top lane?”

The top laner Mo Xiaoyun saw two heroes appear across from him and fell into deep thought: “Mmm…I feel like I’ll probably collapse.”

Who would have thought the enemy marksmen and support would go top lane?

On the bottom lane, Ling Suyu looked at the Nine-tailed Fox across from him, speechless.

He could only say, no wonder SMG was [what the hell]!

Only Tang Yu’an was still calm: “No big deal, Xiaoyun defend under the tower. After you’re done clearing two waves of minions, recall and switch lanes.”

“What about me?” Dou Jiayan said, sounding wronged, “I want to lane against Nine-tailed Fox!”

“You have a longer range than Blade Soul, don’t act ungrateful after receiving a favour.

Pressure them early game, kill if you can, and think about switching lanes after you reach level 6.” After Tang Yu’an finished scolding him, he turned to Ling Suyu, “Feather, let’s stay under the tower and clear minions first, we’ll get him after level 6!”

“Okay—” Ling Suyu replied, relaxedly beginning to clear minions.

Nine-tailed Fox’s skill had a very long range, and could certainly take a 1v2 in the early game. Against this hero, they could really only stay cautious.

But after they reached level 6 and got their ultimates, the story would be different.

Although Cat Spirit didn’t have much wide-area damage, it did have extremely high single-target damage. In a situation where he had his ultimate, wouldn’t insta-killing a fragile mage be a piece of cake?

Ling Suyu would make them regret playing tricks!

At this time, a new message appeared in the chat from the opposing captain.

【Enemy】S.MG-FOX (Nine-tailed Fox): What a coincidence, are you guys also training?

Ling Suyu blinked as Tang Yu’an replied before he could even react.

【Ally】Fight-Yuan (Spider Queen): Nah, we’re just playing around.

【Enemy】S.MG-FOX (Nine-tailed Fox): Us too.

【Ally】Fight-Yuan (Spider Queen): How coincidental.

【Enemy】S.MG-FOX (Nine-tailed Fox): Yeah, it’s such a coincidence.

“Jeez! What kind of Most Awkward Conversation of the Century between captains is this!”

Dou Jiayan couldn’t help but complain, “It’s so awkward I could probably build a mansion with how much I’m picking at my toes!”

“As long as I’m not the one finding it awkward, they’re the awkward ones,” Tang Yu’an said as he helped shave down the minions’ health for Ling Suyu, even dodging one of Nine-tailed Fox’s skills as he did so.

After SMG’s captain finished throwing out all his skills, he continued with the awkward small take.

【Enemy】S.MG-FOX (Nine-tailed Fox): Feather, how are you settling into the new team?

“Huh?” Ling Suyu didn’t expect to be cued by the other side, and paused for a moment before reacting, “Should I respond?”

“Reply if you want to, it’s not like this is an official competition,” Tang Yu’an said with a smile, “If it’s not convenient for you to reply, just tell me what you want to say and I’ll respond for you.”

Ling Suyu thought about it: “I’ll reply.”

After he finished off a wave of minions, he took advantage of the break to type a few words.

【Ally】Fight-Feather (Cat Spirit): In the process.

Since this was the first match he was playing together with his teammates at Fight outside of Tang Yu’an, he still didn’t know how things would go, so saying [settled in] and [not settled in] both sounded off.

[Currently in the process of settling in] shouldn’t be wrong?

Yet after Tang Yu’an saw his curt response, he couldn’t help chuckling as he imagined him saying these out loud with a cold expression on his face.

He had to say, Ling Suyu’s “persona” was way too deceiving.

He spoke few words outside of the game because he wasn’t good at interacting with others, and was similarly taciturn in game because he didn’t want to waste time.

Ultimately, this resulted in those unfamiliar with him misconstruing him as cold.

Indeed, when S.MG’s captain saw Ling Suyu’s reply, the scene of him retorting against Ma Mingxi during the White Fox Cup surfaced, and he couldn’t help sighing: “So hard to converse with.”

His teammates’ reactions were: “Captain, if it’s difficult to converse then stop doing it and play seriously.”

Their captain was generally good, it’s just that he liked playing around and making meaningless small talk.

All by himself, he managed to completely derail their team’s style, leaving other teams with the impression of SMG as [what the hell].

Even though their team’s full name was “s.miling magic gentleman,” with the official Chinese translation being “Smiling Magic Gentlemen,” and their logo featured the silhouette of a man’s head wearing a magician’s hat, so they were once jokingly referred to as the “Black Guy Toothpaste Ad Team”.

Ermmmm...thinking of it that way, being called [what the hell] might not be so bad?

Soon, the heroes on both teams reached Level 6.

They had both claimed they were “just playing around,” but they also played with great caution. They’d clashed before Level 6, but neither side had given the other a chance to secure a kill.

However, after reaching Level 6, both sides began to get restless.

Suddenly, Ling Suyu realized something and glanced at the minimap.

Although the minimap didn’t show the enemy jungler’s position, if he were the enemy jungler, he’d likely be ganking the top lane right now.

And he’d do so by flanking from behind.

He opened his mouth to warn Mo Xiaoyun, but ultimately kept quiet—his hunches weren’t always right, and in the case that he was wrong…

Just as he finished that thought, Tang Yu’an suddenly spoke up: “Xiaoyun, pull back to the second tower. Yu He, head to the top lane—go now. Yanzi, be careful too—don’t leave the tower unless you’re at full health.”

Mo Xiaoyun: “Got it.”

Yu He: “On my way.”

Dou Jiayan: “You don’t need to remind me, Blade Soul has his ultimate! I’d get one-shot even if I leave the tower at full health, you know!”

Tang Yu’an: “Yanzi, shut up.”

Dou Jiayan: “Oh.”

Ling Suyu silently let out a sigh of relief, putting his mind at ease.

A few seconds later, the enemy jungler’s icon appeared on Fight’s minimap—he had indeed gone to gank the top lane!

However, Mo Xiaoyun and Yu He, who had been prepared all along, didn’t panic in the slightest. They waited until the enemy had teleported into the tower before moving in together.

Mo Xiaoyun stunned him with a Heavy Bone Hammer, and Yu He’s Twin Demonblades instantly closed the distance, immediately unleashing a combo of his first, second, and third skills.

Skill 1: Whirling Blade threw out a blade to attack the target; Skill 2: Catch the Blade allowed him to dash to the blade’s location to catch it and perform a swing; Skill 3: Double Slash dealt two consecutive hits.

While each individual skill of Twin Demonblades’ didn’t deal particularly high damage, consecutive attacks provided damage bonuses.

The enemy jungler didn’t even have a chance to counterattack; his health was instantly reduced to less than one-third.

At this point, the enemy support and marksman had also entered the tower, with the support taking the brunt of the tower’s damage.

The enemy marksman unleashed a full skill combo at the tower’s base, but Mo Xiaoyun used his ultimate and healing abilities to tank it, managing to prevent him from harming their own jungler.

Then, with full health, the jungler Yu He used his ultimate to secure the kill on the enemy jungler.

【System】 Twin Demonblades, first blood!

【System】 Enemy Venomous Scorpion has been slain!

Twin Demonblades’ “Cries of Conflict” was an AoE ability that dealt massive damage to all targets within a certain range in front of him and instantly reset the cooldowns of all his abilities.

While the enemy jungler was taken down by this skill, the enemy marksman and support also took damage from it, both losing some health.

“You run, I’ll kill.” After using his ultimate, Yu He calmly uttered these four words, then immediately launched into a skill combo, charging straight toward the enemy marksman.

The enemy marksman’s skills all hit Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress, and it looked like he was about to finish him off with his low health. However, Mo Xiaoyun used a Flash to escape his attack range just as Yu He’s Ghost Blade charged straight at him.

Whirling Blade, Catch the Blade, Double Slash—all executed in one fluid motion!

【System】Twin Demonblades, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

The enemy support used a teleport to chase after Mo Xiaoyun—he hadn’t given up yet, still intent on securing the kill.

Mo Xiaoyun swung his hammer in a backhand strike!

【System】Skeletal Empress, one kill! Dominating!

【System】The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain!

“Want to kill me?” Mo Xiaoyun sneered.

“Watch out! Blade Soul is after you!” Dou Jiayan warned.

No sooner had the words left his mouth did the enemy Blade Soul teleport out of the bushes between the first and second towers, unleashing his first skill, “Blade Shadow Combo”. The second hit of the combo smoothly finished off Mo Xiaoyun’s depleted health.

However, the Skeletal Empress had a passive called “Bone’s Rebirth.” Three seconds after death, she would revive at her original location with 20% of her health restored, with a cooldown of 200 seconds.

His passive was still active.

Thus, after Blade Soul finished off Skeletal Empress’s remaining health, he received no notification that an enemy hero had been killed.

Just as he and his team were waiting for Skeletal Empress to respawn, Dou Jiayan’s Deepsea Faerie caught up to them and activated her second skill, “Patrol”, summoning a jellyfish.

Blade Soul dodged it with a quick maneuver.

Dou Jiayan immediately followed up with his first skill, “Water Lotus,” causing a translucent, aqua-blue lotus to bloom where Blade Soul was standing.

Blade Soul dodged it with a Flash.

Dou Jiayan used his third skill, “Energy Drain”!

It dealt a small amount of damage to Blade Soul.

Dou Jiayan: “…”

Blade Soul: “…”

Mo Xiaoyun: “…”

Three seconds later, Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress revived and immediately struck Blade Soul with a hammer.

Blade Soul nullified the attack with his third skill, “Absolute Defense,” then instantly activated his ultimate, “Nine-Stage Blade Flash”!

Mo Xiaoyun, defeated.

【System】Blade Soul, one kill!

【System】 Ally Skeletal Empress has been slain!

After securing the kill on their top laner, Blade Soul didn’t linger any longer. He immediately used his refreshed movement skill to step over Dou Jiayan’s head and slip into the jungle.

Dou Jiayan dashed after him and unleashed his ultimate, “Deepsea Ballad”!

However, the damage of his ultimate relied on stacked marks.

And the only way to stack marks was by hitting enemy heroes with other skills.

Dou Jiayan had only landed his third skill, so the damage of his ultimate was predictably low.

The enemy Blade Soul, without even looking back, stepped right through Dou Jiayan’s ultimate and strutted out of the jungle.

However, before he could even get back to his lane, a transparent cat appeared right in front of him.

【System】Cat Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Blade Soul has been slain!

Ever since Dou Jiayan had alerted Mo Xiaoyun to Blade Soul’s location, Ling Suyu had realized Blade Soul would head to the top lane for support. After clearing a wave of minions, he pretended to return to base, but actually cut through the bottom jungle straight to the mid lane.

Tang Yu’an knew what he was up to. Without saying a word, he followed him to the mid lane and crouched in the bushes near the first tower.

Then Blade Soul strode right up toward them.

And then came the scene that just unfolded—as soon as Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit appeared in front of the Blade Soul, he stacked “Spirit Mark” and unleashed his ultimate!

“Spiritual Explosion” got rid of nearly half of the Blade Soul’s health. Having already used his dash skill, Blade Soul could only frantically move toward the mid lane.

Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit chased after him, firing away with a barrage of shots.

Blade Soul’s movement ability refreshed, and he used it immediately.

Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit used “Shadow Steps” to catch up, firing away again!

Blade Soul watched helplessly as his health was whittled away layer by layer, finally collapsing on the mid lane in defeat.

【System】Cat Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Blade Soul has been slain!

The two system notifications appeared one after another on Ling Suyu’s screen. Without even glancing at them, he fired his final Spirit Blade and dashed toward his own jungle.

Need to get the lightning strike, need to get the lightning strike— Tang Yu’an had his Spider Queen follow him. Watching his dashing silhouette, he suddenly found him rather handsome.

It was true—though Ling Suyu looked soft and cute, his gameplay style had always been incredibly aggressive.

It’s not that his damage output was overwhelming, but in his gaming style, his objectives were always crystal clear.

Once he decided to kill Blade Soul, he went for it. He simply did not think about consulting with his teammates.

And once he’d taken down Blade Soul, he moved straight for the lightning strike, never pausing to spare Blade Soul a second glance—as if claiming the lightning strike had been his goal all along, and killing Blade Soul was merely a side note.

He truly was a genius.

Tang Yu’an thought to himself as he tanked the purple dragon’s attacks for Ling Suyu, saying, “One for three—we came out ahead.”

“We definitely came out on top, but…” Mo Xiaoyun, the only one who had died, gazed wistfully at Dou Jiayan’s avatar. “Yanzi, don’t leave after this match. Let’s go over the fight we just had in detail.”

“Ahem,” Dou Jiayan swallowed nervously, then began to defend himself, “What’s the big deal! This hero is meant for team fights, okay? Blade Soul is so agile, it’s totally normal that I couldn’t hit him!”

“Give it a rest, you’re just bad!” Yu He, who had secured two kills, insulted while happily farming the jungle, “Look at your gold, even the captain’s almost caught up to you.”

“He’s the captain! Isn’t it normal for him to outpace me?” Dou Jiayan’s voice grew quieter and quieter.

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow. “Yanzi, just come clean, how many peanuts did you eat before this game? I’m the support, how could you let me surpass you?”

Dou Jiayan: “Hmph, just you wait! In the late-game team fights, I’ll shut you all up!”

Ling Suyu blinked.

Being the target of his teammates’ criticism was a daily occurrence for him at UH.

But…it felt a little different this time.

Although he wasn’t good at socializing and often missed the hidden meanings in others’

words, he could still sense the difference—his UH teammates insulted him for real, whereas his Fight teammates’ jabs at Dou Jiayan were mostly in good fun.

Although their verbal jabs were merciless, they all seemed to tacitly acknowledge that Dou Jiayan was a skilled player—he’d simply had an off day, and it was a mistake that could be excused.

As Dou Jiayan had said, the Deepsea Faerie’s strength lay not in laning but in team fights.

Failing to take down Blade Soul in a one-on-one was perfectly normal.

Yet, even knowing this, they still didn’t hold back on their teasing.

Ling Suyu listened quietly; not only did he not feel uncomfortable, but he actually seemed to be enjoying the atmosphere.

Just as he was zoning out, Tang Yu’an suddenly spoke up: “Feather, don’t you think Swallow deserves a good beating?”

“Huh?” Ling Suyu, suddenly called out, looked completely bewildered.

Tang Yu’an: “Mid laners rely on gold to deal damage. With such low gold, he still has the nerve to set up death flags.”

“Hmm…” Ling Suyu was used to being teased by his teammates, but when it came to teasing them back, he really didn’t know how…

In the end, he took the opposite approach and offered some reassuring words: “It’s okay. We have the economic advantage right now, we just need to farm slowly and steadily.”

“Ah, Feather, you’re an angel!” Dou Jiayan was so moved that tears welled up in his eyes.

“I’m sticking with you from now on! With me supporting you, I’ll make sure the enemy heroes don’t lay a finger on you!”

Ling Suyu: ?

Tang Yu’an: ?
