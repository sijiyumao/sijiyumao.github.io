Ling Suyu first heard Tang Yu'an's laughter, and only then did he notice that the audience's reaction was a little off, making him wonder if there was something wrong with his response.

He instinctively wanted to speak up and explain, but before he could continue, Tang Yu’an reached out and took the microphone from his hand.

Ling Suyu had no guard up against Tang Yu’an. The moment his hand reached for it, he subconsciously let go.

As a result, Tang Yu’an effortlessly took the microphone, then explained on Ling Suyu’s behalf: “Sorry, our Yumao isn’t much of a talker, so let me respond for him.”

As he said this, the big screen cut to a close-up of him and Ling Suyu.

Tang Yu’an wore a gentle expression, while Ling Suyu tilted his head slightly to look up at him. Though his face showed no particular emotion, one could sense that his eyes were filled with trust.

What ill will could a member of Fight possibly have?

That simple explanation was enough to convince the entire live audience, as well as XY.

When they first heard Ling Suyu say “no comment,” they’d all assumed Fight didn’t take XY seriously.

So it was just that he wasn’t good with words?

“Is that okay, Yumao?” Tang Yu’an suddenly handed the microphone back to Ling Suyu.

And Ling Suyu almost instinctively let out a soft “Mm-hmm.”

Alright, that fully confirmed he was “not a talker.”

In an instant, the entire audience forgot how “eloquent” Ling Suyu had been in responding to XY’s taunts before the match began. Their minds were now filled solely with the phrase “Yumao isn’t a talker,” and…

—Our Yumao.

Referring to their team as if it were a home, the spaces between his words full of warmth.

Fight really was a great team!

Sensing the atmosphere in the venue warming up again, Tang Yu’an knew he’d successfully turned the tide, so he continued with confidence: “Yumao is happy to still have the chance to lane against a sustained-damage marksman, and so are we. Whether it was your Barrier Sorceress or your Cat Spirit, they both put us under significant pressure in the late game. This wasn’t an easy win for us. Although you lost 0-2 to us today, you’ve truly earned your spot in the quarterfinals, and we’ve seen your strength. We’ll keep giving our all in the upcoming matches, and we wish you the best of luck in achieving results you’re proud of. That’s all.”

When Tang Yu’an took the microphone, he never paid attention to what the host asked or what the opposing team said. Once he’d addressed the points he wanted to address, he’d say what he wanted to say and wrap things up immediately, as if he weren’t conducting a post-match interview, but rather delivering a match summary.

He spoke naturally and confidently. Though his tone was very gentle, he exuded the aura and poise of a leader. By the end, his words clearly implied: all right, that’s enough, let’s wrap this up.

So after he finished, the host, who had clearly had more questions to ask, couldn’t bring himself to ask a single one. Ultimately, he complied with Tang Yu’an’s wishes, delivered the closing remarks, and officially brought the post-match interview to a close.

Tang Yu’an smiled with satisfaction. Amid the music playing in the venue and the audience’s unwavering applause and cheers, he placed his hand on Ling Suyu’s back and escorted him off the stage.

Ling Suyu let out a quiet sigh of relief and murmured, “Thank you.”

“What are you thanking me for? You didn’t say anything wrong,” Tang Yu’an said with a chuckle. “They just don’t understand *you*, so they misunderstood what you said. It’s their fault.”

Ling Suyu: “But you still explained for me.”

Tang Yu’an: “Of course I did. I’m your captain, after all~”

Ling Suyu blinked. “So, thank you.”

Sincere and cheerful.

Tang Yu’an reached up and ruffled his hair. “Little dummy.”

Tang Yu’an could tell that if he hadn’t snatched the microphone, Ling Suyu would have explained it himself.

Ling Suyu might seem aloof on the surface, but deep down he was sensitive and perceptive. Once he sensed the audience’s reaction and realized there was a problem with what he’d said, he would certainly clarify things in his own way.

But even knowing this, Tang Yuan still reached out and took the microphone from his hand.

Because he knew Ling Suyu didn’t like drawing attention to himself, and didn’t like holding a microphone and speaking in front of so many people.

Since that was the case, he’d handle the things Ling Suyu didn’t like.

Ling Suyu should just stick to doing what he enjoyed.

After their match, Fight didn’t linger and headed straight back to the hotel.

However, instead of immediately reviewing the game or training, they turned their attention to the fourth match of the day between Winne and Big.C.

The outcome was no surprise: Winne defeated Big.C 2-0.

This team, which, just like XY, liked to rely on “tanks,” failed to last even 20 minutes in either of their two matches against Winne, losing even more soundly than XY had when they faced Fight.

As expected of a championship winner, truly strong!

Fight’s members all marveled at this, and wanting to relax before bed, they each started playing on their computers.

What to play?

Ling Suyu hadn’t even made a conscious decision yet, but his hand had already instinctively grabbed the mouse and double-clicked to launch 《Glorious Heroes》.

On the hotel computer, he logged in with his secondary account—the one with the ID “soft and chewy osmanthus cake.”

As soon as he logged in, he noticed his friend list flashing, but he didn’t think much of it.

Since he was a skilled player, it was common for teammates from random matches to add him as a friend after a game. He’d gotten used to it.

But when he actually opened his friend list and saw an endless, unimaginably long stream of friend requests, he was stunned.

What was going on?!

With this many friend requests, it clearly wasn’t because people thought he was a good player after playing against him.

It seemed more like… they’d searched for his ID for some reason.

What reason?

He was a pro player, and this was his alt account. What else could it be?

Ling Suyu felt utterly dejected—he couldn’t use this alt anymore.

His hunch was correct. At the same time, he thought of the forum and that post where people had exposed his and Tang Yu’an’s aliases because their rankings were too high.

But he didn’t dare look.

As for Tang Yu’an, as soon as the match ended and he returned to the hotel, he went to the forum and found that post.

There was no need to search at all—it was right there on the front page, floating in plain sight. In fact, because the discussion was so heated, it even had the “hot” tag attached to it, and the moderator had even pinned it.

Tsk…Who was moderating this thing? They certainly did a great job ← (sarcasm).

Tang Yu’an had originally intended to click on it to see if anyone was bashing Yumao. If there were, he’d leave a comment to stick up for Yumao and take the heat himself.

He never expected that this generation of netizens had suddenly gotten smarter. No one suspected Yumao—everyone was bashing himself instead. It almost moved him to tears.

Tang Yu’an: ?

As a mom fan, this breaks my heart!】

Tang Yu’an: ??

That’s going too far.

Yumao belongs to him and him alone.

As if by some strange twist of fate, he logged into his account and replied to the post:

【Tang Yu'an asked me to ask you on his behalf, what colour burlap sack do you want?】

After replying, he logged out of the forum and paid no attention to any subsequent replies.

The forum was anonymous. Although usernames could only be changed once a month, no one except the moderators could see the linked accounts. Tang Yu’an was using a string of random characters as his ID—a very half-hearted choice—so he had absolutely no worries about being tracked down.

Yep, no one except the moderator.

The moderator happened to be a big AnYu shipper and had instinctively pinned this thread. Out of boredom, she kept refreshing the thread while clicking on the replyers’ IDs to see who these big shots were.

Then, upon seeing Tang Yu’an’s reply, she sighed, “Ugh, why can’t replies be pinned?” as she clicked on his ID. She glanced at his linked account and, upon seeing the words “tangyuan with sesame filling” was so startled that she dropped her mouse.

Good news—Oh my god! My ship is real!!

Bad news—I’m the only one who knows this secret, and I can’t tell anyone else yet. It’s so frustrating!!

Before going to bed, Dou Jiayan didn’t choose to play Glorious or browse the forum; instead, he opened Weibo.

He wanted to see how many new followers he’d gained today. His Mobile Artillery had made huge progress, his Phoenix Soul dealt massive damage—it was so cool! There must be tons of people commenting under his Weibo right now, telling him how handsome he is!

Sure enough, he’d gained a ton of followers! So happy!

Sure enough, there were tons of comments! So happy!

When he clicked through…

【Swallow! Your captain is so shameless!】

【Swallow! Has your captain’s shamelessness streak just started recently, or has he always been like this?】

【Swallow! Are your captain and Feather a thing?】

What in the world was all this?

Where were the comments calling him cool and handsome?!

Why were people coming to his Weibo to ask about Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu? Why didn’t they just go to their Weibo accounts to ask?!

Oh, they didn’t really check Weibo much. Well, then, nevermind……

Wait a minute?

Dou Jiayan suddenly realized something and clicked on the Weibo trending topics.

Trending #9: BPL Quarterfinals Day 1 Results

Trending #7: Yu He’s “Dominating Blade”

Trending #6: Feather’s Dark Raven Pentakill Carry

Trending #2: Fight’s Captain Has No Shame;

Trending #1: Fight’s bot lane duo is sending out sugar[^1] 

Dou Jiayan stared blankly for a moment before finally breaking down and shouting, “You guys all have trending topics, but I don’t!! Waaah—”

Chen Jie, who was scrolling through Weibo right beside him, carried a speechless expression—was that really the point here?
