The hallway within Fight’s club building had black floors and walls, but as Ling Suyu followed Tang Yu’an’s line of sight towards the dorm room, once the fluorescent light turned on, a bright room appeared before his eyes.

The white tiles on the ground were so clean that they could reflect one’s appearance, and the walls appeared newly painted, spotlessly white. The similarly snow-white curtains were made from some unknown material; they shimmered beneath the light’s reflections, dazzlingly bright.

Ling Suyu saw the world light up before his eyes, as if he had stepped into heaven.

Emm…that description was a bit exaggerated. But it was true that he was blown away.

Looking at his expression, Tang Yu’an knew that he was satisfied with the room. So instead of asking him about the room, he walked straight in and set the luggage in his hands on the floor: “Remember, the room’s 206. I’ll put your luggage here, you can take your time sorting it out. If you need any help, just shout for me. I’m always around, and I’ll be right there when you call.”

Only after hearing his voice did Ling Suyu finally react, instinctively replying “ok”, before belatedly realizing something and asking confusedly: “Aren’t you heading home for New Year’s Day?”

“Eh, it’s just New Year’s, I’ll just give my family a call and head back during the Lunar New Year,” Tang Yu’an said, asking in return, “What about you?”

Ling Suyu thought about his home in S City, pursing his lips: “Not going back.”

Tang Yu’an looked at him in momentary silence. He couldn’t tell what emotions were going through his mind or what his family situation was like. He decisively dropped the subject, changing the topic: “You can customize this room however you like. If you don’t like any of the furniture, feel free to change it or throw it out. You can stick posters and wallpaper on the walls, and if you want to repaint the walls then just watch out for the ventilation. The only thing I really need to warn you about is…”

He purposefully paused, and Ling Suyu’s eyes were wide open as he listened seriously.

Seeing him like this, Tang Yu’an let out a couple laughs, before saying: “The soundproofing is mediocre, so don’t go wild at night.”

“I won’t.” Ling Suyu breathed a sigh of relief.

“Let me think, is there anything else…” Tang Yu’an stroked his chin, “Oh right, the club’s break just started, and even the unoccupied rooms are frequently cleaned. So you don’t need to clean the room again, although I won’t stop you if you want to. There shouldn’t be anything else, but I’ll let you know if something comes to mind.”

“Okay!” Ling Suyu nodded repeatedly.

Although they were both captains, and both had excellent leadership skills, unlike Jiang Hao, Tang Yu’an was just way too friendly, to the point where even he had started to open up.

You had to know that to his teammates in UH, Ling Suyu was akin to a glacier, unmeltable by fire and unmovable by thunder.

He was usually expressional, reserved, and distant.

However, the real Ling Suyu was only cold when arguing against others. He was mainly introverted and not good with words, in addition to being a bit socially anxious.

Though he looked strong and independent, he’d actually always been looking for someone he could wholeheartedly rely on, who could help direct him.

Even if he hadn’t known Tang Yu’an for long, Ling Suyu felt that, just maybe, he could be the person he’d been waiting for.

As the thought crossed his mind, his phone suddenly vibrated.

Ling Suyu slightly froze, lowering his head in confusion—he’d already blocked UH’s manager, so who could be calling him right now?

As he looked down, the name “Jiang Hao” appeared in his field of vision.

At the same time, Tang Yu’an also saw it.

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow but didn’t say anything, although it didn’t seem like he’d be leaving either.

Ling Suyu stared at the caller ID for two seconds, his gaze momentarily becoming unfocused, before ultimately deciding to pick up.

If it were his teammates calling, he likely would have directly hung up.

But Jiang Hao…

Even if it was because of Jiang Hao’s presence that he was unable to participate in competitions, his and Jiang Hao’s relationship was, in actuality, not bad.

At least he believed it wasn’t bad.

“Hello?” Ling Suyu said rather calmly.

Soon, Jiang Hai’s familiar, deep yet slightly hoarse voice sounded from the phone: “When did you leave the club?”

“This morning, the coach allowed it.”

“You terminated your contract with UH?”

“The manager told you?”

“No, I saw it on Weibo,” Jiang Hao replied.

Due to being banned from competing in the White Fox Cup, Jiang Hao had been allowed an early vacation, and had left the club building for his own home.

He never imagined that less than a week after he left, UH experienced such earth-shattering changes.

Seeing the Weibo post sent by the UH official account, he nearly exploded in anger.

That post was so incredibly stupid. You’d have to have at least ten years’ worth of blood clots in the brain to send a Weibo that was so self-absorbed yet in actuality self-sabotaging.

However, since things had already come to this point, his first thought wasn’t to pin the blame on anyone, but rather to figure out what the next step should be, how he could salvage UH’s image.

Then he thought of Ling Suyu.

“Although I found out through Weibo, the manager just gave me a call,” Jiang Hao said, “He doesn’t understand gaming, so just listen to his words and don’t take them to heart. Since you’re in a new team now, just focus on performing well.”

Ling Suyu let out a breath of relief, but halfway through realized something wasn’t right and glanced guiltily at Tang Yu’an: “I don’t have a new team…”

“There’s no need to deny it,” Jiang Hao cut him off, “If Fight hadn’t signed you, there would be no reason for them to be the first to jump out and antagonize UH. Even if I guessed wrong, there should be quite a few teams looking to sign you right now, so you’ll definitely be able to find one. Don’t misunderstand, you’re no longer one of UH’s people, so you can join whichever team you’d like. I’m not trying to imply anything, I simply wanted to congratulate you.”

Ling Suyu lightly furrowed his brows.

Although Jiang Hao said that, for some reason, he had a bad feeling.

As expected, Jiang Hao’s next words were: “By the way, I wanted to ask you for a small bit of help”

Ling Suyu instinctively looked to Tang Yu’an for help.

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow, lifting his hand and squeezing his shoulder in support. At the same time, he unlocked his own phone and opened a recording app, beginning a voice recording.

Ling Suyu’s heart calmed slightly. He turned the call to speaker mode before asking: “Help with what?“ “Send a post on Weibo summarizing your problems during the White Fox Cup.”

Jiang Hao openly and brazenly made such an unreasonable request. Without waiting for Ling Suyu’s answer, he softened his voice and adopted a soothing tone: “I know that UH’s performance during the White Fox Cup wasn’t your fault. You were always looking for a chance to turn the tables, and you tried your best. Similarly, I know this request of mine is unreasonable, and you must have thousands of reasons to refuse. But if this continues, UH will be ruined—because of the situation with your contract termination, UH’s image has plummeted, and it’ll be difficult to form a youth training team. For my sake, could you help me save UH? You’ve already found a team that’s willing to sign you, so even if you grant me this request, you won’t take any huge losses. I’m not asking you to shoulder all the blame, I just need you to briefly summarize your issues during the White Fox Cup, and I’ll take over from there. Feather, I’ll owe you one, and I will definitely return this favour in the future. As you know, I always keep my word.”

Ling Suyu opened his mouth to reply, but couldn’t say a single word.

It wasn’t that he got soft-hearted, but rather he was shocked that Jiang Hao, like UH’s manager, could be so shameless!

Oh sure, he was just doing Jiang Hao a small favour and wouldn’t take any “huge losses".

After all, he was just a “small professional player,” so where would you get any “big losses”?

Much less Jiang Hao owing him a favour, if they gave him a huge sum of money, or even a mansion, he would rather die than do this for them!

With so many eSports teams and fans supporting him, they wanted him to help UH backstab them? He couldn’t do that.

Even if UH had no shame, he did.

More importantly, he, Ling Suyu, from childhood up until now, had never and would never go against his own conscience!

So, just as Tang Yu’an was observing Ling Suyu’s expression, ready to take the phone and help him retort at any moment, Ling Suyu himself opened his mouth and coldly replied: “Get lost!”

Right after, he hung up and blocked the number, the full-service package.

Tang Yu’an blinked and finally reacted after a moment. He ended the recording and let out an audible laugh.

Ling Suyu looked at him helplessly, speechless.

“My bad, it’s just…” Tang Yu’an said while laughing, “He spoke such a long train of nonsense, and you returned it all with two words, so I wonder how colourful his current expression is? Impressive! You’re really something! As expected of our insult genius! That was brilliant!”

Ling Suyu found himself a bit embarrassed at the praise, so he coughed dryly and quietly muttered: “Even two words felt like too much…”

He’d been pretty angry when he hung up, only to see Tang Yu’an laughing so happily…

Forget it, he could laugh if he wanted, he’ll laugh along.

Ling Suyu couldn’t stop the corners of his mouth from raising, but he still apologized to Tang Yu’an: “Sorry.”

“Why are you apologizing?” This time it was Tang Yu’an’s turn to be exasperated. He patted Ling Suyu’s back helplessly, “If you ask me, every last one of those guys from UH should be blacklisted by you. These regular phone calls are too mood-ruining, do you still have any appetite?”

“No,” Ling Suyu blurted out without thinking.

“That’s what I mean. Picking up their calls and listening to even one more word is enough to make anyone nauseous. That said…” Tang Yu’an changed the topic, “If I were to treat you to a meal, would you have any appetite?”

Ling Suyu blinked.

Tang Yu’an started directly listing foods: “Stewed chicken? Roasted duck? Fish with sour pickles? Steak? Or…burgers?”

Ling Suyu tilted his head.

“Hmm, you might want something sweet right now. If you don’t want to eat then milk tea should also work,” Tang Yu’an said, nodding to him and urging Ling Suyu out of the room, “Doesn’t matter, let’s both go downstairs and check it out together, we’ll eat whatever’s there, come on.”

“Wait…” Ling Suyu verbally wanted to refuse, but his body honestly followed Tang Yu’an downstairs.

Well, it was lunch time.

If Tang Yu’an insisted on treating, then…it wasn't like he couldn’t accept it.

In the end, Ling Suyu didn’t refuse Tang Yu’an’s invitation, although something came to mind as he headed down: “You recorded while I was on call?”

“I was just worried you’d be tricked. Wouldn’t it be good to have some evidence on hand for the police?” Tang Yu’an said, unable to hold back his chuckles, “You…if you were more guarded, you wouldn’t have been scammed by UH to this extent. But it’s fine, you’ve got me now. I’ll help you keep an eye out in these regards, so you can just focus on competing~”

Ling Suyu instinctively let out an “en”.

“What do you think? Aren’t you reassured that you have such a reliable captain?” Tang Yu’an started praising himself again.

Ling Suyu couldn’t hold back his laughter. He suddenly felt like being forthright for once, and said: “Yeah, captain’s the best~”
