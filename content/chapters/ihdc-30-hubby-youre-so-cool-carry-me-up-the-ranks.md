After the post-game review, everyone started practicing in solo queues.

To avoid letting players from other teams see which champions they were focusing on, everyone logged in using their alt accounts.

Of course, this included Ling Suyu as well.

Ling Suyu’s alt account was originally named【so what if I’m using cat spirit】but he immediately changed it to【soft and chewy osmanthus cake】after logging in.

When Tang Yu’an saw it, he raised an eyebrow: “Your original ID was alright, pretty provocative. There’s no need to change it.”

Ling Suyu: ?

Had “provocative” become a compliment now?

Due to the nature of the bottom lane, even though it was “solo queue practice”, Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu were duo queuing.

The two added each other as friends and immediately started a duo queue. Tang Yu’an took a moment to check Ling Suyu’s account stats and discovered he hadn’t used this account at all this season, but in previous seasons, his most-played champion was Cat Spirit, with a 76% win rate.

The second most-played was Guardian Spirit, with an 82% win rate.

It seemed to explain why UH had been pushing Ling Suyu to use the Spirit.

“Your spirit isn’t as strong as your cat, but your stats seem pretty good?” Tang Yu’an asked curiously.

“Umm…I don’t know why, but whenever I play Cat Spirit, I always run into all sorts of strange situations like AFK teammates, supports not following up, getting left behind by my team, or the top and mid lanes collapsing unexpectedly. In those cases, the main tower gets pushed down before I’ve even had a chance to farm. If I could just hold out a little longer, though, there’d still be a chance to turn the game around.”

“So it all boils down to one problem.”

“What?” Ling Suyu turned his head curiously toward Tang Yu’an beside him.

Then, in his field of vision, Tang Yu’an replied with utter seriousness: “You hadn’t met me yet.”

Ling Suyu: “……”

Ling Suyu was speechless for a moment, but when he turned back, he couldn’t help but chuckle.

Well, he wasn’t exactly wrong.

With Tang Yu’an as his support, he’d won games even while his teammates were AFK.

Seeing him smile, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but laugh too.

In high spirits, the two began their first practice match. Ling Suyu played Dark Raven, while Tang Yu’an played Luminous Saintess.

With Luminous Saintess’s healing, Ling Suyu didn’t need to return to their base to replenish his health prior to level 6. His minion-clearing speed outpaced the enemy’s, and he managed to secure an early lead despite playing a late-game champion.

Upon reaching level 6, the duo returned to base for their first round of items. Back on the lane, they encountered the enemy bottom laners and jungler attempting a gank, but they easily secured a 0-for-3 exchange. Immediately afterward, they pushed down the enemy bottom lane’s first tower and, with their own jungler’s assistance, took down the Divine Sunbird.

With a lead in gold, playing Dark Raven required almost no skill; he simply needed to latch onto an enemy and unload a barrage of attacks.

The enemy heroes fell one after another, unable to even get close to Ling Suyu, prompting them to let out agonized screams:

“Help! Who fed him this much?!”

“We’re done for this game! The enemy marksman can take all five of us!”

“Look at our own marksman, he might as well be AFK!”

So, in this game, Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu won very smoothly.

The second game began immediately after. Ling Suyu picked Dark Raven again, while Tang Yu’an picked the mid-lane hero Wind Guardian as support. Before reaching level 6, they had already killed the two enemy bottom lane heroes once, securing an early economic advantage. They pushed the lane all the way to the end, and the round couldn’t have gone any smoother.

After the two games, Tang Yu’an asked Ling Suyu, “Which support champion do you prefer I play?”

Ling Suyu was struck by decision paralysis: “Either one? It depends on the enemy team’s composition.”

Both games were complete steamrolls, so they weren’t really fit for consideration.

However, it did give Ling Suyu a clearer understanding of just how unfathomably deep Tang Yu’an’s champion pool was.

Ling Suyu couldn’t help but ask him, “You practice mid and support champions. Doesn’t that mess with your feel for the game?”

“No problem, as long as my AD is happy, it’s all worth it!” Tang Yu’an blurted out.

Ling Suyu: “……”

Ling Suyu’s face remained expressionless, but internally he was screaming!

Help! This support was great in every way, but the fact that he occasionally blurted out something that could be easily misinterpreted was just too awkward!

The two went on to play several more games together, climbing from Valiant to Famed to Glorious in one go without losing a single match along the way.

At the start of the new season, the Glorious tier was still pretty empty. After they climbed up, both of them ranked within the top ten, with Tang Yu’an at sixth and Ling Suyu at ninth.

Finally, Chen Jie couldn’t take it anymore: “Hey, hey, hey, you guys, tone it down a bit. Stop playing ranked and go play a few casual matches! Aren’t you afraid people will figure out these are your alt accounts?”

Tang Yu'an only realized it after being reminded: “Whoa, we're already this high? Randoms really don’t pose much of a challenge.”

Ling Suyu sneakily stuck out his tongue.

Playing in a duo with Tang Yu'an, he didn't feel like he was training at all. The more they played, the more hyped he got, so he completely forgot to keep an eye on their rank.

Never mind their ranking in the Glorious tier, he hadn’t even checked his individual hero ranking. Glancing at his Dark Raven’s projected ranking for next week, he saw he was already number one in City B.

Sure enough, that very night, a thread exposing them appeared on the official 《Glorious Heroes》forum.

“Which two pro players’ alt accounts are these?”

—The original post prominently displayed the profiles of “soft and chewy osmanthus cake”

and “tangyuan with sesame filling”.

The former specialized in marksmen, playing all kinds of them. This season, they’d picked Dark Raven for about 90% of their ranked matches, with the remaining ones split between Cat Spirit and Desert Hunter.

The latter played everything except marksman and jungler. This season, they’d played mostly top lane and support, with a fair share of mid lane as well. It was impossible to tell which role they specialized in—or if they specialized in any at all?

That wasn’t the point. The point was their win rates this season—the former at 100%, the latter at 97%. It was simply unbelievable!

【That random jungler who’s first in the Glorious tier has played over a hundred games this season, while rice cake made it into Glorious top 10 with just over thirty games. What kind of gods are these?】

“It’s osmanthus cake,” corrected the second poster. “I matched with them in a Famed game.

They were duo-queuing, one playing marksman, the other support, and carried the whole team the entire time. They’re seriously impressive.”

【So here comes the question: which two pro players’ alt accounts are these? Any experts out there who can dig into this?】

【That’s easy—just look at which team’s marksman is playing Dark Raven in the Spring Season.】

【Bro, isn’t that exactly why we’re all here in the first place?】

【Come to think of it, there are so few marksmen playing Dark Raven. Last season, marksmen were either Guardian Spirits or Netherworld Shadow, or they just skipped the marksman role altogether and went with a dual-mage bot lane.】

【By “dual-mage bot lane”, you mean SMG, right? That was really memorable—and the crazy part is, they actually won with that setup.】

【Let’s be honest, with basic attack damage nerfed, if marksmen have to rely on burst damage, they might as well just pick mages.】

【Yeah, at this point nobody even plays marksmen anymore!】

【I wonder if they’ll revert the change this season?】

【The Spring Seasonals are about to start. If they’re going to make such a major change, they’ll probably wait until after the Spring Season. So far this season, they’ve only adjusted the damage of a few dominant mid-laners. I feel like we might see those come off the ban list during the Spring Split.】

【So, is there really no big shot who can give us some info on these two?】

【Is there a possibility—and I mean, this is just a guess, not necessarily true—that these two aren’t actually pro players?】

【No way, right? Non-pros being this good? Wouldn’t they be swamped with DMs from all the top teams?】

【Wait, maybe it’s actually possible. Even though osmanthus cake specializes in marksmen, tangyuan plays every position. They don’t seem like a pro.】

【Just to counter the post above: while tangyuan does play every hero, isn’t it possible that they use them all as supports?】

【Whoa, that’s definitely a possibility. A support with such a deep pool of heroes…

could it be FIght’s Tang Yu’an?】

【Tangyuan = Tang Yu’an. Case solved, guys.】

【Then osmanthus cake must be…Feather?!】

While having dinner, Dou Jiayan came across this post and shared it with Tang Yu'an almost immediately.

Halfway through scrolling through the comments, Tang Yu'an was still feeling quite smug:

“See? This is the advantage of having a deep hero pool. Who would think I’m a pro player?”

As he read further, Tang Yu’an remarked, “Feather’s MVP is so easy to spot. They dug up me before they even found Feather—what’s the logic here? Netizens these days are no good!”

Ling Suyu silently took a bite of rice.

Chen Jie looked at the two of them helplessly. “What’s with you guys? You’re both acting like it’s your first time doing this kind of training. Great, the Spring Seasonals haven’t even started yet, and now every other team knows what lineup we’re going to run in the bottom lane.”

“They’re just guessing; they can’t know for sure,” Tang Yu’an said calmly, putting down his phone. “Do you really think they’d ban Feather’s Dark Raven just based on a hunch? And even if they do ban him, so what? Feather isn’t limited to just this one marksman, right, Feather?”

Ling Suyu hummed in agreement and went back to shoveling his food.

True, he’d just been playing too much Cat Spirit and Desert Hunter and wanted to practice Dark Raven a bit more. Even though he’d reached Glorious today thanks to Dark Raven, Cat Spirit was still his top pick.

However, this was the first time he’d ever had his alt account exposed, so it would be impossible to say he wasn’t panicking at all.

Perhaps sensing his unease, Tang Yu’an quickly added, “I’ve thought of a fix. Come on, Feather, let me show you—”

“Hm?” Ling Suyu looked up curiously.

Even though Tang Yu’an had “tricked” him countless times before, and his gut told him this “fix” might not be entirely legitimate, he still wanted to hear him out.

It was the original members of Fight, on the other hand, who instinctively stepped forward to shield him.

Chen Jie: “Don’t you dare take this opportunity to bully Feather again!”

Yu He: “What are you up to now?”

Mo Xiaoyun: “The competition is about to start. Don’t do anything reckless.”

Dou Jiayan immediately stood up and covered Ling Suyu’s ears with both hands: “Go ahead, I’ll listen. I’ll tell Feather only after I’ve heard it and decided it’s okay!”

Ling Suyu: ?

Tang Yu’an cleared his throat and looked at their reactions with amusement: “What are you all thinking? It’s just a little bit of misdirection.”

Tang Yu’an never actually said what he had in mind. It wasn’t until after dinner, when the break was over, that he whispered to Ling Suyu, “Let’s form a bond.”

Ling Suyu blinked.

In《Glorious Heroes》, two players could form a bond, with three types to choose from— close friends, best friends, and lovers.

Of these, you could have a maximum of ten close friends and best friends combined, but only one lover.

At first, Ling Suyu couldn’t figure out why forming a bond would help hide his true identity.

Until Tang Yu'an changed his in-game gender to female and sent him a request to form a lovers’ bond.

Ling Suyu: “……”

Damn, what a wolf!

Ling Suyu hesitated, but in the end said nothing and chose to accept.

And in every duo match after that, as soon as they entered the game, Tang Yu’an would inevitably call him “husband” at least once.

【Ally】tangyuan with sesame filling: Go, husband! We’re counting on you to carry us again this round!

【Ally】soft and chewy osmanthus cake: Oh.

Ling Suyu channeled his embarrassment into murderous intent and ruthlessly secured a pentakill.

【Ally】tangyuan with sesame filling: Hubby, you’re so cool! Carry me up the ranks and take me to the top!

【Ally】soft and chewy osmanthus cake: Ok.

Their other teammates: Oh, come on! We’re here to play ranked! Not to watch a couple show off their love!

Soon, Tang Yu’an’s “remedial measures” took effect.

That post titled《Which Two Pro Players’ Alt Accounts Are These?》had already begun listing evidence that the two were Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu when the tone suddenly shifted.

【Case solved. These two are a couple duo-queuing. Let’s call it a day, everyone.】

【I knew it. I’ve never seen a pro marksman play Dark Raven. Turns out it was just a random master carrying his girlfriend up the ranks.】

【Is there any chance… Never mind, I don’t think that’s possible.】

【That’s so sweet. I wish I could get a pro marksman to carry me too. I’d even practice my high-pitched voice for him.】

【Help! Why did I click on this thread in the middle of the night? I came here to see you guys dissing pro players, not to be force-fed this random couple’s PDA!】

Upon seeing the follow-up to this thread, Tang Yu’an reacted like this: “Look at that. This is what wisdom looks like!”

Ling Suyu remained expressionless, unwilling to speak, wanting to just lie down right then and there and play dead.

The rest of the Fight team: “Should we say Captain Tang is truly Captain Tang?” Mo Xiaoyun was impressed. “Only Captain Tang could come up with a move that inflicts a thousand wounds on the enemy while taking eight hundred himself.”

“But this actually works!” Dou Jiayan hadn’t yet realized the gravity of the situation. “Who would think that the captain and Feather were duo-queuing as a couple!”

“Couple’s queue…” Yu He coughed.

“Fine, if you’ve got the guts, keep calling him ‘husband’ until the Spring Seasonals start.”

Chen Jie patted Tang Yu’an on the shoulder, giving him a look that said, “I’m just going to sit back and watch you self-destruct”.

Tang Yu’an shrugged: “What’s the big deal? It’s just typing two characters, I’m not shouting it over voice chat.”

Ling Suyu imagined the scene of Tang Yu’an shouting those words over voice chat…

Help! I can’t stay on this team anymore!

“I… I’m going to take a shower.” Ling Suyu said, then quietly slipped away.

He needed some time alone to calm down.

It had to be said that Tang Yu’an’s sneaky tactic had indeed worked; that post had scared off a bunch of single guys, and by the next day, it had vanished without a trace.

However, Tang Yu’an’s tactic only worked on people who didn’t know him and Ling Suyu well enough.

UH, who had some understanding of his shamelessness and knew Ling Suyu better than most teams, wasn’t swayed by the developments following the post.

“He played over thirty matches and got over thirty MVPs. It’s definitely Feather.”

“He actually blocked all of us, but judging by his past stats, this has to be Feather’s alt account.”

“Tang Yu’an really has no shame. How can he be so brazen?”

“No wonder he’s the support in charge of initiating team fights—he sets the pace like a pro.

He’s got Weibo and the forums completely under his control.”

“Playing Dark Raven, huh? We’ll see about the Spring Seasonals!”

Meanwhile, on the other side, S.MG—who unexpectedly got matched against Fight right after logging in on New Year’s Day and got absolutely crushed by him—said:

“That’s Feather, right? I can’t think of anyone else who would specifically practice sustained-damage marksmen.”

“Tang Yu’an is something else. He’s even calling him ‘husband’. I’d love to see Feather’s reaction upon seeing those words.”

“So after studying Cat Spirit, we have to study Dark Raven too?”

“Ah… please, please, don’t let us get matched with Fight in the group stage. I’d go vegetarian for a whole day just to make that wish come true!”

“Uh…” At that moment, Ling Suyu, who had just finished his shower and was lying in bed, had no idea that his online alias had already been completely exposed by two certain teams.

All he could think about right now was tomorrow’s practice match against YJ.

In fact, in the days leading up to the Spring Seasonals, Fight didn’t just schedule practice matches with YJ. Many teams with a shot at the quarterfinals reached out to them as well.

Ye Xiran accepted almost every invitation, filling up Fight’s training schedule for the coming days.

However, among these teams, YJ was the strongest and posed the greatest threat to Fight’s chances at winning the championship.

If they could defeat YJ in a practice match, it would surely boost the entire team’s morale for the upcoming Spring Seasonals.

Which hero should he pick for tomorrow’s match against YJ?

Should he go with Cat Spirit, who he was most comfortable with, or the new Dark Raven he’d been practicing, or…

As Ling Suyu pondered this, his eyelids grew heavier and heavier until they finally closed completely and his breathing evened out.
