The saying, “Those who commit many wrongs will inevitably bring about their own downfall,” suited UH perfectly.

It wasn’t to say that doing too many bad things would inevitably lead to divine retribution. In an atheistic world, “heaven” didn’t exist.

Rather, when you told enough lies, you’d end up believing them yourself, and they would eventually come back to haunt you.

In fact, UH’s coach had wanted to ban Cat Spirit and Candy Witch, knowing full well just how powerful Ling Suyu was.

With Ling Suyu’s skill, if he were to pick Cat Spirit, his specialty, or Candy Witch, who was exceptionally strong in the early game, with his teammates providing seamless protection, he would undoubtedly inflict devastating destruction on his opponents.

But how could he explain to UH’s starters just how powerful Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit was?

After all, he was the one who had originally forced Ling Suyu not to use Cat Spirit, and he was also the one who had drilled into the entire team the idea that “Ling Suyu is only good enough to be a substitute.”

Apart from Jiang Hao, who knew of his “efforts,” everyone else on UH basically believed it. UH’s manager in particular was utterly convinced. Before the Spring Seasonals had begun, he’d always regarded Ling Suyu as an ingrate who could not be trusted.

Even now that UH had been lambasted all over the internet, there were still people who believed the lies UH had so carefully woven.

Especially after Jiang Hao’s statement.

【Exactly, Feather’s decision to terminate his contract was his own choice—what does that have to do with UH?】

【If Feather didn’t want to be UH’s substitute, wouldn’t it have been better to terminate the contract sooner? He took advantage of UH for so long!】

【It’s been a month since all of this happened, and you’re still bringing it up?】

【Fight’s way too petty, just look at how gracious Jiang Hao is!】

—These comments, interspersed among the cheers of support for Fight, stuck out like a sore thumb and were quickly picked up by Fight’s supporters, who all looked on in disbelief.

【I really worry about humanity’s short-lived memory…】

【You seriously think UH being nasty was about Feather’s contract termination? No! It’s that they manipulated Feather for a full five years! And they even had to kick Feather while he was down when they finally terminated the contract!】

【What the f*ck do you mean, “wouldn’t it have been better to terminate the contract sooner”? Were you going to help him pay the penalty fee?】

【It’s obvious that UH didn’t want to use Feather themselves, nor did they want any other team to have him! They deliberately held him back for a full five years and yet, in your mouths, it’s become Feather freeloading off UH?!】

【Maybe they’re the type of people to think that as long as the boss pays the employee’s salary, they can treat that employee however they want. A suck-up with that kind of mindset is sure to be a hit with capitalists.】

【”It’s been a month”? What does the person saying this think five years of an esports player’s life amounts to? Just for this, with how UH treats people like objects, I wouldn’t mind bashing them for ten years!】

【All I can say is, humans really do have a strong capacity for empathy. When they see one side at a disadvantage, they instinctively want to protect them, without even stopping to consider whether they brought it on themselves.】

UH’s supporters were silenced by the backlash; some were pushed to blurt out, “So what if it was five years?”—unable to convince even themselves.

Neither Fight nor UH were aware of the online feud.

After Ling Suyu got the Cat Spirit he’d been longing for, a smile spread across his face. All he wanted was to win this match. Nothing else mattered.

Once he defeated UH, he felt that all their “past history” could be wiped clean.

It was UH who had led him into the world of esports.

It was his own lack of leadership skills that forced him to settle for a bench role.

Although the past four years had been a hazy blur of inaction, and he’d lived life as if he were a ghost, Ling Suyu had never complained about anything.

The only thing that made him angry was that UH, without distinguishing right from wrong, had pinned all the blame on him, smearing his reputation and trampling on his dignity in an attempt to ensure no team would be willing to sign him.

If it hadn’t been for Tang Yu’an’s decisive decision to take him in, perhaps his future would have truly turned out just as UH’s manager predicted, with him spending his days as a benchwarmer on another team, never knowing when he’d finally get to step onto the field and win a match to clear his name, rotting away in the esports scene for the rest of his life until, years later, no one would even remember his ID, let alone his name.

But now, he’d come clean. So many people were willing to support him, support Fight, and help him call out UH.

That alone was enough.

He wanted to prove his worth to UH by defeating them, wake up his former teammates, and make the coach and manager who’d always looked down on him marvel at him.

He didn’t wish for UH’s death.

Both sides quickly selected their lineups for the first game.

Fight: Skeletal Empress, Wind Guardian, Cat Spirit, Spider Queen, Twin Demonblades.

UH: Dark Angel, Nine-Tailed Fox, Guardian Spirit, Abyssal Lord, Venomous Scorpion.

Before entering the game, Dou Jiayan asked, “Yumao, do you want to take down Jiang Hao yourself, or should we help you?”

Ling Suyu paused for a moment before responding with just six words: “Anything, as long as we win.”

Dou Jiayan: “Alright, I’ll beat him up so bad he won’t be able to get back up!”

Ling Suyu seemed to want to say something but stopped himself, ultimately saying nothing.

This was clearly a dispute between him and UH, so why did it feel like his teammates on Fight were even more worked up than he was?

Soon, the match began. Both teams’ heroes rushed to their respective positions and started laning.

Given that his teammates were much more agitated than usual before this match, Tang Yu’an felt compelled to remind them: “ “Everyone, calm down—especially top lane. Xiaoyun, you can’t beat the Dark Angel in a 1v1, so stay under the tower and go for 2v1s. Yanzi, don’t push too deep. After level 6, forget about mid and come to bot.”

“Okay… Huh? Why?” Dou Jiayan instinctively replied before realizing what was wrong.

Weren’t you supposed to use your ultimate to instantly take out the enemy once you hit level 6?!

Ling Suyu spoke up: “You’ll get caught.”

Almost at the same time, Tang Yuan said: “You’ll die.”

Dou Jiayan: “……”

Okay, the two of you are singing in perfect harmony, and you’ve gone and laid out my death in crystal-clear detail.

But Tang Yuan and Ling Suyu still underestimated just how crazy UH could get.

Five minutes into the match, Dou Jiayan, who had just reached Level 5, was farming minions in front of his own tower when the enemy Nine-Tailed Fox suddenly teleported like a madman behind him, right under the turret. She then fired a “Flame Wave” that sent him flying between the two towers, immediately followed by “Fire of Imprisonment”!

At the same time, UH’s jungler Venomous Scorpion burst out of the bushes and landed a “Posion Claw” on him.

Taking advantage of the delay in “Fire of Imprisonment,” Dou Jiayan calmly used his second skill “Windstorm” to push the Venomous Scorpion away, then immediately teleported back under the tower.

Unexpectedly, seeing that he hadn’t died, the Nine-Tailed Fox decided not to leave the tower after all; despite taking tower damage, she unleashed a full burst of “Foxfire” that hit him squarely.

Venomous Scorpion, who had been pushed back, used its dash ability “Poison Fog’s Shadow” to enter the tower, secured the kill with a basic attack, and used Flash to escape the tower.

Immediately afterward, Nine-Tailed Fox was killed by the tower.

【System】: Venomous Scorpion, first blood!

【System】: Ally Wind Guardian has been killed!

【System】: Tragedy! The enemy Nine-Tailed Fox has been slain by our defense tower!

Dou Jiayan: “……”

He’d finally seen it for himself.

UH’s brand of madness.

Although facing UH—a team that took the concept of “shamelessness” to the extreme, trampling the Four Heroes of Qingcheng underfoot and punching Yue Buqun[^1]—Fight really wanted to win in a single, decisive sweep like the audience wanted, reality didn’t seem that simple.

UH was a semifinalist.

Even from Ling Suyu’s perspective, aside from Jiang Hao, no single member of UH could hold a candle to any of Fight’s members when taken individually. Yet there was no denying that, as a cohesive team, they possessed the strength of a Top 4 contender.

Yesterday, the reason UH lost so decisively to igame was because they were completely caught off guard by igame’s Beast Master. But today, Fight had no similar trump card that could catch UH off guard.

Just as Ling Suyu understood UH, UH also understood Ling Suyu.

Even if Ling Suyu had only played Desert Hunter, Candy Witch, Cat Spirit, and Dark Raven in the Spring Seasonal so far.

UH had a clear idea of just how well he could perform with his other heroes.

When they saw Ling Suyu pick Cat Spirit, UH weren’t surprised at all—they even started celebrating.

“Hah, Cat Spirit again.”

“As expected, you can predict his picks in your toes.”

“With a bottom-tier pick, he’ll probably won’t get into the semifinals. Picking Cat Spirit against us means he doesn’t take us seriously.”

UH’s coach hesitated, then decided against saying anything.

In the end, it was Jiang Hao who reminded them: “Don’t underestimate Cat Spirit. The more annoying he is early on, the more troublesome he’ll be late game. We need to suppress him early and not let him farm up.”

UH’s coach let out a sigh of relief and left it up to them.

Perhaps he’d overthought it—Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit wasn’t that strong after all.

Then he watched as Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit, after picking up the lightning strike, led by his teammates from Fight, tower-dived and killed Jiang Hao…

Here’s what happened: a few seconds earlier, at the 6-minute mark of the game.

In just two short minutes, Dou Jiayan’s Wind Guardian had already been ganked three times by UH’s jungler.

The first time, the enemy mid laner died while tanking the tower for the jungler.

The second time, the enemy jungler tower-dived him directly. Dou Jiayan managed to land some damage in return; although he was ultimately killed, the enemy jungler failed to escape the tower’s range after the kill and was killed by the tower.

The third time, the enemy jungler escaped with low health only to be finished off by Yu He, who happened to be “passing by.”

Finally, after his third resurrection, Dou Jiayan couldn’t take it anymore: “UH’s jungler is so damn annoying!”

“Yumao said it before: for UH, even a one-for-one trade is a win,” Tang Yu’an said, unusually calm.

Well, since Dou Jiayan was drawing all the fire, the bot lane remained exceptionally peaceful.

But either way, it was time to launch a counterattack.

So he immediately added, “Yanzi, come bot right now. Yu He, you too.”

“Is it finally time for revenge?” Dou Jiayan rubbed his hands together eagerly. “I’ve been waiting for this!”

Ling Suyu tapped to check both teams’ gold counts. If the enemy mid laner and jungler didn’t make it in time, they could win.

But if they did, they’d be at a significant disadvantage—after all, they were the ones diving the tower.

With just Wang Jingxuan and Cui Rao’s awareness, they would never have anticipated Fight’s move.

But as luck would have it, UH had Jiang Hao.

And as if he knew exactly what Ling Suyu was thinking, Tang Yu’an immediately added, “Xiaoyun, teleport down here, right now.”

Ling Suyu raised an eyebrow.

Over on UH’s side, before Dou Jiayan had even respawned, Jiang Hao spoke up warily: “Cui Rao come bot, and Jingxuan come after clearing your minions. I’m not sure if they’ll gank bot, but if they don’t, we will!”

His prediction landed; Fight was preparing to gank the bot lane.

But what he didn’t anticipate was that shortly after Dou Jiayan respawned, a teleportation rune appeared on a minion in the bot lane.

On Fight’s side, the only player with Teleport in his Talents was the top laner, Skeletal Empress!

By the time Jiang Hao realized that the entire Fight team was rushing to the bottom lane, it was already too late.

Their top laner, Dark Angel, regenerated health so quickly that he didn’t need to recall to the base to recover. So naturally, he didn’t have Teleport and couldn’t make it down in time.

In this team fight at the bottom lane, UH was literally one teammate short.

As soon as Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen unleashed her ultimate, Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress leaped over the tower with a hammer strike, stunning the enemy marksman and support. Dou Jiayan’s Wind Guardian immediately followed up with his ultimate, “Hurricane,” sweeping the two—who were already airborne—even higher into the sky.

Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit quickly locked onto Jiang Hao’s Guardian Spirit, marked him with “Spirit Mark,” then unleashed a barrage—“biu biu biu!”

Three basic attacks triggered his passive ability “Shadow Strike” and the purple dragon’s “lightning strike.” Before Ling Suyu could even cast his ultimate, and before the Guardian Spirit could recover from being CC’d, his health plummeted to zero in the blink of an eye.

【System】 Cat Spirit, one kill!

【System】 The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

Jiang Hao stared at his own character lying on the ground on screen, clenching his mouse tightly before quickly letting go.

What was the most frustrating thing in a game?

It wasn’t getting killed—it was getting killed without even casting a single skill!

Dying without doing anything—it was a death filled with resentment, like when a person, on the brink of death and unable to move, suddenly remembered they still had 100 million in the bank that they haven’t taken care of yet.

But as UH’s captain, commander, and emotional backbone, he couldn’t let those emotions show, so he said calmly, “Don’t come here—we can’t hold the bottom lane’s first tower anymore.”

But… it was too late.

Seeing his marksman instantly killed, Abyssal Lord broke free from the crowd control and hurriedly teleported away.

Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit cast “Shadow Steps” to give chase. After landing several auto-attacks, just before the enemy could escape his attack range, he marked him with a newly refreshed “Spirit Mark.” Then, while turning to exit the tower, he unleashed his ultimate “Spiritual Explosion.”

With a “boom,” green visual effects exploded across Abyssal Lord’s body, transforming into dazzling fireworks that scattered like starlight before vanishing into the air.

Vanishing along with them was Abyssal Lord’s remaining health.

Abyssal Lord collapsed with his back turned to the Cat Spirit.

【System】Cat Spirit, double kill! Peerless!

【System】 The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain!

Be they spectators at the venue or viewers watching the livestream, everyone held their breath.

For the first six minutes of this game, they had watched helplessly as Fight and UH’s gold gap grew wider and wider—with UH holding the lead—leaving them feeling suffocated.

Having their own predictions proven wrong was one thing, but if Feather were to be slapped in the face, they’d be so embarrassed they’d spend the night digging out a mansion with their feet.

After all, they’d merely tossed around casual comments online like “UH is trash” and “Fight Victory,” whereas Ling Suyu was actually part of the whole fiasco.

They could almost picture just how rampant UH’s fans, who were just as shameless as UH themselves, would become if Fight lost to UH.

So, a while into the match, even those who had originally declared “Fight will definitely win” dared not utter a word. Some even reminded their friends, “Don’t jinx it! Believe in Fight!”

Then—whether it was because they’d stopped setting themselves up for failure or not—Fight suddenly launched a fierce assault on UH’s bottom lane, and Ling Suyu, as their carry, easily secured two kills!

Seeing this, Fight’s supporters felt a sense of vindication, yet they nervously clenched their fists, afraid that something might go wrong.

Even the commentators were holding their breath for Fight.

Ah-Mu: “Fight showed perfect coordination, instantly taking down UH’s marksman! But after Cat Spirit killed UH’s support, he emerged from the other side of the tower—and UH’s jungler is right nearby. Without any dash abilities, can he escape?!”

UH’s jungler, Cui Rao’s Venomous Scorpion, was indeed nearby—Ling Suyu knew this all too well. That was exactly why he’d saved his Flash.

At that moment, UH’s top laner had teleported back to base and would be arriving any second.

He had to escape the jungler as quickly as possible and get back to his teammates.

While guiding Cat Spirit to move along the wall toward his teammates, Ling Suyu kept a sharp eye out for Venomous Scorpion, who could appear at any moment.

Cui Rao, lurking in the bushes, eyed Ling Suyu intently: “I can get him!”

Jiang Hao hesitated for only a moment before making his decision: “Do it now!”

In the next instant, Venomous Scorpion hit Cat Spirit right in the face with “Poison Fog’s Shadow,” followed immediately by his ultimate, “Fatal Venom Stinger”!

He had to act as swiftly as possible! He had to cast his ultimate with lightning-fast reflexes! Otherwise, Cat Spirit would escape!

The fragile Cat Spirit couldn’t withstand Venomous Scorpion’s ultimate damage. If the Venomous Scorpion, armed with its ultimate, could tower-dive and instantly kill Dou Jiayan’s Wind Guardian, how could it possibly fail to instantly take down Cat Spirit!

However, the very moment Venomous Scorpion appeared, Ling Suyu’s eyes suddenly widened.

In that split second, in his eyes, every movement of Venomous Scorpion’s was broken down into a series of afterimages, and a moment so brief it would normally go unnoticed was stretched out indefinitely.

Before Venomous Scorpion had even appeared, Ling Suyu’s finger was already resting on the F key. The moment the Venomous Scorpion materialized and raised its claw, Ling Suyu swiftly pressed the F key while simultaneously holding down the left mouse button with his right hand and dragging the cursor toward his teammates’ position.

In the next instant, Venomous Scorpion’s ultimate missed its mark, and Ling Suyu, having narrowly escaped, locked onto it in return.

With a blank expression, he fired!

“Impossible!” Cui Rao exclaimed in disbelief!

He’d used his fastest reflexes—there was absolutely no way he could have missed!

Perhaps because he was too shocked, he stood there and took two basic attacks before using Flash to escape. Yu He, who was already lurking nearby, easily caught up to him and secured the kill!

【System】 Twin Demonblades, one kill!

【System】 The enemy Venomous Scorpion has been slain!

After taking down Cui Rao’s Venomous Scorpion, Yu He didn’t even glance at his fallen body. Instead, he chased down Nine-Tailed Fox, who had been severely weakened by his other teammates, and cleanly secured another kill.

【System】 Twin Demonblades, double kill! Peerless!

【System】 The enemy Nine-Tailed Fox has been slain!

With that, the team fight on the bottom lane came to a complete end. Fight pulled off a near-perfect tower dive, securing a 0-for-4 exchange.

When UH’s top laner, Dark Angel, arrived at the bottom lane, he looked at the enemy’s health bars and realized there wasn’t a single target he could finish off. He could only cower under the second tower, watching helplessly as they took down the first tower, then silently clearing minions.

This team fight left UH feeling incredibly frustrated.

Not only did the carry marksman fail to cast a single ability, but the jungler—who wouldn’t have died if he hadn’t engaged—was counter-killed while trying to take down Cat Spirit, needlessly giving away a kill.

The only consolation was that Fight had initiated this team fight after sacrificing their top lane first tower; shortly after the fight ended, their top lane first tower was pushed down by UH’s minions.

But thanks to this team fight, Fight managed to pull off a comeback against the odds—not only did they close the economic gap with UH, but they even surpassed them!

Seeing Ling Suyu return to the team, Fight’s members happily set off to take down the Divine Sunbird.

Little did they know that both the commentators and the audience were holding their breath for Ling Suyu and still hadn’t recovered from the shock.

“Feather dodged UH’s jungler’s ambush! How did he do that?!” Ah-Mu’s eyes widened in shock!

He was a commentator for 《Glorious Heroes》and also a player at the Glorious tier, specializing in junglers and marksmen, though he’d dabbled in other positions as well.

Venomous Scorpion was his go-to hero, and he’d pulled off these ultimate ambushes more than once, with a very high success rate.

Venomous Scorpion relied on its ultimate to deal damage. As long as it hit a squishy target, even if it didn’t take out all their health in one hit, the follow-up damage was more than enough to finish them off.

However, as much as he enjoyed playing Venomous Scorpion, he hated facing it when playing as a marksman.

He’d been taken down by an enemy Venomous Scorpion’s sneak attack many times—this move was practically unstoppable.

Just now, Cui Rao’s timing and angle were nothing short of perfect. Logically speaking, Feather’s Cat Spirit shouldn’t have been able to dodge it.

But he dodged it!

How?

The director showed a slow-motion replay, but even in slow motion, the time between Venomous Scorpion’s ambush and Cat Spirit’s Flash-escape was incredibly brief. It felt as though Cat Spirit had originally intended to Flash toward his teammates, and Venomous Scorpion just happened to charge out at that exact moment.

But after using Flash to escape, Feather didn’t keep moving forward; instead, he turned around, locked onto Venomous Scorpion, and started auto-attacking. This proved—

It wasn’t an accident! It wasn’t a coincidence! Feather truly dodged Venomous Scorpion’s attack!

In just a fraction of a second!

“Unbelievable! Is that even a reaction speed a human can achieve?!” Ah-Mu exclaimed. “If it were me, I definitely wouldn’t have been able to dodge it! I’m sure there aren’t many in the entire esports scene who could pull that off!”

Although ZhiZhi didn’t play junglers or marksmen, Cat Spirit’s astonishing reaction speed was nevertheless evident to the naked eye, and her eyes were widened in shock. After struggling to find the right words for a while, she finally managed to blurt out: “Feather is awesome!”

“No wonder he’s the legendary 0-death marksman!” Ah-Mu continued to marvel. “That survival instinct’s engraved in his bones!”

ZhiZhi was at a loss for words: “Feather is awesome!!”
