Seeing XY go for Skeletal Empress, Fight aggressively selected Candy Witch and Mobile Artillery, two heroes they hadn’t used in a long time.

Fight hadn’t used this dual-carry combo in any of their matches against the top eight so far.

Any observer could tell that while Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch was extremely powerful, able to secure an advantage in the lane and carry the team all the way, Dou Jiayan’s Mobile Artillery hadn’t shown any standout performance. He played very conservatively, as if he hadn’t practiced it at all—and as a matter of fact, he truly hadn’t practiced much.

So it was completely understandable that they didn’t use this duo when going up against previous quarterfinalists in the group stages—the stronger they were in the early game, the greater the risk in the late game.

Because of this, XY bet that Fight wouldn’t use this duo in the quarterfinals, so they only banned Cat Spirit and didn’t ban Candy Witch. The result? Fight instantly locked Candy Witch and Mobile Artillery as their first two picks!

The entire XY team’s eyes widened in shock.

Their first reaction—Oh no, this game is going to be tough.

Their second reaction—Are you guys at Fight underestimating us?

On one hand, they felt Fight’s lineup wasn’t fully developed; on the other, they knew it would be a tricky matchup.

XY’s feelings were mixed at that moment. They felt that Fight had brought out this lineup again because they didn’t take XY seriously, but the instant they saw the lineup, they genuinely felt a headache coming on.

“What should we do?”

“Swap the mid and bot lanes,” suggested XY’s coach. “That’s our only option for now.”

So, XY immediately locked in the mid laner Deepsea Faerie and the marksman Barrier Sorceress.

【Barrier Sorceress!】

【They actually locked in Barrier Sorceress!】

Upon seeing this marksman, commentators Liang Fan and Remi both let out cries of disbelief.

This was a sustained-damage marksman, one without any movement abilities or speed debuffs, who could only rely on her ultimate to stay alive.

That wasn’t the point though. The real issue was that, in terms of how “niche” the pick was, this season’s Barrier Sorceress was on par with Cat Spirit; its pick rate was practically zero, so seeing it appear in a professional match was nothing short of a miracle.

Under what circumstances would anyone pick Barrier Sorceress as a marksman?

When facing Mobile Artillery—a poke-type marksman whose first ability was a lock-on skill.

To avoid your health being drained, you had to maintain a certain distance.

But if you wanted to poke back, that’s when you picked Barrier Sorceress.

However, neither of Barrier Sorceress’s two poke abilities dealt much damage, so you’d ultimately have to rely on auto-attacks to deal damage.

Nevertheless, these two skills had long ranges. As long as you maintained the right distance from Mobile Artillery, you could poke him back and secure a certain laning advantage.

“Is XY planning to run Barrier Sorceress in the middle lane?” Liang Fan speculated. “Fight’s Feather will likely run Candy Witch in the bot lane, so the mid laner will be Mobile Artillery. XY’s Barrier Sorceress pick seems more like a counter to Mobile Artillery than to Candy Witch, so she’s definitely going mid. Deepsea Faerie going bot to face the Candy Witch—while her wave-clearing isn’t as fast as Candy Witch’s, she isn’t easy to kill either.”

If the commentators could see this, how could Fight possibly miss it?

“Barrier Sorceress in the mid lane?” Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow. “That’s quite a surprise.”

“I haven’t really faced off against a Barrier Sorceress before,” Dou Jiayan admitted, then immediately asked his teammates, “What should I watch out for?”

“Just clear the minions.”

“Don’t worry about her, just focus on farming.”

Ling Suyu and Tang Yu’an answered him almost simultaneously.

Then Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow and glanced at Ling Suyu, gesturing for him to continue.

Ling Suyu continued, “Barrier Sorceress’s first and second skills have a long range, but their damage is limited. Your first skill deals more damage than hers, so there’s no need to be timid. If you can dodge by moving, do so; if you can’t, it’s no big deal. Prioritize farming minions to level up and reach level two, and just poke her.” He paused for a moment before adding, “Keep an eye on the jungler—don’t get ganked.”

“OK~that puts my mind at ease!” Dou Jiayan breathed a sigh of relief. “You’re gonna carry me this game!”

“Here we go again!” Tang Yu’an sighed in exasperation. “Don’t get too cocky just because you’ve practiced a few extra days. With the crit-build you’re running, your auto-attacks won’t do much damage early on. Just play it safe and wait for Yumao to get going, then ride his coattails.”

“Don’t worry, I’ve got this~” Dou Jiayan was raring to go. “It’s my first time showing off the Mobile Artillery I’ve been practicing in a pro match, I’m getting kind of excited! I’m going to show everyone who doubts my Mobile Artillery what the real way to use it is!”

Ling Suyu chuckled at this but said nothing.

He certainly wasn’t going to admit that Dou Jiayan’s Mobile Artillery was the true way to play it.

Building for attack speed and sustained damage was the way to go!

In fact, in《Glorious Heroes》, it was often hard to definitively classify many marksmen as either sustained damage or burst damage types. Ultimately, it comes down to the choice of two different builds: Cat Spirit could be built for burst, and Guardian Spirit could be built for attack speed.

However, a burst-damage Cat Spirit was truly a useless cat—it dealt virtually no damage aside from its ultimate.

Likewise, a Guardian Spirit built for attack speed also dealt no damage, because it would get insta-killed before it could finish off the enemy. Follow-up damage? Doesn’t exist.

For a sustained-damage marksman to deal damage, they had to first ensure they had the ability to deal sustained damage. To put it simply, it came down to three words: “Do not die.”

For a burst-damage marksman to deal damage, they had to ensure that a single skill combo could instantly kill their opponent. To put it simply, it also came down to three words: “Watch the skills.”

Cat Spirit was a classic sustained-damage marksman. Her first skill boosted damage, and her passive enhanced basic attacks, so the higher her attack speed, the more basic attacks she can land in a short period, resulting in higher damage output.

Guardian Spirit was a classic burst-damage marksman. Her skills dealt high damage, but she had weak survival capabilities; she had to ensure she dealt the highest possible damage in the shortest amount of time. Otherwise, if she couldn't instantly kill an opponent, she'd instantly killed herself.

Mobile Artillery was a bit of a mixed bag. His skills dealt high damage, and this season was ideal for building burst-damage items, yet his ultimate is actually a sustained-damage ability.

“Indiscriminate Barrage”: For a set duration, it rained down artillery fire upon all enemies within a fan-shaped area. The number of barrages fired during this time depended on your auto-attack speed; the higher your attack speed, the more barrages you’d fire.

Therefore, when playing Mobile Artillery, Ling Suyu tended to prioritize attack speed. After using skills 1 and 2 to wear down the enemy, he’d chase them down with basic attacks. Midway through a team fight, he’d activate his ultimate to cut in, filling the screen with shells so the enemy couldn’t even see his other skills.

With this hero, it was incredibly fun to use Flash after casting the ultimate to suddenly dive in and catch the enemy off guard.

Dou Jiayan had actually picked up on this tactic, though he still insisted on building burst damage items. However, this season’s Mobile Artillery was indeed better suited for burst damage builds; if you actually built attack speed, the ultimate may look intimidating but dealt limited damage—the visual effects were top-notch, but the damage is just a few tenths of the enemy’s HP.

So Ling Suyu just muttered the rebuttal in his heart—building attack speed for sustained damage was the way to go!

He didn’t say a word out loud.

Soon, both teams’ lineups were set. Aside from XY picking Barrier Sorceress, which took everyone by surprise, the heroes in every other position were exactly as everyone had expected.

It wasn’t surprising at all that XY would pick White Cocoon, a top-lane hero, to play as a jungler; after all, this wouldn’t be the first time they pulled it off.

The lineups for both teams were as follows:

Fight: Black-Armoured Scythe, Mobile Artillery, Candy Witch, Mandara Vines, Tempest Blade.

XY: Skeletal Empress, Deepsea Faerie, Barrier Sorceress, Luminous Saintess, White Cocoon.

The countdown ended quickly, and both teams entered the battlefield.

Just before actually entering the battlefield, Dou Jiayan tentatively asked, “Do we need to swap mid and bot lanes?”

Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu replied almost simultaneously, “No need.”

“Ok~” Dou Jiayan responded, then wore an expression of keen interest.

In the past, whenever he asked questions like this, Tang Yu’an would answer them on his own, while Ling Suyu acted as if he hadn’t heard a thing.

Dou Jiayan knew that Ling Suyu wasn’t ignoring him because he had a problem with him; he simply felt that Tang Yu’an would answer these kinds of questions, so there was no need for him to butt in.

But now… it seemed like Ling Suyu was becoming more and more inclined to express his own thoughts.

Dou Jiayan couldn’t help but chuckle. “Are you two that in sync? Trying to sing a duet[^1]?”

“Oh, come on,” Tang Yu’an shot back immediately. “If your Chinese teachers heard you say that, they’d definitely crawl right out of the internet cable to beat you up! Do you even know how to use words? What we’re doing is called ‘speaking in unison’!”

“Fine, fine, ‘in unison.’ I also know the phrase ‘unintentionally in sync,’” Dou Jiayan pouted.

“I’d rather you’d say we have a ‘tacit understanding,’[^2]” Tang Yu’an replied.

Dou Jiayan: ?

“Do you even know how to use words? ‘Tacit understanding’ is for lovers!” Dou Jiayan finally found a point to fire back, “You must’ve gotten beaten up by your Chinese teacher a lot when you were a kid too, right?”

Ling Suyu blinked at this: “‘Too’?”

Tang Yu’an burst out laughing: “Yes, he used to get beaten up by his Chinese teacher all the time when he was a kid.”

“What are you talking about!” Dou Jiayan nearly jumped out of his skin. “I never did!”

Tang Yu’an: “Ask Yumao if he believes you.”

Ling Suyu: “I have the right to reserve my thoughts.”

Dou Jiayan was about to argue his case further when he suddenly heard Mo Xiaoyun chuckle beside him and say, “Now that’s what you call ‘dueting.’”

Dou Jiayan: ??

“Hmm… Let’s focus on the match.”

Ling Suyu said this and fell silent.

Once the deed is done, he turns and walks away, keeping his achievements and fame hidden.

Dou Jiayan looked sad: “Haah, Yumao has been corrupted by the captain. He’s no longer that little angel who used to stand up for me when others picked on me.”

That depended on who was picking on you, and why…

Ling Suyu silently replied in his heart.

By this point, the loading bar had finished, and both teams’ heroes had entered the battlefield.

Ling Suyu used his mouse to guide his Candy Witch toward the lane. He took a deep breath and focused his attention on the match.

Unnoticed by him, Tang Yu’an’s lips quirked up as he kept his eyes on his screen, before he guided his Mandara Vines to follow him.

‘Singing a duet’?

That sounded pretty good.

They could sing more often in the future~
