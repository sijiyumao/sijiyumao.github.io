Ling Suyu, as a professional player from UH, naturally followed UH’s official account.

As such, as soon as he opened Weibo, the post from UH’s official account appeared on his page.

After reading through the entire thing, his pupils dilated, and his mind went blank.

Although his teammates at UH were unused to his playstyle, and it was true that UH lost more often than won when he competed, he didn’t expect UH to take advantage of his contract termination to place all the blame of their horrible performance during the White Fox Cup on him. They had twisted the narrative of his contract simply expiring into “kicked out of the team because he wasn’t strong enough”.

He’d already found a team that was willing to take him in, so the Weibo post’s actual effect on him was diminished. However, he couldn’t help feeling angry and wronged.

Tang Yu’an watched as Ling Suyu took his phone out, knowing that he was going to check Weibo. Although he’d verbally stopped him, he didn’t take any actual preventative actions. In fact, he was rather curious about the other’s reaction to the Weibo post.

As he drove, he secretly glanced at Ling Suyu beside him, only to see the little white penguin staring unblinkingly at his phone screen, as if he’d crashed.

Tang Yu’an had originally wanted to tease him like he would with Dou Jiayan, but couldn’t bear to after seeing his expression, quickly adding: “Try checking the reposts?”

Ling Suyu froze for a second before coming to his senses. He glanced at Tang Yu’an in confusion, before obediently opening the reposts… “Huh?”

The reposts were arranged by the number of likes, and the first-ranked post was— Fight_eSports_Club: Since UH doesn’t want such a brilliant marksman, then don’t mind us Fight swooping in! Just wondering if Feather@GloriousHeroesFeather is willing? If you’re willing to accept our offer, we’ll directly let you play in the starting lineup for the Spring Seasonal!

Closely following, the second post— Winne_eSports_Club: Feather@GloriousHeroesFeather look over here! Winne’s open to taking in heartbroken marksman, saving you a spot in our team 2’s starting lineup!

The third post— YJ_eSports_Club: We’re very grateful for Feather@GloriousHeroesFeather’s high-level performance during the White Fox Cup. It’s made us realize that we still have much to improve. The YJ eSports Club sincerely invites your joining!

The fourth post— MaMingxiistrash: F*ck you UH! As if you could win against us if Jiang Hao went up! If Feather came to YJ, he could still hang you upside down and beat you up!

Ling Suyu: ?

Many different teams reposted UH’s Weibo, showing recognition for his strength.

No matter if these teams actually wanted to sign him, or if they just wanted to take the chance to step on UH, Ling Suyu still felt grateful towards them.

But Ma Mingxi?

The same Ma Mingxi who provoked him for never competing during the White Fox Cup?

The guy who told him that he’d be able to kill him once before reaching level 6, only to be counterkilled and slapped in the face, that Ma Mingxi?

Although this account had a golden V signifying that it had been certified, Ling Suyu couldn’t help clicking into the account to double check.

【Glorious Heroes professional player, currently playing for YJ eSports Club】

It really was Ma Mingxi 0.0 In addition, his latest post was:

MaMingxiistrash: alright alright, I’m trash, insult me all you like. Besides, no matter how many insults you throw, I’m still the king of YJ!

And the one before that:

MaMingxiistrash: why do you want limits for trash talk? The next time I compete against Feather, I’ll still say the same things! The question is whether or not he’ll still have the chance to compete!

Ling Suyu: ???

Shocking! Immediately after YJ’s marksman finishes mocking his opponent, he turns around and helps said opponent fight against other teams, is this a sign of split personality or moral awakening!

Ling Suyu didn’t understand, but he was deeply astonished!

He tilted his head as he scrolled through Ma Mingxi’s Weibo, as if watching a mental patient who’d escaped from the asylum.

Tang Yu’an took the chance to glance at him again and, seeing his face, couldn’t hold back a chuckle: “What’s with that expression?”

Hearing his voice, Ling Suyu froze momentarily. No longer pondering over Ma Mingxi’s bewildering behaviour, he turned to look at Tang Yu’an’s side profile, asking out of curiosity:

“Did Fight always have the intention of signing me?”

“Yep,” Tang Yu’an admitted, “Right after the Summer Seasonal ended, I took Chen Jie to the hospital. You know Chen Jie, right? Our team’s marksman.”

Ling Suyu made a hum of acknowledgement.

“The doctor said he had severe tendinitis. He needed to immediately stop training and rest for a long period of time,” as he spoke, Tang Yu’an gave a self-deprecating laugh, “He was too good at bearing the pain. Without that trip to the hospital, I wouldn’t have even realized how serious the condition of his hands had gotten.”

“Is that why Fight didn’t participate in this year’s All-Stars and White Fox Cups?” Ling Suyu had a sudden realization.

“Mhm,” Tang Yu’an nodded, “With our rag-tag team, much less a second team, we don’t even have substitutes. If even one person is missing, we can’t compete. So, for Fight, for Chen Jie, we have to sign a new marksman. Although…” his peripheral gaze swept over Ling Suyu, “I sent the Weibo post after you agreed to come to Fight. I intentionally didn’t reveal that you had already agreed, so I’m guessing there should be quite a few teams hopping in?”

Ling Suyu blinked before belatedly realizing something, hurriedly checking Fight’s repost to see that it was sent just a few minutes ago.

And in comparison to the reposts from the other teams, Fight’s was the earliest.

Was this…the result of him intentionally setting the tone?

“How many others joined in?” Tang Yu’an suddenly asked, “Did Winne repost?

“They posted,” Ling Suyu responded, “Not only Winne, but also YJ, and basically all of the top 8 teams.”

Tang Yu’an: ?

“Damn.”

Though this situation was a result of Tang Yu’an deliberate manipulations, it still went beyond his expectations: “You’re so popular. That gives me a lot of pressure…”

Ling Suyu: “I’m already in your car.”

Tang Yu’an: “Hey, we at Fight don’t engage in kidnapping or threats.”

“No need, I’ve already decided on Fight,” Ling Suyu said, unable to stop the corners of his lips from rising.

If it were just a top 4 team, then UH also counted.

The reason why Ling Suyu agreed to join Fight was not because it was in the top 4.

It was because…

Ling Suyu peeked at Tang Yu’an, hesitant to speak, before ultimately deciding not to say anything.

Even though nothing seemed wrong with saying that a marksman decided to join a particular team because of a support.

But…it would be better to not say anything for now, in case he was misunderstood.

Though there was also the chance that it wasn’t a misunderstanding?

Ling Suyu snuck a look at Tang Yu’an again, before guiltily shifting his gaze away.

Tang Yu’an glanced back at him out of the corner of his eye, taking his guilt as shyness. He couldn’t hold back a few light chuckles: “Pretty good. That means that match we played today wasn’t for nothing. I know you want to praise me but feel a embarrassed, no worries, I’ll compliment myself~”

Ling Suyu put a hand on his forehead, his face full of exasperation.

This support was quite good, but he was a bit narcissistic.

“By the way…” Ling Suyu decided to change the topic, “Aside from teams, some players also reposted, including Ma Mingxi.”

“YJ’s Ma Mingxi?” Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow in surprise, “As expected, in regards to a player’s strength, no one’s clearer about it than those who’ve fought against them before.”

“Even though his previous Weibo post said that the next time he competed against me, he’d still trash talk me the same way…” Ling Suyu quietly complained, “the post immediately after supported me against UH, saying that if I joined YJ I could easily hang them upside down and beat them up.”

“Hahaha, as expected of YJ’s darling, he’s truly direct with his words!” Tang Yu’an laughed, “Nevertheless, because of his trash talk, he’s been scolded quite a lot these past few days, so it counts as karma.” After a pause, he asked, “So, after seeing the reposts, do you feel better?”

Ling Suyu made an affirming sound.

Much less good, he was feeling completely refreshed!

When he first saw UH’s Weibo post and the comments underneath it, he’d felt as if someone had thrown him into an ice cellar, and all the blood in his body had turned to ice.

But now…

【Feather, you should join Fight! Chen Jie’s hand is injured now, so if you join Fight you could have the chance of playing as a starting member!】

【This is too good! Feather, just pick any of the top 8! Any one of them is tens of thousands of times stronger than UH!】

【I want to see Feather compete! Destroy UH! Let them know who’s boss!】

【Patting Feather, let’s see if UH dares to block the comments from other teams! You really thought Weibo was your home turf?】

【Go, Feather! Looking forward to your future matches!】

Beneath the reposts from the other teams were countless comments expressing their support. In fact, these voices of support even had the advantage.

On the other hand, UH’s braindead fans could only jump around underneath the original post. As soon as they entered the posts from the other teams, they’d be insulted to the point of logging off.

As Ling Suyu stared and stared, even though he was feeling refreshed in his heart, his vision blurred uncontrollably.

He left Weibo, gazed at the passing scenery outside the car window, and sniffled.

Hearing this, Tang Yu’an tilted his head to look at him, unable to hold back a chuckle: “Hey, is someone making our Fight’s new member sad?”

As he said this, he took a tissue out of the box on the dashboard and handed it to him.

Ling Suyu quietly accepted it, blowing his nose.

“Pft.” Tang Yu’an felt that Ling Suyu was way too cute, and couldn’t stop himself from reaching out a hand and ruffling his hair. “It’s a good thing Fight is on break. Otherwise, if the others see, they might think I bullied you.” Another pause, before his voice grew gentler, “Let it out. I’m the only one here. No one will laugh at you. When you’re finished crying and happy, then you can play well in the Spring Seasonal and bring a trophy back with you.”

“En,” Ling Suyu responded softly. Looking at Tang Yu’an reflection in the car window, he suddenly felt reassured.

His entire life, only his parents had ever patted him on his head, and he was rather averse to it because he felt that he’d already grown up and didn’t want to be treated like a child.

But just then, when Tang Yu’an touched his head, he didn’t feel the urge to reject him at all.

In fact, it even felt…nice?

It was quite magical. Despite this being the first time he’d been in close contact with this person, he felt a sort of unconditional trust. It was as if he had a form of special charisma, one that made him lower all his defenses and let him depend on the other with ease.

Almost like Jiang Hao.

Except, unlike Jiang Hao, Tang Yu’an lacked a feeling of condescension, and in place of that was a sense of warmth and consideration.

Basically, it was comforting to be by his side.

“We’re almost there,” Tang Yu’an suddenly said.

Ling Suyu rubbed his eyes. In the distance, he could see a fancy-looking building, with “Fight eSports Club” on top of the building in the form of a red neon sign, something that would probably look beautiful at night.

Huh? Where was the so-called “rag-tag team”?

This scale was probably even larger than UH’s.

Ling Suyu gazed at it in curiosity, and just as he wanted to ask who else was in the club at the moment, the phone he’d been holding suddenly started vibrating, giving him a jumpscare.

He subconsciously lowered his head, the word “Manager” being displayed on the screen startling him.

It was a call from UH’s manager.

His contract was just about to end, so why was the manager calling him?

Ling Suyu furrowed his brows, instinctively not wanting to pick up this call. But ultimately, he sighed and slid the icon to accept the call on his screen: “Hello?”
