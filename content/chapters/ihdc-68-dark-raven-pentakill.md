Tang Yu'an said that when Yu He picked Blade Soul, he didn’t aim to win the game—he aimed to carry the team.

When he first heard this, Ling Suyu had wondered: How could someone carry the team by picking a champion rarely used in competitive play? He assumed it was probably just an exaggerated claim, like Dou Jiayan’s “I definitely won’t feed with this hero.”

However, this game truly opened his eyes.

Probably frustrated about having his jungle camps stolen, around the 8-minute mark, the enemy jungler, Tempest Blade, took advantage of the fact that his team’s mid laner had secured vision in the top river to sneak into Fight’s top jungle.

Unfortunately, at that very moment, Yu He’s Blade Soul was in the top-jungle area, luring a red dragon into the brush to take it down.

Tempest Blade noticed the red dragon wasn’t in the pit and immediately grew suspicious.

Relying on his full health, he didn’t hesitate to cast “Razor Gale” into the brush by the pit, followed immediately by “Tornado”!

“Junglers have a sixth sense—he must have intuited Blade Soul’s presence!” Liang Fan explained.

Sure enough, Yu He was right there in the bushes. Seeing Tempest Blade’s attack, he immediately used “Absolute Defense” to block it. Then, noticing the red dragon had been whittled down to its last sliver of health, he decisively used Smite!

Almost simultaneously, Tempest Blade also activated Smite!

Both were professional junglers, and their Smite timing couldn’t have been better. With a difference of just a few tenths of a second, the odds of either jungler claiming the buff came down to pure luck.

When she saw the red icon appear above Tempest Blade’s head, Remi couldn’t help but exclaim, “Ah! Tempest Blade snatched it!”

No sooner had she spoken than Yu He’s Blade Soul surged forward, unleashing its ultimate ability, “Nine-Stage Blade Flash”!

Tempest Blade teleported away and countered with his ultimate, “Shattered Blade Whirlwind”!

Blade Soul teleported away as well. As soon as his ultimate finished, he instantly teleported back with “Flash of a Blade” and unleashed “Blade Shadow Combo”!

Tempest Blade dodged the attack with the movement skill “Gale Dash,” then immediately used the freshly recharged “Tornado” to pull himself back in and unleashed “Razor Gale”!

Blade Soul blocked the attack with “Absolute Defense”!

And just like that, these two squishy champions fought for what felt like an eternity, with neither of their health bars dropping at all.

The audience watched as the two junglers spun, leaped, and darted back and forth in Fight’s top jungle, their eyes wide open, bouncing left and right and zigzagging repeatedly, only to eventually revert to the most basic form of brawling: trading basic attacks.

However, when it came down to basic attacks, how could Tempest Blade—an assassin—possibly match up against Blade Soul, who straddled the line between assassin and warrior?

After exchanging just a couple of attacks, they could tell who would live and who would die simply by watching their health bars drop.

Tempest Blade decisively turned and decided to flee.

With two dashes, he tried to escape—and Blade Soul might not actually be able to catch him.

In the past, Yu He would have been too lazy to bother with him; he would’ve just chased him away and gone back to farming his own jungle.

But now, he was playing Blade Soul!

Plus, Tempest Blade was carrying a buff stolen from his jungle!

Without a moment’s hesitation, Yu He raised his blade and gave chase: “Yanzi, block him.”

Dou Jiayan retreated into the tower and planted himself at the entrance to the jungle. Tempest Blade made a sharp turn, but Yu He’s Blade Soul caught up and finished him off with a single strike!

【System】 Blade Soul, one kill! Dominating!

【System】 The enemy Tempest Blade has been slain!

And just like that, the red dragon buff left Tempest Blade and appeared on Yu He’s Blade Soul. Satisfied, Yu He returned to the jungle to continue farming.

Ling Suyu blinked.

He didn’t switch his view over to watch, but he knew that Yu He’s Blade Soul had solo-killed Tempest Blade.

When Tempest Blade appeared in Fight’s vision, neither Mo Xiaoyun nor Dou Jiayan did anything but block—they didn’t rush over to help. Tang Yuan didn’t say anything either; clearly, he trusted Yu He’s skills.

Blade Soul’s damage wasn’t as high as Tempest Blade’s. The health Tempest Blade could finish off with just its abilities alone required Blade Soul to follow up with auto-attacks.

However, Blade Soul’s “Absolute Defense” could block all damage for a full second. Used effectively, it ensured Blade Soul’s sustained damage output.

This was why Blade Soul, a hero with damage and defense that was neither high nor low and nothing particularly outstanding, making it a rather awkward choice, still managed to secure a spot on the ban list.

Players who picked Blade Soul were either powerhouses capable of racking up pentakills and carrying the team, or complete failures who couldn’t do anything right.

Clearly, Yu He fell into the former category.

XY was stunned by Yu He’s skill; they no longer thought Fight was underestimating them. So what if their marksman was Dark Raven? Their jungler had pulled out the Dominating Blade! —a.k.a. a Blade Soul capable of dominating the battlefield.

“Is Fight going to build their strategy around the jungler this game?” Even the commentators were misled by Yu He’s performance, and Liang Fan made an incorrect prediction: “Fight’s jungler is playing very aggressively this game! If he can farm up, he could easily become Fight’s main damage dealer! Fight’s sudden shift in style—could it be because they were inspired by igame?”

igame’s jungler, Ye Xunjie, felt an itch in his nose and was about to sneeze again: “I knew Yu He was strong, but I never expected him to have improved this much… Ah-choo!”

Ling Suyu silently cleared minions.

Although he’d declared before the match that he wanted to compete for MVP, once the game actually started, he didn’t really care about the MVP. He just wanted to win the match.

All those MVPs he’d won in the past weren’t something he’d gone out of his way to get—he’d just played his usual game, and that was all.

So, Ling Suyu continued clearing minions, farming the jungle, and leveling up as usual.

Then, at the 11-minute mark, both teams clashed in the brush near the mid-lane’s frontline towers.

It might be hard to believe, but it was Dou Jiayan who initiated the fight.

Seeing the enemy team clustered together so closely, Dou Jiayan’s fingers itched, and he unleashed a full skill combo right at them!

Phoenix Soul went on a frenzied spree of poke damage before appearing right in the middle of XY’s formation, only to be instantly CC’d and taken down in a matter of seconds—he didn’t even have time to cast his life-saving ultimate.

A decisive kill, a swift death.

But immediately afterward, Mo Xiaoyun’s Black-Armoured Scythe pulled himself right into the fray, and Tang Yu’an’s Skeletal Empress launched a jumping hammer attack, officially kicking off the team fight!

The two of them absorbed damage in the center while Yu He’s Blade Soul flanked and went on a killing spree, and Ling Suyu’s Dark Raven silently followed behind. Once his enhanced skill activated, whoosh!

The marksmen on both sides ramped up their attack speed. Cat Spirit’s green shadow blades came one after another in an unending stream, but Ling Suyu’s Dark Raven was even more devastating—countless black feathers trailed after one another toward the target, leaving afterimages in their wake.

Both teams’ heroes were losing health, and XY’s side shouted, “Take out the marksman first!”

The mid-lane Deepsea Faerie, clinging to her last sliver of health, used Flash to dash right in front of the Dark Raven. As soon as her Water Lotus struck Dark Raven, she impatiently unleashed her ultimate. The marksman, Cat Spirit, used “Shadow Steps” to similarly dash in front of the Dark Raven; the moment her “Spirit Mark” landed, she activated “Spiritual Burst”!

At the same time, the enemy jungler joined the fray, clearly targeting Dark Raven with his ultimate, “Shattered Blade Whirlwind”!

This overwhelming offensive was even more intense than the one that crushed Dou Jiayan’s Phoenix Soul—they had no intention of holding back to deal with the rest of Fight.

However, Ling Suyu didn’t even blink as he activated Dark Raven’s ultimate ability, “Soaring Black Feathers”!

A young girl dressed in elegant robes, with long black hair and black wings peeking out from beneath her delicate sleeves, suddenly spread her wings and flapped them forcefully. Her entire body was flung backward as she fired a barrage of feathers!

Soaring Black Feathers— Dark Raven soared into the air and fired a barrage of feathers forwards, dealing damage to enemies within a fan-shaped area and inflicting 2 seconds of poison damage. After 1 second, it landed in the direction the mouse was dragged, and became immune to all damage until it landed.

Immune to all damage.

XY: Okay, nevermind.

When Ling Suyu’s Dark Raven activated its ultimate, a low-health Deepsea Faerie and a dying Spider Queen were within its range, and were instantly taken down by the Dark Raven’s ultimate.

【System】 Dark Raven, one kill!

【System】 The enemy Deepsea Faerie has been slain!

【System】 Dark Raven, double kill! Peerless!

【System】 The enemy Spider Queen has been slain!

After Ling Suyu’s Dark Raven used his ultimate, he immediately activated his third skill, “Feather Retraction.” Black feathers scattered across the ground suddenly soared into the air, piercing through Cat Spirit and Tempest Blade, both of whom had been whittled down to low health by the ultimate, securing two more kills.

【System】 Dark Raven, triple kill! Unstoppable!

【System】 The enemy Cat Spirit has been slain!

【System】 Dark Raven, quadra kill! Godlike!

【System】 The enemy Tempest Blade has been slain!

Finally, Fight stopped their movements, using only crowd control to trap the enemy top laner, Imperial Knight, welcoming the arrival of their bloodthirsty carry.

【System】 Dark Raven, penta kill! Even the gods bow down!

【System】 The enemy Imperial Knight has been slain!

The beautiful half-human, half-bird maiden spun gracefully, gazing toward the Dragon Pit.

Ling Suyu: “Let’s take the dragon.”

【System】 The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us! 

【System】 No one can stop Dark Raven now!

The pentakill happened in the blink of an eye, the entire sequence taking no more than two seconds.

Some viewers had barely finished blinking before XY went from having a full team to being completely wiped out.

After a brief moment of stunned silence, no one knew who started it, but someone suddenly stood up, followed by more and more people, and in an instant, the entire arena erupted!

“Aaaah!!”

“Feather is so cool!!”

“Awesome!!”

The chaotic cheers eventually coalesced into a single chant: “Fight! Fight! Fight!”

“Feather is just too cool!!” Not to mention the audience—even commentator Remi was captivated by Ling Suyu’s stylish play, her face lighting up with awe. “I suddenly remember why I used to love Dark Raven back in the day. Facing a seemingly hopeless situation, she could turn the tide with a single, effortless move, conquering the battlefield in an instant!”

“That’s right! Feather’s Dark Raven is so cool!” Liang Fan also… 【Holy crap! I take back what I said earlier! Feather is still Fight’s undisputed star player! Even though Yu He’s performance in this game was equally stunning, it still couldn’t outshine Feather’s brilliance! Today I declare: sustained-damage marksmen are the coolest!!】

Dou Jiayan, who wasn’t mentioned at all: 

Fortunately, after Liang Fan calmed down, he did mention him during the commentary replay: “Yanzi’s poke in this exchange was incredibly powerful! You could say it basically sealed the outcome of this team fight between Fight and XY! It’s a shame he didn’t use his cleanse—if he had, he would’ve had a chance to escape with his ultimate! But if he had used it, he might not have dealt as much damage. Fight’s top laner and support reacted way too fast! They immediately followed up and locked down all of XY! XY’s strategy of focusing their attacks on Feather after breaking free from CC was wise—it was their only chance for survival! As long as they could take down Feather, they could turn the tide against the odds with Cat Spirit’s damage! Too bad they didn’t expect Feather to time his ultimate so perfectly!”

Conclusion: “Feather is awesome!”

In a sense, Dark Raven’s ultimate, “Soaring Black Feathers,” was identical to Blade Soul’s third skill, “Absolute Defense”—both granted immunity to all damage for 1 second.

Although “Soaring Black Feathers” not only dealt ultimate damage but also provided follow-up damage and even included a dash, “Absolute Defense” was a purely defensive ability.

However, both skills heavily depend on the timing of their usage.

Use them at the right moment, and you’d be immune to all damage, able to turn the tide of battle and pull off a comeback in a desperate situation!

Use them at the wrong moment, and you’d waste a skill, becoming a useless player with one fewer skill than other heroes, and get spectacularly crushed.

Many people tended to think Dark Raven had poor survival skills simply because they didn’t know how to use her ultimate.

In Ling Suyu’s opinion, although Dark Raven lacked a dash, as long as her ultimate was timed correctly, her survival capabilities were no weaker than those of Cat Spirit or Desert Hunter.

When Yu He’s Blade Soul cast his ultimate at Cat Spirit, Cat Spirit had just used its dash to go kill Ling Suyu, so the ultimate hit XY’s top laner Imperial Knight instead—and he even dodged it with a Flash.

He landed reluctantly, only to see the entirety of XY launch a ferocious attack on Ling Suyu’s Dark Raven—an assault powerful enough to kill him three times over. Terrified, he instinctively tried to use Flash to rush over and block the attack for him, but it was already too late.

Yet, under his anxious gaze, the seemingly fragile girl not only failed to disappear amid the skill effects, which could only be described as “visual pollution,” but instead spread her wings and soared gracefully into the air, her movements crisp and light.

Countless feathers fell as she took flight, blanketing the ground—just as light, yet exuding a sense of danger.

Sure enough, she took down two opponents just by taking flight. After landing, she raised her hand, and the feathers whizzed toward her targets, claiming two more lives.

At that moment, Yu He couldn’t help but let out a laugh—a very faint chuckle.

What was he worried about?

That was Ling Suyu, after all.

Ling Suyu, with his eternal “0 deaths.”

He had known from the very beginning that “0 deaths” wasn’t some divine blessing, nor was it a demonic curse; it was simply a symbol of Ling Suyu’s strength.

Ling Suyu possessed enough strength to escape danger alive time and time again, a fact reflected in his “0 deaths” record.

At the end of the day, he was just that strong.

It was such a simple truth, yet for some reason, so many people just couldn’t wrap their heads around it.

There were more fools in this world than Yu He had ever imagined.

But it didn’t matter. With Ling Suyu on the team, they were ready to fight. This squad, which would never abandon its marksman and would even sacrifice its own flesh and blood to bring glory to him, would showcase his true strength to its fullest, silencing all who had ever doubted him so they could never utter another word of slander against him.

“Let’s take the dragon,” Ling Suyu said after securing the kill, his tone extremely matter-of-fact, neither humble nor arrogant, as if he hadn’t just been teetering on the brink of death or pulled off a spectacular pentakill, but had merely done what any professional player should do.

Yu He stifled a laugh and, mimicking his tone, gave a very flat “Mm-hmm.”

Tang Yu’an, keeping pace with them, spoke casually: “Yumao, you killed them way too fast. Look, the system can’t even keep up.”

At that moment, the system was still announcing, “Dark Raven, pentakill.”

It was entirely because Ling Suyu had secured the kills so quickly, while the system’s announcements could only be processed one at a time.

Ling Suyu tilted his head slightly: “I just didn’t want to die. I don’t know why they all stood right in the range of my skills.”

“Hahahaha!” Dou Jiayan, who was still lying on the ground waiting to respawn, laughed uncontrollably. “Yumao, you’ve changed! You weren’t this cocky before!”

Ling Suyu opened his mouth slightly to object, wanting to say he wasn’t being cocky—he was just telling the truth.

With so many skills hurled at him all at once, he couldn’t just stand there and wait to die, could he? He obviously had to use his ultimate to escape. He never expected that a single ultimate would take out two of them.

And with feathers scattered all over the ground, he couldn’t just leave them there—what if he went bald? (bushi);

Anyway, he had to pick them up—he never expected that doing so would kill two more.

But before he could speak, Tang Yu’an spoke up first: “So what if our team’s carry is a little cocky? Especially since he’s playing Dark Raven. Sure, Dark Raven is a half-demon, but she’s a member of the royal family—she’s a princess, after all.”

Ling Suyu blinked.

He hadn’t really paid any attention to Dark Raven’s background. After all, reading through all that text gave him a headache…

But Dark Raven’s outfit was indeed quite luxurious: a black base with red patterns, gold and silver threads—it was clearly worth a fortune.

Wait, hold on a second?

Dark Raven being a princess—what did that have to do with him?!

Thinking of Ma Mingxi, nicknamed the “YJ’s Little Princess,” Ling Suyu’s face darkened: “I’m not being cocky at all.”

“Mm-hmm, whatever you say~”

Tang Yu’an’s tone was so indulgent, and so prefunctory.

Ling Suyu… Never mind, focus on the Dragon.

After Dark Raven enhanced herself with “Raven Feathers” and “Poison Feathers,” she unleashed a barrage of attacks on the Dragon God: whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!

Black feathers rained down one after another, and the Dragon God’s health plummeted at an astonishing rate.

The Dragon God had just spawned, and the heroes on both teams weren’t very high-level yet. Logically, taking down the Dragon God at this stage should take a long time—long enough for XY to respawn before it was killed, and even have a chance to steal the kill.

It was at this moment that the true strength of sustained-damage marksmen, especially those who don’t rely on ultimate abilities for damage, shone through.

While XY was still agonizingly waiting to respawn, a system notification popped up on their screens:

【System】Dark Raven has successfully conquered the Dragon God and summoned the divine dragon legion!

XY: “……”

As a team that grew stronger with every battle, one that seemed to know nothing of surrender or despair, they tasted despair for the first time and began to entertain thoughts of giving up.

No! We can’t! This is a match!

“It’s okay, we still have a chance.”

“Mm.”

“Don’t be so down, where’s our fighting spirit?!”

“Mm.”

“Uh…” And then… that was it.

The economic gap between the two teams kept widening. Fight didn’t even need to counter-jungle; they simply camped out in XY’s jungle and openly dominated them.

Finally, at the 22-minute mark, Fight took down XY’s main tower and secured the victory in game 2!

“The match is over! Fight has two points!” Remi announced the result of the match.

“After the first day of competition, both Fight and igame have two points each! We look forward to seeing how both teams perform in the future, and we can’t wait to see them face off again!” said Liang Fan. “But for now, let’s congratulate Fight!”

As Liang Fan spoke, the on-site host also congratulated Fight, and the audience erupted in cheers, showering Fight with the most enthusiastic applause and screams!

Fight hadn’t disappointed them. In fact, they performed even better than anyone had imagined. They truly lived up to their reputation as one of City B’s teams!

But immediately afterward, they thought of UH, another team from City B…

Never mind—today, they weren’t from City B; they were simply Fight’s fans!

“Go, Fight!!”

“Fight for the championship!!”

“Fight! Fight! Fight!”

When Ling Suyu took off his headphones, he could hear the audience’s screams through the wall—could even make out them chanting “Fight”—and he couldn’t help but pause in surprise.

He stayed dazed until Tang Yu’an beside him chuckled, “The cheers today are louder than ever. Maybe they were blown away by you.”

“By me?” Ling Suyu instinctively asked, thinking to himself that he’d indeed performed well today, though his face betrayed a hint of confusion.

Tang Yu’an looked at the bewilderment on his face and at his soft, smooth hair, and felt a flutter in his heart.

He couldn’t help but raise a hand to ruffle his hair, saying with conviction, “You deserve those cheers. You’re far stronger than you realize.”

Ling Suyu instinctively murmured, “Mm,” but then, as if remembering something, he turned to look at Tang Yu’an and, smiling, corrected himself: “No, it’s the result of Fight working hard together.”

His Dark Raven had indeed had a field day today and ultimately claimed the MVP spot, leaving no room for Yu He or Dou Jiayan.

But he knew full well that his ability to perform so brilliantly was entirely due to his teammates’ tireless protection.

Mo Xiaoyun’s ‘most damage taken’ and Tang Yu’an’s ‘most assists’ were proof of that.

Although Dou Jiayan’s damage output wasn’t as high as Mo Xiaoyun’s, it was still higher than that of the opposing team’s top damage dealer.

Yu He’s Blade Soul was well-balanced in terms of both damage dealt and damage taken; though seemingly unremarkable, it struck fear into the hearts of the enemy.

Fight was a complete team—all five positions were indispensable.

In terms of damage output, he was the carry, the core of Fight’s offense, and the key to Fight winning team fights and securing victory.

But in his eyes, every member of Fight was a carry. Everyone performed their best in their respective roles.

“I really love…” Ling Suyu murmured softly.

Tang Yu’an’s eyes widened slightly.

“Fight,” Ling Suyu added the object.

“Uh…” After a moment of silence, Tang Yu’an chuckled softly. “I know you like me. Don’t keep saying it out loud—it’s kind of embarrassing.”

“Ah… Never mind,” Ling Suyu kitty-sighed.

His captain’s narcissism was probably incurable at this point.

However.

He really did like it—

The first day of competitions ended just like that. Like the group stages, the quarterfinals also featured a post-match interview session.

But unlike the group stages, the quarterfinal interviews weren’t conducted backstage—they took place on stage.

And everyone would participate.

Amid enthusiastic applause from the audience, the players from both Fight and XY returned to the very spot where they had made their pre-match statements.

The host first asked XY, “You lost 0-2 to Fight. How do you feel right now?”

XY’s captain took the microphone but remained silent for a long time. After a while, he finally managed to say, “Fight is really strong.”

Although they had felt from the start that they couldn’t beat this season’s Fight, actually losing to them 0-2 still left XY feeling somewhat resentful.

However, acknowledging Fight’s strength offered some consolation—since their opponent was strong enough, losing wasn’t exactly a disgrace.

Of course, they couldn’t possibly voice this sentiment openly for others to hear.

All they could bring themselves to say were those few words: “Fight is really strong.”

Seeing that he had no intention of continuing, the host took the initiative to steer the conversation: “Do you have any regrets about this match?”

Ling Suyu stared at the microphone, his gaze as intense as if he were staring down an enemy marksman.

Why did they always make him speak before and after the match? He didn’t want to say anything!

But in the end, he sighed quietly to himself and took the microphone.

Though what he really wanted to say was—please don’t use Cat Spirit anymore, it hurts to watch.
