Tang Yu’an, seeing the emoticon Feather sent, raised an eyebrow and suddenly felt like teasing him.

So, he didn’t rush to “recommend” his team, but rather responded with:

tangyuan with sesame filling: excited? Were you just waiting for me to bring it up?

soft and chewy osmanthus cake: QAQ tangyuan with sesame filling: alright alright I won’t tease you anymore, you should come to Fight.

Before actually interacting with Feather, Tang Yu’an never expected the other to be this soft, to the point where he couldn’t bear to tease him.

This was despite him having such a strong personal style, being able to reply to others’

provocations without showing a hint of weakness, and appearing strong and independent.

He didn’t expect that not only was Feather easy to talk to in private, but even a bit cute?

Fight?

Seeing this word, Ling Suyu took a few seconds to realize that it was the name of a team.

He remembered this team.

This team, which had been created not long ago, dominantly entered the top 4 during this year’s Summer Seasonal Championship. Due to their marksman not being in the best condition, they ultimately lost consecutively to Winne, YJ, and UH, and got fourth place overall.

He remembered the team’s support, who was simultaneously their team captain. His name was…

soft and chewy osmanthus cake: Tang Yu’an?

tangyuan with sesame filling: surprised?

This person really was Tang Yu’an?!

He was actually from Fight?!

In a flash, Ling Suyu felt like he was dreaming.

Many professional players, after ending their original contracts, would enter into teams that were stronger and had a better chance of winning the championship.

But Ling Suyu’s situation was different. Although he’d refused to renew the contract, his last match in UH was terrible. As a result, he’d held no hopes of being able to enter into a top 8 team after dissolving his contract.

He’d originally prepared himself—as long as “tangyuan” was willing to take him in, he’d enter the team even if it was only in the top 16.

He could have never imagined that “tangyuan” was actually Tang Yu’an!

And he was from Fight, a top 4 team of the current BPL season!

Ling Suyu heavily suspected that the other had to be tricking him, but the other’s gaming skills couldn't be doubted, so it didn’t seem wrong to conclude that this was actually Tang Yu’an?

No, there was still something wrong.

soft and chewy osmanthus cake: why are you solo-queueing?

tangyuan with sesame filling: oh, so you can solo queue, but I can’t?

soft and chewy osmanthus cake: it’s not the same, I’m ending my contract…

tangyuan with sesame filling: it’s indeed different, Fight’s on break. Much less a five-stack, it’s hard to find even a duo.

soft and chewy osmanthus cake: you guys are on break so early?

tangyuan with sesame filling: Yeah? Jealous? If you sign with Fight now, you can enjoy vacation in advance, are you tempted?

Ling Suyu was indeed tempted.

But it wasn’t because of the vacation time.

It’s because it was Fight.

A team from the top 4 was extending an olive branch to him. Ling Suyu couldn’t think of any reason to refuse.

In actuality, he’d long made his decision. However, there was an issue he needed to clarify before officially signing.

soft and chewy osmanthus cake: after I sign with Fight, will I get the chance to compete in official competitions?

Seeing this message, Tang Yu’an was slightly startled.

What exactly did this guy experience in UH that would lead him to ask this kind of question?

In order to reassure him, Tang Yu’an responded with three consecutive messages.

tangyuan with sesame filling: of course.

tangyuan with sesame filling: why would we sign you in if we weren’t going to let you compete?

tangyuan with sesame filling: if you can make it in time to practice, you could even compete in January’s Spring Seasonal as a starting member.

Ling Suyu stared at these three messages in shock, emotions surging.

Was he dreaming?

It’s said that when God closes a door, he opens a window.

Yet this window opened way too quickly.

And the view from the window was even better than the one from the door.

It almost felt like a trap.

But even if it was a trap…

tangyuan with sesame filling: what do you think? Have you decided?

soft and chewy osmanthus cake: I will join.

Ling Suyu sent out these three words without hesitation, his heart beating uncontrollably. He couldn’t believe that right after leaving UH, he’d found a new place.

He hated that he couldn’t immediately sign the contract with Fight, but…

soft and chewy osmanthus cake: but my contract with UH hasn’t fully expired yet, there’s still half a month left, would you be willing to wait for me?

tangyuan with sesame filling: when exactly does it expire?

Ling Suyu didn’t even need to open his suitcase before sending over the general date.

He’d lost count of the number of times he’d read over that contract. Although his decision to terminate his contract with UH seemed like one he made on impulse, it was in actuality the result of careful and repeated deliberation.

To UH, he was akin to an anchor chained to a giant vessel, only used when needed. It was neither what he wanted for himself nor what he had wished for when he first joined.

Tang Yu’an, after seeing the date Feather sent over, turned to look at the date on the calendar.

The end of Feather’s contract landed less than a week away from the start of the Spring Seasonal. It was an incredibly awkward time. Did UH do this on purpose?

tangyuan with sesame filling: what’s your current situation, are you still physically at UH?

soft and chewy osmanthus cake: I’ve left with my stuff packed already, I’m currently at an internet cafe looking for a place to stay.

tangyuan with sesame filling: ?

He was actually in an internet cafe looking for a place to stay?

When Tang Yu’an thought he’d “picked up a person”, he didn’t think it would be in the literal sense.

Without another word, he gave the other his phone number.

tangyuan with sesame filling: my number. Save it, and send me yours. Give me your current location, I’ll come pick you up. I’ll call you when I get there.

Fight’s club headquarters was located in B City, adjacent to the area where UH was located, and was only around a half-hour drive away.

Tang Yu’an increasingly felt that him being matched with Feather during a solo queue was a sign from the heavens.

The only thing he wasn’t sure about was if the heavens were guiding him or Feather.

On the other hand, Ling Suyu was stunned by Tang Yu’an actions and took a while to react.

What?

Fight’s team captain Tang Yu’an was personally coming to pick him up?

To where? Fight’s headquarters?

Ling Suyu stared at his computer screen, his brain crashing.

This continued until the other side sent another message.

tangyuan with sesame filling: you still there?

Ling Suyu finally came to his senses, thought for another moment, before carefully replying:

actually, I know where Fight is, I can make it there myself.

tangyuan with sesame filling: spare me, I’m currently stuck alone in the club, I need an excuse to head out for some fresh air.

tangyuan with sesame filling: phone number.

Ling Suyu obediently gave him his phone number along with his current location.

Tang Yu’an saved the number under the contact name “Feather” before getting up, grabbing his key, and heading out the door.

He was swift and decisive. If he said he was going to do something, he’d do it.

As for why he was personally going to pick Feather up?

As Fight’s captain, was there anything wrong with him picking up a team member?

As a support, was there anything wrong with going to pick up his marksman?

In addition, recalling the young man’s small figure and innocent eyes, Tang Yu’an wasn’t quite at ease leaving the other to find his own way to Fight’s headquarters.

Afterwards, Ling Suyu sent a few more messages into the chat, but received no reply from Tang Yu’an.

He guessed that Tang Yu’an had already set out. He was both excited and nervous, and not quite sure what he should do right now.

He searched up Tang Yu’an’s picture online, as well as some information about Fight, in order to ensure he wouldn’t end up not recognizing the other or saying something weird.

From an outsider’s perspective, at that moment, he was merely quietly sitting in front of his computer and researching some information. His face was expressionless, giving a calm, almost cold impression.

Only he himself knew how flustered he currently was. It was a subtle feeling, almost like he was about to meet a boyfriend he’d been dating for years online…

Meanwhile, as he was doing his research and pretending to be calm, waiting for Tang Yu’an to pick him up, on 《Glorious Heroes》’s official forum, a post titled 【Feather and UH have terminated their contract】suddenly exploded in popularity!

【What?! How’s that possible?!】

【Why? Is UH sick?】

【Where did OP get this info? Neither UH’s official account nor Feather’s personal account have posted anything, please note that spreading misinformation is illegal!】

OP:【I just matched with Feather in a solo queue, he confirmed it himself.】

OP:【He even said that he was leaving UH not because UH didn’t want him, but simply because his contract was expiring and he decided not to renew it.】

【He can leave if he wants, it’s not like UH can win with him there.】

【Upstairs[^1]?】

【Where’s upstairs coming from? If you’re one of UH’s brainless fans, I’d recommend you go check out the experts’ analyses on the White Fox Cup and snap out of it. The Weibo comments are done for. Maybe if UH used their money on actually hiring good players instead of water armies, they wouldn’t have done so terribly during the White Fox Cup!】

【Oh, so you guys are the smart ones? Let’s talk with facts, okay? Has Feather ever won a match? With these same starting members, why is it that UH can win when Jiang Hao is the one competing, huh?】

【It’s over, one of UH’s stans got in somehow, this thread is done for!】

And below that were around ten more comments tearing into each other.

Finally, the UH fan:【f*ck it I’m leaving, I can’t talk with you idiots!】

OP: 【......】

【I still don’t believe it’s true, the Spring Seasonal’s about to start, can Feather still find a team if he ends his contract now?】

OP: 【I’ll just post the picture.】

The original poster posted a battle record from a ranked match. In the screenshot, he was displayed as the blue team’s jungler, with the ID “100 years spent jungling”.

The red team’s marksman had the glaring ID UH-feather. Similarly shocking was the MVP title hanging over his hero’s icon, with 0 deaths in the battle record.

【But this only proves the OP really matched with Feather during a solo queue, not that Feather and UH have terminated their contract?】

【But you don’t really see pros solo-queueing all that often. Even if Feather didn’t end his contract, he’s definitely being singled out by UH.】

【Isn’t it a regular occurrence for Feather to be singled out by UH? Have you seen how badly he was screwed over by his teammates during the White Fox Cup?】

【UH is way too disgusting! They dragged Feather down for a whole five years and only let him out right before the Spring Seasonal. It’s obvious they don’t want him to find a new team!】

【UH has always been like this! I’ve been reviewing Feather’s video records from the past few years, and it’s so obvious that his teammates are the ones not pulling their weight. Yet as soon as Jiang Hao pops up, UH immediately puts all the blame of losing on Feather. Did they sign him just to pin all the blame on him?】

【If Feather’s really leaving of his own volition then that’s good, hope that he can find a better team that won’t screw him over before the start of the Spring Seasonals.】

【Hope Feather’s leaving voluntarily +1, for a team like UH, the sooner you leave the better!】

The discussion in the forums continued.

No one realized that, on Weibo, UH’s official account suddenly posted.

@UH_eSports_Club:

Due to less-than-optimal performance during the “White Fox Cup”, after much communication with internal members, we have made the following adjustments:
