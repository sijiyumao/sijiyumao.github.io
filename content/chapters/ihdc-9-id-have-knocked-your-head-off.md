“beefing with whoever steals marksman” was a fan of UH’s.

To be precise, he was UH’s captain Jiang Hao’s fan.

UH’s performance during the “White Fox Cup” was way too bad, and as a result he didn’t even finish watching all of the “White Fox Cup” matches.

But for the first match against YJ, he’d watched the entire thing.

From the very beginning of the match, he’d expected that without Jiang Hao, it was highly likely that UH would lose. Mainly, he didn’t trust the abilities of the substitute.

After all, before the “White Fox Cup” match began, a lot of people like himself had just seen his ID for the first time.

As expected, UH lost. They lost two matches in a row, dropping to the bottom ranks from the very beginning.

But that substitute, Feather, was not only not the one dragging them down, but actually the one who got MVP both rounds.

Those who didn’t understand 《Glorious Heroes》, who only saw the battle records, would definitely think that the one who played the best in UH across those two rounds was Feather.

Those who understood 《Glorious Heroes》, after analyzing the overall performances, would also conclude that the one who played the best in UH across those two rounds was Feather.

“beefing with whoever steals marksman” also thought so at the beginning.

After UH and YJ’s match concluded, the first thing he did was go under UH’s official Weibo account and start flaming.

“What the hell were those plays!”

“A bunch of starters being carried by a substitute!”

“I don’t spend all my time slacking off at work just to see you guys throw!”

Of course, the official account didn’t respond.

For the same duration that the “White Fox Cup” had been ongoing, the official account had been playing dead.

On the other hand, some self-proclaimed UH fans had rushed over to teach him about UH’s performance in the past years’ BPL.

Before Jiang Hao joined the team, they’d only ever entered the top 16.

After Jiang Hao joined, with the same players, only a different marksman, they not only consecutively entered the top 8, but were also the runner-ups for the Spring Seasonals last year and the year before.

Didn’t this prove that Jiang Hao’s strength completely surpassed Feather’s?

So, during the “White Fox Cup”, the reason UH did so bad was because of Feather?

Because he ran during a team fight, his teammates died.

Because he prioritized surviving, instead of taking out low-health enemies.

Because he played dead in the fountain, rather than clearing minions and protecting their towers.

There were so many voices repeating the same thing, and thus “beefing with whoever steals marksman” started to become convinced.

Although the doubts in his heart did not completely disappear, Feather’s 0-death records were way too blinding. They were like a painted target, inviting countless bullets.

In the end, 《Glorious Heroes》was a tower-defense game. No matter how many kills one had, as long as their base was destroyed, the game would be lost.

To see their base being surrounded and in danger and, instead of clearing minions, stay unmoving in the fountain.

Where was his hot-bloodedness as an e-sports player? His will to persevere and not admit defeat until the last moment?

As the GIF of Feather standing in the fountain, watching as his base was destroyed, started spreading around the comment section of UH’s official Weibo account, “beefing with whoever steals marksman” became more and more angry.

He didn’t believe that Feather had no skills, and even flamed those extremist comments that said Feather should get out of UH. However, after being led along by the rhythm of the comment section, he started to believe from the bottom of his heart that the reason for UH’s loss could not be unrelated to Feather.

And so, when he matched with Feather in a solo queue, he didn’t even think twice before locking Guardian Spirit—he wanted to let Feather witness the real way to use Guardian Spirit!

In addition to giving this paranoid marksman a taste of death!

【Enemy】beefing with whoever steals marksman (Guardian Spirit): I just don’t believe you’ll be able to maintain that 0-death streak! Feather, just you wait, I’ll definitely kill you once this match!

After typing out this message with heightened emotions, he quickly received Feather’s reply.

【Ally】UH-feather: ok, i’ll be cheering for you.

Neither servile nor arrogant, gentle yet not weak. He’d behaved the same way in the face of YJ’s marksman Ma Mingxi’s provocations.

After Tang Yu’an saw the reply Ling Suyu sent, he couldn’t help giving a light laugh: “Not bad. Not too harsh yet extremely insulting, you’ve got the essence of trash talk down to a T.”

Even as a kid, Ling Suyu very rarely received compliments from others, so when he suddenly heard these words, he awkwardly coughed, although the words that came out of his lips were still unyielding: “Although my contract with UH is ending, I’m still a professional player…and, aren’t you here to protect me?”

Tang Yu’an let out a burst of laughter: “Good, the understanding between a support and a marksman should start from mutual trust. Since you’re willing to trust me, then I definitely can’t let you down~”

Ling Suyu smiled faintly, not responding.

Their teammates heard this and suddenly felt that something was wrong.

【Ally】if i have feet i can run (Vampiric Progenitor): damn, I’ve got a new understanding of the relationship between marksman and support.

【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler (Blade Soul): ?

【Ally】can die but no need (Skeletal Empress): what do I, a top-lane orphan, have to do with this?

【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler (Blade Soul): ???

What?

Ling Suyu was also confused by the messages in the chat box.

But he didn’t have the luxury to think too much about it, as the enemy heroes had all revived.

The nearby undergrowth was no longer safe, and there could be an ambush at any moment.

Yet before he could even remind the support to check the undergrowth, Tang Yu’an had already thrown a “Spidersilk Trap” into the undergrowth beside the tower, before directly entering with his Spider Queen.

No one.

“Junlger watch out, go wait in the top jungle. Mid-lane, don’t press too deep, retreat under the tower. Top-lane, it’s alright if you press deeper.”

Tang Yu’an spoke while using his first skill “Web of Constraint” to help Ling Suyu deplete the minions’ health, seemingly unaffected by the division of his attention.

Ling Suyu assuredly cleared the minions. Out of curiosity, he glanced towards the minimap, only to see his teammates obediently carrying out Tang Yu’an’s orders.

Their jungler “Blade Soul”, after cleaning off a small monster in the top jungle, squatted in the undergrowth near the first tower of the top lane.

Once their top-laner saw that the jungler had come to gank, they instantly gained confidence. Even if they made a mistake in their movements and accidentally got hit by two skills from the enemy, they still overbearingly chased the other side under their tower.

“Jungler, don’t rush, wait until the enemy top-laner makes their move before going in,” Tang Yu’an said again, “Top-lane, prepare your Flash, don’t get hit by the enemy ultimate.”

Blade Soul shifted in the undergrowth but didn’t head out, though he impatiently spun around a few times in the grass.

The top-laner sent a “My skills are ready” ping.

At this moment, with a “whoosh—”, the enemy jungler Tempest Blade flew out from the undergrowth near the enemy tower, landing directly on top of Skeletal Empress. With a movement and damage skill, he directly unleashed his ultimate “Shattered-Blade Whirlwind”!

Skeletal Empress grasped the timing and flashed to escape, causing Tempest Blade’s ultimate to land on empty ground.

The enemy top-laner Black-Armoured Scythe, seeing his side’s jungler making a move, immediately stepped out of the tower. Dragging his scythe, he rushed towards Skeletal Empress. Yet, at that moment, Skeletal Empress also turned around to face the two, throwing out the control skill “Heavy Bone Hammer”, stunning both in one blow.

“I’ve never won with jungler”’s Blade Soul had long been eager for action. The moment Tempest Blade flew out, he used a movement skill to run to Skeletal Empress’s side.

Following that, he used a series of blade shadows, layering two stacks of damage onto Tempest Blade, instantly draining his health to less than a third!

Tempest Blade, realizing the situation had turned sour, flashed and retreated under the tower. However, Blade Soul didn’t give him the chance to recall; with a flash, he caught up and unleashed his ultimate “Nine-Stage Blade Flash”, simultaneously gaining damage immunity and unleashing a 9-hit combo. Not only did he dodge the tower’s damage, but he also got the kill.

【System】Blade Soul, one kill!

【System】The enemy Tempest Blade has been slain!

Blade Soul’s movement skill refreshed, and he immediately flashed out of the tower’s range, chasing after Black-Armoured Scythe and unleashing a series of basic attacks!

Although Black-Armoured Scythe was tanky, he’d already lost a lot of health laning against Skeletal Empress. In addition, his economy was already an entire level behind Skeletal Empress, and he was missing a piece of gear. In the end, he couldn’t resist the combined attacks of the two, meeting the same fate as the jungler, his soul returning to the fountain.

【System】Blade Soul, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Black-Armoured Scythe has been slain!

“Nice!” Tang Yu’an, on the faraway bottom lane, laughed, “How’s that? Nothing wrong with what I said, right? With me commanding, there’s no need to worry about not getting kills.”

As expected, not too long after, the enemy mid-laner poked their head out on the top lane.

After checking all of the nearby undergrowth yet being unable to find a single enemy to kill, they could only “reluctantly” clear out the nearby minions before slowly returning to the mid￾lane.

Enemy mid-laner: ?

I don’t know whether or not to curse.

Their top-laner “can die but no need”, after successfully recalling, finally reacted.

【Ally】can die but no need (Skeletal Empress): Bro, you’re pretty good.

“You’re too kind.” Tang Yu’an smiled.

Internally: If you were a member of Fight and chose to recall there, I’d have knocked your head off!

【Ally】if i have feet i can run (Vampiric Progenitor): Ah, my bad, forgot to signal orz “No worries, I’ve got my eye on the situation~” Tang Yu’an smiled.

Internally: If you were a member of Fight and didn’t say anything when the enemy hero disappeared, you can kiss goodbye to your dinner!

However, Tang Yu’an’s tone remained relaxed throughout, giving a very reliable and reassuring impression.

Their teammates seemingly didn’t perceive anything wrong with his tone, though Ling Suyu raised an eyebrow.

Hmm? It seemed like a soft and milky but black-hearted tangyuan was “happily” sharpening his knives?

But before he could think too much about it, Tang Yu’an once again opened his mouth: “The purple dragon is Feather’s, no one’s allowed to touch it. Jungler, get the two little monsters in the bottom jungle. Mid-laner, once you finish clearing the way for the minion line, directly come down to take the Divine Sunbird.”

Ling Suyu blinked, and before he could react, Tang Yu’an had already entered the bottom jungle, and he instinctively followed.

Although it was pretty normal for the damage dealer to get the lightning strike, in 《Glorious Heroes》, heroes who brought the talent Smite would get a huge debuff on the economy they could get from clearing minions. This was to prevent junglers from stealing experience and economy from the laning heroes in the early game.

As a result, it was very rare for junglers in a solo queue to give monsters to others. Not to mention snatching, Ling Suyu hadn’t even planned to ask.

Yet, Tang Yu’an directly declared ownership over the purple dragon.

In addition…

“The purple dragon is Feather’s, no one’s allowed to touch it.”

Ling Suyu couldn’t stop the corners of his lips from lifting.

Tang Yu’an: “Come on, Feather, once we get the lightning strike we’ll go get started on the bird.”

Ling Suyu: “Okay~”
