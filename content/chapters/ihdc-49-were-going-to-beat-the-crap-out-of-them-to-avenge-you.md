UH’s starters had their phones confiscated, so they were currently unaware of what was happening online, but coach and manager were in the know.

As for Ling Suyu’s skill level, UH’s coach was actually well aware of it. Ling Suyu was indeed strong. If UH could play like Fight did, if they could adopt a “four-protect-one” strategy every game, fight tooth and nail to shield Ling Suyu, and let him farm up, then UH’s win rate would indeed be guaranteed.

The problem was, for UH, this would be very difficult to pull off, and there was no real need to do so.

It was difficult because, aside from Jiang Hao, no one else on UH was cut out to be a call-maker. Even Ling Suyu, who had exceptional game sense, focused so intently on laning and team fights that even if he sensed what was about to happen, he would keep it to himself rather than communicating it to his teammates. No matter how many times he was reminded, he just couldn't bring himself to do it.

And the reason it was unnecessary was that UH had Jiang Hao. Under his leadership, UH had strong team cohesion. Only when this once-disorganized group came together did they have the strength and qualifications to compete for the championship trophy.

Given that, Ling Suyu’s presence became redundant.

Therefore, UH’s coach’s true intention was this: although Ling Suyu had no chance of playing for UH, he had to be kept on the team as long as possible to prevent other teams from acquiring him, thereby ensuring he did not become a stumbling block on UH’s path to the championship.

This was why, even though Ling Suyu was merely a substitute and his presence at UH was essentially dispensable, UH’s coach still hinted at the team’s desire to re-sign him.

Consequently, before Ling Suyu terminated his contract with UH, UH’s manager had nagged him at length, only to be met with the response: “UH isn’t the right fit for me.”

At the time, UH’s manager simply assumed Ling Suyu was ungrateful, taking UH’s goodwill for granted.

But now, after watching Fight’s matches over the past few days and seeing the online buzz, UH’s manager suddenly felt confused. He asked UH’s coach in private, “Feather…is he really that strong? Has he always been this strong, or has he been holding back all along?

“Did UH ask me to convince Feather to renew his contract because of his skill? Wasn’t it…”

Wasn’t it what, exactly?

Would UH really keep a useless player just for old time’s sake?

Especially since this player didn’t get along well with the others. Hardly anyone paid attention to what Feather was doing. He was practically invisible at UH, just like an actual “ghost.”

UH’s coach took a deep breath and finally decided to be honest, but ended up saying the same thing Ling Suyu had: “He’s strong, but UH isn’t the right fit for him.”

“What do you mean?”

“I’m trying to figure out how to explain this so you’ll understand…” The coach paused to think for a moment before continuing, “Let me put it this way: with our current starting lineup, without Jiang Hao on the field, it’s very difficult to create an environment where Feather can perform at his best.”

“You don’t need to explain that to me, I won’t get it. I just want to know one thing: since Feather’s Desert Hunter and Candy Witch are so strong, why didn’t you let him use those two heroes at the White Fox Cup?”

UH’s manager didn’t play Glorious and wasn't interested in the game; he’d taken the job solely because of UH’s offered salary.

UH, however, thought his resume looked impressive, so they hired him even though he didn’t play Glorious. After all, the manager’s main duties had little to do with the game itself.

As long as he could secure sponsorships and handle conflicts among the team’s players, that was good enough.

In this regard, UH’s manager did a decent job. Before things had come to a head with Ling Suyu, their relationship was actually quite good. He had consistently mediated conflicts between Ling Suyu and UH’s starting lineup.

He knew Ling Suyu was shy, introverted, not very articulate, and not good at dealing with people, so before Ling Suyu left UH, he took fairly good care of him.

Precisely because he’d gone out of his way to look after this essentially useless substitute, when Ling Suyu explicitly stated he wanted to leave UH, the manager felt betrayed— incredulous, angry, and furious—to the point that his tone was very harsh when he called to confront him.

It wasn’t until Ling Suyu participated in this year’s Spring Seasonals as a starter for Fight and dominated several consecutive matches, stealing the spotlight, that things changed.

People said that Fight’s impressive victory was all thanks to Feather.

They said Feather was Fight’s undisputed carry.

Reading these comments, UH’s manager was momentarily overcome by the thought that he had never known Ling Suyu at all.

Now, he was desperate to know the answer.

Did Ling Suyu betray UH, or…

Faced with his question, UH’s coach let out another long sigh before saying, “It didn’t matter what he used. If he couldn’t win a match with Guardian Spirit, he certainly wouldn't win one with Desert Hunter either. Think of it as the relationship between a key and a lock. Feather, as the key, just doesn’t fit UH’s lock. That’s all.”

UH’s manager couldn’t accept this answer. He opened his mouth but ultimately chose to remain silent.

He turned and left, returning to his room, where he stared at the phone number in his contacts—one he could no longer reach—and its label, “Feather,” falling into a long silence.

Meanwhile, UH’s coach stared blankly at the match footage on his computer screen.

In actuality, he hadn’t told the whole truth.

The truth was, if Ling Suyu were allowed to play the Cat Spirit, the hero he truly excelled at, and given the chance to grind his way through to the late game, he’d actually have a shot at leading his teammates to a comeback even against the odds. But by then, everyone at UH, including Ling Suyu himself, would come to realize his true potential, and it would become incredibly difficult to either keep Jiang Hao in the starting lineup or find excuses to keep Ling Suyu tied down at UH.

But what UH’s coach didn’t expect was that even though Ling Suyu hadn’t achieved much at the White Fox Cup, he still chose to leave UH. At the same time, there was actually a team that took a shine to him despite him being lambasted all over the internet and didn’t hesitate to sign him at such an awkward time.

Long before Fight officially announced the signing of Ling Suyu and named him a starter, UH’s coach had already foreseen this backlash from the public.

In response, all he could do was remain silent.

It must be said that UH brought this day upon themselves—it was entirely their own fault, and they deserved it.

But in esports, what ultimately matters is skill.

As long as they could win matches, claim the championship, and bring glory back to the country while representing it, netizens would only remember UH’s glory days—not the unsavory deeds they’d committed in the past.

Therefore, UH had to win against Fight.

On Fight’s side, after a two-hour break, Chen Jie gathered Fight’s starting lineup to analyze their opponent for tomorrow’s match: igame.

During last year’s Summer Seasonals, Fight and igame had faced off in the quarterfinals.

At the time, Fight and igame had nearly identical point totals; essentially, whoever won would advance to the semifinals, while the loser would be eliminated in the quarterfinals.

In the end, Fight defeated igame 2-1. After points were split between the first two games, the third game lasted a full 45 minutes and ultimately ended in a historic “No One Left Alive”

scenario. While both teams’ champions were waiting to respawn, igame watched helplessly as Fight’s minions pushed down their main tower and with it, their hopes of winning the championship.

It was also in this match that Fight’s then-marksman, Chen Jie, suffered a serious hand injury, leaving Fight unable to recover in subsequent matches, which ultimately resulted in them missing out on the championship.

In the end, Fight finished fourth overall that season, with igame coming in fifth.

It was a case of both sides suffering losses.

So, just like the rivalry between UH and YJ, Fight and igame were also archrivals, harbouring complex feelings towards one another.

“We’ve already secured a spot in the quarterfinals, so we can take it a bit easier in tomorrow’s match,” Chen Jie said. After a brief pause, he added, “But we’re up against igame. You all know what that means, right?”

Tang Yu’an: “We’re going to beat the crap out of them and avenge you!”

Dou Jiayan: “We’re going to beat the crap out of them and avenge you!”

Yu He: “Just wipe them out.”

Mo Xiaoyun: “Seriously, the one thing I don’t want is to lose to them.”

Ling Suyu: 0.0; What’s going on?

Noticing Ling Suyu’s confusion, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but chuckle. He patiently explained the “feud” between Fight and igame to him. In short, igame was responsible for Chen Jie’s hand injury, which still hadn't fully healed.

Ling Suyu nodded in understanding and looked seriously at Chen Jie: “We’ll avenge you.”

Chen Jie grabbed the piece of bread next to him and tossed it at Tang Yu’an: “What kind of nonsense is this! This has absolutely nothing to do with my hand injury! The point is, we almost lost to them last season! And we actually did lose one game to them! So…” He took a breath, then adopted a slightly calmer tone. “Tomorrow, let’s win. In the quarterfinals, let’s keep winning. Don’t let igame take a single point from us!”

“Alright!” Dou Jiayan was the first to cheer in support, jumping straight up from the couch.

“Let’s win 2-0! Fighting!”

“Hey, hey, hey, don’t get too carried away.” Tang Yu’an tugged at his sleeve to calm him down, then said, “That said, we still have the quarterfinals coming up, and we haven’t played any practice matches against igame. We definitely can’t reveal our true strength right away in the first game tomorrow, so let’s stick with Desert Hunter for the first game and see how far we can push igame with that pick.”

“Let’s not use Candy Witch,” Chen Jie said.

“Great minds think alike—” Tang Yu’an raised his hand and snapped his fingers.

Hearing the sound, Ling Suyu instinctively looked at his fingers and blinked.

He’d noticed it long ago: Tang Yu’an’s hands, with their slender fingers and well-defined knuckles, were very attractive.

Although his own hands were also attractive, they were a full size smaller than Tang Yu’an’s.

He felt that…if Tang Yu’an were to hold his hand, it would give him a great sense of security.

Tang Yu'an didn't notice that Ling Suyu was distracted; his full attention was on tomorrow's hero picks: "igame is too good at farming. It's really hard to win against them in the early game, and if they farm their way to the late game, Candy Witch's late-game damage won't give us an advantage against them, so we definitely can't pick Candy Witch. Anyway, if they don’t ban Desert Hunter in the first game tomorrow, we’ll pick Desert Hunter. If they do ban it, we can consider Cat Spirit or Dark Raven.”

“Let’s go with Cat Spirit,” Chen Jie said after a moment’s thought. “You guys used Cat Spirit in that random match where you got SMG. UH definitely knows that Feather’s top pick is Cat Spirit—there’s no hiding that. But Dark Raven is a hero Yumao started practicing after joining Fight, so we can keep that under wraps for a bit longer.”

“OK, let’s go with Cat Spirit. Yumao probably wants to play Cat Spirit more anyway, right?”

Tang Yu’an said, suddenly glancing at Ling Suyu.

Ling Suyu had been staring at Tang Yu’an’s hand, lost in thought, when he suddenly sensed his gaze. He froze for a moment before snapping back to reality and muttered, “Mm-hmm.”

Tang Yu’an, noticing his dazed expression, raised an eyebrow and followed the direction of his gaze to look at his own hand resting on the sofa, a thoughtful look crossing his face.

Ling Suyu followed his gaze back to his hand…then quickly looked away, feeling a flush spread across his face.

Had he been caught?

Surely not?

No, he must have been caught.

He wasn’t reading too much into this, was he?

Tang Yu’an, on the other hand, did “read too much into it.”

He was gay to begin with and already had strong feelings for Ling Suyu—how could he not?

However, after noticing Ling Suyu’s gaze on his hand, he said nothing and did nothing; in the next instant, he looked away and continued discussing tomorrow’s team composition: “I don’t think there’s much chance igame will ban Desert Hunter. GQ actually picked the right lineup for the first game—they really put us under a lot of pressure. It’s a shame, but our team was just a cut above theirs. And with the same lineup, igame’s execution is a whole different story.”

Ling Suyu looked up at his profile, blinked, and, thinking he hadn’t noticed his little gesture, secretly let out a sigh of relief.

Little did he know that Tang Yu’an was feeling quite smug inside—Hehe, aren’t my hands beautiful? I think so too! From now on, you won’t just get to look at them, you’ll get to touch them for as long as you want!

Tsk, what is he thinking? Stop!

Actually, Ling Suyu didn’t really care whether he’d get a chance to play Cat Spirit in tomorrow’s match against igame.

Although he liked Cat Spirit, he liked winning matches even more. As long as he could win, he was happy no matter which hero he played. Not to mention that for the past few days, he’d been playing Desert Hunter, his second favorite and most used hero, so he was already quite satisfied.

What he didn’t know, however, was that there was one team that was still holding a grudge against him for not using Cat Spirit in the matches over the past two days: S.MG.

“Why isn’t Feather using Cat Spirit?” SMG’s captain couldn’t figure it out.

“Exactly. His Cat Spirit is so strong—with Desert Hunter banned, he could clearly use Cat Spirit. Why did he have to pick Candy Witch, a hero that offers a big early-game advantage but also carries a lot of risk?”

“Either Fight is keeping something up their sleeves, or Cat Spirit isn’t on Feather’s pick list— or it’s pretty low on his list.”

“I hope it’s the former. Otherwise, it’ll make us, who got absolutely crushed by Cat Spirit, look like a bunch of cripples.”

“Fight’s playing against igame tomorrow? Talk about a fated showdown. I feel like we’ll find out tomorrow whether Cat Spirit is on Feather’s pick list or not.”
