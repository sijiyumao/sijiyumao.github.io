Once the matchmaking was successful and they entered the game, the first phase was the ban phase.

The ban phase was present starting from the Bounty Hunter rank, although from Bounty to Famed both teams were only given two ban spots. It was only when one reached the Glorious rank that both sides would be given three ban spots like in official competitions.

The strong heroes of this version were primarily mid-laners. As such, both sides didn’t dwell much on it before banning five mid-laners and a jungler.

In this match, Ling Suyu was given the first pick for heroes.

Because Tang Yu’an wanted to see which heroes he could play, he didn’t pick Cat Spirit this time. Instead, he picked another continuous-damage type marksman—Desert Hunter.

After selecting it, he whispered: “Can I use this one?”

“Of course,” Tang Yu’an immediately responded, “You can use whatever you like. I don’t have any limits regarding which hero my marksman uses, as long as we can win.”

My marksman.

The corners of Ling Suyu’s lips raised, and he re-assuredly picked Desert Hunter.

When he was still at UH, he was a substitute, and UH didn’t have a substitute support, so he didn’t have a dedicated support.

During the ‘White Fox Cup,’ in the rounds where he was subbing in for Jinag Hao, he felt a strange sense of distance between himself and the support Wen Xu. They were akin to randomly matched passerby teammates, rather than professional teammates who had played on the same team for five years.

But now, he’d only played one match with Tang Yu’an, yet already had the feeling of “Tang Yu’an is my dedicated support,” a feeling that gave Ling Suyu an unprecedented comfort.

The two connected their mics and could hear each other through their headsets, but Tang Yu’an still turned his head whenever he spoke to Ling Suyu.

He knew that for a marksman like Ling Suyu, what he needed most wasn’t in-game technique, but rather trust in his teammates.

Of course, it wasn’t that skills weren’t important, but for Feather, a teammate worth trusting could bring the most out of his strength.

Before he met Feather while solo-queueing, Tang Yu’an had dug out all of Feather’s previous match recordings and reviewed them. He realized that his skills were truly good, but it was also true that he often sacrificed his teammates during competitions. In Tang Yu’an’s eyes, this behaviour was fully due to a lack of trust in his teammates.

As such, if one wanted him to bravely deal damage, they needed to provide him with a sufficient feeling of safety, to let him give them his trust.

Tang Yu’an believed he could do it.

If he couldn’t, he wouldn’t have taken the risk of signing Feather.

Although the other teams had their fun stepping on UH on Weibo, they all knew clearly in their hearts that a player like Feather was a double-edged blade, either slaying their enemies or harming themselves.

So there were only a few teams that actually held out an olive branch to Feather.

Otherwise, the ones continuously phoning Feather wouldn’t be UH.

Tang Yu’an was increasingly looking forward to the Spring Seasonals. He would prove to those teams that his decision to sign Feather was correct and wise. With him here, Feather would definitely become the sharpest blade on the battlefield!

Just as he was thinking this, the chat box suddenly lit up with a message.

【Ally】highly irritable: who plays hunter in this day and age? you trolling?

The hunter he was referring to was the Desert Hunter Ling Suyu had picked.

It was a continuous-damage type marksman. The pros were that it had lots of mobility, a control skill, and was good for both escape and pursuit.

The cons were its fragility, weak early game, and ultimate-reliant late-game damage.

This hero was still usable before basic attack damage was nerfed, but now it was dogwater among dogwater, comparable to Cat Spirit.

Tang Yu’an could see that Ling Suyu enjoyed playing high mobility rapid-attackers, and he was also curious as to why Feather was attached to this type of hero.

But as he’d said, he had no limits for which heroes his marksman used, and even had no limits on the personal style of his marksman.

He trusted Feather, and further trusted in himself.

So, seeing this message, he said without hesitation: “Don’t worry about them, play whatever you want. I’m here, we can win no matter what you play.”

Ling Suyu originally felt a bit uneasy, but hearing Tang Yu’an say this, he immediately relaxed, and even typed up a response.

【Ally】soft and chewy osmanthus cake: no worries, my hunter’s awesome!

Seeing this reply, Tang Yu’an let out a “pfft—”.

Feather had few expressions, and when he imagined him shouting this line with a deadpan face, Tang Yu’an couldn’t hold back.

This was way too cute!

Ling Suyu didn’t know the true reason why Tang Yu’an was laughing, and was afraid the other would misunderstand and think he was arguing against others, so he explained: “The two heroes I’m most comfortable with are Cat Spirit and Desert Hunter. With these two, I can guarantee my win rate.”

“En, I know, it’s just…nevermind,” Tang Yu’an said while laughing, “Same line, with me supporting, even if all the other lanes are wiped out, you won’t die. I’ll take the damage, all you need to do is kill.”

“Okay!” Ling Suyu responded happily, relaxing. They hadn’t even started, and he already felt like they were going to win.

However, after the enemy mid-laner chose Butterfly Dancer and it came time for the one who was just insulting Hunter to pick, he instantly chose Water Ghost.

A hero that only a newbie would use, very good at freeloading and waiting for death, and a support.

“Support?” Ling Suyu froze.

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow, though he wasn’t surprised.

It was way too common to run into position-snatching while duo-queueing.

He immediately typed out a string of words.

【Ally】tangyuan with sesame filling: you want to play support? then I'll top-lane?

【Ally】highly irritable: isn’t he trolling? I’ll troll too.

Seeing the guy’s behaviour, Tang Yu’an no longer paid attention to him, and directly started communicating with their other teammates.

【Ally】tangyuan with sesame filling: mid-lane, mind switching with me? I’ll go mid, you go top with mage.

【Ally】only plays nine-tails: sure, I’m fine as long as I can play nine-tails, I don't care which lane I take.

【Ally】tangyuan with sesame filling: ok.

“You’re playing top lane?” Ling Suyu instantly felt uneasy.

Professional players pretty much all specialized in one lane. After all, playstyles were different for each position, and a person who was good in one wouldn’t necessarily be good in another.

Although this didn’t mean that they didn’t know how to play other positions, how could a position they were always training in compare to one they basically never played?

Ling Suyu was worried that Tang Yu’an wouldn’t be able to play top-lane, and was even more worried that playing top-lane would affect his feel for playing support.

On the flip side, Tang Yu’an said casually: “I can play jungler too.”

Ling Suyu: 0.0 Tang Yu’an tilted his head and cast a glance at Ling Suyu. Seeing his confused demeanor, he had a hard time holding back his laughter.

Feather was the only one who would react this way after hearing that he was going to play top lane.

The others at Fight no longer batted an eye at it.

Pretty good, he’d take this chance to show Feather the depth of his hero pool.

Thinking this way, when Tang Yu’an got his chance to pick his hero, he locked onto Skeletal Empress without thinking, a full-on top-lane hero.

Ultimately, the respective lineups on both sides were:

Ally: Skeletal Empress, Nine-Tailed Fox, Desert Hunter, Water Ghost, Tempest Blade Enemy: White Cocoon, Butterfly Dancer, Guardian Spirit, Abyssal Lord, Venomous Scorpion Ling Suyu was already sick of the Guardian Spirit Abyssal Lord bottom lane duo.

This was the strongest bottom lane combination in the current version of Glorious Heroes.

But in another sense, it was also the duo that Ling Suyu had the highest chance of encountering and therefore had the most experience dealing with.

And so, seeing these two heroes, Ling Suyu not only did not panic, but was also well-prepared.

Though the unexpected situation of his support being stolen and forced to play top-lane had affected him, this wasn’t the first time he’d played bottom lane with a passerby support, and it also wasn’t the first time he’d run into troll teammates, so what could he do? Didn’t he have to play regardless?

Once the game began, Ling Suyu didn’t say anything, controlling his Desert Hunter to head towards the bottom lane.

Tang Yu’an directed his top-lane hero Skeletal Empress to the mid lane.

But that support.

After Ling Suyu reached the lane, he glanced at the minimap to see the support still squatting in the fountain, unknowing whether or not the other had disconnected, and pursed his lips. .

It seems like he could only stay under the tower and duo with the tower in the early game.

Just as he thought this, he heard Tang Yu’an say: “Hold on for a bit, I’ll come down as soon as I reach level 2.”

“No need,” Ling Suyu replied, “You just hold back the mid-laner, don’t draw him down.”

“No worries, they won’t be able to come down,” Tang Yu’an said, strangely confident.

Ling Suyu: ?

Ling Suyu didn’t know why Tang Yu’an could say that with such certainty.

He stayed under the tower while clearing minions while curiously looking at the mini map, occasionally dragging the perspective over to take a look.

Only to see Tang Yu’an move clumsily and eat a few of the enemy’s skills, his health quickly depleting to half, with no choice but to retreat back under the tower.

The enemy mid-laner pursued the victory, wanting to force him back to the tower and continue dealing damage.

However, at this moment, Tang Yu’an Skeletal Empress suddenly exploded, unleashing a “Heavy Bone Hammer” right on top of the other’s head, stunning them, while simultaneously picking off the last minion. With that, he reached level 2 and lit up his second skill, Sea of Corpses.

The damage dealt by the hammer wasn’t small, and directly took out a quarter of their health. Once the other side recovered from the stun, seeing that their blood was still greater than Tang Yu’an’s, they didn’t choose to run but instead decided to attack with their skills.

Yet this time, Tang Yu’an’s movements suddenly became flexible. On top of dodging all of his skills, he even got up close and threw in two basic attacks, once again taking out a section of his health.

The other side finally realized something wasn’t right, and flashed to escape underneath their own tower. Tang Yu’an decisively used his Flash in pursuit while simultaneously casting his second skill “Sea of Corpses,” throwing a bunch of broken bones onto the field.

This was a speed debuff skill, and could only work on moving targets within the skill’s range.

However, with their own tower right in front of their eyes and the enemy hot on their tails behind, how could the enemy not move!

So, even though they knew moving would cause them to take damage, the enemy mid-laner still, with difficulty, moved two steps towards their tower.

Tang Yu’an pursued him with a flurry of basic attack, depleting the last sliver of his blood right before he stepped into the tower’s range. Then, with his own health still unmoving at half, he ran towards the bottom lane without looking back.

【System】Skeletal Empress, first blood!

【System】The enemy Butterfly Dancer has been slain!

“Huh?” Ling Suyu was stunned speechless.

Tang Yu’an lightly chuckled: “Look, I was right, wasn’t I? They won’t be able to come down.”
