As the New Year was only a few days away, many shops were closed, and deserted with few people in sight.

It was a good thing that there were quite a few stores around the Fight club, and the two soon arrived at a small restaurant that was still open.

Ling Suyu recalled his past 20 years of life. Aside from his parents, this was the first time someone was individually treating him to a meal.

However, he didn’t find it weird, and even found it a bit comforting.

Nevertheless, there was no reward without merit, and he hadn’t even competed for Fight yet, so even if he accepted the gesture, he still felt a bit guilty.

It was unknown whether Tang Yu’an had guessed his internal monologue, as he said:

“You’re new here, a guest, and one should treat a guest to a meal. For me, as Fight’s captain, I should similarly be looking after the newcomers. So don’t stand on ceremony with me when the food comes, just eat whatever you like, and in exchange…”

Ling Suyu seriously widened his eyes.

Seeing him like that, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help laughing, saying as he chuckled: “Play a few rounds of Glorious with me after you’re full, and let me see which heroes you’re good at.”

“Okay~” Ling Suyu agreed without hesitation.

He wanted nothing more than to duo-queue with Tang Yu’an.

He also wanted to quickly get used to cooperating with his new support, bringing his best condition to the upcoming Spring Seasonals.

As they were waiting for the dishes, Ling Suyu scrolled through his contacts list, blocking every number connected with UH in case they came to “disturb” him again.

Originally, he was thinking of leaving those he had a good relationship with, but as he went through them, he suddenly realized that throughout his 5 years at UH, he pretty much didn’t have a relationship that could be considered good with anyone.

Well, between the two he had the best relationship with, one was Jiang Hao, and the other was the manager, and they’d both turned on him and been blocked.

“Haa…” Ling Suyu let out a long sigh.

“What’s wrong?” Tang Yu’an said as he poured him a drink, “Did you think of something unpleasant? Tell me, and I’ll try to make you happy?”

Ling Suyu took a deep breath and sighed helplessly: “It’s nothing, I just think that I’m such a failure at being a person.”

“That’s because you hadn’t met me yet,” Tang Yu’an blurted out, “Relationships between people are interesting. Two people could both be good people, but nevertheless incompatible. They end up hurting each other and thinking less of the other person, and simultaneously doubt themselves, but I’m different.”

“How are you different?” Ling Suyu asked curiously.

“Because I can get along with anyone,“ Tang Yu’an said confidently, before asking in reply:

“Do you know why?”

“Why?”

“Because those I can’t get along with have all been one-sidedly rejected by me, and those that are still around me are ones I get along with.”

Ling Suyu: “.....”

“Hey don’t be speechless, I’m being serious,” Tang Yu’an deadpanned, “With some people, since you know you can’t get along, why try to force it? There’s no need. Take your previous captain Jiang Hao. After hearing him say just a few sentences, I know for sure that I’ll never get along with him for as long as I live. And in that case, much less being friends, I won’t even make eye contact with him. But you, for instance, I think we’re pretty compatible, and can get along.”

Can get along?

Ling Suyu thought about it a bit more and felt a bit lost.

Tang Yu’an chuckled, pretending not to notice Ling Suyu’s confusion: “Look at how happily we’re chatting. I’m happy, you’re happy, so I call this ‘getting along’.”

Ling Suyu: “.....”

Even though you’re the only one talking, and I’m just listening.

That said, when Tang Yu’an said he could get along with anyone, Ling Suyu had actually believed it without a hint of suspicion.

He truly believed that Tang Yu’an could do it, and even wanted to know how he did it, but he never thought the answer would be something like this.

He felt like he had been tricked, but he wasn’t angry at all, and was rather happy amidst all the exasperation.

It turns out Tang Yu’an also had people he couldn’t get along with, and he was one of the ones who he could.

—Thinking about it this way, Ling Suyu felt pretty happy, like the distance between himself

and Tang Yu’an had shrunk a lot.

Could it be that Tang Yu’an was one of those legendary social butterflies?

The type that introverts could only gaze upon.

After they finished eating, Ling Suyu followed Tang Yu’an back to the Fight club.

Tang Yu’an told him to first finish unpacking before duo-queueing with him, and Ling Suyu nodded and went obediently.

What Ling Suyu didn’t know was that as soon as he went upstairs, Tang Yu’an called Fight’s manager Ye Xiran.

“Captain Tang, what’s the matter?” a warm and refined voice sounded from the phone.

“I need you to do some overtime,” Tang Yu’an said.

“Huh? What did you say?! The wind’s too loud, I can’t hear you! I think my signal’s bad, I’m going to hang up first!” the warm and refined voice immediately became frantic.

Tang Yu’an snickered: “Act, keep acting, if you really dare to hang up on me, just wait till I dock your pay!”

“Alright, alright,” Ye Xiran accepted his fate and sighed, “So, what’s up?”

“I want to sign Feather, the substitute that just ended his contract with UH,” Tang Yu’an said.

Ye Xiran: “Oh, sure, weren’t you always the deciding authority on these types of things?”

Tang Yu’an: “Here’s the main thing. UH themselves flipped the table and posted a Weibo statement, and they actually want Feather to step up and help them rescue public opinion.

Just in case, as our PR expert, can you help me teach them the correct way of doing things?”

Ye Xiran: “Huh? Why? Since when did Great Villain Tang start having kind thoughts of saving the masses?”

Tang Yu’an: “It’s not for them, but for Feather.”

Ye Xiran: “Oh, I get what you mean, you want them to bend over and apologize to Feather, right? But is there a need to do all this? Hasn’t Feather’s contract with UH not expired yet?”

Tang Yu’an: “Just do as I say, why do you talk so much nonsense? Why do you think I poached you in the first place? Was it because you were clever and efficient, or because you had so much rubbish to say?”

Ye Xiran: “Tsk tsk, seems like this new guy is pretty impressive to get so much attention from our Captain Tang. Sure, I’ll try, but I can’t guarantee whether or not they’ll actually listen.”

Tang Yu’an: “No need to rush, I’ll send you something later, you’ll know what to do once you get it.”

Tang Yu’an said that, and as soon as he hung up, the first thing he did was find Ye Xiran’s contact and send him the voice recording.

Yep, that same voice recording from Ling Suyu’s call with Jiang Hao.

Once Ye Xiran received and gave it a listen, he felt deeply impressed by Tang Yu’an.

gentle sunshine: although I called you Great Villain Tang, you really dared to do this. When a teammate is on call with their previous captain, a normal person’s first reaction would be to give space, but your first reaction was to record the conversation? This newbie is really broad-minded, they just let you record like that?

tangyuan: it’s UH not behaving properly, so what’s wrong with me recording and being a bit more cautious? It’s only villainous if the recording is used to do bad things, but I’m doing good deeds here. Cut the crap, just tell me whether you’ll do it or not.

gentle sunshine: fine ~ I’ll get it done in a flash!

After replying, Ye Xiran went to contact UH.

Tang Yu’an’s meaning was clear. He wanted UH to publicly apologize to Feather and decisively conclude this matter.

This would obviously be good for Feather, as UH would no longer be able to bother him with all sorts of means, and would also help vindicate past injustices.

There were also some benefits for UH in this. They hadn’t broken the law, so as long as they admitted their mistakes in time with enough of a genuine attitude, they could not only stop the fermentation of public opinion on a large scale, but could even win back some favourability.

Originally, Ye Xiran was worried that UH would harbour a false sense of security and be unwilling to cooperate, but after getting this recording, hehe~ And so, not long after Ling Suyu finished unpacking, UH’s official Weibo updated.

It was a long apology message directed towards Ling Suyu.

Within the message, UH deeply reflected on their previous post that mistakenly linked “Feather’s contract termination” with “UH’s abnormal performance during the White Fox Cup”. Using some forum expert’s analysis of the competition, they praised Feather’s performance during the White Fox Cup and admitted that their own starters performed poorly. As for their reasoning, they explained that the starters and substitutes rarely trained together, and thus had a lower degree of understanding. As such, they butted heads during the matches. Finally, they once more expressed apologies towards Feather, and hoped for his forgiveness.

This letter spanned thousands of characters at full length and detail, used sincere language, and abounded with emotions, truly a modern PR masterpiece.

Sure enough, once the message was sent out, public opinion once again fermented wildly, and then began to calm down. The voices criticizing UH also lessened quite a bit, and the voices saying “to know one’s faults and be able to correct them is commendable” began to grow. Then everyone’s attention shifted from UH to Feather’s personal Weibo, waiting for his response.

Yet Feather never replied.

His last Weibo was sent two years ago, and it was unknown whether he even still used the account.

It couldn’t be that he’d forgotten the password, right…

Ling Suyu didn’t have a habit of scrolling Weibo, so he temporarily had no idea of the situation. Tang Yu’an also wasn’t in a hurry to tell him, as he wanted him to see it and reply on his own volition.

Although he hadn’t interacted with Ling Suyu for long, he trusted in his own judgement of character, and knew that Ling Suyu’s reply wouldn’t be anything concerning.

So, as soon as Ling Suyu went downstairs after he finished unpacking, Tang Yu’an directly led him to the training room.

“Sit here.” Tang Yu’an sat at a spot near the door and patted the seat next to him.

“Okay.” Ling Suyu sat down obediently.

Actually, even if Tang Yu’an hadn’t said anything, he would still know where to sit, as only these two monitors were on in the entire room.

“Are you comfortable with this keyboard and mouse?” Tang Yu’an asked, “If not, we can switch them.”

Ling Suyu eyed the mouse and keyboard, noting that it wasn’t the brand he usually used: “I’ll try.”

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow in realization: “No way? UH didn’t even let you take your keyboard and mouse with you? What kind of petty club is that?”

“I just felt sorry taking it, it’s the club’s property after all…It’s okay, there’s still some time left before the Spring Seasonals, I’ll get used to it quickly,” Ling Suyu said as he logged into the game and put on his headphones.

Tang Yu’an chuckled helplessly, also putting on his headphones.

Then, he opened a room, invited him, and clicked on matchmaking.

Let the game begin!
