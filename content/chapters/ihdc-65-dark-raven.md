This season, from the qualifiers to the group stage, Fight had been unstoppable in nearly every match except for the one against igame, sweeping aside opponents with overwhelming force. Their form had been nothing short of phenomenal, as if guided by the championship trophy itself.

As for this season’s XY, from the qualifiers to the group stage, they first lost to a brand-new team, and then were outplayed by the infamous UH, barely advancing to the quarterfinals as the second-place finisher in their group, a performance that raised serious questions about their form.

Fight and XY’s face-off occurred on the first day of the quarterfinals, and few people looked favourably upon XY. As a matter of fact, Fight themselves even fielded Candy Witch and Mobile Artillery, a combination they hadn’t dared to use against igame.

Even with Fight seemingly “underestimating” their opponent, no one doubted they would defeat XY; the only uncertainty was whether it would be 2-0 or 2-1.

However, once the match began, Fight proved to everyone through their performance—not only had they not underestimated their opponent, but they were taking the match very seriously.

About ten minutes into the game, the teams engaged in their nth team fight. On Fight’s side, Tang Yu’an’s Mandara Vines used Flash to land a perfect crowd control and initiate the fight. As he activated his ultimate, Mo Xiaoyun’s Black-Armoured Scythe followed suit with his own ultimate, the two shielding Ling Suyu behind them. Protected by them, Ling Suyu used his Candy Witch to unleash a barrage of damage on the immobilized XY players. At the same time, Dou Jiayan also activated his ultimate along with a Flash—his execution was flawless, without the slightest mistake.

The flurry of damage instantly decimated XY’s health. Even though XY’s marksman Barrier Sorceress managed to dodge the crowd control with keen positioning and immediately returned to activate her ultimate, “Witch’s Barrier,” to protect some of her nearby teammates, the system message “Ally [hero] has been slain” kept flashing across their screens.

Protected by her teammates, Barrier Sorceress managed to deal a significant amount of damage. However, the moment she targeted Ling Suyu with the intention of prioritizing his elimination, her failure was inevitable.

Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch used her movement skill, “Witch’s Clone,” to nimbly hide behind her teammate, even having her doppelganger block two basic attacks for her.

When “Witch’s Clone” is activated, Candy Witch teleports away while leaving an exact doppelganger in her place.

To her teammates, Candy Witch appeared semi-transparent the moment she cast the spell; she became fully visible again after teleporting, while the clone left behind displayed only her appearance, not her health bar.

However, to enemy heroes, Candy Witch became invisible the moment she cast the spell, while the clone left behind still displayed all her stats, including health and mana bars.

Therefore, if enemy heroes failed to notice that Candy Witch had used her dash, they could not immediately recognize the clone as a clone—even lock-on skills would deceive them.

XY’s Barrier Sorceress only realized something was wrong when he saw another Candy Witch appear behind Fight’s top laner, but by then he had already wasted two basic attacks, which pained him immensely.

But what pained him even more was the realization that he could neither kill Candy Witch, nor even escape.

Sure enough, in the next instant, Yu He’s Tempest Blade struck, cleanly securing the kill. XY was wiped out!

【System】 Tempest Blade, one kill!

【System】 The enemy Barrier Sorceress has been slain!

【System】 The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us!

XY played very defensively in this team fight, but they’d done their best—there weren’t any major mistakes.

Their biggest mistake was that, the moment Fight initiated the team fight, their positioning was too clustered, leaving too many players locked down by CC.

However, they had no way of knowing exactly when Fight would initiate. If they’d stayed too far apart and their marksman had been instantly taken out first, they would have been wiped out even faster and more brutally.

“Do we still have a chance?”

“Yes!”

“As long as we can hold out, we’ll turn this around!”

XY didn’t lose heart. They quietly waited to respawn, then looked for a new opportunity to counter-kill Fight.

On Fight’s side, everyone looked at their low health bars and felt a shiver of relief.

Dou Jiayan: “That was close! If I’d joined the fight even a little later, would we have been wiped out?”

“I guess your week of training wasn’t a waste.” After offering a compliment, Tang Yu’an adopted a rare serious expression. “They’re tougher than I expected. We can’t drag this out any longer. If we do, once Barrier Sorceress revives, we’ll be in deep trouble. So… let’s take down the dragon!”

Soon after, XY, who was waiting to respawn, saw—

【System】 Tragedy! The enemy Black-Armoured Scythe has been slain by the Dragon God!

【System】 Tragedy! The enemy Mandara Vines has been slain by the Dragon God!

【System】 Tragedy! The enemy Tempest Blade has been slain by the Dragon God!

XY: ??

The Dragon God scored a triple kill!

Just as you’d expect from the Dragon God, it effortlessly accomplished what they couldn’t…like hell!

“Fight went after the dragon even after we beat them to a pulp?”

“It’d be hilarious if the Dragon wiped them out.”

As it turned out, XY was being overly optimistic and overthinking.

【System】 Candy Witch has successfully conquered the Dragon God and summoned the divine dragon legion!

As soon as Ling Suyu finished off the Dragon God, he and the low-health Dou Jiayan both returned to base to heal up.

They never expected the Dragon God to pull off a triple kill.

Mo Xiaoyun stepped up first to tank the hits, but he was down to just a sliver of health. His healing abilities had been used up in the previous team fight and were still on cooldown, so he had been taken down by the Dragon God in a matter of minutes.

Dou Jiayan had more health and volunteered to tank, but the Dragon God’s furious claws stripped him of a third of his health, causing him to flee in panic. Tang Yu’an, who, like Mo Xiaoyun, was down to just a bit of health, had no choice but to grit his teeth and step in to tank, only to be taken down by the Dragon God in a matter of minutes as well.

Having secured a double kill, the Dragon God raised its golden head high, looking down on the three survivors at his feet with eyes full of arrogance and disdain.

Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch, was also in a precarious state, but at least her health was higher than that of her other two teammates. Seeing that the Dragon God still had a significant amount of health remaining, he tried to tank it, but Yu He used Smite. Since he had lifesteal, using Smite would restore some health. Besides, since XY had been wiped out, he didn’t need to compete with anyone, so there was no need to save Smite for last.

But he was already down to a sliver of health; even after using Smite, his health bar wasn’t in great shape.

The enraged Dragon God swiped its tail, sending him flying through the air.

Yu He—gone.

Then, under the trembling gaze of Dou Jiayan and the composed stare of Ling Suyu, Candy Witch finished off the Dragon God’s last bit of health, and it finally bowed its noble head.

Dou Jiayan cast Recall almost immediately, muttering in disbelief, “Wow, we won 5 to 0 against [chromosome] and went 3 to 1 against a dragon. Are those XY guys laughing at us right now?”

“They might actually be filled with awe,” Tang Yu’an replied. “Look at us—we’re just taking down a dragon, but we’re doing it so heroically! It’s truly moving!”

“Come on, they’d love nothing more than to see us wiped by the dragon,” Mo Xiaoyun stated bluntly.

Yu He looked completely unfazed: “Yumao, take the lightning strike.”

“Mm-hmm,” Ling Suyu acknowledged, then headed to the jungle to farm monsters.

There was still some time before XY could respawn. Even though the top laner, support, and jungler were all down, he and Dou Jiayan had more than enough time to take down at least three of XY’s turrets.

The commentators were also stunned by Dragon God’s triple kill and couldn’t help joking around:

Liang Fan said, “A triple kill by Dragon God! If XY manages to win this game, shouldn’t the MVP go to Dragon God?”

Remi couldn’t stop laughing: “Hahaha, Fight made a huge sacrifice just to secure that dragon!”

“But it’s understandable,” Liang Fan said, adopting a slightly more serious tone. “Fight’s composition is designed for the early game. Candy Witch deals plenty of damage early on, but her late-game damage potential is limited. Although they used Mobile Artillery to make up for the physical damage, Mobile Artillery’s late-game damage is also lacking. By the late game, neither Candy Witch nor Mobile Artillery can outdamage Barrier Sorceress. Plus, on XY’s side, aside from Barrier Sorceress, all the other heroes have very high health. It won’t be easy for Fight to push through them to take down Barrier Sorceress.”

“So Fight’s eager to secure a win in the early game—is that right?” Remi understood.

Early-game compositions were a blast, especially when you were crushing the enemy in gold—while pushing towers and engaging in team fights, you felt unstoppable.

But playing a pro match with an early-game composition was like having the Sword of Damocles hanging over your head; you never knew when it’d come crashing down.

Once the opponent managed to hang on until the late game, the tide would gradually turn. Like a frog in slowly heating water, the temperature would rise until it finally boiled.

This was very dangerous for Fight.

And when it came to XY in particular, being a team that got more and more fired up as the game went on, Fight didn’t want to give them any opportunities.

So even if only Ling Suyu was left alive, they had to secure that Dragon God!

As it turned out, their sacrifice paid off. With Ling Suyu on the bottom lane and Dou Jiayan on the mid lane, the two of them took down all the towers on XY’s mid and bottom lanes in one go, putting XY’s main tower under threat.

“Recall.” Ling Suyu recalled immediately after taking down the three towers on the bottom lane.

Dou Jiayan heard his voice and recalled right after him, only then realizing—Huh? Did Yumao just give him an order?

Ling Suyu didn’t know why he’d called out to warn Dou Jiayan either; maybe it was because Dou Jiayan was just too unreliable.

Dou Jiayan’s game sense wasn’t bad, but he had a penchant for taking risks.

Even knowing the jungler was nearby, he’d gamble that the opponent wouldn’t arrive that quickly, or that their damage wouldn’t be that high—in short, that he’d have time to escape.

Even knowing that enemy heroes would charge at him the moment they respawned, he’d still gamble that they wouldn’t arrive that quickly, and that he’d have time to escape.

In the past, Ling Suyu would wait for Tang Yu’an to speak up. Now, he wasn’t sure if it was because Fight as a whole talked too much, which had made him more talkative in turn, or if, after playing so many matches, he’d realized that Tang Yu’an was, after all, only human, not a god, and couldn’t possibly keep track of every teammate at all times. So he’d started trying to speak up himself, trying to help lighten Tang Yu’an’s load.

Tang Yu’an was delighted by this change in him, so he said nothing.

Soon, all the heroes on both sides had respawned. While XY was busy clearing minions and defending their tower, Yu He solo-pushed the top lane to draw attention, allowing the others to quickly take down the second Divine Sunbird. Then, using the Divine Sunbird’s buff, they advanced directly through the mid lane toward XY’s main tower and engaged in another team fight with XY.

This team fight took place while Tang Yu’an had his ultimate active and was taking damage from the main tower, yet they still nearly wiped out XY’s team before their marksman’s damage had fully ramped up.

Why “nearly”? Because XY’s marksman, taking advantage of the invisibility effect from her ultimate, used Flash to escape with a single layer of health remaining.

But it didn’t matter—the rest of XY was down, and no one was left to protect her.

Without a tank to absorb damage or a support to shield them, a marksman was nothing more than a fragile target.

If he dared to engage, Fight wouldn’t hesitate to take him out instantly.

If he didn’t engage, Fight wouldn’t even bother with him and would focus on pushing towers instead.

Ling Suyu and Dou Jiayan stood a short distance apart in front of XY’s main tower, standing still and auto-attacking.

Red and purple damage numbers shot up from the main tower as its health rapidly dropped.

Soon, the word “WIN” appeared on Fight’s screen.

Despite the minor hiccup of getting triple-killed by Dragon God, nothing could stop Fight from winning this match.

“Let’s congratulate Fight on taking the first point!” said commentator Liang Fan. “Although the outcome was pretty much a foregone conclusion by the middle of the match, as it was clear Fight had been on top of their game the whole time, and XY could no longer stop them, we still want to thank both teams for bringing us such an exciting match!”

“I don’t know if it’s just my imagination, but it feels like not only Feather’s Candy Witch but also Swallow’s Mobile Artillery were much stronger in this match,” Remi pointed out.

“No, your instincts are right,” Liang Fan affirmed. “Swallow’s Mobile Artillery has improved significantly since the group stages. If we say that Fight’s use of this mid-bot lane duo during the group stage was an experiment, then today we’re seeing a much more mature pairing. It feels like this lineup will get plenty of play-time moving forward!”

Candy Witch and Mobile Artillery will see a lot of play?

In response, other teams that hadn’t faced Fight yet said—No! We’d rather die than let that lineup show up in a match against us! Feather’s Candy Witch is way too overpowered!

Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch, just like his Cat Spirit, left people in awe.

When he played Cat Spirit, others said—

That’s actually Cat Spirit?!

Are you telling me this terrifying thing is Cat Spirit?!

How is this hero, who can both farm safely and deal massive damage, even considered a bottom-tier pick?!

When he played Candy Witch, they said—

Help! Is still the Candy Witch I know?!

This Candy Witch is unstoppable!

She’s this strong and they haven’t nerfed her yet? What are the designers thinking!

XY, who had faced the Candy Witch’s terrifying early-game damage firsthand, felt the same way.

“Ban!”

So, in the second game, after Fight started off by banning Grim Reaper, XY gritted their teeth and immediately put Candy Witch on the ban list!

Fight weren’t surprised at all. On the contrary, they felt a sense of delight, thinking, “Great, another hero’s been unbanned.”

“[chromosome]’s attention is completely fixated on Yumao. Do you think He-He will be able to get Twin Demonblades this game?” Dou Jiayan asked curiously.

“Hard to say,” Tang Yu’an replied, casually taking a dig at Dou Jiayan. “I have a feeling Beast Master will be let out—too bad a certain someone won’t be able to carry the team.”

Dou Jiayan’s lips twitched, and he fired back immediately: “I think you guys should focus on figuring out what composition to use in the bot lane. Yumao has fewer and fewer heroes to choose from.”

Ling Suyu: ?

You must have some kind of misunderstanding about me.

“You must have some kind of misunderstanding about Yumao.”

No sooner had Ling Suyu finished thinking this than he heard Tang Yu’an’s familiar voice through his headphones voice his exact thoughts.

Ling Suyu’s eyes widened slightly; for a moment, he suspected he’d been read like an open book.

Tang Yu’an, completely oblivious to his thoughts, continued, “Do you think Yumao’s the same as you? You’ve been playing professionally for so long and still haven’t mastered all the mages. There are plenty of marksmen out there, and he can pick up any one of them and play like a pro!”

“Oh, come on!” Dou Jiayan protested, sounding aggrieved. “Look at how many mages there are, and look at how many marksmen. I’m under twice as much pressure as Yumao!”

This time, Ling Suyu decided to stick up for Yanzi: “All a marksman needs to do is basic attack.”

Dou Jiayan: “Listen to him!”

Immediately after, Ling Suyu added, “But you’re a mid-laner, so you can’t be compared to a marksman like me. You should be compared to mid-laners from other teams, like Taozi[^1] from igame.”

The soul was already boiling; all that was left was to throw the person in to cook.

Tang Yu’an smiled knowingly: “Taozi’s Beast Master just pinned UH to the ground and beat them up—it was so cool. If we knew how to play Beast Master, would we even worry about not dealing enough damage against XY? Haah, it’ll be such a shame if XY actually lets out Beast Master—such~ a~ shame!”

Dou Jiayan: “……”

Ling Suyu: “True.”

Dou Jiayan: “……”

What’s wrong with these two today? Had they gotten addicted to “dueting”?

“But Yanzi, why isn’t your Beast Master any good?” Ling Suyu asked, a bit curious.

Although he didn’t like how Guardian Spirit felt to play, whenever he picked a marksman, he always had the courage to shout in his heart, “I’m going to carry this game.” He assumed that as a mid-laner, Dou Jiayan would also have the courage to carry the team no matter which mid-lane hero he picked—so why wasn’t the Beast Master up to the task?

After all, heroes with mobility like Phoenix Soul and Harp Demon were much harder to play compared to Beast Master.

To his complete surprise, Dou Jiayan replied, “Beast Master is too squishy and too late-game. I practiced him for a while before, but because he was so strong, the enemy team targeted him. Any random player could tower dive and insta-kill me, so I never got to farm properly until the match was over. It was so frustrating—I felt like if I kept using him, I'd end up uninstalling Glorious.”

Ling Suyu: “……”

Okay, he kind of got that feeling—it was probably similar to when he played Desert Hunter and lost the match before he even got a chance to get going.

But he had plenty of patience, and his willpower was strong enough.

If anything, he was rebellious and defiant; the harder it was for him to get a foothold, the more he wanted to prove he could survive until he turned the tide.

With basic attack damage nerfed so severely these days, a reason he was still playing a sustained-damage marksman who relied on basic attacks—aside from genuinely loving Cat Spirit, enjoying the thrill of dominating with basic attacks, and feeling secure with heroes that had mobility—might also be due to a touch of stubbornness and persistence.

He wants to prove that neither Cat Spirit nor Desert Hunter were useless, and that the balance adjustments for Glorious weren’t as bad as people thought. He believed that with good gameplay, even heroes others considered “bottom-tier” could achieve what the meta’s top picks were capable of.

But not everyone possessed his level of determination. Especially since Dou Jiayan had only recently entered this scene and his mindset wasn’t yet stable—it was perfectly normal for him to develop a psychological aversion to a champion after being killed too many times or losing too many matches.

So, after a moment of silence, Ling Suyu replied coolly with two words: “Got it.”

Upon hearing those two words, Dou Jiayan felt as if, for a fleeting moment, he was seeing Ling Suyu again—the expressionless face with which he’d replied to Ma Mingxi’s provocation with just two words: “Read.”

What should he do? He felt an inexplicable sense of reluctance, and his fingers itched to pick up Beast Master again.

However, XY’s second ban slot dashed his hopes—after Fight banned Abyssal Lord, XY banned Beast Master once again.

Dou Jiayan stared at Beast Master’s icon in the ban slot. Far from being pleased that XY had wasted a ban slot just like in the previous game, he instead felt a hollow emptiness in his heart.

It was like a child who’d finally mustered the courage to try bungee jumping, only to discover the cliff was surrounded by a railing and marked with a “No Bungee Jumping” sign.

The rest of Fight had no idea he’d been thinking about picking up Beast Master again. After Tang Yu’an banned Realm God, he said with interest, “Guess which one they’ll pick for their last ban—Twin Demonblades or Cat Spirit?”

“No matter which one they pick, our chances of winning this game are much higher than the last one,” Mo Xiaoyun replied. “Yumao is truly useful.”

Ling Suyu: ?

“Yumao is truly useful," Yu He repeated.

Ling Suyu: ??

Okay sure, it seemed like his picks were being used to trick-out ban slots?

No sooner had he thought that than XY selected the final ban slot—Twin Demonblades!

“Oh-ho?” This choice caught Tang Yu’an off guard.

“Tsk.” It also caught Yu He off guard.

He’d thought he’d finally get to show off with Twin Demonblades.

But he could still show off with Tempest Blade—it didn’t matter.

In the last game, he’d racked up just as many kills as Ling Suyu; if he hadn’t died a few times, he might’ve even snagged the MVP.

However, just as Fight thought they were about to secure Cat Spirit, the opposing team locked in their first champion—Spider Queen.

“Huh?” Dou Jiayan thought he must be seeing things.

Ling Suyu blinked and glanced sideways at Tang Yu’an.

The smile on Tang Yu’an’s face froze, gradually turning into a murderous intent to go on a killing spree as a top laner.

Stealing his hero two games in a row, huh? XY, you guys might not be good at much else, but you sure know how to build up a grudge.

So, when it was Fight’s turn to pick a champion, before his teammates could even ask, Tang Yu’an spoke up: “Skeletal Empress and Black-Armoured Scythe. Let’s take the two tankiest ones—I’ll see what the enemy picks to absorb our damage!”

As soon as Ling Suyu heard this, he knew Tang Yu’an intended to play Skeletal Empress as support, and he inexplicably got the feeling of “this game’s in the bag.”

Lately, Tang Yu’an seemed to favor playing pure support to protect him as much as possible, but what he didn’t know was that no matter what hero Tang Yu’an picked, Ling Suyu always felt completely secure—even when Tang Yu’an played Mobile Artillery. Instead of feeling like “I’m a pitiful marksman with no one to protect me,” Ling Suyu actually looked forward to it, thinking, “Cool, in the late game, someone’ll be there to help me chase down enemies and auto-attack.”

Yeah, that’s just how much Ling Suyu trusted Tang Yu’an.

As for Tang Yu’an’s signature hero, Skeletal Empress—just hearing the words “signature hero” was enough to make Ling Suyu feel like the enemy team was already finished.

However, XY’s choices after Tang Yu’an picked were truly beyond his expectations.

After Fight locked in their top lane and support, XY very decisively locked in Cat Spirit, then hesitated for a moment before locking in Tempest Blade.

Huh?

Ling Suyu, who was about to pick Cat Spirit, and Yu He, who was about to pick Tempest—one was expressionless, but his eyes radiated a chilling coldness; the other was equally expressionless, but tilted his head back slightly, his gaze the kind one might cast upon a corpse.

The commentators clearly recognized this as the lineup Fight had intended to run, and they couldn’t help but wear expressions of gleeful anticipation, as if they were eager to see the chaos unfold.

Remi: “Wow! Walk Fight’s own path and leave them with nowhere to go?”

Liang Fan: “This game is going to be interesting! Though I understand XY’s desire to contain Fight, this isn’t their usual lineup—will they be able to handle it?” He paused. “But XY is a team that excels at adjusting their lineup on the fly to win matches. I wonder what lineup Fight will use to counter their provocation now that their pick order has been snatched away?”

What lineup?

Of course, a lineup that could kill them!

Yu He, his face expressionless, locked Blade Soul.

Ling Suyu, his face expressionless, locked Dark Raven.

Yu He: “I’m going to tank the tower.”

Ling Suyu: “I’m going to kill people.”

Tang Yu’an couldn’t stop laughing when he heard their voices brimming with indignation.

Since both he and Mo Xiaoyun were skilled at tanking towers, that kind of role never fell to Yu He. Yu He would much rather choose an assassin with higher burst damage—either instantly taking down the enemy or getting taken down himself.

As for the hero Blade Soul, with its built-in defense and lifesteal, it was better suited for sustained damage than for burst combos. Played well, it could secure a pentakill; played poorly, it couldn’t kill a single enemy. That was why, although Yu He had practiced with it, he almost never used it in competitive matches.

The fact that he was using Blade Soul in this match meant he was itching to tower-dive and secure a solo kill.

As for Ling Suyu, based on the draft order, it was indeed time for his Dark Raven to make an appearance. But he picked Dark Raven without even asking, showing no intention of hiding his strength by using Desert Hunter or another hero. That meant he was itching to teach the opposing team a lesson.

Great, feels like we’ve got this game in the bag~

Tang Yu’an happily mused to himself, unaware that a small portion of the audience, upon seeing Dark Raven, had their expressions shift from surprise to sudden realization and then to amusement.

Dark Raven?

City B’s number one Dark Raven, [soft and chewy osmanthus cake]—was that you, Feather?
