No sooner had the host finished speaking than enthusiastic applause erupted in the arena, accompanied by faint shouts of “Fight” and “Feather.”

Today’s first match was between Fight and UH. Fans supporting both teams, as well as those who knew what had happened between them, particularly between Feather and UH, had rushed to the venue, eager to find out the outcome of the match.

They had been waiting here for over an hour, some even longer, and were already fully prepared.

So, before the host had even finished speaking, they responded with thunderous applause.

The applause went on and on.

Ling Suyu curiously craned his neck and was surprised to see a light sign displaying his ID. He froze for a moment, feeling as if he were a celebrity rather than an esports player.

Meanwhile, the five members of UH looked a bit glum, because they knew full well that the applause wasn’t for them—it was for Fight.

Even the few people who supported them were swallowed up by the fiery applause of Fight’s fans, unable to shout out “UH” above the crowd.

—Though if they did shout it, they’d probably get beaten up.

【Looks like you’ve all been waiting a long time for this! Well, without further ado, let’s hear what both teams have to say before the match!】

As the host spoke, he deliberately took a step toward where UH was standing, and the crowd’s cheers suddenly died down.

The host playfully turned around and walked toward Fight, and the cheers immediately grew louder; shouts of “Fight,” “Yumao,” and “Feather” became increasingly clear.

Some people even shouted “osmanthus cake” and “tangyuan.”

Tang Yu’an smiled, while Ling Suyu remained silent.

UH members looked even more grim.

This host was playing with fire!

Unlike Fight, UH’s training regimen didn’t care what the players thought. For the past few days, they hadn’t let the starters touch their phones even once—it was a completely closed-door training camp.

But even without browsing forums or checking Weibo, it wasn’t hard to tell from the audience’s reaction just how much UH was in deep waters.

In fact, precisely because they weren’t allowed to check Weibo, each of them had imagined the situation to be far worse than it actually was—they even thought #UHIsTrash# had hit the top of the trending list.

“UH is trash!”

Look, someone in the crowd was actually shouting it. After losing 0-2 to igame yesterday, if they lost 0-2 to Fight today as well, UH would be completely finished.

The host walked up to Fight’s players with the microphone. Instead of directly calling on Ling Suyu as before, or letting Tang Yu’an snatch the mic, he handed it to Mo Xiaoyun, who was standing farthest away, and gave him a look that seemed to ask, “Do you have anything to say?”

This match could be called the biggest highlight of the entire quarterfinals; he didn’t even mind letting everyone on Fight have their say.

However, Mo Xiaoyun shook his head and tilted his chin to his right, signaling the host to pass the microphone to his teammate.

When facing UH, it wasn’t his place to talk big.



So the host passed the microphone to Dou Jiayan, standing next to Mo Xiaoyun. Unexpectedly, Dou Jiayan took a step directly behind Yu He, placed both hands on Yu He’s shoulders, and adopted a “Don’t give it to me! You can’t see me” expression.

Dou Jiayan usually spoke a lot, but that was only because he’d gotten used to hanging out with Fight. When faced with strangers, especially in public, he’d show his rare shy side.

Tang Yu’an still remembered the scene from their first meeting. The little guy was even more expressionless than Ling Suyu, his hands even trembling slightly as he looked over the contract. His obvious nervousness had made Tang Yu’an feel as if he weren’t signing him to Fight, but rather trafficking him off to some remote mountain village.

But in the end, Dou Jiayan still signed his name on the contract, just as Ling Suyu had once signed his name on the UH contract without a moment’s hesitation.

Young people always thought themselves mature and independent, wanting to decide their own lives, only to end up being counting the money for someone else even while being sold.

At least Yanzi signed with Fight.

Seeing how blatantly he’d refused, the host had no choice but to hand the microphone to Yu He—only to get glared at by him.

Glared at indeed…

Yu He’s icy eyes clearly said: Keep that thing away from me, or I’ll slash you!

And since Dou Jiayan was hiding behind him at that moment, his glare could also be interpreted in another way: “Didn’t you see how you scared Yanzi? How dare you get any closer? Come any closer and I’ll slash you!”

With no other choice, the host handed the microphone to Ling Suyu.

And Ling Suyu took it without a moment’s hesitation.

The host’s eyes nearly welled up with tears—Feather is so kind; he’s an angel!

Unexpectedly, after taking the microphone, Ling Suyu immediately looked up and exchanged a glance with Tang Yu’an beside him, then whispered, “Should you speak, or should I?”

His whisper was amplified by the microphone, and a faint blush of embarrassment spread across his face.

Tang Yu’an stifled a laugh and placed a reassuring hand on his back: “You say what you want to say first. If you feel it’s not enough, I’ll add to it.”

“Okay.” Ling Suyu felt relieved, took the microphone firmly in hand, and looked at the five members of UH across from him.

Though it had been a long time since he’d seen them, those five faces were still deeply etched in his memory, as if his departure from UH had been only yesterday.

However, today, it was time to settle things once and for all.

【Actually, I don’t have much to say. The only thing I want to say is, we will win.】

Ling Suyu had rebutted many people before—he’d fired back at Ma Mingxi during the White Fox Cup, bombarded UH’s manager with calls, and yesterday, he’d given a blunt response to XY’s taunts.

But when faced with UH, the team that had hurt him so deeply, he couldn’t even bring himself to say a single harsh word.

He couldn’t speak as recklessly as Ma Mingxi, something along the lines of, “Hey, isn’t this the team I used to be on? How come you’ve gotten so bad after just a few days? You can’t even beat igame anymore?”

Nor could he speak his mind as bluntly as when he’d snapped back at UH’s manager: “You guys at UH have never treated me like a human being, so in this match, I’ll show you what it’s like to be inhumane!”

He couldn’t even throw out a simple taunt: “It’d be pretty embarrassing for the starters to lose to the benchwarmers, wouldn’t it? I can’t wait to see your reaction after I beat you.”

All he could say was a few words: “We will win.”

Facing a team like UH, any and all words felt hollow and meaningless. Defeating them on the field—completely crushing them—was the only thing Ling Suyu wanted to do.

He didn’t just want to prove his own strength.

He wanted to prove that Fight was a thousand, ten thousand times better than UH!

His declaration was undoubtedly rather “gentle,” but as soon as he finished speaking, the venue erupted in applause no less enthusiastic than when they first took the stage—and to his surprise, there were even sobs mixed in.

Someone sobbed, “Waaah, Feather is so kind! He’s truly an angel! How could UH bear to do this? How could they?”

Someone else remarked admiringly, “ Feather is so gracious! If I were him, I would’ve taken this opportunity to cuss out UH’s ancestors going back eighteen generations!”

Someone shouted excitedly, “We’ve got to win! We absolutely have to win, baobei!”[^1]

Baobei?

Ling Suyu caught the word amid the clamour, and his eyebrow twitched.

For a split second, he thought his parents had also come to the venue.

But… that was impossible.

After Ling Suyu finished speaking, he very thoughtfully handed the microphone to Tang Yu’an standing beside him. Tang Yu’an took it without hesitation and then whipped the atmosphere up to a feverish level: “Since our very own center has spoken, you’d better watch out in this round! Never mind winning the match—even if Yumao wants the stars in the sky, we’ll pluck them down for him! Because he deserves it! Anyone who fails to see Yumao’s worth and dares to disparage him will get what’s coming to them!”

“Aaaah!”

“Tang Yu’an is so cool!”

“Holy crap, that speech was absolutely epic!”

“I’m shipping them again!”

Ling Suyu’s eyes widened slightly as he looked at Tang Yu’an with a hint of surprise.

This was the first time Tang Yu’an had ever spoken such aggressive words so bluntly, without beating around the bush.

Until today, no matter which team he competed against, Tang Yu’an’s words had always been “gentle”—so “gentle” that even when laced with tension, one could still sense his respect for his opponents. It was a challenge issued on the basis of acknowledging their strength.

But today, he made no attempt to hide the hostility, aggression, and contempt in his tone.

He told UH, and everyone in the arena, plain and simple: “UH, you’ve disgraced Feather. I look down on you. I’ll help Feather win this match. There’s no way we’ll lose against you.”

After a brief moment of stunned silence, Ling Suyu couldn’t help but let the corners of his mouth curl upward.

Tang Yu’an, who stood a full head taller than him, looked just like his in-game avatar. Carrying his shield, he said to Ling Suyu in a tone that seemed playful but was actually serious: “I’ll protect you. Even if the top, mid, and jungle all die, you won’t.”

That’s exactly how it was—not just in the game, but in real life, too. He charged into battle for him, drew in and absorbed all the damage for him, and led him to deliver the fatal blow to their enemies.

The applause and screams from the audience came in wave after wave, showing no signs of dying down, and the live chat comments were more lively than in any previous match.

【Damn! Tang Yu’an is just too handsome! If I were Feather, I’d like him, too!】

【Even if it were a star in the sky, if Feather wanted it, I’d pluck it down for him—what kind of love confession is that!】

【Hey, that’s not “I,” it’s “we”!】

【I don’t care, I don’t care! Tang Yu’an truly loves Feather so much!】

【I don’t usually engage in shipping, but Tang Yu’an’s line was just so cool! UH really took advantage of Feather! They’re definitely going to get what’s coming to them!】

【Go, go, go! Press UH to the ground and rub them in it! Let them feel just how strong Feather, the player they looked down upon, really is!】

The five UH starters’ faces grew darker and darker.

They suddenly felt that standing there, they looked less like five esports players and more like five clowns.

And the microphone handed to them by the host felt less like a microphone and more like a hot potato—the kind just pulled out of lava, with sparks still flickering on its surface.

But even knowing how scorching that microphone was, they had to take it. If they didn’t, it would feel as though they’d already lost the match completely before it had even begun.

UH’s captain, Jiang Hao, calmly reached out, calmly took the microphone, and spoke in an equally calm tone: “Making Feather a substitute was the club’s decision; terminating his contract was his own choice. We have nothing to say about this matter.”

With that single sentence, he shifted the blame completely.

A distinct chorus of boos rang out from the crowd, but Jiang Hao didn’t even blink. Standing 1.8 meters tall and expressionless among his teammates, he looked every bit the king surrounded by his courtiers: “There’s no point in endlessly discussing what’s already happened. Instead of living in the past, let’s look to the future.”

He calmly mocked those who were still harping on the issue, including Fight.

The boos from the crowd grew louder, but Jiang Hao didn’t even furrow his brow: “Today, we’ve met here as fellow quarterfinalists. Yumao isn’t here, nor am I, Jiang Hao. All that’s here are UH and Fight. UH won’t lose to Fight—after all, our goal is the championship!”

In an instant, he downplayed the conflict between UH and Feather, shifting everyone’s focus to the quarterfinals match between UH and Fight—and making it sound as though Fight’s goal wasn’t the championship, but merely revenge against UH.

Hearing this, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but chuckle—the kind of amused laugh you get when you realize, “There’s actually someone in this world who’s even more shameless than I am.”

“Shifting the blame and lying through his teeth—is that a UH tradition?” he asked sarcastically.

Ling Suyu shrugged: “Maybe.”

It wasn’t until after he left UH that he realized just how shameless they really were.

Compared to UH, Tang Yu’an was, at most, just a little more thick-skinned.

“I’d love to punch Jiang Hao right in the face!” Dou Jiayan roared.

Yu He shot him a dismissive sideways glance: “Go ahead, then.”

Dou Jiayan immediately backpedalled: “I can’t do that. I’d get suspended just like Jiang Hao!”

Mo Xiaoyun spoke, holding back his anger: “Just beat him up during the match—beat him to a pulp!”

After saying that, Jiang Hao handed the microphone back to the host and even gave a polite nod.

But the host wasn’t buying it. He turned around and handed the microphone back to Tang Yu’an: “Fight, do you have anything else to say in response?”

Tang Yu’an chuckled softly: “Nothing really. The other side’s response was quite clever, but the audience isn’t stupid, so there’s no need for us to say much more—you all agree, right?”

As he spoke, he looked out at the audience.

The audience responded with shrieks of “Aaaah!”

Tang Yu’an looked thoroughly pleased. “See you on the battlefield.”

With that, he casually handed the microphone back to the host.

His response brought the pre-match trash-talking segment to a perfect close. The host couldn’t find any reason to pass the microphone back to UH, so he simply announced the start of the match.

The two teams headed to their respective match rooms. Before putting on his headset, Ling Suyu looked at Tang Yu’an, his eyes sparkling like stars: “You’re so cool!”

Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but chuckle, raising his hand to ruffle Ling Suyu’s hair: “I’ve said all I needed to say. Do your best in this match. If we lose this one, it won’t just be us who’re disappointed—it’ll be all those fans here, too.”

“Mm-hmm!” Ling Suyu nodded earnestly. “I won’t let them down!”

“Mm-hmm~” Tang Yu’an put on his headset and said no more.

He knew that unlike him, who fought only for himself, Ling Suyu fought not only for his own benefit but also for everyone who supported him.

Ling Suyu wanted to live up to everyone’s expectations of him—to live up to Tang Yu’an, who saw his worth; to live up to Fight, who had helped him realize his potential; and to live up to all the netizens who supported him, liked him, and spoke up for him.

Their support wouldn’t become a burden for him. It would only serve as his strength, allowing him to play with even greater determination and constantly surpass his limits.

Tang Yu’an would never become like Ling Suyu—someone who cared so much about what others think of him—but he genuinely admired and liked Ling Suyu for exactly who he was.

Because… a guy like Yumao was really amazing and charismatic, wasn't he?

【Hello everyone, it’s me and ZhiZhi again, here to provide commentary for you today! I wonder if you’ve missed us? I’m commentator Ah-Mu!】

After the players entered the match room, the director switched the live stream feed to the commentators.

“Hello everyone, I’m commentator ZhiZhi!” Although Ah-Mu had already introduced her, ZhiZhi still introduced herself, then said cheerfully, “Oh my, I’ve been looking forward to this match between Fight and UH for so long! The pre-match trash talk from both teams was definitely full of fire, huh! I can’t wait to see them go head-to-head!”

“That’s right! Especially that statement from Fight’s captain, Tang Yu’an—both thrilling and sweet! Come on, let’s all chant together—‘Fight’s bot lane duo is real!’” said Ah-Mu.

“Haha, that’s enough! If you keep teasing Fight’s captain and darling, watch out—the whole Fight team might send you razor blades!” After Zhi Zhi made that sarcastic remark, she began her serious introduction of the two teams: “Fight is a new team that was just formed last year. They made it to the Summer Season semifinals in their very first year, meaning their strength is not to be underestimated! UH has been around for nearly six years, making it to the quarterfinals four years in a row, and last year they even took home the Summer Seasonals’ bronze trophy—their strength is equally formidable! Today’s match is truly a clash of titans; it’s hard to say who will win or lose! Which team do you hope wins, Ah-Mu?”

“Fight, of course!” Faced with this trick question, Ah-Mu answered without hesitation, then explained his reasoning: “Just listen to the cheers from the crowd—it’s clear where the people’s hearts lie. Living in a socialist country, I can’t very well go against the people, can I?”

【Haha… actually, that makes sense! I’m rooting for Fight too!】

With Ah-Mu leading the charge, ZhiZhi, who had initially had some reservations, also firmly declared her stance: “After all, sustained-damage marksmen are forever legendary!”

Who didn’t know that UH’s marksmen preferred burst damage?

Other teams might pick sustained-damage marksmen—especially teams like SMG, which played by their own rules, or XY, which prioritized overall team composition over individual hero strength.

But regarding UH, from the first time they made it to the quarterfinals right up until now, Jiang Hao had never picked a sustained-damage marksman.

So, in the match between Fight and UH, if you were rooting for Fight, you could just shout, “Sustained-damage marksmen are forever the best!”

“Speaking of which, it’s such a shame I couldn’t personally commentate on the Fight vs. XY match yesterday! ” said Ah-Mu. 【It’s only been a day since I last saw Feather, and he’s already racked up more titles—following “Glorious’s #1 Desert Hunter” and “Glorious’s #1 Cat Spirit,” he’s now added “Glorious’s #1 Dark Raven” to the list. I wonder how well he plays his other sustained-damage marksmen; maybe he could combine them all to earn the title of “Glorious’s #1 Sustained-Damage Marksman”!】

“Haha, there are way too many sustained-damage marksmen—Feather could never master them all!” ZhiZhi said, then thought for a moment. “But since sustained-damage marksmen rely on auto-attacks in the late game, even though Feather has only played three so far—four if you count Candy Witch—I have a feeling he wouldn’t be bad at playing other marksmen either!”

“I wonder how many marksmen UH plans to ban?” Ah-Mu asked curiously.

Under his curious gaze, Fight and UH rolled the dice. UH rolled higher, so UH got to ban first, and Fight got to pick first.

Without much hesitation, UH selected the first ban: Phoenix Soul.

A squishy marksman without mobility feared burst-damage mid laners with mobility the most.

Tang Yu’an had seen this coming: “Just as I thought. Yanzi, ban a mid laner you’re not good at—we’ll snag the mid lane with our first pick.”

“Got it~” Dou Jiayan banned Beast Master, but immediately added, “I need to practice Beast Master some more before the Summer Seasonals.”

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow: “Why?”

Dou Jiayan: “Nothing, really. I was just banning and suddenly realized I might still be able to salvage this.”

Ling Suyu had been putting champions who normally wouldn’t make the ban list into the ban slot one by one, yet he couldn’t even play the regular ban-list champions at the level required for the ban slot—how could that work?

Tang Yu’an smiled: “Sure, if you want to practice, go ahead.”

Just then, UH banned their second hero: Harp Demon.

Then, on Fight’s side, they banned another mid-laner that Dou Jiayan wasn’t very good at: Realm God.

At this point, both teams had only one ban slot left.

“Will UH ban Feather’s go-to marksman?” Ah-Mu’s tone carried a hint of nervousness. “Perhaps Cat Spirit or Candy Witch? Dark Raven is also a possibility.”

“I’m guessing Candy Witch,” said ZhiZhi. “UH is a very late-game team, so going up against them with an early-game composition gives Fight a big advantage. Compared to Cat Spirit, UH should find Candy Witch a bit trickier… I guess?”

Before she could finish her sentence—before she could even say the final “I guess”—UH selected their final ban slot: Array Sprite.

“Huh?” Ah-Mu was taken aback.

Since the start of the Spring Season, no matter which team faced Fight, banning marksmen had become routine. They’d never expected there would actually be a team so stubborn as to not ban a single marksman.

UH’s coach had actually considered banning Candy Witch, but he knew that regardless of whether Candy Witch was banned or not, Ling Suyu would pick Cat Spirit first. And if Cat Spirit were banned, there was a high probability he’d pick Candy Witch instead. Since that was the case, it was better not to ban either of them.

The rest of UH didn’t think it was that complicated; they simply felt that Feather wasn’t worth banning for.

“No matter how strong his Cat Spirit is, how could it possibly beat Hao-ge’s Guardian Spirit?!”

“Put pressure on him right from the start! Don’t let him get going!”

“Only Fight would treat his Cat Spirit like a treasure. Don’t they see how pathetic his early-game damage is?!”

“Everyone, shut up. If you don’t want to be proven wrong, don’t underestimate your opponent.” Jiang Hao spoke up, and the rest of UH instantly fell silent.

If anyone else had said that while they were complaining, they would have fired back on the spot. But when Jiang Hao said it, they could only admire how impressive he was—he was absolutely right. No matter who the opponent was, they had to take them seriously. Especially against Fight—they absolutely couldn’t lose!

Over on Fight’s side, seeing that UH hadn’t banned a single marksman, they couldn’t help but let out a sigh.

Tang Yu’an: “Yumao’s really being underestimated, isn’t he?”

Dou Jiayan: “Is UH still deluding themselves?”

Mo Xiaoyun: “They’ll regret this.”

Yu He: “Hah.”

“We won’t go for mid—let’s go for the jungle instead. Ban Tempest Blade and pick Twin Demonblades.” Tang Yu’an made the decision.

“Alright—” Ling Suyu banned Tempest Blade and Yu He locked in Twin Demonblades himself.

“What does Yumao want to play this game?” Yu He asked after locking in his pick.

Ling Suyu didn’t even pause to think: “Cat Spirit!”

Yu He chuckled: “Right, I shouldn’t have asked.”

“Sounds good. Let’s settle things with UH using Cat Spirit!” Tang Yu’an said, his mouse already highlighting Spider Queen from the hero list.

He’d been thinking that even though UH was crazy, their captain was calm enough—maybe they’d be like XY, claiming they wouldn’t ban Cat Spirit, only to ban her in the very first game.

As it turned out, UH’s captain was calm, but arrogant.

Were you really that reluctant to ban the hero Ling Suyu excelled at?

Well then, just wait to get crushed by that very hero!
