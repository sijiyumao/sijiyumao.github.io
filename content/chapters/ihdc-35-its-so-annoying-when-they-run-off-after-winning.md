With only a half-hour break, it was impossible to thoroughly review all five games.

Fortunately, Fight only lost the last one, so they only needed to focus on reviewing that final match.

Chen Jie had been watching the entire match on his laptop and marked all the key points for review, so he went through them quickly.

“Here, Yanzi, I won’t even go over such an obvious mistake as wasting your ultimate,so long as you don’t do that in an actual match. But why were you chasing people down when you hadn’t even cleared your minions? Didn’t you see that your teammates weren’t following you?”

“I was distracted after the fight…” Dou Jiayan said, sounding aggrieved.

“Tang Yu’an, you didn’t even remind him.” Chen Jie took the opportunity to call out the captain.

“I was distracted too,” Tang Yu’an shrugged.

Chen Jie rolled his eyes, not buying it for a second.

“I’m serious,” Tang Yu’an blinked earnestly.

Ling Suyu, who was standing and watching nearby, blinked as well.

In that final match, Tang Yu’an had indeed spoken much less. Although he still gave commands, he barely cracked any jokes. He seemed to be operating on pure muscle memory, his mind seemingly elsewhere.

Yet his gameplay remained top-notch. He blocked every bit of damage Ling Suyu needed protection from. Among other things, he’d never missed a single control skill, consistently landing his effects. His game sense was always sharp; he knew exactly when to push forward and when to retreat, and he’d even remind teammates with weaker awareness at the right moments.

But…occasionally he’d forget to remind them, or simply not notice his teammates’ situations at all. He really did seem a bit out of it.

“How should I put this,” Tang Yu’an tried to defend on the team’s behalf, “We won four games in a row. It got boring and ended up just feeling like ranked practice. In ranked, the opponents are mostly random players. You tell yourself to take it seriously, but your hands have a mind of their own. You don’t even realize it until halfway through the game: ‘Wait, the enemy is YJ? It’s only been a few months, so how did they get this bad?’ And then…we paid the price for getting too cocky.”

Dou Jiayan: “Exactly, exactly!”

Chen Jie: “Bullshit!”

Ling Suyu cleared his throat, suppressing a laugh that nearly slipped out.

There wasn’t much to say about the last game. It was just that some of them, or even all of them, were a bit worn out. Mental fatigue had set in, causing them to stop thinking and slip into the “sleepwalking” playstyle that often surfaced during training.

However, their opponents weren’t just random players—they were YJ.

Even if YJ was “sleepwalking” too, their skill level was undeniable. Going head-to-head with a team that had made it to the semifinals for several consecutive seasons, and even represented the country at the World Finals, based solely on muscle memory, it was only natural for Fight to lose.

Though it was natural, the feeling of resentment remained.

Ling Suyu had been feeling a bit down, but hearing Tang Yu’an’s words nearly made him lose his composure.

Chen Jie was at his wits’ end with Tang Yu’an: “What’s gotten into you? Back when you used to lead our debriefs, you were harsher than anyone else. It’s one thing not to call them out today, but now you’re actually siding with them and turning on me?”

Tang Yu’an: “Well…we’re going to keep playing in half an hour. If I talk them into a corner now, how are we supposed to play? Let’s just go through the motions with a few comments now, and do a proper debrief tonight.”

Chen Jie’s lips twitched: “You’re the one who said we should review the match, and now you’re the one saying ‘just go through the motions.’ You’ve really got some nerve.”

“What’s there to be embarrassed about?” Tang Yu’an didn’t look the least bit embarrassed.

“It’s not like I’m the one doing the reviewing—”

Chen Jie rolled his eyes in exasperation. Just as Ye Xiran walked in, he snatched the stack of clothes from Ye Xiran’s hands and flung them at Tang Yu’an’s face!

“Ouch!” Tang Yu’an feigned a cry of pain, then suddenly seemed to realize something and hurriedly pulled the clothes off his face. “It’s fine if you hit me, but don’t ruin Feather’s clothes!”

“Huh? My clothes?” Ling Suyu, suddenly called out, looked completely bewildered.

Chen Jie sighed and said in a resigned tone, “Then just give them to him.”

So Tang Yu’an casually shoved the stack of clothes into Ling Suyu’s hands: “Fight’s team uniforms, you can give them back if you don’t want them?”

“I want them! ” Ling Suyu clutched the clothes tightly, refusing to let go.

Then, realizing Tang Yu’an wasn’t going to actually take them back, he finally brought them up to his eyes to examine them carefully.

There were four sets of clothes in his hands: short-sleeved shirts, long-sleeved shirts, a jacket, and a down coat. All were a fiery, scorching red.

They were neatly folded into square piles inside the plastic packaging, clearly brand-new.

Tang Yu’an propped his head up with one hand, watching Ling Suyu’s happy expression with delight.

Although Ling Suyu’s facial expression didn’t change much, the corners of his mouth that he couldn’t help but turn up, as well as the sparkle in his eyes, clearly expressed his excitement and satisfaction at that moment.

“Want to try them on right now?” Tang Yu’an suggested.

“Huh?” Ling Suyu’s expression suddenly turned a bit bewildered. “Now?”

“You’ve got to try on your new clothes, right? If the shirts are too much trouble, just try on the jacket first.” As he spoke, Tang Yu’an snatched one of the packages from right under Ling Suyu’s nose, opened it with lightning speed, pulled out the jacket, stood up, and gave it a shake. “Stand up.”

Ling Suyu stood up in a daze, and then, with a swish, Tang Yu’an draped the jacket over his shoulders.

The jacket had no heat of its own, but its vibrant color gave Ling Suyu the fleeting illusion that flames were enveloping him, and he was instantly wrapped in a wave of warmth.

He didn’t know how he managed to slip his arms into the sleeves; all he knew was that after he’d put the jacket on, Tang Yu’an was already nudging him toward the full-length mirror in the corner of the hall.

Ling Suyu stared blankly at his reflection in the mirror. It was clearly still him, yet he seemed like a completely different person.

As someone with social anxiety, he didn’t like drawing too much attention to himself, so his clothes were always plain, in either white or black.

The UH team uniform was blue, not exactly a standout color either.

Fight’s uniform was the first time Ling Suyu had worn such a fiery red.

It wasn’t entirely red, but a mix of black and red. Within the black section on the left chest was a bright red flame, Fight’s logo.

The black portion at the hem was also adorned with red flame patterns.

Cool and stylish, yet not gaudy.

Vibrant, yet not garish.

Fiery, commanding, brimming with passion, and thrilling.

After a brief moment of stunned silence, Ling Suyu’s lips curled upward uncontrollably, and he said three words from the bottom of his heart: “I like it.”

“What? Speak up.” Tang Yu’an leaned in.

“I said, I really like this…”

“You like me?”

“I really like this team uniform!” Ling Suyu was forced to raise his voice.

Tang Yu’an chuckled and softened his voice: “I’m glad you do—”

The other members of Fight watched the two of them “flirt” in silence.

Yu He pondered for a moment before suddenly saying, “Was I this annoying when I was pursuing my girlfriend?”

Chen Jie: “To be honest, you’re pretty much the same.”

Yu He: “……”

Dou Jiayan: “Wait, what? HeHe, you have a girlfriend? Why are you already dating at 18?”

Chen Jie: “He doesn’t have an actual girlfriend. She’s just some girl he hooked up with in-game.”

Dou Jiayan: “So that’s the kind of guy you are, HeHe! How’d you do it? Teach me!”

Yu He: “No chance. She plays the same position as you.”

Dou Jiayan: “Who said I was going to hit on your girlfriend! Can’t I just hook up with someone else?”

Mo Xiaoyun: “Girlfriend, huh… Actually, I’ve always wanted a pretty female manager, but the captain insisted on a guy, saying he didn’t want us getting distracted.”

Ye Xiran: “Sorry I’m not a pretty woman, then.”

They weren’t speaking very loudly, but since they were all in the same room, Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu couldn’t help but overhear them.

Tang Yu’an glanced over helplessly: “What on earth are you guys talking about? Are we going to review the game or not?”

Ling Suyu, however, looked completely bewildered. He glanced at Tang Yu’an, then at the other teammates. He wanted to ask but didn’t dare, hoping someone would explain it to him, but no one did.

What did they mean?

Why had the conversation suddenly turned to “girlfriends”?

He thought back to the question Tang Yu’an had let slip earlier: “Do you like me?”

Ling Suyu’s face flushed instantly, and he hurriedly took off his jacket: “It’s a bit warm in here…”

But just as he’d taken off his jacket, before he’d even had a chance to fold it neatly, Tang Yu’an spoke again: “Feather, look at this.”

“Hm?” Ling Suyu looked in the direction he was pointing, puzzled. He glanced at the back of the jacket he was holding and saw that in a spot no one could see when it was worn, the three characters “Ling Suyu” were clearly embroidered in gold thread on the black fabric.

Ling Suyu: !!

Ling Suyu thought he must be seeing things. He picked up the shirt and held it close to his eyes in disbelief, looking as if he were determined to stare a hole right through it.

Only then was he certain that his name was indeed embroidered on the back of the collar!

—This meant the uniform was custom-made! Fight had it made just for him!

UH’s uniforms were mass-produced, one-size-fits-all.

When Ling Suyu left, he’d considered taking the uniform with him, but in the end, he didn’t.

He left his keyboard, mouse, and uniform all at UH, in the room that had once belonged to him.

Because those things were his, but they also weren’t his.

Ling Suyu would never take anything that didn’t fully belong to him without someone else’s permission.

So, aside from his gaming account, he didn’t take a single thing related to UH with him.

But Fight’s jersey had his name printed on it.

In this way, Fight was clearly telling him: this was his, and his alone.

Ling Suyu’s eyes welled up on the spot, but thinking of the upcoming practice match, he forced himself to hold back. Then, in front of everyone on Fight, he gave Tang Yu’an a hug:

“Thank you.”

Tang Yu’an froze for a moment.

The rest of Fight froze as well.

But before they could read too much into it, Ling Suyu had already let go of Tang Yu’an and returned to his seat. He carefully and meticulously folded the jacket in his hands, slipped it back into the wrapping paper, and then, clutching the entire set of custom-made team uniforms, walked unhurriedly toward the stairs, heading up to his room.

Everyone in the hall exchanged glances, taking a long moment to process what had just happened.

Dou Jiayan: “Feather…doesn’t actually have a crush on the captain, does he?”

“No way,” Chen Jie said with absolute certainty. “He was just excited. Don’t you throw yourself at me when you’re excited?”

Dou Jiayan: “That much sense.”

“But Captain must be over the moon, right?” Yu He said.

Tang Yu’an raised a hand to rub his nose and cleared his throat.

He certainly wasn’t going to mention that back when the New Year’s holiday hadn’t even ended yet, Ling Suyu had even curled up in his arms and cried. Today was just a hug— what’s the big deal?

Mmm…but he was still pretty happy about it.

Ling Suyu went upstairs to put his clothes away. After collecting his thoughts, he came back downstairs to continue the post-game analysis.

This time, Chen Jie actually offered him some feedback: “Feather, you shouldn’t have cleared the minions at that moment. You should’ve rushed into the jungle to support Yu He right away. That way, not only would he have survived, but you guys could’ve secured two kills.”

Ling Suyu recalled the situation: “I checked on the minimap, and there was definitely a chance to save him. But I wasn’t sure where the enemy jungler was, and since the captain hadn’t respawned yet, I didn’t dare go alone.”

“If he’d been playing Spirit Cat or Desert Hunter, he probably would’ve gone,” Tang Yu’an cut to the chase. “But he was playing Mobile Artillery, so why would he go without his ultimate?”

“Don’t interrupt,” Chen Jie said. He no longer trusted Tang Yu’an’s attempt at explaining.

The guy had completely lost his head over this sudden romance and wasn’t worth relying on.

Although what he said wasn’t entirely without merit, Mobile Artillery had its own strengths:

“Feather, have you practiced with Mobile Artillery before? His skill damage is higher than you think. Even if the jungler really came to gank you, with the gold you had at that point, you’d have a chance to secure a counter-kill.”

“Yeah,” Ling Suyu admitted. “Mobile Artillery plays more like a mid-laner than a marksman, so I usually only pick him when the marksman role is taken. In that last game, the enemy mid-laner picked Array Sprite, and I was worried I wouldn’t be able to land my auto-attacks on him, so I went with Mobile Artillery. After all… the captain won’t let me use Cat Spirit or Dark Raven.”

As he said that last part, Ling Suyu’s tone even carried a hint of grievance.

Chen Jie looked at Tang Yu’an.

Tang Yu’an: ?

“Come on, isn’t it normal to hold back a bit before a formal match?” Tang Yu’an said in an even more aggrieved tone. “I didn’t even use my best support hero, Skeletal Empress.”

“So, in a situation where you’re not planning to use Cat Spirit or Dark Raven, and Desert Hunter’s been banned, how do you plan to play from here on out?” Chen Jie asked the two.

In 《Glorious Heroes》, there were over 20 marksmen in total, with sustained damage and burst damage types each making up roughly half. However, among the sustained damage marksmen, only two had mobility: Cat Spirit and Desert Hunter.

Dark Raven’s mobility comes from its ultimate ability, so it didn’t count.

Looking at Ling Suyu’s pick history, it was clear he excelled at sustained-damage marksmen with mobility. He’d already played Cat Spirit and Desert Hunter to the top of the server rankings, and Dark Raven was close behind.

However, while Ling Suyu wasn’t bad with other marksmen, he largely relied on the heroes’

inherent strength. Once he fell behind in gold, he became virtually invisible in terms of impact.

Although these three heroes are enough for him for the Spring Seasonals, no one knew if they’d still be viable next patch, so this was an issue that needed to be resolved sooner or later.

Perhaps today’s practice match against YJ was the perfect opportunity.

With that in mind, Chen Jie ran through every marksman he could think of in his head, before suddenly remembering a certain hero: “Feather, do you know how to play Candy Witch?”

Ling Suyu paused for a moment: “Well, I do know…”

But wasn’t that a mid-laner?!

“Alright, go adjust your build right now. Try Candy Witch in the next game, and Yanzi, you’ll play Mobile Artillery.” Chen Jie gave the order directly.

Tang Yuan raised an eyebrow: “That’s actually a good idea.”

Candy Witch was a magic-based mid laner, but she was also the kind who chased enemies down with basic attacks. Her build required both magic damage and attack speed. In fact, aside from dealing magic-based damage, her playstyle wasn’t much different from that of a sustained-damage marksman.

Not to mention casual matches, even in last season’s professional leagues, some teams had used her in the bot lane.

But there was a catch: since she dealt magic damage, if you picked her for the bot lane, you couldn’t have another magic-damage champion in the mid lane, so as to balance the damage types. You’d have to pick an AD—that is, a physical-damage champion.

And when it comes to ADs capable of poking, even after combing through all 100-plus heroes in《Glorious Heroes》, there’s only one that fully meets the requirements:

Mobile Artillery.

Upon hearing this “bad news,” Dou Jiayan let out a wail: “Glorious’s mid-lane heroes are more than double the number of marksmen, right? Aren’t there over forty of them? I’m already practicing six heroes at the same time, if I add one more, I’ll die!”

Chen Jie completely ignored his cries: “Build burst damage on Mobile Artillery instead of attack speed; just make sure the ultimate deals damage. Go adjust your build right now. ”

“Yes, yes, yes.” Dou Jiayan reluctantly went to do as he was told.

Ling Suyu followed him back to his computer, clicked on the details page for the Candy Witch champion, and looked at her sitting on her wand in a gorgeous puffy skirt. He reviewed her skill descriptions and blinked.

It seemed promising; maybe he really could master her.

Tang Yu’an didn’t follow him right away. Instead, he looked at Chen Jie, who was still watching the match replay, and smiled. “You can’t play super well, but your theoretical knowledge sure is getting richer and richer, isn’t it?”

Chen Jie glared at him. “I just said I’m retiring, and now you’re not even going to pretend anymore, are you? You used to say that no matter how bad my skills were, they were still pro-level, but now you’re just saying I’m no good?”

Tang Yu’an cleared his throat nervously. “Your skills being at a professional level doesn’t contradict me saying you’re not playing well…Even if you play badly, you’re still a guy who made it to the semifinals. How bad could you really be?” He realized halfway through his explanation, “Wait, I was complimenting you! Why’d you have to take it the wrong way?”

“I don’t feel like wasting words on you. Get lost.” Chen Jie said, starting to shoo him away.

Tang Yu’an dodged with a laugh and entered the practice room.

Soon, the half-hour break ended, and the sixth practice match between Fight and YJ began.

After five rounds of adjustments, both teams’ ban lists were mostly set, so they quickly wrapped up that phase and moved on to hero selection.

Then, on Fight’s side, they consecutively locked Mobile Artillery and Candy Witch.

YJ: ?

“Swallow’s going Candy Witch with that reckless playstyle?”

“Feather’s using Mobile Artillery again? Is he trying to practice this hero?”

Once the game actually started, seeing Ling Suyu pick Candy Witch for the bottom lane and Dou Jiayan pick Mobile Artillery for the mid lane…

YJ: ??

This was the first time Fight had used this lineup, so YJ was on high alert.

“Let’s play this one seriously,” said YJ’s captain, Cheng Cheng. “We crushed them in the last game, so they might have brought out their trump card.”

“Yeah.” His teammates nodded solemnly, all on high alert.

Then, at 5 minutes, Fight’s mid lane collapsed.

At 8 minutes, Fight’s bot lane collapsed.

At 10 minutes, Fight pulled off a 4-for-0 teamfight, but it was all for naught; YJ secured the Dragon God.

At 15 minutes, the game ended, and YJ won.

YJ: 0.0; Who am I? Where am I? What am I doing?

How should they put it... it felt like picking up a cannon and blasting a mosquito to death.

Was this Fight’s secret weapon?

“Secret weapon, my ass!”

Ma Mingxi let out a sarcastic remark right there in the room:

YJ-Neigh: Did you guys go drinking during the break?

“Wow! This guy really takes every chance he gets to provoke people! How can he be so annoying?” Dou Jiayan couldn’t take it anymore and fired back on the spot.

Fight-Swallow: Yeah, it was pretty good. Want a pint yourself?

“Pfft—”

This response was something Ling Suyu hadn’t expected. Fight as a whole seemed to have a pretty good sense of humour.

In the 7th match, he and Dou Jiayan still went with Candy Witch and Mobile Artillery.

After playing a round and getting a feel for the game, their mistakes decreased significantly, and their skill hit-rates improved.

So, although YJ won this round, it wasn’t a walk in the park. If they hadn’t snagged the Dragon God three times in a row and successfully locked down Dou Jiayan’s Mobile Artillery to interrupt his ultimate, they never would have won the final team fight.

But what was worth celebrating about this game was that they actually managed to secure a kill on Feather!

Since he hadn’t anticipated Dou Jiayan’s positioning mistake that led to his ultimate being interrupted, Ling Suyu miscalculated the damage output on both sides and missed his chance to escape. His Candy Witch was instantly taken down by the enemy jungler’s combo.

Hmm…ultimately, it boiled down to a lack of hero mastery. But hey, it was just practice.

Losing wasn’t embarrassing, and dying even less so.

It was just that the smug look on the enemy’s face was a bit infuriating.

YJ-Neigh: Yo, isn’t that Feather lying on the ground? What happened to the “Phantom Marksman” with zero deaths? Cheap talk!

Ling Suyu resisted the urge to roll his eyes and replied with a single word.

Fight-Feather: Again.

By the way, what the hell was a “Phantom Marksman” anyway!

Game 8: Ling Suyu channeled his frustration into killing intent. He moved with agility, used his dashes strategically, chased enemies down, and finished them off with his ultimate, managing to secure a pentakill with Tang Yu’an’s Abyssal Lord providing full-coverage protection!

Dou Jiayan’s Mobile Artillery was still struggling.

But who cares?

Ling Suyu racked up the highest damage of the entire match, and he got his share too!

“Holy sh*t!” Dou Jiayan, who’d been trailing right behind him yet couldn’t land a single hit, looked at him as if he were a god. “How’d you do that?!”

It had only been three games!

Still riding the wave of his frustration buff, Ling Suyu coldly replied, “We can lose the match, but Ma Mingxi must die!”

“Hahaha!” This was the first time Tang Yu’an had ever seen Ling Suyu look so menacing.

Thinking of his usual soft and cute demeanor, he couldn’t help but laugh out loud. “Ma Mingxi’s biggest failure in life is that he’s managed to piss off our little Feather time and time again—” He paused, then continued, “If we win this game, we’ll call it a day on training. Let’s drive them crazy!”

And so, they won this match. Although there were still many issues waiting to be resolved, those could easily be addressed during regular training. There was no need to reveal their trump card to YJ just yet.

Back in their room, the members of Fight bid farewell to YJ one by one.

Fight-Yuan: It’s getting pretty late. That’s all for today. Good night, everyone. That match just now was really fun— Fight-Swallow: Bye for now, see you in the Spring Seasonals! That match just now was really fun— Fight-Crane: That was a really great game just now. Good night, everyone, and sweet dreams.

Fight-Cloud: I had a lot of fun playing today. See you in the Spring Seasonals.

Fight-Feather: Good night, sweet dreams.

Fight’s members lined up to say their goodbyes, leaving YJ no chance to respond, and one by one, they fled the room as if escaping.

YJ’s members were left in the lobby, furious and helpless!

Ma Mingxi: “Damn it! Those bastards from Fight! If you’re good enough to win, then have the guts not to run away!”

Cheng Cheng: “I told you not to jinx it.”

Ma Mingxi: ?

“When did I jinx it?!”

Cheng Cheng: “Oh, you didn’t jinx it. You just had a big mouth and provoked Feather.”

Xiao Hong: “Damn it, it’s so annoying when they run off after winning! If we don’t crush them in the Spring Seasonals, I’ll write my name backwards!”

Cheng Cheng: ??

“You’re joining in too?!”

Gu Ruicong: “Alright, let’s review the game. We’ll get our revenge in the Spring Seasonals.”

Lin Hao: “Yeah, we’ll show them what’s what in the Spring Seasonals!”
