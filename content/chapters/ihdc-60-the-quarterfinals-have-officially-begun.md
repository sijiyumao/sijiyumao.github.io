The drawing ceremony was still underway, and next up was YJ, last Summer Season’s runner-up.

【Let’s give them a warm round of applause!】

Accompanied by the host’s still-enthusiastic voice, YJ’s starting lineup took the stage wearing their refreshing green uniforms.

YJ’s club was also based in City B, and when they took the stage, they received applause and cheers that rivaled those for Fight.

First to introduce himself was their captain, Cheng Cheng, followed immediately by marksman Ma Mingxi, who had faced off against Ling Suyu several times.

Ma Mingxi was very self-centered and hot-tempered online, but since he was quite good-looking in person, he had plenty of haters as well as loyal fans.

As he introduced himself, the crowd responded with both screams and boos. Hearing the boos, he fired back on the spot: “What are you booing for? If you’ve got the guts, come on stage and fight me!”

No sooner had he said that than the microphone was snatched away by Captain Cheng Cheng, standing right next to him: “Sorry, our marksman’s having one of his usual outbursts. Please bear with him!”

“Ma Mingxi’s hilarious!” Dou Jiayan laughed at his antics. “He really does just do his own thing no matter how much people flame him!”

“That’s what you call ‘being true to himself,’” Tang Yu’an replied. “Take UH, for example—they didn’t dare make a peep when they got booed.”

While the captains from other teams handled the draw, when it was YJ’s turn, they pushed Ma Mingxi forward.

Ma Mingxi was already itching to get started, so after being given a nudge, he happily made his way to the draw podium.

There were only two balls left in the bucket—either number 3 or number 7.

If they drew number 3, they’d face SMG on the first day.

If they drew number 7, they’d face BigC on the first day.

Although both teams were among the top eight, neither was considered a strong opponent for YJ. At least, they weren’t as formidable as Winne or Fight, who would put immense pressure on them. So, either number would do.

But Ma Mingxi still hesitated for a long time before finally selecting a ball, glancing at it, and handing it to the host with a calm expression.

The host took it, looked at it, and called out the number: “Number 3!”

“Holy crap!” Dou Jiayan exclaimed, not because YJ had drawn number 3, but because—

“What did I say? I said if Winne drew number 7, Da Diao’s reaction would be even more spectacular—and now Winne really did get number 7?! Did I just predict the future?”

“Poor Da Diao,” Mo Xiaoyun sighed.

“Having to face the championship team on the very first day, that’s super rough,” Tang Yu’an said, though he seemed in high spirits.

—After all, Fight wasn’t the one going up against Winne.

But even if they were to face Winne, Fight wasn’t afraid; on the contrary, they were curious to see just how well they could hold their own against Winne now.

In last season’s BPL Global Finals, each country was allotted two spots. The two Chinese teams that secured those spots were Winne, the Summer Season champions, and YJ, who ranked second overall in the domestic standings—just behind Winne—and were also the Summer Season runners-up.

Both teams advanced smoothly to the quarterfinals, but in the end, only Winne moved on to the semifinals and finals. In the finals, they came back from two games down to win three in a row, claiming last year’s Global Finals championship.

It was fair to say that while Winne and YJ were both top contenders for the title domestically, on the global stage, there was still a significant gap in strength between the two teams.

Before the quarterfinals began, Ye Xiran had tried to help arrange a practice match between Fight and Winne, but the request was denied on the grounds that Winne had already scheduled a practice match with an international team.

In all fairness, the international team had made the request first, and it was understandable if Winne couldn’t spare the time to practice with Fight.

Still, it left Fight the impression that Winne had underestimated them, which left them feeling a little annoyed.

【Let’s give a huge round of applause to welcome the final team taking the stage for the draw! They are the champions of last year’s Summer Season, and also last year’s World Champions—Winne!】

Amid a wave of applause and cheers that grew louder and louder, Winne’s players took the stage.

Wearing bright yellow uniforms, they stood under the spotlights as undoubtedly the most eye-catching presence in the entire arena.

Although only one numbered ball remained, since this was a “ceremony,” it certainly had to feel ceremonial.

Just like the previous teams, they introduced themselves one by one. Then the captain walked to the draw table, pulled the last ball out of the bucket, and the host announced the number on the ball, just as before: “Number 7!”

As the host spoke, every instance of “7” on the big screen was replaced with “Winne.”

And with that, the schedule for the quarterfinals was finalized.

The theme song for Glorious began sounding in the arena, and a BPL promotional video started playing on the big screen, featuring team videos recorded by each of the eight teams.

Amid the raucous noise of the crowd, Tang Yu’an suddenly leaned in close to Ling Suyu and asked, “Yumao, are you happy?”

Ling Suyu paused for a moment, turning to look at him in confusion, unsure why he’d suddenly asked that, but he answered anyway: “I’m happy.”

“That’s good~” Tang Yu’an breathed a sigh of relief and smiled slightly. “I was worried you wouldn’t like such a noisy environment.”

So that was why.

Ling Suyu looked at the smile on his face and couldn’t stop his lips from curving upwards: “It’s okay. As long as I’m with you guys, I’m happy.”

“With me? Got it.” Tang Yu’an nodded to himself.

Ling Suyu hesitated, then gave a helpless smile and let it go.

After the draw ceremony, the eight teams returned to their respective accommodations to begin their final preparations.

To meet the needs of the esports players, each suite at the hotel was equipped with at least six computers, allowing the players to continue training right there.

But with the competition just a day away, they had to manage their training intensity carefully. It wouldn’t do to let overtraining affect tomorrow’s match.

Moreover, now that the schedule was out, the top priority was, of course, to analyze tomorrow’s opponent.

“XY hasn’t been in top form this season,” Chen Jie analyzed. “But even so, we can’t let our guards down, so let’s go with Cat Spirit for the first match.”

“Alright,” Ling Suyu replied, only too happy to agree.

“If Cat Spirit gets banned, we’ll go with Candy Witch,” Chen Jie added.

They finished their final group stage match on the 18th. The 19th to the 25th, a full week, was more than enough time for Dou Jiayan to master Mobile Artillery.

Right now, although Dou Jiayan’s Mobile Artillery wasn’t quite at the level where it could carry the team, it was at least above the professional player benchmark and was no worse than Tang Yu’an’s Mobile Artillery.

As a result, Candy Witch and Mobile Artillery, champions they had previously been afraid to pick, once again became viable options for competing against the other teams in the quarterfinals.

On the other hand, Desert Hunter was played too often in the group stage, and igame had figured out how to counter it, so they needed to be cautious about picking it going forward.

“If Cat Spirit and Candy Witch are both banned, we’ll pick Dark Raven.” Chen Jie said, “Although there are still the semifinals and finals after the quarterfinals, it’ll all be for nothing if we can’t even make it to the semifinals. So there’s no need to hold back anymore. We’ll use whatever’s strongest.”

“Got it,” Ling Suyu replied obediently.

Chen Jie looked at Tang Yu’an.

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow: “Why are you looking at me?”

Chen Jie: “Yumao’s going with Dark Raven—you don’t have any objections, do you?”

“Of course not,” Tang Yu’an blurted out.

Chen Jie nodded: “As long as you don’t regret it.”

Ling Suyu looked completely bewildered, unsure why Chen Jie had said that.

But Tang Yu’an knew that it was most likely because of that post.

At the start of the season, when he and Ling Suyu were practicing duo queues using alt accounts, they pushed too hard, too fast. People noticed something was off, took screenshots, and posted them on the forum. Someone on the forum uncovered their real identities, so to hide their tracks, they formed a couple bond and pretended to be a real-life couple playing the game.

During that time, Tang Yu’an kept calling him “hubby,” which cured a large chunk of Ling Suyu’s social anxiety.

Later, as more players reached the higher ranks and they were lost in the crowd, they stopped pretending.

But that post still existed, and every now and then someone would pop up to ask, “So are osmanthus cake and tangyuan actually Feather and Tang Yu’an?”

Then someone would reply, “We’ll know if Feather uses Dark Raven in the quarterfinals.”

Tang Yu’an had no doubt that if Ling Suyu used the hero Dark Raven in the quarterfinal ranking matches, that thread would instantly be pushed to the front page, and people would surely mock them for pretending to be a couple just to hide their go-to hero.

But Tang Yu’an didn’t care at all.

So what if he and Ling Suyu pretended to be a couple?

Who knows? Maybe they’d actually end up together someday?

But mainly, he didn’t really care what people online thought of him anyway. As for Ling Suyu, he could just say he was forced into it when the time came. After all, he wasn’t the one constantly calling the other “husband,” and this tactic of “killing 1000 enemy soldiers and losing 800 of your own” certainly wasn’t his idea.

“Let me see if there’s anything else I need to remind you guys about.” Chen Jie flipped through all the XY-related videos in his folder. Realizing he’d pretty much covered everything, he offered one final warning: “This is a team that gets better as they play on. Don’t get too cocky just because you scored one point—watch out or you’ll get bit!”

“Don’t worry, that won’t happen~” Tang Yu’an assured him. “The match right before that will be UH and igame. We don’t want to make fools of ourselves in front of them, right, Yumao?”

“Mm-hmm.” Ling Suyu replied earnestly. “We won’t give the other team a single point.”

“Woah, I think we’ve got this in the bag!” Upon hearing Ling Suyu’s words, Dou Jiayan instantly felt as if they’d already won the match.

“Well said—don’t give the opponent a single point. Let’s win 2-0!” Mo Xiaoyun chimed in.

Yu He also gave a soft “Mm-hmm.”

The next day, the 13th BPL Spring Quarterfinals officially began!

The first match was between igame and UH.

Since the match started at 1:00 p.m., while the Fight vs. XY match began at 7:00 p.m., Fight was still at their hotel when igame and UH were playing and had not yet left for the venue.

They watched the livestream of the match right there on their hotel computers.

Before the match began, as usual, there was a segment where both teams exchanged “greetings.”

The host first handed the microphone to igame.

igame’s captain, Ye Xunjie, took the microphone, but before he could even speak, the comment stream was already flooding with messages:

【This is intense!】

【I can already smell the gunpowder!】

Someone who saw the comments wondered why they were saying that.

Soon, another comment replied: “Xunjie directly bashed UH on Feather’s behalf during the post-match interview after the group stages!”

【Wow! As expected of a team that has a love-hate relationship with Fight! ‘Only I can pick on Fight’?】

【Damn, I ship it!】

Ye Xunjie’s remarks certainly didn’t disappoint the commenters. He said: “During the group stage, Fight’s marksman, Feather, put immense pressure on us at igame and ultimately defeated us. For such an outstanding player to be relegated to the bench at UH makes us incredibly excited for this match. We believe UH’s starting lineup won’t be any worse than Feather, so we’ll definitely give it 200% and take today’s match very seriously!”

Hearing this, the comments burst into laughter.

【Hahaha, nice one!】

【Open praise, hidden dig—insulting people without using a single swear word. Brilliant! Absolutely brilliant!】

【I’m really starting to like Xunjie more and more! How does he manage to be so handsome, so skilled, and so satisfying to watch when he takes a shot at someone?】

It wasn’t just the commenters—Fight’s members were amused too.

Dou Jiayan: “Wow, has it become some kind of tradition now to help Yumao bash UH?”

Chen Jie: “It’s definitely a surefire way to get attention, but Ye Xunjie is a genuine guy. He probably actually meant what he said.”

Mo Xiaoyun: “What a long speech. He must’ve planned it all out before the match started, right? He probably even drafted several versions.”

Tang Yu’an: “Isn’t that great? UH deserves to be called out. The more people who call them out, the more fun we have~”

Ling Suyu smiled but didn’t say anything.

Although it was a bit awkward to be constantly brought up, it was impossible not to be happy when someone was helping him take a dig at UH.

If, when he left UH, he still harbored a shred of nostalgia and reluctance, then now, the stronger his sense of belonging to Fight became, the more he resented UH.

It was the contrast that stings.

The better Fight treated him, the more it made UH look like a despicable team who didn’t treat him like a human being.

He simply couldn’t muster any feelings for a UH like that.

In the end, the past five years had all been wasted away.

After igame made their statement, the host handed the microphone to UH and asked how they planned to respond.

Of the five starters on UH’s side, everyone except Jiang Hao seemed reluctant to take the microphone—they really didn’t want it.

So in the end, Jiang Hao took the hot potato and replied coolly, “Yes, Fight is strong, but today, your opponent is us, UH. If you treat us like Fight, you’ll pay the price.”

【Damn!】

Viewers watching the livestream all looked stunned upon hearing Jiang Hao’s response.

【Is UH implying they’re stronger than Fight? Where’d they get the balls?】

【UH has some serious nerve! If it weren’t for Chen Jie’s hand injury last year, do they think they could’ve beaten Fight?!】

【And isn’t he mimicking what Tang Yu’an said during the group stage? It sounds disgusting coming from him!】

【Go, igame! Crush UH!】

Fight hadn’t expected Jiang Hao to respond like this either. Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow in surprise: “Whoa, they’re pretty bold, aren’t they? Worried we won’t beat them hard enough tomorrow, are you?”

Dou Jiayan immediately rolled up his sleeves: “igame, step back! We’ll take ‘em!”

“Calm down!” Chen Jie grabbed him by the back of his collar. “Our first match tomorrow is against them—we’ll give it our all then. Today’s opponent is XY! I knew I shouldn’t have let you guys watch the game. Get out there and practice!”

“Okay, okay, I was wrong. I’ll watch quietly.” The moment Dou Jiayan heard the words “get back to practice,” he instantly became well-behaved and slowly rolled his sleeves back down.

Ling Suyu watched his movements without reacting.

But once he was done, he couldn’t help but smile before saying, “It’s fine. The tougher the pre-match trash talk, the harder the comeuppance afterward.”

He didn’t like making trash talk precisely because he didn’t want to feel pressure during the match.

Because making trash talk was like planting a flag—you never knew when it would come crashing down.

igame’s trash talk was very well-calculated. It mocked the opponent without putting too much pressure on themselves.

If they won—Wow, you guys aren’t even as good as your own benched players!

If they lost—No wonder Feather used to play for this team; they’ve still got some real skill!

This approach wasn’t just for UH; it applied to their trash talk against Fight as well.

—Either we’ll win the championship, or you’ll watch us win it.

Either way, of our two teams, one of us is going to take the championship. I won’t say which one, because it all depends on our own performance.

As for UH’s trash talk, it was practically a “burn the boats[^1]" move—win or lose, they were bound to piss off Fight.

But because of the Ling Suyu incident, the two clubs were already at loggerheads to begin with, and with their logos being red and blue respectively, they didn’t mind causing a little more friction.

It was just that…

“It’s such a shame there aren’t any live comments for them to see!” Dou Jiayan sighed. “Otherwise, it would definitely be spectacular!”

Right now, the screen was flooded with comments supporting igame in crushing UH’s “dog heads.”

“It’s okay,” Tang Yu’an said. “We still have the live audience’s reaction. Listen to those boos.”

The livestream mainly captured the host, the commentators, and the in-game audio. The audience’s boos weren’t very noticeable, but they must have been quite loud in person.

Even though UH was the team from City B with home-field advantage, and igame was from City S, the cheers for igame still dominated the venue.

But come to think of it, Fight, Winne, and YJ were all teams from City B, so it was impossible for the entire crowd to be made up of UH fans.

Plus, Fight was Feather’s team, Winne had once extended an olive branch to Feather on Weibo, and Ma Mingxi from YJ had directly taken to Weibo to side with Feather and bash UH. After all that, fans from all three clubs had essentially turned into UH haters.

Even if fans from each club made up one-quarter of the crowd, the ratio of UH fans to haters would still be 1:3—UH was clearly at a disadvantage.

Soon, the match between igame and UH officially began.

As soon as the bans were revealed, some people sensed something was off—igame’s midlaner, Curse Master, had never been afraid of Harp Demon or Deepsea Faerie.

Since igame had the first ban and UH the first pick in the first game, UH was unable to force igame’s midlaner to reveal his pick until the very end and could only select the Wind Guardian, a hero capable of countering Curse Master.

Then, igame’s mid lane pick was—Beast Master!

“Damn! Beast Master!” Dou Jiayan cringed the moment he saw that hero.

This hero had a long range and dealt high damage, and was definitely a meta pick right now.

Since he’d always been a frequent ban target, Dou Jiayan hadn’t practiced playing him. If he could ban him, he’d ban him; if not, he’d use Harp Demon or Deepsea Faerie to counter him. He never expected that igame’s mid laner would actually have practiced Beast Master!

“Sure enough, igame didn’t go all out when they played against us.” Tang Yu’an said with great interest, “This is going to be a show. How long do you guys think UH can last? 10 minutes? 20 minutes?”

“Five minutes,” Dou Jiayan replied with a straight face.

Hearing his prediction, everyone couldn’t help but laugh.

Tang Yu’an: “It won’t be that bad—five minutes? The Sunbird would’ve barely even spawned by that point!”

Mo Xiaoyun: “Beast Master’s early-game damage has been nerfed—five minutes is way too exaggerated. I’d say 15 minutes. That thing’s a real pain in the ass in team fights later on.”

Yu He thought for a moment: “16 minutes.”

Tang Yu’an quickly added: “Then I’ll guess 17 minutes.”

Chen Jie: ?

“Are you guys bidding at an auction or something?”

Ling Suyu couldn’t stop giggling and cleared his throat to cover it up.

“Feather, how many minutes do you think?” Tang Yu’an asked.

Ling Suyu: “18 minutes, maybe.”

Tang Yu’an: “Pfft.”

Chen Jie: “You guys have completely corrupted poor Yumao!”

Dou Jiayan: “Hahaha!”

In the end, the match lasted 17 minutes, and Tang Yu’an won.

No wait—igame won.

UH was completely caught off guard when igame’s mid laner picked Beast Master—it had been totally unexpected.

Even if igame’s Beast Master finished with 5 kills, 4 deaths, and 13 assists, and igame’s jungler finished with 15 kills, 3 deaths, and 8 assists, and the system awarded the MVP to the jungler.

Nevertheless, the league’s official referees awarded the MVP to Beast Master, believing he was the key to igame’s victory.

They were right—Fight felt the same way.

They had delighted at UH’s humiliation, but once the joy subsided, they couldn’t help but reflect—igame had become an even tougher opponent, and they weren’t sure if they could pull off a win against them next time like they did in the group stages.

Unlike Fight, who would have to face igame again, the viewers watching the livestream had no such concerns and were happily flooding the chat with comments:

【igame is awesome!】

【Does UH’s face hurt?】

【Hahaha, that was so satisfying! I really want to know what the UH players’ faces look like right now!】

The live stream actually cut to a shot of the UH players, and each of them had a somber expression. They had clearly been stunned by igame’s performance.

After the game ended, both teams began a tense post-game analysis.

UH: “We have no choice—let’s ban Beast Master. While there are ways to counter him, we don’t have time to practice them. Let’s let Tempest Blade out and snatch it.”

igame’s side: “Great. Beast Master has already thrown them off balance, so they’ll definitely ban him next game. We’ll stick with Curse Master, then once they’ve picked their jungler, we’ll snag Guardian Spirit and Abyssal Lord. UH isn’t Fight; they won’t lane against us with such a ridiculous Cat Spirit. No burst-damage marksman is stronger than Guardian Spirit. Once we secure an advantage in the bot lane, we’ll push straight through! ”

“Alright!”
