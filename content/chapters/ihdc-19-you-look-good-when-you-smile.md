After Tang Yu’an finished off the enemy mid-laner, he confidently strutted downwards.

Ling Suyu was still immersed in the shock of watching him use a top-lane hero to solo-kill the enemy mid-laner: “Have you trained with Skeletal Empress before?”

This was the only explanation he could think of.

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow. Originally, he thought of just admitting it, but hearing the surprise in Ling Suyu’s tone, he suddenly felt like teasing him, and replied: “No, it’s just that the other side is way too weak, and can’t even take me out at half health while being at full health.”

“Clearly you purposefully let yourself be depleted to half health to lure them towards the edge of the tower,” Ling Suyu protested.

He had watched the whole thing by dragging his perspective over!

“Oho, you saw?” Tang Yu’an chuckled, “Were you that worried about me?”

“.....”

W, worried?

There’s no way he was worried!

Wait, maybe he was also worried in some sense?

Worried that his mid-lane collapsing would affect the bottom lane…

Ling Suyu was directly stunned by this question and took a while to respond.

It’s a good thing that Tang Yu’an didn’t let the silence between the two drag on, and quickly spoke again: “It’s just that I had good luck and ran into someone with less experience in dealing with top-laners. They got excited seeing I was at half health, but me being at half health and them being at half health are two completely different situations, so it can’t be counted as a counter-kill.”

Indeed, top-laners were in charge of tanking damage during team fights, so how could their health compare to that of a fragile mage?

Wait a minute, that’s not important, the important part was—how was Tang Yu’an so good at playing top-lane as well?

While Ling Suyu remained shocked in his heart, his hands weren’t idle, and he gradually left the tower as he cleared minions.

The enemy bottom-laners were rather cautious, and guessing that Tang Yu’an may come down at any moment after finishing off the mid-laner, they immediately retreated back underneath their own towers.

Ultimately, Tang Yu’an only squatted in the bushes near the enemy tower for a bit before heading back. He didn’t make any moves, but successfully scared the opposing bottom-laners to the point where they didn’t dare venture out from beneath their tower. Ling Suyu reassuredly exited his tower and finished off a wave of minions, reaching level 3.

Soon, the opposing mid-laner revived, and Tang Yu’an also returned to the mid-lane to clear minions.

Although the enemy mid-laner Butterfly Dancer was a close-combat mage, they still had a wider range than Skeletal Empress, so they technically had an advantage when laning.

However, after being killed once by Tang Yu’an, their level and economy were both being suppressed by Tang Yu’an. If originally they had been afraid to leave their tower after being killed once, now they were even more unable to leave, and the economic gap between the two continued to increase.

Tang Yu’an didn’t bring Heal this round, but instead brought Flash and Teleport. After he finished recalling to the base and buying equipment, he immediately used Teleport to return to the first tower on the mid-lane, not giving his opponent any room to breathe.

As Ling Suyu finished up his 6th round of minions, reaching level 6 and unlocking his ultimate, he prepared to recall back to the base to buy equipment. He opened the statistics for both teams to take a look and—in a situation where one of their teammates was offline, their economic advantage was actually crushing the opposing team!

It could be said that this was all thanks to Tang Yu’an!

Finally, when the match reached 7 minutes, the enemy touched the Divine Sunbird, and the first team fight between both sides was initiated in the lower river.

“Keep up!” After Tang Yu’an said these two words, he consecutively used his Flash and first skill. With a smooth soaring strike, he smashed his hammer into the bird pit, directly stunning the enemy jungler, marksman, mid-laner, and top-laner all at once!

Ling Suyu knew to follow up as soon as he heard the words “keep up”. Using his Desert Hunter’s “Hound’s Charge”, he dashed behind him. Using his fastest hand speed, he selected the enemy marksman, placed down “Hunter’s Trap”, and used his ultimate “Roar of the Desert”!

Desert Hunter’s ultimate was a strengthening-type skill, able to raise basic attack damage for 5 seconds.

Within these 5 seconds, every bullet Ling Suyu shot out would deal explosive damage.

Furthermore, he used attack speed equipment, and within these mere 5 seconds, alongside round after round of explosive gunfire, the damage dealt to the enemy marksman Guardian Spirit was frighteningly high. Their health depleted at an insane rate, quickly reaching 0.

【System】Desert Hunter, one kill!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

Once the enemy marksman died, the other opposing heroes recovered from being stunned, collectively aiming their skills at Desert Hunter.

Ling Suyu didn’t even blink, his fingers lightly tapping the ‘W’ key, and with one “exploding bullet”, he solidly hit the enemy mid-laner. At the same time, the duel-wielding, cowboy hat wearing Desert Hunter was sent backwards by the recoil of the gun, and with one backflip dodged all the oncoming skills before landing steadily.

“Beautiful!” Tang Yu’an couldn’t resist complimenting. Meanwhile, his own hands never stopped; his ultimate “Blood Link” connected to the enemy top-laner White Cocoon while he simultaneously summoned a sea of corpses. He continuously stole her health while standing in between her and Ling Suyu with a low bar of health, using his scarily high defense to forcibly tank all of her damage.

Ling Suyu saw that the enemy mid-laner used Flash to try and escape, instantly pursuing with his own Flash, collecting the kill with a couple of bullets.

【System】Desert Hunter, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Butterfly Dancer has been slain!

The opposing jungler had their ultimate dodged by Ling Suyu and couldn’t bear to just run away, so they turned their focus to the mid-laner.

However, their allied mid-laner Nine-Tailed Fox reacted swiftly, pushing them away with one “Flame Wave” before closely following with a “Fire of Imprisonment” to cage them, then finally unleashing their ultimate “Devouring Flames”!

【System】Nine-Tailed Fox, one kill!

【System】The enemy Venomous Scorpion has been slain!

An insta-kill, how about that?

After Ling Suyu finished off the enemy mid-laner, he immediately returned to Tang Yu’an’s side to attack the top-laner.

Both the enemy ADC and APC were dead, so there was no way for the top-laner to turn the table. They directly used their ultimate as a movement skill, throwing out silk threads that connected to a nearby fake mountain and dragging themselves over, trying to escape.

However, their other allied heroes weren’t trolling around, and their allied jungler Tempest Blade flew over with a “whoosh—” as soon as their movement skill refreshed. Comboing their two damage skills, they smoothly finished off her last bit of health.

【System】Tempest Blade, one kill!

【System】The enemy White Cocoon has been slain!

The enemy support had exhausted all of the skills they had but was nevertheless unable to protect their teammates, so they could only choose to run.

However, at this moment, the cooldown on Ling Suyu’s movement skill ended. Using “Hound’s Dash”, he caught up to the enemy support Abyssal Lord, and pulled the trigger twice.

【System】Desert Hunter, one kill!

【System】The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain!

【System】The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory shall belong to us!

They won!

With four against five, they actually got a 0 for 5!

They not only snatched the Divine Sunbird, and even crazier, the Desert Hunter that originally should have been the target was actually close to full health after this team fight!

And this was largely thanks to…

“Aren’t I good at initiating team fights?”

Ling Suyu hadn’t even said anything, but Tang Yu’an had already begun praising himself:

“Look! This timing! This cutting angle! These shiver-inducing maneuvers! And this hand speed that is impossible to achieve unless you’ve been single for 20 years!”

“Pfft—”

Ling Suyu almost spat while laughing at that last sentence, missing a minion as he momentarily lost control of his mouse.

Hearing him laugh, Tang Yu’an himself also couldn’t help chuckling: “However, this is also thanks to our marksman who immediately got rid of the enemy marksman, or else I might not have been able to get out alive.”

In the team fight just now, Tang Yu’an’s Skeletal Empress pretty much took all of the damage alone. After all, their support wasn’t present, and it’s not like they could get the other three fragile heroes to tank.

He was right; if not for Ling Suyu taking out the opposing marksman first-thing, with his magic defense gear, he wouldn’t have been able to tank the physical damage.

Even so, the fact that he survived was still nothing short of a miracle.

Ling Suyu didn’t clearly see how he did it, but he had a general guess.

Tang Yu’an definitely wasn’t only good at initiating. His positioning was also first-class, and definitely used it to dodge a portion of the skills.

Yet what Ling Suyu admired most was still Tang Yu’an’s courage.

Even when he was at low health, he didn’t retreat from his position at all, always bravely standing in front of himself and the other heroes, using himself as a human shield.

This was an expression of his determination to win the team fight, as well as his trust in his teammates.

At this thought, Ling Suyu couldn’t help but smile.

It wasn’t the type of smile that was from amusement, but rather originated from the inside out, from a feeling of happiness from the bottom of his heart.

Tang Yu’an finished recalling and replenishing his status, and as he headed towards the bottom lane, he took the chance to tilt his head and glance at Ling Suyu beside him. Seeing the sides of his lips curved upwards, he suddenly felt an itch in his heart, and a subtle thought emerged.

—He wished to see him make this expression more often.

“You look good when you smile.”

Before his brain could react, he’d already blurted out this line.

Ling Suyu froze for a moment, instinctively turning his head, wanting to see Tang Yu’an’s current expression and deduce what he meant by that sentence.

However, by the time he’d turned his head over, Tang Yu’an had already swiftly retracted his gaze from his face. He stared at his monitor and continued: “You don’t smile all that often, but you look so good when you do, it’s such a shame.”

“Umm…I know how to smile,” Ling Suyu tried to explain, “But it’s not like I can smile at whatever. I’d look like an idiot.”

“That’s true,” Tang Yu’an said, chuckling before continuing, “I’m releasing the Divine Sunbird, come one, take down the bottom lane’s first tower and come to the mid lane.”

“Okay!”

Afterwards, at the 15-minute mark, both sides fought another team battle, and they got a two-for-five.

The enemy heroes were once again wiped out, and they lost their jungler and mid-laner.

Then, with the combination of Ling Suyu and Tang Yu’an, one tanking and the other dealing damage, they swiftly took down the Dragon God. Taking advantage of the enemy heroes not being revived yet, they openly charged into the enemy jungle and took a few monsters.

This round, neither of them opened their in-game microphones, and didn’t interact much with their other three teammates. As a result, between the two of them, the other’s sense of presence felt exceptionally strong.

In the past, when Ling Suyu played Glorious, he went wherever he pleased, attacking and retreating fully based on his own judgement. Yet during today’s match, especially in the late-game, he pretty much became Tang Yu’an’s sidekick, following wherever Tang Yu’an went, constantly trailing after him, always hiding behind him and dealing damage.

When to retreat?

He didn’t even consider this type of question.

Because being by Tang Yu’an’s side was the safest.

Using this dragon army wave, Ling Suyu, under the protection of his teammates, tore down all three towers on the middle lane in one go.

Afterwards, both sides fought two more team fights. Despite the other side having five people, they were destroyed by their team of four in all different types of ways. Near the end, their mentality had probably collapsed, and their gameplay only got worse and worse.

As they were finishing up, their support, who’d been offline the entire time, suddenly came online, and the first thing he said was “what the f*ck!”

The Desert Hunter in his imagination—heavily suppressed in the early game, the bottom lane horribly collapsing, unable to salvage in the late game, always dying before the opposing marksman and dragging down their teammates to also die one after the other.

The Desert Hunter in front of his eyes—21 kills, 0 deaths, and 6 assists!

After his “what the f*ck”, before he could say anything else, the enemy base had already been destroyed by Ling Suyu’s barrage of attacks, the word “WIN” appearing on his screen.

Following that, what appeared was Desert Hunter’s unique MVP animation!

Ling Suyu took off his headphones, looked at his screen that showed he’d once again gotten MVP, smiled triumphantly and said: “Look! Didn’t I say? My Desert Hunter’s awesome!

Tang Yu’an similarly took off his headphones and, hearing his words, directly laughed:

“Hahaha, that’s right! Your hunter’s awesome Ling Suyu only realized what he’d said after hearing his voice, and instantly felt awkward, rubbing the tip of his nose and coughing lightly.

This, why did he start praising himself?

He had to have been infected by Tang Yu’an…

Just as he was still feeling awkward, a notification popped out on his game menu.

Ling Suyu looked over, only to see it was actually a friend request sent by that AFK support?

He didn’t even hesitate before clicking “Reject”.

Tang Yu’an glanced at his movements on the screen, raising an eyebrow: “You’re rejecting it just like that? What if that guy wanted to apologize to you?”

“If apologies are enough, what good are the police?” Ling Suyu blurted out, moving his mouse and clicking “One More Round”, “Again!”

Tang Yu’an laughed and looked briefly at him, also clicking “One More Round”.

This guy, he’d gotten completely carried away, hasn’t he?

The two returned to the matchmaking room, and Ling Suyu clicked on “Begin Matchmaking”.

He wholeheartedly looked forward to cooperating with Tang Yu’an in the next round, and even started considering which marksman he should play next. Little did he know, his support was already fully immersed in stirring up trouble.

Hmm…what position should he play next?

Perhaps mid-laner or jungler.

—That’s it, he happily decided.
