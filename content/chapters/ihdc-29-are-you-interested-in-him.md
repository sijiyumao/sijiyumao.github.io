As the food was held up to his mouth, Ling Suyu opened his mouth almost instinctively. Only after he’d finished eating did he realize what had happened, and he shot a bewildered look at Tang Yu’an.

Tang Yu’an stifled a laugh, unwrapped a new pair of disposable chopsticks, and handed them to him: “Eat by yourself.”

As if nothing had just happened.

Chen Jie could tell at a glance that there was nothing substantial going on between them. It was just that bastard Tang Yu’an “picking on” the newbie, so he shot him a warning glance.

Catching Chen Jie’s look, Tang Yu’an nervously raised a hand to rub at his nose.

He didn’t know what was wrong with himself either. He was usually pretty careful about keeping his distance, but whenever he saw Ling Suyu, he just couldn’t help himself…

He cleared his throat and changed the subject: “That ranked match we played against SMG yesterday—do you want to watch the replay?”

Chen Jie thought for a moment, then nodded. “Yeah, we can watch it right now.”

“Sure.”

Before Ling Suyu could react, Tang Yu’an beside him and Chen Jie across from him both stood up from the sofa and headed toward the practice room.

After getting up, Tang Yu’an casually ruffled Ling Suyu’s hair: “It’s still early for practice. You keep on eating.”

Ling Suyu instinctively mumbled “Mm-hmm”, watching the two walk away. Then, looking at the breakfast spread on the coffee table, he was struck by decision paralysis… eventually picking up another xiaolongbao and popping it into his mouth.

Meanwhile, after entering the training room, Tang Yu’an watched Chen Jie sit down beside him with perfect ease and hesitated before speaking: “Feather’s using this computer right now. If you’re used to the feel of it, do you want me to move it upstairs for you?”

“There’s no need, let him use it. I brought my own laptop,” Chen Jie replied. He glanced toward the lobby to make sure Ling Suyu wasn’t paying attention, then lowered his voice and asked Tang Yu’an, “Are you interested in him?”

Tang Yu’an blinked, feigning ignorance.

“Looks like you are,” Chen Jie stated with certainty. “I didn’t expect him to be your type.”

“What are you talking about? I just…”

Just what?

Admired Ling Suyu’s skills?

Saying that in front of Chen Jie would just be asking for a beating.

Tang Yu’an was at a loss for words for a rare moment, but being as quick-witted as he was, he quickly came up with a suitable answer: “As the team captain, it’s my duty to look after the new recruits.”

Chen Jie simply gave him a look that said, “I’ll just sit here calmly and watch you squirm”, leaving him to figure it out for himself.

“Oh, come on, I’m being serious, don’t doubt me!” Tang Yu’an said as he double-clicked the 《Glorious Heroes》 icon on his now-open desktop, looking as if he couldn’t wait to change the subject.

Chen Jie caught on: “So you’re a little interested, but not ready to make a move yet?”

“Let’s not get carried away. The competition comes first, romance second. Even if I do want to date someone, I’d wait until after we win the championship,” Tang Yu’an said as he quickly logged into his pro account, clicked on his match history, found the ranked match against SMG, and clicked on replay.

Chen Jie raised an eyebrow, hesitated as if he wanted to say something, but ultimately decided to let it slide.

—If you were really that determined, you wouldn’t have been feeding Feather food just now.

If Ling Suyu was straight, it wouldn’t matter. But if he was gay, given Tang Yu’an’s personality and persistence, it was anyone’s guess whether he’d land the championship victory or a boyfriend first.

Once the replay started, Chen Jie shifted his attention to Tang Yu’an’s computer screen.

Although he’d already learned from Dou Jiayan that Ling Suyu had used Cat Spirit in this match, seeing Mo Xiaoyun help him lock in Cat Spirit still surprised Chen Jie: “He’s playing Cat Spirit? None of you had any objections?”

“If even I didn’t have any objections, what objections could the others have?” Tang Yu’an replied, then quickly added, “I’ve duo-queued with him as Cat Spirit before. He’s actually pretty good with it.”

Chen Jie let out a “Whoa” and said nothing more, focusing intently on the match.

In the first six minutes, both teams played with great caution. It was clear they were taking the game seriously.

At the six-minute mark, SMG’s marksman and support coordinated with their jungler in the top lane to tower dive and gank the top lane. Fight’s jungler arrived in time to provide support, leading to a team fight that ultimately resulted in a 0-for-3 exchange.

But immediately afterward, SMG’s mid laner, Blade Soul, rushed to the top lane, securing a kill on Fight’s top laner, Mo Xiaoyun. He then ignored Dou Jiayan’s ultimate, which dealt almost no damage, and cut through the jungle toward the mid lane.

At that moment, Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit, having finished clearing a wave of minions, also cut through the jungle from the bottom lane to the mid lane, arriving just two seconds before Blade Soul.

He successfully intercepted Blade Soul, chased him down, and ultimately took his head without a hitch.

Seeing the kill notification on the screen, Chen Jie’s first reaction was, “Did you give him any reminders?”

Tang Yu’an wore an “I-knew-it” expression as he replied, “Nope, he intercepted him on his own. I just followed along.”

Chen Jie looked surprised.

Seeing his reaction, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but let the corners of his mouth turn up. When he spoke again, a hint of smugness was clearly in his voice: “Feather’s game sense isn’t half bad, is it?”

Chen Jie glanced at him but said nothing.

Looking back on his own relatively short career, there hadn’t been a single play that had ever earned him such praise from Tang Yu’an.

After all, he relied entirely on Tang Yu'an’s commands during matches, so even his most dazzling plays owed some credit to Tang Yu'an.

Suddenly, he felt a bit envious of Feather.

But hearing the smugness in Tang Yu'an’s tone, Chen Jie simultaneously felt a pang of anxiety for Feather as well: “Listen, if you’re really planning to pursue him, you’re better off coming clean with him sooner rather than later. Don’t keep teasing him here and there, it’s kind of creepy.”

Tang Yu’an: ???

“What the—?” Tang Yu’an looked aggrieved, “What in the world did I do? I just paid him a compliment. You’re a straight guy, there’s no way you’re getting jealous over this, right?”

Chen Jie punched Tang Yu’an in the shoulder.

“Cough, cough, cough. Alright, we’re interrupting the replay. Let’s keep watching.” Tang Yu’an rubbed his battered shoulder. Though he said this, his thoughts had been sent elsewhere by Chen Jie’s words, and he was no longer paying attention to the battle unfolding on the screen.

Does he have a thing for Ling Suyu?

Well, maybe just a little.

But was it that obvious?

It couldn’t be that bad…

Unaware that he was the center of discussion, Ling Suyu finished his breakfast and carefully made his way to the training room with his half-finished soy milk in hand.

He was a bit curious to see how Chen Jie would react after watching yesterday’s match, especially upon seeing his performance.

Would he approve of him? Would he think his Cat Spirit was awesome and allow him to use it in official matches?

To his surprise, the moment he pushed the door open, he heard Tang Yu’an’s excited voice:

“Brilliant! Didn’t Feather pull off an amazing play just now? Dodging the ultimate with a movement skill, then flashing back to deal damage! Could a marksman without mobility pull that off? [what the hell]’s plays were completely shut down!”

Chen Jie: “……”

Tang Yu’an: “And that positioning! Look at that positioning! The enemy marksman couldn’t even hit him. I’ve never seen a marksman who makes their support feel this secure!”

Chen Jie: “……”

“Triple kill in an instant!” Tang Yu’an clapped his hands. “He went straight to clear minions and push towers after the team fight—his goals are crystal clear! Yanzi even wanted to go for the dragon, but with Xiaoyun’s health, who’s going to tank the damage if we pick on the dragon?”

Chen Jie hesitated, then chose to remain silent.

He propped his head up with one hand, looking helplessly at Tang Yu’an, who couldn’t go three sentences without mentioning Feather, and felt that he’d been completely swept off his feet by this sudden romance.

Just as Ling Suyu pushed open the training room door, before his other foot even stepped inside, he heard this string of praises and froze.

Then, his face flushed instantly.

In all his life, Ling Suyu had never been praised so openly and enthusiastically!

This was way too much!

So awkward!

Caught off guard by his embarrassment, Ling Suyu suddenly lost his footing. As he tried to step inside with his other foot, he tripped over his own feet, stumbling forward. He managed to steady himself by grabbing the training room door, but caused a loud commotion.

Hearing the noise, Tang Yu’an and Chen Jie both turned around, locking eyes with Ling Suyu.

Ling Suyu: “……”

Oh no! Now it’s even more awkward! Was there any hole in the ground he could crawl into?

However, Tang Yu'an seemed completely oblivious to the concept of shame. When he saw him walk in, he asked nonchalantly, “Hmm? Finished eating already? Why don't you sit next to Ah-Jie for now? I'll grab a chair from next door in a minute, and we'll go over the game right here.”

By this point, Ling Suyu had lost all expression on his face. He gave a blank “Oh” and walked mechanically toward the spot next to Chen Jie.

Perhaps because he was there, Tang Yu’an didn’t continue with the nonstop praises. Still, he couldn’t hide the admiration in his voice: “Blade Soul teleported straight from the bushes behind the wall into the pit, Xiaoyun and Yanzi cast their skills too early here, but Feather timed his perfectly—he waited until the other finished blocking to apply his mark. Yu He’s reaction was good too; he secured the kill and immediately returned to use Smite. We’ll need to have a serious talk with Xiaoyun and Yanzi later. If Feather hadn’t stopped attacking the Dragon in time, with the damage they dealt to the Dragon, Blade Soul might’ve actually had a chance at stealing it.”

Chen Jie raised his hand to rub at his chin: “Your analysis is spot on, but…”

“What’s wrong?” Tang Yu’an looked completely innocent.

Chen Jie sighed: “I’ll handle the post-game analysis later. You’re not allowed to say a single word.”

Ling Suyu silently gave this soon-to-be coach a mental thumbs-up. Yes, under no circumstances should this tangyuan be allowed to give the briefing, otherwise, he’d be mortified.

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow but didn’t press the issue: “Fine, you’re the coach, so you call the shots.”

And so, at noon, after everyone had finished lunch, Chen Jie led them through a debrief of yesterday’s ranked match.

Unlike Tang Yu’an, he barely offered any praise, focusing solely on pointing out problems.

Fight’s starters listened intently.

Ling Suyu could sense that Fight’s members trusted not only Tang Yu’an 100%, but Chen Jie as well.

If Tang Yu’an had earned everyone’s trust through his own skill and excellent leadership, then Chen Jie appeared to rely more on his personal charisma.

Of course, that’s not to say Tang Yu’an lacked personal charm.

On the contrary, in Ling Suyu’s view, Tang Yu’an was more appealing to him and more worthy of his trust than Chen Jie.

But Chen Jie’s mature and steady demeanor—the kind that made it seem like there was no need to worry and that every problem could be solved as long as he was there—truly earned the trust and reliance of those around him.

On top of that, he was the oldest among the group, already 22, making him a year older than Tang Yu’an.

Although he’d only known Chen Jie for a day, Ling Suyu already had the feeling that “this guy is reliable.” So, even though he knew Chen Jie was a marksman just like him, and hadn’t even been playing professionally for as long as he had, he still listened intently to Chen Jie’s post-game analysis.

However, while Chen Jie pointed out a whole list of issues and offered plenty of advice to the rest of the Fight team, when it came to Ling Suyu, he simply said, “As for Feather, just keep doing what you’re doing.”

Ling Suyu blinked, momentarily thinking he was being singled out just like back in his UH days.

Until Tang Yu’an chimed in from beside him: “Yeah, there’s nothing to say about Feather’s gameplay. Both his awareness and execution were flawless. Just keep playing like that against YJ.”

Ling Suyu let out a sigh of relief and obediently replied, “Mm-hmm”.

“But…” Chen Jie spoke up again, “Feather, besides Cat Spirit, what else are you good at?”

“My second best would be Desert Hunter,” Ling Suyu replied. “I’ve also been practicing Guardian Spirit and Netherworld Shadow, and I can handle all the other marksmen heroes too.”

Chen Jie: “I think, since you’re good at sustained-damage marksmen, you should put training Guardian Spirit and Netherworld Shadow on hold for now. Besides Cat Spirit and Desert Hunter, focus on practicing Dark Raven.”

Ling Suyu froze for a moment, thinking he’d misheard.

He exchanged a glance with Tang Yu’an. Once he was certain Chen Jie really was allowing him to train as a sustained-damage archer, his eyes suddenly lit up. His whole being seemed to come alive vividly, as if he were shining, “Alright!”

For the umpteenth time, he felt so grateful to be part of Fight!

Whether it was the practice matches against YJ or the Spring Season, he would do his best to win them all!
