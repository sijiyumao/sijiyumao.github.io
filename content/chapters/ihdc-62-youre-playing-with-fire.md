【Welcome back to the 13th BPL Spring Quarterfinals! Hello everyone, I’m your commentator Liang Fan!】

【I’m commentator Remi! Hello, everyone~】

On the livestream, while the commentary team still consisted of one man and one woman, it was clear that a different pair had taken over.

The first match between igame and UH, as well as the second match between YJ and SMG, were both commentated by Ah-Mu and ZhiZhi.

However, for the third match between Fight and XY, the commentary team switched to Liang Fan and Remi.

The charm of Ah-Mu and ZhiZhi’s commentary lay in their fearlessness in speaking their minds.

Ah-Mu had once taken the lead in shipping Fight’s bot lane duo, adding plenty of fun to the live commentary.

ZhiZhi once asked Ah-Mu whether Feather or Jiang Hao was stronger, and Ah-Mu cleverly gave an answer that didn’t offend either side.

Their professionalism was unquestionable, and they understood exactly what the audience wanted to see, actively sparking conversations.

Compared to them, Liang Fan and Remi’s commentary style was more subdued.

Since Remi wasn’t quite as knowledgeable, the commentary was mostly led by Liang Fan’s deep, booming voice, while Remi mainly used her cute, girlish voice to charm the audience.

After the two commentators finished introducing themselves, the livestream cut to the game’s interface.

By this point, Fight and XY had already rolled the dice to determine the order of bans and picks.

Fight was to ban first, pick later, and XY was to ban later, but pick first.

“The third match is between Fight and XY—I’m sure everyone’s been looking forward to this for a long time.” Liang Fan’s pleasant voice rang out over the livestream. 【Fight’s new marksman, Feather, delivered a truly stunning performance in the group stage. His Desert Hunter, Candy Witch, and Cat Spirit that he used in the final match all left a deep impression on everyone!】

【Feather’s Cat Spirit was absolutely amazing! I’ve rewatched that “waltz” between him and igame’s captain Xunji countless times—it was simply mind-blowing!】 Remi chimed in, then went on to introduce XY: 【In contrast, XY’s performance in the group stage can be described as solid. Nothing too spectacular, but no major mistakes either.】

【XY is known as a “tank team”; their top laner is very strong, and their jungler tends to pick tanks with high health and defense.】 Liang Fan added, “Meanwhile, Fight’s jungler prefers assassins and often starts ganking before level 6, which should make things tough against XY. On top of that, Fight’s new marksman, Feather, favors late-game marksmen, so Fight is likely to struggle against XY in the early game.”

Remi: “I wonder how this match will play out? I’m really looking forward to it! Both teams’ pre-match banter has been fantastic!”

Before the match officially began, as usual, there was a segment where the two teams traded trash talk.

The host asked XY directly, “Fight has been on a roll this season. Does XY have the confidence to beat them?”

XY’s captain took the microphone and replied, “Our team isn’t one that Cat Spirit can move.”

The host was delighted to hear this response and couldn’t wait to pass the microphone to Fight, directly calling out Ling Suyu: “Feather, your Cat Spirit is being underestimated! What’s your response to that?”

Ling Suyu was forced to take the microphone and replied expressionlessly: “It’s fine, I’m used to it. Everyone who’s died at the hands of my Cat Spirit says the same thing before the match starts.”

Hearing his reply, Tang Yu’an beside him couldn’t restrain his smile, while Dou Jiayan straight up burst out laughing with a “pfft.”

Mo Xiaoyun was struggling hard to hold back his laughter, while only Yu He remained relatively composed, though he still raised his hand to rub his nose as if to hide his amusement.

That’s just how their center was—usually so well-behaved it made one’s heart break, but when it came to giving someone a piece of his mind, he wouldn’t hold back.

In truth, Ling Suyu didn’t know why he was so “good” at firing back. After all, he usually hated conflict and arguments the most. The moment he sensed something was off, he’d sneak away as far as he could, making sure he’d never get caught in the crossfire.

Hmm, maybe it was because the other guy chose to question his Cat Spirit of all things—and because he was certain the other guy wouldn’t actually charge over and beat him up—that he dared to speak so boldly.

In any case, the match between Fight and XY began amidst the thick scent of gunpowder and the excited cheers of the audience.

Fight went first in the ban phase and immediately banned Grim Reaper, a champion XY frequently used in the top lane.

If Ling Suyu was Glorious’s number one Cat Spirit and igame’s mid laner was Glorious’s number one Curse Master, then XY’s top laner was Glorious’s number one Grim Reaper.

Love makes one strong—this saying was perfectly illustrated in《Glorious Heroes》.

There were always players who loved a champion so much that they played it even better than the meta dictated, striking fear into the hearts of their opponents.

However, unlike Cat Spirit, which players had all but abandoned, and unlike Curse Master, whose damage ceiling was fixed, Grim Reaper was an inherently strong champion. Even if it didn’t reach T1, it was at least T2.

Therefore, it had to be banned.

“It’s not really surprising that Fight banned Reaper, is it?” commented Remi. “Let’s see what XY chooses for their ban slot!”

After some hesitation, XY’s first ban went to Beast Master.

Dou Jiayan raised an eyebrow: “Have [chromosome] not researched us before? Didn’t they notice that we never use the Beast Master and always ban it?”

“igame never uses Beast Master either, and yet today, one Beast Master pick completely shut down UH,” Tang Yu’an pointed out. “To be fair, given XY’s playstyle, Beast Master is indeed an easy counter—too bad you don’t know how.”

“Even if I could, it wouldn’t matter. They’ve already banned it, no?” Dou Jiayan said this with a touch of smugness in his voice, as if he’d come out ahead.

Indeed, XY wasted a ban slot, so wasn’t Fight the one who profited?

Tang Yu'an, who had considered using a Beast Master as a support, quietly dismissed the idea.

Fight then used their second ban slot on Realm God.

Although it was unclear whether XY would pick him, based on XY’s past style, their protection of the backline was no less effective than Fight’s. If they secured Realm God, who could stand in the backline and deal damage, Fight would be in for a rough time.

When XY’s players saw this ban: Great, we weren’t planning on picking that one anyway. We came out ahead!

After both teams had wasted a ban slot, XY selected their second ban—Twin Demonblades.

Even though he knew the probability of getting Twin Demonblades in a professional match was virtually zero, Yu He still couldn’t help but let out a “tsk.”

“So what should we ban next?” asked Ling Suyu, who had been assigned to the fifth floor.

Tang Yu’an thought for a moment: “Let’s ban Abyssal Lord. They’re already tanky; if they pick Abyssal Lord too, we might not be able to take him down.”

“OK.” Ling Suyu banned Abyssal Lord.

“You can really tell from both teams’ ban choices how much they’re worried about this match, huh.” Liang Fan explained, “Once XY’s top laner, Grim Reaper, gets farmed up, he can completely dominate the battlefield—Fight can’t let them get that pick. At the same time, worried they won’t be able to dive the backline, Fight banned Realm God. XY is equally worried that their backline will get insta-killed by Fight, so they banned Twin Demonblades. As for banning Beast Master, they were probably spooked by today’s match between igame and UH.”

“Beast Master has a very long range, and it gets even longer as the game progresses. XY’s main champions all have relatively short ranges, so facing Beast Master would be a real headache for them.” Remi added, “Now, XY has only one ban slot left. I wonder what they’ll ban? Neither Phoenix Soul nor Harp Demon, champions that Fight’s mid-laner Yanzi excels at, have been banned yet, and all the marksmen that Feather specializes in have been left open. If it was Ah-Liang were facing off against Fight, what would Ah-Liang ban?”

“If it were me…” Liang Fan thought carefully before responding, “Probably Phoenix Soul, after all.” He paused. “Feather’s sustained-damage marksmen are indeed strong, but they need an economic advantage to really shine. In comparison, Phoenix Soul, which is powerful right from the start, is much more of a headache…”

Before he could finish speaking, XY had already chosen their final ban—Cat Spirit!

“Huh?!” Remi was taken aback.

She had always trusted Liang Fan’s predictions, and he usually made a lot of sense.

She never expected to be proven wrong this time.

In contrast to her surprise, Liang Fan remained remarkably calm: “While Phoenix Soul can be countered by Deepsea Faerie, Cat Spirit, with its economic advantage, isn’t something that can be easily contained, so banning Cat Spirit is completely understandable.”

In contrast to his composure, Fight was far from calm.

“What about their promise that they wouldn’t be moved by Cat Spirit?!”

Before Ling Suyu could say anything, Dou Jiayan spoke up first.

“[chromosome]’s too much,” Mo Xiaoyun added. “For OsO, you could at least say they were making life easier for themselves, banning Desert Hunter since they said so. But what about these guys? They made all those pre-match boasts, and we thought for sure they wouldn’t ban Cat Spirit, but they ended up eating their own words right off the bat!”

Yu He: “This is cracking me up. We haven’t even slapped them, yet their faces are already swollen.”

“Who needs pride if you can win?” Tang Yu’an stepped in to defend XY, then quickly added, “It’s fine—Yumao isn’t limited to just Cat Spirit. They banned Cat Spirit, right? Well, then they’d better get ready to get utterly crushed by Candy Witch!”

Ling Suyu blinked: “So are we going with Candy Witch this game?”

“What do you want to play?” Tang Yu’an asked. “I’m flexible. If you take Candy Witch, I’ll support as Skeletal Empress. If you take Dark Raven, I’ll steal the enemy’s Deepsea Faerie and see what they use to lane against Yanzi’s Phoenix Soul.”

“I’m fine with either,” Ling Suyu didn’t have any strong preferences, but he immediately muttered under his breath, “Besides, any one of them is stronger than Cat Spirit.”

That was the truth. Whether it was Candy Witch or Dark Raven, their early-game damage was higher than Cat Spirit’s. But coming from Ling Suyu, it sounded odd no matter how you looked at it.

His teammates couldn’t help but chuckle.

Dou Jiayan said, “Great, Yumao’s mad. The enemy team’s done for.”

Ling Suyu, who wasn’t actually angry: ?

“So they want to ban Yumao’s main! Then they’ll just have to wait for Yumao to teach them a lesson!” Mo Xiaoyun said. “Anyway, I’ll lock Skeletal Empress first. Depending on their lineup, if you need her as a support, I’ll swap with you.”

“Alright,” Tang Yu’an replied instinctively. No sooner had the words left his mouth than he saw the opposing team lock in Skeletal Empress, causing him to raise an eyebrow. “Banning Yumao’s Cat Spirit wasn’t enough, so now they’re snatching our Skeletal Empress too? Great stuff, chromosome—you guys are playing with fire.”

Mo Xiaoyun: “So what are we picking?”

Tang Yu’an: “Do you even need to ask? Candy Witch and Mobile Artillery—let’s take ’em down!”
