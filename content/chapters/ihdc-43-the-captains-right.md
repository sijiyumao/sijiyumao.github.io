As the two commentators spoke, both teams’ bottom lane heroes had already arrived at the lane and spotted each other within their respective fields of vision.

Mobile Artillery’s first ability, “Pursuit Cannon,” was a lock-on skill that automatically tracked a single target within its attack range, dealing damage to that target and nearby enemies.

Therefore, when laning against Mobile Artillery, you can’t get too close in the early-game.

Even though Tang Yu’an’s Mobile Artillery had purchased a support item as its first item, the champion’s base damage was undeniable, and the early-game pressure it put on OsO was even greater than that of Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch.

Under the pressure from Tang Yu’an’s Mobile Artillery, Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch also dominated from the very start, pinning OsO’s bottom lane heroes under the tower and clearing minions on the lane with impunity.

“OsO’s bottom lane has absolutely no lane control—it’s a really tough game! Is there really no way to counter the Candy Witch?” said Ah-Mu.

In the early-game, there really wasn’t one.

Ling Suyu kept pressuring the enemy while clearing minions all the way to level 6. Once he had his ultimate, he waited for their jungler to arrive and immediately launched a fierce assault on the enemy bottom lane’s first tower!

Tang Yu’an was the first to activate his ultimate to deal damage, unleashing “Indiscriminate Barrage” to bombard every target in the area ahead!

OsO’s bot lane was on high alert this time; as soon as Tang Yu’an activated his ultimate, they both instantly teleported toward the second tower. Ling Suyu only managed to land a single auto-attack on the enemy marksman. Upon realizing they’d used Flash, he immediately activated his ultimate, “Candy Salvo”!

However, the three projectiles were all absorbed by the well-prepared enemy support, failing to secure a single kill.

Yu He was about to activate his ultimate to give chase, but Tang Yu’an spoke up just in time:

“Yu He, don’t chase, go for the tower!”

As a result, Fight’s fierce assault failed to secure a single kill, but they did take down OsO’s bottom lane first tower.

In fact, OsO’s jungler was nearby at that moment, but he simply couldn’t find an opening to engage.

Before this match, OsO’s coach had instructed him to pay close attention to the mid lane and coordinate with their mid laner to secure trades—one lane for another.

However, while the strategy made sense, Fight’s mid laner, Phoenix Soul, had reached level 6 and gained a resurrection ability, making it difficult to target. Furthermore, with the bot lane in such disarray, he couldn’t just stand by and watch.

ZhiZhi: “Fight is now taking on the Divine Sunbird. OsO’s jungler is nearby, probably planning to steal it… OsO’s mid laner is coming down! OsO’s marksman is rushing back too!

Is the first team fight about to break out?”

With the bottom lane’s first tower down, the enemy’s vision was limited and their path of retreat was cut off. With that, it was the perfect time to take the Divine Sunbird.

So, after taking down OsO’s bottom lane first tower, Fight immediately engaged the Divine Sunbird.

Tang Yu’an’s Mobile Artillery had low health and defense, barely managing to tank the damage. Fortunately, Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch dealt enough damage, and the Divine Sunbird’s health dropped much faster than Mobile Artillery’s.

But before they could finish it off, OsO’s jungler showed up.

OsO’s Captain: “Their marksman and support don’t have their ultimates yet—we can kill them!”

Tang Yu’an: “The enemy jungler is probably going to steal this. Yu He, get out of there!

Hearing the call, Yu He’s Double Phantom quickly used “Shadow Displacement” to retreat from the bird pit. Before that, Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch had already alertly backed up a fair distance and stopped auto-attacking the bird.

Now, Tang Yu’an was taking all the damage from the Divine Sunbird alone, the two of them like sitting ducks.

Sure enough, almost the moment Yu He teleported out of the pit, the enemy jungler, Venomous Scorpion, used “Poison Fog’s Shadow” to teleport into the pit. Realizing that the Divine Sunbird’s health was too low to be finished off with a “Smite,” he decisively switched targets and used “Poison Claw” on Tang Yu’an’s Mobile Artillery.

Mobile Artillery entered self-repair mode and lost its mobility.

Ling Suyu and Yu He, who had been prepared all along, immediately launched a counterattack!

Yu He activated his ultimate ability “Phantom Transformation,” accelerating through the wall to land behind Venomous Scorpion and deliver a two-hit combo!

Ling Suyu used Candy Witch’s movement skill “Witch’s Clone” to teleport near Venomous Scorpion while leaving a decoy behind to confuse the enemy heroes. He quickly locked onto Venomous Scorpion and unleashed a barrage of basic attacks!

The combined damage from the two was enough to nearly wipe out the Venomous Scorpion’s health bar in an instant, but just before it was completely depleted, he decisively cast “Smite” on the nearby Divine Sunbird. Relying solely on lifesteal, he managed to regain just enough health to survive, then used “Flash” to leap over the wall and escape.

But the very next second— 【System】Phoenix Soul, first blood!

“A low-health target delivered to me on a silver platter, such good things actually exist?” Dou Jiayan said cheerfully as he immediately used his refreshed first skill, firing a “Phoenix Feather” toward the direction the enemy mid laner was approaching from. He then chained his second and third skills together and, with a single combo, instantly reduced the opponent’s health to a quarter.

Yu He’s Double Phantom secured an assist, refreshed its movement ability, and with a “whoosh,” appeared behind the enemy mid-laner Deepsea Faerie, taking the kill with a basic attack!

【System】Double Phantom, one kill!

【System】The enemy Deepsea Faerie has been slain!

【System】Candy Witch has successfully conquered the Divine Sunbird!

At that moment, the enemy marksman and support arrived at the bird pit. Tang Yu’an’s Mobile Artillery had just regained mobility and immediately used a Flash to hide behind Ling Suyu.

Ling Suyu quickly turned around, locked onto the enemy marksman Netherworld Shadow, and attacked!

The enemy support, Mandara Vines, ensnared him with a “Mandara Wreath,” and the enemy marksman, Netherworld Shadow, unleashed his ultimate ability, “Spectral Reaper”!

Netherworld Shadow’s ultimate ability, “Spectral Reaper,” was also a lock-on ability.

After using his ultimate, 10 energy orbs would appear around Netherworld Shadow, automatically pursuing and attacking enemy targets within range, with damage gradually decreasing as consecutive hits landed on the same target.

Although both were burst-damage marksmen, unlike Guardian Spirit, Netherworld Shadow relied entirely on his ultimate to deal damage.

For Guardian Spirit to instantly kill a hero, all of its skills must land.

But for Netherworld Shadow to instantly kill a hero, only his ultimate needs to hit.

Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch, while CC’d, took five energy orbs in a row, causing her health to drop by three-quarters in an instant.

He panicked a bit because Tang Yu’an hadn’t activated his shield.

Fortunately, the duration of “Mandara Wreath” was limited. Once freed, Ling Suyu immediately used Flash to escape behind the low-health Tang Yu’an.

Just then, Tang Yu’an activated his shield. Netherworld Shadow’s remaining five energy orbs struck him and Ling Suyu’s Candy Witch, but the shield absorbed most of the damage.

When the ability ended, both he and Ling Suyu were down to a sliver of health!

“The enemy marksman’s ultimate is on cooldown! Yu He, get him!” Tang Yu’an shouted.

“I’m coming, I’m coming.” Yu He’s ultimate’s speed boost had already worn off, so he used Flash followed by a dash to appear behind Netherworld Shadow, landing a two-hit combo to secure the kill!

【System】Double Phantom, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Netherworld Shadow has been slain!

Ling Suyu blinked. Seeing Tang Yu’an, down to a sliver of health, chase after the enemy support and start attacking, he hurriedly followed suit.

However, Yu He ultimately claimed that kill as well.

【System】Double Phantom, triple kill! Unstoppable!

【System】The enemy Mandara Vines has been slain!

Ling Suyu raised an eyebrow as he looked at the three assists he’d earned.

“Nice! Fight pulled off a 0-for-4 team fight without losing a single hero!” Ah-Mu commented.

“Right now, Fight’s support has picked up the Divine Sunbird’s token. Both he and the marksman are down to low health and are recalling to heal up. Let’s take this opportunity to watch a replay of that team fight!”

As Ah-Mu spoke, the director cut to a slow-motion replay of the team fight.

Ah-Mu provided a more detailed commentary on the replay: “As you can see, when Netherworld Shadow arrived, Mobile Artillery had just regained mobility and was down to one-fifth of its health. He acted decisively, using Flash to hide behind Candy Witch! Mandara Vines rooted Candy Witch, and Netherworld Shadow activated his ultimate! Netherworld Shadow’s ultimate dealt massive damage, almost instantly killing Candy Witch. Note that at this moment, Fight’s support had a shield, but he didn’t use it! As soon as Candy Witch regained mobility, she instantly teleported behind the support. The support activated his shield, protecting both himself and the marksman, and blocked the follow-up damage from Netherworld Shadow’s ultimate!”

“Wow! So in this exchange, the marksman and support were using each other as shields?”

ZhiZhi found it more and more interesting the longer she watched. “But Fight’s support timed both his Flash and shield perfectly—the slightest mistake and he’d be done for!”

“Exactly!” Ah-Mu agreed. “If he’d used Flash a split second later, he would’ve been killed by Netherworld Shadow! If he’d used his shield a split second earlier, given Netherworld Shadow’s follow-up damage, either he or the marksman would’ve died!”

By this point, Ling Suyu and Tang Yu’an had both returned to the fountain. While regenerating his health, Ling Suyu opened the shop to buy items.

Tang Yu’an, standing nearby, suddenly spoke up: “It takes two shields to block the damage from Netherworld Shadow’s ultimate. Just now, Mandara’s crowd control prevented me from getting through, so I figured I’d wait until the CC wore off before going over to help you tank it.”

“I know,” Ling Suyu replied instantly.

Even if he hadn’t understood at first, seeing both his and Tang Yu’an’s low health bars after the team fight, it all clicked—Tang Yu’an hadn’t failed to activate his shield in time; on the contrary, his timing was spot-on.

Hearing Ling Suyu’s quick reply and confirming that he truly understood, Tang Yu’an let out a sigh of relief. When he spoke again, his tone turned playful: “I just saw you running away so fast and didn’t want you to get the wrong idea. Don’t worry, even if I’m the marksman and you’re the support, I’d gladly die for you! I just didn’t want to give the enemy a free kill.”

Up until today, the supports Tang Yu’an had played were mostly tanky champions with high health and defense, capable of soaking damage and applying crowd control, and he’d never hesitated to take hits for Ling Suyu. But today, he’d picked Mobile Artillery and built a harassment-focused build with little defense or health. To secure his gold for the late game, he couldn’t afford to die carelessly. He had to be extremely careful about when to activate his shield, and he was a bit worried that Ling Suyu might feel insecure because of it.

Ling Suyu admitted that when Tang Yu’an didn’t activate his shield right away, he did panic for a moment. However, upon realizing that Tang Yu’an was waiting for the right moment, not only did he not feel insecure, but he actually found Tang Yu’an to be exceptionally reliable.

“Mm, you can decide when to use it,” Ling Suyu completely ignored Tang Yu’an’s comments that were unrelated to the match and responded directly to his explanation. “Anyway, if I really found myself in danger, I’d sacrifice you.”

“True.” Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but chuckle. “If I hadn’t activated my shield just now, it would’ve been me who died, not you. A marksman who can run for cover on their own is reassuring—”

“Huh? What are you guys talking about?” Dou Jiayan looked completely confused. “Was there a problem with that team fight just now?”

“No.” Tang Yu’an instantly regained his serious tone. “This is between Yumao and me. It’s none of your business.”

However, on Fight’s side, the top laner and jungler immediately used their crowd-control breaks. Mo Xiaoyun’s Black-Armoured Scythe broke free from crowd control and immediately countered with his ultimate, turning the tide of the fight. Ling Suyu followed up with damage, Yu He activated his ultimate to clean up, and in a 3v5 engagement, they eliminated both of OsO’s carries.

Ling Suyu took off his headset and let out a long sigh.

He was named MVP in both games—both the system and the referees awarded him the honor.

With the highest gold and damage output of the entire game, he was Fight’s undisputed carry.

Looking at the golden MVP icon on the screen—a symbol of victory—Ling Suyu couldn’t help but let a smile spread across his face.

As he was still quietly savoring the moment, someone suddenly tapped him on the shoulder.

Immediately afterward, Tang Yu’an’s voice rang in his ear: “Come on, don’t just stand there —it’s time for the post-match interview.”

“Huh?” Ling Suyu looked up in surprise. “Post-match interview?”

“Of course there’s a post-match interview. Now that we’ve made it to the group stage, we have one after every match,” Tang Yu’an said. Thinking back to how Ling Suyu had always been a substitute during his time at UH, never even getting a chance to play, let alone give a post-match interview, a pang of sadness welled up inside him, and he couldn’t help but place a hand on Ling Suyu’s head and ruffle his hair.

They’d won the match, so viewers naturally wanted to see their reactions, and the director cut the camera to them.

Then, the moment the cut happened— Fight’s captain stood behind his marksman’s chair with a gentle expression, naturally raising a hand to rest it on the back of his head and ruffle his hair, brimming with tenderness and affection.

Although he’d intentionally paired up Fight’s bot lane as a couple during their first match against OsO, that was just in-game—he hadn’t taken it seriously even while commentating.

In real life… this kind of shipping was generally off-limits.

But what if it were real?

Led by Tang Yu’an, Ling Suyu entered the room reserved for post-match interviews. Only after stepping inside did he realize: “Where’s everyone else?”

Why were only the two of them inside?

“You’re the MVP—who else are they going to interview if not you?” Tang Yu’an replied offhandedly.

He certainly wasn’t going to mention that right after the match, as soon as he took off his headset, he turned around and saw a staff member waving at him. Knowing he was being called for the post-match interview, he gave an “OK” sign and then, without a second thought, dragged Ling Suyu along with him.

The one the league officials wanted to interview was probably himself.

They’d likely realized Ling Suyu wasn’t easy to talk to, so instead of choosing Ling Suyu, who had just taken MVP, they chose him.

But perhaps out of a bit of selfishness, Tang Yu’an wanted Ling Suyu to experience this kind of opportunity a little more.

Tang Yu’an sat side by side with Ling Suyu behind the table. He was in a very good mood, so the corners of his mouth remained turned up the whole time.

Ling Suyu, on the other hand, had completely lost all expression by this point and stared blankly at the hostess.

Hostess: “Did it feel like an easy win?”

Ling Suyu: “Mm-hmm.”

Other ideas? What Tang Yu’an said was exactly what he thought—he didn’t have any other ideas.

Ling Suyu: “Yeah.”

After all, Ling Suyu had already appeared on camera for the pre-match interview, so interviewing Fight’s captain after the match seemed perfectly reasonable.

But now, she couldn’t wait to chat with Ling Suyu a little longer; this player was just too entertaining.

“Of course we would. Why not?”

Hostess: “Desert Hunter isn’t particularly strong in this patch. Why did Fight decide to use him?”
