In the heat of battle, players tended to focus more on the positioning of both their own team and the enemy and on what they themselves needed to do rather than on what their teammates were doing.

To the members of Fight, Ling Suyu’s maneuver—dodging the Venomous Scorpion’s attack with extraordinary reflexes and returning to his teammates—looked like he had suddenly teleported back to them. At the same time, Venomous Scorpion appeared in their fields of vision.

Their first reaction was: “So the enemy jungler’s over there! Get him!”

Their second reaction was: “Oh, so Feather used his Flash to dodge Venomous Scorpion’s attack?”

As the support and Fight’s overall commander, Tang Yu’an kept a close eye on all his teammates’ positions, doing his best to ensure that his ultimate’s buffs, talent shields, and the bonuses and healing provided by his items benefited every single teammate.

Ling Suyu had mobility, and he couldn’t chase after him recklessly. After all, the rest of the team was still nearby.

However, he kept a close eye on Ling Suyu’s position and movements, wanting to warn him about the enemy jungler but afraid that speaking up suddenly might distract him.

In that split second of hesitation, Venomous Scorpion appeared. Ling Suyu dodged it with a Flash, and Tang Yu’an froze for a moment, feeling as though a miracle had occurred before his eyes, one he couldn’t quite grasp.

It wasn’t until the enemy heroes fell one after another, and they had worked together to take down the enemy’s bottom lane first tower and were preparing to take the Divine Sunbird, that Tang Yu’an belatedly realized what had happened. Pondering it, he spoke uncertainly: “Huh? Yumao, did you dodge Venomous Scorpion’s ultimate?”

Ling Suyu gave a noncommittal “mm-hmm” in response, his tone so matter-of-fact that it seemed as if it were the most natural thing in the world.

However, Yu He, who had played Venomous Scorpion before, was stunned: “Huh?”

Dou Jiayan, who had been ganked, tower-dived, and killed by Venomous Scorpion countless times, went completely blank for a moment before finally snapping out of it: “Holy f*ck! No wonder he wasn’t using his ultimate—he’d already used it on you? And you dodged it?!”

Venomous Scorpion was originally a hero who dealt damage by stacking poison; its front-loaded damage wasn’t high, and relied entirely on sustained damage to poison his target to death.

And “Poisoned,” as a debuff, could be removed.

Not only did some supports have abilities that could remove debuffs, but the “Purify” talent could also achieve the same effect—though, of course, if you took “Purify,” you couldn’t take “Heal,” since each hero could only carry a maximum of two talents before entering the game. You certainly can’t replace “Flash” with “Purify.”

In short, because of these abilities and talents, Venomous Scorpion’s survivability was under threat. The developers wanted to preserve the hero’s uniqueness while ensuring she had a place in Glorious—rather than being relegated to the “sewer”—so they buffed her abilities: her passive allowed her to clearly see enemy targets hidden in the brush, and both her first ability and ultimate were insta-cast and hit instantly. As long as her reaction time was fast enough, she could unleash a full combo almost simultaneously, leaving opponents with no way to dodge.

Cui Rao was a professional player—how slow could his reaction time possibly be?

If Ling Suyu managed to dodge it, it certainly wasn’t because Cui Rao was intentionally letting him win. That left only one explanation—Ling Suyu’s reaction speed was faster than Cui Rao’s hand speed!

“How did you do that?!” Dou Jiayan was so surprised he nearly missed his skill. “Teach me!”

Ling Suyu thought about it carefully for a moment, then said, “Just use Flash the moment you see him.”

Dou Jiayan: “……”

Yu He: “……”

Tang Yu’an smiled but said nothing.

Probably realizing his answer was too simple, Ling Suyu paused for a moment before adding, “He used teleports to get there. You can’t use your ultimate while teleporting, so he needed a certain reaction time. You just need to dodge during that window. If he were right next to me and used his ultimate directly on me, there’s no way I could dodge it. But… that’s probably impossible, right?”

Dou Jiayan: “……”

Yu He: “……”

Damn! Just what kind of genius did their captain bring back?

What’s even worse was that, judging by Yumao’s tone, he didn’t seem to think dodging Venomous Scorpion’s ambush was anything special, as if he’d be able to dodge it just as easily if it happened again.

And, it seemed like he was actually taking this seriously and teaching them what to do?

“When you know the jungler is nearby, you must always save your Flash,” Ling Suyu concluded, then tilted his head. “Got it?”

Dou Jiayan: “I’m hopeless, I’m hopeless.”

Yu He’s lips twitched slightly, but he said nothing.

Mo Xiaoyun, with his high health and defense, who had never been one-shotted by a Venomous Scorpion: ?

Unlike his slow-on-the-uptake teammates, the commentators and viewers, who had a bird’s-eye view and knew Cui Rao’s position long before he launched his ambush, truly felt just how lightning-fast Ling Suyu’s reaction was in that split second!

Even if they hadn’t noticed it at the time, after the scene was replayed twice on the big screen—and especially in slow motion—everyone was blown away!

ZhiZhi couldn’t help but shout, “Feather’s awesome!”;

The audience was swept up in the moment and began chanting, “Feather’s awesome!”

In the spectator’s room, Chen Jie watched the replay on the screen. His eyes suddenly widened, and his heart began racing.

As a dedicated marksman, he could empathize more deeply than anyone else with the danger and tension Ling Suyu faced in that split second of the ambush.

He could almost imagine how unsettled he’d feel if he’d pulled off a similar play—that feeling of having narrowly escaped death would have left him too shaken to even glance back, let alone immediately counter-attack the Venomous Scorpion.

But he knew he couldn’t pull off a play like that.

No matter how extensive his theoretical knowledge was thanks to Tang Yu’an’s training, when it came to actual gameplay, he simply couldn’t execute exactly as he intended.

Take dodging Venomous Scorpion’s ultimate, for example. In theory, there was a chance to dodge it, but in practice, very few people could actually pull it off.

Conversely, Venomous Scorpion’s ultimate was, in theory, a guaranteed hit. In fact, very few people could dodge it; most who got hit relied on the “Heal” talent to stay alive.

Yet Ling Suyu dodged it.

Chen Jie didn’t notice the smile curling at the corners of his lips—a look of excitement, as if he’d unearthed a treasure.

Ever since Ling Suyu’s first match after joining the team, Chen Jie had recognized this new marksman’s skill, sensing a kind of instinct, or perhaps natural talent, that he himself lacked.

But he never imagined Ling Suyu could perform at such a level.

Perhaps this year, Fight really could win the championship.

No—he couldn’t even think of a reason why Fight wouldn’t win the championship.

The match continued, and Fight used the Divine Sunbird to take down UH’s mid-lane tower.

After their heroes respawned, UH regrouped in the mid-lane and didn’t let Fight advance a single step further.

But Yu He seized the opportunity to push the top lane minions into the enemy’s tower, taking it down.

At this point, all three of UH’s frontline towers had fallen, and the fans rooting for Fight could finally breathe a sigh of relief.

However, remembering that UH had pulled off quite a few comebacks, they couldn’t help but hold their breaths for Fight.

For quite some time afterward, the livestream’s chat and Weibo comments were sparse; most people were quietly watching and waiting for the match’s outcome.

Even before the match began, there were already posts on the forum predicting which team would win.

Compared to the mixed bag of opinions on Weibo, the official forum was, after all, the official communication platform for Glorious’s players. Whether they supported Fight or UH, the replies there were relatively level-headed.

Of course, only relatively.

The original poster was clearly a UH fan; in his opening post, he laid out a slew of reasons why UH would win.

For example, they were aggressive in the early game and steady in the late game.

For instance, as two late-game teams, UH’s lineup clearly had a greater advantage in late-game team fights. After all, having Abyssal Lord as a support boosted the entire team’s survivability, whereas for Fight, without an economic advantage, Spider Queen’s ultimate was useless.

After all, what was the point of high movement and attack speed if you couldn't deal damage?

Although he supported UH, his analysis was fairly rational, so people in the replies didn’t attack him—instead, they simply shared their own opinions.

In the first 6 minutes of the match, UH’s gold lead had Fight completely on the back foot, and the gap was gradually widening; even Fight’s supporters couldn’t help but hold their breath for them.

However, at the 6-minute mark, Fight pulled off a bold gank on UH’s bottom lane, securing a quadra-kill without losing a single team member and completely flipping the economic advantage.

Seeing this, the gloomy atmosphere on the forum instantly vanished!

【What a brilliant gank! Fight still has a chance!】

【Feather is seriously awesome! His 0 deaths are all skill!】

After Fight’s fans had posted several pages of mindless praise, finally, a reply appeared predicting that Fight would win and offered a rational analysis.

【No need to watch anymore—Fight is definitely going to win. It’s true that UH excels at disadvantageous comebacks, but the teams they’ve flipped the tables against are mostly early-game teams like S.MG and Big.C. Once those teams are dragged into the late game, they’re prone to falling behind. But Fight, just like UH, is a late-game team. If they gain an economic advantage, unless the game drags on into the very-late game—when both sides are max level and have fully built their items—UH’s team composition won’t be able to overcome its economic deficit in team fights. Unless Fight makes a mistake that allows UH to take out their carry first, UH has no chance of winning team fights against Fight. But first of all, Fight won’t give them the chance to drag the game into the very-late game. Secondly, Fight’s current marksman Yuamo is super fast, and his escape skills are off the charts. So I think that after this round, UH won’t have another chance to stage a comeback from a losing position, and victory will ultimately belong to Fight!】

Then, as if to confirm this player’s analysis, the rest of the game became a one-sided affair.

At the 10-minute mark, the Dragon spawned, and Fight and UH clashed in UH’s top-jungle area. UH’s formation was scattered, and they were completely overwhelmed by Fight!

Yu He, playing Twin Demonblades, dived into the fray early in the team fight and instantly took down UH’s marksman with a single combo. Dou Jiayan immediately cast his ultimate, “Hurricane,” right where Yu He had dived in, sending the enemy support and jungler—who were trying to kill Yu He to save their marksman—flying into the air!

Then Ling Suyu followed up, and Tang Yu’an, who had just activated his ultimate, joined in. He took down the jungler first, then used his Flash to chase down the escaping mid laner, applying a mark and finishing him off with his ultimate!

UH’s top laner, Dark Angel, had a significant laning advantage thanks to his high single-target damage and the lifesteal. However, during the team fight, he targeted the Skeletal Empress. Just as he had whittled her health down to half, he was ganged up on and killed by Fight—his healing simply couldn’t keep up with the damage he was taking.

With only one support remaining, Ling Suyu didn’t even glance at him and simply started farming UH’s jungle camps.

After his teammates killed the support, he, now with the lightning strike, immediately ran toward the dragon pit with a clear objective, flanked by his teammates!

After taking down the Dragon, Cat Spirit’s gold lead skyrocketed. From then on, unless the entire UH team coordinated to gank him, all he needed was for Tang Yu’an to stick with him. The rest of the team could go about their own business.

Then, at the 15-minute mark, the defining moment of the game unfolded.

First, Ling Suyu, protected by Tang Yuan, was farming the lightning dragon in his own bottom jungle when UH’s jungler suddenly burst out of the bushes and charged into the pit, seemingly intent on ambushing Cat Spirit. However—

【System】 Cat Spirit, one kill! Swift as lightning!

【System】 The enemy Venomous Scorpion has been slain!

Venomous Scorpion, who had just teleported into the Dragon Pit, quickly cast his ultimate. But before he even had a chance to use his Flash, Cat Spirit finished him off with three auto-attacks.

No marks, no ultimate—just pure basic damage.

Meanwhile, Tang Yu’an blocked Venomous Scorpion’s ultimate, preventing it from harming Ling Suyu in the slightest.

Tang Yu’an used his shield to negate some of the poison damage and healed himself with a spell, as if nothing had happened.

Immediately afterward, Ling Suyu, now with his buff active, went to clear minions in the bottom lane, where he encountered UH’s marksman, support, and mid laner.

The mid laner instantly cast a crowd control spell, but Ling Suyu dodged it with a Flash.

The marksman immediately followed up with Flash and his ultimate, but Ling Suyu repositioned to get out of his attack range, taking no damage at all.

Right after that, Tang Yu’an activated his ultimate, and Ling Suyu sped up to catch up to the three of them.

First, he locked onto the marksman, applied a mark, and activated his ultimate—the marksman was down!

Then he locked onto the mid laner. With a few unremarkable “biu biu biu”s, the mid laner was down!

Finally, he caught up to the support. The support had thick health, high defense, and a shield, so Ling Suyu had to fire a few more shots, but that was all it took.

【System】Cat Spirit, triple kill! Unstoppable!

With the enemy jungler still down, Ling Suyu, having secured a triple kill, had no more reservations. He confidently pushed his minions into the enemy bottom lane’s third tower and began attacking it!

This was UH’s last tower in the bottom lane.

Was that even a tower?

No! It was UH’s last line of defense, their pride and honor!

UH’s top laner had no choice but to Recall and rush to the bottom lane’s third tower in an attempt to salvage the situation.

However, Tang Yu’an charged right in, taking the brunt of his skills and tower damage, while Ling Suyu followed up with a burst of damage.

Dark Angel, down.

【System】The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us! 

And just like that, UH’s players fell, one by one, at the hands of one person.

This dramatic scene couldn’t help but remind people of the classic childhood cartoon “The Calabash Brothers Save Grandpa,[^1]” with heroes falling one after another.

“What on earth is UH doing?” Even the commentators couldn’t bear to watch anymore.

Commentators Ah-Mu and ZhiZhi had expressed their support for Fight from the very beginning, but that didn’t mean their commentary would be biased toward Fight. They still provided rational analysis based on both teams’ performances and predicted future developments—that was their professional integrity.

But UH’s strategy of feeding one after another truly blew their minds!

After ZhiZhi couldn’t help but make a snarky comment, she continued, “Right now, Cat Spirit has a huge lead over UH in terms of both level and gold. For Cat Spirit, any hero on UH’s side is just a few auto-attacks away from being taken down. UH has to take him out as quickly as possible, and that requires everyone to work together. Why aren’t they grouping up to take on Cat Spirit instead of fighting like this, all scattered about?”

Ah-Mu pondered for a moment before saying, “Is there a possibility, and I mean, this is just a guess, that they want to take down Cat Spirit with minimal casualties, but they overestimated themselves and underestimated Feather?”

Ah-Mu had hit the nail on the head. What appeared to the audience as a highly dramatic yet utterly idiotic scene was actually this:

“As long as Feather dies, we’ll have a chance to win the team fight and secure the next Dragon. Cui Rao, see if you can one-shot Feather.”

Cui Rao naturally had no reason to question Jiang Hao’s orders, but recalling how Ling Suyu had dodged his ultimate during the first team fight, a chill ran down Cui Rao’s spine.

Back then, was it a coincidence, or…

No, no, no—it had to have been a coincidence!

If he could even dodge Venomous Scorpion’s attack, what attack could Ling Suyu possibly not dodge? That’d be no different from cheating!

Determined to test the waters one more time, Cui Rao sneaked into Fight’s jungle alone, heading toward where Ling Suyu might be.

He went in fully prepared to die, but he had no intention of dying in vain.

Even if he died, he would drag Ling Suyu down with him!

And then…that was that.

The moment he teleported into the pit, Ling Suyu landed his lightning strike.

The moment he cast his ultimate, Ling Suyu teleported behind Tang Yu’an, who activated his shield.

When he tapped the F key to use Flash, the screen turned black, white, and gray—no matter how many times he pressed the F key, it wouldn’t respond.

Cui Rao fell silent, and Jiang Hao fell silent as well.

Then, UH spotted Ling Suyu pushing the bottom lane on the minimap, along with the rest of the team spread across the top and mid lanes, and made a decisive call—they had to take advantage of the fact that Fight’s heroes were separated to take down Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit!

So Jiang Hao called out to the mid laner, and together with the support, they went to intercept the enemy, leaving only the top laner on the top lane to draw Fight’s attention.

And then… that was it.

Faced with Cat Spirit’s barrage of green Spirit Blades, their health bars drained frantically as if they’d been pierced through. To make matters worse, they’d gambled everything by using Flash right at the start of the fight, completely cutting off their own retreat.

If they couldn’t kill Ling Suyu, they would be the ones to die.

Soon, three corpses lay on the ground.

By this point, Jiang Hao didn’t feel like saying anything, so when the top laner returned to base to defend the bottom lane tower, he remained silent.

After all, he was just holed up under the tower clearing minions—and as a top laner with high health and armor. Surely, he wouldn’t go down that easily…

Led by Spider Queen, Cat Spirit decisively tower-dived, and the top laner Dark Angel was down.

UH: “……”

They didn’t understand—not even Jiang Hao did. They’d only intended to kill Cat Spirit and secure the Dragon; how had they ended up wiped out?!

If Dark Angel hadn’t come, he wouldn’t have died. Although the last tower on the bot lane would’ve fallen, at least they’d still have had a chance to steal the Dragon.

If they hadn’t gone to intercept Cat Spirit in-lane, the dual carries wouldn’t have died, and they’d have had a better chance of stealing the Dragon—maybe even taking it over midway.

If the jungler hadn’t gone to assassinate Cat Spirit, he wouldn’t have died, and there would’ve been a possibility of winning a team fight and then taking the Dragon.

Unfortunately, there were no “ifs” in this world.

The fact was, Jiang Hao made the wrong call, leading to UH being absurdly wiped out by two of Fight’s heroes and handing Fight’s carries five kills.

UH could only watch helplessly as Fight went to take the second Dragon God.

Although they couldn’t see it on the minimap, they knew Fight was taking the Dragon at that moment, because if the roles were reversed, they would have done the same.

While waiting to respawn, Jiang Hao kept wondering—where exactly had things gone wrong?

He’d initially thought it was Cui Rao’s mistake, but even Cui Rao’s second ambush had failed. The fact that Dark Angel fell into Cat Spirit’s hands after just a few basic attacks was completely unexpected.

Although he knew Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit was strong, was it… really that strong?

Jiang Hao considered himself different from the rest of UH.

Everyone else was kept in the dark, simply following his lead and the coach’s instructions.

Only he knew clearly that the coach’s arrangements were all for his sake—to help him better command the team’s respect, and to ensure UH wouldn’t face a Ling Suyu from a rival team on the battlefield.

So even while the entire internet, with UH’s tacit approval and guidance, was bashing Ling Suyu, Jiang Hao still recognized Ling Suyu’s true strength deep down.

Before the match, he verbally warned his teammates not to underestimate their opponent, yet deep down he felt a strange sense of confidence—I know exactly how strong Ling Suyu is, so I’ll definitely be careful and cautious. I won’t give him a chance to show off. If the opposing team thinks I’ll underestimate him, they’re sorely mistaken!

In the end, even with that “confidence” in his heart, he felt powerless in the face of Ling Suyu’s onslaught.

Once, twice, three times…

At the 21-minute mark, after dying for the eighth time at the hands of Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit, Jiang Hao’s hand gripping the mouse began to tremble slightly.

As a team that thrived in the late game, no matter how severe their early-game disadvantage, he could always see a glimmer of hope—he’d play it safe, farm quietly, and bide his time.

But in this match, he couldn’t see any hope at all.

He had even lost the courage to trade blows with Cat Spirit. He didn’t even want to see that hero in his field of vision, as if it were some unspeakable, unmentionable evil god.

“Fight has wiped out UH yet again and secured the Dragon God!” commentator Ah-Mu exclaimed. “My goodness! Three consecutive Dragon Gods, nine waves of the divine dragon legion, and UH is still holding on!”

“UH is clearing minions pretty quickly, but the minions’ health is getting higher and higher—you can tell UH is starting to struggle to clear them!” said ZhiZhi. “Right now, all of UH is tied up by the minion waves, and Fight can do whatever they want! It’s only a matter of time before Fight wins the game!”

By this point, Fight had demolished all three of UH’s lanes, forcing the team to clear minions right in front of their central tower—it was a helpless sight to behold.

Especially since Fight had only lost two towers. By comparison, UH’s situation looked even more hopeless.

The one-sided dominance the audience had been waiting for had finally arrived!

When it came to a team like UH, which shamelessly toyed with its own signed players, suppressing them for a full five years rather than letting them go to other teams, and then shifting all the blame onto them afterward, as if they were some trash team afraid of them being snatched, they deserved to be crushed in a landslide victory, just to show them exactly how weak they were! How ridiculous they were!

—Instead of wasting time on these dirty tricks, they’d be better off using that time to improve their own skills!

Halfway through the match, UH’s coach stopped watching.

As the match neared its end, UH’s coach reluctantly glanced at the screen, then frowned and looked away as if he’d seen something disgusting.

Ever since Ling Suyu joined Fight, he had braced himself for Fight to humiliate him and reduce everything he’d meticulously planned to ashes.

However, when the nightmare actually unfolded before his eyes, he found himself still somewhat reluctant to face it.

Finally, at the 30-minute mark, Fight pushed down UH’s main tower while UH still had players alive.

Held back by the minion wave, UH found they could no longer clear the minions at all and were forced to desperately cast their remaining abilities at the enemy heroes in a final struggle and counterattack!

Their primary target was clearly Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit.

However, Ling Suyu didn’t even glance at them. He used dashes, Flashes, and precise positioning to dodge as much damage as possible, steadfastly attacking the main tower. Finally, with less than one-fifth of his health remaining, he dealt the final blow to UH’s main tower, securing victory in this match!

The entire arena erupted in cheers—for Fight, and for him!

For his spectacular performance, and for the most resounding slap in the face he dealt to UH!

—All the slander and hurt meant nothing in the face of the word “WIN”!

Over on UH’s side, their most hot-tempered jungler, Cui Rao, nearly smashed his headset to pieces as he took it off.

Jiang Hao’s gaze was unfocused as he took off his headphones. For the first time, he didn’t know how to face his teammates.

The others were all frowning, their faces filled with resentment, yet they couldn’t bring themselves to say a single word—not even a simple “Damn it!”

As they each reflected on this humiliating match in their own way, the coach’s voice rang out from behind them, uttering just two words: “I’m sorry.”

UH’s starters turned around in disbelief. There stood their coach, the man who was always domineering and commanding, a figure no one could or would dare to defy, bowing his head to them for the first time and saying, “I’m sorry. As for Feather, it’s all my fault…”

Then, he straightened up, furrowing his brow slightly, and continued in an unusually serious tone: “I’ll explain the details once the match is over. For now, I just want you to know that Feather is strong—very strong! You can’t catch Feather the same way you catch other marksmen! It’s too late to make many adjustments now. To win, there’s only one thing we can do—”

UH’s coach was arrogant. Until today, he had never felt he’d done anything wrong regarding Feather. His only mistake was letting Ling Suyu play in the White Fox Cup, which led to him being discovered by Fight.

But with the outcome of the match at stake—and UH’s chances of winning the championship—he couldn’t afford to worry about that anymore. If he could make the entire team realize just how terrifying their opponent was today, he’d be willing to eat his words, even face eternal damnation, if it meant securing victory.

He hadn’t won a single championship during his years playing esports. Now, he was pinning all his hopes on UH. No matter what the public said about UH, and no matter how UH’s players viewed him, he was determined to lead UH to victory!

Fueled by this intense determination, he met the stunned gazes of UH’s starting lineup and said, enunciating each word clearly:

“In the next game, ban all of his go-to picks—don’t leave a single one!”
