Since their practice match against YJ was scheduled for 1:00 PM, the members of Fight all woke up before 11:00 AM the next day. They wanted to eat early so they could play a game or two to get their bearings before facing off against YJ.

Fight didn’t have a cafeteria, so everyone ate in the lobby.

In the lobby stood a rectangular coffee table with rounded corners, surrounded by three rows of sofas of varying lengths, enough to seat the entire team.

After finishing his morning routine, Ling Suyu stepped into the lobby and immediately spotted Tang Yu’an already seated by the coffee table. Without even pausing to think, he walked straight over and sat down beside him.

Tang Yu'an was pleased with his instinctual “support recognition”, so he took the initiative to pick out the most appealing boxed meal from the table and handed it to him: “Here you go—”

“Mmm, thanks.” Ling Suyu obediently took it, then accepted the disposable chopsticks he handed him, completely unaware that anything was amiss.

It wasn’t until Yu He finished washing up and stepped into the living room that he saw the scene, letting out a sharp “whoa”: “I’ve never seen you be this attentive to us before.”

“What do you know?” Tang Yu’an said after picking out a meal for Ling Suyu and then selecting one for himself. “He’s the marksman, how could it be the same? It’s only natural for a support to help their marksman!”

Yu He: “You think I won’t team up with Yanzi for a mid-jungler gank and take you down right now?”

Dou Jiayan, who was just coming out of the bathroom: “Huh? HeHe, are you planning to gank mid when we play against YJ today?”

Mo Xiaoyun, who was just coming down the stairs: “YJ excels at the early game, so they’ll definitely prioritize ganking the mid lane. You don’t need to come to the top lane, I can handle it on my own. Just make sure to warn me when the enemy mid laner roams up, Yanzi.”

Dou Jiayan: “Don’t worry, I won’t give him a chance to roam!”

Yu He: “……”

Is there anything else in these guys’ heads besides games?

But then again, he wasn’t in a position to judge them.

Tang Yu’an, who had witnessed the conversation quickly derail, was struggling hard to stifle his laughter.

Ling Suyu blinked, his wide eyes filled with utter confusion.

In the end, he decided to ignore them and went about opening his boxed meal to eat.

After eating, everyone entered the practice room. They each played a couple of rounds of Glorious to get their hands back in the groove, then logged into their pro accounts, created a room, and waited for YJ to join.

To avoid distracting them, Chen Jie stayed in another room with his laptop, watching the match directly through the spectator’s view.

Anyway, with Tang Yu’an there, he didn’t need to offer any input on hero picks or the like.

Manager Ye Xiran, who had been busy all morning, rushed into Chen Jie’s room just before the practice match began, carrying a stack of new, unopened uniforms. He sat down beside Chen Jie as if it were the most natural thing in the world, slumped back in his chair, and let out a sigh: “I’m exhausted. I thought I was the manager—since when did I become an errand boy?”

Chen Jie glanced at the clothes in his hands: “What’s this?”

“Fight’s team uniforms, in particular Feather’s—” Ye Xiran said, holding up the stack of fiery red uniforms, which matched Fight’s logo in intensity, for Chen Jie to see. “Under pressure from the Great Villain Tang, we had the factory rush these out. We’ve got sets for all four seasons. I’ll give them to Feather after today’s practice match. Do you think Feather will be happy when he gets them?”

“Probably,” Chen Jie replied instinctively, then, as if a thought had just crossed his mind, added, “He definitely will be.”

The team uniform was a symbol of belonging. It was clear that Ling Suyu had been very happy since joining Fight, so receiving the uniform and truly feeling like a member of Fight would surely make him even happier.

That is, provided they won this practice match against YJ.

If they didn’t win, it was hard to say how he’d feel when he received the uniform.

Soon, YJ’s professional accounts began logging in one after another. Tang Yu’an invited their captain, Cheng Cheng, into the room, and Cheng Cheng then added his teammates one by one.

Once they were all in, Ling Suyu glanced at their IDs:

YJ-LH; YJ-GRC; YJ-Neigh; YJ-Cheng; YJ-XH; All of YJ’s starters.

Like UH, YJ also had substitutes, but unlike UH, YJ’s substitutes were often seen during competitions.

Given that they could have used substitutes, the fact that YJ chose to use their entire starting lineup in the practice match showed just how seriously they took this match against Fight.

Of course, Ling Suyu wasn’t arrogant enough to think they were gunning for him personally.

Clearly, YJ was taking Fight, the semifinalist team that nearly defeated them in the Summer Seasonals, seriously.

And that was exactly the case.

“With Feather on the roster, Fight will definitely be competing in this Spring Seasonal,” said YJ captain Cheng Cheng. “I was worried we wouldn’t be able to schedule a practice match with them, but I didn’t expect them to reach out to us. Let’s all stay focused and give it our all during today’s practice match, then thoroughly review it tonight. Whether we can win the championship this season depends on today.”

“Mm-hmm,” his teammates agreed in unison, all acknowledging Fight’s strength.

As a team that, like Fight, was led by a support player, the YJ members fully recognized Captain Cheng Cheng’s skill and importance as a support. Therefore, they didn’t even consider the possibility that Fight’s strength might have declined after switching marksmen.

A good support could infinitely raise a marksman’s baseline, giving the marksman the maximum room to deal damage.

As long as Tang Yu’an was good enough, Feather’s so-called “problems” weren’t really problems at all.

With that in mind, YJ’s marksman, Ma Mingxi, couldn’t help but complain again: “Supports in UH are just decorative, no? I really don’t get why they didn’t just switch out their support!”

Cheng Cheng’s lips twitched: “Haven’t you had enough of bashing UH after doing it all New Year’s Day?”

Ma Mingxi: “It’s just that seeing Feather reminded me of them. It’s annoying.”

Cheng Cheng: “Fine, fine, go ahead and rant. Once you’re done, let’s play this game properly, okay?”

“Woah, don’t use that tone, like you’re trying to placate a child. I won’t rant anymore.” Ma Mingxi said, and sure enough, he stopped complaining, silently clicking “Ready”.

Once both teams were ready and the 5-second countdown ended, the game began!

While waiting in the lobby, Ling Suyu was so nervous he adjusted his grip on the mouse several times, even starting to worry about what he’d do if he suddenly forgot how to use it.

However, once the game actually started, he quickly got into the zone, his attention fully focused on which champion to pick.

“Let the enemy pick their bottom lane first, we’re in no rush,” Tang Yu’an said in his usual laid-back yet slightly flippant tone. “If we get a chance to snag a mid laner, we should take it.

This season’s meta has nerfed the damage of strong mid laners, but their skill sets are what they are. Whether we can snag them depends on whether the enemy passes them up, so let’s ban Twin Demonblades first.”

“Alright.” Mo Xiaoyun, third on the pick-list, banned Twin Demonblades.

Yu He expressed his dissatisfaction: “Tsk.”

According to the “ban first, pick later” rule in《Glorious Heroes》, the system would have them ban heroes first, then give YJ the chance to pick heroes.

As for whether they’d get to pick one or two, that depended on the system’s allocation.

In any case, since the first hero wouldn’t be Fight’s pick, they naturally had to ban the heroes they considered the most troublesome.

“YJ’s jungler, Xiao Hong, is very strong. We can’t let him have any junglers capable of one-shotting enemies, like Twin Demonblades or Tempest Blade.” After offering a brief explanation, Tang Yu’an continued, “Yu He, can you ban Tempest Blade?”

Yu He: “Alright.”

So, after YJ banned Realm God, the fourth on Fight’s pick-list, Yu He, banned Tempest Blade.

With two junglers banned in a row, YJ sensed something was off.

“Are they trying to snatch a mid laner?”

YJ’s coach: “If that’s what they want, let them have it. Ban Array Sprite and Harpist. In this patch, Phoenix Soul, Beast Master, and Deepsea Faerie are all 50-50 match-ups.”

So, YJ used their second ban on Array Sprite.

“Yanzi, can you handle Beast Master?” Tang Yu’an asked.

“I can carry with any mage!” Dou Jiayan declared confidently.

“Looks like you can’t. Feather, ban Beast Master,” Tang Yu’an said.

“Okay… Huh?” Only after banning Beast Master did Ling Suyu realize something was off.

How did Tang Yu’an figure out that Dou Jiayan couldn’t handle Beast Master?

Noticing his confusion, Tang Yu’an explained, “If he were good with it, Swallow wouldn’t have said, ‘I can carry with any mage’, but rather, ‘Give me a Beast Master, and I’ll carry this round’.”

Ling Suyu: ?

YJ used their final ban slot on the Harpist, leaving the Phoenix Soul unbanned.

Then, YJ didn’t pick Phoenix Spirit; they took Guardian Spirit with their first pick.

“If the enemy picks Abyssal Lord, we’ll take Phoenix Soul,” said YJ’s coach.

On Fight’s side, Tang Yu’an was very decisive: “Take Phoenix Spirit and let them have Abyssal Lord.”

“Got it!” Dou Jiayan instantly locked in Phoenix Soul. “I’m gonna carry this game!”

Ling Suyu: 0.0; “See, that’s what ‘good’ looks like for him,” Tang Yu’an commentated, “But just take Yanzi’s ‘carry’ talk with a grain of salt. He says that every single game, but I’ve never actually seen him carry more than a few times.”

“What are you talking about? I’ve carried people before!” Dou Jiayan protested, “Even though I died 11 times playing this thing in the Summer Seasonals and my stats weren’t great, the refs still awarded me the MVP in the end!”

“Oh, right, right, right.” Tang Yu’an replied half-heartedly.

“Yeah, yeah, yeah.” Yu He chimed in just as half-heartedly.

“That was seriously impressive,” Mo Xiaoyun praised, though it was clearly insincere.

Dou Jiayan: - —∧ Ling Suyu burst out laughing. He couldn’t decide whether Dou Jiayan was the team’s darling or their punching bag.

Just like before, he didn’t join his teammates in teasing him, but instead offered a word of encouragement: “Good luck—”

“Feather’s the best!” Dou Jiayan practically wanted to step over Yu He to give Ling Suyu a big hug. “I’m sticking with you for this game too!”

“Let’s not for this game,” Tang Yu’an interjected. “You can go ahead and self-destruct on your own this time, don’t drag Feather down with you.”

As they spoke, YJ picked Abyssal Lord, and Fight immediately followed with the jungler Double Phantom.

Perhaps because Tang Yu’an could also play jungler, though only as a tank or fighter, Yu He had been practicing assassin-type junglers. Now that his two best heroes were both banned, he was really in a bind.

But there was no choice. YJ’s mid laner and jungler were both strong; if they managed to snag Twin Demonblades or Tempest Blade, Dou Jiayan would be in a tough spot.

Then it was YJ’s turn to pick again, and they selected Black-Armoured Scythe for their top lane.

“Not picking Deepsea Faerie?” Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow. “Then should I take it?”

“Dude,” Dou Jiayan was all for it, “Steal it! Steal it! Steal it! Without Deepsea Faerie, I’ll see what their mid laner uses to lane against me!”

“Alright, then I’ll really take it.” Tang Yu’an locked in Deepsea Faerie.

YJ’s mid laner: ?

YJ’s coach: ?!

The rest of YJ: ??

“Two mages in the bot lane?” YJ’s mid laner speculated, “Then are they putting their marksman in the mid lane? What will they pick? Mobile Artillery?”

If they really did have two mages in the bottom lane, then to balance out the damage types, the mid lane would usually be left to the marksman. However, there weren’t many marksmen heroes capable of poking, and Mobile Artillery was one of them.

To lane against Mobile Artillery, you’d have to pick a hero with longer range.

“Let’s go with Wind Guardian in the mid lane,” said YJ’s coach.

So, YJ picked Wind Guardian for their mid lane.

Their choice was exactly what Fight had expected. Without a moment’s hesitation, Fight instantly locked in Skeletal Empress for the top lane.

YJ selected the jungler Blade Soul.

Then, it was Fight’s turn to pick the final position.

“What do you want to play, Feather?” Tang Yu’an asked.

Ling Suyu thought it over carefully: “If you’re playing Deepsea Faerie as support, I’ll take Desert Hunter.”

More mobility skills, if things go south, he could just run away!

Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow: “Alright, I’ll lock the enemies down, and you just unleash your ultimate to deal damage.”

So, Fight locked in Desert Hunter.

YJ: ??

Desert Hunter, as a sustained-damage marksman, wasn’t good at poking—playing mid was basically a death sentence.

So, that means…

“Deepsea Faerie as support?!” YJ finally caught on.

As a mid laner with crowd control and healing abilities, Deepsea Faerie wasn’t a bad choice for a support.

But what kind of bizarre bottom lane duo was Deepsea Faerie and Desert Hunter?

More importantly, why would a bottom-tier marksman like Desert Hunter even show up in a match between pro players?

“Feather’s playing Desert Hunter?” Ma Mingxi looked completely taken aback. “What’s his deal? Getting ready to bail at any moment?”

“Since Fight’s coach allowed it, there must be a reason,” said YJ’s coach. “But with that, their bottom lane will be at a clear disadvantage early on, so the jungler should consider ganking the bottom lane more often.”

YJ’s jungler, Xiao Hong, gave a soft “Hmm.”

In any case, the lineups for both teams were set. In the order of top, mid, marksman, support, and jungler, they were:

Fight: Skeletal Empress, Phoenix Soul, Desert Hunter, Deepsea Faerie, Double Phantom.

YJ: Black-Armoured Scythe, Wind Guardian, Guardian Spirit, Abyssal Lord, Blade Soul.

Once the countdown ended, the game map began to load.

Ling Suyu took a deep breath and waited nervously for the match to begin.

He was treating this practice match as if it were a real competition.

However, his support clearly didn’t see it that way.

Almost as soon as they entered the map, Tang Yu’an typed out a line of text and sent it.

【Ally】Fight-Yuan (Deepsea Faerie): Hey, isn’t this Ma Mingxi, the guy Feather once

killed before even reaching level 6?

Ma Mingxi: !

Ling Suyu: 0.0; 【Ally】Fight-Yuan (Deepsea Faerie): Feather’s our starter now. Don’t you have anything to say to him?

Who was Ma Mingxi? He was YJ’s damage dealer, the team’s darling—would he just let this slide? He immediately started typing to fire back!

【Enemy】YJ-Neigh (Guardian Spirit): So what if my trash talk backfired once? Can’t

get over it, huh? I really don’t buy it! If I don’t get three kills on Feather today, I’ll write my name backwards!

“Damn!” Tang Yu’an burst out laughing on the spot. “Feather, look at that. He doesn’t just want your head, he wants it thrice. How brazen. Aren’t you going to respond?”

“Is that really necessary?” Ling Suyu didn’t want to get into it during such a formal match…

Huh? It’s just a practice match? Then there’s no problem.

So, after rushing onto the lane, Ling Suyu reluctantly took the time to type two words in response to Ma Mingxi.

【Ally】Fight-Feather (Desert Hunter): Got it.

Seeing this reply, the reactions on Fight’s side were:

Yu He: “Pfft.”

Mo Xiaoyun: “Hahaha, that’s a good one.”

Tang Yu’an: “Hahaha!”

Dou Jiayan: “Hahaha- cough cough!”

Ling Suyu had been waiting for the minion wave with a blank expression, but upon hearing his teammates’ exaggerated laughter, the corners of his mouth couldn’t help but twitch upward.

What’s the big deal! Was it really that funny! Didn’t Tang Yu’an tell him to reply?

“Wait, Feather, you… cough cough.” Dou Jiayan choked on his own saliva while laughing and took a while to recover. “Feather, you talk so softly and you’re such a softie, so why are you always so cold when you’re snapping back at people? It almost feels like your account’s been hacked.”

Ling Suyu: ??

Who are you calling a softie?!

“Hahaha, what do you know?” Tang Yu’an fired back on Ling Suyu’s behalf. “Our Feather just knows how to adapt to whoever he’s talking to. He’s all about keeping it short and sweet, not a single word wasted!”

Dou Jiayan: “Wow, and I wonder who was the one stirring stuff up in the first place?”

“The main thing is…” Mo Xiaoyun pointed out the key point, “One second we heard Yumao hesitating, and the next we saw him type ‘got it’. Feather, does your hand have a mind of its own? That’s hilarious.”

Ling Suyu: =_= Not at all! He just didn’t want to waste time typing!

“There’s nothing much to say. I’ll just prove myself with my skills,” he said.

“Yeah, you’re absolutely right—” Tang Yu’an agreed, “Let’s play this match well and make Ma Mingxi admit defeat gracefully!”

As soon as he finished speaking, there was a moment of silence over the voice chat, followed by a snort from Dou Jiayan.

Ling Suyu smiled helplessly, then quickly took a deep breath to calm himself down.

Seriously, it couldn’t be any easier to make Yanzi laugh.

However, after this little interlude, he suddenly felt much more relaxed. He no longer felt that just because their opponent was YJ, this practice match had to be treated like a must-win official match.

He’d just let things take their natural course.

Just go with the flow, do the best he could, and play a match he wouldn’t regret—that was all that mattered.

With that mindset, Ling Suyu began farming minions methodically.

He allocated Desert Hunter’s first skill point to Ability 1, “Hunter’s Trap”, while Tang Yu’an assigned Deepsea Faerie’s first skill point to Ability 1, “Water Lotus.”

Having played so many games together, the two had developed enough chemistry that they didn’t need much communication early-on to know their shared goal was to clear minions quickly and reach level 2.

On the YJ side, unlike in the “White Fox Cup”, despite having a much stronger bottom lane composition, neither Ma Mingxi nor his support, Cheng Cheng, dared to stray too far from the tower; they too chose to play it safe and clear minions.

Guardian Spirit’s skill damage was higher than the Desert Hunter’s, and before the Desert Hunter’s attack speed kicked in, Guardian Spirit’s wave-clearing speed was noticeably faster.

However, as a mid lane hero, Deepsea Faerie, despite having support runes, possessed base damage that was more than enough to make up for the difference in wave-clearing speed between the two.

In the end, both sides cleared the first wave of minions at nearly the same pace. By the time the second wave arrived, both had reached level 2, with neither gaining a level advantage and the economic gap remaining minimal.

Ling Suyu spent his second skill point on “Explosive Shell”, granting himself a dash ability.

Tang Yu’an allocated his second skill point to “Patrol”, granting himself crowd control.

On YJ’s side, Guardian Spirit gained crowd control, and Abyssal Lord gained a shield.

“I’ll handle the poking—” Tang Yu’an said, no longer playing as conservatively as he had at level 1. He began pushing forward to harass the enemy hero.

One moment he’d cast a jellyfish, the next a lotus, and he constantly harassed the enemy when his own minions were low on health, forcing the enemies to either take the damage to last-hit or dodge it and forgo the last hit.

Guardian Spirit and Abyssal Lord, whose range couldn’t match that of Deepsea Faerie, were thoroughly annoyed by the harassment.

Finally, after struggling to finish off the third wave of minions and reaching level 3, Ma Mingxi, unable to take it anymore, had his Guardian Spirit activate its shield and closed in on Tang Yu'an!

He hurled Entangling Vines, but Tang Yu'an dodged it with swift positioning.

Immediately after, three Arrows of Light struck. While taking the damage head-on, Tang Yu'an activated “Patrol”. A turquoise jellyfish swam out and, upon returning, struck Ma Mingxi's Guardian Spirit—stunning it!

Having reached level 3 and unlocked his third skill, “Hound’s Charge”, Ling Suyu immediately activated it, dashing behind Tang Yu’an with a whoosh and unleashing a barrage of shots at Guardian Spirit!

Guardian Spirit’s shield shattered, dealing damage to Tang Yu’an. Tang Yu’an activated his third skill, “Energy Drain”, leeching a significant amount of health from the enemy to restore his own.

The enemy support, Abyssal Lord, decisively activated his shield and tried to position himself in front of his marksman. Ling Suyu dropped a “Hunter’s Trap” to slow his movement speed, maneuvering while locking onto the Guardian Spirit and continuing to fire away at it!

Guardian Spirit’s auto-attack damage and speed were no match for Desert Hunter’s. Seeing he couldn’t out-damage Ling Suyu, he quickly used a dash to retreat back to his tower.

To everyone’s surprise, Tang Yu’an decisively used Flash to chase after him. Seeing his boldness, Ling Suyu gritted his teeth and used his own Flash to give chase. After two auto-attacks that shattered the talent shield granted by the Abyssal Lord, and noticing that the Guardian Spirit’s health had been whittled down by Tang Yu’an’s “Water Lotus”, he immediately fired an “Explosive Shell”!

With a loud “bang”, the dashing dual-pistol-wielding Hunter fired a powerful bullet forward while simultaneously somersaulting backward, gracefully exiting the tower.

Almost simultaneously, Tang Yu’an’s Deepsea Faerie followed him out of the tower, activating his talent shield to block most of the tower damage, and retreated back to their own tower with a sliver of health left.

By the time he left the tower, his health bar was nearly empty, with the numbers on it starting to rise from single digits.

YJ’s support’s skills were still on cooldown, leaving him powerless as he watched the opponent strut away right before his eyes, clinging to his last bit of health.

And YJ’s marksman?

Ling Suyu’s powerful bullet struck him dead-on, exploding into a dazzling burst of sparks on his body.

【System】Desert Hunter, first blood!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

Kills on Ma Mingxi: +1.
