【System】Tempest Blade, one kill!

【System】Ally White Cocoon has been slain!

【System】Tempest Blade, double kill! Peerless!

【System】Ally Venomous Scorpion has been slain!

【System】Our tower has been destroyed!

Listening to this stream of system announcements, Ling Suyu glanced at his minimap. As he spotted the multiple red dots on the top lane representing enemy minions, he couldn’t help furrowing his brows. 

A few seconds ago, he’d wanted to remind his team’s top-laner that he was pushing too deep, but since he saw his team’s jungler go up to gank[^3], he didn’t say anything. 

As a result…

【UH’s jungler attacks! YJ’s top-laner responds quickly, instantly ducking under his tower. UH’s top-laner gives chase, he really wants this kill, but at this moment YJ’s jungler Tempest Blade is nearby! Tempest Blade strikes! Tempest Blade’s ultimate Shattered-Blade Whirlwind simultaneously hits UH’s top-laner and jungler! YJ’s top-laner Black-Amoured Scythe returns and tosses out his scythe, UH’s top-laner White Cocoon is killed, and the one who got the kill is Tempest Blade! Tempest Blade’s movement skill refreshes, and he wants to catch up with the fleeing Venomous Scorpion. Venomous Scorpion is killed! Nice! This wave of counter-kills by YJ was brilliant!】

—Things happened exactly as Ah-Mu described. UH’s top-laner and jungler wanted to work together to catch the opposing top-laner, even willing to enter the range of the enemy’s tower. The result was they were “peerlessly” counter-killed by the opposing top-laner and jungler. 

If Jiang Hao were here, he would have already started cursing.

But right now, their marksman was Ling Suyu.

He hesitated to speak, and ultimately didn’t say anything.

Their temporary captain, the mid-laner Wang Jingxuan, similarly only gave a “tsk”, before drily reminding: “Top-lane, steady yourself.”

However, the commentators didn’t have the same concerns:

【This wave was a shame for UH! They’ve turned from having the advantage to being at a disadvantage!】ZhiZhi said with regret, 【Because of their top-lane, the economic advantage their bottom-lane gave them has disappeared!}

【Yeah, what a pity!】Ah-Mu echoed, 【Originally UH had a good chance this round, but now that their top-lane has collapsed, the scales of victory are once again tipping towards YJ!】

ZhiZhi: 【Still can’t say for sure though, I seem to remember that UH isn’t too bad at overturning disadvantages?】

Ah-Mu: 【Of the eight teams competing in the White Fox Cup, UH is one of the best at overturning disadvantages, but at the same time, YJ’s one of the best at pushing their advantages. Once they get the economic upperhand, they won’t give the opponent the chance to turn things around!】

Reality proved that Ah-Mu was right. 

Once UH’s top lane collapsed, YJ immediately focused fire on their bottom lane. Ling Suyu could do nothing except fight while retreating as he watched the other side tear down the bottom lane’s first defence tower like a pack of hungry wolves.

As the top and bottom lane both collapsed, the pressure on the mid lane increased. 

UH’s mid-laner Wang Jingxuan finally gave his first command of the match: “Gather at the mid-lane to defend the tower!”

At that moment, the top-laner who was still clearing minions, the jungler who had just left the bottom lane and was heading towards the jungle, and the support beside Ling Suyu all ran towards the middle lane.

Ling Suyu furrowed his brows. Although he felt that there was no saving the mid-lane’s towers, he still listened to the command and headed over. 

Then he watched as YJ crazily pressed them under the tower, instantly killing UH’s mid-laner!

YJ’s top-lane bore the tower’s attacks, and the jungler Tempest Blade, after killing UH’s mid-laner, immediately used a movement skill to move in front of Ling Suyu’s Guardian Spirit.

Ling Suyu decisively used his shield, before using Vine Entanglement, Arrow of Light, Wrath of the Spirits, Heal, and Flash. In that short moment, along with the movement of his mouse, 6 keys were pressed consecutively, all in one go.

On the screen, the fair elf accurately entangled the approaching Tempest Blade with vines, before shooting three light arrows at him!

The opposing mid-laner’s skill destroyed her shield, and as it broke, she raised her hands. A huge tree emerged from the earth, its green branches lashing out in all directions like whips!

“Feel Nature's wrath!”

Along with Guardian Spirit’s shout, Tempest Blade’s instantly disappeared in the midst of the ultimate skill “Wrath of the Spirits”.

YJ’s other heroes also took a blow from this ultimate, their health bars dropping. 

Guardian Spirit as a hero was actually typically used in team fights. Her ultimate dealt AoE damage and could deal massive amounts of damage in team fights, so she had quite a high usage rate in professional matches. 

This was why their coach made his teammate lock in Guardian Spirit even if Ling Suyu himself was unwilling.

This was also why, even though Ling Suyu was standing at the back of his team, YJ still swarmed towards him.

However, since Ling Suyu had long known he’d be targeted, once Guardian Spirit’s ultimate was unleashed, he immediately let her use Flash to escape, and even used Heal before leaving. 

YJ’s marksman Ma Mingxi had been waiting for this team fight to avenge his past humiliations, so how could he let him get away so easily? He immediately used Flash to chase and attack him under the mid-lane’s second tower. 

However, after using Heal, one’s hero would also gain 15% damage reduction for 3 seconds. 

Ma Mingxi’s Netherworld Shadow took two consecutive hits from the tower but still couldn’t finish off Guardian Spirit, so he had no choice but to give up, retreating outside the tower with his remaining bit of health. 

【YJ traded 2 for 4!】Ah-Mu commented, 【YJ’s top-laner was killed by the tower, and the kill was given to Venomous Scorpion, YJ’s jungler Tempest Blade was counter-killed by Guardian Spirit, Netherworld Shadow’s pursuit failed, Feather’s Guardian Spirit escapes with a sliver of blood!】

【Ma Mingxi’s Netherworld Shadow got a triple kill! Looks like Mingxi has already adjusted his condition!】ZhiZhi said.

【Nevertheless, he still couldn’t kill Feather, so he might be feeling a quite frustrated right now?】Ah-Mu chuckled, 【But he’s rational. Because he didn’t die, YJ now has the chance to fight the Dragon.】

As expected, once YJ tore down the first defence tower on the middle lane, they immediately headed towards the upper river.

The upper river ran between the ally and enemy jungles, and would randomly spawn monsters during the first 10 minutes of the match. Once slain, they would give a buff, and once the match time reached 10 minutes the Dragon God would spawn. The side that dealt the last blow to the Dragon God would gain the support of the dragon army, receiving three extra waves of strong minions to help deal damage to the enemy towers. 

YJ no longer had their top-laner or jungler, and their mid-laner and marksman only had one bar of health left. But their support still had quite a bit of health, and with their support tanking the damage, the chances of their marksman killing the Dragon were good. 

Ling Suyu retreated all the way back to his fountain, and once he was fully recovered went back to clearing minions.

Although he couldn’t see the enemy heroes’ location on his minimap, anyone who knew even a bit about the game would know that they were fighting the Dragon at this moment.

“They’re going for the Dragon.” The mid-laner Wang Jingxuan said superfluously, before urging Ling Suyu, “Their jungler’s gone, they don’t have Smite. Feather, go and steal the Dragon!”

Ling Suyu opened his mouth, but was interrupted before he could even make a sound.

“What are you thinking?” their jungler Cui Rao laughed coldly, “He’ll definitely die if he goes, if he still dared to go then he wouldn’t be Feather.”

“But right now he’s the only one alive!” Wang Jingxuan anxiously raised his voice. 

“Alright, stop arguing, let’s defend for this round.” Their top-laner Yao Teng who hadn’t spoken until now said, “It’s not your first day meeting Feather, it’s already a miracle he was willing to join that team fight just now, and you still have the hope that he’d go for the Dragon?”

Hearing his teammates’ words, Ling Suyu completely shut his mouth.

They had always been like this, not treating the marksman like a marksman.

Wanting him, a marksman, to go for the Dragon alone?

What were they thinking?

The other side still had three heroes, if he were to head over right now, no matter if he could deal the final blow to the Dragon, he would very likely be killed. 

If he couldn’t get the Dragon, that’d just be handing his head over on a silver platter. 

If he could get it, without a marksman, how many towers could their team even get with those three minion waves?

They might as well just cut to the chase and let YJ have the Dragon. Then UH could use those three waves of minions to grind and recover their economy. 

Though Ling Suyu ultimately didn’t get the chance to say these words, the commentators saw the situation quite clearly.

Ah-Mu【Feather is still clearing minions, seemingly with no intention of going for the Dragon. I think UH has probably decided to raise their economy first.】

ZhiZhi: 【But YJ is too good at pressing the advantage, do you think UH can hold on?】

Ah-Mu: 【It’s difficult to say. However, YJ’s economy is already quite high, and they’re dealing with the Dragon quickly. So even if UH wanted to steal it, it might be too late after Feather recalls back to their base and fully recovers…..】

ZhiZhi: 【Hm? Feather’s heading over!】

Ah-Mu didn’t even get the chance to finish his words before seeing Ling Suyu’s Guardian Spirit heading towards the direction of the Dragon.

At the same time, two system notifications appeared on Ling Suyu’s screen:

【System】Tragedy! The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain by the Dragon God!

【System】Netherworld Shadow has successfully slain the Dragon God, summoning the dragon army!

YJ’s support couldn’t resist the Dragon’s damage and was killed, but YJ’s marksman successfully slayed the Dragon, gaining Dragon’s Might

Once they dealt with the Dragon, Ma Mingxi’s Netherworld Shadow and the mid-laner’s Nine-Tailed Fox immediately hid in the undergrowth and hit Recall.

For the side that got Dragon’s Might, as long as the dragon army was still alive, both the speed of their recalling and the rate of healing in their fountain would greatly increase. 

But at this moment, a silvery white silhouette charged into the undergrowth, followed by a stream of golden light!

【System】Guardian Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Netherworld has been slain!

【System】Guardian Spirit, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Nine-Tailed Fox has been slain!

Ling Suyu wasn’t confident he could steal the Dragon, but he was confident he could finish off these two low-health heroes.

Once he got these two kills, he leisurely turned around and strode off.

【Damn!】ZhiZhi was shocked, 【Just a little bit more! Just a bit later and YJ’s mid-laner and marksman would have recalled successfully! Feather’s timing is way too good!】

“This!”

In Fight’s practice room, the mid-laner Dou Jiayan watched the situation on the livestream with complex feelings…no, he was about to have a heart attack!

Originally he thought UH was smoothly winning, but then things took a turn for the worse.

Originally he thought YJ could sweep them in one wave, but then came such an unexpected turn of events.

This match was so full of twists and turns!

Not even mentioning his own nervousness, even the livestream comments expressed the same sentiments:

【Thrilling!】

【Could’ve never expected this!】

【Although the current situation isn’t good for UH, I suddenly think Feather’s pretty handsome!】

【Is UH blind, making him a substitute?】

Suddenly seeing this comment, Dou Jiayan paused. 

Right, anyone with eyes could see, the only reason UH could last this long was all thanks to Feather, so how could they let Feather spend so many years as a substitute?

Even substitutes for other teams would occasionally appear on stage for a few matches in order to lessen the burden on the team’s starters. But had it not been for today’s match, Dou Jiayan would have never even known that UH’s substitute existed. 

He suddenly changed his mind, feeling that it wouldn’t be too bad for Fight to poach Feather. Let a pearl among pearls compete openly, rather than leaving it to gather dust in some unseen corner. 

What he didn’t know was, at this moment, his own captain Tang Yu’an was currently pulling up Feather’s past battle records. 

One wouldn’t know without searching, but would be shocked after doing so. 

In the 8th season of 《Glorious Heroes》's professional Spring Seasonal Match, UH stopped at the group stage. In the three matches Feather participated in, his scores were:

Match 1: 22 kills, 0 deaths, 5 assists.

Match 2: 8 kill, 0 deaths, 7 assists. 

Match 3: 16 kills, 0 deaths, 3 assists.

For all three matches he was MVP, but only in the first match was he the MVP on the victor’s side, and in the following two matches he was the MVP on the losing side. 

In the Summer Seasonal Match, UH once again stopped at the group stage. UH lost two consecutive matches 0-2, and was directly eliminated. 

Feather participated in both matches, and similar to the Spring Seasonal had 0 deaths and was the MVP of the losing side.

And then in the second year, in the 9th season of 《Glorious Heroes》's professional Spring Seasonal Match, UH entered the top 8 for the first time. But that year, Feather did not appear for any matches, and UH’s MVP was not its marksman alone.

The more Tang Yu’an searched, the more the enthusiasm in his eyes grew.

Why did this marksman, whose battle records were so brilliant, not only not have the ability to carry the entire team to victory, but even became a tripping stone on their road to the championship, with no choice but to become a substitute?

This was really…getting more and more interesting.
