When he eventually caught on to what his teammates were talking about, he coughed lightly.

He wanted to say something to stop the weird topic, yet the other person in question not only didn’t realize anything was wrong, but also played into it?

“Supports protecting their marksmen is simply the way of the world!” Tang Yu’an said self-righteously, although he also naturally thought this way. “Marksmen are the main sources of damage during team fights, so who’d you be protecting if not the marksman? Marksmen are the team’s central member, and you’ve got to protect the center well. That goes not only for me, but also for the rest of you. The top-laner should stay in front of the marksman. What’s the mid-laner doing staring at the enemy frontliners? If you’ve got to stare, keep an eye on the opposing backliners and jungler, and if you find a chance, take them all out at once, don’t let them near our marksman.”

【Ally】if i have feet i can run (Vampiric Progenitor): yeah yeah, got it boss.

【Ally】can die but no need (Skeletal Empress): tangyuan’s also a pro-player?

Seeing this message, Tang Yu’an tugged the corners of his lips into a faint smile, intentionally not responding to it: “Gather in the undergrowth around the upper river. After we finish another team fight we’ll go for the Dragon God. Same as before, wait for me to initiate.”

【Ally】if i have feet i can run (Vampiric Progenitor): ok 【Ally】can die but no need (Skeletal Empress): got it.

Tang Yu’an didn’t mind Feather realizing he was a professional player. Or rather, he believed that Feather had probably already guessed it.

After all, he was just that good.

But he couldn’t let passersby know, otherwise, how could he keep using this alt account?

Lots of pro-players had their own private accounts, whether it be to play casually in their own time, or to train new heroes with.

And since they were alt accounts, it was only natural that the players didn’t want them exposed.

Even if they were, they wouldn’t admit it.

Actually, Ling Suyu originally hadn’t thought much about it, only thinking that he’d solo-matched with an excellent support.

At the “Glorious” tier, experts abounded. There were many “wild experts”, who didn’t play professionally but had skills that could match up to pro-players. On the other hand, the chances of matching with a professional was much lower, since most professionals played in five-stacks.

Yet, this support, who was pretty chatty, intentionally avoided the “are you a professional player” question. Chances were, he actually was one.

Ling Suyu opened his mouth but ultimately didn’t speak, only pursing his lips.

He felt a slight itch, wanting to ask if the other would be willing to extend an olive branch to him.

However, it was evident that the other side didn’t want to be exposed, so even if he were to ask, he couldn’t do it at the moment.

Soon, everyone had gathered in the upper river’s undergrowth, awaiting the team fight.

Yet, because they’d cleared out the enemy minions on all three lanes, the opposing heroes had their hands full dealing with the minions and didn’t at all have the time to go for the Dragon.

Tang Yu’an made sure there weren’t any enemies in the surrounding undergrowth before decisively commanding: “No need to wait any longer, go!”

As he said this, he controlled Spider Queen to initiate the Dragon God fight.

All of them surged forwards, and Ling Suyu stood behind his teammates with ease and began firing!

Marksmen, whose damage mainly came from their basic attacks, were extremely efficient against monsters. The Dragon God, who was barely moved by the attacks of heroes from other positions, quickly found its health steadily depleting under the continuous strikes of Cat Spirit’s phantom blades, soon reaching zero.

【System】Blade Soul has successfully conquered the Dragon God, summoning the dragon army!

Only after they’d already finished up with the Dragon did the enemy heroes arrive, late to the party.

The enemy jungler had only just poked their head out before being accurately controlled by Tang Yu’an, and died after a few hits from Ling Suyu.

The enemy heroes fled in a panic as Tang Yu’an directly led the charge with an ultimate!

Under the lead of Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen, the assault was swift and powerful, with all members directly pressing towards the enemy towers. The enemy heroes had no strength to counter; ignoring the backliners, even the frontliners were instantly slain.

【System】The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us!

Soon after winning the team fight, they demolished the enemy’s tower.

【System】The enemy’s tower has been destroyed!

Finally, the last tower on the opposing side’s middle lane fell. With no remaining obstacles, the group followed their minions into the enemy’s base and started unrestrainedly unleashing damage onto the base!

After their regular minions fell, the first wave of the dragon army arrived, and the enemy no longer had any chance to turn the tables.

On Ling Suyu’s screen, accompanied by a rumble, the opposing base was reduced to ruins, and soon the “WIN” announcement appeared.

They’d won! They’d achieved victory incredibly smoothly!

Most importantly, be in an official match or in the game’s ranked matches, Ling Suyu had never felt so good playing a match before. He’d spent the entire time attacking, not seeing any chance, or rather reason, to retreat.

During the late game, he could basically play with his eyes closed. The game had never felt this fun!

At that moment, Ling Suyu felt that he had regained the same joy and passion from when he had first begun playing 《Glorious Heroes》.

Simultaneously, the screen began displaying Cat Spirit’s special animation.

After the animation ended, the battle record popped out, and on top of his Cat Spirit’s display image was a golden MVP title, with a bunch of sub-titles.

Highest economy, most kills, highest damage, most damage to towers, triple kill, quadra kill.

The other two sub-titles were awarded to his support: most damage tanked, most assists.

His battle record was 12 kills, 0 deaths, and 2 assists.

His support’s record was 0 kills, 0 deaths, and 14 assists.

Ling Suyu, after being giving a “like” to everyone on the team, stared dazedly at his and his support’s battle records, and finally came to his senses after receiving a series of friend requests.

He didn’t directly accept them, but instead returned to the game’s lobby and opened his friend request list.

The sight itself was a jumpscare!

He’d gotten a total of 9 friend requests!

In other words, after that match, be it teammates or enemies, they’d all sent him a friend request, including the enemy marksman who’d swore that he’d kill him at least once.

Ling Suyu hesitated before accepting all of them.

Yet in the end, only one person sent him a message.

It wasn’t the support, who he’d worked together with seamlessly, but rather the jungler that had been on his team.

But no matter how much they flood the comments, there’s still a lot of people on the forums who are aware of the reality of the situation, so don’t bother checking the Weibo forums.

Indeed, it was just because they weren’t suited for each other.

He didn’t do anything wrong, UH didn’t either, it’s just that he wasn’t suited for the team, and vice versa.

Ling Suyu increasingly felt that he’d made the right decision in ending his contract with UH.

i’ve never won with jungler: Feather, after ending your contract, which team do you think you’ll join? Do you already have your own ideas on the matter?

Ideas…they said as if he could join any team he liked.

Ling Suyu chuckled helplessly, responding with five words: Guess I’ll have to see.

At this moment, a match invite appeared on the page. Seeing the ID “tangyuan with sesame filling”, a thought crossed Ling Suyu’s mind, and he accepted without hesitation.

He then appeared in the ranked team matchmaking lobby, and “tangyuan with sesame filling” was right beside him.

Originally, Ling Suyu had planned to look for team advertisements online after he finished with the previous match, but…

He had to admit that, to a marksman, the allure of a good support wasn’t less than the allure of a good team.

Right, he’d almost forgotten.

Before Tang Yu’an started the matchmaking, Ling Suyu quickly cancelled his preparation.

UH-feather: wait a minute, let me change my ID.

tangyuan with sesame filling: ok Ling Suyu didn’t want to overstep by continuing to use a professional ID.

But, what should he change it to?

He stared at the name “tangyuan with sesame filling” for a few seconds before another food popped up in his mind. Finally, he double-clicked the name-change card and entered the words: soft and chewy osmanthus cake.

Soon after he changed his name, it also refreshed on Tang Yu’an’s page.

Seeing Feather’s new ID, he couldn’t help raising an eyebrow.

tangyuan with sesame filling: hey, we’re matching.

soft and chewy osmanthus cake: …..

Ling Suyu pouted. It’s not like he was intentionally trying to match IDs with him!

After changing his ID, he clicked “prepare”.

His reason told him that he should seize this opportunity to ask the other if he was a professional player, which team he was from, and if they were willing to take him in.

But emotionally, rather than a team, the current Ling Suyu just wanted to enjoy the feeling of duo-queueing with this support.

However, at this moment, the other didn’t rush to start the matchmaking, but instead continued to chat with him in the chat box.

tangyuan with sesame filling: do you still plan to continue on with e-sports?

Ling Suyu paused, trying to reign in his feelings of excitement, and cautiously replied:

continuing, just not with UH.
