At Ling Suyu’s reply, Cui Ran couldn’t help but roll his eyes.

But he didn’t retort, and didn’t give up on ganking, because this response was within his expectations.

After so many years, Ling Suyu’s gameplay had matured compared to when he first signed the contract. But his problem of being “cowardly as a mouse” hadn’t changed at all, otherwise, with his strength, he wouldn’t have stayed a substitute for so long. 

Due to Jiang Hao being banned, Ling Suyu had to compete. So, to win this match, no matter how many qualms Cui Ran had against Ling Suyu, he could only choose to trust him. 

Regardless, as long as he didn’t let the other feel like he was going to die, wouldn’t it be fine?

Ling Suyu noted that Cui Ran hadn’t left, and cooperatively baited the opposing marksman.

Clearing minions as per usual, he intentionally let Ma Mingxi’s Netherworld Shadow hit him with a skill, before immediately ducking back under his tower. 

Netherworld Shadow’s first skill Soul Suppression was a speed debuff, and Guardian Spirit, who had been hit, directly had its speed cut in half. 

Watching as Netherworld Shadow immediately used his second skill Windchaser to pursue him and unleash a flurry of basic attacks, Ling Suyu didn’t panic at all. He backtracked a few steps, suddenly pressing all three ‘E’, ‘Q’, and ‘W’ keys. On the screen, the fair-skinned elven archer raised her arms, a golden shield spawning around her. Simultaneously, she turned around and unleashed her vines, accurately avoiding the enemy support and trapping Netherworld Shadow!

Closely following, as Guardian Spirit used its second skill “Arrow of Light” and continuously shot three arrows at Netherworld Shadow, UH’s jungler, Cui Rai’s Venomous Scorpion moved out of the undergrowth with “Poison Fog’s Shadow” and used its ultimate on Netherworld Shadow!

The purple-veiled maiden appeared in front of Netherworld Shadow like a phantom. She used her long scorpion’s tail to viciously pierce him, before following up with another attack!

Venomous Scorpion was a hero that used poison to deal damage. Both skills were blocked by the opposing support Abyssal Lord casting a shield, and Netherworld Shadow’s health pretty much didn’t change. 

However, Abyssal Lord’s shield could only relieve Venomous Scorpion’s instantaneous damage, and couldn’t prevent any subsequent damage. Guardian Spirit’s three golden arrows flew indomitably through the air, firmly landing on Netherworld Shadow, instantly depleting his health down to the last bar!

Netherworld Shadow saw that the situation was unfavourable and knew he couldn’t escape, so he could only stubbornly attack Ling Suyu’s Guardian Spirit, thinking he could at least kill them. 

Nevertheless, just as YJ had their support, so did UH. 

Luminous Saintess’s shield and healing steadily protected Ling Suyu’s last remaining bit of health. Netherworld Shadow couldn’t get rid of that last drop of health, and by contrast, due to breaking Guardian Spirit’s shield, he received a wave of counter-damage. 

As the golden shield on Guardian Spirit broke, it immediately turned into countless sharp blades that shot in all directions, hitting both Netherworld Shadow and Abyssal Lord!

【System】Guardian Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Netherworld Shadow has been slain!

The opposing support, watching as his marksman died, suddenly exploded in strength, enduring damage from three of UH’s heroes to throw a speed debuff towards Guardian Spirit. Just as he felt he could get rid of that last sliver of health before his eyes, he heard a tinkling sound, and Guardian Spirit’s health fiercely rose by a small section.

—Ling Suyu lightly tapped his F key, using “Heal”.

Every hero in《Glorious Heroes》could bring two “talents” into battle, and Ling Suyu had always brought Flash and Heal.

After all—

How could you run away without Flash!

How you save your life without Heal!

And so, Abyssal Lord’s “Touch of the Abyss” directly hit Ling Suyu’s Guardian Spirit, but only depleted the health he’d just recovered and not the sliver of health he still had. In the end, he could only watch as the other unleashed a flurry of attacks and sent him back to his fountain. 

【System】Guardian Spirit, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain!

After getting two kills, Ling Suyu decisively turned around: “The enemy’s jungler is coming, let’s go.”

Wen Xu let out an “en”, obediently following him back, and Cui Rao, who had gotten two assists, also left in satisfaction. 

Thus, Tempest Blade, who had finally arrived, couldn’t get even half a kill after scouring the undergrowth near UH’s tower and could only leave with his tail tucked between his legs. 

【What a beautiful gank from UH!】

At the same time, the livestream was replaying the battle from just now, and AhMu commented as he watched: 【Feather’s Guardian Spirit intentionally took a hit from Ma Mingxi’s Netherworld Shadow, successfully leading him into Venomous Scorpion’s attack range! Guardian Spirit unleashed all its skills, Venomous Scorpion directly used its ultimate. YJ’s captain reacted very quickly and instantly opened his shield, but unfortunately it couldn’t stop Guardian Spirit’s explosive damage, and Ma Mingxi’s Netherworld Shadow was killed after being hit by the damage from Guardian Spirit’s shield! YJ’s support knew he couldn't escape and wanted to finish Feather off, but Feather used Heal! This Heal was way too well-timed! YJ was helpless! Feather got a double kill!】

【The fight has only just reached the 8-minute-mark, and Feather already has 4 kills!】ZhiZhi said excitedly, 【UH is cooperating well, and YJ’s pressure is on for this match!】

“Damn!”

In Fight’s practice room, their mid-laner Dou Jiayan suddenly let out a loud shout.

“What?” His teammates looked at him in disdain and exasperation. 

Dou Jiayan coughed, on one hand responding “Nothing” and on the other opening a messaging app to poke at their captain. 

*yanzi:* I think UH can win this round!

Receiving his message, Fight’s captain Tang Yu’an raised an eyebrow and looked to his right, ultimately choosing to respond on the messaging app. 

*tangyuan*: can’t say for sure. also why are you messaging me when you’re right beside me?

*yanzi*: isn’t it just because I’m afraid of disturbing the others’ practice~

*tangyuan*: oh, so you know they’re training? then what are you doing?

*yanzi*: getting to know the enemy, captain~ don’t you think that after UH switched to their substitute, they actually became a lot stronger? This cooperation, this tacit understanding!

tangyuan: that’s YJ panicking.

In that fight just now, UH’s bottom lane and jungler cooperated well and seemingly effortlessly got two kills from YJ. However, the fact that YJ’s marksman fell for such an obvious bait had the potential to be a career-ending move. 

Nevertheless, this player named Feather was also not to be underestimated. 

Able to bait calmly, precisely control the timing of his attacks, with a so-far 100% accuracy rate on his skills. Not having made a single mistake yet, he decisively chose to recall after getting a kill, not bothering to get even a single extra minion. 

Tang Yu’an could tell that although the one ganking was the jungler, the bottom lane’s rhythm was entirely controlled by this marksman named Feather, and he had the utmost cooperation of both the jungler and support.

Even though he was a substitute, he became UH’s core in multiple senses. 

He recalled the scene of the youth taking the microphone before the beginning of the match, calmly retaliating against YJ’s marksman. The corners of Tang Yu’an’s lips subconsciously rose. 

A thought suddenly crossed his mind. 

*tangyuan*: oh right, how much money do you need to poach someone from another team again?

*yanzi*: ???

Dou Jiayan saw this message and looked to his left in bewilderment, but only saw his captain typing with an enthusiastic expression. 

*tangyuan*: poaching a substitute should be cheaper, no?

*yanzi*: …dunno, ask the manager?

tangyuan: sure, I’ll ask later.

Fight’s marksman hurt his hand in the previous season, and in order to preserve his hand, though Tang Yu’an received an invitation to the White Fox Cup competition, he decisively refused and gave the marksman Chen Jie a 1-month break. 

However, in a month they would enter the Spring Seasonal Match, and regardless of whether or not Chen Jie’s hands could recover before it began, he would miss an entire month of training, and it was difficult to say if he could get back into the swing of things. 

Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but make other plans. 

Nonetheless, his “plans” made Dou Jiayan a bit nervous. 

Dou Jiayan sat in his seat for a while, before finally giving in to restlessness.

He abruptly got up and sat down in the empty seat beside Tang Yu’an, taking off Tang Yu’an’s headphones and directly asking: “Captain, you want to poach Feather?”

Tang Yu’an took his headphones and hung them around his neck, turning and facing him with an easygoing attitude: “Something wrong?”

“But you just said that YJ was panicking just now…..”

“YJ panicking is one thing, but whether Feather is a good marksman or not is another.” Tang Yu’an replied calmly, “Nevertheless, the match has just begun, and I just pulled up Feather’s past recordings and haven’t had the chance to look at them yet. Although I have thoughts of poaching, nothing’s set in stone yet, so why are you so nervous?”

“I’m just…” Dou Jiayan puffed out his cheeks and lowered his voice, “worried that after you get a new marksman you won’t care about Jie-ge anymore.”

Hearing this, Tang Yu’an couldn’t resist letting out a “pfft” in laughter: “Look at how you’re talking, those who don’t know better would think that something happened between me and A-Jie.” After a moment, he switched to a more serious tone, “A-Jie is a good marksman, but he pushes himself too hard. It’s…about time he got some time to rest.”

Fight was a team that had just been established not too long ago, and they had just hired their manager two months ago, with Tang Yu’an and Chen Jie sharing the role of coach.

Chen Jie was the oldest member of Fight, with the most mature and steady personality, so pretty much everyone in Fight relied on him.

Because they shared the responsibility of coach, Chen Jie had actually confessed to Tang Yu’an in private that the team’s reliance on him was a huge source of pressure. It was tiring, and he wanted to take a break. 

For the sake of winning the competition, Tang Yu’an encouraged him to hang on, and Chen Jie did, but in the end…..

Tang Yu’an sighed, placing his headphones back on: “There’s not much else to say. Regarding poaching, I’ll be sure to discuss it with him after I’ve decided.”

Not “I’ll decide after discussing it with him”, but “I’ll discuss it with him after I’ve decided”. 

Tang Yu’an had always been like this.

Gentle and easy to talk to on the surface, but once he decided on something, he would do it, and no one could stop him.

Fight’s establishment was the result of his continuous determination.

Knowing he had already made a decision, Dou Jiayan didn’t say anything else and returned to his own seat, though he still felt uneasy. 

In other words, it was unclear who Fight’s future starter marksman would be.

Just as he finished this thought, two kill-notifications appeared on-screen.

【System】Tempest Blade, one kill!

【System】Tempest Blade, double kill! Peerless!

Following these two system notifications, the livestream camera cut to the top-lane, with UH’s top-laner and jungler both falling to the ground. YJ’s Tempest Blade got two kills before swiftly moving in on UH’s top lane’s first lane with a wave of minions, unleashing a flurry of basic attacks. YJ’s top-laner also quickly joined him in attacking.

With a rumbling sound, the first tower on UH’s top lane fell.
