Whether Ling Suyu played Cat Spirit or Desert Hunter, he liked to camp under the tower and farm minions in the early game. Not because he was a coward, but because, in most cases, the enemy would pick burst-damage marksman.

If you got hit by a burst-damage marksman’s full skill combo, you’d either die or be left severely weakened; to secure a counter-kill, you’d have to bide your time.

But in this game, XY picked Cat Spirit, a sustained-damage marksman.

Whether in the early or late game, she relied on auto-attacks for her damage.

When two sustained-damage marksmen faced off, if you took the support out of the equation, there was basically no need to dodge skills through positioning. It was just a matter of trading blows.

In situations like this, whoever seized the initiative survived.

Whoever backed down would die.

Playing a sustained-damage marksman placed high demands on a player’s mindset—they had to be both afraid of dying and brave at the same time.

Because only by being afraid of dying and thus avoiding said death could they create more room to deal more damage.

Only by being brave and daring to trade blows could they ensure they outlasted their opponent and secure their own survival.

If you got scared by your rapidly dropping health during a trade and chose to back off, not only would you fail to escape, but you’d actually be finished off by a braver opponent who chose to seize the opportunity.

Laning against Cat Spirit reminded Ling Suyu of a few years ago, when sustained-damage marksmen dominated the meta.

Back then, his death rate wasn’t low—he was basically guaranteed to die at least once or twice per game.

Why did he die?

It was precisely because he gambled that his opponent would chicken out and retreat, since that was the only way he could survive.

But after reaching the Glorious tier, the opposing marksman’s skill could no longer be underestimated. Neither he nor his opponent backed down. In the end, life or death came down to who landed the final shot first, and sometimes, both ended up dying together.

That feeling can be summed up in two words: so thrilling.

Although Ling Suyu hadn’t laned against sustained-damage marksmen in a long time, the experience and instincts from back then were still etched into his very bones and imprinted deep into his soul.

If laning against a burst-damage marksman was all about playing it safe, then laning against a sustained-damage marksman was all about being aggressive and ruthless!

Facing prey just like himself, the wolf no longer donned a sheep’s clothing nor hid its sharp claws and fangs—instead, it pounced and bit down!

As a professional player, XY’s marksman naturally knew how to gain the upper hand when two sustained-damage marksmen faced off.

If Ling Suyu were playing Desert Hunter, he would similarly consider launching a fierce offensive against Feather right from the start to force him under the tower.

But as it happened, Ling Suyu was playing Dark Raven.

And, as it happened, Ling Suyu managed to land the first strike.

On top of that, Fight’s support was exceptionally aggressive, daring to push into the enemy tower at level 1, and in a way that was practically a suicide mission.

During that exchange, XY’s bot lane had absolutely no way to dodge. As a result, after both players respawned and returned to the lane, they completely retreated under their tower to prevent the same thing from happening again, not daring to take even half a step out, like two defeated creatures cowering before the wolf king.

“Fight’s play just now completely stunned XY!” Remi watched XY’s bottom lane, as if looking at two rabbits cowering in their burrow, trembling after being bullied by a wolf. “Although Fight took a risk with that play, it served as a very effective deterrent. XY’s bottom lane has lost lane control, so they’ll have a really hard time farming now!”

“That’s right.” Liang Fan agreed, “Fight was ruthless! XY has clearly been intimidated! Right now, XY’s jungler has finished clearing the bottom jungle but hasn’t left yet—probably afraid Fight will pull the same trick again and he won’t be able to help the bottom lane in time… Hmm? Fight’s jungler has sneaked into XY’s top jungle!”

Tang Yu’an spoke as he returned to his lane to farm, “The enemy jungler is in the bottom.”

Ling Suyu was a bit puzzled when he heard this. His first thought was that he was being warned not to push too deep—but he hadn’t pushed that deep, had he?

Then, immediately afterward, he saw on the minimap that Yu He’s Blade Soul had sneaked into XY’s top and jungle lanes.

Ling Suyu opened his mouth in surprise but ultimately chose to remain silent.

He wanted to say, “Yu He, what are you doing? That’s way too dangerous!”

But then he thought, oh, he’s playing Blade Soul? Then it’s fine.

Yu He used Blade Soul’s dash ability “Flash of a Blade” to dart from one bush to another, carefully avoiding all of the enemy’s areas of vision, and successfully appeared in the enemy top jungle dragon pit. He lured the green dragon from the pit into the bushes and started slashing away!

When the dragon was down to its last bit of health, and after confirming no one had spotted him, he used Smite to finish it off, simultaneously claiming the gold, experience, and buffs that originally belonged to XY.

After taking down the green dragon without being detected at all, Fight could barely resist the urge to laugh out loud.

The commentators were also in disbelief.

Liang Fan: “XY still hasn’t realized their jungle camp was stolen! Is Fight planning to gank top or mid next?”

“Yanzi, go,” Yu He said calmly.

“Ok~” Dou Jiayan, who had been keeping a safe distance from the enemy Deepsea Faerie and slowly chipping away at her health with just his first skill—clearing the lane much more slowly than Deepsea Faerie—suddenly changed his strategy. He used Flash to close in on Deepsea Faerie and unleashed a combo of his first, second, and third skills!

Phoenix Feather—A feather shot toward Deepsea Faerie!

Rebirth from the Flames—She vanished from her spot, and the feather that had struck Deepsea Faerie suddenly burst into flames, with Phoenix Soul being reborn from the fire!

Sky of Flaming Feathers— Phoenix Soul frantically fired flaming feathers in all directions!

Phoenix Soul dealt zero damage with her ultimate; all her damage came from her basic skills, so you could imagine just how high that damage is.

Even though she had been slightly nerfed this season, that skill combo still instantly reduced Deepsea Faerie’s health to a sliver.

XY’s mid laner Deepsea Faerie was completely unprepared for this attack. Even though he knew Phoenix Soul had already used its Flash and that he could have counter-attacked to secure a kill at that moment, upon realizing that Fight’s jungler was nearby, he immediately chose to retreat rather than engage. He Flash-escaped under the tower, on the side closest to his own jungle.

Then, without pausing, he immediately fled towards his fountain.

His hunch was right—Fight’s jungler was indeed nearby.

But his instincts were also wrong—Fight’s jungler wasn’t over by the river, but right in his own jungle. Surprised? Unexpected?

So in the next instant, Yu He’s Blade Soul emerged from the nearby brush with a “Blade Flash,” appearing right beside him. He finished off the low-health enemy with a “Blade Shadow Combo,” used “Absolute Defense” to absorb the tower’s damage, emerged from the tower at full health, and rode off triumphantly.

【System】 Blade Soul, one kill!

【System】 The enemy Deepsea Faerie has been slain!

Deepsea Faerie, who hadn’t expected Blade Soul to be in his own jungle, was startled the moment Blade Soul flashed out—just like when something that shouldn’t be there suddenly appears on the screen while watching a horror movie. He was so scared he completely forgot how to play, though Blade Soul hadn’t really given him any room to react anyway.

It wasn’t until Blade Soul had left the tower and vanished into his own bottom jungle that he finally snapped out of it: “They stole our camps, f*ck! Jungler! Watch your jungle!”

“How much HP does he have?” The jungler replied, still relatively calm, as he steered his Tempest Blade toward the spot where Yu He had disappeared. Before he could even react, a young girl with a longsword taller than her person hovering beside her suddenly flashed right in front of him!

Flash of a Blade! Blade Shadow Combo!

XY’s jungler Tempest Blade instantly lost half his health and let out a “F*ck!”

Especially when he saw that Blade Soul was at full health.

He dove the tower to finish off their mid laner, and was still at full health?!

By the time the mid laner tried to respond “full health,” it was already too late. He could only watch helplessly as his jungler used Flash to escape to the bot lane. Blade Soul decisively used Flash to give chase, landing one auto-attack and leaving just two more to go.

At this point, Tempest Blade had red and blue buffs—healing and mana regeneration. Although his health was critically low, it was recovering at a visible rate.

However, Blade Soul’s buffs were green and purple—healing and lightning strike—which increased his movement speed and attack speed, with three basic attacks triggering the lightning strike effect.

XY’s bottom lane spotted the fight in their bottom jungle and rushed over immediately; Cat Spirit even used a dash ability.

Almost simultaneously, Fight’s bottom lane also headed toward XY’s bottom jungle.

Just as XY’s Cat Spirit was about to arrive, Tempest Blade, knowing he was as good as dead, stopped fleeing. Instead, he countered with a “Razor Gale,” followed immediately by “Tornado”!

Razor Gale: Fires a wind blade forward.

Tornado: Transforms into a tornado to pursue enemies, gaining damage reduction during the pursuit.

He wanted to chip away at Blade Soul’s health a bit more to make it easier for his marksman to take him down, but the moment he started his combo, Yu He activated “Absolute Defense” without batting an eye.

Absolute Defense: Blade Soul’s soul blade circled around its body, automatically blocking all damage.

Yes, all of it.

In the next instant, Blade Soul landed a basic attack, finishing off Tempest Blade with a single strike while remaining at full health.

【System】 Blade Soul, double kill! Peerless!

【System】 The enemy Tempest Blade has been slain!

Just as the Tempest Blade fell, XY’s marksman, Cat Spirit, arrived on the scene.

Cat Spirit locked onto Blade Soul and began auto-attacking!

Yu He’s Blade Soul didn’t dodge or flinch; it chased after Cat Spirit and returned the basic attacks. Since it had just finished cutting down Tempest Blade, the first strike triggered a lightning strike. Cat Spirit’s health bar dropped significantly, while Blade Soul’s health, after taking a small hit, was fully restored thanks to lifesteal and the buffs it had stolen from Tempest Blade.

After two consecutive exchanges, Cat Spirit was down to two-thirds health, while the Blade Soul was at full health.

XY’s marksman: “……”

This pitiful damage was truly enough to break one’s heart and bring tears to the eyes of anyone who witnessed it.

In no time, Tang Yu'an and Ling Suyu arrived. Tang Yu'an rushed forward and brought his hammer down on Cat Spirit! Ling Suyu’s Dark Raven stood its ground, firing off a flurry of shots—whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!

【System】 Dark Raven, one kill!

【System】 The enemy Cat Spirit has been slain!

【System】 Dark Raven, double kill! Peerless!

【System】 The enemy Spider Queen has been slain!

XY’s bot lane duo: “……”

The rest of XY: “……”

That all-too-familiar double kill—truly enough to break hearts and bring tears to the eyes of anyone who heard it!

“Alright, Yumao, go ahead and farm the jungle.” Yu He strode off, leaving Ling Suyu with his back turned, “This jungle’s all mine.”

“Haha—” Ling Suyu couldn’t help but laugh at Yu He’s remark.

To be honest, that domineering CEO-style declaration—coming from Yu He, a teenager who seemed expressionless on the surface but was actually a bit of a drama queen, always trying to act cool and on occasion very punchable—didn’t come across as annoying at all; on the contrary, it actually made him seem quite reliable.

Under Tang Yu’an’s protection, Ling Suyu quickly took down the two newly spawned camps in XY’s bottom jungle, then returned to the bottom lane to continue farming minions. He used the minion wave to poke the tower for a while, and once the minions were gone, he returned to base to replenish his health and buy some gear.

XY, who had vowed before the start of the second game to dominate Fight, now watched as the economic gap between the two teams widened, feeling like crying but unable to.

The impression XY gave, aside from their preference for tanky, high-defense champions, was that they were very slow to get going. They were always out of sync at the start of the game but got more and more fired up as the game progressed. This wasn’t just true in-game—they also adjusted their composition for each match based on the previous one, playing with increasingly targeted strategies that made them increasingly difficult to handle.

However, in this match against Fight, their “team strategy” fell apart.

In Game 2, despite fielding a new lineup, not only did they fail to create a situation that Fight found more challenging, they actually ended up in a much tougher spot at Fight’s hands.

And all of this could be traced back to one question: Why did Feather pick Dark Raven instead of Desert Hunter?!

So, in Feather’s pick preferences, did Dark Raven come before Desert Hunter?!

So, during the group stage, even when playing against igame, had Fight been pulling all their punches[^1]?!

If Ling Suyu could hear the rage roaring in their hearts, he’d surely feel wronged.

During the group stage, Desert Hunter was indeed his second pick. While they did let up a bit, it was only to the extent of a Dongting Lake[^2]—it certainly wasn’t on the scale of the Pacific Ocean.

However, after letting out a mental scream and calming down a bit, XY quickly realized there was another thorny problem.

“How is Yu He’s Blade Soul this strong?”

“Rather than saying he’s strong, it’s more that he’s incredibly bold! How dare he go counter-jungling all by himself?”

If XY had been facing igame today, they would have kept a constant eye on the jungle, because igame’s jungler was elusive as a ghost, with counter-jungling ingrained in his very soul. If he could counter-jungle, he would; if he couldn’t, he’d still take a gamble.

Ultimately, this was because igame revolved around their jungler; when the jungler shone, the entire team thrived.

However, for Fight, a team centered around their marksman, they either counter-jungle right from the start or push the entire team into the jungle late in the game, because even if they counter-jungled, the gold still went to the marksman.

But in this game, Yu He, breaking with his usual style, snuck into the jungle alone and secured the camps himself. Although he still gave the bot lane kill to his own marksman, his current gold count was the highest on the entire map.

XY couldn’t figure it out, but then they suddenly thought of igame, and it all clicked.

“No wonder they’re igame’s archrivals. Were they provoked by igame’s jungler?”

“So now we’re not just playing against Fight—we’re also playing against igame?”

“What a truly heartwarming display of team-to-team camaraderie.”

igame’s captain Ye Xunjie, who had been watching the game after their own match finished: “Ah-choo…who’s cursing me?”
