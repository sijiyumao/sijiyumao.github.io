Due to Ling Suyu’s virality, Fight was already the most talked-about team of the season, and after the first day of the qualifiers, their popularity doubled.

【It looks like the championship this season will come down to Fight and Winne,

right?】

【Did you eat YJ?】

【OP, you’re getting ahead of yourself. It’s still just the qualifiers, too early to tell.】

【Even though the qualifiers are a mixed bag, they’re all pro teams. I’ve never seen a

team dominate a match quite like this, especially during Fight’s first match against LR today. The whole thing lasted less than 10 minutes, and the Dragon God hadn’t even spawned yet. This is just insane!】

【To be fair, they’ve got a point. Even last year’s champions, Winne, never crushed LR

this badly.】

【Let’s see how Fight fares against CSD tomorrow. CSD made it to the Top 16 last

season, after all, so they shouldn’t lose too badly.】

Then, the next day, it was time for the Fight vs. CSD match.

【Hello everyone, I’m commentator Ah-Mu!】

【I’m commentator ZhiZhi—】

Ah-Mu: “We’ll be providing the commentary for this afternoon’s matches again!”

Zhizhi: “That’s right! And the first match is the one everyone’s been eagerly awaiting— Fight’s game! Their opponent today is CSD. I wonder what kind of surprises they’ll have in store for us!”

Ah-Mu: 【Unlike Fight’s opponent yesterday, CSD was one of the teams that successfully qualified for the group stage last season and also participated in the White Fox Cup at the end of last year.】

ZhiZhi: 【Speaking of which, as we all know, CSD only got the chance to participate in the White Fox Cup because Fight, one of last season’s quarterfinalists, was absent from the tournament. Although CSD ultimately didn’t achieve their desired ranking in the White Fox Cup, it was still a valuable experience! Today’s match can truly be called a “destined clash”!】

Ah-Mu: [Hahaha, calling it a “destined” might be a bit of an exaggeration, but the fact that CSD just “stepped in” for Fight at the “White Fox Cup” and is now facing Fight in the spring qualifiers really makes you marvel at how fate works!] ZhiZhi: “Without further ado, let’s tune in to the match between Fight and CSD!”

As the two casters spoke, the live stream switched to the in-game view. At this point, both teams had just finished their ban and pick phase and hadn’t selected their champions yet.

“Which hero will Feather pick today? Will he go with Desert Hunter again?” ZhiZhi wondered.

【It’s hard to say. Against CSD, it’s safer to pick a meta-strong hero. But if Feather is

confident enough in his Desert Hunter, continuing to pick him isn’t out of the question…】

Before commentator Ah-Mu could finish his sentence, Fight, having selected their mid laner, picked Desert Hunter for their second slot!

“Desert Hunter!” Ah-Mu exclaimed excitedly. 【Desert Hunter again! Facing CSD, Feather is still sticking with Desert Hunter as his champion! To be honest, if he continues to pick this champion after advancing to the group stages, I don’t think anyone would disagree if I called him ‘Glorious’s Number One Desert Hunter,’

right?】

“I’d be the first to give him my full support! ” ZhiZhi said, 【He’s the first player to dare use Desert Hunter on the pro circuit after auto-attack damage was nerfed! If he can keep winning matches with Desert Hunter in the group stages, he’ll definitely deserve the title of “Glorious’s Number One Desert Hunter”!】

Ling Suyu, whose main hero was Cat Spirit, silently clicked on the Desert Hunter on his screen with his mouse, triggering interactions; Tang Yu’an glanced over and noticed his little gesture, unable to help but chuckle: “Desert Hunter is pretty cool too. Look at those ripped abs—so sexy. Why isn’t he your favorite character?”

“He’s not handsome enough,” Ling Suyu blurted out, only to realize his mistake a moment later. “Uh?”

Tang Yu’an changed the subject instantly: “Got it, you like animal ears.”

“No, that’s not it…” Ling Suyu’s kink was exposed, and he hurriedly protested, “I like Cat Spirit because of her semi-transparent appearance! It’s just… it’s not easy for others to notice her, which makes me feel really safe!”

“Yumao, no way you’re actually responding to him!” Dou Jiayan couldn’t stand it anymore and spoke up to back Ling Suyu against the captain. “Captain, what’s wrong with you? You can’t even take the match seriously!”

Yu He chimed in: “You’re always picking on Yumao.”

Mo Xiaoyun added: “Stop messing with Yumao right now. Let’s just focus on the match.”

“Man, what did I do? I just asked why Yumao likes the cat but not the hunter.” Tang Yu’an said, sounding aggrieved. “Fine, fine. I get that you all spoil Yumao, that’s great. With your protection, Yumao will be completely invincible!”

Ling Suyu blinked a few times. In the end, he didn’t dwell on whether the “protection” Tang Yu’an was referring to meant protection in the game or something else; he simply gave a serious “Mm-hmm.”

Back in UH, even though he clearly had teammates, he often felt like he was fighting all by himself.

Now that he was with Fight, in every match, and even in his everyday life, he could feel his teammates’ presence and the bond between them. He felt like a kite fluttering in the wind that had finally found someone willing to hold onto it, with a clear direction for the future. His whole being felt much more grounded.

He contentedly enjoyed his teammates’ protection, and in return, he would deliver his maximum damage output!

Having learned from the mistakes of the previous two teams, CSD played this game very cautiously. Their marksman stayed tucked under the tower farming minions, not daring to leave the tower even when their own jungler came to gank.

However, even though they hadn’t actively baited him, after finishing a wave of minions, Ling Suyu retreated back to his own tower as if he’d realized something.

As soon as Tang Yu’an saw him retreat, he immediately issued a command: “Yu He, come down and check the bushes.”

“Got it,” Yu He replied, immediately putting aside the jungle he was halfway through clearing and rushing to the bottom lane, where his Tempest Blade cast “Swift Blade” directly into the bushes.

Just as he cast his first skill, Tang Yu'an led Ling Suyu out from under the tower.

The CSD jungler had been quietly waiting to gank the bot lane, but he never expected to be caught off guard by Fight before he could even make a move, leaving him with no choice but to fight defensively.

Tang Yu’an dashed over and cast a crowd control spell, simultaneously locking down the marksman and the support. Ling Suyu followed up with “Hound’s Charge,” ducking behind him, raising his gun, and taking aim—bang, bang, bang!

Take out the marksman first!

Then the jungler!

Finish off the support!

Triple kill, perfect!

【Fight’s jungler, Tempest Blade, lands a precise strike with his blade, hitting

Venomous Scorpion, who was hiding in the bushes! Tang Yu’an dashes over with a crowd control spell and initiates the team fight! They’re CC’d! Feather follows up with a movement skill! CSD’s marksman falls before he even has a chance to use Flash!

CSD’s jungler tries to escape, but Feather doesn’t give him a chance! Double kill!

Great! CSD’s support used Flash to retreat under the tower, Tang Yu’an pushed in, and Feather followed with Flash—triple kill! Beautiful!】

Ah-Mu commented on the entire sequence at breakneck speed before taking a breath to continue: 【Feather has once again secured an advantage on the bottom lane!

Fight’s performance in the Spring Seasonals can only be described as “unstoppable”!

Does CSD still have a chance to stage a comeback?】

“By the way, how did Fight know CSD’s jungler was in the bot lane?” ZhiZhi wondered.

“CSD’s jungler clearly arrived at the bot lane first, but Fight initiated the team fight. It’s almost as if Fight has a god’s-eye view!”

“I don’t think Fight was certain whether CSD’s jungler was actually in the bot lane, but Fight’s bot lane performed so exceptionally well in the first two games. If I were CSD’s coach, I’d have the jungler target the bot lane. And if I can predict that tactic, I’m sure Fight’s coach can too. He would definitely have reminded his team before the match to watch out for the enemy jungler ganking bot lane,” Ah-Mu analyzed.

“Ah, that makes so much sense!” ZhiZhi was convinced. 【So this play was actually a case of ‘I predicted your prediction’?】

【That’s probably it!】

Actually, it wasn’t that complicated at all.

It was simply that Ling Suyu thought, “At this moment, the enemy jungler is very likely to gank the bot lane,” then Tang Yuan saw him hesitate and thought, “Quick, let’s check if the enemy jungler is really there,” and then Yu He landed a hit showing that “Wow, he really is there,” and then—“Kill, kill, kill!”

So in this game, CSD, who had insisted “Don’t ever let the Desert Hunter get a chance to farm,” ended up handing Fight a wave of experience with their own hands.

And with that economic advantage, Fight couldn’t wait to show them just how terrifying a fully-developed Desert Hunter could be!

After CSD’s marksman, support, and jungler respawned, they immediately rushed back to the bottom lane, but it was already too late.

Fight took advantage of their deaths to quickly secure Divine Sunbird and, in the process, severely damaged their mid laner who had come to check on the situation.

Although CSD’s mid laner managed to escape, he was low on health and had to return to base to heal, meaning that it took quite some time before he could get back to his lane.

Fight placed the Divine Sunbird in the mid lane, and both teams clashed under CSD’s mid-lane first tower. Fight pulled off a 2-for-5 trade, wiping out CSD’s entire team.

And that was that.

The economic gap between the two teams continued to widen as Fight pushed relentlessly, providing a powerful demonstration of what it meant to be “unstoppable”!

In the end, the match lasted 18 minutes, and Fight secured their victory!

“Congratulations to Fight on another win!” ZhiZhi congratulated Fight, saying, “With this win, Fight’s advancement to the group stage is practically a foregone conclusion. Although I never imagined Fight losing in the first place, I didn’t expect them to dominate the match to this extent!”

“Can we say CSD fought honorably despite the loss today?” Ah-Mu asked, sounding uncertain. “After all, compared to Fight’s opponent yesterday, they almost held out until the 20-minute mark!”

“Whoa, with that one comment, you’ve just offended three teams!” ZhiZhi said, quickly trying to smooth things over. “It looks like Fight is in great form this season, with a strong shot at the championship. I can’t wait to see how they perform next!”

Compared to Fight’s opponent from yesterday, CSD could indeed be considered to have “lost with honor,” but getting swept by Fight in under 20 minutes was still a rather embarrassing defeat.

After this match, the voices claiming that “Fight’s decisive victory was entirely due to their opponents being weak” suddenly dwindled. It was as if those who had made such remarks had vanished into thin air.

After all, CSD was one of the 16 teams that made it to the group stages last season. If even they were considered weak, that would leave only the top eight teams as the “strong” ones.

That night, the post on the Glorious Heroes official forum titled 《Stop Overhyping Feather, Don’t Go Too Far》 was pushed to the top by an unknown user, and the discussion heated up rapidly, staying on the front page all night.

Scrolling down to the bottom, the very first reply that day read:

【Three games in, how are your faces holding up? Come out here for a moment so I

can take a look?】
