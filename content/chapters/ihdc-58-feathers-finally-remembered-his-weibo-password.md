Sure enough, once Tang Yu’an said, “Don’t worry,” Ling Suyu truly didn’t have to worry at all.

The poses he struck for the poster were thought up by Tang Yu’an; the concept for the team video was organized and discussed by Tang Yu’an; and the final script was finalized through discussions between Tang Yu’an and the professional team provided by the organizers. As for what he needed to do and say in the video, he just had to follow Tang Yu’an’s instructions.

Tang Yu’an had always been like this. If you had ideas, he’d take them on board. If you didn’t, he’d come up with them himself. If everything was fine, you could go along with it. If there were concerns, you’d work it out together.

His decision-making skills were off the charts!

Ling Suyu silently watched Tang Yu’an bustling about, admiring him and also wanting to help, yet afraid of being a burden. All he could do was tug at the hem of his shirt and hand him a bottle of water when he was busy.

Tang Yu’an felt his shirt hem being tugged and turned around in confusion. Before he could even react, a bottle of water was pressed against his face.

He instinctively took the water, then immediately saw Ling Suyu standing before him with a reserved expression on his face. He couldn’t help but laugh and reached out to ruffle Ling Suyu’s hair.

Ling Suyu let him ruffle his hair, his conflicted expression seemingly say, “Well, seeing how hard you’re working, I guess I’ll make a sacrifice.”

Although he didn’t mind Tang Yu’an ruffling his hair, doing it too often still made him feel a little put out.

He didn’t want Tang Yu’an to treat him like a child. He hoped that, even if he needed protection, Tang Yu’an would still see him as an adult.

Perhaps sensing his thoughts, Tang Yu’an quickly withdrew his hand and patted him on the back: “You’re just in time. I left my phone in my room. Could you go get it for me?”

“Sure—” Ling Suyu agreed immediately. Far from finding it a bother, he actually felt a sense of joy at finally being able to be of use, and cheerfully headed upstairs.

It wasn’t until he was halfway up that it dawned on him—Wait a minute? In ‘my’ room? Tang Yu’an’s room?

Having lived at Fight’s headquarters for so many days, Ling Suyu naturally knew where Tang Yu’an’s room was.

But he’d never actually been inside it.

On the other hand, Tang Yu’an often popped into his room for all sorts of reasons—like asking if he wanted a spare hanger, offering to share snacks he’d bought, or asking what he thought of a new outfit he’d put on…

Huh?

Why did it feel like Tang Yu’an was always finding all sorts of excuses to peek into his room?

Even though they lived under the same roof, none of Fight’s other members had ever really come into his room. Only Tang Yu’an did.

For a split second, Ling Suyu suspected Tang Yu’an was doing it on purpose, but he quickly dismissed the thought.

How could he think that about his captain?

Still, if Tang Yu’an really was doing it on purpose, Ling Suyu wouldn’t feel uncomfortable at all—in fact, he’d be a little pleased.

The dorm rooms of Fight’s players had locks, and on Ling Suyu’s first day there, Tang Yu’an had given him a key to his room.

However, for convenience, and because of the trust they shared, even though the doors had locks, almost no one ever locked theirs.

Tang Yu'an was no different.

Ling Suyu walked to the door of Tang Yu'an’s room, feeling inexplicably nervous.

He tentatively reached out to grasp the doorknob, pressed it down gently, and the door swung open.

As the door opened, a clean room filled with a gaming atmosphere unfolded before his eyes.

Posters for《Glorious Heroes》adorned the clean white walls, and on the desk sat a closed laptop with a chibi sticker of Skeletal Empress on its black case.

A black cell phone lay next to the laptop. Ling Suyu walked over, picked it up, and saw Fight’s logo printed right in the center of the black case, confirming it was Tang Yu’an’s phone.

Although he’d found Tang Yu’an’s phone almost effortlessly, Ling Suyu couldn’t help but take a few more glances around Tang Yu’an’s room before leaving.

Tang Yu’an clearly had more stuff in his room than he did. The bookshelf was crammed full with all kinds of game guides, character art books, and… hey, wasn’t that a handheld console? And there were so many game cartridges!

Monster Hunter! He’d played it for a while, using a bow. At first, he couldn’t aim at all, but later he got really good at it. He wondered what weapon Tang Yu’an used?

The Legend of Zelda? He’d heard it was amazing, though he hadn’t played yet. He wondered if Tang Yu’an would let him borrow if he asked?

Hmm… Although he’d yet to ask, he had a feeling he’d be willing.

As Ling Suyu looked around, he found himself getting completely absorbed.

Tang Yu’an’s room was much cleaner than he’d imagined. Although the comforter wasn’t folded, it was neatly spread out on the bed. Even though there were a lot of things, they were all stacked neatly, making the room look very pleasant at a glance.

Most importantly, he had so many game cartridges that Ling Suyu couldn’t help but feel envious.

It was clear that Tang Yu’an really loved gaming. It wasn’t just 《Glorious Heroes》; he seemed to want to try all types of new games.

Ling Suyu felt the same way, but since he’d entered 《Glorious Heroes》’s professional scene so early, there were many games he’d only heard of but never actually tried. Seeing so many game cards in Tang Yu’an’s room made him eager to give them a go.

Although he wanted to stay and look around a little longer, knowing that Tang Yu’an was still waiting for him downstairs, Ling Suyu reluctantly left his room, closing the door as quietly as possible, as if afraid of disturbing something.

In reality, he hadn’t stayed very long, but when he went downstairs to hand his phone to Tang Yu’an, Tang Yu’an asked with a smile, “What took you so long? Did my room captivate you?”

Ling Suyu instinctively wanted to deny it, but instead just shifted his gaze away guiltily.

Tang Yu’an could tell from his reaction that he had indeed been captivated, so he probed, “I have a lot of game cards. Once the Spring Season is over, if there’s anything you want to play, we can play together. Kirby Star Allies is out too. I tried it out and it felt pretty good. I’ve already ordered the card, but I don’t know when it’ll ship.”

“Huh?” Ling Suyu was pretty happy when he heard the first part, but when he heard the second part… “How can you be paying attention to other games while you’re in a tournament!”

Ling Suyu was this close to blurting out the words “not focusing on what you should be doing.” Seeing the look of disappointment on his face, as if he were scolding a wayward child, Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but chuckle, but immediately put on an innocent expression: “I pre-ordered the game. The Spring Season hadn’t even started yet when I placed the order.”

Ling Suyu: “Don’t think about your console while we’re competing!”

Tang Yu’an: “Yes, yes, yes. You’re absolutely right! It’s my fault. I’ll definitely change!”

“Uh…” Ling Suyu looked at his half-hearted response and was speechless for a moment, but then he couldn’t help but smile exasperatedly.

He really couldn’t stay mad at this guy for even a second.

Oh well, as long as it didn’t affect the match, what was the big deal?

With that in mind, Ling Suyu softened his voice and said, “Okay.”

“Huh?” Tang Yu’an didn’t catch on.

Ling Suyu had no choice but to clarify: “Let’s play together after the Spring Seasonals.”

“Mm-hmm~” Tang Yu’an fiddled with his phone, smiling happily.

After the individual poster shoot wrapped up, Fight put together a nine-grid collage and posted it on Weibo.

The first photo was the promotional poster for the quarterfinal points round, the second was Tang Yu’an, the third was Mo Xiaoyun, the fourth was Dou Jiayan, and the fifth—the one right in the center of the nine-grid collage—was Ling Suyu.

The sixth was Yu He, the seventh was Chen Jie, the eighth was Ye Xiran covering his face with his hand, and the ninth was Fight’s logo.

The caption read: “The carry should be right in the middle!”

Fight’s fans were amused when they saw this Weibo post.

【Wow, other teams either put their quarterfinal round results or own logo in the center, but you guys put Feather right in the middle.】

【Hahaha, the carry’s definitely the center! It’s basically confirmed that Feather is Fight’s darling!】

【By the way, the captain’s spot is right above Feather—was that on purpose?】

【When Feather isn’t smiling, he really gives off this aloof vibe. He’s clearly a cute little thing in videos, but in photos, he turns into an icy beauty.】

【I’m the icy beauty’s fan! Feather is totally badass!】

In Ling Suyu’s photo, there’s nothing particularly striking about his pose—he was simply staring expressionlessly at the camera with his head tilted slightly upwards, his whole demeanour saying “deal with it.” He seemed like someone you wouldn’t want to mess with.

But when they were actually taking this photo...

Tang Yu’an: “Tilt your head up a little.”

Ling Suyu glanced at the ceiling.

Tang Yu’an: “Haha…Look this way. Just tilt it up a tiny bit.”

Ling Suyu turned back to look at Tang Yu’an and blinked.

Tang Yu’an: “That’s right, lift it just a little. Don’t keep it perfectly straight, tilt your head a bit.”

Ling Suyu tilted his head.

Tang Yu’an: “Pfft—hahaha!”

Ling Suyu tilted his head even more: ?

Anyways, even though Ling Suyu’s “icy beauty” vibe came across naturally when he wasn’t smiling, it still took a bit of fiddling to get the photo to look as cool as it did.

And you had to admit, good-looking people really were photogenic.

Take the solo posters, for instance: when Chen Jie took one, it looked like a public service announcement, but when Ling Suyu took one, it looked like a celebrity’s official headshot.

But…

【How come Chen Jie’s got one too?】

【Forget Chen Jie, even the manager’s got one (though you can’t see his face)】

【Chen Jie’s a substitute, why wouldn’t he have one? The manager was probably just thrown in to make up the numbers, right? Haha, only Fight would pull something like this!】

【Sigh. Just to see Feather’s photos, I forced myself to scroll through every single one of UH’s Weibo posts despite feeling uncomfortable, but I couldn’t find a single photo of him. Were they deleted?】

【No, Feather never had any photos taken while he was at UH. I was a UH fan last year, but I quit the fandom this year and came back to troll them, all for Fight and Feather】

【F*ck! UH is unreal! YJ took photos for their substitutes again this time, right?】

【YJ’s subs have always been photographed. All I have to say is, Feather was technically a substitute at UH, but in reality, he was benched.】

【Trash UH! I’m going back to send them ten thousand hate comments!】

【Remember to use an alt account!】

UH never expected that simply shooting a poster would “mysteriously” land them a bunch of haters. The person in charge of managing their Weibo account worked their fingers to the bone trying to control the comments.

After Tang Yu’an logged into Fight’s official account and posted the nine-grid collage, he reminded the other members of Fight to help repost it.

Upon hearing this, Ling Suyu logged into his long-abandoned Weibo account for the first time in two years.

—He reposted the Weibo post from Fight’s official account and added a “cool and aloof” dog emoji.

He’d intended to see if Tang Yu’an would like his post, but after waiting just a moment, the comments under his repost began piling up at a frantic pace, leaving him stunned.

What was going on?

He tapped in, feeling a bit anxious:

【Ahhh, Feather’s finally remembered his Weibo password!!】

【So I wasn’t following the wrong person after all? I’m crying tears of joy!】

【Hahaha, Feather knows that the dog head’s a PR saver!】

【Go for it, Feather! Beat UH and take the championship!】

【Feather, look here! Don’t feel like you’re inferior, don’t carry any mental burden. Whether now or in the future, I’ll always support you!】

As Ling Suyu read on, his expression shifted from its initial unease to surprise, and then to deep emotion.

His expression gradually softened, and the corners of his lips subconsciously curled upwards.

It turned out that, unbeknownst to him, so many people were rooting for him.

He wanted to respond to them—he didn’t want to let them down.

With that in mind, Ling Suyu couldn’t help but post another Weibo: “I’ll win the championship. Fighting!”
