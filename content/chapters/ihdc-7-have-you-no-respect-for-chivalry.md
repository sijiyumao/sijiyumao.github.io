《Glorious Heroes》 had 9 tiers, and from bottom to top they were Novice, Apprentice, Adventurer, Newcomer, Bounty Hunter, Fabled, Valiant, Famed, and Glorious Hero.

Novice was divided into 3 sub-tiers, Apprentice into 4 sub-tiers, and the tiers from Adventurer to Famed had 5 sub-tiers each.

At the Glorious tier, rather than sub-tiers, there were personal rankings.

Ling Suyu’s ID UH-feather was a professional account at the Glorious tier, with a personal ranking of 106.

As such, if there weren’t any unexpected situations, all of his teammates and opponents would be around this rank.

《Glorious Heroes》didn’t allow for custom teams of 4. If it wasn’t a 5-stack, then it could only be solo, duo, or trio.

Ling Suyu decided to check.

【Ally】UH-feather: solo?

“Yep, solo.” Tang Yu’an replied through voice chat, before saying, “your microphone’s already on, so why are you still typing? Isn’t voice-chatting more convenient?”

Indeed, after the two of them had turned on their mics and speakers, their other teammates had also turned on their speakers, albeit not their microphones.

After all, not everyone liked to voice chat while gaming.

Of course, this didn’t rule out the possibility of simply being in an environment where voice chatting would be inconvenient.

The others used the chat box to reply to Ling Suyu:

【Ally】can die but no need: Yeah, solo.

【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler: solo queue.

【Ally】if I have feet I can run: solo.

A full team of solo queues, which meant that the other side was likely also in a similar situation.

As Ling Suyu thought about this, the other side had already quickly chosen their ban positions, and it was their turn to pick their heroes first.

“tangyuan with sesame filling”, who was first in the pick order, didn’t even hesitate before locking in Cat Spirit.

As Cat Spirit was a hero with a very low max health, and because she was too fragile and could easily be killed in a flash, she had a very low usage rate in the professional field.

In addition, she was a marksman that relied on continuous basic attacks. After the damage dealt by basic attacks was tuned down this version, she directly became a bottom-tier hero.

Much less the professional field, even using her in ranked and regular matches would invite a scolding from one’s teammates.

However, perhaps due to their trust in him as a pro-player, surprisingly, when Ling Suyu said he wanted to choose Cat Spirit, none of his teammates objected.

This allowed Ling Suyu to breathe a sigh of relief, and he felt quite happy.

After Tang Yu’an helped Ling Suyu pick Cat Spirit, the other side picked a mid-laner, Deepsea Faerie.

On their turn, the second-in-line “I’ve never one with jungler” insta-locked “Spider Queen”, the support that Tang Yu’an wanted.

“Wait a minute. What positions do you all play?” Ling Suyu only just thought of this problem.

【Ally】if i have feet i can run: I’m a mid-laner.

【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler: I’m a jungler.

【Ally】can die but no need: ?

【Ally】can die but no need: then i’ll top-lane.

“‘I’ve never won with jungler’ playing jungler?” Tang Yu’an chuckled lightly, “You sure?”

【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler: this is what’s called a reverse flag.

“Alright, then once we get in, listen to my command. Gank when I tell you to, and I guarantee you’ll get kills,” Tang Yu’an said with confidence.

【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler: we’ll see.

【Ally】if i have feet i can run: Feather isn’t commanding?

Ling Suyu was startled for a moment, before replying: “Let the support command.”

Although he didn’t really trust the commanding abilities of strangers, he had no experience in commanding himself, and had neither the courage nor confidence to command.

Since the support volunteered to be the commander, why not let him?

As they were chatting, the other side picked out their second hero, Tempest Blade.

This was YJ’s jungler Xiao Hong’s most commonly used hero, and it was unknown whether the other side had done it on purpose.

Then, on Ling Suyu’s side, “can die but no need” locked their own top-lane hero, Skeletal Empress.

Swiftly following, the other side chose their marksman, Guardian Spirit.

Upon seeing Guardian Spirit, Ling Suyu wasn’t surprised at all.

As one of the marksmen that dealt the highest explosive damage, Guardian Spirit’s usage rate in the professional field had reached nearly 100%.

If it weren’t for this version’s strongest mid-laners already taking up the ban positions, Ling Suyu even wanted to leave a ban spot for her.

Since the other side had chosen Guardian Spirit, then they’d probably choose Abyssal Lord as their support.

As expected, after Ling Suyu helped his teammate lock onto the jungler hero Blade Soul, the other side locked Abyssal Lord.

In the end, “if i have feet i can run” chose “Vampiric Progenitor” as his own mid-lane hero, and the other side chose their top-lane, Black-Armoured Scythe.

Once everyone had picked their heroes, they began switching with one another.

Ling Suyu finally got the Cat Spirit he had been longing for, and Tang Yu’an took the Spider Queen.

Ling Suyu glanced at the two sides’ lineups.

In the order of top-lane, mid-lane, marksman, support, and jungler, the respective heroes were:

Ally: Skeletal Empress, Vampiric Progenitor, Cat Spirit, Spider Queen, Blade Soul.

Enemy: Black-Armoured Scythe, Deepsea Faerie, Guardian Spirit, Abyssal Lord, Tempest Blade.

How should he put it? Looking closely, the heroes chosen by both sides were quite similar.

Both top-lanes chose high-defense tanks, the mid-laners brawlers that could heal while fighting, and the junglers agile and low-health assassins that could deal sweeping damage with their ultimates.

The only differences lay in the bottom lane.

Cat Spirit and Spider Queen; agile survivability, continuous damage.

Guardian Spirit and Abyssal Lord; protective shielding, quick and explosive damage.

On one side, it was a combination with a low tolerance for error, a ‘maybe’ team with the amount of damage all depending on the skills of the players.

On the other side was a combination with a high tolerance for error, one of this version’s strongest teams that could practically dominate the early game with their eyes closed.

Just looking at the team composition, anyone would think that the opposing team had the advantage.

However, the appeal of 《Glorious Heroes》lay in the fact that, with sufficient skills and maneuvering, any hero could become top-tier.

Soon, the countdown ended, and the match began!

Ling Suyu watched the Cat Spirit in the middle of his screen, feeling very pleased.

Everyone who played 《Glorious Heroes》had their own favourite hero.

For some, it was the hero they were best at.

For some, it was the hero they felt was the easiest to play.

And others liked them simply because they liked the design.

Ling Suyu’s favourite was Cat Spirit.

This flat-chested girl with a full body of snow-white fur, two soft cat ears, beautiful heterochromatic eyes, and two tails that were gradually becoming transparent, was practically an outline of the person Ling Suyu desired to become(?) Ling Suyu had started playing Cat Spirit because he liked her appearance. As time went on, she became the hero he was the most specialized in, and was also the hero he had the best feel for.

Although she had been nerfed pretty much every season, Ling Suyu still loved to play her, to the point of wanting to use her every match.

After all…..

Fast movement speed! And a speed-boosting skill! She was the best marksman for escaping!

Nevertheless, he didn’t use his first skill point to light up his second skill “Shadow Steps”, but instead gave it to his first skill “Spirit Mark”, which let him mark an enemy target and increase his damage.

He wanted to steal level 2.

As for Tang Yu’an, it was almost like he knew what the other wanted to do, giving his first skill point to his passive “Fear Deterrance”, which could raise his own basic attack damage as well as that of his surrounding teammates within a certain area.

Spider Queen didn’t have a movement skill, but her base movement speed was the fastest among all supports. After leaving the fountain, she soon passed in front of Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit, squatting in the undergrowth near the enemy tower to get a good vantage point.

Ling Suyu raised an eyebrow but didn’t say anything.

Getting a good vantage point was something a support should do.

But this was ranked matches, and in particular a solo match. There was no telling what type of teammates one would match with. For Ling Suyu, who mained the marksman position, much less a good support, even a support that was willing to tank damage for him was hard to come by. He wouldn’t even dare nitpick about other details.

But from the beginning, Tang Yu’an’s moves were those of a professional support, which was quite reassuring for Ling Suyu.

Yet soon after, he saw a red message from the chat box from the enemy team.

【Enemy】beefing with whoever steals marksman (Guardian Spirit): even Feather has copycats?

Without even waiting for Ling Suyu’s reply, the other side’s teammate responded.

【Enemy】love cookies (Deepsea Faerie): probably the actual person.

【Enemy】beefing with whoever steals marksman (Guardian Spirit): if it’s actually him then I have some choice words. You still have the time to play ranked matches after losing that badly? Not practicing?

【Enemy】100-years spent jungling (Tempest Blade): Feather, don’t mind that guy, he’s a brainless fan of UH’s.

Ling Suyu blinked.

His first reaction was—these two are duo-queuing?

His second reaction was—you’re scolding UH, but what does that have to do with me?

Ling Suyu didn’t like chatting while gaming, but because it wasn’t an official match, and he was using a professional account, he reluctantly replied with three words.

【Ally】UH-feather (Cat Spirit): ended my contract.

As soon as these three words were sent, the chat box was immediately refreshed with question marks and exclamation marks. Whether it was the ally or enemy side, they expressed shock and disbelief at this news.

The enemy marksman who was just scolding Ling Suyu for not practicing sent ten question marks in a row.

Soon after, everyone started typing.

【Ally】if i have feet i can run (Vampiric Progenitor): F*ck! There must be something wrong with UH!

【Enemy】100-years spent jungling (Tempest Blade): Why??

【Ally】I’ve never won with jungler (Blade Soul): For real?

【Enemy】beefing with whoever steals marksman (Guardian Spirit): you screwing with me?

Ling Suyu shrugged in exasperation, typing a response:

【Ally】UH-feather (Cat Spirit): it has nothing to do with UH, it was my own decision.

UH isn’t fit for me.

【Ally】UH-feather (Cat Spirit): forgot to change my ID, I’ll change it after this match.

He had used his own phone number to register this account. The heroes and skins were bought with his own money, and UH had never given him an account.

As such, after dissolving his contract, this account was still his.

Since that was the case, he didn’t need to use a side account and could continue using this one as long as he changed his ID.

Ling Suyu cleared minions while thinking of ideas for his new ID.

The chat was still active, be it from his teammates or the opponents. All of them were asking why he left UH, but Ling Suyu didn’t want to answer too much and pretended he didn’t see any of it.

At this moment, a light laugh sounded from his headphones.

Ling Suyu paused for a second, thinking that he’d heard wrong. But soon after, a relaxed and amused voice sounded: “That’s pretty good.”

“What?” Ling Suyu tilted his head.

The corners of Tang Yu’an’s lips curved upwards. While helping Ling Suyu consume the health of the minions, he said: “UH wasn’t suited for you, you deserve better.” Then he said, “Hey, while the enemy jungler is still typing, let’s go steal his monsters.”

Ling Suyu: ?

Have you no respect for chivalry!
