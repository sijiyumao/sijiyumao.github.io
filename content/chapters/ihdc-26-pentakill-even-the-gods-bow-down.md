Ling Suyu had thought Dou Jiayan was just speaking casually, but to his surprise, after saying that, he actually ran over to his side.

So, when Ling Suyu picked up the lightning strike and returned to the bottom lane, he found that besides Tang Yu’an, he now had another little tail following him.

At this point, the enemy top laner, jungler, marksman, and support were all down, with only the mid laner still alive.

Ling Suyu smoothly pushed the bottom lane minions and then focused on taking down the tower.

Dou Jiayan and Tang Yu’an both used their abilities to harass the enemy mid laner.

Under their constant barrage, the enemy mid laner couldn’t find a chance to clear minions, let alone attack Ling Suyu.

Ultimately, facing the onslaught from Fight’s three heroes, he reluctantly chose to retreat.

Green shadow blades shot from Cat Spirit’s hands, striking the tower. The health of the enemy bottom lane’s first tower rapidly dropped, and it soon collapsed with a crash!

【System】 The enemy’s tower has been destroyed!

“Come on, let’s go take down the bird.”

Almost the moment Tang Yu'an uttered those words, Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit used “Shadow Steps” to dart toward the Divine Sunbird.

Ling Suyu decisively attacked the Divine Sunbird.

The Divine Sunbird, its entire body wreathed in flames, fell under attack and let out a piercing cry. Immediately spreading its wings, and took flight, breathing fire at Ling Suyu’s Cat Spirit.

Ling Suyu dodged the attack with a quick sidestep, cast “Spirit Mark” on the bird, and then unleashed a barrage of shots at it!

Just as the Divine Sunbird launched its second attack, Tang Yu’an’s Spider Queen arrived, taking the damage for Ling Suyu and quickly securing aggro.

Immediately after, Dou Jiayan’s Deepsea Faerie joined the fray, attacking the Divine Sunbird as well.

Meanwhile, the jungler Yu He was on his way down the river, and top laner Mo Xiaoyun rushed over immediately after respawning from the fountain.

On SMG’s side, their fallen heroes had all respawned from the fountain as well.

Although the positions of Fight’s heroes weren’t visible on their minimap, anyone with even a bit of game sense would know they were currently fighting the Divine Sunbird.

“I’m going to try snatching it,” the jungler said.

“Wait for me to initiate,” the top laner Blade Soul said.

“Keep an eye on the mid laner,” SMG’s mid laner and captain said, his tone finally turning serious after losing a tower. “Marksman, watch out for his second skill—don’t get CC’d.

Jungler and top laner, take him down if you get the chance. As for the enemy marksman, he doesn’t deal much damage early on, so ignore him for now.”

“Got it,” SMG’s members all responded in unison, their focus sharpened as they kept a close eye on the Divine Sunbird, the fight already halfway done.

Ling Suyu glanced at the minimap.

Although the minimap showed nothing, judging by the respawn times of the enemy heroes at their fountain, they would be arriving in the area in around 1–2 seconds.

Sensing the danger, he fired off a few shots while taking a few steps toward his team’s jungle.

Tang Yu’an noticed his movement, raised an eyebrow, and called out as he caught up to him: “The enemy might come to steal our kill. Yanzi, get your skills ready. Yu He, pull back— watch out for ganks. Use Smite when the time is right.”

Dou Jiayan: “All set!”

Yu He: “Copy that.”

No sooner had he spoken did the enemy top laner, Blade Soul, teleport into the pit, landing two hits of damage on Mo Xiaoyun’s Skeletal Empress with “Blade Shadow Combo,” before immediately followed up with “Absolute Defense” to block his counterattack with the Heavy Hammer.

Mo Xiaoyun immediately followed up with his second skill, “Sea of Corpses” but Blade Soul reacted quickly. Almost simultaneously, he locked onto Dou Jiayan’s Deepsea Faerie and unleashed his ultimate, “Nine-Segment Blade Flash,” dodging Mo Xiaoyun’s skill damage while dealing massive damage to Dou Jiayan!

Dou Jiayan’s Flash was still on cooldown, so he couldn’t dodge the ultimate and had to rely on healing to survive. However, his second skill had been ready for some time, and he seized the moment when the Blade Soul returned to the ground to throw out a jellyfish!

The pale blue, translucent jellyfish swam out and back, striking Blade Soul right as he landed and immobilizing him.

Dou Jiayan seized the opportunity to follow up with his first skill “Water Lotus”!

Ling Suyu also took the moment to unleash a barrage of shots at him!

The enemy Blade Soul drew all of Fight’s firepower, allowing the other enemy heroes to rush into the pit and try snatching the bird.

The mid laner Nine-Tailed Fox struck first, using “Flame Wave” to knock Mo Xiaoyun away, then immediately casting “Fires of Imprisonment” at Tang Yu’an.

Tang Yu’an dodged it swiftly and unleashed his ultimate—Neurotoxin!

The timing of this ultimate was nothing short of perfect.

It fully covered all allied targets and some enemy targets, boosting the allies’ attack speed and movement speed while reducing the enemies’ movement speed.

After casting his ultimate, he immediately followed up with his first skill, “Web of Constraint”

and “Spidersilk Trap,” immobilizing both the enemy mid laner and jungler in the bird pit!

Ling Suyu didn’t miss a beat. Using “Shadow Step” to flash back to the bird pit, he locked onto the enemy mid-laner, and followed up with “Spirit Mark” into “Spiritual Explosion”, then three basic attacks!

Before the enemy mid laner could recover from the stun, his health bar began draining at a terrifying rate, and he ultimately fell under the added lightning strike buff.

【System】Cat Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Nine-tailed Fox has been slain!

“What the hell?! Is Cat Spirit’s damage really that high?!” The enemy mid laner couldn’t believe it!

The enemy jungler, Venomous Scorpion, had intended to take advantage of the chaos to steal the Divine Sunbird. But as soon as he teleported in, he was stunned by Tang Yu’an.

Just as he managed to break free, Mo Xiao’yun smashed him with a bone hammer, stunning him once again.

Ling Suyu immediately locked onto him and stood his ground, firing off a barrage of shots!

The enemy jungler watched helplessly as his health bar dwindled with each strike from the shadow blades, eventually dropping to zero.

【System】Cat Spirit, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Venomous Scorpion has been slain!

Yu He had been hiding in the nearby bushes. Seeing that the Divine Sunbird’s health was nearly depleted, he quickly chained his first and second skills to dash in and use Smite!

【System】Twin Demonblades has conquered the Divine Sunbird!

“Nice move!” Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but praise.

“Kill them!” Yu He shouted as he unleashed his ultimate ability, “Cries of Conflict”, on the enemy marksman and support!

The enemy support had already used up his shield. Seeing Yu He dash toward him, he could only step in front of the marksman to tank the hit.

However, Tang Yu’an and Ling Suyu simultaneously used Flash to keep up with Yu He’s pace.

Ling Suyu locked onto the enemy marksman’s Guardian Spirit, marked him with his freshly refreshed “Spirit Mark”, then dodged behind Tang Yu’an while firing rapidly.

The enemy marksman unleashed his ultimate, Wrath of the Spirits. Tang Yu’an activated his shield, covering himself, Ling Suyu, and Yu He, then stepped in front of Ling Suyu to take the damage head-on.

Yu He landed a dazzling skill combo on the support, while Ling Suyu, expressionless, finished off the enemy marksman.

【System】Cat Spirit, triple kill! Unstoppable!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

Take out the marksman first, then comes the support!

Ling Suyu seized the opportunity and landed a shadow blade on the enemy support, Abyssal Lord, who was already low on health.

【System】Cat Spirit, quadra kill! Godlike!

【System】The enemy Abyssal Lord has been defeated!

Quadra kill!

Just one more until a pentakill!

Ling Suyu, usually calm, suddenly felt a surge of tension, but his hands, guided by muscle memory, deftly flicked the mouse toward the bottom-left corner while lightly tapping the F key.

On screen, his Cat Spirit teleported precisely in front of Blade Soul, locking onto it and launching an attack.

Blade Soul had already been whittled down to half health by him. After recovering from the stun, it aimed to finish off Dou Jiayan’s remaining health.

But unexpectedly, Dou Jiayan unleashed his ultimate ability.

Deepsea Faerie’s ultimate, “Deepsea Ballad,” instantly consumed all marks, dealing two massive hits of damage to a single hero and slowing them.

Unlike during their previous skirmish, this time Dou Jiayan landed all three of his skills. With his marks fully stacked, the damage from his ultimate was devastating.

Blade Soul’s remaining half of health was instantly wiped down to a single sliver.

He hurriedly used Flash at top speed to try to escape.

To his surprise, just as he teleported out of Deepsea Faerie’s ultimate range, a transparent cat appeared right in front of him.

【System】Cat Spirit, pentakill! Even the gods bow down!

【System】The enemy Blade Soul has been slain!

【System】No one can stop Cat Spirit now!

【System】The enemy heroes have all fallen! Victory belongs to us!

Amidst a barrage of system notifications, Ling Suyu looked at the enemy heroes strewn across his screen. His heart was pounding so fast that even the hand gripping his mouse began to tremble slightly.

How long has it been since he last scored a pentakill?

“Wow! A pentakill!” Even though it was Ling Suyu who had secured the pentakill, Dou Jiayan seemed even more excited than he was. “Feather is so strong!”

Ling Suyu froze for a moment, thinking he’d misheard. Once he realized what had been said, he quickly replied, “Umm, this was a team fight. Winning a team fight is the result of everyone’s combined effort; I just finished them off.”

Back when he was still at UH, his teammates would either insist that his kills were entirely thanks to them, or blame him for not carrying the team and instead going for kills.

Actually, they weren’t wrong. Without the support’s protection, the top laner’s tanking, the mid laner’s harassment, and the jungler’s ganks, he would never have been able to deal damage so comfortably.

But every now and then, he’d at least have liked to hear a word of praise.

Now, however, the praise he’d been craving was coming at him in waves, and he found himself a bit overwhelmed.

Tang Yu’an: “Of course Feather is strong! Why else do you think I signed him?”

Dou Jiayan: “Because of his looks?”

Tang Yu’an: “……”

Dou Jiayan: “But this teamfight felt so smooth! I could really feel our damage!”

Mo Xiaoyun: “You have the nerve to say that? I didn’t even see you in that last teamfight!”

Dou Jiayan: “What are you talking about? If I hadn’t held off Blade Soul for you guys, would you have been able to dive the backline so easily?”

Yu He: “Exactly. If you hadn’t been tied up by Blade Soul, would Guardian Spirit have even survived long enough for me to join the fight?”

Dou Jiayan: “…”

Dou Jiayan decided to become a hermit. Dou Jiayan was completely devastated.

Ling Suyu couldn’t help but chuckle as he spoke up in his defense: “Yanzi is actually quite useful. I only dared to step in because I saw you’d stunned Blade.”

“Ahh, Feather, you really are an angel!” Dou Jiayan was so moved that tears welled up in his eyes, “I knew following you was the right choice! You can trust me to watch your back—I’ll stun every single villain who dares come near you!”

Tang Yu’an: ?

“Wow, so you’re just ignoring me, huh?” Tang Yu’an couldn’t help but complain. “Then next time you’ll play support and I’ll go mid lane?”

As he spoke, he picked up the egg left behind by the Divine Sunbird.

【System】Spider Queen has claimed the Divine Sunbird’s token!

After picking up the egg, he watched Ling Suyu run toward the mid lane and couldn’t help but smile slightly as he followed him, egg in hand.

Dou Jiayan followed the two to the mid lane while responding, “Aren’t you supposed to be calling the shots? If you weren’t taking charge, how am I supposed to get a chance to play mid?”

“I’d still get my turn,” Mo Xiaoyun was the one who replied, “If the captain wasn’t busy calling the shots, I’d be the first one to get pushed out.”

“I’d be in danger too,” said the jungler Yu He, “If I had the captain’s game sense, I’d be the one calling the shots.”

Ling Suyu blinked. Although he already knew Tang Yu’an could play any position except marksman, he hadn’t expected his teammates to hold him in such high regard.

No wonder they all listened to Tang Yu’an so much—in the face of absolute skill, nothing else mattered.

“Marksman is the only safe position,” Yu He suddenly said.

“That’s right,” Dou Jiayan agreed. “Feather just needs to compete with Jie-ge.”

Hearing this out of the blue, Ling Suyu froze for a moment.

Perhaps sensing something, Tang Yu’an spoke up: “If your Jie-ge is going to play, it won’t be until after the Spring Seasonals. Barring any surprises, Feather will be the starter from the first match to the last in the Spring Seasonals, so you’d better cooperate well.”

Dou Jiayan: “I know, I know! Haven’t I been coordinating well enough? I helped Feather stun Blade Soul! Right, Feather?”

Ling Suyu cleared his throat.

Tang Yu’an: “Ugh… give him an inch and he’ll take a mile.”

Yu He: “So noisy.”

Mo Xiaoyun: “Chirping away—I guess you could say as expected of a bird?”

Dou Jiayan: ?

“Pfft.” Ling Suyu couldn’t help but laugh.

Actually, he felt that it wasn’t just Dou Jiayan—everyone on the Fight team talked a lot.

But he didn’t mind.

On the contrary, he kind of liked this atmosphere.

It felt rather lively?

Accompanied by the system prompt “Release the Divine Sunbird”, Tang Yu’an placed the Divine Sunbird, which would help push the tower, in the mid lane.

With the help of the bird, Fight smoothly took down the first mid-lane tower and immediately moved on to the second.

Meanwhile, over on SMG’s side, a deep self-reflection session was underway.

“Sorry, I underestimated Cat Spirit’s damage,” the captain and mid laner admitted first.

“He also picked up the lightning strike,” the marksman pointed out.

“Not only that, but he also had the Spider Queen's auto-attack damage bonus,” the support added.

“And he's ahead in gold,” the jungler said. “He's got one more item than us. Ugh—that really hurts.”

“It’s mostly because the enemy mid and support are so annoying,” said the top laner Blade Soul. “Let’s focus on cleansing the controls first, otherwise we won’t even be able to play this match.”

“Yeah, let’s dispel the CCs,” the mid-laner captain replied, outlining the plan for the rest of the game. “We underestimated Feather. From now on, I’ll mark the enemy mid laner, the top laner and jungler will mark Feather, and the marksman should stay back—don’t rush in. If we can kill Feather, kill him first; if not, watch out for the enemy jungler’s ganks. We shouldn’t have let them pick Twin Demonblades.”

“Yeah, it’s so frustrating,” the marksman, who had been instantly killed twice by Yu He, said through gritted teeth, “But there’s nothing we can do since the system gave them first pick.

Besides, compared to Twin Demonblades, those mid laners were the real headaches—we can’t afford to let any of them get picked.”

“True, that Swallow guy on the other team is a real pain too.” The captain, a mid-laner, recalled the terror of being dominated by Dou Jiayan’s Phoenix Soul during the last Summer Seasonal.

Although Dou Jiayan’s Phoenix Soul didn’t end up with great stats—11 kills, 11 deaths, and 21 assists—he dealt the highest damage in the game, nearly double that of the second-highest.

There wasn’t a single weak player on the Fight roster.

If it weren’t for their AD carry Chen Jie’s hand injury during last season’s Summer Seasonal, YJ probably wouldn’t have stood a chance at the World Championship.

But now, Fight has a new carry.

While it’s unclear how this would impact Fight’s overall style and gameplay, judging by that team fight earlier, Fight hadn’t been weakened at all.

On the contrary, they seemed to have become even stronger.

Was it because of Feather?

Yes. And yet, not entirely.

“It feels like Feather has integrated well with Fight,” the mid-laner captain concluded. “It seems they’ve been training together for a while. Let’s review this game right after we finish it!”

“Mm-hmm.”

While SMG was discussing this, their second mid lane tower fell.

By the time they respawned from the fountain and returned to the lane to clear minions, Fight had already stolen both of their early-game dragons.

SMG was furious upon realizing this, but there was nothing they could do.

Their composition lacked crowd control, so they could only focus on the early game; if they couldn’t instantly eliminate the backline in the late game, they had no chance of winning team fights.

So, Fight aggressively took over their jungle, with Ling Suyu handling the steals, and everyone else—except the jungler—surrounding him: Tang Yu’an took the damage, Dou Jiayan helped wear down the enemy, and Mo Xiaoyun secured vision while watching out for enemy ambushes.

The only one not staying close to Ling Suyu was the jungler, Yu He. After clearing his own jungle, he headed to the top lane to push the minion wave and, while he was at it, took down the enemy top lane’s first tower.

Ling Suyu had never felt this at ease raiding the enemy’s jungle while playing Glorious.

Even though he’d always played as the carry, this was the first time he’d actually experienced the treatment a carry deserved.

How should he put it? It felt like the whole team was spoiling him.

As it turned out, Fight’s decision to protect their marksman was a wise one.

To force Fight out of their jungle, SMG opted for a risky strategy: the mid laner used his ultimate to harass, then initiated with his second skill, while the jungler and top laner flanked from behind to gank.

Meanwhile, the marksman followed the support into the jungle, locking onto Cat Spirit and unleashing his ultimate!

The moment Ling Suyu spotted them, he flicked his mouse—a “Shadow Step” carried him out of the enemy marksman’s Guardian Spirit’s range. After dodging the ultimate and skills, he instantly teleported back, counter-targeting the enemy marksman with Spirit Mark and Spiritual Burst!

SMG had expected him to use “Shadow Step” to escape—that was precisely why their jungler and top laner had flanked from behind.

But they never imagined he would use Flash to dash right back in after dodging the ultimate!

SMG’s support hurriedly activated his shield, but despite stacking it on top of Guardian Spirit’s shield, it still couldn’t fully negate the damage from Cat Spirit’s ultimate.

After breaking free from the crowd control, Mo Xiaoyun landed a “Heavy Bone Hammer”, stunning the enemy mid-laner.

The enemy mid laner immediately used a cleansing ability and fled using Flash.

Mo Xiaoyun ignored him and used his ultimate, “Blood Link”, to latch onto the enemy support, positioning himself between the enemy marksman and Ling Suyu.

At that moment, SMG’s jungler, Venomous Scorpion, charged in. He failed to hit Ling Suyu and instead collided with the support, Tang Yu’an.

Thanks to Tang Yu'an's warning, Dou Jiayan knew there was a chance the enemy would flank him, so he wasn't surprised at all when he saw him. His fingers flew across the keyboard as he chained three skills together, and after stacking up his marks, he finished him off with an ultimate!

【System】Deepsea Faerie, one kill! Swift as lightning!

【System】The enemy Venomous Scorpion has been slain!

With the enemy jungler down, Tang Yu'an immediately joined Mo Xiaoyun, standing together in front of Ling Suyu to block nearly all incoming damage for him.

Ling Suyu followed the two as they pushed forward, auto-attacking as they moved. Green shadow blades flew one after another, like the call of the grim reaper.

【System】Cat Spirit, one kill!

【System】The enemy Abyssal Lord has been slain!

【System】Cat Spirit, double kill! Peerless!

【System】The enemy Guardian Spirit has been slain!

【System】Cat Spirit, triple kill! Unstoppable!

【System】The enemy Nine-tailed Fox has been slain!

“Unbelievable! Actually unbelievable!” SMG’s mid laner shouted in disbelief. “This Cat Spirit is just insane!”

“No way! Without my abilities, I can’t out-damage him!” the marksman shouted in response.

“Damn! Dodging ultimates with dashes, re-entering with Flash—is this even possible?” The jungler looked as if he’d seen a ghost. “Who said Feather was scared of dying? Is this what you call scared of dying?!”

The top laner playing Blade Soul had originally flanked from behind with the jungler, but upon seeing Ling Suyu use Flash to return, he decisively abandoned the gank and ran toward the river.

He knew that even if he joined the fight, this teamfight was a sure loss. So, rather than follow his teammates to their deaths, he decided to camp in the bushes near the dragon pit—that way, if Fight went for the dragon, he’d still have a chance to steal it.

However, as he was running away, Feather’s performance at the White Fox Cup suddenly flashed through his mind—during the White Fox Cup, Feather had done exactly the same thing as him, abandoning his teammates who were still fighting and fleeing on his own several times.

There were a few times when it was clear that UH had picked a bad moment to initiate, and even if he had joined the fight, he wouldn’t have won.

But there were also a few other times when joining the fight didn’t guarantee a win, but it didn’t guarantee a loss either.

That’s exactly why Feather got flamed—why give up so easily? Why not try, put in the effort, and fight for the team?

But what if, in Feather’s eyes, joining the fight wasn’t “not necessarily a loss,” but instead “a guaranteed loss”?

In any case, in the eyes of the SMG top laner, a marksman who teleports back immediately after dodging an ultimate with a Flash couldn’t possibly be afraid of dying.

At least, it wasn’t simply a fear of death.

He teleported back knowing full well that the team fight was a sure win.

In other words, he had a clear assessment of whether the team fight could be won.

If he thought they couldn’t win, he’d flee without hesitation—or simply not join the fight at all.

But if he thought they could win, he might charge in more fiercely than anyone else.

“I think we underestimated Feather,” the top laner suddenly said.

“Isn’t it Cat Spirit we underestimated?” the marksman tried to correct him. “I didn’t expect Cat Spirit to be this terrifying once he got an economic advantage! But without Spider as support, his damage is still pretty low.”

“No,” the top laner said, pursing his lips. “It’s not Cat Spirit. It’s Feather. We underestimated Feather as a player.”
